# Main Content

Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), Gandhinagar, celebrated the 11th International Yoga Day with enthusiasm, aligning with the 2025 theme — "Yoga for One Earth, One Health." The event, supported by the Gandhinagar Municipal Corporation, reflected the university’s commitment to health and holistic well-being. A certified yoga instructor led the session, guiding participants through accessible yoga postures, breathing exercises (Pranayama), and relaxation techniques. Designed for all age groups and fitness levels, the session promoted inclusivity. Yoga experts also engaged with students, faculty, and staff, emphasizing the long-term benefits of regular practice for physical health and emotional balance. Through such initiatives, DAU continues to foster a healthy, mindful campus culture that supports both academic growth and overall well-being.
