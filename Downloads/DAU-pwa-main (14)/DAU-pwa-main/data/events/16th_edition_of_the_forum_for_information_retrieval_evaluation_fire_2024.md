# 16th edition of the Forum for Information Retrieval Evaluation (FIRE 2024)

## Overview

DA-IICT hosted the 16th edition of the Forum for Information Retrieval Evaluation (FIRE), a global conference, at its campus from December 12–15, 2024. The event brought together researchers from academia and industry to explore multilingual information retrieval, large-scale evaluation frameworks, and natural language processing, with peer-reviewed research tracks, shared tasks, an industry track, and a doctoral consortium.

## Main Content

- **Venue:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar, Gujarat
- **Dates:** 12–15 December 2024

Faculty, students, or professionals working in IR and NLP were invited to register and join the forum for discussions, collaborative research opportunities, and networking with global experts.

Students presenting a paper, participating in any track, or attending FIRE 2024 were eligible to apply for an ACM SIGIR student travel grant of up to ₹5,000. The application deadline was 12th November, with applicants notified by 18th November.

Overall conference details: http://fire.irsi.org.in/
