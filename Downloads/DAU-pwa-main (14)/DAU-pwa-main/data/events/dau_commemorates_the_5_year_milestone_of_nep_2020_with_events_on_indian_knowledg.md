# DAU commemorates the 5-year Milestone of NEP 2020 with Events on Indian Knowledge Systems

## Overview

"Stories of the Past: The Indian Knowledge System Quiz Competition" — held as part of DAU's commemoration of the 5-year milestone of NEP 2020, promoting India's Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS).

## Main Content

- **Date:** 1 August 2025
- **Time:** 11:00 AM onwards
- **Registration window:** 28 July 2025 onwards
- **Venue:** Resource Centre

First ten winners receive a prize; all participants receive an e-certificate of participation.

### Objectives

The Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS), under the umbrella of NEP 2020, aim to promote, preserve, and propagate India's diverse intellectual traditions. Key objectives:

- **Promote Awareness** — introduce participants to the heritage, science, and philosophy of the Indian Knowledge System, including Vedas, Ayurveda, astronomy, mathematics, literature, and arts.
- **Encourage Appreciation** — foster respect and interest in indigenous systems of knowledge and their modern relevance.
- **Enhance Learning** — reinforce academic concepts related to Indian history, culture, and science in an engaging, interactive format.
- **Test Understanding** — assess participants' grasp of traditional Indian knowledge and its contemporary applications.
- **Inspire Research and Inquiry** — motivate students to explore original Indian texts, practices, and innovations for further academic study.
- **Bridge Traditional and Modern Knowledge** — highlight connections between ancient Indian wisdom and modern disciplines like medicine, architecture, linguistics, and ecology.
- **Encourage Teamwork and Healthy Competition** — develop collaboration, critical thinking, and problem-solving through team-based quizzing.
- **Support NEP 2020 Goals** — align with NEP's emphasis on integrating Indian Knowledge Systems into education curricula.
