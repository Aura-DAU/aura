# Placements and Internship Affairs Action Committee (PIAAC)

**Office Order:** DG/Office Order/2025, dated 06 November 2025

An Action Committee for Placements and Internship Affairs has been constituted. The scope of PIAAC is valid until the concerned student(s) placed under PIAAC are awarded their degree at DAU Gandhinagar's Convocation.

## Scope and Terms of Reference

The Committee is responsible for policy guidelines and implementation strategies for placements and academic internships. It deals with:

1. All disciplinary matters related to a student's placement, job, and academic internship, including but not limited to:
   - Misbehavior of a student in a company
   - Bad-mouthing DAU at a company, or bad-mouthing a company at DAU
   - Doing an industrial internship alongside an Academic Internship under a faculty member (from DAU or elsewhere) without informing both parties
   - Undisclosed parallel internships, breach of placement policy
   - Activities harming DAU's reputation or the employer-university relationship
   - Canvassing/misleading resume info, non-joining after accepting an offer
   - Attendance violations (minimum 90% mandatory for placement activities)

**Process:** PIAAC decides whether a matter needs formal action, informal resolution, or referral to the Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC). Misconduct discovered before degree conferral can still be redirected to the DAC at PIAAC's discretion.

## Indicative Punishment Table

*(Merged in from `student_services/placements.md` during dedup, 2026-08-09 — sourced from the live placements page rather than the PIAAC PDF itself.)*

| Offense | Punishment |
|---|---|
| Absence from mandatory employability sessions >1 day | Ineligible for Day-0 companies (exception: documented medical reason) |
| Canvassing/misleading resume info | Debarred for next 1 company |
| Dress code violation during recruitment process | Debarred for next 1 company |
| Mobile phone use during placement process | Debarred for next 1 company |
| Impersonation/proxy attendance | Debarred for next 1 company |
| Spreading false/misleading placement info | Debarred for next 1 company |
| Failure to attend a process after registering | Debarred for next 2 companies (exception: documented medical reason) |
| Direct/indirect contact with company reps outside official channels | Debarred for next 2 companies |
| Misconduct toward company officials | Debarred for next 2 companies |
| Misconduct toward Placement Cell/SPC reps | Debarred for next 2 companies |
| Unlawful/prohibited activity connected to placements | Debarred for next 2 companies; possible DAC referral |
| Unfair practices in company assessments (unauthorized devices, remote test locations) | Debarred for next 3 companies (exceptions possible for documented reasons) |
| Interference in official placement procedures (slot manipulation, lobbying) | Debarred for next 3 companies |
| Unauthorized disclosure of company-specific info (CTC, schedule) to other orgs | Debarred for next 4 companies |
| Forgery/impersonation/identity misuse | Debarred for next 4 companies |
| Financial misconduct/fraud at the company | Ineligible for placement; referred to DAC + concerned faculty body |
| Gender-related complaint from company | Same as above (ineligible + DAC referral) |
| Defamatory/inappropriate content about company/DAU on social media | Same as above |
| Declining an already-accepted campus job offer | Ineligible for further placement |
| Defaming the University/Placement Cell | Same as financial misconduct tier |
| Leaving an internship early without written approval | Referred to DAC + faculty body, possible denial of future references |
| Unethical practices reported by company (leaving after training, lack of commitment) | Same as above |
| Any indiscipline/misconduct during internship, reported before degree awarded | May be referred to DAC at PIAAC's discretion |

**Fines & Appeals:** PIAAC may impose monetary fines (deducted from caution deposit) tracked in a dedicated "PIAAC Fine" ledger used solely for placement activities. Students may submit **one appeal to the Director General within 2 weeks** of a PIAAC decision, with documentary evidence; the Director General's decision is final and binding.
