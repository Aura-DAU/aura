# Company Categorization Rules

### Overview

Dhirubhai Ambani University categorizes recruiting companies based on the Cost to Company (CTC) offered for Job (J) or Internship + Job (I+J) profiles. For Internship (I) only offers, the post-internship CTC (if converted to a full-time job) is used for categorization.

### The Categories

* **Category I:** Companies offering a CTC of **10 LPA and Above**.
* **Category II:** Companies offering a CTC **Below 10 LPA**.
