# E-Governance Policy (From 2017-18)

## Scope

Covers: General Administration, Student Admission, Examination, Accounts and Finance.

## Objectives

- Implement e-governance across all institute functions for simpler, efficient governance.
- Promote transparency and accountability.
- Achieve a paperless environment.
- Provide easy, quick access to information.
- Make campus **Wi-Fi enabled**.
- Make classrooms **ICT-enabled** (desktops, laptops, smartboards, projectors, etc.).

## Policy Areas

### Website
Acts as the institute's information center for activities, notices, and courses. A dedicated service provider/web designer is appointed; staff receive training for updates. A **Website Committee** administers/maintains/updates it regularly and identifies needed changes. Important notifications must go live promptly.

### Student Admission
Open, transparent process backed by ethical practices/regulations; brochure published on the website with admission guidelines. An **Admission Portal** manages applications, withdrawals, and fee submission. Students submit a separate Online Application Form via the Admission Cell's software.

### Accounts
Maintained on **Tally** (kept updated) for financial records, P&L, and balance sheets; all analysis reports generated through Tally, with appropriate security for transaction confidentiality. Staff receive regular training. Also uses **SARAL – Payroll Management System** for salary calculation, salary slips, and bank disbursement (TDS, Provident Fund, allowances, etc. all managed by this system). Staff reports can be generated for all members. Payments made/received online (NEFT, RTGS, bank transfers).

### Administration
- **Attendance Management Software** for administrative staff and teaching faculty — tracks attendance, generates monthly/semester-end reports, auto-calculates internal-assessment attendance marks. Includes a **biometric system**.
- Administrative office uses Advanced Excel and File Management System tools.
- Goal: hassle-free, convenient, paperless administration.
- Students should get maximum services online.
- Institute continually looks for administrative automation opportunities.
- Admin staff get adequate training on new technology.

### Examination
Uses various online systems for different activities — **EOG, OPUS, SRS, SEGS**, etc. Regulated by the **Controller of Examination** under defined institute policy; the e-governance policy is adopted accordingly for this area.
