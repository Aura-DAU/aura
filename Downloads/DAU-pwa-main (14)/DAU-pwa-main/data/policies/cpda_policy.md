# Cumulative Professional Development Allowance (CPDA) Policy

## Purpose

CPDA supports faculty in presenting research at academic conferences and related professional development. Covers:
- Presenting a paper at international or national conferences/symposiums.
- Publication expenses (overlength charges, open-access charges, etc.).
- Books, computer accessories for research (e.g. external hard disks), and contingent items — **max ₹20,000/year**.
- Membership of professional societies — **max ₹20,000/year**.
- Attending training/research programs for professional development.

Faculty are encouraged to publish in high-visibility, well-attended reputed conferences and journals.

## Terms of the Financial Grant

- After service confirmation, each faculty member gets a **CPDA grant of ₹1.0 lakh on 1 April of every year (Y)**, valid through the current financial year plus the next two years — i.e., until **30 March of Y+3**.
- The grant received on **1 April of Year Y-3** which remains unutilized by **30 March of Y** **lapses**.
- So the aggregate CPDA available on 1 April of Year Y is **₹3 lakhs** (one lakh each for Y, Y-1, Y-2).
- If the ₹3 lakh is depleted, faculty can request up to **₹2 lakh advance** from Years Y+1 and Y+2 — **max negative balance: ₹2 lakh**. Any negative balance must be repaid upon employment termination.
- Faculty can request support multiple times within a financial year, up to the established limit. **CPDA funds cannot be used while on lien or extraordinary leave.**

**Prioritization parameters** for support requests: (i) sponsored projects earned by the faculty member (as PI/co-PI), including financial outlay; (ii) number of doctoral students guided.

## Incentive for Excellence in Faculty Research

Faculty receive financial awards for publications in top-tier journals/conferences. Once an individual hits the annual incentive cap, further awards are credited as a **bonus to their CPDA** — this bonus remains valid for as long as the faculty member is a full-time employee, and can be used for conference travel, electronic accessories, and professional memberships.

## Application Procedure

Applications go to the **Dean of R&D via email**, including:
a. Conference dates and location.
b. Conference rank: A*, A, B, DAU-Approved, or other.
c. Acceptance notification/rate, if applicable.
d. Paper title, abstract, authors' list.
e. Manuscript draft of the accepted paper.
f. Requested amount broken down by registration, travel, accommodation.
g. Travel/accommodation/other expenses must conform to institutional guidelines (use the Host Institution's Guest House if available).
h. Available balance in the faculty's CPDA.

- Faculty may use their CPDA balance to support conference travel for their **UG, PG, or PhD students** — the faculty member endorses and sends the application on the student's behalf.
- Grants **under ₹1 lakh**: Dean R&D approves directly. **Over ₹1 lakh**: needs Director's approval.
- Decision communicated by Dean R&D to the applicant via email.

**Effective date: 1 January 2026.** This policy supersedes the Conference Travel Support Policy dated 15 May 2024.
