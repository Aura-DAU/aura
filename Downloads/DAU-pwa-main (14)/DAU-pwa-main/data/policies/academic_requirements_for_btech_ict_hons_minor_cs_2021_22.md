# Academic Requirements for the B.Tech (Honours) in ICT with Minor in Computational Science Program

*(Effective from Autumn 2021-22)*

## Source

- **Institution:** Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT), Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_BTech(ICT_CS)2021_wef Aut 2021-22.pdf`
- **Dated:** October 2022 (DA-IICT, Gandhinagar)

## Scope

This document contains rules **specific** to the B.Tech (Honours) in ICT with Minor in Computational Science program. It supplements — rather than replaces — [Academic Requirements for the B.Tech (ICT) Program](./Academic-Requirements-for-BTech-ICT-Program-2021-22.md): per the source document, "Regulations 1 and 2" (Registration; Auditing/PG Courses) and "Regulations 3.2 to 8" (Summer Semester through Minimum Academic Performance) apply uniformly from the base B.Tech (ICT) document. Only the sections below are specific to this Honours/Minor program.

## 3.1 Course Load — Regular Semesters (Program-Specific)

For this program only: a student may register for additional courses provided the total course count does not exceed 7 (excluding Co-Curricular Activities) and total credits do not exceed **30** (higher than the base B.Tech (ICT) program's 28-credit cap) — rural internship is excluded from this limit. Underload: minimum 4 courses / 12 credits (fewer than 4 courses permitted after completing 7 regular semesters, as in the base program).

## 9. Award of Degree (Program-Specific)

**9.1 Requirements for the B.Tech (Honours) in ICT with Minor in Computational Science degree:**

a. Pass all foundation and elective courses, including internships and BTP/ITP, prescribed in the curriculum.

b. **Minimum 147 total course credits:** 90 foundation + 18 from designated Honours/Minor in Computational Science courses + 39 elective credits, split as:
   - ICT electives: minimum 3 courses, at least 1 with a Computational Science feature
   - Technical electives: minimum 4 courses, at least 1 with a Computational Science feature
   - Science electives: minimum 2 courses, at least 1 with a Computational Science feature
   - HASS electives: minimum 1 course
   - Open electives: minimum 2 courses

   Plus a minimum of 19 credits from Internships and BTP/ITP.

c. Minimum 735 grade points from coursework (147 × 5.0; internship/BTP credits excluded from this count). Shortfalls require additional elective courses (up to category maximums).

d. Minimum Final CPI of 6.5. A student who does not reach CPI 6.5 but meets the base B.Tech (ICT) degree requirements receives the standard **B.Tech (ICT)** degree instead.

e. At least 8 registered regular semesters (excluding summer semester), with all institute dues paid.

f. No pending case of indiscipline.

**9.2 Final CPI and Class:** Computed on the same basis as described in §9.1 (best-CPI combination subject to program-specific minimums); other courses count as extra credits, excluded from final CPI. BTP grade points count; ITP (Pass/Fail) does not. Credit/grade-point requirements may change with Director approval if program/course credit structures change. **Distinction:** CPI ≥ 9.0. **First Class:** CPI ≥ 6.5 and < 9.0.

**9.3 Certificate of Academic Accomplishment:** Same provision as the base B.Tech (ICT) program (see Clause 8.5 of that document) — available on application for students who don't complete degree requirements within the maximum period.

## 10. Transfer Between B.Tech (ICT) and this Honours/Minor Program

- **10.1 Continuation:** A student admitted to this Honours program must maintain a minimum CPI of 6.5 after Semester III to continue; otherwise, they move to the standard B.Tech (ICT) program (subject to meeting its requirements).
- **10.2 Transfer in:** A B.Tech (ICT) student with CPI ≥ 7.5 may apply to the Dean (Academic Programs) at the end of Semester III for transfer into this Honours program. Granted subject to seat availability, in descending order of CPI.

## 11. Glossary

Same definitions as the base B.Tech (ICT) document: Backlog Course, Course Credit, Grade Points, Semester, Semester Grade Report, Transcript, UG Committee — see [Academic Requirements for the B.Tech (ICT) Program](./Academic-Requirements-for-BTech-ICT-Program-2021-22.md#11-glossary).
