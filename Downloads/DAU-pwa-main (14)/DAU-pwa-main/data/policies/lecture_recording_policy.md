# Policy on Recording of Lectures and Academic Sessions

## Overview

This document outlines DAU's guidelines on recording lectures, tutorials, and academic sessions by students and faculty.

## Main Content

### General Policy

- Recording of lectures (audio or video) by students **requires explicit prior permission** from the course instructor.
- Unauthorized recording of lectures is considered a **violation of the Plagiarism Prevention Framework and Academic Integrity Policy**.
- Recording in **examination halls is strictly prohibited** at all times.

### Permitted Recording

| Scenario | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Student recording for personal study | Prior written/verbal permission from instructor |
| Institute-recorded lectures (online/hybrid) | Managed by the academic office or LMS |
| Research or documentation purposes | Written approval from HOD and instructor |

### Prohibited Activities

- Recording lectures without instructor permission.
- Sharing recordings on social media or public platforms without consent.
- Using recordings for commercial purposes.
- Recording other students without their consent.

### Consequences of Violation

- Violation treated as an act of academic misconduct.
- Referred to the **Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC)**.
- May result in grade penalty, academic warning, or more severe disciplinary action.

### Faculty Rights

- Instructors retain **intellectual property rights** over their lecture content.
- Instructors may record their own sessions for distribution to enrolled students on the LMS.

## Important Information

- When in doubt, **always ask the instructor** before recording.
- Institute-provided lecture recordings (on Moodle/LMS) are for enrolled students only — redistribution is not permitted.
- This policy applies to **all modes of instruction**: in-person, online, and hybrid.

## Contact Information

- **Office:** Academic Office / Registrar, DAU, Gandhinagar
