# Malpractices/Improper Conduct in Examinations and Consequences

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Rules-Guidelines/Examinations Related/Guidelines of Exams Malpractices.pdf`
- **Dated:** 26 February 2021

## Overview

The examinations authority (invigilators, course instructors, Controller of Examinations, or Examinations Scrutiny Committee) examines malpractice/improper conduct cases reported or noticed during in-semester and end-semester exams (physical or online), as authorized by the Director or Dean (Academic Programs). A student committing any of the acts below is considered to have committed examination malpractice and faces the indicated consequence.

## Clauses: Nature of Malpractice and Punishment

**Clause 1 — Possession of unauthorized material:** Possessing any book, class notes, cell phones, smart gadgets, or other subject-related material during the exam (open book/notes exams follow the specific course announcement on what's allowed).
> **Punishment:** Expulsion from the exam hall; cancellation of performance in that exam/course; **not eligible for any supplementary exam** for that course's exam component.

**Clause 2 — Communication with other candidates:** Assisting or receiving communication (verbal, digital, or other) with other candidates about course matter, inside or outside the hall.
> **Punishment:** Expulsion and cancellation of performance for **all candidates involved**; none eligible for supplementary exams for that course's exam component.

**Clause 3 — Copying:** Copying from papers, gadgets, or other material relevant to the exam subject.
> **Punishment:** Expulsion; cancellation of performance in that exam **and potentially all other courses already examined**, based on severity; barred from remaining exams in the current exam schedule.

**Clause 4 — Objectionable language:** Using objectionable, abusive, or offensive language in the exam hall/setup, in the answer paper, or in writing to the examinations authority.
> **Punishment:** A violation letter from the Controller of Examinations; cancellation of performance in that subject/component.

**Clause 5 — Mass cheating:** Sharing answer papers, cheat sheets, or soft copies (or other means) in mass cheating.
> **Punishment:** Cancellation of performance for **all candidates involved**, not eligible for supplementary exams; the course instructor may also cancel the evaluative component for the **entire class**, based on severity.

**Clause 6 — Impersonation:** Impersonating another candidate.
> **Punishment:** Fail grade in the subject; recommended to appear before the Disciplinary Action Committee.

**Clause 7 — Repeat offenses:** Identified in malpractice on more than one occasion.
> **Punishment:** Fail grade in the subject; debarred from subsequent exams of other courses that semester; recommended to appear before the Disciplinary Action Committee.

## Unresolved or Unlisted Matters

Any matter not fitting the above clauses is referred to the Dean (Academic Programs). Unresolved matters that do fit the clauses are also referred to the Dean. The Dean (Academic Programs) or an appointed Examinations Scrutiny Committee may re-examine such matters and recommend additional penalty or relax an imposed one.

## Decision-Making Process

The examinations authority (as authorized by the Director or Dean) examines all malpractice matters and recommends its decision to the Controller of Examinations office. The examinations authority **must** involve the Dean (Academic Programs) before finalizing any decision. The Dean may consult the Director for unresolved or disputed matters.
