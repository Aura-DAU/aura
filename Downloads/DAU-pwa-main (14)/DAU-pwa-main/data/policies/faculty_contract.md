# Special Recruitment Drive – Teaching Track Faculty (on Contract)

Applications are invited from suitable candidates for teaching track faculty positions (on contract) in the area of **Computer Science** and **Data Science**.

---

## Eligible Areas

Candidates who satisfy the following eligibility criteria and have substantial industrial experience in the areas of:

- **Software Engineering**
- **Cloud Computing**
- **Systems Programming**
- **Computer Networking**
- **Artificial Intelligence**
- **Databases**
- **Data Analytics**
- And related areas

are encouraged to apply for this position.

---

## Contract Details

- **Initial contract period:** 3 years
- **Renewable:** Based on satisfactory performance
- **Teaching Load:** Two courses per semester = **four courses in an academic year**

---

## PhD Opportunity

Candidates without a PhD degree, who joined as Teaching Track Faculty, will be encouraged to join the PhD studies at the Institute after fulfilling the admissions criteria of the PhD program, along with the primary job engagement.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

### (i) Educational Qualification
A **Master's degree** in:
- Computer Science
- Information Technology
- Or allied disciplines from an Institute of repute

**Also encouraged:** Candidates with a master's degree in:
- Statistics
- Applied Mathematics
- Economics
- Or allied areas with substantial experience in Data Analytics, Data Visualization

### (ii) Experience
**5+ years experience** in the **software industry** and/or **academia** in the areas as mentioned above (Software Engineering, Cloud Computing, Systems Programming, Computer Networking, AI, Databases, Data Analytics).

---

## Remuneration

**INR 12 Lakh per annum CTI (consolidated)**

Deserving candidates may be offered additional incentives commensurate with qualification and experience.

---

## Benefits & Perquisites

- **Insurance Coverage:** Rs. 10 lakh per year for self and family (floater). Premium paid by the Institute
- **Medical support:** Doctors on campus. Basic medicines are disbursed from the Medical Centre
- **Leave benefits:**
  - 8 days of casual leave
  - 60 days' vacation in a year

---

## Application Process

Applicants should submit:
1. A detailed CV
2. A statement of teaching interest

**Next screening will be done by the end of December 2024.**  
The screenings will continue till all the positions are filled.

---

## How to Apply

Applicants should email the following to **dean_faculty[at]dau[dot]ac[dot]in** with Subject line: **Teaching Track Faculty**

**(a)** CV  
**(b)** A statement on teaching interests/expertise  
**(c)** Names and contact details of **two references**
