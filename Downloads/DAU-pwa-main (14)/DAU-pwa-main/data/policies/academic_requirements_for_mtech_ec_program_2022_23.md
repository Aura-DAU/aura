# Academic Requirements for the M.Tech (EC) Program with Specialization in Wireless Communication and Embedded Systems

*(Effective from the 2022-23 batch)*

## Source

- **Institution:** Jointly offered by DA-IICT, Gandhinagar and **CR Rao AIMSCS, Hyderabad**
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_M Tech_(EC)_Program_wef 2022-23.pdf`
- **Dated:** May 2023

## Scope

Master of Technology (Electronics and Communications) with specialization in Wireless Communication and Embedded Systems — M.Tech (EC) — is a 2-year (4-semester), full-time postgraduate program **jointly offered by DA-IICT and CR Rao AIMSCS, Hyderabad**, governed by these rules, covering post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

> This document's rules (Registration, Academic Requirements, Academic Performance, Teaching Assistantships, Migration Rules, and Glossary) are **word-for-word identical** to the M.Tech (ICT) 2022-23 Academic Requirements, except for the two differences noted below. Rather than duplicate the full rule text, see [Academic Requirements for the M.Tech (ICT) Program](./Academic-Requirements-for-MTech-ICT-Program-2022-23.md) §§1–6 for the complete shared rule text (Registration categories, Late Registration, Academic Advising, Semester Load — 15/16 credits in semester 1, 14/15 in semester 2, 12/13 in semester 3, 12 in semester 4, min. 58 total credits — Adding/Dropping, Withdrawal, Duration (min. 58 credits, max. 3 years / 6 semesters, sponsored category 4 years / 8 semesters), Audit Courses, the full AA–F grading table, S/X research-credit grading, Academic Performance (min. CPI 6.0 for graduation), Academic Probation, Teaching Assistantships, and Migration Rules to/from PhD).

### Differences from the M.Tech (ICT) Academic Requirements

**§2.4 Sponsored Category:** In addition to a signed agreement between DA-IICT **or CR Rao AIMSCS** and a sponsoring agency, this program also allows sponsored admission for students **from government organizations, Indian research and development institutes, or leading PSUs** — a broader sponsorship pathway than stated in the M.Tech (ICT) document. ## Course Structure — M.Tech (EC) Program (2022 onwards)

### Semester-Wise Program Structure

**Semester I — Total Credits: 15**
- Two General Elective courses (6 credits): one from a designated Mathematics basket, one from a designated Technical Elective basket.
- Two Program Core courses (5 credits, common to all specializations): **Communication Skills and Technical Writing** (2 credits), **Programming Lab** (3 credits).
- One Specialization Core course (4 credits).
- *Note: each student is assigned a faculty advisor for general academic guidance.*

**Semester II — Total Credits: 14**
- One Specialization Core course (4 credits).
- Two Specialization Elective courses (7 credits).
- One Minor Project (3 credits), faculty-supervised.

**Summer — Total Credits: 4**
- Major Project I (0-0-8-4), supervised — preliminary problem-definition work. Minor/Major Project I mentor may be the same or different.

**Semester III — Total Credits: 13**
- Two Specialization Electives (7 credits).
- Continuation of Major Project I (0-0-12-6).

**Semester IV — Total Credits: 12**
- Major Project II (on-campus continuation) **or** Industrial Training Project (industry internship). Successful Major Project II completion earns a thesis certificate.

**Program totals:** 33 course credits + 25 project credits = **58 credits**.
