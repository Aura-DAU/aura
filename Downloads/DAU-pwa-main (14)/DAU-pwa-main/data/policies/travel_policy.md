# Policy on Reimbursement of Travel-related Expenses

From: HR & Admin Department. Governs reimbursement of official-travel expenses for DAU employees. Prior approval via email is mandatory before any official travel (conference, workshop, sponsored-project meeting, etc.) — reimbursement won't be processed without it.

## Sanctioning Authorities

| Employee Category | Purpose | Approving Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Faculty | Research-related | Dean Research |
| Faculty | Institute/Academics-related | Dean Faculty |
| Research Associates | Sponsored-research-related | Principal Investigator, then Dean Research |
| Teaching Assistants | Academics-related | Dean AP |
| Administrative Staff | Institute-related | Reporting Manager/Head-HR & Admin, then Executive Registrar |

## Allowed Mode of Travel

| Designation | Air | Rail | Road |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faculty | Economy | AC First Class | AC Taxi |
| Staff (Group-A) | Economy | AC First Class | AC Taxi |
| Staff (Group-B) | Economy | AC 2-tier | AC Taxi |
| Research Associates/Project Personnel/TA | — | AC 3-tier | AC Taxi/Bus |
| Staff Group C/D | — | 2nd AC/AC Chair Car | Taxi/Bus |

- Local conveyance (airport↔hotel↔venue): capped at ₹5,000/trip.
- Private vehicle use: needs prior approval; reimbursed at ₹20/km, no receipts needed.

## Daily Allowance (with receipts)

| Designation | Hotel (Metro) | Hotel (Non-Metro) | Food/day | DA without receipts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Faculty & Staff Group-A | ₹10,000 | ₹7,500 | ₹1,200 | ₹3,000 |
| Staff Group-B | ₹7,000 | ₹4,500 | ₹900 | ₹2,000 |
| Research Associates/Project Personnel/TA | ₹5,500 | ₹3,000 | ₹900 | ₹1,500 |
| Staff Group-C/D | ₹5,500 | ₹3,000 | ₹900 | ₹1,500 |

- Hotel accommodation covers incidentals (laundry, phone, internet). Tips and alcohol are **not reimbursable**.
- DA without receipts only applies if there's no separate hotel/food claim.
- Expenditure beyond limits needs special Director approval.

## Reimbursement Claims

- Must be submitted within **3 months** of return journey, or the advance is recovered from salary (late claims need sanctioning-authority approval).
- Institute travel-agent bookings: bills routed through the Administrative Section.
- Reimbursable extras: seat/berth reservation charges, Tatkal charges, IRCTC e-ticketing fees, recognized agent charges, and cancellation charges for official-reason cancellations.

## International Travel Daily Allowance

| Region | Faculty/Staff Cadre A |
|---|---|
| Indian subcontinent, SE Asia, Mauritius, Africa, Eastern Europe | $175/day |
| Far East Asia, Hong Kong, Middle East, Australia, NZ, South Africa | $200/day |
| North/South America | $225/day |
| Western Europe/Japan/Korea | $250/day |
| Staff below Cadre A, students, research associates | $140/day (any region) |
| Self-arranged university-visit trips (training/functional visits) | $80/day, all employees |

Limits cover lodging, food, and local conveyance — not alcohol or entertainment. International air travel is economy class for all employees.

Issued with Director General approval. **Effective date: 1 December 2025.**
