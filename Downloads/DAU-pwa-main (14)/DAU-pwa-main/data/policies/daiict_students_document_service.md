## Overview

The Institute issues a range of academic documents to students, including:

1. **Combined Grade Report:** A comprehensive record of a student's academic performance throughout their tenure at DA-IICT — grades for individual courses, cumulative CPI, and any relevant academic distinctions.
2. **Bonafide Certificate:** Verifies a student's enrolment status at DA-IICT, including duration of program/course details.
3. **Fees Structure:** Detailed breakdown of costs for a particular program — tuition fees, hostel fees, and any additional charges relevant to the service or program.
4. **Semester-wise Mark Sheet:** Provided at the end of each semester/academic term — courses taken, grades achieved, credits earned, and other relevant academic information.
5. **Other:** Any additional documents requested by the student for official or academic purposes.

## Process to Obtain Documents

Students submit a formal request through the online portal, providing document type, student identification, and any required additional documentation or fees. The Institute verifies eligibility and processes the request; the approved document is prepared and issued within a specified timeframe per Institute policy.

### Application Process Steps

1. **Fill up Online Application Form:** Complete the application form for the specific document(s) needed.
2. **Provide Necessary Information:** Full name, student ID, program of study, registration number, and specific documents requested.
3. **Pay Applicable Charges:** Complete payment via the payment gateway after filling the form.
4. **Submit the Application:** Auto-submitted to the Registrar's Office for processing after form completion and payment. Queries are handled via the student's registered email/mobile number.
5. **Processing:** Documents are typically ready within **2 working days** for in-person collection. Courier/post delivery time depends on the courier/postal service.
6. **Receive the Documents:** Notification is sent once the application is processed and the document is ready for pickup or delivery.
