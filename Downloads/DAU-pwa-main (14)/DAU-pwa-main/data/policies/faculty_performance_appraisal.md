### 6.3.1 — Performance Appraisal System, Promotional Avenues, and Welfare Measures

The faculty is the most valuable resource of an institute, besides possibly the students. To attain and sustain an eminent status, an institute has to utilize its faculty resource to its fullest potential — achieved by aligning the objectives of the institute with those of the faculty. This requires sending a clear message to faculty about what constitutes performance and how it will be measured. A fair, transparent, objective, and well-understood annual appraisal process is designed to reward performers and encourage others to improve.

DAIICT has evaluated the performance of its faculty members for the last five years across four buckets: **teaching, research, consulting, and admin**. Beginning 2020–2021, the institute put in place a formal process of collecting annual self-appraisal from faculty members via an online form, recording activities across the four buckets. Earlier, publication data was provided by the Resource Centre (Library); Dean (Academic) regularly collects student feedback through the Registrar's office; and Dean (Research)'s office provides data on sponsored research and consultancy. The institute also collects information on awards received by individual faculty for inclusion in the Annual Report.

For evaluation, a scoring system has been developed. Overall faculty performance is graded into three categories: **"Excellent," "Good,"** and **"Fair."** The system allows faculty to self-assess and benchmark against peers, and helps the institute reward better performers with appropriate annual incentives.

The institute recognizes that no appraisal process is free of issues, and has constituted a committee to review the process regularly and recommend improvements.

Staff members (non-faculty) are appraised on integrity, spirit of teamwork, attitude, capability to accept challenges, and proficiency in work. This input is captured annually to decide each staff member's annual incentive.

### Welfare Measures for Teaching and Non-Teaching Staff

**Health Centre Facilities**
- Two visiting doctors attend the health centre daily at fixed hours on all working days, for students, faculty, and staff.
- A panel of medical specialists is available; the institute has a stress management centre.
- DA-IICT has tie-ups with reputed hospitals in and around Gandhinagar for cashless treatment of staff and faculty.
- All regular employees are sent for a medical fitness test immediately upon joining, and a fitness certificate is obtained and kept on record.
- All permanent staff, faculty, and their family members are covered for cashless hospitalization of Rs. 10,00,000, including COVID-19.

## Important Information

- Buckets used for faculty self-appraisal: Teaching, Research, Consulting, Admin
- Formal annual self-appraisal process in place since academic year 2020–2021
- Performance grading categories: Excellent, Good, Fair
