# Teaching Assistantship Policy (Effective from June AY2024-25)

Teaching Assistants (TAs) play an important role in teaching-learning activities across all programmes; the institute provides financial support via teaching assistantship. Stipends are revised periodically. TAs gain skill-upgrading opportunities through close interaction with course instructors and students.

## Stipend (effective June AY2024-25)

| Category | Stipend |
|---|---|
| PhD Students — first two years | ₹35,000/month |
| PhD Students — last three years | ₹40,000/month |
| MTech (GATE-admitted) | ₹15,000/month |
| MTech (Non-GATE-admitted) | ₹12,500/month |
| Contractual TA (existing PhD student at DAU 5+ years) | ₹25,000/month |
| BTech students | Half the MTech GATE-qualified rate |

PhD financial support is a mix of Teaching Assistantship (TA) and Research Assistantship (RA), for a max of 5 years per existing PhD guidelines.

## TA Responsibilities/Duties

Physical presence during lab/tutorial; lab preparation; assist in assignment/solution preparation; assist in course-material prep; assist in grading lab/tutorial assignments; formal office hours; taking lecture attendance; other academic support as needed.

## Duty Hour Requirement

- **10-12 hrs/week.**
- Must mark presence at the Main Security Gate attendance machine, and during labs/tutorials.
- **4 late marks = 1 day's stipend deduction.** Failing to mark at all = considered absent, loses 1 day's stipend.

## Resources (who can TA)

Full-time PhD students; MTech students; senior BTech students (preferably final year), if required.

## Eligibility

- **MTech (full-time):** CPI ≥ 6.0.
- **PhD (full-time):** enrolled full-time; not receiving other financial assistance/salary from external sources unless approved; must maintain satisfactory academic/research progress.
- **BTech:** CPI ≥ 7.5, and grade ≥ 8 (BB) in the course they'll TA for.
- **Contractual TA:** existing full-time PhD student at DAU 5+ years; must be recommended by the course instructor or PhD supervisor at semester start.

## Extraordinary Allocation

For skill-based TA needs, faculty/instructors request the PG-Convenor/Dean(AP) with required skill parameters and recommended student names — eligibility criteria may be relaxed.

## TA Load Formula

Lab-credit courses: **1 TA per 40-50 registered students.** Tutorial-credit courses: **1 TA per 90-100 registered students.**

## Process for TA Allocation

Dean (AP) finalizes offered courses → PhD "Tutors" excluded from TA allocation → some senior PhD students may be "head TA" (excluded from remaining allocation) → Dean (AP) office provides eligible-student list (priority: PhD, MTech, BTech) → TA preferences collected at semester start → PG Committee allocates TAs → allocation list approved by Director/Dean-AP, announced to faculty/students, forwarded to HR.

- MTech/BTech TAship runs from class commencement to results-submission deadline each semester (excludes summer semester).
- PhD stipend starts from official joining date: ₹35,000/month for first 2 years, revised to ₹40,000/month from year 3 through year 5.
- TAs appointed as JRF on externally-funded DAU projects must inform course-instructor/PG-Convenor/HR before formally joining — TAship is then discontinued (but the already-assigned task for that semester must be completed).
- MTech/BTech/MSc internship/project TAs may assist with evaluation/invigilation duties.
- Courses by external/visiting faculty (3-4 visits/semester, accelerated mode): TAs get limited tasks; may not get a TA at all if eligible TAs are limited, or share 1 TA across multiple such courses (possibly half-time).
- HR office handles TA attendance policy, leave, and stipend disbursement matters.

## Termination and Suspension

Stipend continuation depends on meeting academic requirements. TAship may be suspended/terminated for:
- Unsatisfactory academic performance or missed research milestones (PhD).
- Failing to deliver labs/tutorials per course outcomes.
- Failing to submit the monthly TA form (due by the 20th, signed by reporting instructor) — stipend for that month may be withheld.
- Academic misconduct, plagiarism, or breach of institutional policies.
- Voluntary PhD withdrawal or conversion to part-time.
- Accepting external financial aid without prior approval (violates scholarship agreement).
