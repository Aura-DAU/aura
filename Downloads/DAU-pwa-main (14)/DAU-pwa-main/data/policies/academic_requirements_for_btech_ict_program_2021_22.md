# Academic Requirements for the B.Tech (ICT) Program

*(Effective from Autumn 2021-22 — applicable to students admitted from AY 2021-22 onwards)*

## Source

- **Institution:** Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT), Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_BTech(ICT)2021_wef Aut2021-22.pdf`
- **Dated:** October 2022 (DA-IICT, Gandhinagar)

## Scope

This document contains the Rules governing the B.Tech (Bachelor of Technology) program, a four-year undergraduate program, covering post-admission academic activities only (not admission eligibility/procedures). Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue instructions to implement these Rules; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional situations (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

- Registration occurs within the first two days of each semester via the Student Registration System (SRS). Late registration is permitted for valid reasons with Deputy Registrar approval and a late fee, up to the last date in the Academic Calendar. Students with outstanding dues cannot register.
- A student with no backlog courses may register for the full prescribed course set; a student with backlogs or on academic probation may be recommended a different course set by the UG Committee Convenor.
- **Withdrawal:** Pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean (Academic Programs) approval before the late-registration deadline. Post-registration withdrawal is permitted only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before the end-semester exam start date. Only one semester may be withdrawn at a time; failing to register or withdraw properly results in discontinuation. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show "withdrawn" status on the transcript.

## 2. Auditing & PG-Level Courses

- **Audit courses:** Permitted from semester 5 onwards, maximum 2 for the entire program, one per semester. Requires instructor approval and no timetable clash. Not counted as overload or toward SPI/CPI. Graded P (appears on transcript) or F (does not appear).
- **PG-level courses for credit:** Students with CPI ≥ 7.4 may register for available M.Tech-level courses as credit courses from semester 5 onwards, with program convenor approval and no timetable clash; counts toward the regular course load.

## 3. Course Load

- **Regular semester:** Maximum 7 courses (excluding Co-Curricular Activities), maximum 28 credits. Minimum underload: 4 courses / 12 credits (after completing 7 regular semesters, fewer than 4 courses is allowed).
- **Summer Semester** (~8 weeks, May–July): Maximum 3 backlog courses. Internship counts as 2 courses; BTP Part II as 3 courses; BTP Part I as 1 course. No other course may be combined with an industrial/rural internship. Students who have completed 8 regular semesters may also use Summer Semester for grade improvement (see §5.2).

## 4. Course Assessment and Grading

- Assessment modes: assignments, lab work, group work, quizzes, tests, viva-voce, mini-projects, end-semester exams, and (at instructor discretion) attendance — an instructor may make ≥80% attendance mandatory and award F for non-compliance.
- **Grading scale (10-point):**

  | Letter Grade | Points | Explanation |
  |---|---|---|
  | AA | 10 | |
  | AB | 9 | |
  | BB | 8 | |

  | DD | 4 | |
  | DE | 3 | Pass |

  | P | — | Passed |

- A grade of AA–DE is a pass; F is a fail. Pass/Fail-graded courses use P/F only and are excluded from SPI/CPI. F may also be awarded for exam/evaluation malpractice.
- **"I" (Incomplete):** Awarded when overall performance is satisfactory but the student misses the end-semester exam due to illness or accident/death in the family, with Dean (Academic Programs) approval and supporting medical/other evidence. Must be converted to a performance grade via a make-up exam by the date specified in the next semester's Academic Calendar, or it converts to F.

## 5. Repeating a Course

- An F grade in a credit course must be repeated (becomes a backlog course, subject to §3 registration rules). A backlog elective can be replaced with another elective of the same category (ICT/Technical/HASS/Open/Science).
- **Grade Improvement:** A student with CPI < 5.0 may repeat a course graded DD or DE, in a regular semester only, for grade improvement. The repeat grade counts toward CPI; the original grade still appears on the transcript but is excluded from CPI.
- A student may register for one Backlog/Grade-Improvement course per regular semester, subject to §5.1/5.2 and the §3.1 course-load cap, with no evaluation clash with regular courses.

## 6. B.Tech Project / Industrial Training Project

All students must complete the BTP or Industrial Training Project (ITP), with credits as prescribed in the curriculum from time to time.

## 7. Performance Indices

- **SPI (Semester Performance Index):** Weighted average of grade points across a semester's registered courses, to 2 decimal places.
- **CPI (Cumulative Performance Index):** Weighted average of grade points across all credit courses since entry, to 2 decimal places.

## 8. Minimum Academic Performance Requirements

- **Academic Probation:** Placed on probation (with written intimation) entering semester 2 if semester-1 SPI < 4.5; in later semesters, if previous-semester CPI < 5.0 or previous-semester SPI < 4.5. Dean (Academic Programs) may prescribe a minimum SPI target for a probationary student, based on their SPI/CPI relative to graduation minimums.
- **Discontinuation for poor performance:** Assessed from the end of semester 2 onward, each subsequent semester, based on CPI and SPI.
- **Repeat-a-year rule:** CPI < 4.0 at the end of semester 2 or 4 triggers a requirement to repeat a year (with course-improvement options). Such a student may take up to 3 available summer-semester backlog courses (F or DE grade). Achieving CPI ≥ 4.0 (including summer results) allows continuation; otherwise the student repeats a year or is discontinued.
- **Program duration:** Minimum 4 academic years; maximum 6 academic years (including any withdrawn semester), beyond which the student must leave.

## 9. Award of Degree

**9.1 Requirements for the B.Tech (ICT) degree:**

a. Pass all foundation and elective courses, internships, BTP/ITP, co-curricular activities, and exploratory projects prescribed in the curriculum.

b. **Minimum 154 total credits:** 129 from core + elective courses (90 foundation + 39 elective, split as minimum 3 ICT electives, 4 technical electives, 2 science electives, 1 HASS elective, 2 open electives), 19 from internships + BTP/ITP, 6 from co-curricular activities + exploratory projects.

c. Minimum Final CPI of 5.0.

d. Minimum 645 grade points from coursework (129 × 5.0; internship/BTP credits excluded from this count). If credits are met but grade points are short, additional elective courses (up to category maximums) are required.

e. At least 8 registered regular semesters (excluding summer/withdrawn semesters), with all institute dues paid.

f. No pending case of indiscipline.

**9.2 Final CPI and Class:**

- Final CPI is computed from the best-CPI combination of courses meeting the minimum credit/category requirements (≥129 credits, 90 foundation credits, and category electives minimums); extra courses beyond this don't count toward final CPI. BTP counts toward CPI; ITP (Pass/Fail) does not.
- **Distinction:** CPI ≥ 9.0. **First Class:** CPI ≥ 6.5 and < 9.0.

**9.3 Certificate of Academic Accomplishment:** Available on application for students unable to complete degree requirements within the maximum period (§8.4), per criteria set by the Institute.

## 10. B.Tech (ICT) Honours Degree

Awarded if, in addition to all B.Tech (ICT) requirements:
- (a) minimum 5 additional courses / 15 additional credits from designated Honours courses, starting semester 4;
- (b) passing grade in all 5 designated Honours courses;
- (c) minimum Final CPI of 6.5.

If (c) is not met but regular B.Tech (ICT) requirements are, the student receives the standard B.Tech (ICT) degree (not Honours). Total minimum credits for Honours: 169 (129 foundation/elective + 15 Honours + 19 internships + 6 co-curricular/exploratory). Honours course grade points are included in the CPI computation.

## 11. Glossary

- **Backlog Course:** A curriculum-prescribed course either not registered or failed.
- **Course Credit:** Weighted sum of Lecture (L), Tutorial (T), Practical (P) hours; L and T weighted 1.0, P weighted 0.5.
- **Grade Points:** Credits × grade points of the letter grade.
- **Semester:** Autumn (July–December) and Winter (January–May), ~16 weeks each; Summer Semester is a special ~8-week term (May–July).
- **Semester Grade Report:** Official record of a semester's grades.
- **Transcript:** Official record of all grades, issued after degree completion.
- **UG Committee:** Institute committee responsible for undergraduate policy guidelines and implementation.

### Elective Category Details

- **ICT Electives:** Technical courses spanning ≥2 of Computer Systems, Communication Technology, and Electronics Engineering (or supporting mathematical foundations). Minimum 3 courses (12 credits); extra passed courses count as Technical electives (not reverse).
- **Technical Electives:** From Electronics; Signal Processing & Communication; Information Technology & Computer Science; or Interdisciplinary groups. Minimum 4 courses (12 credits), maximum 6 (20 credits).
- **Science Electives:** Physics, Mathematics, Life Sciences, Engineering Sciences. Minimum 2 (6 credits), maximum 4 (12 credits).
- **HASS Electives:** Humanities, Social Sciences, Management, Engineering Arts/Design (not already Technical electives). Minimum 1 (3 credits), maximum 3 (9 credits).
- **Open Electives:** Two 3-credit slots, fillable from any eligible category.
- **New Elective Proposals:** Faculty submit a course plan to the Dean (Academic Programs); the UG Committee runs a ≥2-reviewer review process; the Dean approves the final proposal.
- **Internships:** Mandatory Rural Internship (3 credits, typically between semesters 3–4) and Research Internship (4 credits, typically Summer Semester after semester 6).
- **BTP/ITP:** 12 credits total. BTP may be split into Part 1 (3 credits, semester 7) and Part 2 (9 credits, semester 8), or both parts taken in semester 8. ITP (semester 8, off-campus, industry-mentored) is graded Pass/Fail, assessed by the industry mentor via the ITP coordinator.

### Graduation Requirements Summary

**B.Tech (ICT):** 154 total credits (incl. 4 co-curricular + 2 exploratory project) — 129 course credits, 19 internship/BTP-ITP credits, minimum Final CPI 5.0.

**B.Tech (ICT) Honours (additional):** 5 Honours courses, minimum 15 Honours credits, minimum Final CPI 6.5.
