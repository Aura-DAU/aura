## Overview

The 5-Year dual degree program provides an alternative route for B.Tech (ICT) students considering R&D as a career choice — reducing total time by a year (vs. doing B.Tech and M.Tech separately) while retaining the essential preparatory education components. Intake is limited to students of the B.Tech (ICT) program at DA-IICT.

## Salient Features

- Dual-degree students complete all requirements of both B.Tech (ICT) and M.Tech (ICT).
- Maximum **10 students** selected per year.
- Open to B.Tech (ICT) students with a minimum CPI of **2.50/4.00** (or equivalent on the 10-point scale) at the end of Semester VI.
- Selection purely on the basis of CPI.
- Summer semesters between Semesters VIII–IX and after Semester X are added, to allow dual-degree students to complete all credit requirements.
- Courses are designated as B.Tech or M.Tech courses; CPI is computed separately for each degree based on its designated courses.
- Both degrees are awarded together upon completing the full program, normally at the end of 5 years. A student may be awarded only the B.Tech degree at (or after) the 5-year mark if they've completed the B.Tech component but not the M.Tech component.
- Subject to satisfactory progress, dual-degree students are offered financial assistance (TAship/RAship) in their 5th year, on par with M.Tech students.
- Students selected for the Dual Degree program are **not eligible for campus placement** at the conclusion of Semester VI.

## Course Structure (for Entrants of 2004 Batch)

- Semesters I–VI: Same as regular B.Tech.
- 3rd Summer: Research internship.
- Semester VII: 5 B.Tech courses (3 Technical Electives + 2 Open Electives).
- Semester VIII: 4 M.Tech courses + BTP I (6 credits).
- 4th Summer (added specifically for dual-degree students): BTP II (18 credits).
- Semester IX: 4 M.Tech courses + M.Tech Research Unit 1 (3 credits).
- Semester X: M.Tech Research Units 2–6 (15 credits).
- 5th Summer: M.Tech Research Units 7–8 (6 credits) and M.Tech thesis submission.
