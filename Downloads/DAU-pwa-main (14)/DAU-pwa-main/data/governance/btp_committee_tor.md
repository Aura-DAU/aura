## Terms of Reference

BTP, BSI, and BEP Coordinators work in consultation with the UG Convenor. Terms of reference fall under the purview of the **UG Committee**.

The UG Committee (Convenor: Prof. P M Jat, AY 2024-25) is responsible for:

- Finalization of academic calendar, in consultation with Dean (Academic Programs) and Registrar, at the beginning of the academic year
- Matters dealing with course registration, adjustments, credit requirements, academic load per semester, academic probation and related matters
- Monitoring and analysis of student performance and mechanism for slow learner student mentoring and its implementation
- Review of academic program structure and requirements and feedback for the same to Dean (Academic Programs)
- Participation in Timetable Committee for preparing Semester Timetable at the beginning of each semester
- Any other matters connected with the academic programs and students' difficulties as may come up for consideration
- Any other matters referred by the Director
