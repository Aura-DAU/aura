## Terms of Reference

The task of the Committee is to achieve the goal of obtaining the desired placement offers for students in terms of both profiles and organizations. The Committee is responsible for:

1. Devise placement policy and procedure, and announcing the same to all stakeholders
2. Planning and implementing placement policies, procedures and guidelines
3. Prepare the students for placement and enrich the placement scenario of the Institute
4. Overall management of placement of students
5. Develop and sustain mutually beneficial long-term relationships with recruiters
6. Assisting students for internship as per the curriculum requirement
7. Any other matter referred by the Director
