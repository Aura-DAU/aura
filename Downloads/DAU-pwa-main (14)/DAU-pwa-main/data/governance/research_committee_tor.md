## Functions of Dean (Research) Office

- Carrying out / monitoring sponsored projects and consultancy, including manpower requirements
- Submission of project proposals to funding agencies
- All sponsored projects monitored by internal committee: Director + Dean (Research) + PI

**Purchase policy (externally sponsored projects):**
- Rs. 20,000+: Dean (Research) approval required
- Rs. 1 lakh+: Purchase committee constituted by Dean (Research)

## CPDA

- **Amount:** Rs. 3 lakhs per 3-year block (reimbursable)
- **Eligibility:** Faculty after confirmation of service
- **Use:** National/international conferences and workshops

## Seed Grant

- New faculty: up to Rs. 5 lakhs — approved through Research Promotion Committee

**Terms of Reference:**
1. Review and approve all proposed research involving human subjects before participant recruitment and data collection can start
2. Minimize any risk for subjects while allowing the researcher to collect necessary information
3. Protect privacy and confidentiality of participants within the research setting
4. Any other matter referred by the Director
