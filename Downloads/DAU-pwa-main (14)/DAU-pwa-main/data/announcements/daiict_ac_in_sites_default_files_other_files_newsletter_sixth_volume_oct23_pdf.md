## Institutional Achievements

National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC), an autonomous body 
funded by UGC. This recognizes DAIICT's commitment to academic excellence, 
infrastructure, faculty quality, and overall educational standards.

## Academic Initiatives

**UG Admission Portal (ERP):** DAIICT launched a new UG Admission Portal 
as part of its ERP system to streamline the admission process, providing 
applicants a centralized hub for applications, status tracking, and information.

**Value-Added Course with IRMA:** A 30-hour course titled 'Econometric 
Methods for Causal Inference' was organized in collaboration with IRMA 
(Institute of Rural Management Anand) for MSc (Agriculture Analytics) students, 
taught May 15–30, 2023 by IRMA Prof. Vivek Pandey.

**Value-Added Course with Amnex:** 'Applications of Advanced Geospatial 
Technologies in Agriculture' for MSc (Agriculture Analytics) students.

**MSc (Agriculture Analytics) Program:** Initiated in 2022, a collaborative 
program between DAIICT, Anand Agriculture University, and the Indian Institute 
of Remote Sensing (IIRS).

**QR Coding for Campus Flora:** DAIICT collaborated with Navsari Agricultural 
University (NAU) to identify and QR-code trees, shrubs, and herb species on 
campus, providing smartphone-accessible information about each species.

**NCM-ATM Schools — Mathematics for Data Science (Jan 2–14, 2023):** 
Organized at DAIICT, jointly supported by IIT Bombay and TIFR Mumbai. 
Organized by Prof. Bhaskar Chaudhury and Prof. Tathagata Bandyopadhyay.

**Workshop on Intellectual Property Rights (April 15, 2023):** Open to 
MTech students, PhD scholars, faculty, and startup enthusiasts. Speakers 
included Anil Gupta (ex-IIM Ahmedabad), Andrew Michael Ong (WIPO), and 
others.

**CALDAM 2023 (Feb 9–11, 2023):** DAIICT hosted the 9th Annual 
International Conference on Algorithms and Discrete Applied Mathematics. 
Proceedings published in Springer's Lecture Notes in Computer Science.

**High-End Workshops (July 2023):**
- 'Image Processing and its Applications using VLSI Architectures' 
  (July 3–8, 2023) — organized by Dr. Manish Khare and Dr. Yash Agrawal
- 'AI/ML Algorithms and Applications in VLSI Design and Technology' 
  (July 17–22, 2023) — organized by Dr. Sreeja Rajendran and Dr. Yash Agrawal
