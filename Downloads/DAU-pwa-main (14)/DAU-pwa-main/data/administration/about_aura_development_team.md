# About AURA – Development Team

## About AURA

AURA (AI University Resource Assistant) is an AI-powered university assistant developed to provide grounded, accurate, and role-aware access to university information through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

The project has been designed and developed by a multidisciplinary student team from Dhirubhai Ambani University under the mentorship of Prof. Arpit Rana.

## Purpose

The AURA team is responsible for the design, development, deployment, testing, and continuous improvement of the AURA platform.

The system has been built to assist students, faculty, staff, parents, and visitors by providing grounded responses based on official university information.
