# Proforma for Inspection by UGC — 2015

## Overview

DA-IICT's submission to UGC for inspection under norms for State Private Universities, dated 30 December 2015. Provides a comprehensive institutional snapshot as of 2015.

## Main Content

### Legal Status (as of 2015)
- **President:** Shri Anil D. Ambani
- **Director:** Dr. Nagaraj Ramrao
- **Registrar:** Mr. Soman Nair
- **Finance Officer:** Mrs. Geeta Mehta
- **Established:** 6 August 2001; Act: Gujarat Act No. 6 of 2003
- **Promoting body:** DA-IICT Society (Societies Registration Act 1860 + Bombay Public Trust Act 1950)
- **Type:** Unitary university, no off-campus/off-shore centres, no distance education

### Faculty (2015)
- Sanctioned posts: 15 Professor, 13 Associate Professor, 27 Assistant Professor
- Permanent teachers: 55 (51 with PhD)
- Faculty:Student ratio: 1:10
- CTI pay: Assistant Professor ₹11 lakh, Associate Professor ₹16 lakh, Professor ₹20 lakh
- Faculty facilities: individual AC room, desktop, laptop, Wi-Fi, full-time secretarial support

### Infrastructure
- 50 acres, 60,000 sqm built-up area
- Library: 1,571 sqm, 40 computers, 1005 reference books, 4,674 e-journals, 140 print journals
- Sports: open ground, basketball, squash/tennis courts, indoor gym, badminton, volleyball (no swimming pool, no athletics track)
- Hostels: 2 girls' blocks, 8 boys' blocks

### Governance
Board of Governors (75% external), Academic Council (40% external), Finance Committee (40% external), Board of Studies (26% external).

### Malpractice Cases (last 3 years at time of report)
Two cases reported (April 2013) — both resulted in ineligibility for teaching assistantship.

### Accreditation Status at Filing
- NAAC: LOI accepted 1 July 2015; SSR submitted 3 November 2015; inspection due
- AIU member

### Strengths & Weaknesses (Self-assessed, 2015)
**Strengths:** Governance autonomy, faculty profile, research-driven pedagogy, ICT infrastructure, high-caliber students/alumni.
**Weaknesses:** Inter-disciplinary research collaboration, consultancy/patents/IPR, international academic collaboration, faculty residency, changing government admission policy.
