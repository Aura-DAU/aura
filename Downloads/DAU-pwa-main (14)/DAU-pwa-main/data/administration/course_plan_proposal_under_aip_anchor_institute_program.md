# Course Plan Proposal under AIP (Anchor Institute Program)

## Overview

Official DA-IICT template form used by instructors/anchors to propose a course, workshop, symposium, seminar, or conference under the Anchor Institute Program (AIP). Institutional header notes DA-IICT's NAAC A+ accreditation, Centre of Excellence status (Govt. of Gujarat), and 5-Star GSIRF ranking.

Choose one: (a) Participation, (b) Pass/Fail, or (c) Grade

### G. Course Notes
- The final course plan must be submitted to the AIP office **at least 45 days** before the tentative course start date.
- For offline AIP programs, scheduling should preferably align with vacation periods or extended semester breaks (due to infrastructure limitations). If run on working days, it must not interfere with regular classes/labs.
- Any course must have **at least two instructors**.
