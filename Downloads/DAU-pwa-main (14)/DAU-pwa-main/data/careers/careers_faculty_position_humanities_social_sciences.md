# Overview

Dhirubhai Ambani University (formerly DA-IICT) periodically invites applications for faculty positions in **Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS)** at the Assistant Professor and Associate Professor levels. The institute seeks scholars whose research and teaching contribute to a multidisciplinary environment, bridging the humanities, social sciences, technology, data science, artificial intelligence, design, public policy, and sustainability.

# Main Content

## Areas of Interest

While applications from all areas in Humanities and Social Sciences are welcome, the institute is specifically looking for expertise in:
* **English Literary Studies**
* **English Language Teaching (ELT)**
* **Social Anthropology**
* **Science and Technology Studies**
* **Urban Studies**

> [!NOTE]
> The institute currently has faculty in Psychology, Social Psychology, Linguistics, Computational Social Science, and Economics, and is **not** actively hiring in these specific sub-areas at this time.

## Minimum Eligibility Criteria

### Educational Qualifications
* A Ph.D. in a relevant discipline (e.g., English Literature, Comparative Literature, Geography, Sociology, Anthropology, etc.) with a first class or equivalent in the preceding degree and an excellent academic record throughout from a reputed institution.
* Candidates who have submitted their thesis and are awaiting defense may also apply.
* A strong research record with publications in reputed journals and conferences.

### Assistant Professor
Requires a Ph.D. with strong research capabilities and a strong passion for teaching at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Postdoctoral experience is preferred.

### Associate Professor
Requires a minimum of six years of post-Ph.D. teaching/research/professional experience, of which at least three years should be at the level of Assistant Professor (or equivalent) at higher educational institutions. An established record of scholarly achievement as evidenced by publications, external research grants/projects, and experience in doctoral supervision is expected.

> [!TIP]
> We also welcome applications from candidates with a strong research background and relevant industry experience. New faculty members are eligible for a seed grant for research and are encouraged to engage in collaborative projects and industrial consultancy.

## Application Submission Process

Prospective candidates are invited to submit their applications electronically by sending an email to **dean_faculty@dau.ac.in** with the Subject line: **“Application for Faculty Position in Humanities and Social Sciences”**. 

Please ensure your application includes the following documents:

1. **Curriculum Vitae (CV):** Detail your educational qualifications (starting from Class XII or equivalent, including institution names, years of completion, and marks/CGPA), academic, research, industrial, and professional experience, and a list of publications (journals, conferences, books, patents, etc.). Please provide names and contact information of three references who may be contacted for a letter of reference.
2. **Research Statement:** Provide an overview of your research background and expertise, a summary of key contributions and outcomes, ongoing activities, clear short-term and long-term future research objectives, and potential opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration and funding.
3. **Teaching Statement:** Outline your teaching philosophy and approach, prior teaching and mentoring experience, a list of foundation/core/advanced courses you are qualified to teach, and any elective courses you propose that align with your expertise.

Faculty will be responsible for conducting independent research within their respective fields and teaching both undergraduate and postgraduate courses. Candidates with interdisciplinary expertise are strongly encouraged to apply. They will play an important role in contributing to the Institute’s mission through their teaching, research, and participation in various institutional activities.

Selection Process: Shortlisted candidates will typically be invited to the Institute for a research seminar and an interview, which includes a teaching presentation. For candidates located overseas, these stages may be conducted in online mode.
