# Overview

Official Halls of Residence rules for Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar, covering room allocation, behavior/discipline, vehicles, maintenance, visitors, security, and pets. Signed off by the Warden (Men's HOR), Warden (Women's HOR), and Dean (Students).

# Main Content

## 1. Room Allocation
- Room allotment for new students is done by the Wardens; no room-change requests are entertained.
- Dean of Students, Wardens, and Hostel Supervisors reserve the right to allot/re-allot/withdraw accommodation.
- Only registered students are allotted rooms; residents must vacate within 5 days of ceasing to be a student.
- UG students get hostel rooms for the first 4 years only (subject to availability beyond that if degree isn't completed).
- Hostellers from outside Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar districts must provide contact details of at least two guardians/relatives at allocation.

## 2. Behavior and Discipline
- Maintain peace and tranquility; loud music disrupting others is not allowed.
- Men must avoid unwanted attention-seeking behavior toward women that could constitute harassment.
- Alcohol, intoxicants, and illegal activities are strictly prohibited (prohibition is enforced in Gujarat) — violations go to the Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC) and may lead to termination.
- The entire campus is a no-smoking zone; violations go to the DAC.
- All residents must return to campus by midnight; no resident may be outside campus between midnight and 6 AM, except medical emergencies (with a security-gate register entry).
- Residents must email the Hostel Supervisor in advance if staying away from the HoR (even one night).
- Entry into the other gender's HoR is prohibited.
- Room/property changes require Hostel Supervisor approval — unauthorized changes are punishable with fines/disciplinary action.
- Ragging is a criminal offence — contact the Anti-Ragging Vigilance Committee or the Resident Warden immediately if suspected or experienced.
- Dress and language must not offend others; sitting on parapets, railings, staircases, or unsafe areas is prohibited.
- No vulgar posters/text/drawings in rooms.
- Residents must check the "Snail Mail" link on the hostel website daily; mail older than 10 days is disposed of.
- Minimum fine of ₹1,000/day (both occupants) for unauthorized guests staying in a room.
- All electrical switches/gadgets must be off when leaving the room.
- Drying clothes on windows/staircase railings is prohibited — use designated clotheslines only.
- Food is not allowed inside the HoR — ₹1,000 fine, payable within 72 hours, or the case is referred to the Disciplinary Committee. Sick students (doctor-certified) may have food brought to their room with prior notice to the Hostel Supervisor.
- Only computers and table lamps are permitted as electrical appliances; irons, heaters, ovens, refrigerators, TVs, and kettles are strictly prohibited.
- No cooking in the HoR. Furniture may not be transferred between rooms.

## 3. Vehicles
- 4-wheelers (cars, jeeps) are NOT allowed on campus.
- 2-wheelers (bicycles, scooters, motorcycles) are allowed — max one vehicle per student.
- Parking only in authorized areas; all traffic rules (helmet, speed limit, max two persons per vehicle) must be followed.
- **First offence:** monetary fine + community service hours + letter to parents.
- **Second offence:** vehicle privileges revoked for remaining stay; possible hostel expulsion depending on severity.

## 4. Maintenance of Halls of Residence
- Residents keep their own rooms/bathrooms clean; common areas are institute-maintained but residents must avoid littering.
- Residents must follow electricity/water conservation plans — misuse can trigger fines for an entire wing/floor.
- Complaints go in the designated register first, then escalate to Hostel Supervisor, then Wardens.
- Damage must be reported immediately to the Hostel Supervisor.
- The Institute reserves the right to make unannounced spot checks (with effort to respect privacy/dignity).
- Drilling/structural civil work is strictly prohibited.
- Each room has a fixed inventory the occupant is responsible for maintaining.
- No private furniture, except with a doctor's medical certificate submitted to the Hostel Supervisor.
- Residents must bring their own mattress, pillow, linen, and personal clothing.

## 5. Visitors
- Visitors are NOT allowed into the HoR complex; the cafeteria is the suggested meeting place.
- Guest Rooms (both HoRs) are available only for medical emergencies, with Hostel Supervisor permission and advance rent payment.
  - Rent for short-stay non-resident DAU students: ₹200/person/day.
  - Rent for outside visitors, including parents: ₹250/person/day (inclusive of GST); Registrar's Office may decide case-by-case.
- Unauthorized/unregistered visitor hosting: minimum ₹1,000 fine plus guest charges.
- Parents/guardians visiting outside normal times need special Hostel Supervisor permission.
- Overnight visitor stays are not allowed except for medical emergencies.
- Non-resident students staying temporarily must register with the Hostel Supervisor (via course instructor/guide), specify the exact stay period, and pay ₹200/day rent. They must follow all HoR rules during their stay.
- The list of temporary residents is shared with the Security Supervisor for monitoring.

## 6. Security
- The Institute is not responsible for loss/damage of personal property; students must keep belongings locked.
- Rooms must be locked even for brief absences — unlocked, vacant rooms may be locked by the Hostel Supervisor; unjustified cases draw a ₹500 penalty.
- Avoid keeping valuables (gold, large cash) in rooms — residents bear the risk.
- Report a missing roommate at night to the Hostel Supervisor immediately.
- One key issued per resident; lost key fine: ₹500 + cost of new lock.
- Residents may NOT use their own locks — only institute-issued locks; using a personal lock without informing the Hostel Supervisor about a damaged lock draws an additional ₹1,000 fine (on top of the ₹500 lock-replacement fine).
- Residents must keep their profile (phone numbers, guardian addresses) and photo (on SBS/ERP) updated.

## 7. Pets
- No pets allowed in the HoR. Residents should not encourage stray cats/dogs and should inform security if they find any.

## 8. Hostel Management
- The Hostel Management Committee (HMC) may revise rules at any time and will inform residents of changes.
- Minimum fine for any rule breach: ₹1,000, scaling with severity; repeat/serious offences go to the Disciplinary Action Committee.
- **HMC composition:** Dean (Students), Warden (Men's HoR), Warden (Women's HoR), Hostel Supervisor, Resident Wardens (students), Electrical Maintenance Engineer, elected student representatives.
