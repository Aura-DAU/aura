Main Alumni portal for Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar — messages from leadership, alumni statistics, reunion/award announcements, and links to alumni services.

## Message from Director General — Dr. Tathagata Bandyopadhyay
Reflects on DA-IICT's 20+ years of contributions, the institute's evolution toward becoming a multidisciplinary "Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)" (adding law, management, health sciences), and recognitions such as Best University for Innovation in Gujarat (2017) and Centre of Excellence Award from the Gujarat Government (2022). Alumni contact: alumni_office[at]dau[dot]ac[dot]in

## Message from Dean (Alumni & External Relations) — Dr. Anil Roy
Encourages alumni engagement through mentorship, guest lectures/workshops, the Continuing Education Program (CEP), networking events, regional alumni chapters, and financial/non-financial support for scholarships and research.

## DAU Silver Jubilee Celebrations and Alumni Reunion
Reunion for batches of 2001–2004 held **Dec 27–28, 2025**, with hostel-stay option available. Contact: alumni_office[at]dau[dot]ac[dot]in. Event/FAQ details were linked from the alumni page.

## The Distinguished Alumni Awards 2025–26
Inaugural awards recognizing alumni in three categories: (A) Academics & Research, (B) Corporate Leadership, (C) Entrepreneurship, Public Administration & Social Service. Nominations were sought for the pioneering UG batch of 2005 and PG batch of 2004; deadline was December 31, 2025. Winners honored at the 20th Convocation on January 17, 2026.
