# Overview

Describes the documents DAU (formerly DA-IICT) issues to alumni and the application process to request them.

# Main Content

## Documents Issued
1. **Transcript** — comprehensive academic record (course grades, cumulative CPI, distinctions); issued only to convocated/graduated students.
2. **CPI to Percentage Conversion Certificate** — shows the formula used to convert CPI to percentage.
3. **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** — for joining a program/course at another institute.
4. **Other** — any additional documents requested for official/academic purposes.

## Application Process
1. Fill out the online application form for the specific document(s) needed.
2. Provide required details: full name, student ID, program of study, registration number, and documents requested.
3. Pay applicable charges via the payment gateway.
4. Submit — the application auto-routes to the Registrar's Office; you'll be contacted by email/phone with any queries.
5. Processing — typically ready within two working days for in-person collection; courier/post delivery time depends on the courier/postal service.
6. Receive the documents once notified they're ready for pickup or delivery.
