## 1. Examination Rules for Students (PDF — full text verified)
https://www.daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/Examination_Rules_for_Students.pdf

These rules apply to every DA-IICT student for all written examinations forming part of course evaluation.

### I. Reporting to the Examination Hall
1. No mobile phones allowed in the exam hall.
2. Identity Card must be carried and produced for verification if required.
3. Bring only material explicitly allowed by the course instructor.
4. Students should be seated 5 minutes before the scheduled start.
5. Carrying unauthorized material into the hall is treated as a deliberate attempt to use unfair means.

### II. At the Start of the Examination
1. Doors close 5 minutes before commencement for material distribution; reopened for latecomers only after all on-time seated students have received their material.
2. Latecomers take their seats and wait for material.
3. Entry cutoff: 15 minutes after start for in-semester exams; 30 minutes after start for end-semester exams.

### III. During the Examination
1. No exchanging (borrowing/lending) of material.
2. Any use of unfair means results in immediate expulsion from the exam hall and referral to the course instructor and Dean (Academic Programs).
3. Both the student copying and the one assisting are punished.
4. Leaving one's seat for any reason (including restroom) requires the invigilator's permission.
5. No supplementary sheets given in the last 5 minutes.

### IV. On Completion of the Examination
1. Students may not leave in the first 15 minutes of an in-semester exam (typical duration 1 hour) or first 30 minutes of an end-semester exam (typical duration 3 hours), nor in the last 5 minutes.
2. Students present in the last 5 minutes must wait until all material is collected and the invigilator permits them to leave.
3. Students should leave quietly without discussing the paper, out of consideration for exams still in progress elsewhere.

## 2. Rules & Guidelines for Conducting Written Examinations for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PDF — full text verified)
https://www.daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/6.2.2-6.3.1-EXAMINATION-RULES-CUM-GUIDELINES-FOR-PHYSICALLY-DISABLED-STUDENTS.pdf

**Who is covered:**
a) Any student admitted to DA-IICT under the Physically Disabled category.
b) Any student with a disability certificate (prescribed format) from the Chief Medical Officer/Civil Surgeon/Medical Superintendent of a government healthcare institution, stating the candidate is found to be limited in writing exams.
c) Any student with a temporary disability (e.g. a fracture/plaster), supported by a medical certificate from an authority listed in (b) and/or endorsed by the DA-IICT Medical Officer.

**Procedure:** Students in the above categories (other than temporary disability under (c), who go through the Medical Officer) should write to the Controller of Examination (CoE) at the beginning of the semester, mentioning their final registration details (course name, code, CI name as per e-campus) and the type of assistance needed for each registered course's exams.

**Entitlements** — students may request:
i. **Compensatory Time**: 20 minutes per hour of examination (pro-rata for shorter exams).
ii. **Writer**: a scribe to assist in writing the exam, arranged via the CoE.
iii. **Both of the above.**

**Arrangements on request:** The CoE can arrange the exam hall on the ground floor for easier accessibility and will notify the concerned course instructor of the arrangement.
