## Disciplinary Guidelines for Students (PDF — full text verified)
https://www.daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/Disciplinary-guidelines-for-students.pdf

### The Breach of Discipline
Students are expected to behave, on and off campus, in a manner befitting a sincere student of a reputed institute. Unseemly behaviour or activities showing a lack of integrity/honesty are a breach of discipline, including:
- Physical assault
- Property damage or attempted damage to Institute property
- Theft (including Institute property — components, street signs, furniture, library books)
- Forgery, including forging signatures
- Sexual harassment or other sexual misconduct
- Misrepresentation in seeking financial aid or other Institute benefits
- Misuse of Institute computer equipment or e-mail/internet
- Sending threatening/obscene messages via e-mail, phone, or voicemail
- Rough driving on campus
- Storing/consuming alcohol/drugs on campus
- Violating Institute rules and regulations
- Any other act of unseemly behaviour

### The Disciplinary Committee
- Handles disciplinary infractions from students' general behaviour/conduct.
- Constituted/reconstituted by the Director from time to time.

### The Process
1. Any breach is referred, in writing, to the Disciplinary Committee by a faculty/staff member through the relevant function/activity in-charge.
2. The Committee investigates and holds hearings, giving concerned parties an opportunity to make representations.
3. The Committee documents the process and issues a written order on actions/penalty.

### The Penalty
- The Committee decides the action/penalty and forwards it to the Executive Registrar for execution.
- No standard penalty applies — infractions may range from a warning, fines, and disciplinary probation up to hostel/Institute suspension and permanent dismissal, depending on the offence's nature, seriousness, motivation, and precedent.
- An infraction during disciplinary probation leads to stricter penalties, from multi-semester suspension to permanent dismissal.
- Temporary suspension or permanent dismissal requires the Director's approval.

### The Appeal
- A student may appeal to the Director (final appellate authority) within **2 weeks** of receiving the order (later appeals may be entertained for sufficient cause).
- The Director may confirm, modify, set aside, or enhance a penalty, or remit the case for further review — but cannot enhance a penalty without first giving the student an opportunity to respond.
- The Director's decision is final and binding.

*(Document dated: DA-IICT, Gandhinagar, 30 April 2003)*
