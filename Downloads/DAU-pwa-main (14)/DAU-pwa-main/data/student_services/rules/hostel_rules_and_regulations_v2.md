# Overview

Condensed quick-reference version of the Halls of Residence Rules and Regulations at DAU (formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar. See `daiict_ac_in_sites_default_files_other_files_hostel_rules_and_regulations_pdf.md` for the full rule text.

# Main Content

## Curfew & Movement
- Return to campus by **midnight**; no resident may be off-campus between midnight and 6 AM (except medical emergencies, logged at the Security gate).
- Prior email to the Hostel Supervisor required for any night away from the HoR.

## Prohibited Items & Substances
- No 4-wheelers on campus; max one 2-wheeler per student.
- Alcohol, intoxicants, and smoking are strictly prohibited campus-wide — referred to the Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC).
- Banned appliances: irons, heaters, ovens, refrigerators, TVs, kettles. Only computers/table lamps allowed.
- No cooking or food inside the HoR (₹1,000 fine); no pets.

## Fines at a Glance
- General rule breach: minimum ₹1,000/-, scaling with severity
- Unauthorized guest in room: ₹1,000/day (both occupants)
- Food inside HoR: ₹1,000
- Unlocked/vacant room without justification: ₹500
- Lost room key: ₹500 + new lock cost
- Using a personal (non-issued) lock: additional ₹1,000
- Visitor rent: ₹200/person/day (DAU non-residents); ₹250/person/day incl. GST (outside visitors/parents)

## Visitors
- Not allowed inside the HoR complex — meet in the cafeteria instead.
- Guest rooms available only for medical emergencies, with advance permission and payment.
- No overnight visitor stays except medical emergencies.

## Governance
- **Hostel Management Committee (HMC):** Dean (Students), Warden (Men's HoR), Warden (Women's HoR), Hostel Supervisor, student Resident Wardens, Electrical Maintenance Engineer, elected student reps.
- HMC may revise rules at any time; residents are kept informed.
