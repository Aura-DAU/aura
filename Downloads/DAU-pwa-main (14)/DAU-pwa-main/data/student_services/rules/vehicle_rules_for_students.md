# Overview

Vehicle rules for students at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar. Confirmed still the live linked policy as of this rescrape.

# Main Content

## Permitted Vehicles
- Students may drive in and park only **two-wheelers** on campus (bicycles, scooters, motorcycles). Four-wheelers are not permitted.
- Students must carry their DA-IICT ID card at all times.
- Vehicles must be parked only in designated student parking areas — near the Halls of Residence (HoR) and the parking sheds by the Lecture Theatres.

## Traffic Rules
- All campus traffic rules must be followed: helmet required at all times, driving within the speed limit, and a maximum of two persons per vehicle.
- Students are also expected to follow traffic rules outside campus.

## Penalties for Violations
Disciplinary action is taken (with parents informed) as follows:
- **First offence:** ₹300 fine
- **Second offence:** ₹1,000 fine
- **Third offence:** ₹3,000 fine, plus **permanent debarment** from keeping any vehicle on campus (applies regardless of who was riding), placement under Disciplinary Probation until graduation, and a possible appearance before the Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC).

## Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC) Composition
- Dean (Students) — Convener (ex-officio)
- Warden, HoR-Men — Member (ex-officio)
- Warden, HoR-Women — Member (ex-officio)
- Deputy Registrar — Member (ex-officio)
- Faculty Member — nominated by the Director
- Two Student Representatives (Boy & Girl) — nominated by the DAC
