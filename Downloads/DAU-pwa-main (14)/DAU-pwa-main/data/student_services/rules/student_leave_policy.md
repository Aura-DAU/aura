# Overview

Student Leave Policy at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar. Effective from AY 2021-22; confirmed still the live linked policy as of this rescrape.

# Main Content

## General Principle
There is no formal concept of "leave." Students must adhere to attendance requirements of registered courses. If a student misses academic sessions for more than **three consecutive weeks**, they may opt for semester withdrawal via written application to the Dean (Academics). Without withdrawal, no relaxation is given on continuous evaluation components (quizzes, tutorials, lab assignments, etc.).

## In-Semester and End-Semester Examinations
Students unable to appear (in part or whole) for an in-semester or end-semester exam due to illness, accident, or death in the family should apply to the Dean (Academics) office:
- Collect the leave application proforma from the Dean (Academics) office.
- Submit it within **3 days** of the missed exam date(s).
- Must be supported by (i) a proper medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, or (ii) adequate evidence for accident/death in the family.
- Upon approval, the Dean (Academics) office communicates the outcome to the faculty and student.

## Re-Examinations or Evaluation
- Only after leave application approval can the concerned faculty conduct a re-examination or alternate assessment for the missed component.
- Instructions/schedule are communicated by the Controller of Examinations or the concerned faculty.
- If a student also misses the re-examination/assessment, **no further adjustment** is made.
