### Course Objectives

Machine Learning has evolved to play a significant role in problem -solving in several domains such
as computer vision , pattern recognition , natural language processing and speech processing . ML-
based techniques usually outperform  approaches that use handcr afted features. Although, a more
specialized class  of machine learning algorithms - neural networks and their variants have largely
dominated in recent times, an in -depth understanding of classical machine learning algorithms goes
a long way in developing in-depth understanding of the subject as a whole.

 The objectives of this course include:
1. To develop the understanding of fundamental concepts of Machine Learning (ML)
2. To develop the understanding of working of a variety of ML algorithms (both supervised as  well
as unsupervised)
3. To learn to apply ML algorithms to real world data/problems
4. To develop a strong base for the further exploration/self -study in the domain of machine
learning

Course Contents Supervised v s. Unsupervised learning, Linear and Polynomial regression , Least

and kernels , Principal component Analysis , K means clustering,  , Gaussian mixture models, I
ntroduction to neural networks and  Multilayer perceptron.

Expected Outcomes After studying this course, students will be able
1. to formulate the problem as one of regression, classification, clustering and/or dimensionality
reduction
2. to understand the underlying Math  and implement  variety of classical machine learning
algorithms
3. to read and understand research papers  based on applications of classical ML algorithms
