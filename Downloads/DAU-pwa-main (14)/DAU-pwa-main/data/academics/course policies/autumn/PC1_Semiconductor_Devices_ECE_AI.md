## Course Description

To introduce the physics, operation, and characteristics of semiconductor devices, including PN junctions, BJTs, FETs, and MOS devices. The course also introduces semiconductor fabrication processes and modern semiconductor devices, enabling students to relate device physics to electronic system design and applications.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

- Explain semiconductor fundamentals, carrier transport, and the operation of PN junction diodes.
- Analyze the operating principles and characteristics of BJTs and field-effect transistors.
- Examine the electrostatics of MOS capacitors and the operation of MOSFET devices.
- Interpret semiconductor fabrication processes and relate them to the performance of modern semiconductor devices.

## Course Structure

| Module/Unit | Content | Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| **Semiconductor Fundamentals and PN Junction Diode** | Semiconductor Fundamentals: Crystal structure, Energy bands, Intrinsic & extrinsic semiconductors, direct and indirect band-gap semiconductors, Carrier concentration & Fermi level, Drift & diffusion, Carrier mobility, resistivity, Generation–recombination, Continuity equation. PN Junction Diode: Depletion region, Built-in potential, Diode equation, Diffusion & depletion capacitances, Junction breakdown (Zener & avalanche) and Special diodes. | 10 |
| **Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs) and Basic FETs** | Bipolar Junction Transistors: Structure, operation & current components, Modes: active, cutoff, saturation, Input & output characteristics, Small-signal hybrid-π model. JFET structure, pinch-off, I–V characteristics, Depletion and enhancement MOSFET vs JFET comparison, MESFET basics. | 10 |
| **MOS Capacitor and MOSFET Devices** | MOS Capacitor: Energy-band diagrams (accumulation, depletion, inversion), Threshold voltage & flat-band voltage, C–V characteristics. MOSFET: Structure, operation & channel formation, MOSFET I–V characteristics (linear & saturation), Threshold voltage variations, Second-order effects, Body effect, Channel length modulation, Subthreshold conduction, Mobility degradation, DIBL and short-channel effects. | 12 |
| **Semiconductor Fabrication Technology and Modern Devices** | Semiconductor Fabrication: Oxidation, Diffusion & ion implantation, Photolithography, Etching (wet and dry), Thin-film deposition, Metallization, CMOS process flow. Introduction to Modern Devices: FinFET, SOI MOSFET, HEMT fundamentals, Basic TFT operation & structures, Emerging materials. Summary Lecture: Exploration of how AI techniques enhance and optimize semiconductor device design and performance for modern hardware applications. | 10 |

## Laboratory Experiment

The laboratory component includes experiments on the characterization of PN junction diodes, BJTs, FETs, and MOS devices, along with basic semiconductor fabrication and device analysis. It provides hands-on experience in measuring device characteristics, validating theoretical concepts, and developing practical skills in semiconductor device testing and analysis.
