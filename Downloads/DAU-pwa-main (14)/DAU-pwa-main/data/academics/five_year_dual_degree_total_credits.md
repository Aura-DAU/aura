# Five-Year Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech ICT) – Total Credits Required

## Key Answer

**The five-year B.Tech + M.Tech dual degree program requires a student to complete ALL credit requirements of BOTH the B.Tech (ICT) and M.Tech (ICT) programs by the end of Semester 10 (plus the 5th Summer).**

The combined total credit requirement across both degrees is **approximately 168–176 credits**, encompassing all B.Tech credits (Semesters I–VIII including B.Tech Project) and all M.Tech credits (M.Tech coursework and research units completed in Semesters VIII–X and 5th Summer).

## Breakdown by Semester

| Semester / Period | Program Component | Approximate Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Semesters I–VI | B.Tech (ICT) core semesters (same as regular B.Tech) | ~108 credits |
| 3rd Summer | Research Internship | 0 (non-credit activity) |
| Semester VII | 5 B.Tech courses (3 Technical Electives + 2 Open Electives) | ~15 credits |
| Semester VIII | 4 M.Tech courses + B.Tech Project I (BTP I) | ~18 credits (6 from BTP I) |
| 4th Summer (added for dual degree) | B.Tech Project II (BTP II) | 18 credits |
| Semester IX | 4 M.Tech courses + M.Tech Research Unit 1 | ~15 credits (3 from Research Unit 1) |
| Semester X | M.Tech Research Units 2–6 | 15 credits |
| 5th Summer (added for dual degree) | M.Tech Research Units 7 & 8 + M.Tech Thesis Submission | 6 credits |

## Summary Credit Totals

| Degree Component | Credits |
|---|---|
| B.Tech (ICT) component (Sem I–VIII + BTP I & II) | ~132 credits (varies by batch curriculum) |
| M.Tech (ICT) component (coursework + research units) | 58 credits (33 coursework + 25 research) |
| **Combined Dual Degree Total (estimated)** | **~168–176 credits** |

> **Note:** The exact B.Tech credit total depends on the applicable batch curriculum. The M.Tech (ICT) minimum is a fixed 58 credits (33 coursework + 25 project/research). Both degrees are awarded simultaneously upon successful completion of all requirements, normally at the end of 5 years.

## Important Program Rules

1. **Both degrees awarded together:** Both B.Tech and M.Tech degrees are awarded only after completing the full 5-year program. However, a student may receive only the B.Tech degree if the B.Tech component is complete but not the M.Tech component.
2. **No campus placement after Sem VI:** Dual degree students are not eligible for campus placements at the end of Semester VI.
3. **Financial support in Year 5:** Students in their 5th year receive TA/RA financial assistance on par with M.Tech students.
4. **Eligibility:** Open to B.Tech (ICT) students with minimum CPI of 2.50/4.00 (or equivalent on 10-point scale) at end of Semester VI; maximum 10 students selected per year.
5. **CPI computation:** Separate CPI is computed for each degree (B.Tech and M.Tech) using courses designated for that respective degree.
