## Course Description
The course is aimed at providing a broad overview of the various paradigms of Artificial Intelligence. It will provide insights into approaches beyond the popular Machine learning models and delve into classic AI (search, planning, reasoning), Nature inspired AI (genetic algorithms, evolutionary AI) and generative AI (foundation models). We will also touch upon explainable AI and fair AI.

## Course Structure

| IE301 Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Philosophy of Learning | Core components of Intelligence – memory, planning, search; Contrast between biological intelligence and AI | 3 |
| Classic AI | Logic, Planning, Reasoning; A/A* Search Algorithms; Constraint Satisfaction Problem | 9 |
| Probability and Energy based models | Uncertainty and Bayesian Learning; Boltzmann Machines, Contrastive Divergence | 6 |
| Biologically Inspired and Reinforcement Models | Genetic Algorithms; Reinforcement Learning | 6 |
| Self-supervised Learning | Foundation models, LLMs, Attention models; Generative Models; Diffusion Models | 9 |
| Explainable and Fair AI | Explainable and Fair AI | 6 |
