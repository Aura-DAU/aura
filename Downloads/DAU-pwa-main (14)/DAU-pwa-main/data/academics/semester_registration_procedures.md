# Semester Registration Procedures

> [!NOTE]
> The source URL for this information (https://login.dau.ac.in) is a portal that requires active login credentials. The details below are preserved from the original scraped source.

## Overview

Semester registration at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT) is conducted through the **eCampus / ERP portal (UniRP)**. Registration must be completed within the designated window (see Academic Calendar). Each semester's window — Autumn, Winter, and Summer — opens separately.

## Steps for Semester Registration

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Log in to the eCampus / ERP portal during the open registration window |
| 2 | Select your courses for the upcoming semester |
| 3 | Verify your registration on the Academic page of the ERP portal |

## Important Notes for Specific Groups

| Group | Note |
|---|---|
| Fresh UG Students | Registration dates are separate — check the Academic Calendar |
| Fresh PG Students | Separate registration date; typically the Monday before classes commence |

## Key Portals

| Portal | Purpose | Link |
|---|---|---|
| eCampus / ERP Portal | Semester registration and academic management | [eCampus — DAU](https://login.dau.ac.in) |
| Intranet | Internal access for registered students | [DA-IICT Intranet](https://ecampus.daiict.ac.in/webapp/intranet/index.jsp) |

## Registration Window Dates

Refer to the Academic Calendar for the exact dates each semester. General timelines:
- **Autumn 2026–27 Registration:** 6 July – 17 July 2026

## Additional Notes

- Registration is not confirmed until the **FINAL SUBMIT** button is clicked and the status shows "Successfully Completed."
- Editing a step after confirming resets all subsequent steps.
- For queries: registration_query@dau.ac.in
