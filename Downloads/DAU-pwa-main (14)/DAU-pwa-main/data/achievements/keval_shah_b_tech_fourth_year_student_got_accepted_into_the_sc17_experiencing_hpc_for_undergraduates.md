# Keval Shah — Accepted into SC17 Experiencing HPC for Undergraduates

## Overview

Keval Shah, a B.Tech 4th Year student, was accepted into the **SC17 Experiencing HPC for Undergraduates** program at the International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage and Analysis (SC17).

## Main Content

Congratulations to **Keval Shah** (B.Tech 4th Year) on being accepted into the prestigious **SC17 "Experiencing HPC for Undergraduates"** program. SC17 is the International Conference for High Performance Computing, Networking, Storage and Analysis, one of the most prestigious conferences in the field of High Performance Computing (HPC).

The "Experiencing HPC for Undergraduates" program provides selected undergraduates with immersive hands-on experience in high performance computing, including access to world-class computing facilities and mentorship from HPC experts.
