# Ms Malvika Singh — Selected for PennApps XV, University of Pennsylvania

## Overview

Ms Malvika Singh, a B.Tech 2014 batch student, has been selected for **PennApps XV**, a prestigious hackathon hosted at the University of Pennsylvania, USA.

## Main Content

Congratulations to **Ms Malvika Singh** (B.Tech 2014 batch) on being selected for **PennApps XV**, one of the world's premier collegiate hackathons hosted at the **University of Pennsylvania**, USA.

PennApps is a student-run hackathon at the University of Pennsylvania and one of the oldest and most prestigious hackathons in the world. Selection to PennApps represents recognition of both technical skill and innovative thinking. Ms Singh's selection brings recognition to DA-IICT on the international stage.
