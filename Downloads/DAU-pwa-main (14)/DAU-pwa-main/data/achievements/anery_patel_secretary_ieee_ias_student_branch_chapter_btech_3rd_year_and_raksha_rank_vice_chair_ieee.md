# Anery Patel and Raksha Rank — IEEE IAS Travel Grant, WIE ILS 2018

## Overview

Anery Patel (Secretary, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter, BTech 3rd Year) and Raksha Rank (Vice Chair, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter, BTech 3rd Year) received an IEEE IAS Travel and attendee grant to attend the Women In Engineering International Leadership Summit (WIE ILS 2018).

## Main Content

Congratulations to **Anery Patel** (Secretary, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter, BTech 3rd Year) and **Raksha Rank** (Vice Chair, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter, BTech 3rd Year) on receiving the IEEE IAS Travel and attendee grant to attend the **Women In Engineering International Leadership Summit (WIE ILS 2018)** held at Kochi, Kerala.

The WIE International Leadership Summit is a globally acclaimed program by IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE), providing regional opportunities to foster networking, mentorship, leadership, and collaboration among women engineers and scientists.

## Important Information

- **Award:** IEEE IAS Travel and Attendee Grant
- **Event:** Women In Engineering International Leadership Summit (WIE ILS 2018)
- **Venue:** Kochi, Kerala
- **Recipients:** Anery Patel (Secretary, IEEE IAS SB Chapter) and Raksha Rank (Vice Chair, IEEE IAS SB Chapter)
