# Chosen for Award of 'Center of Excellence' by the Government of Gujarat

## Overview

DA-IICT (Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology) has been chosen for the award of **'Center of Excellence' (CoE)** by the Government of Gujarat — a recognition of the university's significant contributions to higher education, research, and technology in the state.

## Main Content

DA-IICT / Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU) has been designated as a **Centre of Excellence (CoE)** by the **Government of Gujarat**. This prestigious recognition acknowledges the institution's sustained commitment to academic excellence, quality research, and its role in promoting Information and Communication Technology education in Gujarat and India.

As a Centre of Excellence, DAU receives continued support from the Government of Gujarat to further its research, innovation, and outreach initiatives.

## Important Information

- **Designation:** Centre of Excellence (CoE)
- **Awarded by:** Government of Gujarat
- **Significance:** Recognizes DA-IICT's contributions to higher education, research, and ICT in Gujarat

- For CoE notification, see: [CoE Notification 28 April 2025 (PDF)](https://www.daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/CoE_Notification_28_April_2025.pdf)
