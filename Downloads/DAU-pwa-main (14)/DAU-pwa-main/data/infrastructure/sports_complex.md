# Sports Complex – Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)

## Overview

All students will be physically educated, have motor skills to perform a variety of physical activities, and pursue a healthy and active lifestyle in an increasingly changing world. Sports and games are an important and unique part of the daily programme at DAU.

---

## 1. Vision

> "All students will be physically educated, have motor skills to perform a variety of physical activities and pursue a healthy and active lifestyle in an increasingly changing world."

In DAU sports and games are an important and unique part of the daily programme. Not only does it contribute to the overall goals of education, but also contributes to the development and maintenance of fitness, motor skills, social skills and health. The sports department of the institute has always endeavoured to fulfil the basic objectives of sports and games by providing the appropriate infrastructural facilities and the right kind of motivation to aspiring students.

## 5. Sports Committee & Events

The **Sports Committee** under the Student Body Government (SBG) promotes physical fitness, teamwork, and healthy competition. Flagship events include:

| Event | Description |

## 6. Quick Reference – Sports FAQs

**Q: How many basketball courts does DAU have?**
DAU has **2 basketball courts**.

**Q: Does DAU have a cricket field?**
Yes, DAU has **1 cricket field**.

**Q: What is the length of the athletic track at DAU?**
The athletic track is **400 metres** long.

**Q: How many volleyball courts does DAU have?**
DAU has **2 volleyball courts**.

**Q: Does DAU have a gymnasium?**
Yes, DAU has **1 gymnasium** with all modern facilities.

**Q: Is there a yoga and meditation zone on the DAU campus?**
Yes, DAU has a **yoga and meditation zone** on campus.

**Q: When does Phase I of sports coaching take place?**
Phase I coaching takes place from **January to March**.

**Q: When does Phase II of sports coaching take place?**
Phase II coaching takes place from **August to October**.

Concours is DAU's flagship **inter-university sports tournament**, organized annually by the Sports Committee under the Student Body Government.
