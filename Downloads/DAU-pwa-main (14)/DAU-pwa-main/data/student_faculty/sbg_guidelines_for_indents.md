# Guidelines for Indents

> Reformatted (2026-08-09): minor line-break cleanup; content unchanged.

## Procedure for Clearance Indents (per the SBG Constitution)

a. The student convener/member of the respective Club or Committee will collect all the bills after the event.
b. The student convener/member of the respective Club or Committee will get the bills approved by the faculty mentor of the particular Club or Committee.
c. The student convener/member of the respective Club or Committee will submit the indent to the SBG Treasurer for approval.
d. The SBG Treasurer will get the clearance or settlement approved by the Chief Accounts Officer and the Executive Registrar.

## General Guidelines

a. The indent for an event needs to be submitted within **7 days** after the event ends. Any delays should be addressed to the treasurer via email with a valid reason, or there may be consequences for late submission.
b. Attach the original bills/payment screenshots in the sequence in which they are listed in the table (in the indent form).
c. In case of cash dealings, **do not** forget to take the bill or receipt from the vendor proving the purchase — the money won't be reimbursed without bills/receipts.
d. Print the pages on **both sides** in hard copy to avoid wasting paper.
e. Do not forget to attach the cancelled cheque of the bank account into which the amount will be deposited.
f. Attach the printed pages in a neat stack with a single staple.
