# SBG General Meeting [August 2025] — Minutes of the Meeting

- **Date:** 04/08/2025
- **Time:** 6:30 PM onwards
- **Location:** LT-3
- **Attendees:** SBG Core; all committee members, IEEE SB, Sambhav, DCEI and all club cores; general students as audience

> Reformatted (2026-08-09): fixed several missing spaces after periods lost in PDF extraction (e.g. "events.The members"), and cleaned up stray mid-sentence headers. Content otherwise unchanged.

## Agenda

1. MSTC: increase 1 core seat of the club.
2. Formation of new club: Reader's Society.
3. Increase 3 open selection seats in the Annual Festival Committee.
4. General Student Concerns (Non-Voting Agenda).

## Minutes

### 1. MSTC — Increase 1 Core Seat

A proposal was put forth to increase the number of core committee seats for MSTC from 6 to 7, citing increased workload and uneven work distribution during flagship events. The members discussed whether regular club members could take on some of these responsibilities instead of adding an additional core member. The club representatives clarified that while general members are capable of handling the tasks, core members are selected for their higher level of responsibility and commitment, which is crucial for the successful execution of major events.

**Conclusion:** The agenda was not approved — it failed to reach a passing majority (two-thirds required for seat-increasing agendas).

### 2. Formation of New Club — Reader's Society

The Reader's Society expressed interest in coming under the Student Body Governance (SBG) to gain formal recognition, which would help them reach a broader student base. It was discussed that, upon recognition, the club would operate independently of the Resource Center (RC) and would be required to adhere to SBG rules regarding room bookings, event approvals, and other protocols. The club also mentioned that, if formed, they would not request a budget allocation or voting rights during the first year of its formation.

**Conclusion:** The agenda was approved — it reached a passing majority (50%).

### 3. Increase 3 Open Selection Seats — Annual Festival Committee

A proposal was presented highlighting the growing workload within the committee, which is disproportionate to the current number of core members. It was noted that although associate members are part of the committee, they face challenges such as limited responsibilities and a lack of formal recognition within the administration. The committee acknowledged that several associate members have contributed at a level comparable to core members. As a result, it was proposed that the number of core seats be increased to better reflect the current workload and contributions.

**Conclusion:** The agenda was approved — it reached a passing majority (two-thirds required for seat-increasing agendas).

### 4. General Student Concerns (Non-Voting Agenda)

Students raised various concerns. The major ones were as follows:

1. **ACs in Classrooms:** It was discussed that larger classrooms — especially CEP 110, CEP 102, LT 2, and LT 3 — require air conditioning, as the atmosphere (especially during humid days year-round) gets very uncomfortable and negatively impacts the teaching-learning environment.
2. **Lab Infrastructure:** It was discussed that lab infrastructure has become really outdated, with a lot of equipment not working properly. The General Lab reportedly has only 1 or 2 PCs working. EVD Second Year students also lack sufficient soldering equipment, which needs to be addressed immediately. More Cadence software licenses also need to be made available.
3. **Speakers in Classrooms:** It was briefly discussed that many classrooms lack good-quality speakers, which hampers the teaching-learning process.
4. **Club Rooms:** It was discussed that clubs face significant challenges without club rooms — including but not limited to no space to practice, no space or limited shared space to store inventory, and problems sharing rooms (e.g. Radio and EHC).
5. **Gym Infrastructure:** It was raised that gym infrastructure is really outdated, with a majority of machines not working, and that the gym requires a complete overhaul to be usable.
6. **Hostel Renovations:** The top floors of the Boys Hostel, as well as the entirety of the Girls Hostel, need major refurbishment — there are leakage problems on the top floors, as well as cracks, old tiles, etc.

The SBG Representatives ensured that these points will be raised with relevant authorities. The SBG also encouraged students to be more proactive in raising their concerns. Students were made aware of the Grievance Redressal Mechanism and its levels, and were encouraged to make use of it as and when necessary.
