## Minutes

### 1. Club Voting Rights

Henceforth, in general meetings, each club is entitled to one vote under the one-vote-per-club rule. Clubs conduct internal voting to determine their stance; in the event of a tie, the convener casts the deciding vote. The presence of at least one club member is deemed sufficient for participation.

**Conclusion:** Approved with a majority vote in favor.

### 2. Dormant Clubs Process

Clubs that fail to attend consecutive meetings (including C/DC meetings, general meetings, and budget sessions) must execute at least one activity. Failure to do so results in the club being classified as dormant, at which point SBG no longer assumes responsibility for its activities.

**Conclusion:** Approved with a majority vote in favor.

### 3. Increase in Internship Period for MTech VLSI and WCSP

Will not be governed by the SBG.

### 4. Increase in Wi-Fi Quota

Will not be governed by the SBG.

### 5. Impeachment Process of SBG Core

Proposal to modify the impeachment voting criteria from a two-thirds majority to approximately 51% was discussed. Concerns were raised that biases could exist on both sides regardless of the required majority; it was noted that open discussion helps eliminate most partiality, but that reducing the threshold may let larger committees unfairly influence outcomes. The student who proposed the agenda maintained that the overall voting criteria should be lowered.

**Conclusion:** Rejected — the majority voted against it.

### 6. Felicitation of Sports Team Members and Captains

A proposal was made to enhance recognition of team members and captains, since acknowledgment solely during Concours was deemed insufficient. Going forward, SBG will felicitate team captains with certificates signed by the Dean (Students) and the Sports Officer.

**Conclusion:** Approved with a majority vote in favor.

### 7. Addition of One More Selection Seat for 1st-Year Students in SPC

A proposal to increase the 1st-year seat matrix, since assigning CRs to only five students accelerates their transition to handling another company's CR duties faster than other batches. Concerns were also raised about increasing workload, particularly with the EVD batch now joining the summer internship drive. Discussion covered whether EVD inclusion should be mandatory, and whether future branch additions should scale the seat matrix — left to be decided based on workload.

**Conclusion:** Approved with a majority vote in favor.

### 8. Proposal to Increase a Second-Year Election Seat in TSC

Discussion centered on the current seat matrix and the growing number of events, highlighting the need for an additional member and greater second-year interest. The audience questioned why the committee needed an elected (rather than associate) seat, and suggested TSC include associate members year-round. It was decided the agenda would proceed only for the selection seat; other aspects remain unchanged.

**Conclusion:** Approved with a majority vote in favor, for the selection seat.

### 9. Formation of Core + Future Plans for Sambhav

A proposal to convert Sambhav into a club under the SBG, primarily to establish stability and attract more student participation/volunteers. Discussion covered the implications: funding might be requested for large-scale events in future, but the club would primarily rely on donations rather than an SBG budget allocation, and if Sambhav's initiatives benefit the non-DA public, SBG funding won't be granted. Many participants argued Sambhav should remain an organization rather than become a club, structured and operated accordingly.

**Conclusion:** Deferred to the next general meeting for further discussion.

### 10. Increase in 3 Selection Seats (2 General + 1 Third-Year) for AFC (Synapse)

A proposal highlighting increased committee workload relative to current membership — departments like production and events need additional members due to overlapping responsibilities and department clashes. Associate members contribute at the same level as core members in many cases but face limited responsibilities and recognition, prompting a request for core-seat recognition. However, per the SBG constitution ("the request for seat increment can only be proposed once in an academic year"), and since a similar request was already proposed at the last general meeting, this proposal could not proceed to a vote.

**Conclusion:** Not put to a vote (constitutional limit already used this academic year).

### 11. Farewell/Freshers Committee (2nd and 3rd Year Students)

Due to certain issues, the Freshers' event could not take place this time, but there's a strong desire to continue the tradition. A proposal was made to establish a dedicated club/committee responsible for organizing both the Freshers' event and the Farewell, with its budget allocated from the SBG.

### 12. Yearbook Introduction by Press Club

The Press Club has proposed taking responsibility for producing the yearbook for future batches — collecting data from students and professors, incorporating photos from events and fests, while aiming to minimize student costs and ensure successful execution.
