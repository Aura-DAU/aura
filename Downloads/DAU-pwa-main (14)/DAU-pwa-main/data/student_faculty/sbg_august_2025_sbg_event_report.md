# August 2025 SBG event report

Dhirubhai Ambani University

Student Body Government (SBG)

SBG activities report

AUGUST 2025

## Mosaic of Dhirubhai Ambani

The Cubing Club created a mosaic of Dhirubhai Ambani on the occasion of TEDxDAIICT.

The event had a participation of around 125.

Date of event : 01/08/2025

## Janmashtami Celebration

Cultural Committee organised the matki-phod competition and mud holi on the occasion of Janmashtami.

The event had around 65-70 participants.

Date of event : 16/08/2025

## Techloop

Google Developer Groups on Campus organized Techloop, an event focused on introducing the UG 2025 batch to the world of development.

There were around 385 participating students from UG 2025 batch

Date of event : 25/08/2025

## Open Jamming session

The Music Club hosted an open jamming session to create a vibrant musical culture on campus.

The event was held near the cafeteria entrance and attracted participation of around 150 students.

Date : 25/08/2025

## Mosaic of Lord Ganesha

Cubing Club created a mosaic of Lord Ganesha on the occasion of Ganesh Chaturthi

There were around 40 participants.

Date of event : 26/08/2025

## Ganesh Chaturthi Celebration

The cultural committee celebrated Ganesh Chaturthi on campus.

There were around 400 participants who gathered to welcome lord Ganesha.

Date of event : 27/08/2025

## Master Cydo’s Scroll

The Cyber information and network security club organized this event.

The event was divided into 2 phases, the first phase was a brief overview of the cybersecurity field and the second phase consisted of a Catch-the-flag event in which students participated in a team of two.

There were 59 participants in phase 1 and 39 in phase 2.

Date of event : 28/08/2025

## Book 1: Prologue

The Reader’s Society hosted its inaugural event : Book 01: Prologue.

The session aimed at bringing together students and faculty members with a shared interest in literature, stories and conversations.

37 students and 3 faculty members participated in this event.

Date of event : 28/08/2025

## BuzzQuiz

Headrush (Quizzing club) organized a quiz on pop and meme culture.

The quiz attracted a participation of around 70.

Date of event : 28/08/2025

## Bidding war

Bidding War was a competitive ad-skit event where teams performed company ads, and the scores were decided through judges, bidding, and audience response.

The event was organized by business club

There were around 70 participants in the event

Date of event : 29/08/2025
