# Synapse Committee — DAU Overview

Synapse is the annual cultural-and-technical festival of Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT) in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. It is run by the student body under the direction of the Synapse Committee and stands as one of the most vibrant and anticipated college festivals in the region.
