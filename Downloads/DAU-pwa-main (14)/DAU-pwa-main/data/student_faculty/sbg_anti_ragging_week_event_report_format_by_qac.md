### Short Event Description

In accordance with UGC guidelines and DAU's ongoing commitment to a safe, inclusive, ragging-free environment, DAU celebrated Anti-Ragging Day & Week with the following:

a. **Orientation for Undergraduate Students** — The day began with a dedicated orientation session for newly admitted undergraduate students, addressed by the Dean (Students), who emphasized the university's zero-tolerance policy toward ragging and the importance of mutual respect, harmony, and inclusivity among students.

b. **Screening of Informative Videos** — Following the address, informative videos on anti-ragging themes were screened, covering:
   - Legal provisions and punishments related to ragging
   - Rights and responsibilities of students
   - Support mechanisms and grievance redressal systems available at the university

c. **Awareness through Selfie-Stand Activity** — A themed selfie-stand was installed to promote awareness interactively; students participated by taking and sharing photographs, reinforcing the message of staying vigilant and committed to a ragging-free campus.

d. **Information on Committees & Vigilance Squads** — Students were informed about the Anti-Ragging Committees and Anti-Ragging Vigilance Squads (comprising faculty, administrative staff, and student representatives), whose role is to monitor student interactions, handle complaints promptly, and ensure the safety and well-being of all.
