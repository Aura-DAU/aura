## Purpose
Plan future knowledge acquisition to improve completeness of faculty-related information in the knowledge base.

## 1. Faculty Research Collaboration Details
- **Source Type:** Institutional Research Database / Department Records  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (Research & Development) / Department Heads  
- **Information Required:** Detailed mapping of faculty-to-faculty and faculty-to-industry collaborations, including ongoing joint projects, research labs, and cross-department research groups.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Better understanding of collaboration networks and research ecosystem within the institute.

---

## 2. Faculty Teaching Load & Course Allocation System
- **Source Type:** Academic Administration Record  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (Faculty) / Academic Section Office  
- **Information Required:** Official structure for course allocation, teaching workload distribution, and semester-wise teaching assignments.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Ability to answer queries about teaching responsibilities and workload fairness.

---

## 3. Faculty Evaluation & Appraisal Metrics (Detailed)
- **Source Type:** Internal HR / Academic Performance Policy  
- **Person/Committee:** Faculty Affairs Committee / Dean (Faculty)  
- **Information Required:** Exact evaluation metrics including weightage of teaching, research, publications, student feedback, and administrative work.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Transparent explanation of faculty performance assessment system.

---

## 4. Faculty Office Hours & Student Interaction Policy
- **Source Type:** Academic Policy Document  
- **Person/Committee:** Department Heads / Faculty Handbook Committee  
- **Information Required:** Standardized office hours, mandatory student consultation requirements, and communication guidelines between students and faculty.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Improved clarity on student–faculty interaction norms.

---

## 5. Faculty Funding & Research Grant Allocation Rules
- **Source Type:** Research Funding Guidelines  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / Sponsored Research Office  
- **Information Required:** Eligibility criteria, funding cycles, proposal approval process, and internal/external grant distribution mechanisms.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Clear guidance on how faculty research projects are funded.

---

## 6. Faculty Leave & Sabbatical Policy Details
- **Source Type:** HR Policy Document  
- **Person/Committee:** Institute Administration / HR Office  
- **Information Required:** Rules for sabbatical leave, research leave, personal leave, and conditions for long-term academic absence.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Accurate handling of faculty availability and absence-related queries.

---

## 7. Faculty Industry Engagement & Consultancy Rules
- **Source Type:** Institutional Consultancy Policy  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / Finance & Administration Office  
- **Information Required:** Guidelines for faculty consulting work, industry collaboration limits, revenue sharing, and conflict-of-interest rules.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Proper governance of faculty external engagements.
