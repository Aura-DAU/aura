# DA MUN 3.0 - Model United Nations Conference at DAU

# Overview

DA MUN 3.0 is the third edition of the Dhirubhai Ambani Model United Nations conference, hosted by the debate society at Dhirubhai Ambani University (formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar. The offline conference brings together seasoned debaters and first-time participants to experience diplomacy, politics, and global governance through committee sessions, debates on global policies, and a social evening.

# Main Content

**Dates:** Friday, 20 June 2026 – Saturday, 21 June 2026
**Venue:** Dhirubhai Ambani University (Formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar, Gujarat
**Format:** Offline Conference
**Registration Deadline:** June 9, 2026

## About

DA MUN 3.0 is a platform for students and aspiring leaders to step into the shoes of diplomats and policymakers. Whether you are a seasoned debater or a first-time participant, this conference offers a rich experience in diplomacy, politics, and global governance. The event features committee sessions, debates on global policies, and a social evening.

## Committees

### Diplofest (Youth Parliament & Election Simulation)
This committee focuses on simulating democratic processes. Delegates step into the roles of political leaders and policymakers to engage in debates, draft bills, and navigate democratic decision-making.

### UNGA War
A United Nations General Assembly simulation focused on conflict resolution and international security, where delegates debate and negotiate solutions to global security challenges.

## Who Can Participate
The event is open to all college students. Both seasoned MUN debaters and first-time participants are welcome to register and participate.
