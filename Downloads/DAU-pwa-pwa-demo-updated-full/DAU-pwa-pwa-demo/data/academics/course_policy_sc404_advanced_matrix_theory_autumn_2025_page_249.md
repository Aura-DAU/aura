# Advanced Matrix Theory (SC404)

## Main Content

Advanced Matrix Theory (SC 404)

Course Placement: It is an elective course for BTech MnC, ICT and CS program.
Credit Structure: 3-0-0-3
Prerequisite: Linear Algebra (MC215/SC223), Real and Complex analysis (SC107/MC222)

Course Objective: Notions of matrices are of fundamental importance in Mathematics as well as several
other areas including Machine learning, Data Science, Computer vision, Networking and Optimization,
Computer Science, Signal processing etc. This course aims to develop a strong foundation for matrix
analysis, developing essential tools. The emphasis will be on analytic aspects as well as algebraic aspects
of Matrix Theory.

Course content: (The selection of topics is variable depending on time constraints.)

1. Review: eigenvalues, eigenvectors, special types of matrices.

2. Schur triangular form, spectral theorem, singular value decomposition, polar decomposition,

generalized inverse, least squares solutions.

3. Positive semidefinite and Positive definite matrices: Properties and characterizations

4. Matrix norms, spectral radius formula, relationship between matrix norms and singular

values.

5. Perron–Frobenius theory: Matrices with positive and non-negative entries.

Suggested Textbook/references:

[1] F. Zhang. Matrix theory. Basic results and techniques. Springer, 1999.
[2] R.A. Horn and C.R. Johnson. Matrix analysis. Cambridge University Press, 2012.

Assessment method: In-Sem 1 - 30%, In-Sem 2 - 30%, End-Sem - 40%

Course Outcomes:
After completing the course, the students should be able to:

 Understand basic concepts related to matrix theory [PO12]
 Handle abstract ideas of mathematics and learn to write mathematical proofs [PO1, PO2]
  Understand different types of representations of a matrix [PO4].
 Develop skills towards solving problems using properties and characterization of special
matrices [PO3].
