# Scholarships and Financial Aid

## Overview

DAU (formerly DA-IICT) offers a range of merit-based and merit-cum-means scholarships to support students across BTech, BS+MS, and PG programs.

## Main Content

### Institute Scholarships

| Scholarship | Eligibility | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Merit Scholarship | Top SPI performers each semester | Full or partial tuition fee reimbursement |
| Merit-cum-Means Scholarship | SPI criteria + Family income < ₹6–8 lakh/year | Partial tuition fee waiver |
| UG Institute Fellowship | Top JEE ranks / board performance | 100% tuition fee coverage |
| BS+MS Institute Fellowship | National-level entrance rank or board merit | 100% tuition fee coverage |
| DAFS Merit Scholarship | NRI/Foreign national category students | Merit-based fee support |

### External Scholarships

| Scholarship | Criteria | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Cybage Khushboo Scholarship | ICT/law domain, family income < ₹5 lakh | Up to 80% tuition fee or ₹1,50,000 p.a. (whichever is lower) |
| Satnaam WaheGuruji Scholarship | BTech, SPI/CPI criteria + family income | Merit-cum-means financial support |

### General Eligibility Conditions

- Must maintain minimum SPI/CPI as specified for each scholarship.
- **No backlogs** in any credit course.
- **No disciplinary proceedings** against the student.
- All fees must be paid by due date before scholarship disbursement.
- Students must be duly registered for the current semester.

### Additional Financial Assistance

- Students facing exceptional financial hardship may apply for additional support from the institute directly.
- Applications are reviewed on a case-by-case basis by the Scholarship Committee.

## Important Information

- Scholarships are **disbursed semester-wise**, not annually.
- Scholarship amounts are credited directly to the student's account after fee payment.
- Students must **re-apply or maintain eligibility** each semester — scholarships are not automatically renewed without meeting criteria.
- Financial hardship cases: Contact the Academic Office directly.
