# DAU PWA - Demo Questions
This document contains a curated list of high-impact, realistic questions grouped by categories to demonstrate the capabilities of the DAU RAG Assistant during live presentations.

---

## 🏛️ Category 1: About the University & Accreditation

### Question 1.1: General Overview
* **Query:** "What is the history of Dhirubhai Ambani University, and when did it become operational?"
* **Expected Grounding Source:** `https://www.daiict.ac.in/about-us`
* **Key Information to Look For:** Operational since August 6, 2001 (initially as DA-IICT); established under Act No. 6 of 2003 of the Gujarat Legislature.

### Question 1.2: Accreditations and Rankings
* **Query:** "What certifications or ratings does DAU hold from NAAC, GSIRF, and other bodies?"
* **Expected Grounding Source:** `https://www.daiict.ac.in/about-us`
* **Key Information to Look For:** NAAC A+ Grade (CGPA 3.32/4 in 2023), GSIRF Five Star rating, FSSAI Five Star "Eat Right Campus" rating.

## 🎓 Category 2: Academic Programs & Curriculum

### Question 2.1: Course Load and Curriculum details
* **Query:** "What is the curriculum structure for the B.Tech in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (CSAI) program?"
* **Expected Grounding Source:** `https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-csai`
* **Key Information to Look For:** Combine shared institute core (17 courses) with program core (12 courses), program electives (8 courses), free electives (2 courses), and 2 online pass/fail certificate courses.

### Question 2.2: Advanced Subjects
* **Query:** "Are there courses on Trustworthy AI and Reinforcement Learning in the CSAI curriculum?"
* **Expected Grounding Source:** `https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-csai`
* **Key Information to Look For:** Yes, Trustworthy AI is `PC-311` (Semester V) and Reinforcement Learning is `PC-312` (Semester VI).
