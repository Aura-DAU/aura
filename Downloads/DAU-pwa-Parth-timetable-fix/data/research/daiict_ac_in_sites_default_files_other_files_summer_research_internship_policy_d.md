Stipend and Housing
•     The intern shall receive a stipend of Rs.1000 per week for a maximum of eight weeks.
•     The intern shall reside on the DAU campus. The hostel accommodation charges shall be waived.

•     The Institute will provide a travel allowance of up to INR 3000. The selected candidate will need
to submit the original receipts/tickets of travels to/from DAU during the internship period.
•     Meals and other expenses shall be the responsibility of the intern.
Application Process
•     Faculty members interested in mentoring summer research interns will post an advertisement on
the DAU website by March of each year.
o     The advertisement will typically outline the sRl project, required student skills and pre-
requisites, specific eligibility criteria, expected leaning outcomes, and the duration of the
internship.
•     Interested students shall directly submit a formal application to the DAu faculty mentor. The
application shall include:
o     The student's resume or curriculum vitae highlighting their academic achievements and
relevant experiences.
o     A statement of interest outlining their research interests, relevant coursework, and
reasons for applying to the specific internship(s).
o   , No objection certificate from their parent Institute
o   . Optionally, letters of recommendation
;     Based on the application received, the faculty mentor shall decide and select the candidate for the
SRI.
•     The final approval shall be given by the office of Dean Research.
Notes
•     The intern shall actively participate in the research project as directed by the faculty mentor,
maintain regular communication with the faculty mentor, complete assigned tasks and meet
deadlines, and shall be present at the Institute's campus for at least 40 hours per week.
•     The intern needs to produce an evidence of the outcome of the research in the form ofa report.
The report has to be approved/signed by the faculty mentor and the approved report has to be sent
to the Dean Research Office. The report shall be provided to the Resource Center for inclusion in
the Institute's Repository.
•     All interns shall adhere to DAU's policies on safety, ethical conduct, and research integrity.
•     The intern shall also acknowledge the faculty mentor and the DAU SRl program in any
publications or presentations resulting from the research.
•     The rights of the Intellectual property, if any, resulting from the sRl program shall be with DAU.
•     On successful completion of the internship, after the approval by the faculty mentor, and
submission of the internship report to Dean Research Office, the student may receive a certificate
of SRI completio.n from DAU.
