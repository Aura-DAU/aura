# B.Tech Mini Project (BMP) Guidelines

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Rules-Guidelines/Project-Thesis Guidelines/BTech Mini-Project Guidelines.pdf`
- **Dated:** March 1, 2023

## Overview

B.Tech Mini Project (BMP) is a course offered to B.Tech (ICT) and B.Tech (ICT+CS) students, effective from the 2016-17 batch. Credit structure: **0-1-4-3**. Classified under the Free/Open Elective category, offered to encourage supervised project work.

## Terms and Conditions of BMP Registration

- Minimum CPI of **7.5** required to register.
- Must be done **on-campus**, under faculty supervision.
- Maximum **1 BMP per semester**.
- Maximum **2 BMPs during the entire program** (combined credits not exceeding 6).
- A BMP is **not** considered an overload.
- May only register for BMP in a semester where **no Audit course** is also registered.
- Group size: **maximum 2 students**.
- A faculty member can supervise **maximum 3 BMPs per semester**.
- BMP registration is available from **Semester 6 onwards**.
