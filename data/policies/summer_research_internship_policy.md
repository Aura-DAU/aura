# Summer Research Internship (SRI) Policy

## Objective

Provides students **not enrolled at DAU** (UG, PG, or PhD elsewhere) a short-term research internship opportunity at DAU during the summer.

## Student Eligibility

Open to students currently enrolled in a recognized UG/PG program elsewhere who meet DAU's academic and eligibility criteria for the specific internship.

## Faculty Mentors

- Any full-time DAU faculty member can serve as an SRI mentor.
- Mentors advertise available SRI positions and select their own intern(s).

## Duration and Structure

- Internship runs a maximum of **8 weeks between May and July**.
- The intern commits a minimum of **40 hours/week** to the research project.
- The faculty mentor guides the intern's project scope, expected outcomes, and any research preparation/presentation requirements.

## Stipend and Housing

- Stipend: **₹1,000/week**, for a maximum of 8 weeks.
- Free hostel accommodation on the DAU campus (fees waived).
- Travel allowance up to **₹3,000** (original receipts/tickets required).
- Meals and other personal expenses are the intern's own responsibility.

## Application Process

- Faculty post SRI project advertisements on the DAU website by **March** each year, outlining the project, required skills, eligibility, learning outcomes, and duration.
- Students apply directly to the faculty mentor with: resume/CV, a statement of interest, a **No Objection Certificate from their home institute**, and optionally recommendation letters.
- The faculty mentor selects the candidate; final approval rests with the **Dean Research** office.

## Intern Responsibilities

- Actively participate in the project as directed, maintain regular communication with the mentor, meet deadlines, and be on campus at least 40 hours/week.
- Submit an outcome report, approved/signed by the mentor, sent to the Dean Research office and then to the Resource Centre for inclusion in the Institute's Repository.
- Follow DAU's safety, ethics, and research-integrity policies.
- Acknowledge the faculty mentor and DAU's SRI program in any resulting publications/presentations.
- **IP rights from the SRI program belong to DAU.**

On successful completion (mentor approval + report submission), the student may receive a **certificate of SRI completion** from DAU.

*Issued by DAU, Gandhinagar.*
