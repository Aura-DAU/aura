# Teaching Fellow Policy

The Teaching Fellow (TF) Program at DAU strengthens the teaching-learning experience in UG/PG programs, giving motivated individuals hands-on academic-instruction/student-engagement experience while collaborating closely with faculty, refining pedagogical skills, and building academic/professional competencies for a future in academia or research.

## Job Category & Compensation

- **Full-Time Contractual Position.**
- Initial appointment: **1 year**, renewable on performance/institutional need.
- After 1 year: eligible for a **TF completion certificate**; encouraged to apply to DAU's PhD program per guidelines.

| Qualification | Monthly Compensation |
|---|---|
| Master's degree | ₹55,000/month (consolidated) |
| Ph.D. degree | ₹65,000/month (consolidated, starting) |

A token amount is reserved from the monthly stipend and refunded on successful semester completion (discourages mid-semester withdrawal).

## Role Summary

TFs contribute to UG/PG course delivery — **3 courses/semester.** Faculty typically lead lectures/discussions (morning or afternoon sessions); TFs coordinate the complementary session, attending/participating in faculty-led sessions and overseeing the other.

## TF Responsibilities/Duties

Physical presence during lab (setup + leading lab sections); assist in designing lab assignments, demonstrating experiments, preparing solutions; handle online lab assignment/report submissions; prepare lab manuals; assist in grading; formal office hours; take lab attendance and track participation; manage announcements/deadlines; provide LMS admin support; help faculty prepare instructional/reading materials; other assigned academic-support tasks.

## Eligibility

- Minimum Master's degree + skills/qualifications per the specific TF advertisement.
- Strong academic background from a reputed institute.
- Prior teaching/research/industry experience preferred.
- **GATE/NET qualified candidates preferred.**

## Desired Skills

Relevant domain/technical knowledge; strong English proficiency; enthusiastic, eager to learn, student-friendly attitude.

## How to Apply

Email your CV (with full contact details) to the **Office of the Dean-AP** at `dean-ap_office@dau.ac.in`, subject line "Application for Teaching Fellow."

## Terms of Appointment

- Purely contractual, annual, renewable subject to performance/continuing need.
- Must assist **3 courses/semester**; may work with more than one faculty member, up to the max course load.
- Reports to: **Faculty Mentor and PG Convenor.**

## Process

Post Graduate Committee (PGC) generates each semester's TF requirement → advertised on institute website/portals → PGC shortlists applications → selection panel interview → approval by Dean (AP) and Director → HR issues offer letters → PGC-designed orientation for all TFs.

## TF Load & Attendance

- **1 TF per 60 registered students** (lab-credit course), or as decided by PGC.
- Must mark presence at the Main Security Gate attendance machine and during labs.
- **4 late marks = 1 day's stipend deduction**; not marking at all = absent, loses 1 day's stipend.
- Some TFs may become "head TF" (in consultation with instructor/prospective TF).
- PGC allocates and announces TFs to faculty/students each semester; approved list forwarded to HR.
- TFs appointed as JRF on external DAU-funded projects must inform course-instructor/PGC/HR before joining — TF role is then discontinued (already-assigned task for that semester must still be completed).
- HR handles TF attendance policy, leave, and stipend disbursement.

## Confidentiality

TFs must keep student records/performance data confidential — disclosure only for academic purposes to authorized personnel.

## Termination and Suspension

- Stipend may be discontinued if a TF fails to deliver labs/tutorials per course outcomes.
- Must submit the monthly TA-style form to the PG Convenor (signed by reporting instructor) by the **25th** of each month, or that month's stipend may not be released.
- Violating the scholarship agreement (external financial aid without approval) is grounds for termination.
- Other terms per the appointment letter.
