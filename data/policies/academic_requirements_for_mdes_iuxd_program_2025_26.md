# Academic Requirements for the MDes Program in Intelligent User Experience Design — MDes (IUxD)

*(Effective from Autumn 2025-26)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_MDes(IUxD)_wef 2025-26  .pdf`
- **Created:** March 2025

## Scope

Master of Design (Intelligent User Experience Design) — MDes (IUxD) — is a two-year postgraduate program governed by these rules, covering post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

**1.1 Categories of Registration:** Resident registration only, requiring registration for a duly approved course programme and payment of prescribed fees, including outstanding dues.

**1.2 Provisional Registration:** New entrants awaiting qualifying-exam results register provisionally, submitting certificates for verification by the Academic Calendar deadline (~8 weeks from registration); originals returned, self-attested copy retained.

**1.3 Late Registration:** ~1 week window with a late fee for compelling reasons. Dean (Academic Programs), on PGC recommendation, may permit registration beyond that in exceptional cases.

**1.4 Academic Advising:**
- Faculty adviser appointed by the Dean (Academic Programs) in consultation with the PGC.
- A student who gets **DE or F** may either: (i) complete additional coursework during the semester for a grade review, in consultation with Course Faculty and the MDes (IUxD) coordinator, or (ii) retake the course the following academic year, in consultation with the MDes (IUxD) Coordinator and Course Faculty (registration per Institute policy). Governed by §2.2.

**1.5 Semester Load:** Prescribed from time to time in the approved program course structure document.

**1.6 Adding/Dropping and Withdrawal:**
- **Adding/dropping courses is not permitted — all courses in MDes (IUxD) are mandatory.**
- Pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean approval before the late-registration deadline; post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin. **Withdrawal permitted for only one semester during the entire program.** Improper non-registration/withdrawal risks being struck off the rolls.
- Returning students register per the prescribed curriculum, subject to prerequisites. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show as "withdrawn" and count toward maximum program duration.

**2.1 MDes (IUxD) Specialisation:** Total credits required: **80** (subject to revision per the approved curriculum for the batch). **Maximum permissible duration: 3 years.**

## 3. Academic Performance Requirement

**3.1 SPI/CPI:** Same weighted-average definitions as MDes (CD) and other postgraduate programs, to 2 decimal places.

**3.2 Minimum CPI for graduation:**

| Program | CPI for Graduation |
|---|---|
| MDes (IUxD) | **6.0** |

**3.3 Academic Probation and Dismissal:** CPI below 6.0 at semester-end triggers probation (written intimation) for the next semester. PGC **may** (not "will") prescribe a specific course load and/or minimum SPI target — *(same "may" phrasing as MDes (CD), differing from the M.Sc programs' "will")*. PGC monitors probationary students; continued poor performance leads to a Director recommendation to leave, with a termination letter from the appropriate authority.

## 4. Glossary

Same definitions as MDes (CD) and other postgraduate programs: Academic Probation, CPI, Grade Points, PGC, Semester (Autumn: July–November; Winter: December/January–April), Semester Credits, Semester Grade Points, SPI.
