# Fee Structure — BTech and PG Programs

## Overview

This document outlines the fee structure for undergraduate (BTech) and postgraduate programs at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), formerly DA-IICT.

## Main Content

### BTech Fee Structure (2025-26)

| Fee Component | Amount (INR) |
|---|---|
| Tuition Fee | ₹1,92,500 per semester |
| Registration Fee | ₹2,500 per semester |
| Caution Deposit (refundable, one-time) | ₹25,000 |
| **Total at Admission (Sem 1 + Deposit)** | **₹2,17,500** |

### International / NRI Students (DAFS Category)

| Fee Component | Amount (USD) |
|---|---|
| Tuition Fee | ~$6,000 per semester |
| Registration Fee | As applicable |
| Hostel Fee | As applicable |

### Additional Notes

- Fees are revised **annually by approximately 8–10%**.
- Students must pay fees in full by the due date to remain eligible for scholarships and registration.
- **BTech EVD program** may have additional value-added course fees.
- All fees are subject to revision by the university at its discretion.

### Payment Timeline

- Semester 1 fees (including caution deposit) are due at the time of counselling/admission.
- Subsequent semester fees are due before the start of each semester.

## Important Information

- Contact for fee queries: **ug_admissions@dau.ac.in**
- Helpline for NRI/Foreign category: **(+91) 8488876445**
- Fee receipts are issued by the Finance Office.
- Hostel fees and electricity charges are charged separately.
