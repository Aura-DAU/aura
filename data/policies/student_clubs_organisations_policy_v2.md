# Student Clubs and Organisations Policy

## Overview

Student clubs and activities at DAU are governed by the **Student Body Government (SBG)**, which is the official representative body for the student community.

## Main Content

### Student Body Government (SBG)

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Total Members | 100+ |
| Committees | 9 (Cultural, Sports, Hostel Management, and others) |
| Hobby Clubs | 22–25 clubs |
| Governance | SBG Core Team, elected by parliamentary elections |
| Mentor | Dean (Students) |

### SBG Committees (Main)

- Cultural Committee
- Sports Committee
- Hostel Management Committee
- Technical Committees (IEEE, Google Developers Group, etc.)
- Social Activities Committee
- And others

### Hobby Clubs (Examples)

- Music Club
- Dance Club
- Drama Club
- Photography Club
- Robotics Club
- Coding/Programming clubs

### Forming and Running a Club

- New clubs must be proposed to and approved by the **SBG Core Team**.
- Clubs must operate within the budget allocated by SBG.
- Clubs must follow the **SOP for Event Organisation at DAU** when hosting events.
- All club activities must be consistent with the Student Code of Conduct.

### Responsibilities of Club Members

- Maintain an active event calendar.
- Submit activity and financial reports to SBG.
- Ensure all events have proper administrative approval.
- Uphold the reputation of DAU in all activities — including outreach events.

## Important Information

- SBG serves as the **primary interface between students and administration**.
- Club budgets are managed and allocated by SBG.
- Students can join multiple clubs as long as academic performance is maintained.
- Elections for SBG Core Team are held annually via parliamentary election process.

## Contact Information

- **Authority:** Dean (Students) / SBG Core Team
- **Office:** Student Affairs Office, DAU Campus, Gandhinagar
