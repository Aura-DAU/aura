# Late Registration or Course Adjustment Policy and Fixation of Fine

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Rules-Guidelines/Policy for Late Registration or Course Adjustment and Fixation of Fine.pdf`
- **Dated:** 24 February 2014
- **Signed by:** Jalpesh Pandya, Asst. Registrar (Academic)

## Policy

1. **Late registration** (for fee payment purposes only) is allowed from the start of classes until the last date of late registration specified in the Academic Calendar.
2. **Registration changes beyond** the Academic Calendar's Add/Drop and Drop-Course-adjustment windows are **not allowed**.
3. Amendments beyond the time limit are allowed only in **special cases**, subject to fine payment (after following the appropriate procedure), per the schedule below.

## Fine Schedule

| Situation | Fine Amount |
|---|---|
| **No registration in e-Campus within the Add/Drop period** — fees paid | Rs. 2,500/- |
| **No registration in e-Campus within the Add/Drop period** — fees not paid | Rs. 2,500/- + late-payment fine (@ Rs. 300/day, or Rs. 2,500/-, whichever is less) |
| **No registration in e-Campus after the Add/Drop period** — fees paid | Rs. 5,000/- |
| **No registration in e-Campus after the Add/Drop period** — fees not paid | Rs. 5,000/- + late-payment fine (@ Rs. 300/day, or Rs. 2,500/-, whichever is less) |
| **Missed registering for some course(s)** (after the last Add/Drop date) — addition only, subject to convener's approval | Rs. 2,500/- |
| **Amendment to registration** (after the last Add/Drop date) — course replacement, subject to convener's approval | Rs. 5,000/- |
| **Dropping a course** (after the last Add/Drop date), subject to convener's approval | Rs. 1,000/- per course, or Rs. 2,500/-, whichever is less |
