# M.Sc (Data Science): Final Semester Industry Internship / Research Project Guidelines

*(Effective from AY 2021-22)*

## Overview

M.Sc (DS) students undertake a Research Project / Industry Internship (course code **PC610**) in their final semester, for **13–16 weeks**. Graded **Pass/Fail** based on performance. Can be done in two modes: On-campus or Off-campus.

## On-Campus Project

Under supervision of a DA-IICT faculty member. Students identify faculty mentors and explore project opportunities based on their research interests. The semester-long project can be on any topic (Engineering, Computational, Natural, or Social Sciences) where Data Science skills acquired in the first three semesters can be applied to a data-driven/data-science problem.

> **Note:** A student may register for a **maximum of one course** alongside the project if their CPI is below 6.0, or they have a course-work backlog.

## Off-Campus Project (Industry Internship)

Under supervision of a faculty member at a reputed academic/research organization. Students must identify an on-campus mentor before the project starts (any faculty member, by request; by default the M.Sc-DS coordinator).

1. **Via the placement office:** All matters/communications up to the project start are handled by the placement office. Any NOC needed before starting is requested from and issued by the placement office.
2. **Once the project starts:** The student provides internship details, company information, and the industry mentor's contact details to the on-campus mentor.
3. **Self-arranged internships** (without placement office help): Student submits the offer letter, work details, and off-campus mentor/supervisor details to the M.Sc-DS coordinator for review. If satisfactory, permission is granted; otherwise the student must find another option (in consultation with the industry mentor/supervisor). Any needed NOC is requested via the program coordinator, who forwards it to the placement office for issuance; the placement office keeps all off-campus internship records.

> **Note:** A student may **not** register for any course alongside an off-campus internship/project.

## Evaluation

Internship/project work should fall within the broad area of Data Science (AI & ML, Data Analysis, Statistics, Mathematics, Modeling and Prediction, Computer Science, Analytics, Informatics, Data Mining, IT, Data pipeline infrastructure, Data lake architecture, and domain-knowledge-based projects). Evaluated on:

- Complexity of the problem and scale of work
- Novelty of the work
- Data Science skills learned/used
- Quality of solution/deliverables/results
- Any innovative contribution

**Two-part evaluation:**

- **Part 1:** For off-campus projects, the on-site supervisor forwards a recommended score to the on-campus mentor. For on-campus projects, the project mentor recommends the score directly.
- **Part 2:** After completion, students present their work to a faculty panel formed by the M.Sc-DS coordinator and approved by the Dean (AP) and PG Convener.

**Documents considered for evaluation:**
1. Student's presentation of the internship/project work.
2. Project report (see Appendix A format below).
3. Feedback from the project/internship supervisor (see Appendix B).

Final grade combines the Part-1 (mentor) score and Part-2 (presentation to evaluation panel) performance; grades uploaded to e-Campus.

## Appendix A — Recommended Project Report Format

1. Introduction (problem statement)
2. Block diagram showing project structure and flow
3. Detailed methodology / implementation / techniques / tools used
4. Case studies / use cases addressed
5. Key contribution, results, and discussion
6. GitHub link to the project (where applicable)

## Appendix B — Supervisor Feedback

Collected from both on-campus and off-campus supervisors, scored 0–10, on:

1. Sincerity and Diligence
2. Competency
3. Ability to take challenges
4. Proactivity / Proactiveness
5. Quality of Work Produced
6. Quantity of Work Produced
7. Documentation Work

**Sample Feedback Form fields:** Date; Organization Name and Address; On-site supervisor name/email; Student ID and Name; Date of Joining/Leaving; brief project description (3–4 lines); the 7 parameters above scored /10 with remarks; expected knowledge/skills vs. whether the student met them; any other comments/suggestions/feedback.
