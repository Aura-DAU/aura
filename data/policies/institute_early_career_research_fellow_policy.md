# Institute Early Career Research Fellow

Recognizes and supports outstanding DAU doctoral scholars with excellent research productivity/scholarly impact during their PhD. Offers a chance to continue independent research at DAU right after PhD thesis submission (while the thesis is under review), nurturing the next generation of researchers.

## 1. Eligibility Criteria

**Publications:** minimum **2 peer-reviewed publications as first author** from the PhD research at DAU. Must be accepted/published in SCImago (SJR) Q1 or Q2 journals and/or A/A*/B category conferences (per core.edu or Aminer rankings) at time of publication. **At least one publication must be Q1 journal or A/A* conference**; the other must be Q1/Q2 journal, A/A*/B conference, or a DAU-approved conference (list prepared by Dean-Research office).

**Thesis Submission:** must have officially **submitted the PhD thesis within 5 years of admission** to the DAU PhD program. Official submission date (certified by Dean-AP office) governs eligibility.

## 2. Duration of Fellowship

- **1 year (not extendable)** if thesis submitted within **4.5 years** with the required 2 publications.
- **(12 - X) months (not extendable)** if thesis submitted within **5 years (4.5 years + X months)** with the required 2 publications.

## 3. Fellowship Grant and Travel Support

- **Monthly fellowship:** consolidated **₹75,000** (revised/announced by the institute periodically). **No HRA.**
- **Travel grant:** **₹1,00,000** per Fellow, to support presenting research at national/international conferences — covers registration, air travel, visa charges, and daily allowance, per the existing CPDA policy. Fellows may combine this with any unutilized financial assistance from their full-time PhD. **Valid only during the 1-year fellowship**, administered via the Dean-Research office.
- **Accommodation:** on-campus hostel accommodation may be provided, subject to availability/institute norms.

## 4. Research Work and TAship

- The Fellow must be hosted by a DAU **faculty mentor** (preferably the PhD supervisor or another faculty in a related area) overseeing the fellow's research.
- Expected to engage full-time in research and produce quality outputs (journal/conference publications).
- All fellows are assigned **TA duties for 1 course/semester**, per institute academic requirements.

## 5. Application Procedure

Apply **within 1 month** of official PhD thesis submission, to the **Office of the Dean-AP** via email, with:
- Curriculum Vitae.
- List of publications from PhD work (with DAU affiliation).
- PhD Thesis Submission Certificate (from Dean-AP office).
- Recommendation letter from a prospective Faculty Supervisor for the fellowship research.
- Research proposal, prepared jointly with the faculty supervisor.

## 6. Selection Process

Applicants meeting **both** eligibility criteria (thesis within 5 years + 2 first-author publications, at least one Q1/A-A*) and submitting all required documents are awarded the Fellowship.

**Effective date: 1 January 2026.**

Signed: **Dr. G. Venkatesh** (Director – School of Technology) and **T. Bandyopadhyay** (Director General), DAU, Gandhinagar.
