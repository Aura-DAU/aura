# Hostel Allotment Policy — Halls of Residence

### Overview

DA-IICT / DAU provides on-campus residential facilities (Halls of Residence) for students. This document outlines room allotment rules, conduct requirements, and fees.

### Main Content

### Room Allotment Rules

| Rule | Details |
|---|---|
| Allotment Authority | Wardens (with Dean of Students oversight) |
| Eligibility | Registered DAU students only |
| Duration (UG students) | Typically allotted for 4 years |
| Room Change Requests | Generally not entertained, especially in Year 1 |
| Vacating Deadline | Within 5 days of ceasing to be a student |

### Mandatory Residence

- **BTech students are required to reside on campus** (hostel accommodation is mandatory).
- Students from outside Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar must provide **contact details of at least 2 guardians** preferably local to those districts.
- The Dean, Wardens, and Hostel Supervisors reserve the right to **re-allot or withdraw accommodation** at any time.

### Hostel Fees

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| Hostel Rent | Paid upon allotment |
| Electricity Charges | Charged separately in addition to base hostel fees |
| Caution Deposit | Collected at time of admission (refundable) |

### Hostel Conduct Rules

- **Strictly prohibited:** Consumption of alcohol/intoxicants, smoking (DAU is a no-smoking campus), gambling.
- **No ragging** — violations result in immediate disciplinary action.
- Maintain peace and tranquility; respect other residents' privacy.
- Noisy music or disturbance is not allowed.
- **Curfew:** No entry/exit during late-night hours (approx. 10:30 PM – 6:00 AM) without specific permission or medical emergency.

### Penalties for Hostel Violations

- Fines
- Community service
- Expulsion from hostel for a semester
- Termination from the institute (for severe violations)

### Important Information

- Once allotted, room changes are generally **not permitted** during the first year.
- If a student takes longer than 4 years to complete their degree, further allotment is **subject to availability**.
- On-campus medical facilities with 24/7 emergency vehicle access are available.

## Contact Information

- **Authority:** Warden / Dean of Students, DAU
- **Office:** Hostel Administration, DAU Campus, Gandhinagar
