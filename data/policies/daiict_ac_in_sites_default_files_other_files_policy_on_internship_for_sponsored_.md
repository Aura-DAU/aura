Hiring Process

e A faculty mentor who serves as the Principal Investigator (Pl) on a sponsored research project
shall determine the eligibility criteria pertaining to the specific research project, and shall
advertise the available internship opportunity on the Institute website.

e The faculty mentor should obtain the approval from Dean Research prior to posting this
advertisement. The main requirement for the approval will be the availability of sufficient fund
under the manpower subhead of the sponsored project to support the Intern’s stipend and
HRA.

e@ The advertisement shall include details regarding required skills and prerequisites, specific
eligibility criteria, and the expected duration of the internship.

e Interested candidates shall apply to the Pl directly. The PI shall select an intern based on the
applications received. A formal round of interviews is not required.

Guidelines

e Duration of the internship is typically few weeks up to a few months. The PI should hire the
research associate as a junior research fellow (JRF) instead of an intern if the duration of the
appointment exceeds six to eight months.

e The intern shall also acknowledge the faculty mentor and the DAU sponsored project program
in any publications or presentations resulting from the research.

e The intellectual property rights, if any, resulting from the internship shall belong to DAU,
unless specifically mentioned otherwise in the sponsored research sanction letter.

e Upon successful completion of the internship, after approval by the faculty mentor and
submission of the internship report to the Dean's R&D Office, the student may receive a
certificate of completion from DAU.
