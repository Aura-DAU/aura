# M.Tech Thesis Template — Structure and Formatting Guide

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT
- **Original file:** `Academic/MTech-thesis-template.DOC` (Microsoft Word 95 format, converted for extraction)
- **Document metadata:** Created 24 March 2004, last saved 24 March 2004, last printed 25 February 2004. Original template appears to derive from a specific past thesis titled *"Tracking Antenna Architectures based on an Integrated Mixer Microstrip Patch Array."*
- **Note:** ⚠️ This is a **formatting template**, not a policy document — it provides the structural skeleton a student fills in, not normative rules. Given its 2004 origin (predating essentially every other document in this audit), formatting conventions may not reflect current Institute requirements — see extraction notes.

## Template Structure

**Title Page:**
- Title of the Thesis
- by [Student's Name]
- "A Thesis Submitted in Partial Fulfilment of the Requirements for the Degree of Master of Technology in Information and Communication Technology to Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology"
- Month, Year

**Declaration** (signed by student): Certifies that (i) the thesis comprises the student's original work toward the M.Tech (ICT) degree and has not been submitted elsewhere for a degree, and (ii) due acknowledgement has been made in the text to all other material used.

**Certificate** (signed by Thesis Supervisor(s)): Certifies that the thesis work titled `<Thesis title>` was carried out by `<Name of Student>` (`<ID no.>`) for the M.Tech (ICT) degree at the Institute under the supervisor's/supervisors' supervision.

**Acknowledgements:** Student expresses gratitude to supervisor, colleagues, and others — should not exceed one page.

**Contents (Table of Contents structure):**
- Abstract
- List of Principal Symbols and Acronyms
- List of Tables
- List of Figures
- Chapter 1: Introduction (1.1 General, 1.2 Organisation of Thesis)
- Chapter 2 onward: `<Title of Chapter>`, sub-numbered sections
- Final chapter: Discussions and Conclusion
- References
- Appendices: `<Title of Appendix I>`, etc.

**Abstract:** Describes the main objective of the work — should not exceed one page.

**List of Principal Symbols and Acronyms:** Sample entries given (λ Wavelength, μ Permeability of a Medium, θ Angle, c Velocity of light, C Capacitance, E Electric field, h height, H Magnetic field, I Current, k Boltzmann's constant, k₀ Propagation constant in free space, Y Admittance, Z Impedance, DC Direct Current, GaAs Gallium Arsenide) — illustrative only, to be replaced with the student's own thesis-specific symbols/acronyms; other minor symbols are defined at first occurrence in the text.

**Chapter 1 — Introduction:**
- 1.1 General: general introduction of the subject, with citation example `[1]`.
- 1.2 Organisation of the Thesis: chapter-by-chapter outline of the thesis's scope.

**Middle Chapters:** Placeholder structure only (`<Title of Chapter>`, sub-sections).

**Final Chapter — Discussions and Conclusion:** Discussion of Results; Conclusion (may include suggestions for further work along the thesis's line of research).

**References:** Template instructs: *"Please follow the exact format for references as given in: IEEE Computer Society Style Guide"* — with a URL to computer.org's author style guide (URL is stale/likely dead given the 2004 source; not independently verified here per this audit's no-network-access constraint).

**Appendix:** Placeholder section for supplementary material.
