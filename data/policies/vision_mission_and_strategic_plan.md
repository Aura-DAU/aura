# Vision, Mission and Strategic Plan (DAIICT, Aug 2022)

## Vision

To be a globally recognized institution offering an innovative ICT program, outstanding faculty, a culture of innovation, responsive administration, and a vibrant, collaborative campus that adapts to the evolving ICT landscape.

## Background

Founded in 2000 as an ICT-focused university. This plan lays out DAIICT's transition from an ICT-centric institute toward a **multidisciplinary university**, targeting growth from ~1700 students at the time of writing to **4,000 in 5 years and 8,000 in 10 years**.

## Key Strategic Areas

**1. Academics** — Move toward a multidisciplinary university; introduce Honours/Minors specializations (ML/AI, robotics, UAV engineering, biomedical engineering, quantum computing, etc.); strengthen international partnerships and student/faculty exchange.

**2. Research** — Formalize existing informal research clusters into 5 proposed Schools: Computer Science & IT; Natural Sciences & Mathematics; Electronics & Communication Engineering; Humanities, Social Sciences & Design; Computational & Data Science. Named research verticals include ICT4D, ICT & Public Policy, Design for Inclusive Development, ICT & Health Science, Quantum Computing, Deep Space Missions & Networks, "Fiber in the Sky" satellite networks, and Digital Agriculture/Remote Sensing. Lists 23 active sponsored-research projects spanning NLP, speech processing, mmWave/SATCOM, IoT, and more.

**3. Innovation & Entrepreneurship** — Proposes a "Startup Track" extended BTech option (up to 2 years for Innovation phase + up to 2 more for Entrepreneurship phase, min. GPA 7.0, culminating in "Certificate of Innovation"/"Certificate of Entrepreneurship"), plus a Deferred Placement Service letting entrepreneurship-track graduates access placement services for up to 2 years post-graduation if their venture doesn't work out. Builds on DCEI (DAIICT's Centre for Entrepreneurship & Innovation, est. 2011).

**4. Outreach** — Mandatory rural internship for all UG students; the Anchor Institute Program (AIP, started 1 June 2018) trains faculty/students/professionals across Gujarat (18 programs, 180 faculty trained in year one).

**5. Faculty Growth** — Target: recruit 10-15 outstanding faculty/year plus postdoctoral fellows; build a diverse, internationally networked faculty base.

**6. Infrastructure** — Plans (2021-26) for new academic buildings, hostels, upgraded labs, and a Resource Centre extension, with a total proposed investment of ~₹190 crore across the period. Target: **student strength of 3000+ by 2028**, faculty:student ratio of 1:25.

**7. Digital Campus** — A goal to unify digital library, e-learning, and e-commerce/admin systems into a seamless "Digital Campus," with attention to data protection training and digital literacy for staff and students.

**8. Governance** — Restructure from a flat faculty organization into Schools/Centres, eventually adopting an IIT-style administrative structure (dedicated offices for faculty affairs, planning/resource generation, alumni relations) as the institute grows.

**9. Campus Life & Student Welfare** — Describes DAU's 25+ student clubs/committees (IEEE branch, Programming Club, cultural clubs, Khelaiya, cubing/quizzing/debate/research/business clubs, Sambhav) and annual fests: **Synapse** (cultural), **Concours** (sports), **i.Fest** (technical).
