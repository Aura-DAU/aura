# Certificate of Academic Accomplishment — Eligibility and Procedure

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Certificate of Academic Accomplishment.pdf`
- **Dated:** 12 November 2009 (approved by the Academic Council's 14th Meeting, 5 September 2009)

## Introduction

The Academic Council decided that B.Tech program students who are unable to complete degree requirements within the maximum stipulated period should be issued a **"Certificate of Academic Accomplishment"** and a transcript of grades. The certificate indicates the number of regular semesters (Autumn/Winter only) registered/completed by the student, and the incomplete status of degree requirements within the stipulated period. A corresponding clause was added to Section 9 of the Academic Requirements for the B.Tech Program *(matches §9.3 "Certificate of Academic Accomplishment," referenced across all the B.Tech Academic Requirements documents already processed in this audit)*.

## Eligibility

The student must have been a full-time student for a **minimum of seven regular semesters** (Autumn or Winter only) in the program, registered for at least **3 courses / 9 credits** in each of those semesters.

- The student must have registered for **all core/foundation courses** of the curriculum applicable to their batch, studied them through to the end, and appeared in the end-semester exam for each — though a passing grade in all of them is **not** required.
- The compulsory-registration requirement does **not** cover internships or the B.Tech Project.
- Maximum duration for completing these requirements is the same as for the B.Tech program itself.
- A student whose admission was cancelled on **disciplinary grounds** is **not eligible**.

## Procedure

1. The student must **apply** for the Certificate.
2. The Registrar's Office checks eligibility, in consultation with the Undergraduate Committee and, if necessary, the Dean (Academic Programs).
3. If eligible, the Certificate is issued on payment of a suitable fee.
4. The certificate format explicitly states whether or not the student obtained a passing grade in all core/foundation courses.
5. A transcript of grades is also provided on request and payment of a suitable fee — explicitly stating that the student has not completed the degree requirements.
