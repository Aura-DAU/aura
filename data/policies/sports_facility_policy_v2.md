# Sports Facility Usage Policy

## Overview

DAU's Sports and Cultural Complex provides a wide range of indoor and outdoor sports facilities available to all registered students. This document outlines available facilities and usage rules.

## Main Content

### Available Facilities

#### Outdoor Facilities
| Facility | Details |
|---|---|
| Cricket / Football Ground | With walking track |
| Basketball Courts | Multiple courts |
| Volleyball Courts | Available |

#### Indoor Facilities
| Facility | Details |
|---|---|
| Badminton Courts | Available |
| Table Tennis | Multiple tables |
| Gymnasium | Full gym equipment |
| Yoga Hall | Available |
| Indoor Games | Carrom, chess, etc. |

### Usage Rules

- Facilities are intended **primarily for sporting use**.
- **Proper sports attire and shoes are mandatory** at all times in sports areas.
- Students may be required to **book facilities in advance** (gymnasium is typically open access).
- Students must **present their Student ID** upon request.
- Maintain cleanliness and respect all equipment.
- Practice good sportsmanship at all times.
- Any damage to institute sports property is the responsibility of the student.
- **No food or beverages** (other than water) inside courts or the gymnasium.

### Booking Process

- Most facilities can be booked through the Sports Committee (SBG).
- Check the institute notice board or SBG portal for booking schedules.
- Facility bookings for tournaments or inter-college events require prior administrative approval.

## Important Information

- Facilities are available to **registered DAU students** only.
- Faculty and staff may use facilities during designated hours.
- Equipment borrowing (rackets, balls, etc.) may be available from the Sports Office.
- Inter-college events require SOP for Event Organisation compliance.

## Contact Information

- **Authority:** Sports Officer / Sports Committee (SBG)
- **Office:** Student Affairs / Sports Complex, DAU Campus, Gandhinagar
