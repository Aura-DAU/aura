# Refund and Withdrawal Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the fee refund rules for students who withdraw from courses or from the program at DAU, based on UGC guidelines and DAU's admissions policies.

## Main Content

### Caution Deposit

| Item | Policy |
|---|---|
| Caution Deposit (₹25,000) | **Refundable** at the end of the program |
| Condition for refund | No outstanding dues, no damage to institute property |

### Fee Refund on Withdrawal (UGC Guidelines)

DAU follows UGC refund policy guidelines for students who withdraw after admission:

| Withdrawal Timing | Refund Amount |
|---|---|
| Before commencement of classes | Full refund (deducting processing fee) |
| Within 15 days after commencement | 80% refund of tuition fee |
| 16–30 days after commencement | 50% refund of tuition fee |
| 31–90 days after commencement | 25% refund of tuition fee |
| After 90 days | No refund |

### Course Withdrawal (Within Semester)

- Students may drop courses within the **add/drop period** (first 2 weeks of semester) without academic penalty.
- After the add/drop deadline, course withdrawal may result in a "W" grade.
- Fee adjustments for dropped courses (within add/drop period) are processed by the Finance Office.

### Program Withdrawal

- Students withdrawing from the program must submit a formal application to the Registrar's Office.
- Caution deposit refund is processed after no-dues clearance from all departments.
- Hostel fees, mess fees, and other charges are refunded on a pro-rata basis depending on timing.

## Important Information

- Refund processing may take **4–6 weeks** after application.
- No-dues clearance from Library, Hostel, and Finance is required for full refund.
- For mid-semester medical withdrawal, consult the Academic Office for special provisions.
