# Policy Document on Environment and Energy Usage

Aims to manage DAIICT's energy usage systematically to minimize environmental impact, including exploring renewable resources (Solar Power) as substitutes for conventional energy. Binding on all institute components/users and activities.

## Policies

- Reduce local air-pollution emissions using environment-friendly vehicles (E-Loaders, bicycles) and pedestrian-friendly roads.
- Install a **Solar Power Plant** for alternate energy generation.
- Phase-wise replacement of old bulbs with energy-efficient **LED bulbs**, aiming for full-campus coverage.
- Develop systematic waste management for horticulture and liquid waste.
- Develop **recharge wells** in phases for rainwater harvesting.
- Tree plantation drives.
- Continuously improve energy consumption via energy-saving equipment, sensor-based water supply, timer-based area lighting, etc.
- Ensure resource availability to meet these objectives.
- Undertake green initiatives and run programs to save energy and protect the environment.
- Provide information/training on energy-saving measures.
- Count **CO2 emissions** from institute transportation/vehicles.

This policy is communicated to students/employees via internal channels, and objectives/targets are reviewed regularly by the concerned department.
