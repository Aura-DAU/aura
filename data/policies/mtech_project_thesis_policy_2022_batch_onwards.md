# M.Tech Project/Thesis Policy

*(For the M.Tech 2022 batch onwards)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Rules-Guidelines/Project-Thesis Guidelines/MTech_Project_Thesis_Policy_updated 10-May-2023.pdf`
- **Updated:** May 1, 2023

## Overview

M.Tech in Information and Communication Technology is a full-time, two-year (four-semester) program. As part of graduation requirements, a student undertakes multiple projects starting from semester 2. In the curriculum effective from the 2022 batch:

- **Minor Project-I** — Semester 2
- **Major Project-I** — Summer Semester
- **Major Project-I (continuation)** — Semester 3
- **Major Project-II or Industrial Internship** — Semester 4

**Thesis:** A student may complete Major Project-II on-campus (as a continuation of Major Project-I) or undertake an industry/R&D internship. On-campus Major Project-II successfully completed earns a thesis certificate.

## Terms and Conditions for Minor and Major Project

- Before semester 2 begins, the M.Tech Coordinator arranges a presentation with the institute's research groups to share their research activities with students.
- Students identify a faculty mentor in their area of interest.
- Work may be done individually or in a group (max 2 students), under faculty supervision. A faculty member is expected to supervise **no more than 2 M.Tech students as Primary supervisor** and **no more than 2 students as Co-supervisor**.
- Students should undertake a research problem of interest, identify research challenges, and develop techniques to address it — sufficient foundational coursework knowledge in the relevant specialization area is advisable.
- The M.Tech Coordinator communicates schedule, evaluation committee, and grade-related matters to students/faculty, with help from Specialization Coordinators as needed.
- The M.Tech Coordinator forms evaluation committees (in consultation with the PG Convenor) for minor/major projects — comprising the Supervisor(s), a domain expert, and a member from the student's specialization area.
- Each project must be presented before the evaluation committee per the announced schedule; the project report must be submitted at least **1 week before** the presentation.
- **Assessment weighting: 50% supervisor(s) grade + 50% evaluation committee members' grade.** The M.Tech Coordinator tabulates the final grade.

## Industrial Internship

A student may opt for an industrial internship via the Placement Office, decided before semester 4 begins.

1. Should be technical, combining development and research flavor.
2. Student identifies an on-campus faculty mentor (in addition to the off-campus mentor), who monitors performance periodically and serves as the on-campus contact point.
3. All interning students must present before an evaluation committee per the announced schedule.
4. **Assessment weighting: 50% on-campus supervisor + 50% off-campus supervisor.**
5. Self-arranged internships (student's own arrangement) must be at a research establishment or a reputed organization; same evaluation/assessment criteria apply.

## Expectations and Deliverables of Minor/Major Project

- Technical problem with a research-and-development blend in mind.
- Substantial literature study in the area of exploration.
- Devise/develop a novel methodology.
- Analyze/experiment/simulate results.
- Summarize project outcomes as a technical report.
