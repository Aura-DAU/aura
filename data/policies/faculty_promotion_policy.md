# Faculty Promotion Policy

## Overview

Guidelines for the Promotion of Faculty at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), effective from January 1, 2025. Covers eligibility criteria for promotion from Assistant Professor to Associate Professor, and from Associate Professor to Professor, along with the application and selection process.

## Main Content

### 1. Promotion from Assistant Professor to Associate Professor

**Eligibility Criteria**

The candidate must fulfil one of the following criteria (A, B, or C) to participate in the selection process:

**A.** Completed at least four years of post-PhD service in academics, R&D organization, or industry, of which three years should be as an Assistant Professor at DAU; **and** has at least four publications in peer-reviewed journals/conferences in Q1, Q2/A*, A, B category.[^1] In lieu of journal/conference publications, publication of books/monographs[^2] by reputed international publishers will be considered.

**B.** Served at least eight years as Assistant Professor at DAU; **and** has published at least two articles in peer-reviewed journals/conferences in Q1, Q2/A*, A, B category, or a book/monograph as defined above.

**C.** Served at least ten years as Assistant Professor at DAU and contributed significantly in teaching/services.

> **Note:** Apart from teaching and service to academic administration, external funding and PhD student supervision will be given due consideration as research output during the selection process. Long leaves from the institute during service will not be considered as part of service to the institute.

[^1]: Q1, Q2 journal categories are as per Scimago journal rank; conference categories A*, A, and B ranks are as per core.edu or Aminer rankings, or the DAU-approved list.
[^2]: A book/monograph published by a reputed international publisher as an author will be considered equivalent to two journal/conference publications in the Q1, Q2/A*, A, B category.

### 2. Promotion from Associate Professor to Professor

**Eligibility Criteria**

The candidate must fulfil one of the following criteria (A or B):

**A.** Completed at least eight years of post-PhD service in academics, R&D organization, or industry, of which four years should be as an Associate Professor at DAU; **and** has at least four publications in peer-reviewed journals/conferences in Q1, Q2/A*, A, B category during their tenure as Associate Professor (books/monographs by reputed international publishers may substitute, as defined above); **and either**
- has worked as principal investigator in at least one externally funded project with JRF support during their tenure as Associate Professor, **or**
- has supervised (as primary advisor) at least one Ph.D. student who graduated during their tenure as Associate Professor.

**B.** Served at least fifteen years as an Associate Professor at DAU and contributed significantly in teaching/services.

> **Note:** Apart from teaching and service to academic administration, external funding and PhD student supervision will be given due consideration as research output during the selection process. Long leaves from the institute during service will not be considered as part of service to the institute.

### Application Process

Applications are invited from faculty members who satisfy the eligibility criteria for promotion to Associate Professor/Professor. Eligibility to apply does not mean automatic promotion. The faculty promotion process typically takes place every two years and will be announced by the administration.

### Selection Process

1. The Faculty Development and Evaluation Committee (FDEC) reviews all applications submitted according to the eligibility criteria above.
2. Candidates recommended by the FDEC are invited to give a presentation — either on research or on innovation in teaching pedagogy, as applicable — and engage with a selection panel. This panel, formed by the Director, includes the Director, the Dean (Faculty), two external subject matter experts, and two members of the FDEC.
3. The panel engages with candidates and assesses their contributions to teaching, research, and service in academic administration. The Director acts on the panel's final recommendation, which is then presented to the Board of Governors for approval.
