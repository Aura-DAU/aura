# Data Retention Policy for Student Records

## Overview

DAU collects and retains student data for academic, administrative, and alumni relations purposes. This document outlines how long data is retained and how it is protected.

## Main Content

### Types of Student Data Collected

| Data Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Personal Information (name, address, email, phone) | Communication, official records |
| Academic Records (grades, attendance, transcripts) | Academic administration |
| Financial Records (fee receipts, scholarship data) | Finance and compliance |
| Disciplinary Records | Institutional compliance |
| Alumni Data | Alumni relations and fundraising |

### Retention Periods

| Data Category | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Academic records (transcripts, degrees) | **Permanent / indefinite** |
| Financial records | Minimum 7 years (as per legal requirements) |
| Disciplinary records | Duration of enrollment + 5 years |
| Alumni personal data | As long as alumni consent is valid |
| Admissions data (unsuccessful applications) | 2 years |

### Data Storage and Security

- Student data is stored on **local institute computers and servers** — not on cloud servers.
- Access is restricted to **authorized personnel** only.
- Data is **not shared with external parties** for marketing or sales purposes.
- The institute's E-Governance Policy governs digital record keeping.

### Student Rights

- Students may request access to their personal data by contacting the Administration Office.
- Alumni can **opt out of communications** by emailing alumni_office[at]daiict[dot]ac[dot]in or clicking "unsubscribe" in communications.
- For data correction requests, contact the Academic or Administration Office.

## Important Information

- Official academic transcripts are issued by the Registrar's Office.
- Students can request copies of their academic records at any time (fee may apply).
- Data retention practices comply with applicable Indian laws and UGC guidelines.
