# Library (Resource Centre) Usage Policy

## Overview

The DA-IICT / DAU Resource Centre (RC) provides library and digital resource services to students, faculty, and staff. This document outlines borrowing privileges, rules, and late fee policies.

## Main Content

### Borrowing Privileges

| Category | No. of Books | Loan Period |
|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate Students | 6 books | 14 days |
| Postgraduate Students | 8 items | 14 days |
| PhD Students | 12 books | One semester |
| Faculty | 30 books | One semester |
| Staff | 6 books | 30 days |

### Special Loan Categories

| Category | Loan Period |
|---|---|
| Short Loan Collection (current semester books) | 3 days (faculty and students) |
| Reference Books and Journals | Inside RC only |
| Bound Volumes / AV Materials | 5 days |

### Borrowing Rules

- A valid **Institute Smart ID Card** is mandatory for borrowing — non-transferable.
- Always inspect books for damage before borrowing; you are responsible for condition.
- Latest journal/magazine issues and newspapers are **not issued for home use**.
- Faculty and staff on long leave must return all borrowed materials before leaving.
- Borrowing privileges may be suspended for misuse or misbehavior.

### Late Fees

| Type | Late Fee |
|---|---|
| General Books — first 7 days overdue | ₹2.00 per day |
| General Books — from 8th day onwards | ₹5.00 per day |
| Short Loan Books — first 7 days overdue | ₹5.00 per day |
| Short Loan Books — from 8th day onwards | ₹10.00 per day |

## Important Information

- Students **cannot borrow overnight** from the Short Loan collection — it is a 3-day loan.
- Reference materials must be used **within the Resource Centre premises only**.
- Damaged or mutilated books may need to be replaced by the borrower.
- The RC is open during institute working hours; check the notice board for holiday hours.

## Contact Information

- **Location:** Resource Centre, DAU Campus, Gandhinagar
- **Contact:** Resource Centre Circulation Desk
