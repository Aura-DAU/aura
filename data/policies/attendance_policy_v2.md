# Attendance Policy — BTech and PG Students

## Overview

DA-IICT / DAU requires a minimum attendance level in each course for students to remain eligible for end-semester examinations. Non-compliance can result in academic penalties.

## Main Content

### Minimum Attendance Requirement

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum Attendance Required | **80% in each course** |
| Consequence of Non-Compliance | "F" grade or "DX" grade (course repeat required) |

### Key Rules

- **Per-course basis:** Attendance is tracked individually for each course, not as an overall average.
- **Instructor discretion:** Some instructors may set stricter attendance requirements or include attendance as part of the grade. This is communicated at the start of each semester.
- **DX Grade:** Awarded when attendance falls below the threshold; requires the student to repeat the course in a future semester.
- **F Grade:** Failure due to non-completion of course requirements including attendance.

### Degree Credit Requirements

| Program | Minimum Credits to Graduate |
|---|---|
| BTech (ICT) | 129 credits (approx.) |
| BTech Honours | Additional minor credits required |

### Registration Requirements

- Students must register for all courses at the start of each semester.
- Students with backlogs are guided by the UG Committee on course registration.
- Eligibility for registration requires no outstanding unpaid fees.

## Important Information

- Attendance records are maintained by each course instructor.
- Students are advised to track their own attendance regularly.
- Medical absences: Consult the Academic Office for medical leave provisions.
- Always refer to the **Academic Regulations document** from the Registrar's office for the most current rules applicable to your batch.
