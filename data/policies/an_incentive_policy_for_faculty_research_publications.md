# An Incentive Policy for Faculty Research Publications

## Objective

To recognize and incentivize faculty-led original research resulting in publication at a reputed journal or leading conference.

## Award

A financial award to faculty members who publish in top-quality journals and conferences.

## Criteria

1. All full-time DAU faculty members are eligible.
2. Awarded per qualifying journal/conference article published in a calendar year (January–December).
3. The financial award (P_max) per publication depends on category:

| Category | Qualifying venues | Max award per publication |
|---|---|---|
| **I** (higher tier) | Q1 journals per Scimago Journal Ranker (excluding predatory/payment-based Open Access journals), **or** A*/A conferences per Core.edu / Aminer rankings, **or** full-length books/monographs (excluding edited books/chapters) at a reputed *international* publishing house with international circulation | **[AMOUNT UNCLEAR — OCR illegible, likely "Rs. X lakh"]** |
| **II** (lower tier) | Q2 journals per Scimago (same Open Access exclusion), **or** B-category conferences per Core.edu / Aminer, **or** conferences not in Core/Aminer but approved by the Dean Research Office ("DAU Approved Conferences," see Annexure A below), **or** full-length books/monographs at a reputed domestic or international publisher with domestic circulation | **[AMOUNT UNCLEAR — OCR shows "Bsr 02> lakhs", likely a fraction of a lakh]** |

## Notes / Procedure

- Only conference papers in the **main technical track** qualify (workshops, tutorials, invited talks excluded).
- Only **research articles** qualify for journals — book reviews, editorials, etc. do not.
- Q1/Q2 Open Access papers qualify **only if the Institute did not pay any article processing/publishing charges (APCs).**
- Faculty affiliation must be listed as **DAU/DA-IICT** in the publication.
- Faculty must be **full-time DAU-affiliated at the time of initial submission**. Faculty on lien or extraordinary leave receive their award upon rejoining.
- Publication date is determined by the **DOI** date.
- **Multiple DAU co-authors:** if N > 1 DAU faculty co-author a paper, each receives an equal share — P_max / N.
- **Annual cap: ₹4 lakh per faculty member per calendar year.** If a faculty member's qualifying awards exceed this, the excess is credited to their **CPDA** instead.

### Category II conference-list process

For conferences not already covered (i.e. outside Q2/B-ranked lists), each area coordinator — in consultation with faculty in that area — proposes conferences for inclusion in Category II, submitting the list to the Dean of Research by **end of September** each year. Dean Research has this list reviewed/approved by a panel of external experts (nominated by the area coordinators) before year-end.

### Claim process

1. Faculty send publication details to the Dean of Research to claim the incentive.
2. Dean's Research Office (with the Resource Centre) verifies the claim by confirming the DOI, then obtains the **Director's approval**.
3. The recipient list is shared with the Communications Office and displayed on the Institute's website.

**Date: 24 December 2025.** Signed: **Dr. G. Venkatesh** (Director, School of Technology) and **T. Bandyopadhyay** (Director General), DAU, Gandhinagar.
