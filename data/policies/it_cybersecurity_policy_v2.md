# ICT and Cybersecurity Policy for Students

### Overview

DAU's ICT Policy governs the acceptable use of computing, network, and internet resources on campus. It applies to all students, faculty, staff, and authorized guests.

### Main Content

### Acceptable Use

- Computing and network facilities are provided **primarily for academic and official purposes**.
- **Personal use is permitted** provided it does not violate Indian law, DAU policies, or the IT Act, and does not interfere with academic duties.
- Students **may use personal laptops** on the campus network subject to identity-based internet login.

### Network Access & Authentication

- The institute uses **identity-based internet login** for all network access.
- NAT (Network Address Translation) syslog records are maintained as per regulatory requirements.
- Infrastructure includes **Sophos UTM Firewall** for gateway-level anti-virus/anti-spam and web filtering.

### Prohibitions (Cybersecurity Rules)

| Prohibited Activity | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Unauthorized access to systems or networks | Disciplinary action by DAC |
| Guessing passwords or bypassing security | Disciplinary action / probation |
| Sharing passwords or accounts | Disciplinary action |
| Bypassing firewalls or security systems | Disciplinary action |
| Sending offensive or harassing emails | Disciplinary action |
| Misusing computer/network resources | Disciplinary action |
| Downloading or distributing pirated content | Disciplinary action / legal action |

### Disciplinary Authority

- Violations are referred to the **Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC)**.
- Student Code of Conduct explicitly lists "violation of computing practices of DA-IICT" as an act of indiscipline.
- Consequences range from warnings and probation to suspension or expulsion.

### Policy Review

- The ICT Policy is reviewed **periodically (typically annually)** by the Director, incorporating feedback from stakeholders and updates based on new technologies.

### Important Information

- **Password sharing is strictly prohibited.**
- Attempting to access systems without authorization is a serious offense.
- The institute monitors network usage for security compliance.
- For network issues or policy queries, contact the IT Department.
