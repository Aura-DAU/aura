# Whistle Blowers Policy

Memorandum dated 31 December 2024, addressed to all employees. Formulated in compliance with the Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014.

## Purpose

Lets any employee (past or current) who observes illegal, immoral, or dishonest conduct — and is unsatisfied with their immediate superior's response — report it to the university's top authorities, without fear of retaliation.

## What Can Be Reported

General or academic malpractice, breaches of law/regulation (e.g. health & safety), criminal offences (fraud, bribery), financial/procedural irregularity, manipulation of university data, breach of university policy, violation of confidential/proprietary information, or IP rights violations.

**Not for:** personal grievances or malicious/unfounded allegations against colleagues — the regular grievance procedure covers those instead.

## How to Report

- Report **in writing/email to the Director General, DAU**, along with your identity, as soon as practicable.
- **Anonymous reports are not investigated further.**
- Must be in good faith, backed by sufficient supporting documentation, genuine and significant (not trivial or already public) — and should be a last resort, not a first step.
- Clearly mark the complaint as filed under the Whistleblower Policy.
- No retaliatory or discriminatory action is permitted against a genuine whistleblower; retaliation triggers disciplinary action against whoever retaliates.

## Confidentiality

The university encourages self-identification to enable investigation, but will protect confidentiality if requested, revealing identity only to those investigating. (Anonymous reports are harder to investigate properly, per the above.)

## Investigation

The university determines the investigation's scope/manner. The compliance office receiving a report investigates and reports outcomes to the Director General's Office, which forwards corrective-action reports to the University President as needed.

## Records

Retained confidentially for **5 years** (or per the Record Retention and Disposal Policy in effect), disclosed only under lawful court order or written government request.
