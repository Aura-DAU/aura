# DAU Student Research Excellence Award — Policy & Application Process

## Objective

To recognize and celebrate student-led original research resulting in publication at a reputed journal or leading conference.

## Award

A certificate and plaque/medal, presented by the Director General annually (the day before each year's convocation ceremony) to students who published in high-quality journals/conferences.

## Eligibility Criteria

All UG, PG, and PhD scholars of DAU — either currently enrolled or graduated within one year prior to the upcoming award ceremony — are eligible, provided they have a paper published in a journal/conference classified under Category I or II:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **I** | Q1 journals per Scimago ranking, or A*/A ranked conferences per Core.edu or Aminer |
| **II** | Q2 journals per Scimago ranking, or B-ranked conferences per Core.edu or Aminer, or one of the DAU Approved conferences (Annexure A) |

## Notes

- The student's primary affiliation on the published paper must be **DA-IICT or DAU**.
- For an award at the Pre-Convocation ceremony in year Y, the publication date (per DOI) must fall between **January 1 and December 31 of year Y-1**.
- Only conference papers in the **main technical track** qualify (workshops, tutorials, invited talks excluded).

## Application Process

- Once a paper is published with a listed DOI, the student applies via a **Google Form**.
- **Deadline: 3rd January of Year Y**, to be considered for the ceremony held in Year Y. Students are encouraged to submit as soon as their paper is published, rather than waiting.
- A student with multiple eligible publications receives **a single award**, plus an additional certificate for each paper beyond the first.
- Selected students are notified by email **by 5th January of Year Y** (earlier applicants may hear back within days of submitting). The awardee list is published on the DAU website. Students unable to attend the ceremony can contact the Research Officer to collect the award later.

**Version 1.0, effective 28 May 2025.** Signed: **T. Bandyopadhyay**, Director General, DAU, Gandhinagar.
