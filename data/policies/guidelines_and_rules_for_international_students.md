# Guidelines and Rules Concerning International Students at DA-IICT

## Source

- **Institution:** Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT)
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Rules-Guidelines/International Students_Rules and Guidelines.pdf`

## Constitution of the International Students Cell (ISC)

Matters relating to international students fall under the International Students Cell (ISC), as required by the University Grants Commission and other government authorities. Membership (all ex officio, coterminous with holding the underlying position):
- International Students Adviser (ISA) — Convener
- Dean (Academic Programs) — Member
- Executive Registrar — Member
- Convener (Admissions Committee) — Member

## 1. Role and Responsibilities of the ISC

1.1 Provides information/counseling on programs of study, structure, eligibility, administrative procedures, accommodation, health insurance, and related matters, per DA-IICT regulations.

1.2 Handles all international-student admission, eligibility, and course-registration processes; must be informed of any academic or disciplinary proceedings involving an international student.

1.3 Links international students to Government of India bodies (e.g., ICCR) for scholarships, exchange, collaborations, etc.

1.4 Administers DA-IICT's MoUs with foreign universities/international higher-education bodies.

1.5 Monitors grade-transfer issues per Institute norms, reciprocal with signed MoU terms.

1.6 Formulates and administers leave rules/procedures for international students.

## 2. Definition of an International Student

**2.1 International Students:** Holders of foreign passports, including people of Indian origin who acquired foreign nationality.

**2.2 Non-Resident Indians (NRI):** Treated as international students for B.Tech admission purposes only; applicable definitions/procedures/fees are published on the Institute website for the relevant admission year. For all other purposes, treated on par with Indian national students.

**2.3 Sub-categories:**
- **International Degree Students:** Enrolled full-time toward a DA-IICT degree.
- **International Exchange Students:** Not enrolled toward a DA-IICT degree — pursuing a degree at another university, present at DA-IICT under an MoU with their home institution, governed by that MoU's terms.

## 3. Admission and Continuation Process

### A. For International Degree Students

3.1 Application in the designated format; program-wise eligibility, admissions procedure, documents, selection process, and fees are posted on the Institute website in a timely manner, formulated by ISC in consultation with the Admissions Committee. ISC ensures compliance with applicable government norms.

3.2 Approved applicants receive a provisional admission letter for the student visa.

3.3 Securing the student visa (endorsed to the Institute) and all other government clearances is the candidate's responsibility; ISC provides guidance.

3.4 Candidates must report by the designated date to complete admission formalities (registration, fees, documents).

3.5 Bona fide certificates for visa extension are issued as needed for the program duration.

### B. For International Exchange Students

3.6 Admitted only under an existing DA-IICT–home-institution MoU; selection, stay duration, fees, and academic work follow the MoU's guidelines.

3.7 Issued a letter detailing the exchange studentship, used to obtain a student visa endorsed to DA-IICT.

3.8 Securing the visa and other government clearances is the student's responsibility; ISC provides guidance.

3.9 Must report by the designated date to complete exchange-studentship formalities (registration, fees, documents).

## 4. Post-Arrival Procedures

Every international student meets the ISA on arrival, who informs them of the following:

**4.1 Medical Examination:** Required for all student-visa entrants per Government of India rules.
- 4.1.1 ISC (via the Executive Registrar's office) coordinates/facilitates the exam through Institute facilities if needed; cost borne by the student.
- 4.1.2 Results go to ISC and the student file, after consulting the Institute's Medical Officer.
- 4.1.3 Admission may be denied on health grounds, at DA-IICT's discretion, on the Medical Officer's advice.

**4.2 Health Insurance:** Required — adequate arrangements must be made.
- 4.2.1 Students may arrange their own international medical insurance, informing ISC and submitting documents for their file.
- 4.2.2 Otherwise, students must use the Institute-arranged health insurance; premium borne by the student.

**4.3 Police Registration:** Required with local police within a week of arrival; ISA assists.

**4.4 Hostel Accommodation:**
- 4.4.1 **International Degree Students:** B.Tech admits must reside on campus. Female students are provided hostel accommodation on campus; PG male students are provided accommodation if available. Fees per current rules.
- 4.4.2 **International Exchange Students:** Per MoU terms, generally similar to §4.4.1; fees per the MoU/exchange agreement.

**4.5 Documents to Submit to ISC (for the student file):**
- 4.5.1 Visa (original shown for verification) + notary-attested passport photocopy.
- 4.5.2 Copies of other government-authority documents on international-student status.
- 4.5.3 Degree/pass certificate of qualifying examination(s).
- 4.5.4 All semester-wise mark lists/grade reports for qualifying examination(s).
- 4.5.5 (Exchange students only) Letter from the home institution's concerned authority.
- 4.5.6 Letter from local guardian (if applicable).
- 4.5.7 Emergency contact details.
- 4.5.8 Information on allergies, regular medication needs, or other health issues (students on medication are recommended to carry a one-week supply on their person).

## 5. Facilities Provided to International Students

1. Identity Card with student ID
2. ID for local area network / e-Campus access (International Degree Students)
3. Email access and ID

## 6. Academic Requirements, Degrees, and Credit Transfer

6.1 International Degree Students follow the same academic requirements as students of their program, and are awarded degrees on completion.

6.2 International Exchange Students undertake coursework/project/research per the MoU's broad terms and their specific admission letter; graded per the standard evaluation mechanism for their registered course(s).

6.3 Grade reports are sent to the home institution's authority under the MoU, in DA-IICT's standard format with explanation of terms/symbols. Interpretation of credits/grades is the home institution's responsibility; credit allocation follows DA-IICT norms.

6.4 Instruction medium is English. International students may need to take an English Proficiency Test (EPT); those who fail may need additional English classes or a suitable course.

## 7. Discipline

7.1 International students follow the same disciplinary rules/code of conduct as all students, plus all Government of India guidelines for foreign citizens; must submit an undertaking to this effect.

## 8. Conclusion

1. Doubts on interpretation are resolved by the ISC's opinion, which is final.
2. Points not covered by these rules are decided by DA-IICT authorities (or by mutual agreement for MoU-governed exchange students).
3. All international-student regulations/norms are published on the DA-IICT website and available as a hard-copy document.
4. ISC is the primary point of interface for international students.
