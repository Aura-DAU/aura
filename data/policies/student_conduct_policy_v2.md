# Student Code of Conduct — Campus Behaviour, Dress Code, and Social Media

## Overview

All students at DAU are expected to maintain a high standard of personal conduct, both on and off campus. This document covers dress code, alcohol/substance policy, social media conduct, and general campus behaviour expectations.

## Main Content

### Dress Code

- DAU does not enforce a strict uniform policy.
- Students are expected to dress **modestly and appropriately** for an academic environment.
- **Sports attire and proper sports shoes** are mandatory when using sports facilities.
- Clothing that is offensive, disruptive, or violates the dignity of others is not permitted.

### Alcohol and Substance Use Policy

| Substance | Policy |
|---|---|
| Alcohol | **Strictly prohibited** on campus |
| Smoking / Tobacco | **Strictly prohibited** — DAU is a no-smoking campus |
| Intoxicating substances / drugs | **Strictly prohibited** |
| Gambling | **Strictly prohibited** |

**Consequences:** Violations can lead to severe disciplinary action including fines, community service, hostel expulsion, or termination from the institute.

### Social Media Policy

- Students must not post content that **defames, harasses, or discriminates** against other students, faculty, or staff.
- Sharing **confidential institute information** on social media is prohibited.
- Misuse of social media that violates the Student Code of Conduct is treated as an act of indiscipline.
- Violations are referred to the **Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC)**.
- Students are personally responsible for content posted from their accounts.

### General Campus Conduct

- Ragging in **any form** is strictly prohibited and punishable by law.
- Damage to institute property is the responsibility of the student.
- Students must respect the privacy and personal space of all members of the campus community.
- Peaceful and respectful communication is expected in all academic and social interactions.

### Disciplinary Authority

- The **Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC)** oversees all violations.
- Penalties: warnings, fines, community service, hostel expulsion, academic suspension, or termination.

## Important Information

- All students sign a declaration of adherence to the Code of Conduct at admission.
- Ragging complaints can be filed at the National Anti-Ragging Helpline: **1800-180-5522**.
- Social media posts made even outside campus hours are subject to this policy if they impact campus life.

## Contact Information

- **Authority:** Dean of Students / Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC)
- **Office:** Student Affairs Office, DAU Campus, Gandhinagar
