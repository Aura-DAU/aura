# Academic Requirements for the B.Tech (MnC) Program

*(Mathematics and Computing — Effective from Autumn 2021-22, applicable to students admitted from AY 2021-22 onwards)*

## Source

- **Institution:** Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT), Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic requirements_BTech(MnC)_wef_2021-22.pdf`
- **Dated:** 23/02/2023, DA-IICT, Gandhinagar

## Scope

Rules governing the B.Tech in Mathematics and Computing (MnC) program, a four-year undergraduate program, covering post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

- Registration occurs within the first two days of each semester via SRS. Late registration permitted with Deputy Registrar approval and fee, up to the Academic Calendar deadline. Outstanding dues bar registration.
- No-backlog students register for the full prescribed set; students with backlogs/on probation may get a different set recommended by the UG Committee Convenor.
- **Pre-Requisite Courses** *(specific to this program)*: A course with a **Hard Pre-requisite** requires a minimum grade of DD in the prerequisite; a **Soft Pre-requisite** requires a minimum grade of DE.
- **Withdrawal:** Same rules as the base B.Tech (ICT) program — pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean approval before the late-registration deadline; post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin; one semester at a time; failure to register/withdraw properly results in discontinuation. A student returning from withdrawal registers for the prescribed courses subject to prerequisites. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show as "withdrawn" on the transcript.

## 2. Auditing & PG-Level Courses

- **Audit courses:** From semester 5 onwards, maximum 2 for the whole program, 1 per semester. Requires instructor approval and no timetable clash. Not an overload; excluded from SPI/CPI; graded P (appears on transcript) or F (does not appear).
- **PG-level courses for credit:** Students with CPI ≥ 7.4 may register for available M.Tech-level courses as credit courses from **sixth semester onwards** *(note: one semester later than the base B.Tech (ICT) program's fifth-semester threshold)*, with instructor approval and no timetable clash; counts toward the regular course load.

## 3. Course Load

- **Regular semester:** Maximum 7 courses, maximum 28 credits. Minimum underload: 4 courses / 12 credits (fewer than 4 permitted after 7 completed regular semesters).
- **Summer Semester** (~8 weeks, May–July): Maximum 3 backlog courses. Internship = 2 courses; BTP Part II = 3 courses; BTP Part I = 1 course. No other course combined with an internship. Students past 8 regular semesters may also use Summer Semester for grade improvement (§5.2).

## 4. Course Assessment and Grading

Assessment modes, attendance policy, and the grading scale are identical to the base B.Tech (ICT) program:

AA–DE passes; F fails. Pass/Fail courses use P/F and are excluded from SPI/CPI; F may result from exam malpractice. "I" (Incomplete) rules — missed end-semester exam due to illness/family accident-death, Dean approval, evidence required, converted via make-up exam or defaults to F by the next semester's Academic Calendar deadline — are identical to the base program.

## 5. Repeating a Course

- F grade in a credit course must be repeated (backlog course, subject to §3). A backlog elective can be replaced by another elective of the same category — for this program: **technical/open/science** *(note: this program's category list differs slightly in wording from the base B.Tech (ICT) program)*.
- **Grade Improvement:** CPI < 5.0 allows repeating a DD/DE-graded course in a regular semester only; repeat grade counts toward CPI, original grade stays on transcript but excluded from CPI.

## 6. B.Tech Project

All students must complete the BTP, credits as prescribed in the curriculum from time to time. *(Note: unlike the base B.Tech (ICT) program, this program's core rules document does not mention an Industrial Training Project alternative in this section — see the curriculum section below for Independent Projects, which are specific to MnC.)*

## 7. Performance Indices

**SPI** and **CPI** are defined identically to the base B.Tech (ICT) program (weighted grade-point averages, semester and cumulative respectively, to 2 decimal places).

## 8. Minimum Academic Performance Requirements

- **Academic Probation:** Same thresholds as base program — probation into semester 2 if semester-1 SPI < 4.5; later semesters if previous CPI < 5.0 or previous SPI < 4.5. Dean may set a minimum SPI target for probationary students.
- **Discontinuation for poor performance:** Same assessment cadence (from end of semester 2 onward) as the base program.
- **CPI < 4.0 at end of semester 2 or 4:** ⚠️ **Differs from the base B.Tech (ICT) program** — this document states the student "shall be discontinued from the Institute" (not "repeat a year," as in the base program), though they may register for up to 3 available summer backlog courses (F or DE grade) and **re-enter** the program if they reach CPI 4.0 including summer results. The base program's wording instead frames this as a "repeat a year OR discontinue" choice. This distinction is preserved as written in each respective source document.
- **Program duration:** Minimum 4 years, maximum 6 years (including withdrawn semesters).

## 9. Award of Degree

**9.1 Requirements for the B.Tech (MnC) degree:**

a. Pass all foundation and elective courses prescribed in the curriculum. **Minimum 136 total course credits:** 100 foundation + 36 elective, split as minimum 10 MnC electives + minimum 2 open electives.

b. Minimum 680 grade points from coursework (136 × 5.0; internships and BTP excluded from this count). Shortfalls require additional elective courses (up to category maximums).

c. Must pass all foundation courses (including Pass/Fail courses), all prescribed internships, and **Independent Project-1**.

d. Must obtain a passing grade for the BTP as prescribed in the curriculum.

e. At least 8 registered regular semesters (excluding summer/withdrawn semesters), with all institute dues paid.

f. No pending case of indiscipline.

**9.2 Final CPI and Class:** Computed from the best-CPI combination meeting minimums (≥136 credits, ≥100 foundation credits, elective category minimums); other courses count as extra credits, excluded from final CPI. CPI includes coursework, Independent Projects, and the BTech Project. Subject to change with Director approval if credit structures change. **Distinction:** CPI ≥ 9.0. **First Class:** CPI ≥ 6.5 and < 9.0.

**9.3 Certificate of Academic Accomplishment:** Same provision as base program — available on application per Institute-set criteria for students who don't finish within the maximum period.

## 10. Glossary

Same definitions as the base B.Tech (ICT) program: Backlog Course, Course Credit, Grade Points, Semester, Semester Grade Report, Transcript, UG Committee.

### Elective and Project Details

- **MnC Electives:** Minimum 9 courses / 30 credits; maximum 10 courses.
- **Open Electives:** 2 courses / minimum 6 credits, from areas not covered under MnC electives.
- **Independent Projects (IP):** Credit structure 0-0-6-3, under faculty supervision, only in semesters 6–7, at most 1 IP per semester. Every student must take **at least 1 IP**, at most 2 IPs total. Cannot be combined with any part of BTP. Independent Project-2, if taken, counts as an MnC elective.
- **B.Tech Project:** Part 1 (4 credits) and Part 2 (9 credits) — Part 1 in semester VII and Part 2 in VIII, or both in VIII.
- **Internships:** Rural Internship, 4 credits (typically between semesters III–IV); Industrial/Research Internship, 6 credits (typically Summer Semester after semester VI). *(Note: these credit values — 4 and 6 — are higher than the base B.Tech (ICT) program's 3 and 4 credits respectively.)*
