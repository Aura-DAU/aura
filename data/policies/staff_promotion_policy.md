# Staff Promotion Policy

## Overview

DA-IICT's Staff Promotion Policy (Preamble and Personnel Policy), covering the classification of staff (Academic and Teaching, Other Academic, Non-academic Related, Administrative and Technical), retirement age, and performance appraisal principles. Note: this PDF is a scanned document on the DAU website; the text below was transcribed from the scan and could not be independently verified against machine-readable source text.

## Main Content

### Preamble

Recognizing that human resources are basic to achieving the goal, DA-IICT considers its PEOPLE to be the real asset in all its endeavors to be a world class Institute in its own right. Believing in the concept of flexibility, much needed for an Institute which looks forward to being world-class, these policies and rules have been framed. These are intended to be more directional than detailed, rigid and watertight compartments.

### A New Class of People

- The PEOPLE of DA-IICT are talented, committed, innovative and efficient as professionals, and sensitive to national needs, in particular for industrial and economic development.
- DA-IICT believes that competent and committed faculty are the foremost building block in the development of an academic institution, who enjoy academic freedom and flexibility to create, innovate, and implement new frontiers in teaching and learning, focus on research and publications, and do all that will benefit the student body and society at large. A high quality, committed, and responsive academic-related and other staff provide support for an ideal and conducive environment for achieving excellence in teaching, learning, and research activities.

### Personnel Policy

- The personnel policy of DA-IICT is governed by its goal of building a world class educational institution with the best talent from anywhere in the world. DA-IICT is committed to providing opportunity to its people to achieve excellence.
- DA-IICT does not discriminate on any basis, including caste, creed, religion, nationality, colour, and sex. Merit and performance are the sole criteria in selection and recognition of employees at all levels.
- Discipline and high standards of ethics are an important guiding philosophy at DA-IICT. Personnel at all levels are required to appreciate the importance of high standards of conduct in their personal and professional accomplishments, and should strive to emerge as role models to others and to society at large.

### Classification of Staff

**Academic and Teaching**
- Director; Chair Professors; Professors; Associate Professors; Assistant Professors
- Distinguished Professors; Adjunct Professors; Visiting Professors

**Other Academic**
- Research Associates; Research Assistants; Technical Assistants; such other posts as may be created to assist in academic activities

**Non-academic Related**
- Registrar, Controller of Examination, Jt. Controller of Examination, Dy. Registrar, Asst. Registrar
- Librarian, Dy. Librarian, Asst. Librarian
- Data Processing Staff
- System Manager/System Analyst/System Engineer

**Administrative and Technical**
- Estate Manager/Engineers and other Engineers
- Other staff members, including Secretarial staff, Laboratory and Workshop staff

Persons appointed to the level of Asst. Registrar/Asst. Librarian and above are officers of different levels. Those below the level of Asst. Registrar/Asst. Librarian in terms of salaries are supervisory staff. Non-supervisory staff are those separately declared as such by the Executive Board.

### Age of Retirement

- For Faculty — 62 years
- For employees — 60 years

### Upgradation of Knowledge and Skill

- To continually upgrade knowledge and skill, staff members are encouraged to attend national and international conferences, seminars, and specific training programs. The Institute provides incentives for this purpose as may be decided by the Board of Governors from time to time.
- Staff members who wish to undergo training for more than one month are required to assure that they will serve the Institute for a certain minimum period, as may be decided by the Director depending on the length of the training period. Where the Institute selects/deputes a staff member for training, a similar undertaking is required, in the form of an Indemnity Bond.

### Performance Appraisal

In the process of continuous improvement, performance appraisal of employees is a sine-qua-non. For teaching staff, the Board of Governors/Executive Board prescribes the mechanism for performance appraisal. For all other staff members, performance is evaluated on prescribed formats. Such evaluation reports may be used for various purposes, such as career growth, suitability for specific jobs, and deployment.
