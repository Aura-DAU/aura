# DAU Policy against Sexual Harassment (2026)

DAU maintains a zero-tolerance policy on sexual harassment for all students and employees. The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) is the body that handles complaints, in line with the POSH Act 2013 (India) and the Vishaka guidelines.

## Scope

Covers all DAU students (including those completing programs, on placement, or awaiting admission), all categories of employees (permanent, temporary, contract, visiting, honorary), and defines "workplace" broadly — campus, transport, field trips, conferences, off-campus official activity, and online platforms used for DAU work.

## ICC Composition (per this policy)

At least 10 members, including the Convenor:
- 1 senior faculty member (Convenor, must be female)
- 3 faculty members (1 male, 2 female)
- 2 non-teaching staff members (at least 1 female)
- 2 BTech student representatives (1 male, 1 female)
- 1 PG student representative (female; MTech/MDes/MSc/PhD)
- 1 external member (NGO/legal-expertise background)

Faculty/staff terms: 3 years. Student terms: 1–2 years, selected via written response + interview.

## How to File a Complaint

- File within 3 months of the incident (ICC can extend this in justified cases)
- Submit in writing to the ICC Convenor in person, or email **icc@dau.ac.in**
- Include as much detail as possible: dates, times, witnesses, and any evidence (emails, screenshots, recordings)
- The ICC can also act on its own initiative (suo motu)

## Process Overview

1. Convenor calls an ICC meeting to determine if a prima facie case exists
2. If yes, a copy goes to the Respondent (7 days), who must respond within 10 days (max 15 days total)
3. Either an **informal/conciliation process** (only at the Complainant's written request, no monetary settlements allowed) or a **formal inquiry** (both parties present their case, evidence is examined, sessions are video-recorded where possible)
4. Investigation must complete within 90 days; the report follows within 10 more days
5. Interim protective measures are available during the inquiry (e.g., removing a faculty Respondent from examining/teaching the Complainant, hostel restrictions, suspension)
6. Confidentiality is mandatory for all parties and ICC members throughout

## Penalties (illustrative range, not exhaustive)

- **Students:** warning, counselling, written/public apology, fine, debarring from exams or club/SBG positions, suspension (1–4 semesters), withheld degree, expulsion (up to lifetime ban)
- **Employees:** warning, apology, salary cut (up to 12 months), unpaid suspension (2–6 months), adverse record entry, fine, termination
- **Outsiders/service providers:** warning, permanent campus ban, contract termination

A Respondent can also be placed on "ICC Probation" — one more proven offence triggers automatic expulsion.

## Appeals

Complainant/Respondent can appeal within 30 days of the ICC report to the "Authority" (Director General, or Board of Governors if the complaint is against the DG). The Authority's decision is final.
