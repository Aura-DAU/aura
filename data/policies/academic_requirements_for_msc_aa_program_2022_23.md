# Academic Requirements for the M.Sc (AA) Program

*(Effective from Autumn 2022-23)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar (jointly implemented with **AAU** — Anand Agricultural University — and **IIRS** — Indian Institute of Remote Sensing)
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_M Sc (AA)_wef 2022-23.pdf`
- **Dated:** December 2022

## Scope

Master of Science (Agriculture Analytics) — M.Sc (AA) — is a two-year postgraduate program governed by these rules, covering post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

**1.1 Categories of Registration:** Resident registration only — requires registration for a duly approved course programme and payment of prescribed fees, including outstanding dues.

**1.2 Provisional Registration:** New entrants awaiting qualifying-exam results register provisionally, submitting certificates for verification by the Academic Calendar deadline (~8 weeks from registration); originals returned, self-attested copy retained.

**1.3 Late Registration:** ~1 week window with a late fee for compelling reasons. Dean (Academic Programs), on PGC recommendation, may permit registration beyond that in exceptional cases *(note: unlike other M.Sc programs, this document does not restrict late-exception registration to "thesis credits only")*.

**1.4 Academic Advising:** Faculty adviser appointed by the Dean (Academic Programs) in consultation with the PGC. Students may repeat/substitute courses graded DD, DE, or F, per §2.3.

**1.5 Semester Load:** Prescribed from time to time in the approved program course structure document.

**1.6 Adding/Dropping and Withdrawal:**
- Adding/dropping permitted during the Add/Drop period, with instructor + Convener PGC endorsement, by Academic Calendar deadlines.
- Pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean approval before the late-registration deadline; post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin. **Withdrawal permitted for only one semester during the entire program.** Improper non-registration/withdrawal risks being struck off the rolls.
- Returning students register per the prescribed curriculum, subject to prerequisites. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show as "withdrawn" and count toward maximum program duration.

## 2. Academic Requirements

**2.1 M.Sc (AA) Program:** Total credits required: **at least 62** (per the approved curriculum for the batch). **Maximum permissible duration: 3 years.**

The program is **jointly implemented by DA-IICT, AAU, and IIRS**: Semester I classes at DA-IICT, Semester II at IIRS, Semester III at AAU, with Semester IV as an industry internship or faculty-supervised research project. Credit transfers from AAU and IIRS to DA-IICT are implemented as part of the program.

**2.2 Audit Courses:** Same rule as other M.Sc programs — satisfactory audit performance earns 'P'; unsatisfactory performance leaves no grade/transcript entry.

**2.3 Grades, SPI, and CPI:**

| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| AA | 10 | |
| AB | 9 | |
| BB | 8 | |
| BC | 7 | |
| CC | 6 | |

| DD | 4 | |
| DE | 3 | |
| F | 0 | Fail |
| I | — | Incomplete |
| P* | — | Pass |

\* For Pass/Fail and Audit Courses only.

- (a) "I" converts to a regular grade by the Academic Calendar deadline or auto-converts to F.
- (b) **F in a core/elective course = backlog**, which the student must repeat **or substitute with a relevant MOOC course** (preferably on the SWAYAM platform), as advised by the three institutes' Program Coordinators and approved by DA-IICT. Requires submitting course registration + completion certificate with grades/percentage. Max 1 MOOC per semester, max 2 for the entire program. *(Note: this MOOC-substitution mechanism for a failed course is unique to this program among the M.Sc documents reviewed so far.)*
- (c) **DD or DE** in a core/elective may be substituted with a relevant MOOC course (per clause b) if CPI is below the graduation minimum.
- (d) Repeated/substituted courses: old grade stays on transcript, excluded from CPI.

## 3. Academic Performance Requirement

**3.1 SPI/CPI:** Same weighted-average definitions as other M.Sc programs, to 2 decimal places.

**3.2 Minimum CPI for graduation:**

| Program | CPI for Graduation |
|---|---|
| M.Sc (AA) | **6.0** |

**3.3 Academic Probation and Dismissal:** Same framework as M.Sc (IT)/(DS) — CPI below 6.0 at semester-end triggers probation (written intimation); PGC will prescribe a specific course load and may set a minimum SPI target; continued poor performance leads to a Director recommendation to leave, with a termination letter from the appropriate authority.

## 4. Glossary

Same definitions as other M.Sc programs: Academic Probation, CPI, Grade Points, PGC, Semester (Autumn: July–November; Winter: December/January–April), Semester Credits, Semester Grade Points, SPI.
