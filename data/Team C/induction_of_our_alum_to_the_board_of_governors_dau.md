# Main Content

We are delighted to announce that our alumnus, Pavitar Singh (B.Tech. 2001- 05) has been inducted to the DAU Board of Governors by the President Ms. Tina Anil Ambani. Professor Tathagata Bandyopadhyay, Director General, DAU welcomed Pavitar on behalf of the President, Board of Governors and the DAU community. Sharing his response, Pavitar expressed his pride in the transformation of DA-IICT into DAU and said he was eager to contribute to its continued development.
Pavitar has been closely engaged with the Institute even after graduation, contributing in many ways, and we hope his presence on the Board will continue to benefit the Institute. Currently, Pavitar Singh is the Co-Founder & CEO of UnifyApps, UAE. After completing his B. Tech. from DA-IICT, Pavitar earned his PG degree in management from MDI Gurgaon. Over the years he has held roles as Product lead, VP- product Development, Director R & D and CTO and has been associated with organisations such as Pramati Technologies, Ixigo.com, co-founder- UserRules.com (which was acquired by Sprinklr) and Sprinklr before founding UnifyApps.
