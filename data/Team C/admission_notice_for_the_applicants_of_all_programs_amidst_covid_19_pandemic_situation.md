# Main Content

We are facing an unprecedented COVID-19 pandemic situation. However, DA-IICT would like to assure prospective applicants that all efforts are on to complete the admissions cycle in time and start the next academic session.
On the basis of the guidelines issued by the University Grants Commission, Government of India vide D.O. No.F.1-1/2020(Secy) dated 29th April 2020 and successive directives issued by the Central and State Governments to follow social distancing, we will be introducing a few changes in the selection processes of M. Sc (IT), M. Sc (Data Science ), M. Des (Communication Design) and PhD programs. We shall announce the changes in the admission pages of respective programs shortly.
Please visit our web site regularly for news and updates.
