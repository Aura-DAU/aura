# Main Content

Dr. Rutu Parekh has published a book on Nanoelectronics, Physics, technology and applications.
Nanoelectronics is the basis of future electronics. It demands interdisciplinary knowledge in terms of quantum mechanics, material science, electronics, circuit design, simulation, and fabrication. To design a future miniaturized circuit, one needs to understand the device, its physics and fabrication process, as well as its limitations.
This book provides comprehensive coverage for fundamental and advanced courses in nanoelectronics. It provides insight into the future of electronics, emerging devices, logic and memory, sensors, systems architecture, nanofabrication, and the fundamental physics behind nanoelectronics. After reading the book, students will be able to understand the applications and design principles of the future emerging circuits and systems.
