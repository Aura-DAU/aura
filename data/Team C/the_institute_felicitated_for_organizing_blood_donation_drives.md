# Main Content

The Institute regularly organizes blood donation camps on the campus, as a part of its community service activities. Each camp typically sees participation from over 100 students who donate their blood to support those in need.
In recognition of these efforts, the Institute received accolades on June 14th, the World Blood Donor Day. During a felicitation ceremony organized by the Blood Bank at BJ Medical School and Civil Hospital in Ahmedabad, the Government of Gujarat acknowledged the Institute's contributions to blood donation.
Mr. Dhanjay Dwivedi, the Principal Secretary of the Health and Family Welfare Department of the Government of Gujarat, honored the Institute for its dedication to organizing these life-saving blood donation camps.
