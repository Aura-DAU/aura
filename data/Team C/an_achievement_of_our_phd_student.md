# Overview

Congratulations to Pratik Ghosh, Doctoral Scholar at DAIICT, who has been selected as the recipient of American Physical Society's GEC Student Travel Grant (worth $1000) to attend the 75th Annual Gaseous Electronics Conference (https://www.apsgec.org/gec2022/index.php). Pratik is a co-author of the work titled "Deep Learning based approach for investigating Electromagnetic Wave Propagation in Plasmas" which has been submitted at the GEC-2022 conference
