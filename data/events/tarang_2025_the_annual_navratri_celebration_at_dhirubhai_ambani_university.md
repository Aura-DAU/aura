# Main Content

On September 30 and October 1, Tarang 2025, the annual Navratri celebration, was organised at the Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU) campus. The event was a spectacular display of cultural vibrancy and community spirit, bringing the entire DAU fraternity together for an unforgettable experience of music, dance, and tradition.
The vibrant energy on the Garba ground was palpable. Attendees, dressed in traditional attire, participated with great enthusiasm, creating a festive atmosphere. The sheer joy and non-stop enthusiasm displayed by the students, faculty, and staff underscored the deep cultural connection and unity within the university community.
A major highlight of Tarang 2025 was the presence of renowned Gujarati musical talents. The stage came alive with incredible performances by state sensations, Rishikesh Gadhvi and Ishani Dave, whose musical performaces ensured the energy levels remained high throughout the celebration.
The seamless execution of this truly vibrant event is a testament to the dedication and tireless efforts of the Tarang 2025 Cultural Committee. Their meticulous planning and hard work ensured every moment of the celebration was steeped in tradition, spectacular music, and enjoyment.
