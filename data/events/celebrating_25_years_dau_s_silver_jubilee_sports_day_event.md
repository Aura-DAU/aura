# Main Content

As part of the Silver Jubilee celebrations, Dhirubhai Ambani University organised a Sports Day for its employees on Sunday, November 23, 2025.
The event focused on promoting health, physical fitness, stress relief, and strong social bonding, among the employees, embracing the spirit of enjoyment and togetherness over competition.
Employees participated in a variety of engaging activities, including Cricket, Badminton, Basketball, Chess, and Carrom. The day was filled with energy, excitement, and countless memorable moments, perfectly reflecting the true spirit of DAU's 25 glorious years. Mementos, T-shirts, and refreshments were arranged for all participants.
We extend our sincere gratitude to all participants and supporters for ensuring the Silver Jubilee Sports Day was a resounding success.
