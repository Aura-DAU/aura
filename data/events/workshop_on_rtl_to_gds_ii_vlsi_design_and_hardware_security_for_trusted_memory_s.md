## Workshop on RTL to GDS-II: VLSI Design and Hardware Security for Trusted Memory Systems

Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU) , Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India is hosting a one-week workshop titled “ RTL to GDS-II: VLSI Design and Hardware Security for Trusted Memory Systems ” from 06 th to 10 th July 2026 .

This program is designed to offer participants a comprehensive understanding of the RTL to GDS-II design flow , along with key concepts in VLSI design, hardware security and memory systems . The course combines theoretical insights with extensive hands-on training sessions.

The workshop is tailored for industry professionals, faculty members, Ph.D. scholars, postgraduate students , and senior undergraduate students. Participants will gain practical exposure through hands-on sessions using Cadence and SPICE EDA tools .
