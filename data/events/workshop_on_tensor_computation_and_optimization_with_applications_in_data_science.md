# Main Content

The School of Technology, Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), organised a workshop on Tensor Computation and Optimization with Application in Data Science on November 20, 2025, at the DAU campus in Gandhinagar.
The workshop had sessions from experts including Prof. Ram Narayan Mahapatra from the University of Central Florida, Orlando, USA; Dr. Ratikant Behra from IISc Bangalore, India; Dr. Saroj Kumar Pradhan from VSS University of Technology, India; and Dr. Nabin Kumar Sahu from Dhirubhai Ambani University, India.
The workshop focused on equipping attendees with essential skills for handling complex, multi-dimensional data sets. The participants learned how to operate effectively with tensors and explored new optimization techniques designed to solve cutting-edge problems in Data Science. The engaging sessions provided valuable theoretical knowledge coupled with practical insights into this critical field.
