Dhirubhai Ambani University hosted the Faculty and Staff Sports Event on 15th February, celebrating teamwork, sportsmanship, and friendly competition. Faculty and staff participated with great enthusiasm and competitive spirit. Cricket – Team DAU-1 crowned champions!
Badminton Doubles – Prof. Pritam Anand & Prof. Nabin Sahu won , Mr. Krunal Patel & Mr. Bhavesh Shah were runners-up . Winners received medals & certificates, making it a memorable event. Kudos to all participants!
