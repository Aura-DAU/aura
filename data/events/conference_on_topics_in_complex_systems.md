# Conference on Topics in Complex Systems

## Overview

The multidisciplinary field of complex systems has witnessed significant advances over the past two decades, driving an understanding of behaviors and phenomena across disciplines such as biology, economics, and the social sciences. The Conference on Topics in Complex Systems gives researchers at all levels the opportunity to engage with leading experts and explore the various facets of complex systems research. Graduate students, young researchers, and teachers with backgrounds in physics, applied mathematics, computer science, sociology, and physiology will find the conference beneficial.

The conference is preceded by an optional workshop on December 7 introducing participants to the fundamentals of complex systems. Participants can register for the conference alone, or for both the conference and workshop.
