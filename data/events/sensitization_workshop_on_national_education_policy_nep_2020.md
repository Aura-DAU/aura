# Sensitization Workshop on National Education Policy (NEP) 2020

## Overview

Dr. Dubey spoke on the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 at DAU, covering the reforms it introduces to higher education.

## Main Content

- **Date:** 19 March 2025
- **Time:** 2:15 PM – 5:30 PM
- **Venue:** LT-1

### Abstract

NEP 2020 introduces major reforms in higher education to make it more holistic, flexible, and multidisciplinary. It emphasizes a four-year undergraduate degree with multiple entry and exit options, enabling students to earn certifications, diplomas, or degrees at different stages. The policy promotes academic flexibility, credit transfer, and a multidisciplinary approach through Multidisciplinary Education and Research Universities (MERUs). It aims to dismantle rigid subject boundaries, encourage research and innovation, and enhance digital learning. The Higher Education Commission of India (HECI) will be established as a single regulatory body, ensuring quality standards while granting institutional autonomy. NEP 2020 also prioritizes equity, inclusion, and global collaboration, aiming to position India as a leading knowledge hub.

### Key Objectives

- **Enhancing Awareness and Implementation** — educate stakeholders on key NEP 2020 reforms, including curriculum flexibility, accreditation standards, research initiatives, skill development, and digital transformation, to improve the quality and accessibility of higher education.
- **Developing Strategic Action Plans** — facilitate discussions on practical implementation strategies, international collaborations, equity and inclusion, and innovative teaching methodologies to align institutions with the policy's vision.
