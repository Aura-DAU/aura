# The IEEE Smart Village Symposium, an international conference

The IEEE Smart Village Symposium , an international conference will be held at DA-IICT, Gandhinagar on the 7 th & 8 th November 2024 . The conference will have participation from foreign delegates including members from the IEEE ISV team and IEEE Power and Energy (PES) Society and others.

The IEEE Smart Village (ISV) has been transforming underserved communities through electrification, education, and entrepreneurship. ISV has impacted over 2 million lives globally, creating sustainable, community-driven businesses. Its efforts have also been recognized by the UN.

Explore key topics such as community engagement, sustainability, and the role of social enterprises and NGOs. Whether you're an entrepreneur, technology enthusiast, or NGO, this symposium offers insights into building sustainable enterprises. Don't miss this chance to contribute to global change! For more details, visit https://smartvillage.ieee.org/ or email isvindiaconference@gmail.com .
