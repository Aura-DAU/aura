# Overview

The conference-cum-workshop serves as an exceptional platform for sharing knowledge, exchanging ideas, and fostering collaboration. Participants will benefit from engaging keynote addresses, specialized workshops, and dynamic discussions that delve into the latest innovations in differential equations, mathematical modeling, and dynamical systems. Additionally, this is an excellent occasion for young researchers, doctoral students, and early-career faculty to network with leading experts from around the world. The aim is to equip participants with knowledge of current methodologies, emerging trends, and research opportunities. Don’t miss this opportunity to be part of an inspiring community dedicated to advancing research and applications in these vital fields.
