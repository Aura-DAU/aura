# Silver Jubilee Celebrations and Alumni Reunion: Honouring 25 Years of Excellence of DAU

Celebrating 25 years of excellence of Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), the university is organising an exclusive Silver Jubilee Alumni Reunion for its founding batches. This momentous occasion is specifically for the trailblazers of the 2001, 2002, 2003, and 2004 classes, honouring their significant role in establishing the institution's esteemed legacy. The university extends a special invitation to these foundational alumni to return to the campus for a celebration like no other.

# Confirm your place now : Register Here!

Join us to reconnect with former classmates, relive cherished campus moments, and celebrate the original passion that launched the institution's journey. This gathering will serve as a tribute to a quarter-century of innovation, inspiration, and shared history.

# Event Details:

- Date: December 27 – 28, 2025
- Location: DAU, Gandhinagar
