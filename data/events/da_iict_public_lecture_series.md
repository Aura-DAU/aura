# DA-IICT Public Lecture Series: Dr. Subeer S. Majumdar

## Overview

The September edition of DA-IICT's monthly public lecture series featured Dr. Subeer S. Majumdar, Director General of Gujarat Biotechnology University, Gandhinagar, speaking on "Use of Crucial Animal Models in Medical Biotechnology and Development and Use of SNP Chips for Indian Cows."

## Main Content

- **Venue:** LT-1
- **Date:** 4 September 2024
- **Time:** 4:15 PM

### Abstract

Human beings cannot be used for experimentation, so animals are the best choice for studying physiological conditions, disorders, and diseases. Transgenic animals — created by inserting or deleting important genes (sometimes of human origin) — help researchers understand gene function or the effects of gene perturbation. Dr. Majumdar's group developed a new and simple transgenesis technique that avoids animal deaths, aimed at producing humanized disease models for studying human diseases.

### Speaker Bio

Dr. Subeer Majumdar served as a scientist at the National Institute of Immunology, Delhi, before becoming Director of the National Institute of Animal Biotechnology, Hyderabad, and later a distinguished professor at the same institute. He is currently Director General of Gujarat Biotechnology University. He has used differential and functional genomics via transgenic mice to identify genes regulating male fertility, established a testicular-route transgenesis technique (published in Nature Methods), and contributed to metabolic disorder/diabetes research (published in Nature Medicine). His group developed India's first SNP chip using next-generation sequencing of indigenous cow genetic resources for native breed conservation, and he leads a DBT-funded One Health consortium.
