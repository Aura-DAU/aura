## Objectives:

The Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) , introduced under the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 , aims to enhance flexibility and student-centric learning in higher education.

#### 1. Raise Awareness about ABC

To inform students about the concept, structure, and purpose of the Academic Bank of Credit as envisioned in the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 .

#### 2. Explain Functionality and Benefits

To help students understand how ABC functions, including credit accumulation, transfer, multiple entry/exit, and credit redemption , and how it benefits their academic flexibility and career planning.

#### 3. Demonstrate Registration and Usage

To guide students on how to register on the ABC portal, access their academic credit records , and track their learning progress digitally.

#### 4. Promote Multidisciplinary Learning

To encourage students to explore courses across disciplines and institutions , thereby supporting holistic and multidisciplinary education.

#### 5. Encourage Lifelong Learning and Re-entry

To make students aware of how ABC allows them to pause and resume education, facilitating lifelong learning and continuous skill development.

#### 6. Clarify Roles and Responsibilities

To communicate the role of students, institutions, and academic advisors in effective implementation and monitoring of ABC credits.

#### 7. Address Queries and Concerns

To provide a platform for students to raise doubts, clarify misconceptions , and receive accurate information about ABC implementation and opportunities.

#### 8. Promote NEP 2020 Goals

To align student understanding with the broader goals of equity, accessibility, and quality education under NEP 2020.
