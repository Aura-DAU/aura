Biography

Dr. Parul Gupta is an Assistant Professor of Economics at Dhirubhai Ambani University. Previously, she has taught economics at the Shiv Nadar University (Delhi NCR), Indian School of Business and Finance and Lady Shri Ram College. She has also worked at Sattva Consulting and World Bank. Her current research explores the drivers of education access and quality in developing regions.

Outside of academics, her interests lie in music, dance and fine arts. She is trained in Indian Classical Vocal Music and Bharatnatyam dance and she enjoys working on art and crafts in her free time.
