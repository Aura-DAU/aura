### Vision for Education & Technology

> *"Intellectual competitiveness is critical to India achieving global leadership in the information age."*

Dhirubhai's vision for education emphasised several key themes:

- **Investment in education** is an imperative to build intellectual competitiveness
- **Technology education is critical** because technology is impacting life, living, and living systems like never before
- Technology education is like a **cradle to global leadership**
- India can exploit the spiralling need for technology professionals and **change her economic and social landscape dramatically**
- India's present technology professional resource base is **grossly inadequate**
- India will need to create **ten times the current resource base** to succeed in the knowledge economy

### The DA-IICT Legacy

The **Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT)** is inspired by this vision of building intellectual competitiveness for India.
