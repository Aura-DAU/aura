# Overview

Recruitment announcement for a Post-doctoral Fellow position at the Smart Energy Learning Centre (SELC) at Dhirubhai Ambani University, funded by BSES Delhi. The project focuses on improving efficiency of solar energy harvesting using metasurface design.

# Main Content

### Post-doctoral Fellow: RF-Microwave Smart Energy Learning Centre

**Date**: 05 May, 2026

**A New Research Centre at DAU funded by BSES Delhi, India**

**Ref. No.**: CSR-25/BSES/A10-PK/SELC

Applications are invited from highly motivated candidates with good academic records for a full-time, fixed-term Post-doctoral Fellow position with the Smart Energy Learning Centre (SELC) at the Dhirubhai Ambani University, (formerly known as DA-IICT), India.

### Project Details

**Project Title**: Improving Efficiency of Solar Energy Harvesting using Metasurface Design

### Position and Fellowship

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Position | Post-doctoral Fellow |
| Number of Posts | 1 |
| Fellowship | INR 75,000 per month |

### Eligibility Requirements

#### Essential Qualifications

- PhD with specialization in RF-Microwave
- M.E./M.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering, RF-Microwave, VLSI, ICT, with at least 60% marks
- B.E./B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering, ICT or equivalent, with at least 70% marks
- Preference may be given to GATE-qualified candidates
- Strong command of oral and written English

#### Desirable Qualifications

- Proficiency with programming and modeling software (e.g. CST, Matlab, Python)
- Prior exposure to metasurface related devices

### Period of Appointment

This position is temporary, with an appointment for **nine months**, and it will not be extended further. This does not confer any right for the candidate to claim an extension or absorption at DAU, Gandhinagar.

**Upper Age**: No Age Limits

### How to Apply

Interested candidates must send their detailed Curriculum Vitae with a Cover Letter by **20th May, 2026**, through an email to PI at **pankaj_kumar[at]dau[dot]ac[dot]in**

Please write "**Application for Post-doctoral Fellow position for project CSR-25/BSES/A10-PK/SELC**" in the subject line of the email.

Only shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview, which they will attend at their own expense; no TA/DA will be provided.

During the interview, candidates' knowledge will be assessed in the areas of RF-Microwave, Metasurface Design, Matlab, CST and Python programming.
