### Dr. G. Venkatesh Joins as the Director, School of Technology, DAU

We are pleased to announce that Dr. G. Venkatesh, an eminent academician and accomplished industry leader has joined as the Director of the School of Technology at Dhirubhai Ambani University.

### Background and Qualifications

Dr. Venkatesh holds a degree in electronics from IIT Madras and a Ph.D in Computer Science from TIFR, Mumbai. He is currently also associated with IIT Madras as a Professor of Practice and with Sasken Technologies Limited as a Fellow and Director.

### Awards and Accolades

A testament to his contributions, Dr. Venkatesh has received numerous accolades, including:

- Fellow, Indian National Academy of Engineers (2006)
- Fellow, IETE (2012)
- Technovation award, Indian Electronics and Semiconductor Association (2013)
- Vasvik award for industrial research (2015)

### Contributions to Research, Innovation, and Entrepreneurship

Beyond academia and industry, Dr. Venkatesh has been deeply engaged in advancing research, innovation, and entrepreneurship:

- Serves as Director on the boards of five companies, including the Section 8 companies **IITM Pravartak** and **MaDeIT Innovation Foundation**, both focused on technology commercialisation
- Founder of the **Communications Systems and Networks Association**, which organises flagship conferences such as **COMSNETS** and **AIML Systems**
- Contributes to the governance of institutions such as **CeWiT**, **NIT Nagaland**, and **DAU**
- Serves on faculty selection and promotion committees at leading institutions including **IIIT Bangalore**

### Vision for School of Technology

With deep expertise in microelectronics, telecommunications, and technology-led innovation, Dr. Venkatesh is uniquely positioned to strengthen the School of Technology's research ecosystem, deepen industry partnerships, and nurture a culture of innovation and excellence.

We extend our warmest welcome to Dr. G. Venkatesh and look forward to his visionary leadership in shaping the next phase of growth and impact at DAU.
