# Crèche Supervisor

**Location:** DAU Campus

Job Summary:

The Crèche Supervisor will be responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of the crèche facility, ensuring a safe, hygienic, and nurturing environment for children. The role includes supervising children, managing daily routines, and supporting their overall well-being and development.

Key Responsibilities:

  * Supervise and care for children, ensuring their safety and well-being at all times.
  * Maintain hygiene, cleanliness, and a child-friendly environment within the facility.
  * Plan and conduct age-appropriate activities for children’s learning and development.
  * Monitor children’s daily routines, including meals, rest, and playtime.
  * Follow safety protocols and provide basic first aid when required.
  * Liaise with parents, provide updates, and incorporate their feedback when appropriate.
  * Maintain attendance records and basic documentation related to the crèche.
  * Perform any other duties assigned by the Administration.

Eligibility Criteria:

  * Minimum 12th pass in any discipline; graduates with a Diploma/Certificate in Day Care/Crèche Supervision preferred.
  * Minimum three years of experience in a crèche/play school/daycare.

### **Working Hours:**

#### **9:00 AM to 6:30 PM**

### **Additional Information:**

  * **Crèche facility will cater to children aged 6 months to 6 years.**
