## Important Information

- The university follows a hierarchical governance model headed by the **President**
- The **Board of Governors** oversees the **Director General**
- Two advisory bodies â€” **Academic Council** and **Board of Studies** â€” and the **Finance Committee** report to the Director General
- Five key administrative roles report to the Director General: Director of Technology, Director of Law, IQAC, Executive Registrar, and Librarian
- Six Deans handle specialized academic and student functions
- Faculty members serve under the Directors (Technology and Law)
