## Overview

Rules and Regulations for Halls of Residence at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU) / DA-IICT, Gandhinagar. These rules provide a framework of common understanding and mutual concern for all residents.

## Main Content

### 1. Room Allocation

1. The allotment of rooms for new students will be done by the Wardens.
2. No requests for a change of rooms will be entertained.
3. The Dean of Students, Wardens, and Hostel Supervisors reserve the right to allot/re-allot and withdraw the accommodation provided.
4. The allotment will only be made to a registered student of DA-IICT.
5. The resident has to vacate the room within five days of his/her ceasing to be a student of DA-IICT.
6. A UG student will be allotted a room only for the first four years of stay on campus. If a student cannot complete his degree requirements within that period, a room will be allotted subject to availability only.
7. Hostellers from outside Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar districts must give complete contact details of at least two guardians/relatives, preferably from Ahmedabad or Gandhinagar, at the time of room allocation process.

### 2. Behavior and Discipline

1. Residents must ensure peace and tranquility within the Halls of Residence area so that they do not disturb other residents.
2. Residents will behave according to social ethics and respect the privacy of others at all times. Loud music disrupting the peace of others will not be allowed.
3. Men will avoid unwanted attention-seeking behavior towards women that can constitute as 'harassment'.
4. **Consumption or possession of alcoholic drinks**, usage of any form of intoxicating materials, and all forms of illegal activities are **strictly prohibited**. Prohibition is enforced in the state of Gujarat. Strict action including termination from the Institute will be taken via DAC.
5. The entire campus of DA-IICT is a **no-smoking zone**. Strict action will be taken via DAC.
6. All Halls of Residence residents must return to the campus by **midnight 12 AM**.
7. No resident student will be allowed outside the campus from **midnight till 6 AM**. Exception only for medical emergencies. Entry must be made in the register at Security gate.
8. Residents must email the Hostel Supervisor (prior intimation) if they intend to stay away from the Halls of Residence (even for one night).
9. Entry is prohibited for men and women into the other gender's HOR.
10. Residents are not permitted to change rooms or exchange Halls of Residence property without approval of the Hostel Supervisor. This is a **punishable offense** with rigorous fines and disciplinary action.
11. **Ragging is a criminal offence** and strictly prohibited. Contact the Anti-Ragging Vigilance Committee or Resident Warden immediately. May lead to termination and legal action.
12. Outside rooms, residents will not be dressed in a manner that would offend anyone.
13. Residents will not use loud or abusive/offensive/inciting language.
14. Residents and visitors are not permitted to sit on parapets, railings, staircases, or any other unsafe area.
15. Vulgar posters, text, or drawings will not be permitted on walls or elsewhere in the room.
16. Residents must check the Snail Mail link at the hostel website daily. Mails older than ten days will be disposed of.
17. A minimum fine of **â‚¹1,000/- per day** would be charged to both occupants if anyone else stays in a resident's room without permission.
18. All electrical switches and gadgets must be switched off while leaving the room.
19. Drying clothes on room windows and staircase railings is strictly prohibited. Use designated clotheslines only.
20. **Residents cannot bring food inside the Halls of Residence.** Fine of â‚¹1,000 to be paid within 72 hours. Failure to pay leads to disciplinary action.
21. A sick student (certified by a doctor) can get food in the room with prior information to Hostel Supervisor.
22. Residents are permitted to use computers and table lamps.
23. **High power-consuming electrical equipment is strictly prohibited:** irons, heaters, ovens, refrigerators, TVs, and kettles. Severe punishment for violation.
24. No cooking is permitted in the Halls of Residence.
25. Residents should not transfer furniture from one room to another.

### 3. Vehicles

1. Students are **NOT allowed to keep 4-wheelers** on campus (cars, jeeps, etc.).
2. Students can keep **2-wheelers** on campus (bicycles, scooters, motorcycles). Maximum one vehicle per student.
3. Parking must be done in authorized parking areas ONLY.
4. Students must obey all traffic rules on campus, including always wearing a helmet, driving within the speed limit, and having maximum two persons in a vehicle.
   - **First offence:** Monetary fine, community service hours, letter to parents
   - **Second offence:** Vehicle banned from campus for remaining stay. May also be expelled from hostel depending on severity.

### 4. Maintenance of Halls of Residence

1. Residents are individually responsible for keeping rooms, bathrooms/toilets neat and clean. Common areas must be kept clean. Institute undertakes housekeeping of common areas only.
2. Residents must adhere to electricity, water conservation plans. Misuse results in fines (may apply to whole floor/wing).
3. Complaints should be written in the concerned register only. Escalate to Hostel Supervisor, then Wardens if unresolved.
4. Any damage to Halls of Residence property must be reported immediately to the Hostel Supervisor.
5. The Institute reserves the right to make **spot checks of rooms without prior notice**.
6. Drilling and other civil works altering the structure are strictly prohibited.
7. All rooms have fixed inventory; copy handed to occupant who is responsible for maintenance.
8. No private items of furniture allowed (exception with medical certificate).
9. Residents are expected to bring mattress, pillow, linen, and personal clothing.

### 5. Visitors

1. Visitors are **NOT allowed** into the Halls of Residence Complex. Cafeteria is the suggested meeting place.
2. Guest Room available only for medical emergencies (with Hostel Supervisor permission, rent paid in advance):
   - Non-resident DA-IICT students: **â‚¹200 per person per day**
   - Outside visitors including parents: **â‚¹250 per person per day** (inclusive of GST)
3. Entertaining a visitor without prior intimation: minimum fine of **â‚¹1,000/-** plus guest charges.
4. Parents/Guardians visiting at other times need special permission from Hostel Supervisor.
5. Visitors are not allowed to stay overnight except in medical emergencies.
6. Non-resident students must:
   - Inform Hostel Supervisor for registration through course instructor/guide
   - Mention exact period of stay
   - Pay â‚¹200/- per day rent
7. List of temporary residents given to Security Supervisor for monitoring.
8. Security personnel will report students staying beyond registered time.
9. Temporary rooms provided based on availability.
10. Temporary residents must follow all Halls of Residence rules.

### 6. Security

1. The Institute is **not responsible** for loss or damage of any personal property.
2. Students must keep belongings under lock and key.
3. Students must lock room before leaving, even for a few minutes. Unlocked vacant rooms will be locked by Hostel Supervisor. Penalty of **â‚¹500** if no proper justification. Repeated violation attracts suitable penalty.
4. Avoid keeping valuables like gold ornaments, large amounts of cash in Halls of Residence.
5. Any student finding roommate(s) missing during the night must intimate Hostel Supervisor immediately.
6. Each resident gets one key. Loss of key: fine of **â‚¹500/-** plus cost of new lock.
7. **Residents are not allowed to use their own locks.**
8. Damage to provided lock: inform Hostel Supervisor, get new lock with fine of â‚¹500/- plus cost. Using own lock without informing: **â‚¹1,000/-** additional fine.
9. Residents must update their profile (phone numbers, addresses of parents/guardian) whenever there is a change.
10. Residents must ensure their photographs have been uploaded on SIS/ERP.

### 7. Pets

1. **NO PETS** are allowed in the Halls of Residence.
2. Residents must not encourage stray cats/dogs. Inform security guard if any are found.

1. The Hostel Management Committee (HMC) reserves the right to revise Rules and Regulations from time to time.
2. Minimum fine of **â‚¹1,000/-** for breach of any rules (may increase depending on severity). Repeated/punishable offenses reported to DAC.
3. The Hostel Management Committee comprises:
