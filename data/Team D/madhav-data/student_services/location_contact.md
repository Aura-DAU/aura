## The City â€” Gandhinagar

Gandhinagar is the capital city of Gujarat, established as a modernized twin city for Ahmedabad. It was visualised as a planned city along the lines of:

- **New Delhi** (by Edwin Lutyens)
- **Chandigarh** (by Le Corbusier)
- **Bhubaneshwar** (by Otto H. Koenigsberger)

Located on the banks of the **River Sabarmati**, the city is the administrative centre of Gandhinagar and the capital of Gujarat.
