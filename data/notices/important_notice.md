# Main Content

It has come to our notice of that some unscrupulous people are granting false and fabricated admissions to aspiring students for our teaching programs. The public at large is hereby notified that we have not appointed any person, agent or organization to conduct and offer admissions to our teaching programs. Aspirants are advised not to fall prey for any such false and illegal acts of others.
This notice is to warn that those who are involved in fabricating our letterheads and signatures of faculty, officers and staff and issuing fabricated admission letters and those who are accepting them and claiming admissions will be dealt with severely and legal action will be initiated against all those who are involved.
