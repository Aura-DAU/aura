# VLSI & Embedded Systems Research Group — DA-IICT / DAU

## Core Research Areas
- Low power processor design, edge computing
- Ultra-low power IC design; Embedded systems and IoT
- MEMS (Nano/Micro sensors); Nanoelectronics
- AI/ML chip design, robotics, system cybernetics
- Machine learning for VLSI architectures
