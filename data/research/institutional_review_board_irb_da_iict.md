## Institutional Review Board (IRB) - DA-IICT

### DA-IICT has established the Institutional Review Board (IRB) for research involving human participants. The main tasks of the IRB are as follows:

* The IRB must review and approve all proposed research involving human subjects before participant recruitment and data collection can start.
* The primary concern of the IRB is to minimize any risk for subjects arising out of the study while allowing the researcher to collect the necessary information.
* Privacy and confidentiality of the participants are sought to be protected within the research setting.
