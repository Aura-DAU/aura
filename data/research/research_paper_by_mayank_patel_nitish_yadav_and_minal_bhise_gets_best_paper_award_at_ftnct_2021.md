# Overview

A Distributed Databases Research Group's work by Mayank Patel, Nitish Yadav, Minal Bhise on "Workload aware Cost-based Partial loading of Raw data for Limited Storage Resources'' has received the best paper award in the Network and Computing track at Futuristic Trends in Network and Communication Technologies FTNCT 2021 held during 10-11 December, 2021.
