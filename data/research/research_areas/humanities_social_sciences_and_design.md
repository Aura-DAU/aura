## Research Overview

The Humanities, Social Sciences and Design group is an interdisciplinary community of scholars working across Anthropology, Philosophy, Political Theory, Literary Studies, Visual Culture, Economics, History, Cultural Studies, and Design. Teaching and research focus on social life and human endeavors.

Undergraduate Core curriculum includes:
- Approaches to Indian Society
- Introductory/Development Economics
- Science & Technology Studies

Electives available:
- Visual Anthropology
- Gender Studies
- Modern Philosophy

MDes in Communication Design includes:
- History of Design
- Narratology
- Photography

PhD program offers well-funded opportunities for doctoral research with faculty mentorship.

Faculty also collaborate with civil society groups and government agencies to shape public policy for marginalized communities.
