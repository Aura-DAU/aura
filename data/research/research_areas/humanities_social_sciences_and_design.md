## Research Overview

### Interdisciplinary Research

The group's research is characterised by its interdisciplinary nature, with faculty members working across multiple disciplines to address complex social and cultural issues.

### Teaching and Research

The group's teaching and research are guided by a commitment to understanding the diversity of social life and human endeavour, with a focus on the complexities of social and cultural issues in India and beyond.

## Curriculum

### Undergraduate Curriculum

* Core courses: Approaches to Indian Society, Introductory/Development Economics, Science & Technology Studies
* Electives: Visual Anthropology, Gender Studies, Modern Philosophy

### Postgraduate Curriculum

* MDes in Communication Design: modules on the History of Design, Narratology, Photography

### PhD Program

* Well-funded program offering doctoral candidates an opportunity to work closely with faculty members in a supportive and resource-rich environment
