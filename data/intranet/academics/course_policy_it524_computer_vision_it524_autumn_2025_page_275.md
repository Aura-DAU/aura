## Main Content

COMPUTER VISION (IT524)

Objective:

Broad topics include discussions on approaches to 3D (object distance from 
camera) estimation using 2D image pixels. Face recognition. Basics of deep 
learning and its use in high resolution image generation, image generation 
from random noise, etc.

Pre-requisites :  
Good Undersatnding of:

- Fundamentals of Digital Image Processing 
- Linear Algebra 
- Probability and Random variables 
- Programming skills

Topics:

Brief ideas on image processing, Geometric transformation (Affine, 
Projective), Camera Models, Projective Geometry, Image formation, Camera 
matrix, Camera Calibration, Shape from stereo (stereopsis), correspondence 
problem, Ideas on estimation techniques LS (least squares), CLS (constrained 
least squares), Maximum likelihood (ML) estimation, Maximum Aposteriorie 
(MAP) estimation),  super-resolution imaging, Principal Component Analysis 
(PCA) for face recognition, Motion Field and Optical flow, Concepts of MRF 
(Markov random fields), introduction to neural networks, Generative models 
(GANs).

Outcome: The course would help the graduate students to understand the basic 
concepts in computer vision (how the computer can be used to perform the 
tasks of human vision system). Through lectures, programming assignments 
and presentations the students are provided with the learning experience of

1. Analysis of images for extracting the 3D coordinates of the object
