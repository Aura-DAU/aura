# IT694 - Computer Networks (Winter’2026)

## Overview

## Main Content

IT694 Computer Networks (Winter’2026)

Instructor: P.S. Kalyan Sasidhar, 2109, Faculty Block-2

Course Placement: MSc IT, Semester-II

Course format: 3 Lecture hours and 2 Lab hours.

Course Content: 
This course will cover the fundamental principles of wired computer networks 
with focus on layered architecture, protocols, implementation and issues specific 
to the Internet. The objective is to provide an understanding of how the Internet 
works which include how data flows from a source to a destination. Focus will 
also be laid on certain topics such as network design and automation. network 
management, administration and debugging which are ideally required for 
networking IT professionals. 
 
The associated laboratory component is designed to expose students to the basic 
LAN hardware and configuration, and network simulation tools for the analysis 
of traffic and network protocols. Cisco laboratory exercises will be strongly 
referred to provide an industry level exposure to real life networking scenarios 
based labs. 
 
Textbook: 
1. James F. Kurose and Keith W. Ross. 2016. Computer Networking: A Top-
Down Approach (7th ed.). Pearson. 
 
References: 
1. Douglas E. Comer. 2013. Internetworking with TCP/IP (6th ed.). Addison-
Wesley Professional. 
2. Andrew S. Tanenbaum and David J. Wetherall. 2010. Computer Networks 
(5th ed.) Prentice Hall Press, Upper Saddle River, NJ, USA. 
3. Larry L. Peterson and Bruce S. Davie. 2011. Computer Networks, Fifth 
Edition: A Systems Approach (5th ed.). Morgan Kaufmann Publishers Inc., San 
Francisco, CA, USA
