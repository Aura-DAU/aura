# HM333 - Human Behavior Management

## Overview

This document presents the detailed policy and syllabus structure for the course Human Behavior Management (HM333). It includes details on course objectives, credits, prerequisites, syllabus content, evaluation schemes, and contact information where available.

## Main Content

Human Behavior Management

Course Title: Human Behavior Management

Course Code: HM 333

Target Audience: Students of 7th Semester of B.Tech. program and PG (MSC IT)

Course Structure: 3 – 0 – 0 – 3

Course Objective:  
Managing human behavior is becoming increasingly important for students and professionals to be able 
to realize their true potentials and be successful in life, in general, and careers, in particular. This course 
will help students in developing an understanding of human behavior and help them in managing their 
own behavior. It will help students find an answer to the questions:

Why does one behave the way one does in their personal and professional lives?

And  
Why do others behave the way they do in their personal and professional lives?  
It will deal with the subject matter from the perspectives of individuals trying to improve their 
relationship with self and others. It covers aspects of human resource management, business 
communication, grooming and etiquette, attitude and group dynamics.

Learning Outcomes:

Upon successful completion of the course, the student should be able to:

• 
Understand behavioral issues at individual (self) and group levels.  
• 
Develop interpersonal skills and become effective goal oriented team member.  
• 
To become a professional equipped with idealistic, practical and moral values.  
• 
To re-engineer attitude and understand its influence on human behavior.  
Course Contents:

| 1. | Psychology of Human Development: Introduction to psychology, Life skill Development, Self-
Awareness, SWOT analysis. |
|---|---|
| 2. | Behavioral aspects in human relations: Sigmund Freud's Psychoanalysis, Understanding,
Personality, Behaviorism (Skinner, Pavlov), Humanistic Psychology (Maslow, Rogers). |
| 3. | Communication: Transactional Analysis, Communicate and Articulate in personal and
professional life, Presentation skills, Mock Interview. |
| 4. | Social Behavior: Leadership, Goal setting, Fostering Team work, Group Dynamics. |
| 5. | Ethics and Human Responsibility, Attitude, Aggression. |
| 6. | HUMANE Traps, Psychological Disorders. |
