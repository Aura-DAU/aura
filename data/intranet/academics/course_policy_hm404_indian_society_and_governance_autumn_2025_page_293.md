# HM404 - Indian Society and Governance

## Overview

This document presents the detailed policy and syllabus structure for the course Indian Society and Governance (HM404). It includes details on course objectives, credits, prerequisites, syllabus content, evaluation schemes, and contact information where available.

## Main Content

Indian Society and Governance

HM404

Instructor: Dr Vinod Kumar Mall

Email: vinod_mall@daiict.ac.in 
 
Course Placement: This course is an Open elective to 4th year B Tech students. 
 
Prerequisites:  
                         The engineering students of any semester with school level 
exposure to history, culture and literature will find this course useful. This course 
will be beneficial to those students who are trying to gain better social awareness 
about present times and have some interest in finding their positive role in the 
society.  The course will push further their curiosity which they have been able 
to develop during school and early engineering courses. This course will be very 
useful to specially those students who chose to go to social sector and use their 
engineering background to make a difference in the society.

Course Objectives:  
1. To understand the evolution of India as a nation with reference to 
Democracy, Socialism, Equality and Secularism mentioned in Indian 
Constitution. To get insight into social, philosophical and cultural context in 
which Indian political system has evolved.

2. To help students to understand the functioning of union, state and local 
self-government and the associated issues.  
 3. To provide understanding of National Security Structure, response 
mechanism and working of security agencies.  
 4. To give insight into some contemporary social and security problems such as 
communalism, naxalism and corruption 
 
Course Description:

The course will cover the evolution of India into a plural, multi-cultural and 
democratic nation over the centuries. It will have a brief introduction to Multi-
culturalism, Secularism and Diversity in Indian society. It will describe some 
important events, personalities and acts which led to India’s emergence as 
democratic, secular, republic. The relationship between Indian culture and its

philosophical traditions with the constitutional principles will be brought out. 
Relevant portions from Indian and western philosophy will be covered to 
explain democracy, secularism, socialism and role of religion in the Indian

The course will comprise of topics related to making of the post-independence 
India such as general elections, five year planning, ethnic and religious 
conflicts along with other social and national issues. Important issues such as 
Communalism and Left Wing Extremism will be included which are related to 
governance and security problems facing India. Course will further include 
structure and functioning of Central and State governments, Panchayat system 
and local self-government. There will be exposure to governance at grass root 
and district level.

Course will contain exposure to State police organization, its functioning and 
Central Police Organizations (CPOs). It will have introduction to National 
Security Structure and its components. Serious social problems like 
Communalism and Corruption will be covered which are related to day to day 
governance. There will be brief exposure to Coastal Security.

 3. To provide understanding of National Security Structure, response

 4. To give insight into some contemporary social and security problems such as

 Lecture Themes:  
                          The lectures have been designed to give an insight into the 
characteristics of Indian society, it’s historical, cultural and philosophical 
evolution into a modern, secular and democratic nation. India holds unique 
position in respect of its diversity and composite culture which has deeply 
impacted on our freedom struggle, making of our constitution and post-
independence governance. The lecture’s theme will incorporate the socio-
political dynamics and government apparatus responsible for realizing the 
principles enshrined in our constitution. Theme will include the dynamics behind 
growing communalism facing the nation and ideological resistance movement 
such as left long extremism in certain parts of the country. Some national security 
issues will be included in the theme. 
 
 
Evaluation: Evaluation will be continuous, based on presentations, panel 
discussions, class tests and assignment. It is proposed to have module-wise 
evaluation of 4 modules (30%). Term paper writing based on in-depth 
study/discussions and data collection/survey will be 30%. End term will be of 
40% weightage which will have written component.

Learning Outcomes: After completion of the course the student will be able

2. Understand the evolution of India into modern, secular and democratic 
republic.  
3. Understand the functioning of the government.  
  4. Have understanding of National Security structure to deal with the security
