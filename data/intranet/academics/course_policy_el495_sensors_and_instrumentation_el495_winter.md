## Main Content

Sensors and Instrumentation (EL495)

Course: Sensors and Instrumentation (EL495)

Credit Structure: (L-T-P-Cr): 3-0-2-4

Course Placement: Technical Elective

Prerequisite Courses: Fundamentals of Electronics and Circuits, and Basic Measurement Techniques.

Instructor: Ajay Beniwal (ajay_beniwal@dau.ac.in)

Couse Content:

| Unit | Content | Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Introduction to Sensors: Sensor measurements, Sensing measurand, Sensor transfer
characteristics, Sensor characteristics: Sensitivity, Linearity, Resolution, Hysteresis,
Repeatability, Accuracy, Principle working of various sensors technology, Sensor
design optimization methods. | 10 |
| Unit 2 | Sensor’s Instrumentation: Interface electronics for various sensors technology,
Understanding of sensor transfer characteristics and Instrumentation design, System
design for the sensors. | 8 |
| Unit 3 | Sensor Fabrication Technology and Advanced Sensors: Fundamentals of
fabrication processes, Contamination and clean room concepts, Wafer cleaning,
Oxidation, Photolithography, Mask Design, Wet and Dry Etching, Thin Film
Deposition Techniques, Advanced Sensor Concepts (Introductory Level): Chemical,
MEMS, and Flexible sensors. | 14 |
| Unit 4 | Intelligent Sensing and Applications: Sensor interfacing (Arduino/ESP32), IoT-
enabled sensor systems, Applications: Healthcare, Agriculture & Environmental
monitoring, Consumer electronics, Automotive & Defence. | 10 |


Suggested Text Book(s)/References

1. Jacob Fraden, Handbook of Modern Sensors: Physics, Designs and Applications, 5th edition, Springer, 2016. 
2. Tai-Ran Hsu, MEMS & Microsystems: Design and Manufacture, Tata McGraw-Hill, latest edition. 
3. Jon S. Wilson (Ed.), Sensor Technology Handbook, 2nd edition, Elsevier/Academic Press, 2004. 
4. S. C. Mukhopadhyay, Intelligent Sensing, Instrumentation and Measurements, Springer, 2013. 
5. Julian W. Gardner, Florin Udrea, Microsensors: Principles and Applications, 2nd edition, Wiley-Blackwell,

2021.  
Relevant research papers from scientific publisher like IEEE, IOP, ACS, AIP, Elsevier, RSC and others.

Course Objectives

• Understand the fundamentals of sensors, sensing measurands, and sensor characteristics. 
• Learn sensor instrumentation and interface electronics for system-level design. 
• Explore fabrication, advanced sensors and IoT-enabled applications. 
Course Outcomes

• Identify and analyse appropriate sensors for different measurands and applications. 
• Design and implement sensor systems with appropriate interfacing and measurement techniques. 
• Apply knowledge of advanced sensors and intelligent sensing systems in real-world applications. 
 
Evaluation: Mid-Sem Exam – 25%, Quiz/Assignment – 15%, End-Sem Exam – 30%, Laboratory – 30%.

Laboratory Experiment: The laboratory component includes experiments on sensor measurements, signal 
conditioning, sensor fabrication, testing, and IoT-enabled applications to provide hands-on experience, 
reinforce theoretical concepts, and develop practical skills in sensor interfacing, measurement, and intelligent 
sensing systems.
