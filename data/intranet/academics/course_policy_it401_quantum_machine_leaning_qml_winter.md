Objectives/
Brief Course
Description | In recent years, machine learning has expanded into virtually every
domain—from military and aerospace to agriculture, manufacturing, finance,
and healthcare. As ML models continue to grow in complexity, often
containing billions of parameters, training them efficiently has become
increasingly challenging. Quantum computing offers a promising path
forward by addressing problems that are intractable for classical computing
systems. Its ability to manipulate and compute over multiple Quantum bits
(qubits) simultaneously enables a form of massive parallelism grounded in
the principles of superposition, entanglement, and interference. Unlike
classical systems that rely on sequential information processing, quantum
computers leverage these quantum mechanical properties to accelerate
computation in fundamentally new ways.
This course introduces students to the foundational concepts of quantum
computing and explores how these ideas can be applied to machine learning
and deep learning. Through IBM’s Qiskit (an open source SDK) tool and
hands-on course projects on ML/DL, students gain experience in
implementing supervised and unsupervised ML/DL algorithms using
quantum and hybrid quantum–classical approaches. |  |  |
