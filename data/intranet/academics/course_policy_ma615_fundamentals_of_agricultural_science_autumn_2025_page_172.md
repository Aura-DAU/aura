This document presents the detailed policy and syllabus structure for the course Fundamentals of Agricultural Science (MA615). It includes details on course objectives, credits, prerequisites, syllabus content, evaluation schemes, and contact information where available.

Credit :

Course Objective:  The course presumes that the participants have no exposure of subjects related 
to agriculture science and skills. The aim of this course would be to expose with basic information in 
agriculture e.g. agriculture economics, crop and soil science and agrometeorology. The level of 
information will be of undergraduate level.
