| 8. Abstract Content | The course is designed as per Industry requirements, covers details of
various processes, techniques and methods employed in
manufacturing of semiconductors, based on silicon or compound
semiconductors like GaN, GaAs, AlGaN and MEMS devices.
The course deals with manufacturing process cycle for
semiconductors. The focus is on knowing the Global semiconductor
Industry and its impact on society. The details of various
microfabrication techniques are covered from substrate preparation
to wafer fabrication
About substrate material - knowing the material property, Silicon as a
material, Wafer Growth techniques like CZ, FZ etc, Clean room
concepts, wafer cleaning techniques.
Various fabrication techniques : Diffusion process and kinetics of
diffusion, CVD and PVD, Deposition techniques and Furnace used, Ion
Implantation techniques and equipment, Dry and wet etching, etch
profile etc, wafer bonding. |
