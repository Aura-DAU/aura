# Introduction to Language and Literature (PC110)

## Overview

This document presents the detailed policy and syllabus structure for the course Introduction to Language and Literature (PC110). It includes details on course objectives, credits, prerequisites, syllabus content, evaluation schemes, and contact information where available.

## Main Content

Course Title: Introduction to Language and Literature 
Credit Structure: (L-T-P-Cr): (3-0-0-3) 
Course Code: PC110 
Prerequisites (if any)/desired skill set: None 
Mode: Offline

Course Description: This course is designed to introduce students to the study of the English 
language and literature at the undergraduate level. It will follow a two pronged approach, first, of 
teaching English language through literature, and second, of introducing students to the world of 
literature and teaching them strategies of reading and interpreting texts. Literature offers 
perspective to readers on their own experiences and introduces them to worlds and worldviews 
alien to their understanding and experience. Serious engagement with literature can sensitize 
the reader to themselves and to the world around them, and enhance their ability to think critically 
and creatively. This course will harness the power of literature to launch students into debates 
on relationships, sexuality, family, war, media and technology among other themes relevant to 
their lives. Students will be exposed to a broad variety of texts and will be asked to engage with 
them in multiple ways through class discussions, written analysis, presentations etc.

Course Objectives: The activities will be centered around reading, speaking, listening, writing 
and critical thinking. After completion of the course, students will be able to better comprehend 
and analyze written and audio-visual texts and to respond confidently in coherent English. 
Rigorous group activities conducted in the class will ensure that they develop good 
interpersonal and public communication skills.

Texts: The instructor will circulate readings that students are supposed to get photocopied. 
Students will be expected to bring these to the classroom.

Grading Scheme: 
Mid Sem Exam : 35% 
End Sem Exam: 30% 
Group Project: 35%

Attendance Policy: Students need a minimum of 75% attendance to pass the course. Attending 
the class means having done the reading assigned for the day and being ready to respond to 
questions.

Grading Policy: Students need to have attempted both exams and have done the assignment in 
order to pass the course.

Group Project: Each student will be assigned a group consisting of 10 students. Together the 
group will be asked to work on the assignment. Every student will be expected to have 
participated at every stage of the assignment.
