Tentative Course Content 
Calculus: Limit, continuity, differentiation, application of derivatives, integration, definite 
integrals, method of substitution, integration by parts, application to finding area and 
volume, double and triple integrals, improper integrals. 
 
Matrix: 
Matrix addition, subtraction, multiplication, determinants, rank of a matrix, eigenvalues, 
eigenvectors and applications. 
 
      Vectors: Vector algebra, products of vectors, vector triple product, vector equations,

Differentiation and integration of vectors, vector calculus.

Differential Equations: 
Formation and solution of ODE, solution of first order ODEs, Linear inhomogeneous 
second order ODEs, free and forced oscillators and applications. 
Formation and solution of PDEs, Second order linear PDE, Diffusion equation and 
applications.

Expected Outcome 
The students after completing the course will get a basic overview of calculus, linear 
algebra, differential equations, complex analysis and numerical analysis. Students will get 
an insight of fundamentals of these topics and their vast applications. In some of the 
tutorials we will use some computer programming to visualize and understand the 
solutions. All these outcomes will be assessed with homework, quiz and exams.

At the end of the course the students will be able to
