# CT548 - Advanced Wireless Communication  (3-0-2-4)

## Overview

This document presents the detailed policy and syllabus structure for the course Advanced Wireless Communication CT548 (3-0-2-4) (CT548). It includes details on course objectives, credits, prerequisites, syllabus content, evaluation schemes, and contact information where available.

## Main Content

Advanced Wireless Communication CT548 (3-0-2-4)     
 
Instructor: Dr. Manish Kumar 
 
Course Placement: It is a Technical elective course for MTech first year (second semester) students and 
BTech third year (sixth semester) students. 
 
Course Pre-requisite: Fundamental understanding of courses like Probability and Statistics, Signals and 
Systems, and Digital Communication. 
 
Course format: It would be 150 minutes’ lecture and 2-hour lab session (coding) every week.  
 
Course content: The course primarily starts with the evolution of wireless technologies, followed by 
wireless channel modeling and representation. Later, important concepts like MIMO, OFDM which emerged 
as key technologies for 4G and 5G wireless communications are discussed. Important aspect of channel 
estimation for wireless communication systems will be discussed in detail. Subsequently, the Massive 
MIMO and milli-meter wave systems will be discussed for next-generation wireless communication 
systems. The prime motive of the course is to provide an understanding and background in the domain of 
wireless communication which could pave the way forward to academic research in various areas of wireless 
communication. Certain modules of the course could be helpful and serve as an important pre-requisite in 
the relevant companies working in the domain of wireless communication. 
 
 Few Text Books and Reference Books: 
 
1. “Wireless Communications”, Andrea Goldsmith, Cambridge University Press.  
  
2. “Fundamentals of Wireless Communication”, David Tse & Pramod Vishwanath, Cambridge

3. “MIMO Wireless Communications”, Ezio Biglieri et. al., Cambridge University Press.

4. “Wireless Communication Principles and Practice” T Rappaport 
 
Assessment Method: Continuous evaluation will be carried out throughout the semester with Mid-Semester 
Examination, End-Semester Examination and some weightage to the assignment. There might be a very 
small change in this assessment method but in that case it would be duly notified well in advance. 
 
Laboratory Component: Students will be given weekly problems which need to be simulated in 
MATLAB. Focus will be on the different components of the system under study. Tentatively, a report 
containing the results need to be prepared for evaluation. The students can either use the institute MATLAB 
or use a personal license. The idea is to enhance coding expertise and understanding of wireless and digital 
communication system and their modeling.   
  
 
 Course Objectives:

 After the successful completion of the course it would lay forward the foundation towards working

upon further academic research problems and assignment in the broad domain of wireless 
communication and aligned research areas.  
 
 The candidates will gain important theoretical concepts whose knowledge is mandatory for fresh

graduates working in the relevant wireless communication and associated industries. The MATLAB 
programing learnt during the practical sessions would be of prime help in correlating and better 
understanding of the fundamentals of wireless communications.  
 
 Problem Analysis and Design of Systems: The candidate would be able to utilize the concepts learnt

in the course in various principles of wireless communications like algorithm design, performance
