# IE416 -Robot (IE416)

## Overview

This document presents the detailed policy and syllabus structure for the course IE416 -Robot (IE416). It includes details on course objectives, credits, prerequisites, syllabus content, evaluation schemes, and contact information where available.

## Main Content

IE416 -Robot Programming 
 
Course Placement: Robot Programming is an ICT and Technical Elective course for 3rd year students of BTech 
ICT, RAS (Robotics and Automation Systems) minor programs. It is also offered as an elective for MTech 
program.  
 
Course format: It is 3 hours lecture and 2 hour lab every week.  
 
Prerequisites: Python Programming, C++ Programming, and Embedded System Software 
 
Course content: This course introduces students to the robot arm, autonomous car, and ROS (Robot Operating 
System) as well as numerous available tools VSCode, Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow, and GitHub. Course focus on 
theory and hands-on, whereas hands-on will focus on applications and implementations motor, camera, GPS, 
computer vision, and artificial intelligence. Students learn how to create robot model, interface to sensors and 
actuators, and integrate AI/ML algorithms for autonomous robot. The course works through exercises involving a 
number of autonomous robots (i.e., ground) that students could eventually use in their future robotics research and 
innovation. The course consists of two parts which are (i) interactive lecture section, (ii) lab exercises on ROS 
which will provide a platform to understand a real robot from scratch. 
 
Text Book: Yoon Seok Pyo, Han Cheol Cho, Ryu Woon Jung, and Tae Hoon Lim, Robot Programming (1st  
Eds.), ROBOTIS Co., Ltd. 2017, ISBN 979-11-962307-1-5 .  
 
Reference Book:

1) Learn Robotics Programming: Build and Control AI-enabled Autonomous Robots Using the Raspberry Pi

and Python by Danny Staple, (2nd Eds.), Packt Publishing, 2021 
2) Robotics, Vision and Control, Fundamental Algorithms in Python, 3rd edition, Peter Corke, Springer,

2023. 
 
Assessment method: One in-semester and a final examination, and lab assignments. Evaluation scheme: (i) 
Second In-Sem Exam: 30%, (ii) End-Sem Exam: 40%, (iii) Labs: 30%. 
 
Course Outcomes: This course will give an opportunity to students to enhance their skills in basics of with a 
custom robotic arm and autonomous car (PE1, PE2). Students will gain understanding on 7 degree of freedom 
robotic arm, self-drive car, lane assist, cruise control, local navigating, and global navigation which are required for 
future automation and transportation. Students will acquire huge skills in Ubuntu, ROS, Python, OpenCV, 
TensorFlow, and GitHub (PSO1, PSO2, PSO3, PE3). Today, world leading companies such as Google, TCS, 
Amazon, Boston Dynamics, Softbank, DENSO, Disney, HONDA, Hyundai, etc., are looking for the people who 
are expertise in computer vision, artificial intelligence and robotics. This program will provide a platform to develop 
skills for which companies are interested to hire professionals (PEO3). After successful completion of the course 
the student will have the ability to apply engineering knowledge to identify the problem using modern tools for 
social development. (PO1, PO2, PO5, PO6). Robotics which is interdisciplinary in nature, having complexity in 
development required-long term goal, need to learn ethics to use in society (PO4, PO8, PO12, PE4). Ability to 
work in a group project of 3~4students in a team (PO9, PO11, PE5)
