Course Objective

Digital signal processing hardware is an important element in real time application that enhances 
the performance of the system. This course provides various VLSI architectures used in digital 
signal processing such as discrete orthogonal transformations, digital filtering etc. The course 
aims to provide advanced understanding of hardware designs of various Digital Signal Processing 
operations.  
 
 
Course Outcome: At the end of the course, students will be able to:

• 
Understand the hardware and design concepts of DSP filtering techniques and 
transformations and their circuit implementations
