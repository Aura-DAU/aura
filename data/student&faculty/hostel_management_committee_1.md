# Hostel Management Committee

## Hostel Rules and Regulations (Behavior and Discipline)

1. **Peace and Tranquillity:** The residents must ensure peace and tranquillity within the Halls of Residence area so that they do not disturb other residents. 
2. **Social Ethics and Noise:** The residents will behave according to social ethics and respect the privacy of others at all times. Loud/Noisy music disrupting the peace of others will not be allowed in the Halls of Residence.
3. **Harassment:** Men will avoid unwanted attention-seeking behaviour towards Women that can constitute as 'harassment'. 
4. **Alcohol, Gambling, and Illegal Substances:** Consumption or possession of alcoholic drinks, usage of any form of intoxicating materials, and all forms of gambling or illegal activities in the Halls of Residence are strictly prohibited. Prohibition is enforced in the state of Gujarat. The case will be referred to the disciplinary action committee (DAC), and strict action including termination from the Institute will be taken against a student as per DAC policy if he/she is found consuming or possessing alcohol or gambling.
5. **Smoking Policy:** The entire campus of DAU / DA-IICT is a no-smoking zone. Strict action will be taken against a student as per DAC policy if he/she is found smoking. 
   - First offence: A fine of Rs. 500, and 10 hours of community service and a letter will be sent to the parents.
   - Second offence: Expulsion from the hostel for one semester.
   - Third offence: The matter will be reported to DAC, which will take strict disciplinary action, which may include termination from the Institute.
6. **Gender Segregation:** Entry is prohibited for men and women into the other gender's HOR. Men are not allowed inside HOR Women. Women are not allowed inside HOR Men.
7. **Room Switching Policy:** Residents are not permitted to change rooms or exchange any of the Halls of Residence property without the approval of the Hostel Supervisor. This is a punishable offense, and you will be asked to return to your officially allotted room immediately, and you are likely to face rigorous fines and disciplinary action. Residents must get approval from the Hostel Supervisor to shift their room officially. 
8. **Anti-Ragging Policy:** Ragging is a criminal offence, and it is strictly prohibited under Central Government Act. If anyone suspects or comes to know about it, or becomes a victim of ragging inside or outside the campus, please contact any of the members of the Anti-Ragging Vigilance Committee. One can immediately contact the Resident Warden. Details of this Committee are uploaded on the institute's website. Strict disciplinary action will be taken if a student is found ragging another student. This may lead to termination from the Institute and legal action. 
9. **Dress Code:** Outside the rooms, the Residents will not be dressed in a manner that would offend anyone within or outside the Complex. 
10. **Appropriate Language:** Residents will not use loud or abusive/offensive/inciting language while conveying their viewpoints. 
11. **Unsafe Areas:** Residents and visitors are not permitted to sit on parapets or railings or stair cases or any other unsafe area.
12. **Vulgar Content:** Inside the room, vulgar/cheap posters, text, or drawings displaying vulgarity will not be permitted on the walls or elsewhere in the room. 
13. **Mail Disposal:** Residents must check the Snail Mail link at this hostel website daily. Mails that are older than 5-10 days will be disposed of by the Hostel Supervisor. 
14. **Unauthorized Guests Fine:** A minimum fine of Rs.1000/- per day would be charged to both occupants if anyone else stays in a resident's room without the permission of the Hostel Supervisor. This is in addition to the guest charge of Rs. 100-200 per day.
15. **Electrical Switches:** All electrical switches and gadgets must be switched off while leaving the room. 
16. **Drying Clothes:** Drying clothes on the room window and staircase railing is strictly prohibited. Drying of clothes should be done at the place designated for the same, e.g., the clotheslines provided in the designated areas. 
17. **Outside Food/Plates Policy:** Residents cannot bring food or food plates inside the Halls of Residence. If any student, Hostel Supervisor, or any administrative authority finds a student carrying food/plates inside the Halls of Residence, they will be fined Rs.1000. This amount should be paid within 72 hours to the Hostel Supervisor, and the receipt for the same should be collected from the Supervisor. If the student fails to pay the penalty within the stipulated time, the warden will forward this case to the Disciplinary Committee for appropriate disciplinary action. 
18. **Food for Sick Students:** A sick student (certified by a doctor) can get food in the room. To bring food for a sick student, the sick student or his friends must inform the Hostel Supervisor in advance. 
19. **Permitted Appliances:** Residents are permitted to use computers, table lamps, and Audio Systems. Hostel Supervisor should be informed in writing if a resident wishes to keep any of these items in the room.
20. **Internet Access:** Men’s Halls of residences are wi-fi enabled. Women’s Halls of residences have lan connectivity in the rooms.
21. **Prohibited High-Power Appliances:** Use of high power-consuming electrical equipment is strictly prohibited. The following items are not allowed: irons, heaters, ovens, refrigerators, TVs, and kettles. Keeping such gadgets in your room is against the rules and highly dangerous. Any resident with the same in his/her room will be severely punished. 
22. **Cooking Policy:** No cooking is permitted in the Halls of Residence. 
23. **Furniture Transfer:** Residents should not transfer furniture from one room to another.
24. **Rule Breach Fines:** A minimum fine of Rs.1000/- will be applicable in case of breach of any rules of the Halls of Residence, and it may increase depending on the severity of the action. The repeated offense or punishable offense would be reported to the disciplinary action committee.

## Room Allotment Procedures

1. **Allotment Authority:** The allotment of rooms for the new students will be done by the Wardens. 
2. **Room Changes:** No requests for a change of rooms will be entertained until the end of the first year.
3. **Right to Withdraw:** The Dean of Students, Wardens, Hostel Supervisors, and Management reserve the right to allot/re-allot and withdraw the accommodation provided. 
4. **Eligibility:** The allotment will only be made to a registered student of DAU / DA-IICT. 
5. **Vacating Rooms:** The resident has to vacate the room within five days of his/her ceasing to be a student of DAU / DA-IICT. 
6. **UG Duration Limit:** A UG student will be allotted a room only for the first four years of stay on campus. If a student cannot complete his degree requirements within that period, a room will be allotted to him/her, subject to availability only. 
7. **Outstation Students:** Hostellers from outside Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar districts must give complete contact details of at least two guardians/relatives, preferably from Ahmedabad or Gandhinagar, at the time of room allocation process.

## Hostel Timings & Leave Procedures

1. **Curfew Timing:** All Halls of Residence residents must return to the campus by 10:30 PM - 12:00 AM (midnight depending on specific hall rules). If a resident is required to stay beyond these hours, a special permission has to be sought through email, at least 24 hours in advance, from the Hostel Supervisor.
2. **Night Restrictions:** No resident student will be allowed outside the campus from curfew till 6 AM. An exception will be made only in the case of medical emergencies. An entry needs to be made in the register maintained at the Security gate.
3. **Leave Intimation:** The residents are responsible for emailing the Hostel Supervisor (prior intimation) if they intend to stay away from the Halls of Residence (even if it is for one night). However, if the absence includes missing of the academic curriculum, then specific permission must be obtained from Dean Academic Program.
4. **Leave Form:** A google form is also provided where Residents have to fill the expected date of return, place to visit (if it's not their home), guardian's contact number etc.

## Vehicles

1. **Four-Wheelers:** Students are NOT allowed to keep 4-wheelers in the campus (cars, jeeps etc are NOT allowed).
2. **Two-Wheelers:** Students are allowed to keep 2-wheelers on campus (bicycles, scooters, motorcycles etc). Each student is allowed to keep a maximum of one vehicle only.
3. **Parking and Traffic Rules:** Parking of vehicles must be done in authorized parking areas ONLY. Students must obey all traffic rules, within the campus. This includes wearing a helmet at all times, driving within speed limit, max of two persons on a vehicle etc. If a student is found violating any traffic rule, disciplinary action will be taken against the student.
   - First offence: This will include a monetary fine and a few hours of community service. A letter will also be sent to the parents.
   - Second offence: The student will not be allowed to keep a vehicle in the campus, for the remaining part of his/her stay at the Institute. Depending upon the severity of the offence, the student may also be expelled from the hostel.

## Maintenance & Complaint Procedures

1. **Cleanliness:** Residents are individually responsible for keeping their rooms, bath rooms/toilets neat and clean. The common areas too must be kept clean and tidy at all times and residents should avoid littering the place. The Institute will undertake the housekeeping of common areas only. 
2. **Resource Conservation:** Residents will be responsible for adhering to the electricity, water etc. conservation plans announced from time to time. Any misuse observed or detected will result in an imposition of appropriate fine. This may be for the whole floor of the affected wing(s). 
3. **Complaint Register:** Complaints should be written in the concerned register only. The HMC members of the respective floors/wings or the Hostel Supervisor should be informed by the students through emails or personally for problems pertaining to water, electricity, furniture etc. If a problem remains un-resolved for reasonably long time, the students can escalate the matter by reporting it to the respective warden.
4. **Damage Reporting:** Any damage to Halls of Residence property must be reported immediately to the Hostel Supervisor. 
5. **Spot Checks:** The Institute reserves the right to make spot checks of the rooms without prior notice to the students. Every effort will be made to respect the privacy and dignity of the residents. 
6. **Structural Modifications:** Drilling and other civil works, which may alter the structure of the Halls of Residence, are strictly prohibited. 
7. **Room Inventory:** All rooms will have their own fixed inventory, copy of the same will be handed over to the occupant who will be responsible for their maintenance in good condition. 
8. **Private Furniture:** No private items of furniture are allowed in the Halls of Residence. However, students may bring their personal furniture, if advised by a doctor, by producing medical certificate to the Hostel Supervisor. 
9. **Personal Belongings:** The residents are expected to bring mattress, pillow, linen and personal clothing.

## Visitors

1. **Visitor Restrictions and Timings:** Visitors are NOT allowed into the Halls of Residence Complex except between 8.00 am to 10.00 p.m. Cafeteria is a suggested place to meet visitors. Male visitors are not allowed in Halls of Residence, Women. Female visitors are not allowed in Halls of Residence, Men.
2. **Guest Room Availability:** Guest Room in both Halls of Residences will be available only for medical emergencies. To avail of this facility: 
   - **Permission:** The students must get the permission from the Hostel Supervisor. 
   - **Rent Charges:** The rent should be paid in advance to the Hostel Supervisor. Rent for the non-resident DAU / DA-IICT students (who will stay for a short period) is Rs. 100-200 per person per day. Rent for the outside visitors, including parents is 250 INR per person per day (inclusive of GST). However, the Registrar's Office may take a call on a case-to-case basis. 
3. **Unauthorized Visitor Fine:** Any resident found entertaining a visitor without prior intimation or booking will be liable to be fined a minimum of Rs.1000/-in addition to the guest charge for the duration of stay. 
4. **Special Permission:** Parents and Guardians wishing to visit at other times should obtain special permission from the Hostel Supervisor. 
5. **Overnight Stay:** Visitors are not allowed to stay overnight in the Halls of Residence except in the case of a medical emergency. 
6. **Non-Resident Students:** All students who are non-resident of Halls of Residence must: 
   - **Registration:** Inform the Hostel Supervisor first for registration through your course instructor or guide. 
   - **Duration:** Mention the exact period of stay. 
   - **Rent:** Pay Rs. 100-200 towards rent per day during their stay. 
7. **Security Monitoring:** A copy of the list of temporary residents of the Halls of Residence will be given to the Security Supervisor for monitoring purpose. 
8. **Overstay Reporting:** The Security personnel will report to the Hostel Supervisor if he finds some students staying beyond the registered time. 
9. **Availability Basis:** Rooms for temporary stay will be provided based on the availability. 
10. **Temporary Resident Rules:** Once a student is permitted to stay for a short-period, the student will become the temporary resident of the Halls of Residence and hence must follow all the rules and regulations of the Halls of Residence applicable to other residents.

## Security

1. **Property Liability:** The Institute is not responsible for loss or damage of any personal property.
2. **Locking Belongings:** Student must keep their belongings under lock and key.
3. **Locking Rooms:** Students must lock their room before leaving, even if it is for a few minutes.
4. **Valuables:** Residents must avoid keeping any valuables in Halls of Residence, like gold ornaments, large amount of cash, etc.
5. **Missing Roommates:** Any student, who finds his/her room mate(s) missing during the night, must intimate the Hostel Supervisor immediately.
6. **Key Loss:** Each Resident will be given one key to his / her room. In case of loss of the original key a fine of Rs. 200/- will be charged along with the cost of a new lock.
7. **Personal Locks:** The residents are not allowed to use their own locks.
8. **Lock Damage:** In case of any damage to the provided lock, the resident must inform to the hostel supervisor and should get a new lock issued by paying a fine of Rs. 200/- along with the cost of the new lock. If a resident fails to inform the Hostel Supervisor about it and is found using his/her own lock, he/she would be charged a fine of Rs. 1000.
9. **Profile Updates:** Residents must update their profile (like phone numbers and addresses of parents and guardian) whenever there is a change in the same.
10. **SRS Photos:** Residents must ensure that their photographs have been uploaded on SRS.

## Computer Policy

1. **Registration Requirement:** It is mandatory for all residents of Hall of Residences that they register their computer or laptop within 7 days of bringing it into the campus.
2. **Proof of Ownership:** For Registering Desktop/Laptop, you need to contact Hostel Supervisor with the proof of ownership, like original bill.
3. **Security Entry:** While bringing a computer/laptop into the campus, a resident must make an entry at the gate (in a register kept with security guard). Failing to do so will incur a fine of Rs 1000/-.
4. **Unregistered Devices:** Any resident found keeping a laptop/desktop unregistered, (s)he will not be allowed to keep a laptop/desktop for that and/or next semester.
5. **First Year Restrictions:** It should also be noted that first year students are NOT allowed to have a laptop.

## Pets

1. **No Pets Allowed:** NO PETS are allowed in the Halls of Residence.
2. **Stray Animals:** Residents must not encourage stray cats/dogs. They should inform security guard in case they find any.

## Laundry Services

1. **Washing Machines:** Haier-branded washing machines have been installed at designated points across the hostel for shared use by all residents. Centralized locations ensure accessibility for all students.
2. **Usage Process:** Residents can avail the service by scanning the QR code on the machine, making the payment online, booking a slot through the app, and entering the OTP received to start the wash cycle - ensuring a contactless, cashless, hygienic, and queue-free experience.
3. **Drying Clothes:** Drying of clothes should be done at the place designated for the same, e.g., the clotheslines provided in the designated areas.

## Hostel Management & Emergency Contacts

1. **Rule Revisions:** The Hostel Management Committee (HMC) reserves the right to revise the Rules and Regulations from time to time and will keep the residents informed of any changes.
2. **Disciplinary Actions:** The students found violating any of the Halls of Residence rules will be referred to Disciplinary Committee for appropriate punitive action. Those involved in serious breaches will be asked to vacate the Halls of Residence.
3. **Floor Representatives:** Annual election will be held for floor representatives of the Halls of Residence.
4. **Committee Members:** The Hostel Management Committee comprises of: 
    - Dean Students
    - Warden: Halls of Residence, Men
    - Warden: Halls of Residence, Women
    - Hostel Supervisor
    - Resident Wardens (Students)
