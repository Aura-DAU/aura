# Arduino Based Embedded System Design

Online certificate course under the Anchor Institute Program (AIP), 2024-25.

## Overview

- **Dates:** October 5, 6, 12, 13, and 19, 2024 (weekends), 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM
- **Mode:** Online (Google Meet link sent to eligible participants)
- **Course Duration:** 40 hours
- **Target Audience:** Professionals, Faculties, Ph.D. Scholars, PG and Final year UG Students
- **Course Fee:** ₹5,000, fully refundable for Gujarat-state participants (non-refundable for candidates from other states)
- **Refund Policy:** Maintain 75% minimum attendance
- **Certificate:** Participation certificate for ≥75% attendance
- **Registration Deadline:** 27th September 2024

## 1. Course Overview

Provides a thorough understanding of embedded systems, focused on the Arduino platform, emphasizing hands-on hardware interfacing, programming, and real-world application development. Prepares participants for roles in IoT, robotics, automation, and electronics design.

## 2. Course Objectives

- In-depth knowledge of embedded systems architecture
- Proficiency in hardware-software integration using Arduino
- Skills in designing, prototyping, and implementing embedded systems projects

## 3. Expected Outcomes

1. Understanding of embedded systems architecture and microcontrollers
2. Hardware-software integration experience (sensors, actuators, peripherals)
3. Proficiency in Arduino programming (functions, libraries, debugging)
4. Design and prototyping skills through project-based learning
5. Problem-solving and innovation skills
6. Applications across home automation, environmental monitoring, robotics, IoT
