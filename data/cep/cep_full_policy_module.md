## Anchor Institute Program (AIP)

DA-IICT is selected as an Anchor Institute by CED (Department of Industries, Govt. of Gujarat) in ICT specialization.
- **Scope:** 5-year program; DA-IICT faculty train faculty from nodal institutes across Gujarat
- **Past AIP topics:** OS, Digital/Analog Communication, Image Processing using VLSI, Multicore Processor Architecture, Data Structures, Software Engineering, .Net/C#, Embedded Systems

## Application Procedure (Faculty Proposing a Course)

1. Submit **Intent for Proposal (IFP)** form
2. Submit **Course Plan Proposal** for full review
3. CEPC handles marketing, registration, logistics, certificates
