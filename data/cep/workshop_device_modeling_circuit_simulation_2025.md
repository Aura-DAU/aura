# Workshop on "Device Modeling and Circuit Simulation for Integrated Circuit Designs"

Workshop under the Anchor Institute Program (AIP), 2025-26.

## Overview

- **Dates:** 09.06.2025 to 13.06.2025
- **Venue:** Dhirubhai Ambani University (formerly DA-IICT)
- **Course Duration:** 40 hours (20 hours theory + 20 hours lab)
- **Target Audience:** Professionals, Faculties, Ph.D. Scholars, PG and Final year UG Students
- **Course Fee:** ₹5,000 for Gujarat-state participants (non-refundable for candidates from other states); fee collected after shortlisting
- **Accommodation:** Complimentary accommodation, tea/coffee, and lunch
- **Refund Policy:** Maintain 75% minimum attendance
- **Registration Deadline:** 1st June 2025

## 1. Course Objective

Delivers fundamentals of electronic devices (BJTs, MOSFETs) for digital/analog circuit and system design; SPICE circuit simulation (commands, analyses, schematic realization, netlist extraction, layout-based design); device analytical formulation using MATLAB correlated with SPICE; modeling/simulation of next-generation graphene-derived FETs and interconnects.

## 2. Expected Outcome

- Design and analyze circuits for various applications; support research projects and industrial needs
- Hands-on training in simulative modeling, analytical formulation, and their correlation
- Understand VLSI system design process flow for integrated circuits

- **Dr. Yash Agrawal** — Faculty, DAU. Specialization: VLSI, Nanotechnology, AI/Models for VLSI, FDTD, device modeling/simulation, high-speed on-chip VLSI interconnects, NoC design, flexible electronics, image processing using FPGA.
