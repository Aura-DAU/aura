# Workshop on "Human Experiences through Interaction Design"

Workshop under the Anchor Institute Program (AIP), 2024-25.

## Overview

- **Dates:** 26.07.2024 to 31.08.2024
- **Mode:** Online, sessions on Fridays and Saturdays
- **Course Duration:** 35 hours (23 hours theory + 12 hours lab)
- **Target Audience:** UI/UX Professionals, Faculties, PG students, Senior UG students
- **Course Fee:** ₹5,000 for Gujarat-state participants (non-refundable for candidates from other states); fee collection deferred until shortlisting is complete
- **Refund Policy:** Maintain 75% minimum attendance
- **Certificate:** Participation certificate for ≥75% attendance
- **Registration Deadline:** 16th July 2024

## 1. Course Objective

Two fundamental modules: (1) basic human-computer interaction — the three components of HCI (human, machine, interface), human capabilities, design principles, and interaction models; (2) Interaction Design — design strategies, research methods, evaluating design research, prototyping, and evaluation techniques, covering motion, space, sound, and image.

## 2. Expected Outcome

- Understand fundamental HCI principles and components
- Apply design principles and interaction models
- Develop interaction designs incorporating motion, space, sound, image
- Employ design strategies and research methods
- Evaluate design research and apply prototyping/evaluation techniques
- Solve real-world problems and build prototypes
