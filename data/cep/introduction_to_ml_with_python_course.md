# Online Certificate Course on "Introduction to ML with Python"

Course under the Anchor Institute Program (AIP), 2023-24.

## Overview

- **Funded by:** The Centre for Entrepreneurship Development (CED), Government of Gujarat
- **Organized by:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Mode:** Online
- **Tentative Start Date:** 05th January 2024
- **Schedule:** Weekends (Friday and Saturday evenings, IST)
- **Course Duration:** 40 hours (22 hours theory + 18 hours lab)
- **Target Audience:** Faculty members, Professionals, PG students, Senior UG students
- **Course Fee:** ₹5,000, fully refundable
- **Refund Policy:** Maintain 75% minimum attendance

## About the Course

Familiarizes participants with basic machine learning concepts, probability theory, random variables, matrix computations, and optimization. Covers digital image processing, deep neural networks (DNN), and concludes with adversarial machine learning.

## Topics Covered

1. Mathematical Preliminaries
2. Introduction to Digital Image Processing
3. Supervised Learning
4. Unsupervised Learning
5. Deep Neural Networks
6. Adversarial ML
