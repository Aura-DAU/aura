# Workshop on "Speech and Audio Signal Processing using FPGA"

Workshop under the Anchor Institute Program (AIP), 2025-26.

## Overview

- **Dates:** 16.06.2025 to 20.06.2025
- **Venue:** Dhirubhai Ambani University (formerly DA-IICT)
- **Course Duration:** 40 hours (20 hours theory + 20 hours lab)
- **Target Audience:** Professionals, Faculties, Ph.D. Scholars, PG and Final year UG Students
- **Course Fee:** ₹5,000 for Gujarat-state participants (non-refundable for candidates from other states); fee collected after shortlisting
- **Accommodation:** Complimentary accommodation, tea/coffee, and lunch
- **Refund Policy:** Maintain 75% minimum attendance
- **Registration Deadline:** 8th June 2025

## 1. Course Objective

Fundamental algorithms in speech/audio processing and corresponding hardware accelerators implemented on FPGA — convolution, hardware elements (adder, delay, multiplier), difference equations, 2nd-order digital resonators (biquad circuits), FFT architectures, spectrograms, sigma-delta ADC design, sample-and-hold circuits. Hands-on training in MATLAB/Python, Verilog HDL, and FPGA.

## 2. Expected Outcome

- Basic understanding of audio/speech processing
- HDL (Verilog) skills for FPGA-based embedded system boards
- Hands-on training with FPGA boards, MATLAB, Python, Verilog
- Hardware accelerator and neural-network conceptualization for audio/speech processing
- Exposure to real-time project development

## 3. Organizers and Course Instructors

- **Dr. Yash Agrawal** — Faculty, DAU. VLSI, Nanotechnology, AI/Models for VLSI, device modeling/simulation, high-speed VLSI interconnects, NoC design, image processing using FPGA. Postdoc, University of Rennes, France.
- **Dr. Hemant A. Patil** — Ph.D., IIT Kharagpur (2006). Faculty at DAU since 2007; developed the Speech Research Lab (ISCA-recognized speech lab). ~226 publications; PI on MeitY-sponsored ASR/TTS/QbE-STD projects; co-PI on DST India-Digital Heritage (Hampi) project. Research interests: speech/speaker recognition, TTS, infant cry analysis.
