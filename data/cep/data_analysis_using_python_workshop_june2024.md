# Workshop on "Data Analysis using Python" (24–28 June 2024)

Workshop under the Anchor Institute Program (AIP).

## Overview

- **Dates:** 24.06.2024 to 28.06.2024
- **Venue:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Course Duration:** 40 hours (20 hours theory + 20 hours lab)
- **Target Audience:** Professionals, Faculties, Ph.D. Scholars, PG and Final year UG Students
- **Course Fee:** ₹5,000, fully refundable for Gujarat-state participants (non-refundable for candidates from other states)
- **Accommodation:** Complimentary accommodation, breakfast, lunch, and snacks (accommodation currently available only for male participants)
- **Refund Policy:** Maintain 75% minimum attendance
- **Certificate:** Participation certificate for ≥75% attendance
- **Registration Deadline:** 14th June 2024

## 1. Course Overview

Same subject matter as the July 2024 offering: machine learning and data science applications across engineering disciplines, artificial neural networks, deep learning, statistical/ML approaches to data analytics.

## 2–3. Objectives & Expected Outcome

Identical framing to the July 2024 offering — foundational Python scripting, real-world ML application design, project-based learning, career development.
