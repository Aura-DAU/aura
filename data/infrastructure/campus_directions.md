# Exhaustive DA-IICT Campus Navigation and Walking Directions

**Last Modified:** Unknown

## Overview

This document presents the course syllabus, policy guidelines, and lecture breakdown for Exhaustive DA-IICT Campus Navigation and Walking Directions.

## Main Content

* **To Library (F):** Enter Main Gate, walk straight to the circular Lotus Point (LP). Take the red-brick pedestrian path curving East (right). Walk past Faculty Blocks 1, 2, 3, and 4 on your right. The Library is on the left.
* **To LAB Building (H):** Enter Main Gate to the Lotus Point (LP). Take the direct pedestrian path curving slightly left (North-West). The large central LAB building will be right in front of you.
* **To CEP Building (G):** Walk straight North past the Lotus Point (LP), taking the path between the LAB (left) and Library (right). CEP is directly North of the Lotus Point (LP).
* **To Lecture Theatres 1, 2, 3 (I, J, K):** From Lotus Point (LP), take the far-left (West) pedestrian path. Follow it along the South side of the LAB building. The LTs are clustered at the end of this path on the West boundary.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** From Lotus Point (LP), you can either take the West Peripheral Road (for vehicles) all the way North, OR walk straight through the campus core passing the LAB and CEP, continuing North-West on the red pathways until reaching the Boys Hostel complex.
* **To Girls Hostel (Q) & SAC-2 (P):** Immediately upon entering the Main Gate, turn right (East) onto the yellow peripheral vehicle road. Follow it past the Faculty Blocks and Library. The Girls Hostel and SAC-2 are on the far East side.
* **To Admin Block (A):** Walk to the Library via the Lotus Point (LP). Once at the Library, take the path heading North. The Admin block is just North of Faculty Block-4 (B).
* **To Canteen (M), Food Court (N), SAC-1 (O) & OAT:** Walk straight North through the campus core from the Lotus Point (LP), passing the LAB and CEP. Continue North on the pedestrian paths to reach the Canteen hub.
* **To Playground & Sports Courts:** Take the East peripheral road all the way North, or walk through the campus core past the Canteen, heading East towards the large circular ground.
* **To Bungalows (R):** Enter Main Gate, take an immediate right on the East peripheral road. The Bungalows are in the South-East corner before the Girls Hostel.

## 2. Routes FROM the Library (F)
* **To LTs (I, J, K):** Head South-West across the central gardens towards the Lotus Point (LP). Take the path heading West, passing the South face of the LAB building to reach the LTs.
* **To LAB Building (H):** Walk West through the central gardens. The LAB is directly across from the Library on the other side of the central greenery.
* **To CEP (G):** Walk a short distance North-West along the connecting paths. CEP is adjacent to the Library.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** Walk West towards the LAB, then turn North-West, following the winding paths past the North face of the LAB to reach the Boys Hostel.
* **To Girls Hostel (Q) & SAC-2 (P):** Exit East, crossing the East Peripheral Road. The Girls Hostel complex is directly East of the Library area.
* **To Canteen (M) & SAC-1 (O):** Walk North-West, passing CEP. Follow the path North past the Open Air Theatre to reach the Canteen hub.
* **To Admin Block (A):** Walk directly North. The Admin block is immediately North of the adjacent Faculty blocks.
* **To Playground:** Walk North past the Admin block and CEP, continuing North-East towards the large circular sports ground.

## 3. Routes FROM the LAB Building (H)
* **To Library (F):** Walk East across the central green core. The Library is directly opposite the LAB.
* **To LTs (I, J, K):** Exit from the South-West side of the LAB and walk West along the pedestrian path. The LTs are adjacent to the West of the LAB.
* **To CEP (G):** Exit from the North-East side of the LAB. The CEP building is directly East/North-East of the LAB.
* **To Canteen (M), Food Court (N) & OAT:** Exit from the North side of the LAB and walk straight North on the red-brick pathways.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** Exit from the North-West side of the LAB. The Boys Hostel is directly North-West from the LAB.
* **To Admin Block (A):** Walk East past CEP and the Library, then head North.
* **To Girls Hostel (Q):** Walk East across the entire campus core, past the Library, and cross the East Peripheral Road.

## 4. Routes FROM Lecture Theatres (I, J, K)
* **To Library (F):** Walk East along the main pedestrian path, passing the South face of the LAB building. Curve left through the gardens to reach the Library.
* **To LAB Building (H):** The LAB is immediately East of the LTs. Just walk East.
* **To CEP (G):** Walk East past the LAB, then head North towards the CEP building.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** Walk directly North. The Boys Hostel is immediately North of the LT cluster. You can use the West Peripheral Road or the internal pathway.
* **To Canteen (M) & OAT:** Walk North from the LTs along the West side of the LAB, then turn East towards the Northern hub.
* **To Lotus Point (LP) / Main Gate:** Walk East along the path passing the South side of the LAB until you reach the Lotus Point (LP), then head South to the gate.

## 5. Routes FROM CEP Building (G)
* **To Library (F):** Walk South-East. The Library is right next door.
* **To LAB Building (H):** Walk West/South-West across the central greenery.
* **To LTs (I, J, K):** Walk South-West past the LAB, then continue West.
* **To Canteen (M) & SAC-1 (O):** Walk straight North. The Canteen and SAC-1 are immediately North of CEP.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** Walk North-West along the pathways.
* **To Admin Block (A):** Walk East directly from the CEP building to reach the Admin block (A).

## 6. Routes FROM Boys Hostel (L)
* **To LTs (I, J, K):** Walk straight South along the West side of campus. The LTs are directly South.
* **To LAB Building (H):** Walk South-East along the internal pathways.
* **To Canteen (M) & Food Court (N):** Walk East along the Northern boundary of the campus to reach the Canteen hub.
* **To Library (F):** Walk South-East, passing North of the LAB, until you reach the Library on the East side of the core.
* **To Playground:** Walk East across the entire Northern boundary, passing the Canteen and SAC-1, to reach the Sports facilities.
* **To STP/RO Plant (S):** Walk slightly North. The plant is in the extreme North-West corner next to the hostel.

## 7. Routes FROM Girls Hostel (Q) & SAC-2 (P)
* **To Library (F):** Walk West, crossing the East Peripheral Road. The Library is straight ahead.
* **To Faculty Blocks (B, C, D, E):** Walk West across the peripheral road and head South-West.
* **To LTs (I, J, K):** Walk West across the entire campus core, past the Library, Lotus Point (LP), and South of the LAB.
* **To Canteen (M) & SAC-1 (O):** Walk North-West, passing the Playground and Open Air Theatre.
* **To Admin Block (A):** Walk West across the peripheral road, then head North.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** Walk West across the entire campus, passing North of the Library and LAB.

## 8. Routes FROM Canteen (M), Food Court (N), SAC-1 (O) & Open Air Theatre
* **To LTs (I, J, K):** Walk South-West, passing the North side of the LAB, then turning South along its West face.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** Walk directly West along the Northern boundary.
* **To Library (F):** Walk South-East, passing the CEP building.
* **To LAB Building (H):** Walk straight South.
* **To Girls Hostel (Q):** Walk South-East, passing the Playground, and cross the East Peripheral Road.
* **To Playground:** Walk directly East from SAC-1.

## 9. Routes FROM Admin (A) & Faculty Blocks (B, C, D, E)
* **To LTs (I, J, K):** From Faculty Blocks (E, D, C), walk South-West to the Lotus Point (LP), then continue West past the South face of the LAB (H). From Admin (A) and Faculty-4 (B), walk West past the CEP building (G) and the LAB (H), then head South-West to the LTs.
* **To Canteen (M):** Walk North-West past the CEP building.
* **To Boys Hostel (L):** Walk North-West across the campus core, past CEP and North of the LAB.
* **To Girls Hostel (Q):** Walk East across the East Peripheral Road.
* **To Main Gate:** Walk South towards the Lotus Point (LP), then South to the gate.

## 10. To Sports Facilities (Playground, Basketball, Volleyball)
* **Location:** Extreme North-East corner of the campus.
* **From Main Gate:** Drive/walk North along the entire East Peripheral Road.
* **From Hostels:** From Boys Hostel, walk East across the Northern boundary. From Girls Hostel, walk North along the East Peripheral Road.
* **From Core (Library/CEP):** Walk North-East towards the top right corner.

## 11. To Utility Locations & Parking
* **Parking Zones:** Major parking is available immediately upon entering the Main Gate. Take the East or West Peripheral Roads to access the long stretches of designated parking zones wrapping the outer campus.
* **STP & RO Plant (S):** Located in the extreme North-West corner. Reach it by going North past the Boys Hostel.
* **DG Room / Sub Station (T):** Located in the extreme South-West corner. Reach it by taking the West Peripheral Road from the Main Gate and heading to the South-West corner parking area.
* **Bungalows (R):** Located in the extreme South-East corner. Reach them by taking the East Peripheral Road from the Main Gate and heading right (South-East).
