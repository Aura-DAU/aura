## 5. Group Mediclaim Insurance Coverage

All students of DAU are covered under a Group Mediclaim Insurance Policy:

- **Insurance Company:** Reliance General Insurance Company
- **Coverage Amount:** ₹40,000/- per student per annum
- **Facility:** Cashless hospitalisation (subject to hospital tie-up)
- **Card Generation:** Online at [Reliance General Insurance Portal](https://www.reliancegeneral.co.in/insurance/self-help/online-ecard.aspx)
- **Who is covered:** All students of DAU

Students can avail **cashless in-patient medical treatment** in hospitals approved by Reliance General Insurance Company. In cases where cashless treatment is not available, **reimbursement claims** may be submitted to DAU via email along with valid bills and authorised documents.

> **Note:** The older policy stated coverage of ₹2.50 lakh per annum. The current SOP (2025-26) specifies **₹40,000/- per student**. Use the SOP figure as the authoritative source.

## 9. SOP for Medical Emergencies

### Step-by-Step Protocol

1. **Initial Assessment:** Student approaches on-campus nurse for primary medical assessment and initial treatment.
2. **Medical Decision:** Nurse determines whether a doctor visit is sufficient or hospital referral is needed.
3. **Emergency Transfer:** If immediate attention is required beyond on-campus facilities, nurse (in coordination with Security Supervisor) initiates ambulance services without delay.
4. **Critical Cases:** Student accompanied by nurse and/or Hostel Supervisor/Warden; receiving hospital informed in advance.
5. **Outside Nurse Hours:** Hostel Supervisor/Warden arranges first response and coordinates ambulance services.
6. **Reporting:** Security Supervisor and Hostel Supervisor report incident to Warden, Manager (Administration), and Dean (Students).

### Definition of Medical Emergency

A medical emergency refers to any illness or injury posing an **immediate risk to life or health**, requiring urgent medical intervention.

Examples: Electric shock, road accidents, poisoning, drug overdose, severe burns, haemorrhage, chest pain, snake/animal bite, food poisoning, asphyxia, hyperthermia, bone fractures, etc.

## 10. Insurance Claim Process

### Cashless Claims
- Present Medi-claim card at an empanelled hospital.
- Hospital processes cashless authorization with Reliance General Insurance.

### Reimbursement Claims
- Submit valid bills and authorised documents to DAU via email.
- Claims processed through Reliance General Insurance.

### Claim Assistance Channels
- **Self-service:** Reliance General Insurance portal
- **WhatsApp:** Claim support via WhatsApp
- **Call Centre:** Phone-based claim assistance
