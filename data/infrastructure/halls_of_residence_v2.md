# Halls of Residence – Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)

## Overview

DAU (formerly DA-IICT) has **three Halls of Residence (HoRs)** on campus — two for Men and one for Women.

**Hostel accommodation on campus is only for B.Tech programs. For other UG/PG programs, allocation is subject to availability and governed by the Hostel Rules and Regulations.**

---

## 1. Halls of Residence – Overview

| HoR | Wings | Capacity | Occupancy Type |
|-----|-------|----------|----------------|
| New Men's HoR | 4 wings: L, M, P, R | 476 residents | Triple and double occupancy rooms |
| Men's HoR | 8 wings: A to H (~60 rooms/wing) | ~1025 students | Twin sharing basis |
| Women's HoR | 2 wings: J and K | 400 residents | Twin sharing rooms |

**Total Men's capacity:** ~1500 residents (combined both Men's HoRs)

## 6. Guest Room / Visitor Facilities

- The **Women's HoR has a guest room** available for **medical emergencies** (specifically for mother's stay during medical issues).
- Guest rooms in **both HoRs** are available only for medical emergencies.
- To avail the Guest Room facility:
  - Students must get permission from the Hostel Supervisor.
  - Rent must be paid in advance.

### Guest Room Charges

| Guest Type | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| Non-resident DA-IICT students (short stay) | ₹200 per person per day |
| Outside visitors (including parents) | ₹250 per person per day (inclusive of GST) |

> Any resident found entertaining a visitor **without prior intimation** will be fined a minimum of ₹1000/- in addition to guest charges for the duration of stay.

## 10. Rules & Discipline Highlights

- Ragging, gambling, consumption of alcoholic drinks and smoking are **strictly prohibited**.
- All HoR residents must return to campus by **midnight (12 AM)**.
- No resident is allowed outside campus from **midnight till 6 AM** (except medical emergencies).
- Students are **NOT allowed** to keep 4-wheelers (cars, jeeps) on campus.
- Students **can** keep 2-wheelers (bicycles, scooters, motorcycles) — maximum 1 vehicle per student.
- Minimum fine of ₹1000/- for breach of any hostel rules.

## 12. Hostel FAQs (Official)

**Q: How are the hostels of DAU?**
DAU has Men's Halls of Residence with capacity of almost 1500 residents (double and triple rooms). Women's Halls of Residence has a capacity of 400 residents (twin sharing rooms). Hostel accommodation on campus is only for B.Tech programs. For other UG/PG programs, allocation is subject to availability.

**Q: What are the criteria to avail hostel facilities?**
All undergraduate programs are residential and hostels are mandatory. For postgraduate students, hostel facility is optional and subject to availability.

**Q: Are the hostel rooms furnished?**
All hostel rooms are furnished with cot, chair, study table, cupboard, fan, and tubelight. **Mattresses are NOT provided.**

**Q: Is internet available in hostels?**
Yes. Men's HoR is fully Wi-Fi enabled. Women's HoR has Wi-Fi as well as LAN connection.

**Q: Are washrooms attached to the hostel rooms?**
No. Common washrooms are available on all floors of the hostels.

**Q: What about the security of hostels?**
Men's HoR are fully covered with CCTVs. Women's HoR has round-the-clock female security guard and CCTV coverage at entry-exit points.

**Q: Is there a Mess in the Hostel?**
No. Multiple cafeterias on campus offer food on an à la carte basis.

**Q: Can PG students get hostel accommodation at DAU?**
Yes, but it is optional and subject to availability. Hostel accommodation is mandatory only for B.Tech students.
