# Food Court & Cafeteria Operations – Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)

## Overview

DAU campus has **7 food courts** offering a wide variety of food covering almost all cuisines and tastes — from North India to South India. There is **no hostel mess** at DAU; students use the campus cafeterias on an à la carte basis.

---

## 1. Food Courts – Overview

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Total Food Courts | **7** food courts on campus |
| Food Type Coverage | North Indian, South Indian, Breakfast/Snacks, Pastries, Fruits & Juices, Non-Vegetarian/Egg dishes |
| Outsourcing Model | Outsourced to experienced caterers on a yearly basis |
| Pricing | Affordable — contractors provided modern infrastructure at low cost |

## 4. Hostel Mess Policy

> **There is NO hostel mess at DAU.**

DAU does not operate a traditional mess (pre-paid meal plan) system in its hostels. Students eat at the campus food courts on an **à la carte** (pay-per-item) basis.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Is there a mess in the hostel? | **No** |
| How do hostellers eat? | Multiple cafeterias on campus, à la carte basis |
| Are there mess rebates? | N/A — no mess system |
| Is food charged separately? | Yes — students pay per item at food courts |

---

## 5. Food Court Operations Policy

- Food courts are **outsourced** to run on a yearly basis to experienced caterers.
- Contractors are provided with modern infrastructure facilities at **low cost** in order to work out affordable prices for students.
- A **special arrangement with Amul** ensures Amul products are available on campus.
- **Bringing food inside the Halls of Residence** is prohibited. Students found carrying food inside the hostels are fined ₹1000/- (exception: sick students with doctor's certification and prior Hostel Supervisor approval).

---

## 6. Campus Cafeteria Timings

> **Note:** Specific cafeteria timings are managed by individual vendors/contractors and are not published centrally on the DAU website. Students should refer to notice boards at each food court or contact CMC (cmc@dau.ac.in) for the current operational schedule.

General pattern:
- Most food courts open from **morning (breakfast hours)** through **late evening**.
- Campus food courts are accessible to all students, faculty, staff, and visitors.
