# Overview

Full academic program listing for Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar, spanning Undergraduate, Dual Degree, Postgraduate, and Doctoral levels.

# Main Content

## Undergraduate Programs (B.Tech., 4 years / 8 semesters)
1. B.Tech. (ICT) — https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-ict
2. B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with minor in Computational Science — https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-honours-ict-minor-computational-science
3. B.Tech. (Mathematics and Computing / MnC) — https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-mnc
4. B.Tech. (Electronics and VLSI Design / EVD) — https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-evd
5. B.Tech. (Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence / CS and AI) — *commencing AY 2026-27* — https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-csai
6. B.Tech. (Electronics and Communication Engineering – Artificial Intelligence / ECE-AI) — *commencing AY 2026-27* — https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-ece-ai

## Dual Degree Programs (BS-MS, 5-year integrated)
- **BS-MS in Information Technology** — computational foundations, software systems, cybersecurity, distributed/cloud computing, HCI — https://www.daiict.ac.in/bs-ms-information-technology
- **BS-MS in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence** — mathematics, statistics, ML, scalable AI systems, ethical tech practice — https://www.daiict.ac.in/bs-ms-data-science-artificial-intelligence

## Postgraduate Programs (2 years)
1. M.Tech. (ICT) — specializations: Machine Learning, Software Systems, VLSI and Embedded Systems — https://www.daiict.ac.in/mtech-ict
2. M.Sc. (Information Technology) — https://www.daiict.ac.in/msc-it
3. M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics) — https://www.daiict.ac.in/msc-agriculture-analytics
4. M.Sc. (Data Science) — https://www.daiict.ac.in/msc-data-science
5. M.Des. — Communication Design and Intelligent User Experience Design (IUxD) specializations — https://www.daiict.ac.in/mdes-communication-design, https://www.daiict.ac.in/mdes-intelligent-user-experience-design
   - **Note:** For AY 2026-27, M.Des. admissions are offered only for the IUxD specialization.

Financial assistance/stipend is available to most M.Tech. (ICT) students via Teaching Assistantships.

## Doctoral Program
- Ph.D. (Regular & Part-Time) in ICT and allied disciplines, Mathematical & Computational Sciences, and Humanities & Social Sciences — https://www.daiict.ac.in/phd
- Full-time Regular Ph.D. students receive financial assistance as Teaching Assistants.

## Context
DAU has offered ICT undergraduate education since 2001, integrating IT, Communication Technology, Electronics Engineering, Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, and Social Sciences. Two new UG programs (CS and AI; ECE-AI) launch in AY 2026-27 to address AI convergence with computer science and electronics/communications.
