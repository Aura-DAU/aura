# Programs of Study

## Overview

Programs of Study at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), formerly DA-IICT, Gandhinagar. The University offers undergraduate, dual degree, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across Information and Communication Technology, Computer Science, Electronics, Mathematics, Data Science, Design, and allied disciplines.

## Undergraduate Programs

Dhirubhai Ambani University has played a pioneering role in shaping undergraduate education in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in India since its establishment in 2001. The University has consistently focused on creating innovative and interdisciplinary academic programs that integrate Information Technology, Communication Technology, Electronics Engineering, Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, and Social Sciences. This holistic approach reflects the evolving nature of technology-driven disciplines and prepares students to address complex real-world challenges beyond the scope of conventional engineering education.

Over the years, the University has expanded its undergraduate portfolio to address emerging academic and industry needs. Building upon its pioneering, first-of-its-kind undergraduate curriculum in ICT introduced in 2001, the University subsequently launched the B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with a Minor in Computational Science, aimed at providing advanced interdisciplinary training in Computational Modeling and High-Performance Computing. The B.Tech. in Mathematics and Computing (MnC) offers strong foundations in Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science, while the B.Tech. in Electronics and VLSI Design (EVD) prepares students for careers in the rapidly growing semiconductor and VLSI industry.

Beginning Academic Year 2026-27, the University has further expanded its offerings with two new programs aligned with emerging technological transformations. The B.Tech. in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (CS and AI) is designed for students seeking strong foundations in Computer Science along with modern Artificial Intelligence technologies. The B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering - Artificial Intelligence (ECE-AI) addresses the growing convergence of electronics, communication systems, and AI-driven technologies.
