# Overview

Guidance page for parents of first-year students at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar. Lists the "Top 9 things every Parent of a 1st year Student should know."

# Main Content

## Top 9 things every Parent of a 1st year Student should know

1. **Academic Expectations are High** — University is a new academic environment; grades may be lower than in school, especially in first year, as students adjust. Parents are encouraged to promote "doing your best" rather than "being the best."

2. **Know What Services are Available for Your Child** — Seeking help (e.g. academic skills workshops for struggling with programming) is a sign of self-awareness, not weakness. Parents should familiarize themselves with campus services and resources.

3. **Be Prepared for Change** — Students are exposed to new ideas and may change lifestyle, appearance, habits, or interests during university.

4. **Adjust to a New Adult Relationship** — Parents' role shifts from close monitoring to mentorship/advising as students become more independent.

5. **Keep Track of Important Dates** — Tuition deadlines, class drop dates, and exam schedules matter; parents may want to track these and be mindful during exam periods.

6. **Money Matters** — Open discussion about finances, account management, and expectations around debit/credit cards is encouraged.

7. **Support Your Student's Choices** — Many students change programs or interests after first year; parental support for these choices matters.

8. **Understand the Unique Experiences of Students in Hall of Residence** — Adjusting to hostel life (roommates, privacy, chores) takes time; parents should stay in touch without expecting immediate responses.

9. **Expect University Culture Shock** — First-year students receive a lot of advice (safety, time management, plagiarism, finances, health); parents should remain patient and celebrate milestones.
