## Correspondence Protocol

**For individual students:** Step 1 — Contact SBG. Step 2 — If unresolved, escalate to Faculty Coordinator/Mentor. Step 3 — Faculty Coordinator/Mentor forwards to Office of the Dean (Students).

**For clubs/committees:** All official communication, proposals, approvals, reports must be routed through the Faculty Coordinator/Mentor, carry required approvals, and be submitted to the Office of the Dean (Students).

**Direct communication with the Dean (Students):** Only for urgent/exceptional situations with valid justification, or to continue an already-ongoing email thread.
