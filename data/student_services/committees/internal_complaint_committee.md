# ICC

## Overview

DAU aims to provide safe working and learning campus life for faculty, staff and students free from gender discrimination and sexual harassment. It further cultivates the atmosphere where men and women work together towards the growth and prosperity of the institution in a safe and healthy academic environment.

## Internal Complaints Committee

The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) shall be responsible for Redressal of formal complaints on gender discrimination and sexual harassment and to redress it. It shall receive complaints, conduct inquiries and recommend disciplinary action, if any, in accordance with the Prevention of Sexual Harassment in the Workplace (POSH) Act and UGC guidelines.

## Complaint Submission Process

Any written complaint of sexual harassment by the aggrieved women shall be submitted directly to the Internal Complaints Committee by sending an email to [icc@da.ac.in](mailto:icc@da.ac.in). Complaints must be submitted within three months from the date of incident (or last incident in a series). Exceptions to this timeline may apply: refer to the [policy](https://www.daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/DAU-Policy-against-Sexual-Harassment_2026.pdf) for details.

The ICC is bound by law to maintain confidentiality and committed to ensuring the safety and well-being of complainants.

## Terms of Reference

The Internal Complaints Committee will:
- Provide guidelines for protection from sexual harassment
- Advise DAU on matters of sexual harassment
- Prepare detailed guidelines defining harassment and dignity-related acts
- Handle all complaints of sexual harassment
- Conduct inquiries and submit recommendations to the Director General
- Recommend measures to deter harassment
- Address connected matters and issues
- Arrange gender sensitization sessions
