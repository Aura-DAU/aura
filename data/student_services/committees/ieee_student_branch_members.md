# Students / IEEE Student Branch / Members
## Overview
### Introduction to DA-IICT IEEE Student Branch
DA-IICT at Gandhinagar represents Wave-4 of educational innovation in Gujarat. The first wave was the nationalist wave and led to Gandhian experiments in education including Nai Talim. 

### History of DA-IICT
The Gujarat Vidyapith established in 1920 was a hybrid model of a university based on Gandhian principles. The second wave led to the establishment of a whole range of educational institutions in Gujarat, mainly private colleges. 

### Establishment of DA-IICT
The third wave, an inspired one, was spearheaded by the industrialist Kasturbhai Lalbhai and the scientist Vikram Sarabhai and led to the establishment of a network of national institutes of international renown. DA-IICT was established in Gandhinagar in 2001.

## About DA-IICT
### Campus and Architecture
The architecture of DA-IICT is functional, but what surrounds it is a fascinating garden. The entire design was oriented to "Preserving the Environment". 

### Environment and Sustainability
The different blocks are located such that there is minimum damage to trees. Most of the 2566 trees chosen are indigenous varieties. The lawn area measures roughly 50,000 square meters with 3900 square meters of wild St Augustine grass.

## IEEE History
### Formation and Purpose
The IEEE formed in 1963 with the merger of the AIEE (American Institute of Electrical Engineers, formed in 1884), and the IRE (Institute of Radio Engineers, formed in 1912). 

### Activities and Scope
From its earliest origins, the IEEE has:   
· Advanced the theory and application of electro technology and allied sciences.   
· Served as a catalyst for technological innovation.   
· Supported the needs of its members through a wide variety of programs and services.
