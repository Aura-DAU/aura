# Hostel Rules and Regulations – Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)

## Overview

The Halls of Residence rules at DA-IICT / DAU result from considerable thought and discussion amongst residents and administrators. These rules provide a framework of common understanding and mutual concern. The Hostel Management Committee (HMC) reviews and updates these rules periodically.

**Source:** Official PDF – [Hostel-Rules-and-Regulations.pdf](https://daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/Hostel-Rules-and-Regulations.pdf)

---

## 1. Room Allocation

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Room allotment | Done by the Wardens for new students |
| Room change requests | **Not entertained** |
| Eligibility | Only registered students of DA-IICT/DAU |
| Duration (UG) | Room allotted only for the **first four years** of stay; extension subject to availability |
| Vacating | Must vacate within **5 days** of ceasing to be a student |
| Authority | Dean of Students, Wardens, and Hostel Supervisors reserve the right to allot/re-allot/withdraw accommodation |

## 2. Behaviour and Discipline

### Anti-Ragging Rules

> **Q: Is ragging allowed at DAU and what are the consequences?**

**Ragging is a criminal offence and is STRICTLY PROHIBITED at DAU.**

- If anyone suspects or knows about ragging, or becomes a victim of ragging (inside or outside the campus), contact any member of the **Anti-Ragging Vigilance Committee** or the **Resident Warden**.
- **Consequences:**
  - Strict disciplinary action
  - This may lead to **termination from the Institute**
  - **Legal action** may also be taken

### Other Discipline Rules

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Return to campus | All HoR residents must return by **midnight (12 AM)** |
| Outside campus at night | **Not permitted from midnight till 6 AM** (except medical emergencies; entry in security register required) |
| Night-out intimation | Must email the Hostel Supervisor **prior to staying away** (even for one night) |
| Gender entry | Men and women are **prohibited** from entering each other's HoR |
| Ragging | **Criminal offence, strictly prohibited** |
| Alcohol/intoxicants | **Strictly prohibited** — Gujarat is a dry state; case referred to DAC; may lead to termination |
| Smoking | **Strictly prohibited** — entire DA-IICT/DAU campus is a no-smoking zone |
| Noise | Loud music or noise that disturbs others is not allowed |
| Dress code | Must not dress in a manner that would offend anyone outside the room |

### Fines for Discipline Violations

| Violation | Fine |
|-----------|------|
| Unauthorized person staying in a resident's room | ₹1,000/- per day (both occupants) |
| Bringing food inside the Halls of Residence | ₹1,000/- |
| Room found unlocked and vacant | ₹500/- (if no proper justification) |
| Breach of any hostel rule (minimum) | ₹1,000/- (increases with severity) |

## 3. Vehicles

> **Q: Can students park 4-wheelers (cars) on the DAU campus?**

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 4-wheelers (cars, jeeps, etc.) | **NOT allowed** on campus |
| 2-wheelers (bicycles, scooters, motorcycles) | **Allowed** — maximum **1 vehicle per student** |
| Parking location | Must park in **authorized parking areas ONLY** |
| Traffic rules | Must obey all campus traffic rules (helmet mandatory, speed limit, max 2 persons per vehicle) |

### Penalties for Vehicle Rule Violations

| Offence | Penalty |
|---------|---------|
| First offence | Monetary fine + a few hours community service + letter to parents |
| Second offence | Not allowed to keep a vehicle on campus for remaining stay; possible hostel expulsion |

## 4. Maintenance of Halls of Residence

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Room cleanliness | Residents individually responsible for keeping rooms, bathrooms, and toilets clean |
| Common areas | Institute undertakes housekeeping of common areas only |
| Complaints | Written in the concerned register only; escalate to Hostel Supervisor then Wardens if unresolved |
| Room inspection | Institute reserves the right to make **spot checks** without prior notice |
| Drilling/civil works | **Strictly prohibited** — may not alter HoR structure |
| Private furniture | Not allowed unless advised by a doctor (with medical certificate) |
| **Mattress / Linen** | Residents are **expected to bring mattress, pillow, linen, and personal clothing** |

## 5. Visitors

> **Q: What is the fine for entertaining a visitor without prior intimation?**

**Fine: Minimum ₹1,000/- in addition to guest charges for the full duration of stay.**

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Visitors in HoR Complex | **NOT allowed** — Cafeteria is the suggested meeting place |
| Guest Room availability | Only for **medical emergencies** (requires Hostel Supervisor permission + advance rent payment) |
| Overnight stay | **Not allowed** except in medical emergency |
| Fine for unauthorized visitor | **Minimum ₹1,000/-** + guest charge for duration of stay |

### Guest Room Charges

| Guest Type | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| Non-resident DA-IICT/DAU students (short stay) | ₹200/- per person per day |
| Outside visitors, including parents | ₹250/- per person per day (inclusive of GST) |

### Parent / Guardian Visits

- Parents and guardians wishing to visit must obtain **special permission from the Hostel Supervisor**.
