# Overview

Anti-ragging policy summary for Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar.

# Main Content

Ragging of any nature is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offence under Central Government Act. Violation may attract any one or more of the following punishments, depending on severity:

- One year suspension from the institute
- A fine up to ₹2,500/-
- Expulsion from the institute
- Suspension from the institute or classes for a limited period
- Fine with a public apology
- Withholding of scholarships or other benefits
- Debarring from representation in events
- Withholding of results
- Suspension or expulsion from the Halls of Residence

Full regulatory text: UGC Regulations for Curbing Ragging (PDF) — https://www.daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/UGC-Regulations-for-Curbing-Ragging.pdf

DAU follows a **zero-tolerance policy** toward ragging; anyone directly or indirectly involved is dealt with strictly, with expulsion from the Institute plus police action as per law. All new students and parents must submit UGC-prescribed undertakings.
