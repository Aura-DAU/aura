# **Dean (Students) Message**

### **Dr. P S Kalyan Sasidhar** , Associate Professor

"A vibrant campus life awaits you as a student of DAU (Formerly DA-IICT) once you become a campus resident. Classes, lab sessions research projects, and exams are perfectly balanced with musical performances, sports, theatre, and spending time with friends at cafeteria hangouts. Our students ensure life happens 24*7 on campus. In the process, they become pros in choosing their priorities and managing their time effectively.

DAU is uniquely positioned to give you the best of ICT education infused with a multi-disciplinary way of learning. Along with innovative courses, a wide choice of electives, a cutting-edge curriculum, and research opportunities, it offers an excellent learning and research experience. The learning extends beyond the classroom with a plethora of club activities that are geared towards professional as well as personal development. Additionally, students benefit from our faculty members who are both teachers as well as mentors to the students.

At DAU, we believe in the overall holistic development of the students. As the Dean -Students, my role is to encourage and nurture the holistic development of our students through a portfolio of well-planned extra-curricular activities driven through 8 committees and 22 student clubs. I am also delighted to share that our students continue to make us proud every year by winning several state-level and national-level inter- collegiate and industry-driven competitions.

We are one of the first institutes to introduce rural internship which is a mandatory requirement of the B.Tech curriculum. The objective of this program is two-fold- to give students an understanding of the socio-economic realities of rural lives and sensitize them to the challenges and above all, to create a belief in the significance of inclusive growth which they can contribute to in the future.

Besides all this, DAU offers you an opportunity to build friendships across barriers like language, region, religion, and caste and create bonds, memories, and experiences for a lifetime. I invite you to join us and become part of the rewarding experience at DAU.

As the Dean -Students, I am always reachable to my students and parents, whenever required."

# Hostel Management Committee

The Hostel Management Committee (HMC) at DAU (Formerly DA-IICT) works to ensure a peaceful, clean, and student-friendly environment in the hostels. It acts as a bridge between students and the administration, helping raise and address concerns, complaints, and suggestions. HMC supports hostel management under the guidance of wardens and supervisors, identifies and resolves issues on each floor, and ensures efficient use of hostel resources. It also conducts surveys to address maintenance problems related to electricians, carpenters, and plumbers, maintains common areas like the TV room, and issues indoor games like chess and carom for recreation. Overall, HMC strives to enhance the quality of hostel life through active student involvement and administrative support.

# Student Placement Cell

The Student Placement Cell (SPC) acts as a crucial bridge between the student community and the placement office, ensuring the smooth functioning of placement drives. The primary objective of the SPC is to facilitate a smooth and efficient process for both students and recruiters.

SPC plays a pivotal role in organizing various activities designed to enhance students' readiness for the recruitment process. These include conducting pre-placement tests to help students strengthen their aptitude and coding skills, arranging mock interviews to build confidence and refine their performance, and hosting interactive sessions led by company representatives to provide insights into industry expectations. Additionally, SPC facilitates experience-sharing sessions with alumni, where students can gain valuable guidance and inspiration from their seniors. Group discussions are also conducted to develop critical communication and teamwork skills, which are essential for professional success.

Through these efforts, SPC not only ensures the smooth coordination of placement-related activities but also fosters a culture of preparedness and excellence among students.

This year’s lineup is packed with exciting events such as Vaani - Performance Night, a platform for freshers to showcase talents in poetry, storytelling, mimicry, and monologues, and the Commentator Cup, where students can channel their inner sports commentator during Asia Cup matches. Ink Chronicles invites participants to write heartfelt letters to beloved characters, while the Discussion Forum encourages open and authentic conversations on topics like Taylor Swift’s impact. The Weekly Poetry Writing Challenge celebrates the art of poetry in all its forms, and the Audio Editing Workshop provides a hands-on opportunity for aspiring editors to hone their skills. The Radio Club also marks National Radio Day and International Dubbing Day with special celebrations, making this year a dynamic blend of creativity, expression, and learning.

# Microsoft Student Technical Club - MSTC

We are individuals who come together and share common interest, putting our ideas into existence in order to contribute to the Open Source Community.

Yes, we are the MSTC-Microsoft Student Technical Club of DAU. We try to make "I don't know" to "I don't know yet".

Many people have genuine ideas related to app and web development but there is no proper platform provided to them. We, as MSTC, come here and help them give the right environment to inculcate their ideas and bring it to life. We aim to do this in as exciting and simplified manner as possible.

If you want to find answers to your questions, share ideas, solve problems, or whatever may be your goal, MSTC is here to help you get more out of your ideas and technology.

We also come up with various events and competitions which would help you to create a strong foundation for your dream competitions like those of Imagine Cup/GSoC.

_**List of Events and Sessions include**_ :  
**1\. Think Like a Developer Session and Workshop:** Students are made aware of different development platforms(Frontend ,Backend, AI/ML, Android Application etc). Followed by a Hands on session of Basic Development Technologies  
**2\. Hacktoberfest Competition along with Git-Github session:** Spread the word of open-source along with the hands-on tutorial on version control systems. Followed by a month long Open Source Competition  
**3\. Winter-of-Code(WoC):** A month long development competition featuring 8-9 different tech-stack. Implement a real-life project while learning a new technology.  
**4\. Face-off:** 1v1 Competitive Coding Contest. With each round being elimination round enjoy the thrill of solving problems.

### **1) How are the hostels of DAU (Formerly DA-IICT)?**

DAU has Men's Halls of Residence with capacity of almost 1500 residents having double and triple capacity rooms. Women's Halls of Residence has a capacity of 400 residents having twin sharing capacity of rooms.  
**Pls note the hostel accommodation on campus is only for BTech programs. For other UG/PG programs, allocation is subject to availability and governed by the Hostel Rules and Regulations.**

### **2) What are the criteria to avail hostel facilities?**

All undergraduate programs are residential programs and hence hostels are mandatory for them. For Post graduate students the hostel facility is optional and subject to availability.

### **3) Are the hostel rooms furnished?**

All hostel rooms are furnished with basic facilities like cot, chair, study table, cupboard, fan, tubelight. Mattresses are not provided.

### **4) Is the internet available in hostels?**

Yes, Men's HoR is fully Wi-Fi enabled and Women's HoR has Wi-Fi as well as LAN connection.

### **5) Are washrooms attached to the hostel rooms?**

Washrooms are not attached with the room but common washrooms are on all the floors of hostels.

### **6) What about the security of hostels?**

Men's HoR are fully covered with CCTVs. Round the clock female security-guard is there in Women's HoR and entry-exits are covered with CCTV.

### **7) Is there Mess in the Hostel?**

No, there are multiple cafeterias on the campus where students can avail food as per their choice on a la carte basis.
