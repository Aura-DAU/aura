## Hostel FAQs
- Men's Halls: ~1,500 residents (double/triple rooms). Women's Halls: 400 residents (twin-sharing). Hostel is only for B.Tech programs; PG allocation is subject to availability.
- All UG programs are residential (hostel mandatory); PG hostel is optional/subject to availability.
- Rooms are furnished with cot, chair, study table, cupboard, fan, tubelight — mattresses not provided.
- Men's HoR: full Wi-Fi. Women's HoR: Wi-Fi + LAN.
- Washrooms are common (per floor), not attached to rooms.
- Men's HoR fully covered by CCTV; Women's HoR has round-the-clock female security guard plus CCTV at entry/exit.
- No mess — multiple à la carte cafeterias on campus.
