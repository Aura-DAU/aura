# Overview

Official academic calendar for Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar, covering registration, class, exam, and break dates for the Autumn, Winter, and Summer terms. The live page shows tabs for 2026-27, 2025-26, and archived calendars (PDF downloads back to 2018-19). Page last updated (per site footer): 04-08-2026.

# Main Content
