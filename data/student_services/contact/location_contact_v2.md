# Location, Contact & University Identity – Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)

## 1. Official University Name

- **Full Official Name:** Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)
- **Former Name:** Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT)
- **Short Name / Acronym:** DAU (current) / DA-IICT (legacy)
- **Named After:** Shri Dhirubhai Hirachand Ambani — founder of Reliance Industries

### Name Change History

- The institution was originally established as **DA-IICT** (Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology), Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
- Following expansion of academic programs beyond ICT into multidisciplinary areas, the institution was granted **university status** and renamed to **Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)**.
- The formal change is recognized in all legal and academic documents. The institution is referred to as **DAU** in all current communications.
- Some legacy documents, website URLs, and PDFs still use the **DA-IICT** branding/domain (daiict.ac.in), which redirect to or coexist with the new **dau.ac.in** domain.

## 3. Campus Location

DAU (formerly DA-IICT) is located in **Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India**.

- **City:** Gandhinagar — the **capital city of Gujarat**
- **Address:** DA-IICT Road, Gandhinagar – 382 007, Gujarat, India
- **Region:** Located on the banks of the River Sabarmati
- **Nearby Major City:** Ahmedabad (~20–30 km)

### How to Reach

- **By Air:** Ahmedabad International Airport (~20 minutes drive from campus) — has direct international flights to Europe and the USA.
- **By Train:** Kalupur Railway Station (main railway station, Ahmedabad) — ~50 minutes drive from campus.
- **From Ahmedabad:** ~20–30 km via Sarkhej-Gandhinagar (SG) Highway.

## 7. FAQ – University Name

**Q: What is the full official name of the university (DAU)?**
The full official name is **Dhirubhai Ambani University**, abbreviated as **DAU**. It was formerly known as Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT).

**Q: What does DA-IICT stand for?**
DA-IICT stands for **Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology** — the former name of the institution before it became a full university (DAU).

**Q: Is DA-IICT and DAU the same institution?**
Yes. DA-IICT (Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology) was renamed and elevated to **Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)** upon receiving university status. Both names refer to the same institution in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.
