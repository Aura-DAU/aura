# Overview

Comprehensive guide combining DAU's (formerly DA-IICT) location, contact details, university identity/branding notes, and an overview of the city of Gandhinagar — DAU is the renamed identity of DA-IICT, established by a Gujarat State Government Act, with campus in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.

# Main Content

## Identity
- Full name: Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), formerly Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT)
- Status: A Private University established by The State Government of Gujarat Act
- Established: 2001, in Gandhinagar

## How to Reach
- Nearest airport: Ahmedabad (Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport) — about a 20-minute drive from campus; has international connectivity to much of Europe and the USA.
- Nearest railway station: Kalupur Railway Station, Ahmedabad — about a 50-minute drive from campus.

## About Gandhinagar
Gandhinagar is the capital of Gujarat, planned as a twin city to Ahmedabad along the Sabarmati River, in the tradition of planned capitals like New Delhi, Chandigarh, and Bhubaneswar. It hosts institutes such as IPR, IIM Ahmedabad, NID, PRL, CEPT, SAC-ISRO, Gujarat Vidyapeeth, NIFT, and Gujarat National Law University. Climate is largely hot and dry except during the monsoon (mid-June to mid-September); winter is mild, with max temps typically 36–42°C in summer and averages around 29°C/14°C (max/min) from November to February.

Points of interest around Gandhinagar/Ahmedabad include Akshardham (Gandhinagar), Sabarmati Riverfront, Adalaj Vav, Sarkhej Roza, Kankaria Lake, Gujarat Science City, and the Calico Museum of Textiles.
