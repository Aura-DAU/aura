## Structure
The Dean (Students) office coordinates student life through:
- **Student Body Government (SBG)** — self-governing student organization (sbg[at]dau[dot]ac[dot]in)
- **Research Body Government (RBG)** — for PhD scholars (rbg[at]dau[dot]ac[dot]in)
- **8 Student Committees** — Academic, Annual Festival, Cafeteria Management, Cultural, Hostel Management, TechSupport, Sports, Student Placement Cell
- **22 Student Clubs** — spanning technical (AI Club, Programming Club, GDG, IEEE, MSTC, CINS, Electronics Hobby Club, Research Club, Cubing Club), cultural/arts (Dance, Music, Theatres, Film, PMMC, Muse, Khelaiya/Garba, Radio), and other interest areas (Press Club, Debating Society, Headrush Quizzing, Business Club, Chess Club)

## Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC)
Chaired (ex-officio) by the Dean (Students), with Hostel Wardens (Men/Women) and the Deputy Registrar as members. Governs student conduct per the Disciplinary Guidelines (2024) and the Act of Indiscipline & Punishment list (2024).
