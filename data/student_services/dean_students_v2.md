# Dean (Students) – Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)

## Overview

The Dean of Students at DAU oversees holistic student development through extra-curricular activities, **8 committees**, **22 student clubs**, hostel management, and disciplinary matters. The current Dean is **Dr. P. S. Kalyan Sasidhar**, Associate Professor.

- **Email:** dean_students@dau.ac.in
- **Phone:** (+91) 079-68261560 (Extn: 560)

## 1. Student Body Government (SBG)

The students of DAU have constituted a self-governing democratic organisation called the **DAU Student Body Government (SBG)** to achieve the following:

- Monitoring and regulation of all student activities
- Ensuring justice and equality in all aspects of student life
- Enhancing the overall development of all students

**Email:** sbg@dau.ac.in

The Student Body Government consists of **8 committees** and **22 hobby-driven clubs**.

## 3. Flagship Events – Detailed Descriptions

### HackOut (Annual Hackathon)

- **Type:** Annual hackathon (coding competition)
- **Organized by:** Annual Festival Committee (under SBG)
- **Part of:** Synapse – the Annual Techno-Cultural Fest of DAU
- **Duration:** 72-hour continuous coding competition
- **Edition:** HackOut'25 was the **12th edition** (held August 29–31, 2025)
- **Description:** HackOut is a 72-hour continuous coding competition where teams build solutions to real-world problems. It features career guidance sessions, international education sessions, and has attracted participants from across India.

---

### YouthRun (Annual Marathon)

- **Type:** Annual marathon / social initiative
- **Organized by:** Annual Festival Committee (under SBG)
- **Part of:** Synapse – the Annual Techno-Cultural Fest of DAU
- **Description:** YouthRun is DAU's annual marathon, conducted as a social initiative alongside Synapse. It promotes physical fitness and community participation.

---

### Synapse (Annual Techno-Cultural Fest)

- **Type:** Annual Techno-Cultural Festival
- **Organized by:** Annual Festival Committee (under SBG)
- **Scale:** The **Largest Annual Techno-Cultural Fest of Gujarat**
- **Key events under Synapse:**
  - HackOut (Hackathon)
  - YouthRun (Marathon)
  - Tarang (Cultural performances)
  - Concours (Sports Tournament)
  - Various technical and cultural competitions

---

### Concours (Inter-University Sports Tournament)

- **Type:** Inter-university sports tournament
- **Organized by:** Sports Committee (under SBG)
- **Description:** Concours is DAU's flagship **inter-university sports tournament**. It is an annual event where students from multiple universities/colleges compete in various sports. It is organized as part of Synapse, DAU's annual festival.

---

### Freshers' Weekend

- **Type:** Sports and social activities for newly admitted students
- **Organized by:** Sports Committee (under SBG)
- **Description:** Freshers' Weekend is a sports and recreational programme organized specifically for **first-year students** at DAU. It is designed to help new students get acquainted with the campus, their batchmates, and the sporting culture at DAU through friendly matches and activities across various sports.

---

### i.Fest (Annual Technical Festival)

- **Type:** Annual Technical Festival
- **Organized by:** **IEEE Student Branch** (DA-IICT / DAU)
- **Scale:** One of the biggest technical fests in Gujarat; one of the three annual festivals of DA-IICT/DAU
- **Features:** Over 20 events, including hackathons, technical competitions, workshops (Manual Robotics, Summer School, Tic-Tech-Toe, MUN, Think Tank), and speaker sessions/bootcamps
- **Participation:** More than 1000 students from various universities and colleges participate annually
- **Description:** i.Fest is the Annual Technical Festival of DAU, organized by the IEEE Student Branch. It features a wide range of technical and non-technical activities aimed at showcasing technology and promoting engineering culture.

> **IEEE Student Branch Contact:** ieee@dau.ac.in

## 5. Anti-Ragging Information

Ragging is a **criminal offence** and is **strictly prohibited** at DAU.

- **Consequences:** Strict disciplinary action including possible termination from the Institute and legal action.
- **Where to find info:** The Anti-Ragging Committee & Squad details are published on the **DAU website at daiict.ac.in/dean-students**.
- **Committee:** Anti-Ragging Vigilance Committee (details uploaded on institute's website)
- **Immediate contact:** Resident Warden in case of any incident.

Anti-ragging information is available on the **Dean (Students) page** of the DAU website: [www.daiict.ac.in/dean-students](https://www.daiict.ac.in/dean-students). This page contains links to the National Ragging Prevention Programme and the Anti-Ragging Committee & Squad details.
