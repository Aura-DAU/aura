# Curriculum Guide

This document defines the progression of courses and prerequisites required to complete various degrees.

## 1. B.Tech (Honours in ICT with minor in Computational Science) - 2016 Batch Onwards

### Semester-wise Progression

**Semester I**
- Basic Electronic Circuits
- Introduction to Programming
- Calculus
- Engineering Design Workshop
- Language and Literature

**Semester II**
- Digital Logic Design
- Introduction to Communication Systems
- Data Structures
- Discrete Mathematics
- Approaches to Indian Society

**Semester III**
- Signals and Systems
- Computer Organization
- Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- Groups and Linear Algebra
- Electromagnetic Theory
- Science, Technology, Society

**Semester IV**
- Analog Circuits
- Analog Communication and Transmission Line Theory
- Systems Software
- Probability, Statistics and Information Theory
- Engineered Materials
- Environmental Studies
- Introductory Computational Physics (Core)

**Semester V to VIII**
- Embedded Hardware Design, Digital Communications, Computer Networks, Database Management Systems, Principles of Economics, Numerical and Computational Methods (Core).
- High Performance Computing (Core), Modelling and Simulation (Core), Software Engineering.
- Extensive Electives (ICT Electives, Science Electives, Technical Electives) and B.Tech Project.
- Rural Internship (4 credits) after Sem III and Research/Industrial Internship (6 credits) after Sem VI.

## 2. B.Tech in Mathematics and Computing (MnC) - 2020 Batch Onwards

### Semester-wise Progression

**Semester I**
- Mathematical, Algorithmic, and Computational Thinking
- Computer Organization and Programming
- Functions of Single Variable and ODEs
- Mathematical and Computational Tools
- Language and Literature

**Semester II**
- Probability and Random Processes
- Object Oriented Programming
- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Discrete Mathematics
- Approaches to Indian Society

**Semester III**
- Mathematical Statistics
- Operating Systems
- Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- Algebraic Structures
- Theory of Computation
- Science, Technology, Society
- Rural Internship (Winter Break)

**Semester IV**
- Functions of Several Variables and PDEs
- Data Storage and Management
- Parallel and Distributed Algorithms
- Linear Algebra
- Numerical and Computational Methods
- Environmental Studies

**Semester V to VIII**
- Mathematical Optimization, Modelling and Simulation, Machine Learning, Principles of Economics, Real and Complex Analysis.
- Electives (MnC Electives, Open Electives).
- Independent Projects (IP) and B.Tech Project (BTP).
- Summer Industrial/Research Internship.

## 3. Prerequisite Chains and Elective Rules
- **Hard Prerequisites**: Require a minimum grade of 'DD' to pass and advance.
- **Soft Prerequisites**: Require a minimum grade of 'DE' to advance.
- **Elective Selection Rules**: Students must balance core foundations with required categories of electives (e.g. 9-10 MnC electives for MnC program, minimum 18 credits in Computational Science core for ICT Honors).
