# PhD Engineering (2017-18) – Coursework Credit Requirements (Explicit Summary)

## Document Authority

Academic Requirements for the PhD Program (Effective from Autumn 2017-18)
Issued by: DA-IICT, Gandhinagar, created as of August 2017.

## Coursework Credit Requirements by Admission Category

The following table explicitly states the coursework credit requirements for PhD students admitted under the 2017-18 rules:

| Admission Category | Minimum Total Credits | **Minimum Coursework Credits** | Minimum Research Credits | Minimum Courses | Max Duration (Years) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PhD after B.Tech / M.Sc | 96 | **33** | 48 | 11 | 7 |
| PhD after M.Tech / M.Phil | 72 | **12** | 48 | 4 | 6 |
| PhD (Sponsored) after M.Tech / M.Phil | 72 | **12** | 48 | 4 | 7 |
| PhD (Sponsored) after B.Tech / M.Sc | 96 | **33** | 48 | 11 | 8 |

## Key Facts on Coursework Credits

### For B.Tech/M.Sc Entry:
- **Minimum coursework credits: 33**
- Minimum 11 courses required
- Includes compulsory courses:
  - HM502: Communication Skills and Technical Writing (3 credits)
  - SC526: Mathematical Methods for ICT (3 credits)
  - SC527: Probability and Statistics (3 credits)
  - Plus ONE of: IT576 Computer Systems (4 cr), CT542 Basics of Communication Systems (3 cr), or EL522 Introduction to Digital Design (3 cr)
- Remaining credits from M.Tech level electives and BTech 300/400 level courses

### For M.Tech/M.Phil Entry:
- **Minimum coursework credits: 12**
- Minimum 4 courses required
- Includes compulsory courses:
  - HM502: Communication Skills and Technical Writing (3 credits)
  - Research Methodology (compulsory)
- Remaining credits from approved M.Tech level courses

## Additional Coursework Rules (2017-18)

- **Regular semester load:** 12 credits (can be 9–15 with PGC approval)
- **Minimum CPI for comprehensive exam eligibility:** 7.0/10
- **300-level (B.Tech) courses:** Up to 2 such courses (3 credits each) may count toward the 33-credit requirement for B.Tech/M.Sc entry candidates if needed for foundation strengthening
- **Self-study courses:** Maximum of 2 allowed overall (max 1 per semester); each earns 3 credits
- Research credits (PC801–PC830 etc.) are with S/X grading and do NOT count toward the coursework credit total

## Important Note on Sponsored Students

For sponsored candidates entering with B.Tech/M.Sc degree and appropriate research experience, the 33-credit coursework requirement **may be relaxed** based on recommendations of the PGC and approval of Dean-AP.

## Quick Reference Answer

**Q: How many coursework credits does a PhD Engineering student (2017-18 batch) need?**

**A:**
- Entry via B.Tech or M.Sc: **33 coursework credits** (minimum 11 courses)
- Entry via M.Tech or M.Phil: **12 coursework credits** (minimum 4 courses)

This is separate from the 48 minimum research credits required in both cases.
