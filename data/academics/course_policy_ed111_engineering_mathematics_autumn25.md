## Detailed Course Content


- Calculus: Limit, continuity, differentiation, application of derivatives, integration, definite integrals, method of substitution, integration by parts, application to finding area and volume, double and tripl e integrals, improper integrals. Matrix: Matrix addition, subtraction, multiplication, determinants, rank of a matrix, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and applications. Vectors: Vector algebra, products of vectors, vector triple product, vector equations, Differentiation and integration of vectors, vector calculus. Differential Equations: Formation and solution of ODE, solution of first order ODEs, Linear inhomogeneous second order ODEs, free and forced oscillators and applications. Formation and solution of PDEs, Second order linear PDE, Diffusion equation and applications. Complex Numbers: Com plex numbers and its properties, polar representation, Euler’s formula, exponential form. Numerical methods: Newton’s method for finding roots, numerical integration methods, finite element method. Expected Outcome The students after completing the course will get a basic overview of calculus , linear algebra, differential equations, complex analysis and numerical analysis. Students will get an insight of fundamentals of these topics and their vast applications. In some of the tutorials we will use some computer programming to visualize and und erstand the solutions. All these outcomes will be assessed with homework, quiz and exams. At the end of the course the students will be able to

- Understand and define limit, continuity, differentiability of a function at a point.

- evaluate limits using the definition and using algebraic properties of limits .

- compute indefinite and definite integration and its application.

- interpret the area bounded by curves, volume of a solid of revolution as a definite integral etc.

- represent the system of linear equation in matrix and vector form.

- solve system of linear equations using matrices.

- solving real life problems using matrices

- find and understand determinant and linear transformation.

- find eigen value and eigen vector of a square matrix and its applications.

- understand vector algebra and vector calculus.

- form ODE of first and second order and will learn about the solutions.

- understand the concept of a general solution, a particular solution and initial conditions.

- draw slope fields by hand and also by using computer.

- solving first order ode (linear and nonlinear) using different methods.

- solution of second order linear ODE with constant and variable coefficient.

- form PDE and learn about the solutions.

- learn about the applications of PDEs

- understand the idea of complex numbers

- represent complex numbers in its polar form

- find complex differentiation and integrations

- find the roots using different methods
