# Laboratory-Related Information

## Overview

Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT) maintains a dedicated Laboratory Complex housing the equipment and facilities used for practical components of courses. Lab-related information — including manuals, timings, guidelines, and safety rules — is typically provided by the **respective course instructor** or **lab in-charge** at the start of each semester.

## Laboratory Complex

| Resource | Link |
|---|---|
| Laboratory Complex Overview | [Lab Complex — DAU](https://www.daiict.ac.in/laboratory-complex) |
| Moodle (Lab Manuals & Resources) | [Moodle — DAU](https://moodle.daiict.ac.in) |

## What Is Covered

Lab-related information typically includes:
- **Lab Manuals** — step-by-step instructions for each lab experiment or project
- **Lab Timings** — scheduled hours for each lab section
- **Lab Guidelines** — conduct, attendance, and submission rules specific to each lab course
- **Safety Rules** — mandatory safety procedures for working in electronics, chemistry, and other physical labs

## How to Access Lab Resources

1. Attend the **lab orientation session** at the start of the semester — the instructor or lab in-charge will provide or point to all relevant documents.
2. Check the course page on **Moodle** for any uploaded manuals, safety sheets, or guidelines.
3. Contact your course instructor or lab in-charge directly for resources not yet uploaded.

## Additional Notes

- Lab attendance is generally **mandatory** and tracked separately from lecture attendance. Refer to the specific course policy.
- Lost or damaged lab equipment must be reported immediately to the lab in-charge.
- Safety violations may result in disqualification from lab sessions.
