# Ph.D.  

## Overview  
The Doctoral Program leading towards the award of the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) provides the students an opportunity for a career in academia or in Research & Development Establishments. A strong foundation and original contribution to knowledge in a chosen area of interest is expected from Ph.D. graduates of DA-IICT.  

## Program Structure  
### PhD Programme and Disciplines  
Depending on student’s educational background and fulfilling the eligibility criteria of the program, a student can pursue PhD study in:  
- Engineering in ICT disciplines, Science and Mathematics  
- Humanities, Social Science and Design  

### Categories of Registration  
Students are selected for admission in PhD program in three categories:  
1. **Regular (Full-time) registration**  
   - Candidates must be residential for a minimum of 3 years from the first semester registration.  
2. **Sponsored (Part-time) registration**  
   - Requires employment at an organisation with an MoU with DA-IICT. Application must include a sponsorship certificate.  
3. **External (Part-time) registration**  
   - Requires employment at an R&D organisation/university with at least 3 years of relevant experience and a NOC.

## Courses  
### Course Credits  
#### Engineering  
Students must complete minimum course credits as per Table-1. Course selection is advised by the supervisor/mentor based on the research area.  

#### Humanities, Social Sciences and Design  
- **Core Requirements**: 9 of 21–24 credits (6 credits for MPhil candidates).  
- **Methods Course**: Mandatory discipline-specific methods course in the second semester.  
- **Electives**: Remaining credits from Directed Study, Independent Study, or Reading Courses.  

## Research Credits  
- Research credits (e.g., PC801 Research-1) are registered after passing the comprehensive examination.  
- Minimum 9 credits and maximum 15 credits per semester.  

## Sponsored/External Categories  
Additional requirements:  
- Sponsorship by employer with NOC for course work and comprehensive examination.  
- Minimum 3 years job experience in a reputable organisation.  
- No stipend/scholarship or teaching assistantship provided.  

## Residential Requirement  
- **Regular Category**: Full residency during coursework and research.  
- **Sponsored/External**: 10 working days residency per semester.  
- Exceptions allowed for online courses or external credit transfer with Dean approval.  

## Academic Advising  
- Students are assigned a faculty supervisor/mentor by the Dean (Academic Programs) after passing the comprehensive examination.  

## Comprehensive Examination  
### Engineering  
- **Qualifier Exam**: Tests competency in the broad discipline. Must be passed by:  
  - MTech/ME/MS/MPhil: By third semester.  
  - MSc/MA/BTech/BE/MCA: By fourth semester.  
- **Proposal Defense**: Presented within two months of passing the qualifier.  
- Maximum two attempts; minimum CPI of 7.0/10 required.  

### Humanities, Social Sciences and Design  
- Comprehensive examination evaluates comprehension in relevant subjects.  
- Committee formed by Dean (Academic Programs) to evaluate qualifier and proposal defense.
