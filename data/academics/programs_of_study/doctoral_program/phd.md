# Ph.D.

## Overview
The Doctoral Program leading to the Degree of Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) offers students a career in academia or Research & Development establishments. Graduates are expected to provide a strong foundation and original contribution to knowledge in their chosen area of interest.

## Programme Details

### PhD Programme and Disciplines
Students may pursue Ph.D. studies in:
- Engineering in ICT disciplines, Science and Mathematics  
- Humanities, Social Science and Design  

Research interests of faculty are available [here](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty).

### Categories of Registration
Ph.D. candidates are admitted under three categories:

#### Regular (Full‑time)
Candidates meeting the minimum eligibility criteria register as regular students and are expected to be residential for a minimum of three years from the first semester.

#### Sponsored (Part‑time)
Applicants must be employed by an organization with which DA‑IICT has an MoU. A sponsorship certificate/letter from the head of the department/organization is required.

#### External (Part‑time)
Applicants must be employed by an R&D organization or university/institution with at least three years of relevant experience and must submit a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the head of the organization/institution.

## Program Structure

Students have 3–6 years to complete the program. Requirements vary by background and registration category.

## PhD Process
The typical milestones and processes involved during a Ph.D. are outlined by the institute, including registration, coursework, comprehensive examinations, research work, thesis submission, and viva‑voce.

## Courses

### Engineering
Course credits required depend on the registration category (see Academic Requirements). Supervisors advise appropriate courses based on the student’s research domain.

### Humanities, Social Sciences and Design
All first‑semester students enroll in a mandatory Ph.D. Colloquium. Coursework comprises core and elective components (21‑24 credits total, with 9 credits core). Core requirements are discipline‑specific; MPhil holders have a 6‑credit core. A mandatory methods course is usually taken in the second semester. Courses may include Directed Study, Independent Study, and Reading Courses, approved by the Dean‑AP/PGC.

## Research Credits
Research credits (e.g., PC801 Research‑1, 3 credits) are taken after clearing the comprehensive examination. Students may register for a minimum of 9 and a maximum of 15 credits per semester, matching the course‑credit load.

## Sponsored/External Requirements
Additional conditions for sponsored or external candidates:

- Must be sponsored by their employer.  
- Must complete coursework and comprehensive examination as per academic requirements.  
- Sponsorship letter (and NOC, if applicable) must be submitted at admission, confirming release for coursework and examination and outlining post‑completion employment terms.  
- Minimum three years of relevant job experience required.  
- No stipend/scholarship or teaching assistantship is provided by the institute.

## Residential Requirement
Residency implies campus presence for all working days of a semester.

- Regular students must be residential for both coursework and research credits.  
- Sponsored and external students are not required to be residential for credits but must be on campus for at least ten working days each semester.  
- Under special circumstances, residential requirements may be waived, allowing web‑based courses or credit acquisition from reputable organizations with faculty and Dean (Academic Programs) approval.

## Academic Advising
The Dean (Academic Programs) advises students on course selection and faculty mentor/supervisor assignment. Students must secure a faculty supervisor before the semester in which they clear the comprehensive examination.

## Comprehensive Examination

### Engineering
- Consists of a written qualifier and a proposal defense.  
- Qualifier tests competency in the broad discipline; the syllabus is based on foundation subjects of the candidate’s interest.  
- Proposal defense must be presented within two months of clearing the qualifier.  
- Comprehensive Committee is formed by the Dean (Academic Programs).  
- MTech/ME/MS/MPhil candidates may attempt the qualifier after the first semester (must pass before the third semester).  
- MSc/MA/BTech/BE/MCA candidates may attempt after the second semester (must pass before the fourth semester).  
- Maximum of two attempts; only one comprehensive exam per semester.  
- Minimum CPI of 7.0/10 required.  
- Failure in both attempts leads to discontinuation.  
- Passing the comprehensive exam admits the student to candidacy.

### Humanities, Social Sciences and Design
- Candidates must pass a comprehensive examination assessing overall subject comprehension.  
- The Comprehensive Committee is formed by the Dean (Academic Programs).  
- Additional discipline‑specific details follow the same procedural framework as for Engineering.
