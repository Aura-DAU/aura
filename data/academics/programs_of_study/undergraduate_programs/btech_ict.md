# B.Tech. (ICT)

## Overview
DA‑IICT offers a unique four‑year undergraduate program in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) leading to the degree of **B.Tech. (ICT)** and **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with minor in Robotics and Autonomous Systems**.

## Program Overview
The ICT programme converges Computer and Communication systems and is delivered on a semester‑based, choice‑based credit system.  
Students may graduate with the standard B.Tech. (ICT) or opt for **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with a Minor**. The Minor requires a minimum of 15 additional credits (five courses) taken from Semester 4 to Semester 7. The first Minor offered is **Robotics and Autonomous Systems (RAS)**, available to the B.Tech. (ICT) 2021 batch. A separate program, **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with Minor in Computational Science**, is not covered under this Minor section.

## Program Structure

### Foundation or Core Courses
Compulsory courses taken by every student during the first five semesters. They cover technical areas of Computer Science & Information Technology, Electronics & Communication, as well as Humanities, Mathematics and Basic Sciences.

### Elective Courses
Electives strengthen technical depth and humanities/social‑science skills. Available from the fifth semester onward and grouped as:

* **Program Elective (PE)**
* **Free Elective (FE)**

### Internships and BTech Projects
* **Rural Internship:** Winter break after the 3rd semester with an NGO or government organization.  
* **Industrial/Research Internship:** Summer break after the 6th semester (6–8 weeks).  
* **BTech Project (BTP) / Industrial Training Project (ITP):** Semester‑long on‑campus or off‑campus project undertaken in the final year.

### Co‑curricular Activities and Exploration Project
* **Co‑curricular Activities:** Non‑class activities (sports, cultural, technical clubs) run over the first four semesters, graded Pass/Fail.  
* **Exploration Project:** Two‑semester, group‑based (8–10 students) design‑oriented project undertaken in the 2nd and 3rd semesters, graded Pass/Fail.

## Honours in ICT with Minor
Students may pursue additional coursework to graduate with **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with a Minor**. Completion requires meeting all standard B.Tech. (ICT) requirements plus the extra 15 credits (five courses) specific to the chosen Minor.

## Semester‑wise Course Sequence
Credits follow an L‑T‑P‑C system (C = L + T + P/2). A typical 3‑credit lecture course corresponds to ~40 lecture hours per semester (13–14 weeks).  

*Key abbreviations:* ICTE = ICT Elective; TE = Technical Elective; HASSE = Humanities and Social Science Elective; OE = Open Elective; BTP = BTech Project.
