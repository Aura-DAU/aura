# B.Tech. (ICT)  
[https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-ict](https://www.daiict.ac.in/btech-ict)

## Overview

DA-IICT offers a unique four-year undergraduate program in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) leading to the degree of:  
* **B.Tech. (ICT)**  
* **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with minor in Robotics and Autonomous Systems**

ICT embodies the convergence of Computer and Communication systems and has obtained wide acceptance as a distinct discipline. Programs operate on a semester-based framework with a choice-based credit system.

---

## Program Overview

The B.Tech. (ICT) program provides flexibility through elective courses and specializations. Students may pursue a **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with Minor in Robotics and Autonomous Systems** by completing 15 additional credits (5+ courses) in aligned electives from Semester 4 to 7. The Minor in Robotics and Autonomous Systems (RAS) was first offered to the 2021 batch. Other minors require separate eligibility criteria.

## Program Structure

### Foundation or Core Courses

Compulsory courses in Computer Science, Electronics, Humanities, Mathematics, and Basic Sciences for the first five semesters.

### Elective Courses

Electives grouped into:  
- **Program Elective (PE)**  
- **Free Elective (FE)**  

Available from Semester 5 onward.

### Internships and B.Tech. Projects

- **Rural Internship:** 3rd semester winter break (NGO/Govt. organization).  
- **Industrial/Research Internship:** 6th semester summer break.  
- **B.Tech. Project (BTP):** Semester-long on-campus or off-campus project (Industrial Training Project - ITP).  

### Co-curricular Activities and Exploration Project

- **Co-curricular:** Pass/Fail graded activities in sports, cultural, and technical clubs (Semesters 1–4).  
- **Exploration Project:** Group-based design/hardware projects under faculty mentorship (Semesters 2–3).

## Honours in ICT with Minor

Students may graduate with **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with Minor** by completing 15+ credits in specialized electives. The first Minor offered was **Robotics and Autonomous Systems (RAS)** for the 2021 batch. Other minors require separate eligibility.
