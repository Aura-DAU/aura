# Main Content

**Dhirubhai Ambani University (Formerly DA-IICT)** has played a pioneering role in shaping undergraduate education in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) in India, since its establishment in 2001. The University has consistently focused on creating innovative and interdisciplinary academic programs that integrate Information Technology, Communication Technology, Electronics Engineering, Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, and Social Sciences. This holistic approach reflects the evolving nature of technology-driven disciplines and prepares students to address complex real-world challenges beyond the scope of conventional engineering education.

Over the years, the University has expanded its undergraduate portfolio to address emerging academic and industry needs. Building upon its pioneering, first-of-its-kind undergraduate curriculum in ICT introduced in 2001, the University subsequently launched the B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with a Minor in Computational Science, aimed at providing advanced interdisciplinary training in Computational Modeling and High-Performance Computing. The B.Tech. in Mathematics and Computing (MnC) offers strong foundations in Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science, while the B.Tech. in Electronics and VLSI Design (EVD) prepares students for careers in the rapidly growing semiconductor and VLSI industry.

Beginning Academic Year 2026-27, the University has further expanded its offerings with two new programs aligned with emerging technological transformations. The B.Tech. in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (CS and AI) is designed for students seeking strong foundations in Computer Science along with modern Artificial Intelligence technologies. The B.Tech. in Electronics and Communication Engineering - Artificial Intelligence (ECE-AI) addresses the growing convergence of electronics, communication systems, and AI-driven technologies.

Currently, the University offers the following six undergraduate programs leading to the degree of Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.), each of four years (eight semesters) duration:

**1. Bachelor of Technology in Information and Communication Technology – B.Tech. (ICT)**  
**2. Bachelor of Technology (Honours) in ICT with minor in Computational Science – B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with minor in Computational Science**  
**3. Bachelor of Technology in Mathematics and Computing – B.Tech. (MnC)**  
**4. Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and VLSI Design - B.Tech. (EVD)**  
**5. Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (CS and AI)**: [Commencing from AY 2026-27]  
**6. Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Communication Engineering – Artificial Intelligence (ECE-AI)**: [Commencing from AY 2026-27]

The curricula across all undergraduate programs are carefully structured to provide a balanced combination of foundational courses, advanced electives, interdisciplinary learning opportunities, research and industry internships, and project-based experiences. The University also emphasizes the development of socially responsible and ethically aware graduates. Through its humanities and social sciences courses and internship opportunities, students are encouraged to develop a deeper understanding of societal, organizational, and technological challenges while cultivating leadership, professionalism, and civic responsibility.
