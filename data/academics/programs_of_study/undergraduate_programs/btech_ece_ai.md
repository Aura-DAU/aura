# B.Tech. (ECE-AI)  

## Overview  
The **B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering – Artificial Intelligence (ECE-AI)** at Dhirubhai Ambani University addresses the convergence of electronics, communication technologies, and AI. Modern systems increasingly rely on intelligent algorithms for performance, adaptability, and efficiency in domains like IoT, wireless networks, and autonomous systems.  

The program combines core ECE disciplines (electronic circuits, signals, electromagnetics, communication engineering) with rigorous training in mathematics, programming, and computing. It emphasizes AI, machine learning, and data-driven techniques applied to signal processing, wireless communication, embedded systems, and intelligent electronics. Hands-on learning with tools like simulation environments, embedded platforms, and AI frameworks is prioritized. Graduates are prepared for careers in electronics, semiconductors, telecommunications, and AI industries, as well as advanced research.
