# BS–MS in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

## Overview
A comprehensive five-year dual degree that integrates mathematics, statistics, machine learning, scalable AI systems, and ethical technology practices, enabling graduates to lead in research, innovation, and data‑driven transformation.

## About the Program

### Why This Program?
- **Rigorous Modelling & Systems Thinking** – Strong mathematical foundations combined with scalable, production‑ready AI systems.  
- **Advanced & Trustworthy AI** – Modern machine learning, generative and autonomous systems, built with interpretability and responsibility at the core.  
- **Real‑World & Multi‑Domain Application** – Hands‑on problem solving across finance, energy, healthcare, climate, and public policy.  
- **Flexible, Immersive Learning** – Multi‑exit options, industry internships, applied projects, and research‑driven capstones.

### Data Science vs. Artificial Intelligence
**Data Science** focuses on analysing data, uncovering patterns, and enabling informed decision‑making.  
**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** focuses on building systems that can learn, adapt, reason, and make decisions autonomously.  
The program integrates both, preparing students to move from data understanding to intelligent system creation.

### Who Is This Program For?
- Strong interest in mathematics, statistics, and computing; desire to understand the science behind intelligent systems.  
- Seek depth as well as application, combining rigorous analytical training with hands‑on system design and deployment.  
- Aspire to build next‑generation AI systems, including advanced machine learning, autonomous models, and scalable data platforms.  
- Motivated by research, innovation, and real‑world problem solving across domains such as finance, healthcare, energy, climate, and public policy.  
- Prefer a seamless five‑year academic pathway, progressing from strong foundations to advanced specialization and thesis‑level work within a single integrated program.

### Program Overview
The BS–MS Dual Degree in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence is a comprehensive five‑year program that builds expertise across the full spectrum of data‑driven intelligence: from foundational mathematics to advanced autonomous AI systems.

### Progressive Learning Path
- **Year 1 – Foundations:** Core grounding in programming, mathematics, and introductory data science.  
- **Year 2 – Analytical Depth:** Systems‑oriented computing and statistical modelling.  
- **Year 3 – Applied Machine Learning & AI:** Machine learning, big data systems, and applied AI.  
- **Year 4 – Advanced AI & Deployment:** Specialized AI architectures, scalable deployment frameworks, and domain‑centric applications.  
- **Year 5 – Research, Innovation & Industry Immersion:** Thesis or industry internship with independent problem formulation, solution design, and evaluation.

### Beyond Technical Training
- Humanities and Social Sciences  
- Economics of Data and AI  
- Product Management  
- Entrepreneurship and Technological Innovation  

### Experiential and Applied Learning
- Industry internships  
- Applied laboratory projects  
- Interdisciplinary problem‑solving  
- Research‑focused capstone thesis  

## Structure and Exit Options

### Exit Options
- **After 3 Years – B.Sc. (Bachelor of Science):** Strong grounding in programming, statistics, data science, and core machine learning.  
- **After 4 Years – BS (Bachelor of Science – Honours):** Advanced specialization in data engineering, AI systems, and applied analytics.  
- **After 5 Years – MS (Master of Science / Master of Science by Research):** Expertise in autonomous and trustworthy AI, completed through a research thesis or industry‑focused internship.

## Curriculum Details

### Program Credit Framework
The BS–MS Dual Degree is a **200‑credit** five‑year program balancing theoretical depth, practical capability, and experiential learning.

- **Core Curriculum Distribution – 110 Credits (55 %):**  
  - Computing – 17.5 % (Programming, data structures, algorithms, systems, scalable computing)  
  - Data Science & AI – 16 % (Statistical modelling, machine learning, deep learning, AI systems)  
  - Mathematics – 14 % (Linear algebra, calculus, probability, optimisation, analytical methods)  
  - Humanities & Social Sciences – 7.5 % (Ethics, economics, policy, communication, responsible technology)

- **Elective & Applied Components – 90 Credits (45 %):**  
  - Program Electives – 15.5 % (Advanced AI, data engineering, autonomous systems, domain‑specific analytics)  
  - Open Electives – 4.5 % (Courses from other disciplines)  
  - Online Open Electives – 4.5 % (Curated external courses)  
  - Projects, Seminars & Internships – 18 % (Industry immersion, research engagement, applied AI system design)  
  - Co‑Curricular Activities – 2.5 % (Innovation labs, technical competitions, student clubs, professional development)

### Program Elective Basket Structure
- **Modeling in AI & Machine Learning** – Advanced mathematical, statistical, and algorithmic foundations for intelligent models.  
- **Systems & Engineering for AI** – Scalable, reliable, production‑ready AI system design, deployment, and infrastructure.  
- **Domains & Applied Analytics** – Application of data science and AI techniques to real‑world sectoral challenges.  
- **Ethics, Governance & Emerging AI** – Responsible AI, policy, interpretability, and emerging trends in trustworthy intelligent systems.  

*Elective courses are indicative and may be revised based on faculty expertise and resource availability.*  
*Students must select courses from two different domains listed under the “Domains & Applied Analytics” category.*
