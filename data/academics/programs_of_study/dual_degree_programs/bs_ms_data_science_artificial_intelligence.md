# BS–MS in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (newly launched)

## Overview  
The BS–MS Dual Degree in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence is a 200-credit, five-year program designed to balance theoretical depth, practical capability, and experiential learning.  

## About the Program  
### Why This Program?  
This program equips students with advanced skills in data science and AI, preparing them for leadership roles in technology-driven industries. It emphasizes interdisciplinary learning, combining computing, mathematics, and domain-specific applications.
