# Program Regulations

This document outlines the rules for specific degree programs, research projects, and graduation requirements at DA-IICT.

## 1. B.Tech (Honours in ICT with minor in Computational Science)

### Course Load
- **Maximum Load**: Students may register for up to 7 courses or 29.5 credits per semester.
- **Minimum Load**: Students may under-load to a minimum of 4 courses or 12 credits.

### Award of Degree
To graduate, a student must fulfill the following:
1. **Credits**: Pass a minimum of 170 course credits (129 foundation, remaining electives). 18 credits must come from Computational Science core courses.
2. **Grade Points**: Earn a minimum of 850 grade points (170 x 5.0).
3. **CPI Requirement**: Achieve a final CPI of at least 6.5. (Students completing the requirements but falling below 6.5 CPI will be awarded a standard B.Tech in ICT).
4. **B.Tech Project**: Obtain a passing grade for the B.Tech Project.

### Transfers Between B.Tech Programs
- **Continuation in Honours**: Must maintain a CPI of 6.5 after Semester III; otherwise, moved to standard B.Tech (ICT).
- **Transfer to Honours**: Students in standard B.Tech (ICT) who obtain a CPI of 7.5+ may apply for transfer to the Honours program at the end of their third semester.

## 2. Ph.D. Program Regulations

### Course & Research Credits
- **Ph.D. after B.Tech/M.Sc.**: Must complete a minimum of 33 course credits and 48 research credits (Minimum residency: 8 semesters).
- **Ph.D. after M.Tech/M.Phil.**: Must complete a minimum of 12 course credits and 48 research credits (Minimum residency: 6 semesters).

### Comprehensive Examination
1. **Eligibility**: Students must maintain a minimum CPI of 7.0/10.
2. **Attempts**: A maximum of two attempts are allowed to pass the comprehensive examination. Failing both attempts results in discontinuation from the program.
3. **Timeline**: 
   - B.Tech/M.Sc entrants must pass before the end of their fourth semester.
   - M.Tech/M.Phil entrants must pass before the end of their third semester.

### Academic Performance
- **Minimum CPI**: Ph.D. students must maintain a minimum CPI of 7.0 for graduation.
- **Unsatisfactory Performance**: Obtaining more than 5 unsatisfactory ('X') grades during the research phase leads to termination from the program.

### Synopsis & Thesis
- Students must register for and successfully defend a Ph.D. Synopsis (3 credits).
- Following a successful synopsis, the Ph.D. thesis must be submitted within 6 months.

## 3. M.Tech Migration from Ph.D.
Ph.D. students who fail the comprehensive examination may migrate to the M.Tech program if they have completed at least 18 course credits with a minimum CPI of 6.0. They must fulfill all M.Tech requirements within two years of migration.
