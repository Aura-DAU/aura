## Course Description
ICT in itself may not generate a vivid picture of itself, but it encompasses almost all walks of our lives. The prime objective of this course is to make students aware of the ubiquity of ICT. Therefore, this is a foundation course designed to provide students a contextual understanding of different facets of ICT and how the information and communication technologies (ICT) manifest in various existing and emerging verticals of science, technology and engineering.

Lectures are planned in a way to expose the students to many of the STEAM verticals. They comprise various problem statements considering real-world issues and the need to provide ICT-based solutions to abate those issues. While the lectures help students to "think and ideate," lab work involves "visualization" of spaces and objects and ultimately the solutions – 1D, 2D, 3D projections, isometric views of lines, and embedding of solids are introduced in the Lab classes. 

As future ICT engineers, these students have to observe challenging issues around them and solve them to improve the quality of life for society at large. These solutions, within the scope of ICT, may be from a pure design part, from a mechanical design aspect and/or from an electrical/electronics point of view. The Lab classes try to give them a glimpse of these three modules as far as possible.

## Course Structure

| IC101 Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Lecture Plan | Applications of ICT towards topics such as AI/ML/Deep Learning, Communication Technology, Computational/Data Science, Design, Fashion & Music, Healthcare, IoT/Automation/Robotics, Landscaping, Mathematical Science, Photography & Art, Quantum Computing, Science Education, Semiconductor industry, Social Media, Projects demonstration, and TRLs | Not specified |
| Lab: Product Design | Introduction to AutoCAD, AutoCAD + Video, Multiview drawing, Dimensions, Object Design, Demo-Rhino, 3D Printer | Not specified |
| Lab: Mechanical Design | Drilling & Milling Machine, Dremel, Bench Lathe Machine, Octagon Tabletop CNC Lathe Machine, Welding machine, Grinder Machine | Not specified |
| Lab: Electronic Design | Recognizing electronic components, Footprints and PCB, Power consumption, Reading spec sheets, PCB Design on DipTrace/Eagle, Soldering, PCB fabrication and populating components, Final designed PCB performance testing | Not specified |
