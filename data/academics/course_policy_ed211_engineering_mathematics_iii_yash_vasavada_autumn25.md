## Detailed Course Content

The course ED211 introduces the students to the various mathematical and engineering techniques related to time and frequency analysis and synthesis, Fourier Transformation, Fourier Series, etc. The course demonstrates application of elegant mathematical principles to practical engineering design concepts underlying many algo- rithms implemented in today’s electronics and VLSI systems. The students gain hands- on skills on these topics by performing software laboratory experiments throughout the semester. The specific topics covered are continuous-time Fourier Transform, Fourier Series, effect of sampling, discrete time Fourier Transform, DFT, Z transform, convolu- tion and linear time/shift-invariant systems, design of digital filters, eigenanalysis and its application to OFDM signaling, etc. Course Description: Introduction to Signals and Systems.Fourier Transformation, Fourier Series, Discrete Time Signals.Sampling and its effects Analysis and Processing of Discrete Time Signals.Discrete Time Fourier Transform (DTFT), Linear Shift Invariant (LSI) systems, impulse response, frequency response Z transformation.Z transformation and its relation to Laplace transform and DTFT, Poles and Zeros, Region of Convergence. Digital Systems.Design and analysis of digital filters.
