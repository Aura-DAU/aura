# Careers/ Faculty on Tenure

## Overview
Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA‑IICT) invites applications for full‑time faculty positions from highly motivated individuals with a strong record of independent, high‑quality research and a commitment to teaching undergraduate and postgraduate students.

## Faculty Recruitment
### Current Openings
DA‑IICT seeks faculty at the Assistant and Associate Professor levels in the following broad areas:

- Computer Science – [link](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-computer-science)  
- Data Science – [link](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-data-science)  
- Electronics & VLSI Design – [link](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-electronics-and-vlsi-design)  
- Mathematics & Statistics – [link](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-mathematics-statistics)  
- Humanities & Social Sciences – [link](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-humanities-and-social-sciences)  
- Communication Design – [link](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-communication-design)

### Minimum Eligibility Criteria *(all disciplines except design area candidates)*
1. **Ph.D.** with first class or equivalent and an excellent academic record.  
2. A strong research record with publications in reputed journals and conferences.

### Assistant Professor
- Ph.D. with strong research capabilities and a passion for teaching at UG and PG levels.  
- Post‑doctoral experience is preferred.

### Associate Professor
- Minimum six years post‑Ph.D. experience (including at least three years as Assistant Professor).  
- Strong research record demonstrated by publications, external grants/projects, and doctoral supervision.

## Application Submission Process
Prospective candidates should email **dean_faculty[at]daiict[dot]ac[dot]in** with the subject “_Faculty position in Disciplines/Areas_” (e.g., Computer Science, Humanities & Social Sciences). Attach:

1. **CV** – education details from 12th standard (marks/CGPA, year, specialization), work experience, publications, and three references.  
2. **Research Statement** – background, outcomes, and future plans.  
3. **Teaching Statement** – methodology, experience, core and elective courses you wish to teach.

Candidates with interdisciplinary expertise are strongly encouraged to apply.

## About the Institute
DA‑IICT is a State Private University spread over 50 acres in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. Recognised as a Center of Excellence in 2021, it offers UG, PG, and doctoral programs in:

- Information and Communication Technology  
- Mathematics & Computing  
- Electronics & VLSI Design  
- Information Technology  
- Computational and Data Sciences  
- Agriculture Analytics  
- Communication Design  

Further program details: <https://www.daiict.ac.in/programs-of-study>. Research areas: [here](https://www.daiict.ac.in/research-areas).

## Faculty Position Philosophy
Since its founding in 2001, DA‑IICT has emphasized equal importance of teaching and research. Prospective faculty are expected to:

- Conduct independent research.  
- Teach UG and PG courses.  
- Participate in institutional activities.  

The institute hosts ~1,700 UG and 600 PG students, selected on merit. Ph.D. students receive a generous stipend; M.Tech. students receive TA stipends.

## Academics and Teaching Infrastructure
- Faculty teach three courses per academic year (two in one semester, one in the other).  
- Core courses are supported by Teaching Assistants (TA numbers depend on course registration).  
- Facilities include three air‑cooled lecture theatres (capacity >300), modern AV systems, well‑equipped classrooms, and dedicated research laboratories.  
- Campus-wide Wi‑Fi.

## Research Environment
DA‑IICT promotes interdisciplinary research across technology, basic sciences, humanities, and social sciences. Support includes:

- Access to digital libraries (ACM, IEEE, Springer LNCS, JSTOR).  
- Need‑based funding for labs and equipment.  
- Encouragement for industry collaborations and international grant participation.  
- One consulting day per week for faculty.  
- Detailed research information: [research overview](https://www.daiict.ac.in/research-overview); faculty publications: [here](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-publications).

## Quality of Living
Gandhinagar offers well‑planned sectors, rental housing, and reputable schools (DPS, Hillwoods, Podar, Jamnabai Narsee). Ahmedabad (30 km away) provides additional housing, schooling, and healthcare options. Campus amenities include a visiting doctor, a network of consultants, and arrangements with hospitals such as Apollo. The city enjoys uninterrupted water and power supply. The nearest airport (20 min drive) and railway station (≈50 min drive) in Ahmedabad provide domestic and international connectivity.
