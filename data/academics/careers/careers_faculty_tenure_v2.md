# Careers/Faculty on Tenure  

## Overview  
Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT) invites applications for full-time faculty positions from highly motivated individuals with a good record of conducting independent, high-quality research and a strong commitment to teaching and educating a diverse group of students at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels.  

## Faculty Recruitment  
DA-IICT seeks highly motivated faculty members at the **Assistant** and **Associate Professor** levels in the following disciplines:  
- Computer Science  
- Data Science  
- Electronics and VLSI Design  
- Mathematics & Statistics  
- Humanities and Social Sciences  
- Communication Design  

### Current Openings  
[Computer Science](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-computer-science) | [Data Science](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-data-science) | [Electronics and VLSI Design](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-electronics-and-vlsi-design)  
[Mathematics & Statistics](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-mathematics-statistics) | [Humanities and Social Sciences](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-humanities-and-social-sciences) | [Communication Design](https://www.daiict.ac.in/faculty-positions-communication-design)  

### Minimum Eligibility Criteria  
**(i)** Ph.D. with a first class or equivalent in the preceding degree and an excellent academic record throughout.  
**(ii)** A strong research record with publications in reputed journals and conferences.  

### Assistant Professor  
- Ph.D. with strong research capabilities and a passion for teaching at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.  
- Postdoctoral experience is preferred.  

### Associate Professor  
- Minimum six years post-Ph.D. teaching/research/professional experience, with at least three years at the Assistant Professor level.  
- Strong research record evidenced by publications, external grants, and doctoral supervision experience.  

## Application Submission Process  
Prospective candidates should email **dean_faculty[at]daiict[dot]ac[dot]in** with the subject line: “Faculty position in [Disciplines/Areas]”. Attach the following:  
1. **CV**: Include education (12th standard onward), work experience, publications, and three reference names.  
2. **Research Statement**: Background, outcomes, and future plans.  
3. **Teaching Statement**: Methodology, experience, and course preferences.  

## About the Institute  
DA-IICT, a State Private University in Gandhinagar, Gujarat, was recognized as a Center of Excellence in 2021. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in:  
- Information and Communication Technology  
- Mathematics & Computing  
- Electronics & VLSI Design  
- Information Technology  
- Computational and Data Sciences  
- Agriculture Analytics  
- Communication Design  

Doctoral programs span **Computer Science**, **Electronics & Communication**, **Mathematical, Physical & Computational Sciences**, **Humanities**, **Social Sciences**, and **Design**.  

## Research Environment  
DA-IICT emphasizes interdisciplinary research with well-equipped labs and access to digital libraries (ACM, IEEE, Springer LNCS, JSTOR). Faculty are encouraged to:  
- Pursue bilateral/international research grants.  
- Collaborate with industry and academia.  
- Apply for seed money (up to ₹10 lakh) and professional development funds (₹3 lakh every three years).  

## Quality of Living  
Gandhinagar offers:  
- Residential options in sectors near the campus.  
- Schools (DPS, Hillwoods, Podar).  
- Healthcare: Campus doctors, Apollo hospital partnerships.  
- Proximity to Ahmedabad (30 km) for additional housing and amenities.  
- Reliable utilities and connectivity to Ahmedabad’s airport (20-minute drive).
