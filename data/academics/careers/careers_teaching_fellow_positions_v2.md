# Careers / Teaching Fellow Positions Open at DAU

## Overview

## Teaching Fellow Positions Open at DAU

### Autumn Semester AY 2026–27 (2nd round)

The **Teaching Fellow (TF)** position offers a unique chance to contribute to undergraduate and postgraduate learning while gaining valuable academic experience.

- Work closely with experienced faculty  
- Lead labs and support innovative courses  
- Engage in interdisciplinary learning  
- Fields include AI, ML, Data Science, Systems, Electronics, Networks and more  

### Preference Domains

- Computer Science and Information Technology  
- Data Science and Artificial Intelligence  
- Electronics and Communication Engineering  
- Electronics and VLSI  
- Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS)
