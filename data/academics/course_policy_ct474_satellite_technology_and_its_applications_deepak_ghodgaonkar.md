Objectives:

The objectives of this course is to develop understanding  of satellite technology and its wide
ranging applications in communications, remote sensing, weather forecasting , navigation and global
position ing system, scientific research and military  systems. After study of this course, students
will develop knowledge regarding orbits, launch vehicles, satellite hardware and subsystems,
multiple access, link design and earth stations.
