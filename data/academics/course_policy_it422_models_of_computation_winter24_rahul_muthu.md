# IT422 Models of Computation Winter24 - Rahul Muthu

## Overview
This document presents the course syllabus, policy guidelines, and lecture breakdown for IT422 Models of Computation Winter24 - Rahul Muthu. **Last Modified:** 2023-12-15  

## Course Details
### Format & Prerequisites
- Elective offered in the third and fourth years of the B.Tech programmes.  
- Course format: 3 lectures per week, one hour each.  
- Prerequisites: Discrete Mathematics and Data Structures/Algorithms.

### Objectives
After successfully completing this course, students will:
- Be equipped to model problems, both abstract and real, in the areas of computability, algorithmic complexity and formal language framework.  
- Be able to deploy this knowledge in the software disciplines of compiler design as well as formal specification and verification.

### Textbooks & References
- **Textbook:** *Automata and Computability* by Dexter C. Kozen. Springer, 1997/2012/2013.  
- **Reference books:**  
  - *Introduction to Theory of Computation*, 2nd Ed, Michael Sipser, Wadsworth Publishing Co Inc, 2012.  
  - *Languages and Machines*, 3rd Ed, Thomas A. Sudkamp, Addison Wesley, 2006.  
  - *An Introduction to Formal Languages and Automata*, Narosa Publishing House, 2007.

## Evaluation
- One in‑semester exam (20%)  
- One end‑semester exam (50%)  
- Quizzes/assignments (20%)

## Lecture Schedule
### Topics and Lecture Count
| Sl. No. | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mathematical Preliminaries | 4 |
| 2 | Finite State Automata & Regular Languages | 12 |
| 3 | Pushdown Automata & Context‑Free Languages | 8 |
| 4 | Turing Machines & Undecidability | 6 |

### Detailed Contents
1. **Mathematical preliminaries:**  
   - Sets, Strings and operations on them.  
   - Formal languages and their applicability in modelling computational problems.  

2. **Finite state automata & regular languages:**  
   - Finite state machines (deterministic, non‑deterministic and ε‑moves), subset construction.  
   - Closure properties and fancy regular languages.  
   - Regular languages, regular expressions and equivalence.  
   - Regular grammars.  
   - Finite equivalences.  
   - Pumping lemma.  

3. **Pushdown automata & context‑free languages:**  
   - Context‑free grammars.  
   - Deterministic and non‑deterministic CFLs.  
   - Acceptance by empty stack or final state.  
   - Pumping lemma.  

4. **Turing machines & undecidability:**  
   - Deterministic and non‑deterministic Turing machines.  
   - Decision and computational variants.  
   - Undecidability.
