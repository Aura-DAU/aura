# B.Tech Project (BTP) Guidelines and Related Information

## Overview

The **B.Tech Project (BTP)** is a major capstone component of the B.Tech programme at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT). BTP presentations are scheduled at the end of the Winter semester. Detailed guidelines, evaluation criteria, and supervisor allocation are managed by the Dean (AP) office.

## Important Dates

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| BTP Exploration Project Demo (Autumn 2026–27, fresh batch) | 2 December 2026 |
| B.Tech Project Presentation (Winter 2026–27) | 3 – 4 May 2027 (tentative) |

## Process Overview

- BTP supervisor allocation is coordinated by the Dean (AP) office.
- Students and supervisors must follow the evaluation criteria specified in the BTP guidelines document.
- The Exploration Project (EP) demo precedes the full BTP presentation.
