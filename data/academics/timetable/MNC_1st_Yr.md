## Important context

DA-IICT's 1st-year curriculum is genuinely common across branches — it runs on a shared "Common Curriculum Framework," and this matches what your own semester-1/2 course-structure sheets show (Institute Core courses like Intro to ICT, Calculus, Programming, Basic Electronic Circuits, Language and Literature are identical across every branch table you sent). Branch identity barely shows up in the raw timetable data at all — the ONE place it does is a set of six parallel "PC1" (Program Core 1) sessions run at the same three time slots (Mon 9, Wed 8, Thu 10), each in a different room:

- PC1 (ICT) — Rm CEP-106, faculty RM
- PC1 (MNC) — Rm CEP-204, faculty VS
- PC1 (EVD) — Rm CEP-205, faculty AR
- PC1 (CS-AI) — Rm CEP-103, faculty SCN
- PC1 (unlabeled) — Rm CEP-108, faculty SB2
- PC1 (unlabeled) — Rm CEP-206, faculty AB

**Two flags to verify before this goes anywhere official:**
1. "CS-AI" is a separate DAU program (B.Tech Computer Science & AI) per the program-of-study pages in your data — it is NOT the same as the "ICT-CS" honors/specialization track in the images you sent. ICT-CS only diverges from plain ICT starting Semester 4 (per your own charts), so at 1st year, ICT and future-ICT-CS students are the same PC1 (ICT) group — there is no separate 1st-year PC1 for ICT-CS.
2. The two unlabeled PC1 rows (SB2/CEP-108 and AB/CEP-206) have no branch tag in the source data at all. I can't confidently assign them to a program — they may be a second section of one of the four named branches (most likely ICT, since it's typically the largest cohort) or something else entirely. Flagging rather than guessing.
