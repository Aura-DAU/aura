# Notices and Circulars

## Overview

All official notices — academic, administrative, and co-curricular — are published on the **Intranet** and **eCampus portal** at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT). Important announcements are also posted on the DAU website's Announcements page. Each student and faculty member receives an institutional email ID through which personal notices and sensitive communications are shared.

## Where to Find Notices

| Source | Type of Content | Link |
|---|---|---|
| DAU Announcements Page | Public announcements, press releases | [DAU Announcements](https://www.daiict.ac.in/announcements) |
| Intranet (Internal Circulars) | Internal administrative and academic circulars | [DA-IICT Intranet](https://ecampus.daiict.ac.in/webapp/intranet/index.jsp) |
| eCampus Portal (Student Notices) | Course-specific and student-facing notices | [eCampus — DAU](https://ecampus.daiict.ac.in) |
| Institutional Email | Personal notices, exam dates, fee reminders | Provided to all registered students and faculty |

## Categories of Notices

- **Academic Notices** — exam schedules, result announcements, policy changes
- **Administrative Notices** — fee payment deadlines, registration reminders, hostel notices
- **Co-Curricular Notices** — club events, SBG announcements, competitions
- **Examination Department** — seating arrangements, malpractice notices, grade sheets

## Additional Notes

Students and faculty should check the Intranet and their institutional email regularly, as some notices are circulated only via email and may not be posted publicly.
