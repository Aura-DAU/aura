3. Prerequisite Concepts: Calculus - Taylor’s series, Taylor’s remainder theorem, Con-

function, Jacobian matrix, Hessian matrix of a function. Linear/Matrix Algebra - Rank

4. CourseSummary: Alotofproblemsacrossengineeringandmanagementcanbemod-
eled as optimization problems. Optimization problems come in various forms - Con-
tinuous and Discrete or Combinatorial Optimization being two of the most popular
ones. This course deals exclusively with Continuous optimization, and aims to intro-
duce both, theory and algorithms in Continuous optimization. The course will intro-
duce students to basic terminology in optimization, theory - conditions for existence
ofpointsofoptima(maxima/minima),andalgorithmstosolveoptimizationproblems.
We will deal with both, linear and non-linear continuous optimization problems, fo-
cusingmoreonthelatter.
5. CourseContent:
(a) BasicterminologyinOptimization: Localoptima,GlobalOptima,Equivalenceof
minimizationandmaximization,Convexsetsandfunctions.
(b) SetConstrained/Unconstrainedoptimizationtheory: FirstandSecondorderNec-
essary/Sufficientconditionsforoptima.
(c) Unconstrained optimization algorithms: Gradient Methods, Newton’s method,
Conjugatedirectionmethods.
(d) Constrained non-linear optimization theory: Feasibility set and surface, Active
and inactive conditions, Lagrange multiplier, Karush-Kuhn-Tucker(KKT) condi-
tion.
(e) Special cases of Constrained Optimization: Linear programming, Basic feasible
solutions,Simplexmethod.
(f) Duality: Lagrangian duality, Geometric interpretation, Duality gap, Saddle point
interpretation.
(g) (Iftimepermits)ConstrainedOptimizationalgorithms: Barrier&Penaltymethods
6. References:
(a) AnIntroductiontoOptimization,ChongandZak,Fourthed.,Wiley.
(b) LinearandNonlinearProgramming,LuenbergerandYe,Fifthed.,Springer.
