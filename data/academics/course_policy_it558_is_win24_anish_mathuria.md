### Evaluation

There wi ll be two mid -semester exa ms at the officially scheduled time for the course. Pop-quizzes
(not announced in advance) will be held  in class throughout the semester. There will be no makeup
quizzes for any reason.  There will be  no final  exam . Project presen tations will be held during
the end -of-term class periods.

 40%    mid-semester  exams  20% project  20%  lab assignment s  20% in-class pop-quizzes .

Course Project Students (in teams of 2) will be responsible for a semester long project about
computer security. The project can involve design/implementation or survey/analysis. The topic of
the p roject needs approval of the instructor. The project will be evaluated based on a written
report and oral presentation.
