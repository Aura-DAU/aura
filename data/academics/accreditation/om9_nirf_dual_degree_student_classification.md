# NIRF Reporting Classification of Five-Year Dual Degree Students at DAU

## Key Fact

For NIRF (National Institutional Ranking Framework) reporting purposes, the five-year B.Tech + M.Tech dual degree students at DAU are classified based on their **current year of study**:

- **Years 1–4 (Semesters I–VIII):** Classified as **Undergraduate (UG)** students, since they are primarily completing B.Tech (ICT) requirements during these years.
- **Year 5 (Semesters IX–X + 5th Summer):** Classified as **Postgraduate (PG)** students, since they are completing M.Tech (ICT) requirements during this final year.

## NIRF Data Collection Context

Under the NIRF Data Collection Manual:
- Students enrolled in integrated programs (e.g., 5-year B.Tech + M.Tech) are counted in the **UG category** during the years they are completing undergraduate-level coursework.
- Upon transition to the postgraduate phase (when M.Tech-specific courses and research credits dominate, typically Year 5), students are counted in the **PG category**.
- Since both degrees are awarded simultaneously at the end of 5 years, the student is reported under UG for years 1–4 and under PG for year 5 in NIRF data submissions.

## DAU NIRF Submissions

DAU submits NIRF reports under two discipline categories:
1. **Overall** – covers all students
2. **Engineering Discipline** – covers Engineering, Technology, and ICT programs

Dual degree students appear in both the Overall and Engineering Discipline reports, split across UG and PG categories as described above.

## Summary Table

| Year of Program | Phase | NIRF Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 (Sem I–II) | B.Tech | UG |
| Year 2 (Sem III–IV) | B.Tech | UG |
| Year 3 (Sem V–VI) | B.Tech | UG |
| Year 4 (Sem VII–VIII + 4th Summer) | B.Tech transition + M.Tech start | UG |
| Year 5 (Sem IX–X + 5th Summer) | M.Tech completion | PG |

## Summary Answer

**Five-year dual degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) students at DAU are classified as UG students in NIRF reporting for Years 1–4 and as PG students in Year 5, reflecting the phase of their degree completion at the time of reporting.**
