# Overview

Details of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at DA-IICT (now Dhirubhai Ambani University), including its objectives, functions, current membership, past compositions, meeting minutes, and annual quality assurance reports.

# Main Content

### About IQAC

In pursuance of the National Action Plan of the **National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC)**, Bangalore, for performance evaluation, assessment and accreditation and quality up-gradation of institutions of higher education, the NAAC proposes that every accredited institution establish an **Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)** as a post-accreditation quality sustenance measure.

Quality enhancement is a continuous process and hence IQAC is expected to be an important part of the system. It works towards enhancement and sustenance of the entire institute. IQAC is expected to help the institute to realize the goals to become an **institute of excellence**.

The focus of the IQAC is to develop a system for conscious and consistent improvement in the performance of the institute. The IQAC will make a significant and meaningful contribution in the post-accreditation phase of institutions.

### Objectives of IQAC

- Ensuring a continuous improvement in the operation, both academic and administrative, of the Institute
- Obtaining continuous and useful feedback from all stakeholders (faculty, staff, students, parents, alumni, employers, and funding agencies)
- Developing quality benchmarks/parameters for various academic and administrative activities
- Exploring various quality parameters of higher education and best practices followed by other institutions and implementing those in the institute
- Preparing the **Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR)**

### Functions of IQAC

- Assess the goals and objectives of the Institute as stated in its vision and mission
- Develop a prospective plan for quality improvement through action plans in:
  - Teaching-learning process
  - Infrastructure
  - Student support
  - Research and Development
  - Retaining and attracting faculty
- Make resolutions and frame policies and plans for quality improvement
- Evaluate and monitor the resolutions passed and framed policies
- Assess stakeholder feedback for corrective measures and improving institutional effectiveness
- Monitor academic leadership and faculty participation in functional committees
- Assess the management of human resources
- Make strategies to empower faculty
- Monitor the effective use of financial resources and financial management
- Take inputs from external/industry experts and incorporate them in institutional development
