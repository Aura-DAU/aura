# Grading Policy

Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU) follows a 10-point letter grading system to evaluate student academic performance. Academic standing is measured through the Semester Performance Index (SPI) and Cumulative Performance Index (CPI).

# Main Content

### 1. Grading Scale
Letter grades are awarded at the end of each semester for registered courses, corresponding to numerical grade points on a 10-point scale:

| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Performance Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **AA** | 10 | Outstanding |
| **AB** | 9 | Excellent |
| **BB** | 8 | Very Good |
| **BC** | 7 | Good |
| **CC** | 6 | Fair |
| **CD** | 5 | Average |
| **DD** | 4 | Pass (Marginal) |
| **DE** | 3 | Poor |
| **F** | 0 | Fail |
| **I** | - | Incomplete (Assigned under exceptional circumstances) |
| **W** | - | Withdrawn |
| **P** | - | Pass (Used for audit courses, not included in CPI) |

---

### 2. SPI & CPI Calculation
- **Semester Performance Index (SPI)**: The weighted average of the grade points obtained in all courses registered during a specific semester.
  $$\text{SPI} = \frac{\sum (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum C_i}$$
  where $C_i$ is the credits of the $i$-th course and $G_i$ is the grade points obtained in that course.
- **Cumulative Performance Index (CPI)**: The weighted average of the grade points obtained across all semesters.
  $$\text{CPI} = \frac{\sum_{\text{all semesters}} (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum_{\text{all semesters}} C_i}$$

### 3. Graduation & Degree Requirements

#### Standard B.Tech (ICT) Graduation Requirements
To graduate and qualify for the award of the B.Tech degree, a student must meet the following:
- **Minimum Credits**: Completion of at least **175 credits** (comprising at least 152 course credits and 23 credits from internships and the B.Tech Project).
- **Minimum Grade Points**: A minimum total of **760 grade points** (equivalent to an average CPI of 5.0).
- **Minimum CPI**: Maintain a final Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) of at least **5.0**.
- **Course Clearance**: Pass all prescribed courses (no outstanding 'F' grades).

#### B.Tech (Honours) Requirements
For students pursuing B.Tech (Honours) in ICT with a minor:
- **Additional Credits**: Completion of a minimum of **15 additional credits** (at least five courses) in the minor area.
- **Honours CPI Threshold**: A minimum final CPI of **6.5** is required. If a student falls below 6.5 CPI but meets standard requirements, they receive the standard B.Tech (ICT) degree.

---

### 4. Grade Appeal Process
Students who believe there is an error in their assigned grade can appeal through the Controller of Examination (CoE) within the timeline specified in the academic calendar. The appeal is reviewed by the Course Instructor and Dean (Academic Programs).
