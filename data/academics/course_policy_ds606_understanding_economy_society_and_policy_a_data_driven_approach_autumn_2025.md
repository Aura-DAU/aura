## Course Description
This course introduces students to foundational economic concepts in macroeconomics and development economics. The interconnections and complementarities between economics, society and government policies will be discussed. Students will be encouraged to recognise and appreciate the two-way relationship between economics and policy-making. 

The course will focus on economic reasoning through the analysis and interpretation of real-world data. Lectures will be supplemented with guided empirical analysis of real-world economic problems.

## Course Structure

| DS606 Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding what economics is (and what it is not) | Introducing basic concepts in economics such as opportunity costs, incentives and trade-offs. Discussing the connections between economics, society and policies. | 2 |
| Understanding prosperity and well-being | Defining GDP, GDP/capita, economic growth, HDI. Critiquing these measures. Lab exercise: Identifying trends in GDP, economic growth and HDI for various countries. | 4 |
| Understanding poverty and inequality | Measuring poverty, inequality using alternative methods. Interpreting Lorenz curves and GINI coefficients. Lab exercise: Plotting Lorenz curves and interpreting them for various countries. | 3 |
| Understanding unemployment and labour market frictions | Discussing the different types of unemployment and their implications for society and the individual. Discussing policies to address unemployment. Lab exercise: Discovering the relation between work status and life satisfaction. | 3 |
| Understanding global socio-economic concerns | Discussing the causes and implications of global concerns through the lens of Sustainable Development Goals framework. Lab exercise: Investigating trends in global temperatures and carbon emissions. | 3 |
| Understanding the motivation and implications of government policies, with a focus on Indian governance issues | India's growth and development story over the years. Recognizing challenges and success stories in governing India. Discussing effects of government policies. Lab exercise: Group discussion based on contemporary podcasts of economic and policy experts; Critiquing the cash handout program of the Hong Kong government. | 6 |
| Understanding the banking and credit sector | Introducing the role of banks and the flow of credit in the economy. Recognising the constraints faced by households in accessing credit. Lab exercise: Comparing banking systems across the world; Analysing the determinants of credit access for households. | 3 |
| Presentation skills | Lab exercise: Preparing dos and dont's list on delivering good presentations. | 4 |
| Group project presentations | Group project presentations | 6 |
