## Detailed Course Content

The WSSOA course provides an in-depth exploration of modern distributed computing paradigms, focusing on the design, development, and deployment of interoperable services across heterogeneous platforms. The course covers foundational concepts such as XML, JSON, and RESTful principles, along with advanced topics including SOAP-based services, WSDL, UDDI, and security standards like WS- Security and OAuth. Students will gain hands-on experience with API design, micro-services architectures, cloud-based service deployment, and integration techniques, while critically examining scalability, reliability, and performance considerations in enterprise environments. Through case studies and lab work, learners will bridge theoretical understanding with practical implementation, preparing them for research or industry roles in building robust, scalable, and secure service-oriented solutions. Books and Literature: •Service-Oriented Architecture: Concepts, Technology, and Design by Thomas E. •SOA Principles of Service Design by T Erl. •Service Design Patterns: Fundamental Design Solutions for SOAP/WSDL and RESTful Web Services by Robert Daigneau •Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture by Martin Fowler •Enterprise Integration Patterns: Designing, Building, and Deploying Messaging Solutions by Gregor Hohpe & Bobby Woolf •Building Microservices by Sam Newman Assessment Method: 6-8 lab assignments, a group project, class tests, and end-semester examination. Course Outcomes: By the end of the Web Services and Service-Oriented Architecture course, postgraduate students will be able to design, implement, and deploy interoperable, scalable, and secure service-based applications that integrate seamlessly across heterogeneous systems. They will demonstrate proficiency in creating both RESTful and SOAP-based services, applying industry standards such as WSDL, UDDI, XML, JSON, WS-Security, and OAuth, and utilizing micro-services and cloud platforms for distributed deployments. Students will be capable of critically evaluating service design patterns, ensuring reliability, scalability, and maintainability in enterprise environments. Furthermore, they will develop the analytical and practical skills to architect service-oriented solutions that address real- world business challenges, bridging the gap between theoretical concepts and professional implementation. After successful completion of the course the student will have the ability to, ●Understand, analyze, plan, manage, and implement heterogeneous software systems (P1, P2, P12). ●Apply software design principles for cloud implementation / handling software projects using various tools and techniques (P3, P5, P11). ●Plan, monitor, track, and review software projects in a large group setup (P9, P11). ●Create software architecture and related documents, detailed project plans, schedules, along with necessary software implementation strategy.
