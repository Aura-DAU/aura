# Academic Requirements for the PhD Program (Effective from Autumn 2017-18)

## Registration

### Categories of Registration
- **Resident registration** – counts toward the residence requirement for the degree. A foreign student on a student visa cannot register as an external student.  
- **External registration** – for a duly approved research programme; the PG Committee may permit/require registration for coursework as well.  
- A student is **not required to register** in the semester in which the final PhD thesis is submitted for evaluation.  
- **Residency requirement:**  
  - Holders of an M.Tech, M.Phil, or equivalent postgraduate degree must complete **six semesters** of residency (regular campus attendance and attendance verification).  
  - Holders of a B.Tech, M.Sc, or equivalent degree must complete **eight semesters** of residency.  
- After fulfilling the residency requirement, irrespective of full‑time or external status, a student must pay the prescribed semester tuition fee and registration fee.  

### Provisional Registration
New entrants awaiting the results of the qualifying examination may be allowed *provisional* registration. By the date given in the Academic Calendar (usually about 8 weeks from registration), candidates must submit, for verification, the certificates of having passed the qualifying examination. Original certificates will be returned; a self‑attested copy will be kept for records.

### Late Registration
- If a student cannot register on the official registration day due to a compelling reason (e.g., illness), the student may register during the **late registration period** specified in the Academic Calendar (approximately one week after the regular date). A late registration fee is payable.  
- In exceptional cases, the Dean (Academic Programs), on recommendation of the Post‑Graduate Committee (PGC), may allow registration beyond the late‑registration deadline. In such cases, the student will be allowed to register for **thesis credits only**.

### Academic Advising
- A faculty adviser appointed by the Dean (Academic Programs) in consultation with the PGC will advise the student on course selection.  
- A thesis supervisor is assigned after the student passes the comprehensive examination (see Section 4).  

### Semester Load Requirements
- A standard semester load equals **12 credits** (typically 4 courses).  
- The PGC may permit a load of **9–15 credits** at the beginning of the semester, depending on merit.  
- For external registration (research‑only), the maximum is **12 credits** and the minimum is **3 credits** per regular semester.

### Adding/Dropping Courses and Withdrawal
- **Add/Drop**: Allowed only during the Add/Drop period, with endorsement from the course instructor and the Convener PGC. Deadlines are in the Academic Calendar.  
- **Withdrawal before registration**: Requires formal approval from the Dean (Academic Programs) before the late‑registration deadline.  
- **Withdrawal after registration**: Permitted only on medical or other exceptional grounds, with Dean’s approval before the start of the end‑semester examination. Only one semester may be withdrawn at a time. Failure to register or obtain permission to withdraw may result in the student being struck off the Institute rolls.  
- A student who withdrew in a previous semester may re‑register for courses prescribed for the current semester, subject to prerequisites.  
- The transcript will reflect the appropriate status (e.g., “withdrawn” or “suspended”).  
- The maximum period for program completion includes any semester with “withdrawn” status.

## Academic Requirements

### PhD Program Credit Requirements
| Category | Minimum Total Credits | Minimum Course Credits | Minimum Research Credits | Minimum Number of Courses | Minimum Residency (Semesters) | Maximum Duration (Years)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PhD – after B.Tech/M.Sc | 96 | 33 | 48 | 11 | 8 | 7 |
| PhD – after M.Tech/M.Phil | 72 | 12$ | 48 | 4 | 6 | 6 |
| PhD – sponsored after M.Tech/M.Phil | 72 | 12$ | 48 | 4 | 1 | 7 |
| PhD – sponsored after B.Tech/M.Sc | 96 | 33* | 48 | 11 | 2 | 8 |

- **$**: The course requirement includes two compulsory courses. This requirement may be waived for students who have already taken these courses.  
- **Self‑study courses**: A student must apply to the Dean (Academic Programs) with (a) detailed content, (b) expected outcome, and (c) consent from the supervising faculty. The student must deliver a public seminar for each self‑study course at semester end.  
- **Synopsis**: Students must register the Synopsis as one Research Unit (3 credits).  
- **\***: Course credits for sponsored candidates with B.Tech/M.Sc degree and appropriate research experience can be relaxed based on PGC recommendation and Dean‑AP approval.  
- **\*\***: If a candidate does not successfully defend the PhD Synopsis before the maximum duration, admission is terminated. The candidate may re‑apply for fresh admission by submitting an application to the Admission Committee; if approved, the candidate must fulfill all academic requirements anew.

### Audit Courses
Students may audit courses and receive a “P” grade, which is entered in the grade card if they satisfy the instructor’s requirements. If requirements are not met, no grade is recorded.

### Registration for B.Tech First and Second Year Core Courses
PhD students may register for B.Tech first‑ and second‑year core courses with prior approval of the Dean (Academic Programs) during their coursework period. A minimum grade of **‘C’** is required to be eligible for the Comprehensive Examination.

### Grade Policies
- **Incomplete (I)**: Awarded when a student cannot complete course requirements for a genuine reason. The instructor must convert the “I” to a regular letter grade by the deadline specified in the Academic Calendar; otherwise it becomes an “F”.  
- **Fail (F)**: A student receiving an “F” in a core course must repeat the course. Elective courses must be repeated or substituted as directed by the PGC.  
- **Low grades (DD/DE)**: May be substituted by another course if the student’s CPI is below the prescribed minimum for the degree and the student is allowed to continue. For PhD students, substitution is permitted if CPI is below the minimum required for the comprehensive examination.  
- **Repeated/substituted courses**: The original grade remains on the transcript but is excluded from CPI calculation.  
- **Research credits (S/X)**: At semester end, the thesis supervisor(s) assess progress and award “S” for each satisfactory set of 3 credits, or “X” for each unsatisfactory set.  
- **Partial leave or mid‑semester thesis submission**: The PGC may reduce research credits accordingly.

## Sponsored Category (PhD)

A student may be admitted as a sponsored candidate if DA‑IICT signs an agreement with the sponsoring agency. The agreement specifies the aspects of the degree programme that differ from the standard Academic Requirements. All other requirements remain as outlined in this document.
