## Detailed Course Content

(a) Basic terminology in Optimization: Local optima, Global Optima, Equivalence of minimization and maximization, Convex sets and functions. (b) Set Constrained/Unconstrained optimization theory: First and Second order Nec- essary/Sufficient conditions for optima. (c) Unconstrained optimization algorithms: Gradient Methods, Newton’s method, Conjugate direction methods. (d) Constrained non-linear optimization theory: Feasibility set and surface, Active and inactive conditions, Lagrange multiplier, Karush-Kuhn-Tucker(KKT) condi- tion. (e) Special cases of Constrained Optimization: Linear programming, Basic feasible solutions, Simplex method. (f) Duality: Lagrangian duality, Geometric interpretation, Duality gap, Saddle point interpretation. (g)(If time permits) Constrained Optimization algorithms: Barrier & Penalty methods 6.References: (a) An Introduction to Optimization, Chong and Zak, Fourth ed., Wiley. (b) Linear and Nonlinear Programming, Luenberger and Ye, Fifth ed., Springer. 1 (c) Convex Optimization, Boyd and Vandenberghe, Cambridge University Press. 7.Course Outcomes : The student will be able to (a) use the first and second order conditions to compute optima for small uncon- strained optimization problems, (b) implement line search, gradient descent, and Newton’s method for unconstrained optimization, (c) use the KKT conditions to derive conditions that an optima or a constrained non- linear optimization problem has to satisfy, (d) appreciate importance of optimization in Data Science applications. 8.Evaluation: (a) Quizzes, Assignments/Lab exam - 30 −40%.
