# Academic Requirements for the M.Tech (ICT) Program

*(Effective from the 2022-23 batch)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original files:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_M Tech_(ICT)_Program_wef 2022-23.pdf` (core rules, dated November 2022) + `Academic/Appendix - Specialization-wise M Tech (ICT) Course struture from 2024-25.pdf` ("Appendix I," dated 2024 — provides the specialization course structures, including the new WCSP specialization added in 2024; per the appendix's own text, all other rules are governed by the core document)

## Scope

Master of Technology (Information and Communication Technology) — M.Tech (ICT) — is a 2-year (4-semester), full-time postgraduate program governed by these rules, covering post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

**1.1 Categories of Registration:**
- **Resident registration:** Requires registration for a duly approved course programme and payment of prescribed fees, including outstanding dues. Only resident registration counts toward the residence requirement for the degree.
- **External registration:** Requires registration for a duly approved research programme and payment of prescribed registration fees; the Dean (Academic Programs), in consultation with the PGC, may permit/require coursework registration too. A foreign student on a student VISA **cannot** register externally. A regular M.Tech student may not use external registration to obtain an 'S' grade for thesis work (see §2.3(e)).

**1.2 Provisional Registration:** New entrants awaiting qualifying-exam results register provisionally, submitting certificates for verification by the Academic Calendar deadline (~8 weeks from registration); originals returned, copy retained.

**1.3 Late Registration:** Late registration window (~1 week) with a late fee. Beyond that, in exceptional cases, the Dean (Academic Programs) on PGC recommendation may allow registration — but **only for thesis credits**.

**1.4 Academic Advising:** Faculty adviser appointed by the Dean (Academic Programs) in consultation with the PGC. Thesis supervisors are assigned by mutual consent of student and faculty after one semester of coursework. A student may repeat/substitute courses graded DD, DE, or F, per §2.3.

**1.5 Semester Load:** 15/16 credits (semester 1), 14/15 (semester 2), 12/13 (semester 3), 12 (semester 4). Minimum total degree credits: **58** — 33 coursework (13 core + 20 elective) + 25 major/minor project. The PGC may, case-by-case, permit 12–18 credits in a regular semester.

**1.6 Adding/Dropping and Withdrawal:**
- Adding/dropping permitted during the Add/Drop period with instructor + Convener PGC endorsement, by Academic Calendar deadlines.
- Withdrawal: pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean approval before the late-registration deadline; post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin; **withdrawal permitted for only one semester at a time** *(note: phrased as "at a time," not a lifetime one-semester cap as in the M.Sc programs)*; improper non-registration/withdrawal risks being struck off the rolls.
- Returning students register per the prescribed curriculum, subject to prerequisites. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show as "withdrawn" and count toward maximum program duration.

## 2. Academic Requirements

**2.1 Duration:** Total credits required: **at least 58** (per approved curriculum for the batch). **Maximum duration: 3 years (6 semesters)**, except **sponsored-category M.Tech: 4 years (8 semesters)**.

**2.2 Audit Courses:** Same rule as M.Sc (IT)/(DS) — satisfactory audit performance earns 'P'; unsatisfactory performance leaves no grade/transcript entry.

**2.3 Grades, SPI, and CPI:**

| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| AA | 10 | |
| AB | 9 | |
| BB | 8 | |
| BC | 7 | |
| CC | 6 | |
| CD | 5 | |
| DD | 4 | |
| DE | 3 | |
| F | 0 | Fail |
| I | — | Incomplete |
| P* | — | Pass |

\* For Pass/Fail and Audit Courses only.

- "I" (Incomplete): converts to a regular grade by the Academic Calendar deadline or auto-converts to F.
- F in a core course must be repeated; F in an elective must be repeated **or substituted**, per PGC.
- **DD or DE:** may substitute another course if CPI is below the degree minimum and the student may continue.
- Repeated/substituted courses: old grade stays on transcript, excluded from CPI.
- **(e) Research credits — S/X grading:** At semester end, the thesis supervisor(s) assess research progress and award **S** (satisfactory) or **X** (unsatisfactory) for each set of 3 research credits.
- **(f)** If a student is on partial-semester leave or submits their thesis mid-semester, the PGC may reduce research credits appropriately.

**2.4 Sponsored Category (M.Tech):** Requires a signed agreement between DA-IICT and the sponsoring agency. Terms:

| Requirement | Sponsored M.Tech |
|---|---|
| Course Work | 33 credits |
| Research | Minimum 25 credits; part/all may be at the sponsoring agency per the agreement |
| Guidance | DA-IICT Guide (mandatory), sponsoring-agency Co-Guide (optional) |
| Infrastructure | DA-IICT / sponsoring agency, respectively, for time spent at each |
| Financial Support (TA/RA) | Not applicable unless specified in the agreement |
| Intellectual Property Rights | Jointly DA-IICT and sponsoring agency, as specified in the agreement |

All other requirements follow the standard M.Tech (ICT) Academic Requirements.

## 3. Academic Performance Requirement

**3.1 SPI/CPI:** Same weighted-average definitions as M.Sc (IT)/(DS), to 2 decimal places. **Courses graded S or X are excluded from SPI/CPI computation.**

**3.2 Minimum CPI for graduation:**

| Program | CPI for Graduation |
|---|---|
| M.Tech (ICT) | **6.0** |

**3.3 Academic Probation and Dismissal:** CPI below 6.0 at any semester-end, **or an X grade in a research course**, triggers probation (written intimation) for the next semester. PGC will prescribe a specific course load and may set a minimum SPI target. Continued poor performance leads to a Director recommendation for the student to leave, with a termination letter from the appropriate authority.

## 4. Teaching Assistantships

Students may receive stipend support (at par with GATE scholarship rates) as Teaching Assistants, based on need/merit; TAship performance weighs into continuation/stipend decisions. Eligibility and stipend amount are set by the Institute's academic administration. Non-GATE-category students may be employed as TAs only if the Institute requires it, subject to minimum academic performance and appropriate skills; stipend decided separately by the Director.

## 5. Migration Rules (M.Tech ↔ PhD)

**5.1 Eligibility to migrate to PhD:**
- Entered the program with a B.Tech/BE degree or equivalent.
- Completed a minimum of 2 semesters of M.Tech with at least 18 credits.
- Minimum CPI of 7.0/10.

**5.2 Admission Process:** Application to the Dean (Academic Programs) in the PhD-admission format for that academic year, including a research statement and 3 letters of recommendation from faculty instructors. Processed per standard PhD admission procedure — **no application or admission fee applies.**

**5.3 PhD Requirements After Migration:** Subject to all PhD requirements for B.Tech/BE-entry students. Semesters registered and credits earned as M.Tech carry over; the prescribed duration for degree completion and the comprehensive exam is counted from the original M.Tech admission date.

**5.4 Eligibility for M.Tech Degree After Failed PhD Comprehensive Exam:** A PhD student (direct-entry or migrated) who fails the comprehensive exam within the specified duration may still receive the M.Tech degree if they: (a) meet M.Tech program eligibility criteria, (b) meet M.Tech continuation criteria, and (c) submit an M.Tech thesis meeting requirements within a maximum of 2 semesters from the semester following the failed exam — with **no financial support** during this period.

**5.5 Completion Requirements for M.Tech (ICT) After Migrating from PhD:** Must complete all M.Tech (ICT) degree requirements within 2 years (4 semesters) of migration; credits earned as a PhD student carry over.

## 6. Glossary

- **Academic Probation:** Indicates sub-expected academic performance; beyond §3.3 consequences, may affect financial support, medals/prizes eligibility, etc.
- **CPI:** Cumulative weighted average of grades earned to date.
- **Grade Points:** Credits × letter-grade points.
- **PGC:** Institute committee for postgraduate policy guidelines and implementation.
- **Semester:** ~16 weeks; Autumn (July–November), Winter (December/January–April).
- **Semester Credits:** Sum of credits registered in a semester.
- **Semester Grade Points:** Sum of (credits × points) for a semester's courses.
- **SPI:** Weighted average of grades in a given semester.

## Course Structure

### Semester-Wise Program Structure

**Semester I — Total Credits: 15**
- Two General Elective courses (6 credits): one from a designated Mathematics basket, one from a designated Technical Elective basket (baskets may include upper-level UG or refresher courses).
- Two Program Core courses (5 credits, common to all specializations): **Communication Skills and Technical Writing** (2 credits), **Programming Lab** (3 credits).
- One Specialization Core course (4 credits).
- *Note: each student is assigned a faculty advisor for general academic guidance.*

**Semester II — Total Credits: 14**
- One Specialization Core course (4 credits).
- Two Specialization Elective courses (7 credits).
- One Minor Project (3 credits), supervised by a faculty mentor.

**Summer — Total Credits: 4**
- Major Project I (0-0-8-4), supervised — preliminary work toward problem definition/formulation. The Minor Project and Major Project I mentor may be the same or different.

**Semester III — Total Credits: 13**
- Two Specialization Electives (7 credits).
- Continuation of Major Project I (0-0-12-6).

**Semester IV — Total Credits: 12**
- **Major Project II** (on-campus continuation of Major Project I) **or Industrial Training Project** (industry internship) — student's choice. Successful completion of Major Project II earns a thesis certificate.

**Program totals:** 33 course credits + 25 project credits = **58 credits**.

## Detailed Program Structure by Specialization

### Specialization: Software Systems

- **Semester I:** Communication and Technical Writing (2), Programming Lab (3), Specialization Core I – Advanced Algorithm (4), General Elective (Maths) (3/4), General Elective (Technical) (3/4)
- **Semester II:** Specialization Core II – Advanced Software Engineering (4), Minor Project (3), Specialization Elective I (3/4), Specialization Elective II (4/3)
- **Summer:** Major Project I (4)
- **Semester III:** Major Project I continued (6), Specialization Elective III (3/4), Specialization Elective IV (4/3)
- **Semester IV:** Major Project II / Industrial Training Project (12)

**General Elective (Mathematics):** Probability & Random Variables, Linear Algebra, Optimization, Graph Theory, or other relevant course.

**General Elective (Technical):** Operating Systems, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Human Computer Interaction, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives I/II:** Distributed Systems, Distributed Databases, Approximation Algorithms, Information Security, Multimedia Security & Forensics, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives III/IV:** Big Data Processing, Blockchains and Cryptocurrency, Advanced Computer Networks, Software Specification and Verification, or other relevant course.

### Specialization: Machine Learning

- **Semester I:** Communication and Technical Writing (2), Programming Lab (3), Specialization Core I – Foundations of Machine Learning (4), General Elective (Maths) (3/4), General Elective (Technical) (3/4)
- **Semester II:** Specialization Core II – Advanced Machine Learning (4), Minor Project (3), Specialization Elective I (3/4), Specialization Elective II (4/3)
- **Summer:** Major Project I (4)
- **Semester III:** Major Project I continued (6), Specialization Elective III (3/4), Specialization Elective IV (3/4)
- **Semester IV:** Major Project II / Industrial Training Project (12)

**General Elective (Mathematics):** Probability & Statistics, Linear Algebra, Optimization, Graph Theory, or other relevant course.

**General Elective (Technical):** Advanced Algorithm, Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Human Computer Interaction, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives I/II:** Digital Image Processing, Information Retrieval, Recommendation Systems, Adversarial Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Multimedia Security & Forensics, Speech Technology, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives III/IV:** Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Reinforcement Learning, Computational Shape Modeling, Wavelet Image Processing, Time Series Forecasting, or other relevant course.

### Specialization: VLSI & Embedded Systems

- **Semester I:** Communication and Technical Writing (2), Programming Lab (3), Specialization Core I – VLSI System Design (4), General Elective (Maths) (3/4), General Elective (Technical) (3/4)
- **Semester II:** Specialization Core II – Embedded Hardware Design (4), Minor Project (3), Specialization Elective I (3/4, choose one of ASIC Design / Analog VLSI Design), Specialization Elective II (4/3, choose one of Digital System Architecture / Cyber Physical Systems and IoT)
- **Summer:** Major Project I (4)
- **Semester III:** Major Project I continued (6), Specialization Elective III (3/4), Specialization Elective IV (3/4)
- **Semester IV:** Major Project II / Industrial Training Project (12)

**General Elective (Mathematics):** Probability & Random Variables, Linear Algebra, Optimization, Graph Theory, or other relevant course.

**General Elective (Technical):** Digital Programming, Internet of Things, Machine Learning, Robotics, Edge Computing, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives I/II:** ASIC Design, Analog VLSI Design, Digital System Architecture, Cyber Physical Systems and IoT, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives III/IV:** VLSI Testing and Verification, Low Power VLSI Design, VLSI for Digital Signal Processing, Device Modeling and Simulation, Nanoelectronics, or other relevant course.

### Specialization: Wireless Communications and Signal Processing (WCSP)

*(New specialization, introduced in 2024)*

- **Semester I:** Communication and Technical Writing (2), Programming Lab (3), Specialization Core I – Introduction to Wireless Communication (4), General Elective (Maths) (3/4), General Elective (Technical) (3/4)
- **Semester II:** Specialization Core II – Advanced Digital Signal Processing (4), Minor Project (3), Specialization Elective I (3/4), Specialization Elective II (4/3)
- **Summer:** Major Project I (4)
- **Semester III:** Major Project I continued (6), Specialization Elective III (3/4), Specialization Elective IV (4/3)
- **Semester IV:** Major Project II / Industrial Training Project (12)

**General Elective (Mathematics):** Probability & Random Variables, Linear Algebra, Optimization, Graph Theory, or other relevant course.

**General Elective (Technical):** Wireless System Design, Detection and Estimation Theory, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives I/II:** Advanced Wireless Communication, Application of ML to Wireless Communication Systems, Speech Technology, Digital Image Processing, or other relevant course.

**Specialization Electives III/IV:** Next Generation Communication Systems, Cyber-Physical Systems and Internet of Things, Adaptive Signal Processing, or other relevant course.
