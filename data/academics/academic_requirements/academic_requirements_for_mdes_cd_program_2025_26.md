# Academic Requirements for the MDes Program in Communication Design — MDes (CD)

*(Effective from Autumn 2025-26)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DAU (Formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_MDes(CD)_wef 2025-26  as of August 2025.pdf`
- **Created:** March 2025

## Scope

Master of Design (Communication Design) — MDes (CD) — is a two-year postgraduate program governed by these rules, covering post-admission academic activities only (not admission eligibility/procedures). Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

**1.1 Categories of Registration:** Students register in the **resident registration category only** — requiring registration for a duly approved course program and payment of prescribed tuition/fees, including outstanding dues.

**1.2 Provisional Registration:** New entrants awaiting qualifying-exam results may register provisionally; they must submit certificates of having passed the qualifying exam for verification by the date in the Academic Calendar (usually ~8 weeks from registration). Originals are returned; a self-attested copy is kept on record.

**1.3 Late Registration:** Students unable to register on the scheduled day (e.g., due to illness) may register during the late-registration period (~1 week from the registration date) specified in the Academic Calendar, with a late fee. In exceptional cases, the Dean (Academic Programs), on PGC recommendation, may allow registration beyond the late-registration date.

**1.4 Academic Advising:**
- Students are advised on course selection by a faculty adviser appointed by the Dean (Academic Programs) in consultation with the Post-Graduate Committee (PGC).
- A student who obtains **DE or F** may either (a) complete additional coursework during the semester for a grade review, in consultation with the Course Faculty and MDes (CD) coordinator, or (b) re-take the course the following academic year (per Institute registration policy). Repeat permission is governed by §2.2.

**1.5 Semester Load:** Prescribed from time to time in the approved program specialisation course structure document.

**1.6 Adding/Dropping and Withdrawal:**
- **Adding/dropping courses is not permitted — all courses in MDes (CD) are mandatory.**
- Pre-registration withdrawal requires Dean (Academic Programs) approval before the late-registration deadline. Post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin. **Withdrawal is permitted for only one semester during the entire program** *(stricter than the B.Tech programs, which allow one semester at a time but not a lifetime cap of one)*. Failure to register or withdraw properly may result in the student's name being struck off the Institute's rolls.
- A returning student registers per the prescribed curriculum for that semester, subject to prerequisites.
- Withdrawn/suspended semesters show "withdrawn" status on the transcript; such semesters count toward the maximum program duration.

## 2. Academic Requirements

**2.1 MDes (CD) Specialisation:** Total credits required: **80** (subject to revision per the approved curriculum for the concerned batch). **Maximum permissible duration: 3 years.**

- "I" (Incomplete) is awarded when a student doesn't complete course requirements for a genuine reason; must be converted to a regular grade by the Academic Calendar deadline or automatically becomes F.
- **F in a Core or Open Elective course must be repeated**, either during the semester or the following academic year.
- If a course is repeated/substituted, the old grade still appears on the transcript but is excluded from CPI calculation.

## 3. Academic Performance Requirement

**3.1 SPI/CPI:** SPI = weighted average of grades in a given semester; CPI = cumulative weighted average of all grades earned to date. Both calculated to 2 decimal places.

**3.2 Minimum CPI for graduation:**

| Program | CPI for Graduation |
|---|---|
| MDes (CD) | **6.0** |

**3.3 Academic Probation and Dismissal:** A student whose CPI falls below 6.0 at the end of any semester is placed on Academic Probation for the next semester (written intimation). The PGC may prescribe a specific course load and/or a minimum SPI target for that semester. The PGC monitors probationary students; if performance remains poor, it recommends to the Director that the student leave the Institute, and the appropriate authority issues a termination letter.

## 4. Glossary

- **Academic Probation:** Indicates academic performance below the expected level; beyond the consequences in §3.3, may also affect financial support, medals/prizes eligibility, etc.
- **CPI (Cumulative Performance Index):** Cumulative weighted average of grades earned to date, including the current semester.
- **Grade Points:** Credits × letter-grade points.
- **PGC (Postgraduate Committee):** Institute committee responsible for postgraduate policy guidelines and implementation.
- **Semester:** ~16 weeks; Autumn Semester (July–November), Winter Semester (December/January–April) — *(note: these date ranges differ slightly from the B.Tech programs' stated Autumn (July–December) / Winter (January–May) ranges)*.
- **Semester Credits:** Sum of credits of courses registered in a semester.
- **Semester Grade Points:** Sum of (credits × points) across a semester's registered courses.
- **SPI (Semester Performance Index):** Weighted average of grades obtained in a given semester.
