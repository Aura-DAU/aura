# Academic Requirements for the PhD Program in Engineering (ICT Disciplines), Science and Mathematics

*(Effective from Autumn 2024-25)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_Ph D Program_EnggScience_wef 2024-25 as of July 2024.pdf`
- **Dated:** July 2024

## Scope

Governs the PhD (Doctor of Philosophy) degree, typically completed in 4–6 years, for the **Engineering (ICT disciplines), Science and Mathematics** areas. Covers post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases.

> This document's content is **near word-for-word identical** to the companion [Academic Requirements for the PhD Program in Humanities, Social Sciences and Design](./Academic-Requirements-for-PhD-HSS-and-Design-2024-25.md) — same Registration rules, the same Table 1 (credit/duration requirements by category), the same grading scale and marks-to-grade mapping, the same CPI-for-graduation threshold (**7.0**), the same TA/RA stipend structure (**Rs. 35,000/month for years 1–2, Rs. 40,000/month from year 3**), the same Comprehensive Examination structure, Migration Rules, Termination conditions, and Glossary. See that document for the full text of all of these sections; only genuine differences are detailed below.

### Confirmed Differences from the HSS & Design Document

1. **Scope/title:** This document governs PhD study in the **Engineering (ICT disciplines), Science and Mathematics** areas (of DA-IICT's 7 total areas: CSP, CS, EnV, Phy, Math, HSS, Design — presumably this document covers CSP/CS/EnV/Phy/Math, with the HSS & Design document covering the remaining two).
2. **§1.4(a)(ii) wording:** This document's phrasing of the "3 courses in semester I, plus 1 additional course" requirement is structured slightly differently in the source text (referencing "one more course other than the 3 courses in the first [semester]"), though the underlying minimum-course-count requirements (3 in semester I, including 2 area core courses; a Research Methods course among the first 4 core courses; max 1 MOOC) are the same in substance.
3. **No Appendix:** Unlike the HSS & Design document, this source PDF **does not include** an Appendix listing tentative core courses or definitions of Directed Study / Independent Study / Reading Course. This is a genuine difference in document completeness, not an extraction gap — flagged for manual review in case a separate appendix document exists for these disciplines.

All other rules — Registration categories and procedures, Late Registration, Academic Advising (course modes, supervisor assignment for full-time and part-time/sponsored students), Semester Load, Adding/Dropping/Withdrawal, Table 1 (credit/duration requirements — identical figures), Audit Courses, Level 1/2/3/6 course rules, the full grading table and marks-to-grade mapping, Sponsored/External category requirements, Residency requirements, PhD Synopsis process, Academic Performance (CPI 7.0 for graduation, probation, >5 X grades → discontinuation), Fellowships (Rs. 35,000/40,000 stipend structure), Comprehensive Examination (Parts I & II, timing, max 2 attempts), Supervisor Selection & RPC Formation, Research Progress Seminar, Migration Rules (PhD ↔ M.Tech), Termination conditions, and the Glossary are **identical in substance** to the HSS & Design document — see that document for full text.
