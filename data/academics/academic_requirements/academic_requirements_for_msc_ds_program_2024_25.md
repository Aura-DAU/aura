# Academic Requirements for the M.Sc (DS) Program

*(Effective from Autumn 2024-25)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic requirements_MSc(DS)_wef_2024-25 as of October 2024.pdf`
- **Updated:** October 2024

## Scope

Master of Science – Data Science, M.Sc (DS), is a two-year postgraduate program governed by these rules, covering post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

**1.1 Categories of Registration:** Resident registration category only, requiring registration for a duly approved course program and payment of prescribed fees, including outstanding dues.

**1.1.3 Provisional Registration:** New entrants awaiting qualifying-exam results register provisionally, submitting original certificates for verification by the Academic Calendar deadline (~8 weeks from registration); originals returned, self-attested copy retained.

**1.2 Late Registration:** Late registration window (~1 week per Academic Calendar) with a late fee for compelling reasons. Dean (Academic Programs), on PGC recommendation, may permit registration beyond that in exceptional cases.

**1.3 Academic Advising:** Faculty adviser appointed by the Dean (Academic Programs) in consultation with the PGC. A student may repeat or substitute a course graded **DD, DE, or F**, governed by §2.3.

**1.4 Semester Load:** Prescribed from time to time in the approved program course structure document (Appendix A).

**1.5 Adding/Dropping and Withdrawal:**
- Adding/dropping permitted during the Add/Drop period, with endorsement from both the course instructor and Convener PGC, by Academic Calendar deadlines.
- Withdrawal rules identical to M.Sc (IT): pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean approval before the late-registration deadline; post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin; withdrawal permitted for only one semester during the entire program; improper non-registration/withdrawal risks being struck off the rolls.
- Returning students register per the prescribed curriculum, subject to prerequisites. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show as "withdrawn" and count toward the maximum program duration.

## 2. Academic Requirements

**2.1 Minimum Credits and Maximum Duration:** Total course credits required: **at least 60** (per the approved curriculum for the concerned batch). **Maximum permissible duration: 3 years.**

**2.2 Audit Courses:** Students may audit courses; satisfactory performance (per instructor requirements) earns a 'P' grade card entry; unsatisfactory performance results in no grade and no transcript entry — same rule as M.Sc (IT).

**2.3 Grades, SPI, and CPI:**

| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| AA | 10 | |
| AB | 9 | |
| BB | 8 | |
| BC | 7 | |
| CC | 6 | |
| CD | 5 | |
| DD | 4 | |
| DE | 3 | |
| F | 0 | Fail |
| I | — | Incomplete |
| P* | — | Pass |

\* For Pass in Audit Courses only.

- "I" (Incomplete): converts to a regular grade by the Academic Calendar deadline or auto-converts to F.
- F in a core course must be repeated; F in an elective must be repeated **or substituted**, as suggested by PGC.
- **DD or DE** in an elective may be substituted if CPI is below the degree minimum and the student is permitted to continue.
- Repeated/substituted courses: old grade stays on transcript, excluded from CPI.

## 3. Academic Performance Requirement

**3.1 SPI/CPI:** Same definitions as M.Sc (IT) — weighted semester average (SPI) and cumulative weighted average (CPI), both to 2 decimal places.

**3.2 Minimum CPI for graduation:**

| Program | CPI for Graduation |
|---|---|
| M.Sc (DS) | **6.0** |

**3.3 Academic Probation and Dismissal:** Identical framework to M.Sc (IT) — CPI below 6.0 at any semester-end triggers probation (written intimation) for the next semester; PGC **will** prescribe a specific course load and may set a minimum SPI target; continued poor performance leads to a Director recommendation for the student to leave, with a termination letter issued by the appropriate authority.

## Glossary

Same definitions as M.Sc (IT): Academic Probation, CPI, Grade Points, PGC, Semester (Autumn: July–November; Winter: December/January–April), Semester Credits, Semester Grade Points, SPI.
