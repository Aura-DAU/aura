# Academic Requirements for the M.Tech (CS&ML) Program

*(Communication Systems and Machine Learning — Effective from the 2021-22 batch)*

## Source

- **Institution:** Jointly offered by DA-IICT, Gandhinagar and **CR Rao AIMSCS, Hyderabad**
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_MTech(CS&ML)_Program_wef 2021-22.pdf`
- **Dated:** August 2021

## Scope

Master of Technology in Communication Systems and Machine Learning (CS&ML) — a full-time, 2-year (4-semester) program jointly offered by DA-IICT and CR Rao AIMSCS, Hyderabad. Covers post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases.

> This document's rule structure closely mirrors [Academic Requirements for the M.Tech (EC) Program](./Academic-Requirements-for-MTech-EC-Program-2022-23.md) and [Academic Requirements for the M.Tech (ICT) Program](./Academic-Requirements-for-MTech-ICT-Program-2022-23.md) — same Registration categories, Late Registration, Academic Advising, Adding/Dropping/Withdrawal, Audit Courses, the AA–F grading table, S/X research-credit grading, and Teaching Assistantship provisions. **This program has a substantially different credit structure and semester load**, detailed below.

## 1. Registration

Identical framework to M.Tech (EC)/(ICT): resident vs. external registration categories; provisional registration for entrants awaiting qualifying-exam results; late registration window with fee (Dean/PGC exception for thesis-credit-only registration); faculty adviser + thesis supervisor assignment after one semester; repeat/substitute permission for DD/DE/F grades (§2.3); Add/Drop with instructor + Convener PGC endorsement; withdrawal rules identical (one semester at a time, Dean approval, risk of being struck off rolls).

**1.5 Semester Load Requirements (program-specific):** ⚠️ **Differs substantially from M.Tech (EC)/(ICT):**
- Semester I: **17 credits**
- Semester II: **19 credits**
- Summer Semester: **2 credits** (Research or Industrial Internship)
- Semester III: **10 credits**
- Semester IV: **12 credits**
- **Total degree credits: 61** (40 coursework + 21 research) — vs. 58 for M.Tech (EC)/(ICT).
- Of the 40 coursework credits: **24 core + 16 elective** (vs. 13 core + 20 elective for EC/ICT).
- PGC may permit a maximum of **21 credits** or minimum of **9 credits** in a regular semester (vs. 18 max / 12 min for EC/ICT).

## 2. Academic Requirements

**2.1 Duration:** Total credits required: **at least 61**. Maximum duration: 3 years (6 semesters); sponsored category: 4 years (8 semesters) — same duration caps as EC/ICT despite the different credit total.

**2.2 Audit Courses:** Same rule as other M.Tech programs.

**2.3 Grades, SPI, CPI:** Same 10-point grading table and rules (a)–(f) as M.Tech (EC)/(ICT), including S/X research-credit grading — see that document for full text.

**2.4 Sponsored Category:** Same eligibility (DA-IICT/CR Rao AIMSCS agreement, or government/R&D-institute/PSU sponsorship) as M.Tech (EC). Credit split differs to match this program's structure:

| Requirement | Sponsored M.Tech (CS&ML) |
|---|---|
| Course Work | **40 credits** |
| Research | **21 credits minimum**; part/all may be at the sponsoring agency, per the CR Rao AIMSCS agreement |
| Guidance | DA-IICT Guide (mandatory), sponsoring-agency Co-Guide (optional) |
| Infrastructure | DA-IICT / sponsoring agency, respectively, for time spent at each |
| Financial Support (TA/RA) | Not applicable unless specified in the agreement |
| Intellectual Property Rights | Jointly DA-IICT and sponsoring agency, as specified in the agreement |

## 3. Academic Performance Requirement

**3.1 SPI/CPI:** Same definitions as other M.Tech programs; S/X courses excluded from computation.

**3.2 Minimum CPI for graduation:**

| Program | CPI for Graduation |
|---|---|
| M.Tech (CS&ML) | **6.0** *(same as EC/ICT)* |

**3.3 Academic Probation and Dismissal:** Same framework as M.Tech (EC)/(ICT) — CPI below minimum or an X grade triggers probation; PGC prescribes course load/SPI target; continued poor performance → Director recommendation to leave, with termination letter.

## 4. Teaching Assistantships

Same provisions as M.Tech (EC)/(ICT) — GATE-scholarship-rate stipend based on need/merit; Non-GATE students may TA only if required, with separately-decided stipend.

## 5. Migration Rules (M.Tech ↔ PhD)

Same framework as M.Tech (EC)/(ICT): eligibility requires B.Tech/BE-or-equivalent entry, minimum 2 semesters / 18 credits completed, minimum CPI 7.0/10; application to Dean (Academic Programs) per PhD-admission format with research statement + 3 faculty LoRs, no application/admission fee; carried-over credits and duration count from original M.Tech admission date; a PhD student failing the comprehensive exam may still receive the M.Tech degree under the same conditions (eligibility + continuation criteria + thesis within 2 semesters, no financial support); completing M.Tech (CS&ML) after PhD migration requires finishing within 2 years (4 semesters) of migration, with PhD credits carried over.

## 6. Glossary

Same definitions as other M.Tech programs, with one addition: **Semester** definition here specifies Autumn Semester extends "July to November/December" (vs. just "July to November" in the EC/ICT documents) and Winter Semester "December/January to April" — both noted as "subject to change."
