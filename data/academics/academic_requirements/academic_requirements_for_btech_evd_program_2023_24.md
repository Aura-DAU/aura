# Academic Requirements for the B.Tech (EVD) Program

*(Electronics and VLSI Design — Effective from Autumn 2023-24)*

## Source

- **Institution:** DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic requirements_BTech(EVD)_wef_2023-24.pdf`
- **Dated:** May 2023

## Scope

Rules governing the B.Tech in Electronics and VLSI Design (EVD) program, a four-year undergraduate program, applicable to students admitted from AY 2023-24 onwards. Covers post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

- Registration occurs within the first two days of each semester via SRS. Late registration permitted with Deputy Registrar approval and fee, up to the Academic Calendar deadline. Outstanding dues bar registration.
- No-backlog students register for the full prescribed course set; students with backlogs/on probation may get a different set recommended by the UG Committee Convenor.
- **Withdrawal:** Same framework as the base B.Tech (ICT) program — pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean approval before the late-registration deadline; post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin; one semester at a time; failure to register/withdraw properly results in discontinuation. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show "withdrawn" on the transcript.

## 2. Auditing & PG-Level Courses

- **Audit courses:** From semester 5 onwards, maximum 2 for the whole program, 1 per semester. Requires instructor approval and no timetable clash. Not an overload; excluded from SPI/CPI; graded P (appears on transcript) or F (does not appear).
- **PG-level courses for credit:** Students with CPI ≥ 7.4 may register for available M.Tech-level courses as credit courses from semester 5 onwards, with program convenor approval and no timetable clash; counts toward the regular course load.

## 3. Course Load

- **Regular semester:** Maximum 7 courses (excluding Co-Curricular Activities), maximum 28 credits. Minimum underload: 4 courses / 12 credits (fewer than 4 permitted after 7 completed regular semesters).
- **Summer Semester** (~8 weeks, May–July): Maximum 3 backlog courses. Internship = 2 courses; BTP Part II = 3 courses; BTP Part I = 1 course. No other course combined with an internship. Students past 8 regular semesters may also use Summer Semester for grade improvement (§5.2).

## 4. Course Assessment and Grading

Assessment modes and attendance policy identical to the base B.Tech (ICT) program. Grading scale:

AA–DE passes; F fails. Pass/Fail courses use P/F and are excluded from SPI/CPI; F may also result from exam malpractice. "I" (Incomplete) — missed end-semester exam due to illness/family accident-death, Dean approval, evidence required, converted via make-up exam or defaults to F by the next semester's Academic Calendar deadline — identical to the base program.

## 5. Repeating a Course

- F grade in a credit course must be repeated (backlog course, subject to §3). A backlog elective may be replaced by another elective of the same category — **Specialization/ICT/Technical/MnC/HASS/Science** *(note: this category list is broader than the base B.Tech (ICT) program's, reflecting EVD's open-elective structure spanning multiple other programs' elective pools)*.
- **Grade Improvement:** CPI < 5.0 allows repeating a DD/DE-graded course in a regular semester only; repeat grade counts toward CPI, original grade stays on transcript but excluded from CPI.
- Maximum 1 Backlog/Grade-Improvement course per regular semester, subject to §3.1 course-load cap and no evaluation clash.

## 6. B.Tech Project / Internship

All students must complete the B.Tech Project (BTP) or Internship, with credits as prescribed in the curriculum from time to time.

## 7. Performance Indices

**SPI** and **CPI** defined identically to the base B.Tech (ICT) program (weighted grade-point averages, semester and cumulative respectively, to 2 decimal places).

## 8. Minimum Academic Performance Requirements

- **Academic Probation:** Same thresholds as base program — probation into semester 2 if semester-1 SPI < 4.5; later semesters if previous CPI < 5.0 or previous SPI < 4.5. Dean may set a minimum SPI target for probationary students.
- **Discontinuation for poor performance:** Same assessment cadence (from end of semester 2 onward) as the base program.
- **CPI < 4.0 at end of semester 2 or 4:** Student is asked to repeat a year with course-improvement options; may register for up to 3 available summer-semester backlog courses (F or DE grade). Achieving CPI ≥ 4.0 (including summer results) allows continuation; otherwise the student repeats a year or is discontinued.
- **Program duration:** Minimum 4 years, maximum 6 years (including withdrawn semesters).

## 9. Award of Degree

**9.1 Requirements for the B.Tech (EVD) degree:**

a. Pass all foundation and elective courses, internships, BTP/Internship, co-curricular activities, and exploratory projects prescribed in the curriculum.

b. **Minimum 163 total credits:** 138 from core + elective courses (93 foundation, 17 individual/group projects, 28 elective — split as Specialization electives 19 credits/minimum 5 courses, and Open electives 9 credits/minimum 3 courses), 19 from internships + BTP/Internship, 6 from co-curricular activities + exploratory projects.

c. Minimum Final CPI of 5.0.

d. Minimum 690 grade points from coursework (138 × 5.0; internship/BTP credits excluded from this count). Shortfalls require additional elective courses (up to category maximums).

e. At least 8 registered regular semesters (excluding summer/withdrawn semesters), with all institute dues paid.

f. No pending case of indiscipline.

**9.2 Final CPI and Class:** Computed from the best-CPI combination of courses meeting minimums (≥138 credits, 93 foundation credits, elective category minimums); extra courses beyond this don't count toward final CPI. BTP counts toward CPI; ITP (Pass/Fail) does not. Subject to change with Director approval if credit structures change. **Distinction:** CPI ≥ 9.0. **First Class:** CPI ≥ 6.5 and < 9.0.

**9.3 Certificate of Academic Accomplishment:** Same provision as other B.Tech programs — available on application for students who don't complete degree requirements within the maximum period, per Institute-set criteria.

## 10. Glossary

Same definitions as the base B.Tech (ICT) program: Backlog Course, Course Credit, Grade Points, Semester, Semester Grade Report, Transcript, UG Committee.

### Elements of the Curriculum

- **4.1 Foundation/Core Courses:** Compulsory courses for the first six semesters, spanning Electronics, VLSI Systems and Design, plus Humanities, Mathematics, and Basic Sciences.
- **4.2 Specialization Electives:** Technical depth in Electronics Systems Design and VLSI Design pathways.
- **4.3 Open Electives:** May be drawn from ICT Electives, Technical Electives, MnC Electives, Humanities and Social Sciences Electives, or Science Electives (i.e., other programs' elective pools).
- **4.4 MOOC electives:** Permitted with faculty-mentor consent (mentor assigned by Dean-Academics); max 2 for the whole program, max 1 per semester.
- **4.5 Rural Internship:** Mandatory, 4 weeks, Winter break after semester 3, with an NGO/government organization; graded Pass/Fail.
- **4.6 Research/Industrial Internship:** 6–8 weeks, summer break after semester 6; student chooses industrial or research track. Graded Pass/Fail.
- **4.7 Internships:** Rural Internship (3 credits, between semesters III–IV) and Industrial/Research Internship (4 credits, Summer Semester after semester VI) — both mandatory, must pass.
- **4.8 BTP/Internship:** 12 credits; may continue from a prior internship. Individually or in faculty-supervised groups. Students may alternatively do an off-campus Industrial Internship in semester 8, at an approved site with a designated mentor and well-specified project.
- **4.9 Co-Curricular Activities and Exploration Project:** Co-curricular (sports/cultural/technical club activities) run over the first four semesters, graded Pass/Fail. Exploration projects run over semesters 2–3 in groups of 8–10 under a faculty mentor.

### Graduation Requirements Summary

1. Total credits: **163** (including 4 co-curricular + 2 exploratory project credits)
2. Course credits: **138**
3. Internships and BTP/Internship credits: **19**
4. Minimum Final CPI: **5.0**
