# Academic Requirements for the M.Sc (IT) Program

*(Effective from Autumn 2023-24)*

## Source

- **Institution:** Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT), Gandhinagar
- **Original file:** `Academic/Academic Requirements_M Sc (IT)_wef 2023-24 as of August 2023.pdf`
- **Created:** August 2023

## Scope

Master of Science (Information Technology) — M.Sc (IT) — is a two-year postgraduate program governed by these rules, covering post-admission academic activities only. Dean (Academic Programs)/Registrar may issue implementing instructions; the Director, as Academic Council Chairman, may relax/exempt provisions in exceptional cases (reported to the Academic Council at its next meeting).

## 1. Registration

**1.1 Categories of Registration:** Resident registration category only — requires registration for a duly approved course programme and payment of prescribed fees, including outstanding dues.

**1.2 Provisional Registration:** New entrants awaiting qualifying-exam results may register provisionally, submitting certificates for verification by the Academic Calendar deadline (~8 weeks from registration). Originals returned; self-attested copy retained.

**1.3 Late Registration:** Late registration permitted (~1 week window per Academic Calendar) with a late fee for compelling reasons (e.g., illness). Dean (Academic Programs), on PGC recommendation, may allow registration beyond that in exceptional cases.

**1.4 Academic Advising:**
- Faculty adviser appointed by the Dean (Academic Programs) in consultation with the PGC.
- A student may repeat or substitute a course graded **DD, DE, or F** — governed by §2.3.

**1.5 Semester Load:** Prescribed from time to time in the approved program course structure document.

**1.6 Adding/Dropping and Withdrawal:**
- **Adding/dropping courses is permitted** during the Add/Drop period, only with endorsement from both the course instructor and the Convener PGC, by Academic Calendar deadlines. *(Note: this differs from MDes (CD), where adding/dropping is not permitted at all.)*
- Withdrawal rules are otherwise identical to MDes (CD): pre-registration withdrawal needs Dean approval before the late-registration deadline; post-registration withdrawal only for medical/exceptional reasons, approved before end-semester exams begin; **withdrawal permitted for only one semester during the entire program**; improper non-registration/withdrawal risks being struck off the rolls.
- Returning students register per the prescribed curriculum, subject to prerequisites. Withdrawn/suspended semesters show as "withdrawn" and count toward the maximum program duration.

## 2. Academic Requirements

**2.1 M.Sc (IT) Program:** Total credits required: **at least 78** (per the approved curriculum for the concerned batch). **Maximum permissible duration: 3 years.**

**2.2 Audit Courses:** Students may audit courses. A satisfactory outcome (per the instructor's requirements) earns a 'P' grade on the grade card; if requirements aren't met, no grade or transcript entry is made at all *(note: this differs from B.Tech programs, where a failed audit gets an explicit F grade that simply doesn't appear on the transcript — here, non-satisfactory audits leave no record whatsoever)*.

**2.3 Grades, SPI, and CPI:**

| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| AA | 10 | |
| AB | 9 | |
| BB | 8 | |
| BC | 7 | |
| CC | 6 | |
| CD | 5 | |
| DD | 4 | |
| DE | 3 | |
| F | 0 | Fail |
| I | — | Incomplete |
| P* | — | Pass |

\* For Pass/Fail and Audit Courses only.

- "I" (Incomplete): awarded for genuine incompletion; must convert to a regular grade by the Academic Calendar deadline or auto-converts to F.
- **F in a core course must be repeated.** F in an elective course must be repeated **or substituted**, as suggested by the PGC.
- A **DD or DE** grade in an elective course may be substituted by another course if the student's CPI is below the minimum required for the degree and they're permitted to continue in the program.
- Repeated/substituted courses: the old grade stays on the transcript but is excluded from CPI.

## 3. Academic Performance Requirement

**3.1 SPI/CPI:** Same definitions as MDes (CD) — SPI (semester weighted average), CPI (cumulative weighted average), both to 2 decimal places.

**3.2 Minimum CPI for graduation:**

| Program | CPI for Graduation |
|---|---|
| M.Sc (IT) | **6.0** |

**3.3 Academic Probation and Dismissal:** A student below the minimum CPI (6.0) at the end of any semester is placed on Academic Probation (written intimation) for the next semester. The PGC **will** (not "may," as in MDes (CD)) prescribe a specific course load for that semester, and may also prescribe a minimum SPI target. PGC monitors probationary students; poor continued performance triggers a recommendation to the Director for the student to leave, with a termination letter issued by the appropriate authority.

## 4. Glossary

Same definitions as MDes (CD): Academic Probation, CPI, Grade Points, PGC, Semester (Autumn: July–November; Winter: December/January–April), Semester Credits, Semester Grade Points, SPI.
