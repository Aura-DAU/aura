# Co-Curricular Activities Course Policy

## Overview

The **Co-Curricular Activities Course Policy** at DA-IICT (DAU) is governed by the **Student Body Government (SBG)**. This policy defines how students earn co-curricular credit through event attendance, club membership, and competition participation.

## Event Attendance Requirement

Students must attend a **minimum of 5 events per semester** out of the total number of events conducted by clubs and committees under the Student Body Government.

## Attendance Credit Rules

| Activity | Credit Awarded |
|---|---|
| Active club/committee member | Pass Grade directly (verified by club certificate) |
| Intra-DAIICT or inter-college event participant | Pass Grade (verified by certificate) |
| Regular sports participation | Pass Grade (verified by Sports Committee certificate) |
| SBG-approved College Hackathon — Top 10 | 3 attendance credits |
| SBG-approved College Hackathon — Top 3 | 5 attendance credits |
| Company Hackathon — Ideation submission | 1 attendance credit |
| Company Hackathon — Mid evaluation clearance | 2 attendance credits (not cumulative with ideation) |
| Company Hackathon — Shortlisted for final round | 5 attendance credits |
| National/international competitions (RoboFest, RoboCon, DataThons, poster presentations, etc.) | Reviewed case-by-case |
| GSoC contributions in verified repositories | Considered (GDG and MSTC in loop) |
| ICPC Regionals qualification | 5 attendance credits |

> Note: Company Hackathon criteria may vary by hackathon. Exact criteria will be communicated via email. There is no limit on the number of hackathons.

## Grade Policy for Shortfall

If a student falls short of the required co-curricular attendance and receives an **'I' Grade**:
- The attendance deficit is **carried forward** to the next semester.
- The student must make up the backlog **in addition to** fulfilling the current semester's attendance requirement.

## Additional Notes

- All participation certificates must be submitted to the respective clubs/committees for verification.
- Representing DAU at national/international competitions is reviewed based on the nature and scope of work.
