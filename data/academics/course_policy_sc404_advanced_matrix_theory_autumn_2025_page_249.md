## Course Description

Notions of matrices are of fundamental importance in Mathematics as well as several other areas including Machine learning, Data Science, Computer vision, Networking and Optimization, Computer Science, Signal processing etc. This course aims to develop a strong foundation for matrix analysis, developing essential tools. The emphasis will be on analytic aspects as well as algebraic aspects of Matrix Theory.
