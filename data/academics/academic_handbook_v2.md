# Academic Handbook

This handbook covers general academic rules, examination procedures, and guidelines for students with benchmark disabilities.

## 1. General Examination Rules

The following rules apply to all students taking written examinations:

### Reporting & Setup
- **Mobile Phones**: Strictly prohibited inside the examination hall.
- **Identity Card**: Must be carried during all examinations.
- **Seating**: Students must take their seats 5 minutes prior to the scheduled commencement.
- **Latecomers**: No entry is allowed 15 minutes after the start of in-semester exams and 30 minutes after end-semester exams.

### During the Examination
- **Material Exchange**: Borrowing or lending any material is strictly prohibited.
- **Unfair Means**: Attempting to use unfair means will result in immediate expulsion from the hall. Both the student providing assistance and the one seeking it will be punished.
- **Leaving Seats**: Permission must be sought from invigilators before leaving a seat or visiting the restroom.
- **Supplements**: No supplements will be provided in the last 5 minutes.

### Completion
- **Early Exit**: Not allowed in the first 15 minutes (in-semester) or first 30 minutes (end-semester), nor in the last 5 minutes.
- **End of Exam**: Students present in the last 5 minutes must remain seated until all material is collected. Discussion of papers outside the hall is discouraged to prevent disturbing other ongoing exams.

## 2. Guidelines for Students with Benchmark Disabilities

Students admitted under the Physically Disabled category or possessing a valid medical certificate are entitled to specific examination assistance:

### Entitlements
1. **Compensatory Time**: 20 minutes per hour of examination will be provided as compensatory time (pro-rata for shorter exams).
2. **Writer/Scribe**: A writer can be requested via the Controller of Examination (CoE).
3. **Accessibility**: Examination halls will be arranged on the ground floor to ensure easy access.

### Procedure
Eligible students must apply to the CoE at the beginning of the semester, mentioning their registration details and the type of assistance required for each course.
