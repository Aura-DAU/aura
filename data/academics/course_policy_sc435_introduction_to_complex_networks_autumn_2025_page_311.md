## Course Description

Many systems spanning multiple disciplines are composed of many interacting components. Some examples of such systems are neurons in the brain, interaction of people, entities in markets, businesses, computers interacting over the internet, etc. Universali ty and emergence are two essential properties of such systems. Two directions of exploration, which are also intertwined, are analyzing large -scale data and constructing simple models.
