## Overview

This document presents the course syllabus, policy guidelines, and lecture breakdown for EL497 Introduction to Autonomous Vehicles Winter24 - Sujay Kadam.

## Main Content

EL497: Introduction to Autonomous Vehicles Dr. Sujay Kadam, DA-IICT, Gandhinagar Course Format: For
the EL497: Introduction to Autonomous Vehicles course, we have (L-T-P-C) = (3-0-2-4), meaning that
it is a four credit course having three lectures, and one laboratory session weekly.

### Pre-requisites

First year mathematics, and any probability course Course Overview: This course aims to introduce
the students to the fundamentals of autonomous vehicles (ground, underwater and aerial), through
lectures, lab sessions, and hands-on projects taking place as a part of the course. The students
will learn about control systems, sensors, perception, actuation methods, path planning, decision
making and other methods, algorithms and techniques typically required in autonomous vehicles and
the integration of these elements seen in autonomous vehicles.

### Course Objectives

“The course discusses autonomous vehicles concepts and principles from an introductory standpoint.”

### Course Outcomes

After completion of this course, students should be able to: •[CO1] Understand the foundational
concepts of autonomous vehicles, including control systems and vehicular dynamics. [PO1] •[CO2]
Explain the role of sensors and actuators in perception and control for autonomous vehicles. [PO2,
P12] •[CO3] Implement basic simulations for simple vehicles along with perception algorithms and
sensor fusion techniques.

[PO1, PO5] •[CO4] Develop path planning and decision-making algorithms for autonomous navigation.
[PO3] •[CO5] Recognise ethical and safety considerations in autonomous vehicle design and
deployment. [PO8]
