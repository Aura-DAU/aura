Registration and Fees Payment for Winter Semester 2025-26

1. Commencement of Classes of returning students for the Winter Semester 2025-26 will be on 05 
January 2026.

2. Course Registration Form (CRF) for the semester (in the new UniRP (ERP) system only) is required to be

submitted by all students as per the details/schedule, which is being shared separately.

Please note that submission of CRF in the new ERP system is mandatory for registration.

above.

5. Students will need to pay the fees through Eazypay, ICICI Bank only (using the new UniRP system) during

10 to 24 December 2025. The payment can be made in NEFT/RTGS, Net Banking, UPI, Credit card or

6. Please note that the fees deposited should be of accurate amount. Inadequate payment of fees shall 
lead to non-acceptance of registration whereas in case of excess payment, no refund will be made 
except for Loan cases. Please check all the details before payment of fees and in case of discrepancy 
please contact Registrar’s Office.

7. Please note that late payment of fee (on genuine unavoidable grounds) will be allowed up to 30 
December 2025 only, with a late registration fee of Rs. 300 per day for fees deposited after 24 December 
2025. Thereafter, admission of the unregistered students shall stand cancelled.

8. Unregistered students are not allowed to attend the classes/tutorials.
