# IT584 Approximation Algorithms Winter2026

## Main Content

IT 584 : Approximation Algorithms

Course Placement : Approximation Algorithms is an elective course for the first year (2nd
Semester) students of the M.Tech program with software systems specialization. It is also open
as an elective to B.Tech and B.Tech MnC students (recommended for those interested in
theoretical analysis, mathematical and algorithmic aspects of CS).

Course Format: It is 3 hours of lecture and 1 hour of tutorial every week.

Credit Structure: 3-1-0-4 (L-T-P-C)

Course Content: Many problems in computer science are known to be “hard”. It is
computationally very expensive to solve these exactly. The aim of this course is to learn various
techniques to solve such problems in an approximate manner. The course will discuss
algorithmic techniques like greedy algorithms, local search, approximation schemes, LP based
algorithms, randomized algorithms and a few others. For each technique the course will cover
one or more problems and also focus on detailed analysis of the approximation obtained.

Prerequisites: A course on basic algorithms and data structures, discrete mathematics.
Knowledge of probability and random variables, linear algebra and optimization will be useful.
Mathematical maturity and interest will help appreciate and profit from the course.

Reference Books:
There is no prescribed textbook. We will follow material from different sources. Few good
reference books are:


| ● Approximation Algorithms by V. Vazirani |
|---|
| ● The Design of Approximation Algorithms by David Williamson and David Shmoys |
| ● Randomized Algorithms by Rajeev Motwani |
| ● Probability and Computing Mitzenmacher and Upfal |

Course Outcomes:
After successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
● Understand the notion of hard problems and need of approximation algorithms
● Understand techniques to design approximation algorithms for various problems
● Analyze approximation algorithms for different problems
