# Total Number of Academic Areas in Engineering Departments at DAU

## Key Fact

**Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU) has seven (7) broad academic areas** that support all its programs. There are no formal "departments" at DAU; instead, faculty are organized into academic areas.

## Complete List of Academic Areas

| No. | Academic Area |
|-----|--------------|
| 1 | Computer Science |
| 2 | Communication and Signal Processing |
| 3 | Electronics and VLSI |
| 4 | Humanities and Social Sciences |
| 5 | Physics |
| 6 | Mathematics |
| 7 | Design |

**Total: 7 academic areas**

## Engineering-Specific Areas

Of the 7 total academic areas, the following **3 areas are primarily Engineering / Technology domains**:

| No. | Engineering Academic Area |
|-----|--------------------------|
| 1 | Computer Science |
| 2 | Communication and Signal Processing |
| 3 | Electronics and VLSI |

The remaining areas (Humanities & Social Sciences, Physics, Mathematics, Design) support the engineering programs through interdisciplinary course offerings but are not classified as Engineering departments per se.

## Governance of Academic Areas

- Each academic area has an **Area-Coordinator** appointed by the Director on a rotation basis for a term of **two years**.
- Area-Coordinators help Deans govern academic policy in a consultative, decentralised manner.
- Faculty members may belong to **one or more areas** depending on their academic interests.
- The primary objectives of the area-wise organisation are: sharing teaching responsibilities, program administration, fostering collaboration, and academic governance.

## Summary Answer

**DAU has 7 total academic areas. Of these, 3 are primarily Engineering/Technology areas (Computer Science; Communication and Signal Processing; Electronics and VLSI). There are no formal departments; faculty are organised by these academic areas instead.**
