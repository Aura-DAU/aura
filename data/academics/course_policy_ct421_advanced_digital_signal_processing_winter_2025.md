## Course Description
After a few minutes in a restaurant we cease to notice the annoying sound of surrounding conversations, but a sudden silence reminds us of the presence of neighbors. Our attention is clearly attracted by transients and movements as opposed to stationary stimuli, which we beautifully ignore in our auditory perception. Concentrating on transients is probably a strategy for selecting important information from the overwhelming amount of data recorded by our senses.

This course will prepare students in advanced signal processing algorithms along with practical lab experiments.

## Course Outcomes (COs)
After successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| CO | CT421 Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Build a foundation for advanced signal processing algorithms such as short-time Fourier transform (STFT), Gabor transform and wavelet transforms (CWT and DWT). |
| CO2 | Understand recently developed auditory transform and its applications in speech processing. |
| CO3 | Gain MATLAB-based hands-on training for implementation of various signal processing algorithms discussed coherently in the class. |
| CO4 | Understand various practical applications such as instantaneous frequency estimation, image compression, image super resolution, compressive sensing, iris image feature extraction, and speech/speaker recognition. |
| CO5 | Apply and appreciate theoretical concepts to real practical problems of engineering and technological interest through course project work. |
| CO6 | Equip skills to read and understand research papers related to the area of wavelets and time-frequency analysis. |
| CO7 | Apply learned concepts in thesis or project work (SRI, Mini BTP, BTP, Masters, Doctoral) for image and speech-related pattern recognition, machine intelligence, and signal coding. |

## Course Structure

| CT421 Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | **Introduction to Signal Processing and Fourier Analysis**: LTI Operator (System) and Impulse Response, Fourier transform in L1(R) and its properties, Defining Fourier Transform in L2(R), via Density Extension, Global regularity of signal and spectrum decay, 2-D Fourier transform and its properties. Gibbs Oscillations. | 4 - 6 |
| Unit 2 | **Sampling and Reconstruction**: Shannon’s three-step sampling paradigms and its limitations, Removal of Aliasing, Generalized Sampling Theorem in Hilbert Space Framework, Compressive sensing, Concept of frames (linearly dependent vectors). | 4 - 6 |
| Unit 3 | **Basic Mathematical Background**: Review of Basic Functional Analysis: Metric Space, Inner Product Space, Hilbert Space Introduction to Hilbert spaces, L2 norm, inner product, L2(R) spaces. | 4 - 6 |
| Unit 4 | **Time-Frequency analysis and wavelets**: Spectrogram vs. Scalograms, Wavelets: CWT, Admissibility condition, real vs. analytic wavelets. | 4 - 6 |
| Unit 5 | **Multirate DSP**: Upsampling, Downsampling, Quadrature Mirror Filterbank (QMF), Paraunitary Filterbanks, Wavelets and Filterbanks. | 4 - 6 |
| Unit 6 | **Feature Dimensionality Reduction and Machine Learning**: Principal Components Analysis (PCA), Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT). | 4 - 6 |

## Program Structure Context
This course is offered as a Technical Elective for B.Tech students in Semesters VI and VIII, and as a Core Course for M.Tech (ECE) with a Specialization in Wireless Communication and Signal Processing. It is also open to PhD students, forming a foundation for Communication, Signal Processing, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning.

## Additional Notes
**Key Laboratory Experiments (Tentative):**
- **LAB1:** Introduction to MATLAB Programming
- **LAB2:** Significance of Phase of Fourier Transform: A case study for speech and image signals
- **LAB3:** Verification of Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle in Signal Processing Framework
- **LAB4:** Implementation of Time-Frequency Representations: Spectrograms and Scalograms
- **LAB4:** Estimation of Instantaneous Frequency (IF) using Hilbert Transform
- **LAB5:** Image Compression using Wavelet Transforms
- **LAB6:** Implementation of Auditory Transform of Speech Signals
- **LAB7:** Implementation of Constant Q Transform (CQT) and Application to Analysis of Notes of Music Signals
