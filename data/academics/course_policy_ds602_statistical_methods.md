## Course Description
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to statistical methods tailored for data science. It covers fundamental concepts ranging from data summarization, probability theory, and standard probability models to advanced topics such as statistical inference, regression analysis, time series analysis, and resampling techniques like Bootstrap. Students will learn how to apply these methods using R to analyze data systematically and make data-driven decisions.

## Course Structure

| DS602 Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Data summarization: Data types, Data visualization and summarization with the help of tables and the important descriptive measures. | 5 |
| Unit 2 | Probability: Concepts of probability (both objective and subjective). Statistical interpretation of probability, Bayes’ theorem and its applications in solving real-life decision problems. | 5 |
| Unit 3 | Standard probability distributions: Standard probability models (Binomial, Poisson, Uniform, Exponential, Gaussian), Probability distributions of transforms of random variables, Data generation from probability distributions using Monte Carlo method. | 10 |
| Unit 4 | Sampling: Concepts of population and sample (both random and non-random), Parameter & Statistic, Probability distribution of a statistic (small sample & large sample), Central limit theorems, Illustration of sampling distribution using Monte Carlo simulation. | 5 |
| Unit 5 | Statistical inference | 5 |
| Unit 6 | Statistical estimation: Point and interval estimation, Methods of estimation (method of moments & maximum likelihood method) | 3 |
| Unit 7 | Statistical testing of point null hypotheses: Null and alternative hypotheses, Significance testing using p-value, Some standard parametric and non-parametric tests | 4 |
| Unit 8 | Analysis of Variance | 3 |
| Unit 9 | Regression Analysis: Linear Regression & Logistic Regression | 10 |
| Unit 10 | Time Series Analysis | 5 |
| Unit 11 | Bootstrap estimation of Bias and Standard Error | 3 |
