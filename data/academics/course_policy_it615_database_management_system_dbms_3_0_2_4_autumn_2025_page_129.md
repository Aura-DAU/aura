## Course Structure

| Lectures | Topic |
|---|---|
| 4 | Course Overview: basic definitions, data storage, queries, transaction management, administration |
| 6 | Requirements collection and analysis, Data Models, E‐R Model, Conceptual Design using E‐R Model, E‐R to Relational Mapping |
