# Academic Requirements for B.Tech (MnC) Program  
*(Effective from Autumn 2020‑21)*  

## 1. Registration  
### 1.1 Procedure for Registration  
The registration schedule is announced in advance, and registration is normally carried out within the first two days of each semester through the prescribed procedure (Student Registration System or SRS). Late registration may be permitted for valid reasons on submission of an application to the Deputy Registrar and only on payment of the prescribed late registration fee. In any case, registration must be completed before the prescribed last date for late registration in the Academic Calendar. Students having any outstanding dues to the Institute or hostel will not be permitted to register.  

### 1.2 Eligibility for Course Registration  
A student with no backlog courses (i.e., who has passed all the previous courses) will be eligible to register for all courses prescribed in the curriculum for that semester, inclusive of the specified number of electives. A student who has backlog course(s) or is on academic probation may be recommended a different set of courses by the Convenor of the UG Committee.  

### 1.3 Pre‑Requisite Courses  
A student registering for a course must have successfully completed the pre‑requisite course(s), if any, for that particular course. For a hard pre‑requisite, a minimum grade of **DD** is required; for a soft pre‑requisite, a grade of **DE** is required (see Section 4.2 below).  

### 1.4 Withdrawal from Semester and Discontinuation for Failing to Register  
A student who wishes to withdraw **prior** to registration for a semester must obtain formal approval from the Dean (Academic Programs) before the prescribed last date for late registration for the concerned semester. Withdrawal **after** registration for a semester is permitted only on medical grounds or other exceptional reasons, and formal approval must be obtained from the Dean (Academic Programs) before the date of commencement of the end‑semester examination for that semester. Withdrawal, whether prior to registration or after registration, is permitted for only one semester at a time.  

If a student does not register for a regular semester or does not withdraw with permission from the Dean (Academic Programs) as indicated above, he/she will be discontinued from the Institute.  

A student who registers for a semester after having withdrawn in the previous semester may register for the available courses prescribed in the curriculum for that particular semester, subject to fulfilling any pre‑requisites.  

The transcript of a student who has **withdrawn** a semester will show the appropriate status for the corresponding semester(s). The transcript of a student who is suspended from a semester for an academic or disciplinary reason will also show **withdrawn** status in the corresponding semester.  

## 2. Auditing of Courses and Registration for PG Courses  
### 2.1 Registration of Courses for Audit  
Auditing of courses allows students to gain exposure to additional subjects without unduly increasing their overall course load. Registration of courses for audit is permitted from the fifth semester onwards under the following conditions:  

1. A student can take a maximum of **two** courses under audit during the entire program.  
2. The courses to be audited must be entered in the Course Registration Form while registering for the semester, with the word “Audit” specially mentioned in the remarks column.  
3. Both conditions must be satisfied:  
   - (i) The course instructor permits and approves the registration.  
   - (ii) The lecture, lab and tutorial timetables strictly permit it.  
4. An audit course will not be considered as an overload.  
5. If the student’s performance is satisfactory, a grade of **P** (Pass) will be awarded; otherwise a grade of **F** (Fail) will be awarded by the instructor.  
6. An audit course is **not** considered for the calculation of Semester Performance Index (SPI) / Cumulative Performance Index (CPI). However, the course will be reflected in the Semester Grade Report and Transcript as an audit course **provided a grade of P is obtained**; otherwise it will not appear.  
7. In a semester, only **one** course can be registered as an audit course.  

### 2.2 Registration for Post‑Graduate Level Courses for Credit  
Students with a CPI of **7.4** or above may register for available M‑Tech level courses as credit courses from the sixth semester onward. Such courses are regarded as part of the prescribed course load for the regular semester. A student can register a post‑graduate level course provided the following two conditions are satisfied:  

- (i) The course instructor permits and approves the registration.  
- (ii) The lecture, lab and tutorial timetables strictly permit it.  

## 3. Course Load  
### 3.1 Regular Semesters  
A student is permitted to register for additional courses over the prescribed curriculum for a regular semester provided the total number of courses does not exceed **6** and the total credits do not exceed **26**.  

A student may underload his/her prescribed academic load in a regular semester by dropping one or more courses, provided the number of courses is at least **4** and the registered credits are not less than **12**. After completion of the seventh regular semester, a student will be permitted to register for fewer than four courses.  

### 3.2 Summer Semester  
The summer semester is a special semester of approximately eight weeks between May and July. In a Summer Semester, a student can register for a maximum of **three (3)** backlog courses. For the purpose of this rule, internship is regarded as **two (2)** courses and BTech Project Part II as **three (3)** courses; BTech Project Part I is regarded as a single course. No other course can be taken together with an industrial/rural internship.  

For Summer Semester only, the term “backlog courses” indicates a course which a student has previously registered and failed. For a student who has completed eight regular semesters, “backlog course” has the standard interpretation given in the Glossary. Students who have completed eight regular semesters are also permitted to register for grade improvement in the Summer Semester (see Section 5.2).  

## 4. Course Assessment and Modes of Assessment  
### 4.1 Course Assessment  
The various modes of assessment used for rating students’ performance in a course include home assignments, tutorial assignments, laboratory work, group assignments, quizzes, tests (open or closed book), viva‑voce, mini projects, etc., and the end‑semester examination. Attendance in lectures/labs/tutorials may also be given due weightage in course assessment. The instructor may make attendance compulsory (80 % or less) and award an “F” grade to students who do not achieve the prescribed level of attendance. The distribution of weightage for continuous evaluation will be indicated by the course instructor at the beginning of the semester.  

*Note: Academic requirements such as projects and summer assignments, which are prescribed in the curriculum, are regarded as courses for the purpose of assessment.*  

### 4.2 Grading  
#### Grading Scale  

| Letter Grade | Points | Explanation |
|--------------|--------|-------------|
| AA | 10 | |
| AB | 9 | |
| BB | 8 | |
| BC | 7 | |
| CC | 6 | |
| CD | 5 | |
| DD | 4 | |
| DE | 3 | Pass |
| F  | 0 | Fail |
| I  | – | Incomplete |
| P  | – | Passed |

A student passes the course if he/she obtains any grade from **AA** to **DE**; a grade **F** denotes failure. Certain courses are designated as Pass/Fail; in those courses a grade of **P** or **F** is awarded. An **F** grade may also be awarded in case of reported malpractice in examinations or continuous evaluation.  

Pass/Fail courses are not considered for calculation of SPI/CPI.  

The **I** (Incomplete) grade is awarded when a student’s overall performance is satisfactory but the end‑semester examination is missed due to illness, accident, or death in the family, provided the student obtains formal approval from the Dean (Academic Programs) under exceptional circumstances. The student must apply to the Convener of the Undergraduate Committee with appropriate medical or evidentiary support. An **I** grade will be converted into a performance grade after the student takes an equivalent examination; conversion must occur by the date specified in the Academic Calendar for the next semester, otherwise the **I** will be converted to **F**.  

## 5. Repeating a Course  
### 5.1 Requirement to Repeat Failed Courses  
A student must repeat a course in which he/she has obtained an **F** grade for credit. Such a course is regarded as a **[illegible]**.
