# Academic Requirements for B.Tech (MnC) Program  
(Effective from Autumn 2020-21)  

## Overview  
This document outlines academic rules for the four-year B.Tech (Mathematics and Computing) program. Rules apply to students admitted in 2020-21 and onwards. Post-admission academic activities are covered; admission criteria and procedures are excluded.  

## Registration  
### Procedure for Registration  
- Registration occurs via Student Registration System (SRS) within the first two days of each semester.  
- Late registration requires Deputy Registrar approval and payment of fees before the Academic Calendar's deadline.  
- Students with unpaid dues (institute/hostel) cannot register.  

### Eligibility for Course Registration  
- Students with no backlogs may register for all prescribed courses, including electives.  
- Students with backlogs or academic probation may receive a modified course list from the UG Committee Convenor.  

### Pre-Requisite Courses  
- Hard Pre-requisites require a minimum grade of DD.  
- Soft Pre-requisites require a minimum grade of DE (see Section 4.2).  

### Withdrawal and Discontinuation  
- Withdrawal before registration requires Dean (Academic Programs) approval.  
- Post-registration withdrawal permitted only for medical/exceptional reasons.  
- Non-registration without approval results in discontinuation.  
- Withdrawn semesters show "Withdrawn" status on transcripts.  

## Auditing and PG Course Registration  
### Auditing Courses  
- Allowed from 5th semester, max 2 courses total.  
- Audit courses must be listed in registration forms with "Audit" noted.  
- Instructor approval and timetable compatibility required.  
- Audit grades: P (Pass) or F (Fail).  
- Audit courses excluded from SPI/CPI calculations but appear on transcripts with grade P.  

### Postgraduate Course Registration  
- Students with CPI ≥ 7.4 may register for M.Tech courses from 6th semester.  
- Requires instructor approval and timetable compatibility.  

## Course Load  
### Regular Semesters  
- Max 6 courses (26 credits); min 4 courses (12 credits).  
- 7th semester onwards: fewer than 4 courses allowed.  

### Summer Semester  
- Max 3 backlog courses (including internship as 2 courses, B.Tech Project Part II as 3).  
- Grade improvement permitted for students with 8 completed regular semesters.  

## Course Assessment  
### Assessment Modes  
- Includes assignments, labs, quizzes, projects, and end-semester exams.  
- Attendance (≥80%) is mandatory; below threshold results in F grade.  

### Grading System  
| Letter Grade | Points | Explanation         |  
|--------------|--------|---------------------|  
| AA           | 10     | Excellent           |  
| AB           | 9      | Very Good           |  
| BB           | 8      | Good                |  
| BC           | 7      | Satisfactory        |  
| CC           | 6      | Fair                |  
| CD           | 5      | Marginal Pass       |  
| DD           | 4      | Pass                |  
| DE           | 3      | Pass                |  
| F            | 0      | Fail                |  
| I            | -      | Incomplete          |  
| P            | -      | Pass (Pass/Fail)    |  

- Pass/Fail courses excluded from SPI/CPI.  
- "I" grade requires make-up exam within Academic Calendar deadline; otherwise converted to F.  

## Repeating Courses  
- Courses with F grade must be repeated.  
- Backlog courses follow registration regulations (see Section 3).
