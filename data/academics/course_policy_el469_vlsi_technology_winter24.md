**Abstract**  
The course is designed per industry requirements and covers processes, techniques, and methods used in semiconductor manufacturing (silicon and compound semiconductors such as GaN, GaAs, AlGaN) and MEMS devices. Topics include semiconductor industry overview, material properties, wafer growth, clean‑room protocols, diffusion, CVD/PVD, ion implantation, etching, wafer bonding, packaging, and more.

**Suggested Textbooks**  
- *VLSI Technology* by S. Z. Lee  
- *Microfabrication* by Plummer  
- Industry manuals (provided as study material)

## Detailed Course Contents
| Topic | Lectures |
|-------|----------|

| Wafer Specification, Clean‑room Concepts, Wafer Cleaning Techniques (metrology tools, classifications, defect impact) | 5 |

| Etching (isotropic/anisotropic, wet etching of Si/SiO₂/Si₃N₄/Al, dry plasma‑assisted etching, edge profile, step coverage) | 3 |
| PVD Metallization (integrated flow at 130 nm, Vt setting, HKMG concepts) | 3 |

## Outcomes and Objectives
- Spark interest in semiconductors and VLSI.  
- Understand the complete manufacturing cycle of semiconductors.  
- Grasp product development specifications for qualified products.  
- Learn micro‑fabrication techniques and silicon ingot production.  
- Comprehend oxidation mechanics and related models.  
- Master lithography, mask design, and thin‑film deposition methods.  
- Recognize device packaging types and packaging processes.
