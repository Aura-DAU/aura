## Course Content

### Introduction to Semiconductor Industry
- Overview of semiconductor industry, market size, key companies, and products.
- Global and Indian semiconductor scenarios.
- Elementary semiconductor and compound semiconductor (GaN, SiC) introduction.
- **Lectures:** 5

### Material Properties and Wafer Growth
- Process flow for BiPolar and MOSFET.
- Crystal structures, silicon properties, wafer growth techniques (CZ, FZ).
- Types of wafers used in industry.
- **Lectures:** 5

### Wafer Specifications and Clean Room Concepts
- Wafer specifications and metrology tools.
- Clean room classifications, protocols, and wafer cleaning techniques.
- Defects and dislocations impacting yield.
- **Lectures:** 5

### Deposition Techniques
- Thin film deposition (nitride, oxide).
- Oxidation mechanisms, Deal-Groove model, furnace operations.
- Dry and wet oxidation techniques.
- **Lectures:** 5

### Photolithography Techniques
- Basics of optics and lithography process.
- Masks, photoresists, pattern transfer, GDSII.
- Stepper and scanner technologies.
- Next-gen lithography (E-beam, X-ray, DUV, EUV).
- **Lectures:** 5

### Diffusion and Ion Implantation
- Diffusion process, Fick's laws, well formation.
- Ion implantation types, equipment, and process models.
- **Lectures:** 3

### Etching Techniques
- Wet and dry etching methods.
- Isotropic/anisotropic etching, etch profiles, and mechanisms.
- **Lectures:** 3

### PVD and Metallization
- Integrated process flow at 130nm.
- Vt setting and HKMG concepts.
- **Lectures:** 3

## Outcomes and Objectives
- Spark interest in semiconductors and VLSI.
- Understand semiconductor manufacturing cycles and product development.
- Learn microfabrication techniques, silicon ingot making, oxidation mechanics, lithography, and packaging.
