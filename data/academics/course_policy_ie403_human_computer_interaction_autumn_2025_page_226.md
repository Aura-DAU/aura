## Course Description

This course will dive into the fundamentals of how man machine interaction happens and the principles governing the interactions. We will also look at how HCI relates to other fields like user experience design, user interface design, human factors engineering, and psychology. We will learn the three different roles of a user in interface design namely the ‘processor’ view, the ‘predictor’ view, and the ‘participant’ view.
