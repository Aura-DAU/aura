# Registration Notice Summer 2025 26

## Main Content

12 May 2026

Registration for Summer Semester 2025-26

1. Commencement of classes for the Summer Semester 2025-26 is scheduled on 20 May 2026. However, Registration Form
(in the ERP) is required to be submitted during the registration period i.e. between 12 to 19 May 2026.

2. Students of B Tech 2023 and M Sc (IT) 2025 have to register for their respective Internships, commencing from 20 May 2026.
M Tech (ICT) 2025 students have to register for the Major Project I. M Sc (DS) and M Sc (AA) 2025 students have to register for
Value Added Courses. M Sc (DS) 2025 students have to register in addition for Minor Project 1.

non-availability of faculty or due to lack of sufficient registration.

4. Refer academic requirements, available in the folder http://intranet.daiict.ac.in/~daiict_nt01/Academic/ for the rules
applicable to summer registration. For UG program see rule for Summer semester registration in the respective academic
requirement PDF.

5. Your application for registration, submitted through the ERP, will be deemed accepted only after the fees are deposited. The Fee
schedule is as under:


| Particular | All Programs |
|---|---|
| Tuition Fee* [for course(s) other than internship / Value Added Courses] | Rs. 25,000/- per course with maximum tuition fees applicable will not be more than the half of the semester tuition fee applicable to respective batch for students admitted through other than DAFS (NRI-FN) category. |
| Registration Fee | Rs. 1,000/- |


*Tuition Fee is not applicable to internships and thesis type of course. Therefore, the students, who are registering just for internships / value added courses,
are required to pay registration fee only.

6. Students will need to pay the fees through Eazypay, ICICI Bank only (using the new UniRP system) during 14 to 25 May 2026.
The payment can be made in NEFT/RTGS, Net Banking, UPI, Credit card or Debit card only). Detailed instructions for fees
payment are available on the http://intranet.daiict.ac.in/~daiict_nt01/Announcement/Fees-Payment/. The student must retain
copy of payment with them.

7. Please note that the fees deposited should be of accurate amount. Inadequate payment of fees shall lead to non-acceptance of
registration whereas in case of excess payment, no refund will be made except for Loan cases. Please check all the details before
submitting fees and in case of discrepancy please contact Registrar’s Office (fees_query@dau.ac.in).

8. Late registration (only on genuine unavoidable grounds) will be allowed up to 28 May 2026 with a late registration fee of Rs. 300
per day for fees deposited after 25 May 2026.

9. Unregistered students are not allowed to attend the classes/tutorials.
