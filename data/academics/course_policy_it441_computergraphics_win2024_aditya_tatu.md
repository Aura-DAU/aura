## Main Content

IT441 - Computer Graphics (3-0-2-4) Winter 2023-24
1.Course Title: Computer Graphics.
2.Credit structure: 3-0-2-4.
3.Course Type: Technical Elective
4.Prerequisites: Programming, Linear algebra.
5.Course Summary: Computer Graphics deals with simulating the process of formation
of images of 3D objects. Some well known applications include Data visualization, Animation, Virtual
reality, and Medical Imaging.

In the course we will deal with all entities involved in the process of image formation: The object,
camera/viewer and light. The course will additionally cover interaction with the user. Rather than
de- veloping libraries bottom-up, we will use available libraries in a top-down fashion to analyze
various aspects of Computer graphics. The course intends to rely on WebGL for all programming labs
and assignments.

The core of the course consists of Geo- metric transformations, viewports - parallel and perspective
projections, lighting & shading, and how to get these done using a GPU efﬁciently.
6.Content:
(a) Introduction to Computer Graphics and its applications (b) The Graphics systems, Image formation
model, Camera Models, Graphics (c) Implementing 2D graphics in WebGL (d) Programming user
interaction (e) Geometric Objects, transformations (f) Projections - Parallel and Perspective (g)
Lighting and Shading (h) Texture Mapping
7.References:
(a) Edward Angel & Dave Shreiner, Interactive Computer Graphics: A Top-Down Ap- proach with WebGL ,
