## Course Description

This course introduces the fundamentals of complex networks and nonlinear dynamics, focusing on the structure and behavior of real-world interconnected systems. Topics include network models, statistical properties of networks, epidemic spreading, synchronization, and chaos theory. Students will study dynamical processes on static and time-varying networks, including intervention strategies and spreading phenomena in heterogeneous systems. The course also includes recent research literature, problem formulation, and project-based learning to develop analytical and computational understanding of complex systems.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1: Networks | A general introduction. Dynamical processes in networks. Fundamentals of Network theory: Mathematics of networks, Measures and metrics. Network Models: Erdos-Renyi, Scale-free networks, and small-world model. Statistical properties and degree distribution. Random graphs with general degree distributions. Percolation Process in complex networks | 13 |
| Module 2: Dynamics | Autonomous systems, flows on the line and their dynamics. Fixed points and linear stability analysis. The logistic equation, art forgery, item response, tumour growth, etc. Bifurcations | 10 |
| Module 3: Dynamical Processes on Networks | Epidemics in complex networks, Role of heterogeneity in disease spreading. Pharmaceutical and nonpharmaceutical intervention strategy. Epidemics in metapopulation networks, epidemics in time-varying networks, and epidemics in multiplex networks, Activity-driven networks. Synchronization in coupled nonlinear oscillators. Resilience of dynamical systems on networks | 10 |
| Module 4: Problem design | Recent literature survey, and problem design, project | 7 |
| **Total** | | **40** |
