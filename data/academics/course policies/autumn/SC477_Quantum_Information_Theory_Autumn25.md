## Course Description

**Objective:** This 4th-year B.Tech course deals with the essential physical and mathematical processes involved in Quantum computation and quantum communication — an introduction to the emerging field of Quantum Computers. Students are expected to know Quantum Mechanics, but it is not mandatory. The phenomenon of entangled states and its use in controlled operations are introduced. With these basic tools from the physics of Quantum Mechanics, the course intends to prepare students to understand some of the important algorithms in quantum computation, quantum information, and cryptography.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Quantum Theory: (i) Qubits; (ii) Quantum Evolution; (iii) Measurement — Projective and POVM; (iv) Composite system; (v) Entanglement, Teleportation, Quantum Key |
| 2 | Noisy Quantum Theory: (i) The density operator; (ii) Composite quantum system; (iii) Partial trace and reduced density operator; (iv) Schmidt decomposition and Purification; (v) EPR paradox and Bell inequalities |
| 3 | Quantum Evolution: (i) Evolution of a pure state; (ii) Noisy system; (iii) Noisy system evolution; (iv) Bit flip and phase flip channel; (v) Depolarizing channel; (vi) Amplitude damping; (vii) Phase damping |
| 4 | Quantum Error Correction: (i) Three qubit bit flip code; (ii) Three qubit phase flip code; (iii) The Shor code |
| 5 | Entanglement as a physical resource: (i) Transforming bi-partite pure state entanglement; (ii) Local operation and classical communication (LOCC); (iii) Entanglement distillation and dilution |

## Additional Notes

- Course material is based primarily on Nielsen and Chuang's *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information* (Reference 1). Reference 2 (Wilde) covers "much more than the course needs." For introduction to basic ideas of Quantum Mechanics, Sakurai's text is recommended.
- This course is closely related to MC313 (Algebraic Structures) and other advanced mathematics courses in this batch, given its strong linear algebra foundation requirement, and shares the same instructor (Gautam Dutta) as MC313.
