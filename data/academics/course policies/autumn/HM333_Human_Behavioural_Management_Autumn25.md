## Course Description

Managing human behavior is becoming increasingly important for students and professionals to be able to realize their true potentials and be successful in life, in general, and careers, in particular. This course will help students in developing an understanding of human behavior and help them in managing their own behavior. It will help students find an answer to the questions: Why does one behave the way one does in their personal and professional lives? And why do others behave the way they do in their personal and professional lives?

It will deal with the subject matter from the perspectives of individuals trying to improve their relationship with self and others. It covers aspects of human resource management, business communication, grooming and etiquette, attitude, and group dynamics.
