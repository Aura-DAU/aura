## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Introduction to general optimization problems | Analytical solutions; First order necessary and sufficient conditions; Second order necessary and sufficient conditions | 5 |
| 2. Numerical Solution of Convex Optimization | Gradient Descent, Steepest Descent; Newton's Method; Conjugate Direction and Conjugate Gradient Methods | 6 |
| 3. Introduction to optimization methods | Introduction; Formulation of an LPP; Graphical Method to solve an LPP; Nature of a solution | 4 |
| 4. Linear programming problem | Algebraic method; Simplex method; Revised simplex method; Duality; Dual simplex method; Sensitivity analysis | 10 |
| 5. Transportation Problem | North-west corner Rule; Row-minimum method; Vogel's Approximation; u-v method | 6 |
| 6. Game Theory | Introduction to Game Theory; Game with pure and mixed strategies; 2×n and m×2 Games; LPP Formulation of m×n Games | 6 |
| **Total** | | **37** |
