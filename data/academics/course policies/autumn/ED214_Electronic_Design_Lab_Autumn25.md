## Course Description

This module equips students to learn different CAD, EDA tools, and HDL for hardware, and be conversant with measuring instruments.

## Course Outcomes (COs)

After successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Use function generators, oscilloscopes, and complex devices such as logic analysers and spectrum analysers. |
| CO2 | Understand data sheets and debug hardware and software systems. |
| CO3 | Design simple digital ICs and perform synthesis of simple electronic circuits and systems. |
