## Course Description

This course offers a rigorous foundation in algorithm design and analysis. Students will master asymptotic notation, fundamental searching and sorting, and core problem-solving paradigms, including divide-and-conquer, greedy, and dynamic programming. The curriculum also covers advanced graph traversals, network flows, and string matching algorithms. Finally, students will explore computational complexity and NP-completeness, learning practical strategies like approximation algorithms and heuristics to tackle computationally intractable problems.

## Course Structure

*APPENDIX-I: Detailed Course Content (Session-wise/Module-wise)*

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1 | Fundamentals of Algorithm Analysis & Searching: Notions of problems and algorithms, asymptotic notation, and performance analysis. Linear search, binary search, and finding ranks in linear lists | 6 |
| Module 2 | Sorting Algorithms & Analysis: Comparison-based sorts (insertion, selection, bubble, merge, quick, heap) and their time complexity analysis. Linear time sorting algorithms (counting sort, radix sort) | 6 |
| Module 3 | Algorithmic Paradigms & Greedy Strategies: Introduction to iterative and divide & conquer paradigms. Greedy algorithms: principles, fractional knapsack problem, and Huffman codes | 7 |
| Module 4 | Dynamic Programming: Principles of dynamic programming. Applications: search-frequency weighted BST, all-pairs shortest paths, matrix chain multiplication, and longest common subsequence | 7 |
| Module 5 | Graph & String Algorithms: Graph traversals (BFS, DFS), minimum spanning trees (Prim's, Kruskal's), Dijkstra's algorithm for single-source shortest paths, and network flows. String matching: Rabin-Karp and KMP algorithms | 10 |
| Module 6 | Complexity Theory & Intractable Problems: Basics of complexity theory, NP-completeness, and concepts of reductions. Standard methods for handling NP-Complete problems: approximation algorithms, special cases, and heuristics | 4 |
| **Total** | | **40** |
