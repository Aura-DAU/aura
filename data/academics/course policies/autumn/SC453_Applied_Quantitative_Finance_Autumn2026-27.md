## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Risk measurement and management | Brief overview of finance. Stylized facts about financial time series, and key departures from the Gaussian distribution. Value at Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall (ES) models | 8-10 |
| Risk measurement and management (contd.) | GARCH and related models. Regime change models. Filtered Historical Simulation (FHS). Extreme Value Theory (EVT) | 4-5 |
| Portfolio Optimization | Mean variance efficient portfolios with and without short selling. Constrained portfolio optimization. Robust estimation of mean and covariances. Other risk measures instead of variance | 4 |
| Factor Investing and Quantitative Investing | Introduction to single factor (CAPM) and multi-factor models. Review of some important factors (value, size, momentum, quality, investment, low volatility). Computation of factor returns and estimation of factor risk premia. Optimization of factor allocation. Factor timing | 6-8 |
| Statistical Arbitrage and Algorithmic Trading | Introduction to statistical arbitrage. Cointegration and pairs trading. Bid-ask spreads and impact costs. Execution shortfall, trading costs versus execution risk. Economics of market making, and types of orders. High frequency and algorithmic trading | 6 |
| Econometrics and Machine Learning approaches | Dimension reduction: principal component and related methods. Nonlinear models and regularization of over-parametrized models: ridge regression, lasso, and Bayesian approaches. Time-varying coefficients, Kalman Filter and other state-space models. Neural network, regression trees, and other machine learning approaches to over-parametrization and non-linearity | 8-10 |
| **Total** | | **36-43** |
