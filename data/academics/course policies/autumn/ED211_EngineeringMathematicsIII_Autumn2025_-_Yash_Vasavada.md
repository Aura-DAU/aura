## Course Description

ED211 introduces students to various mathematical and engineering techniques related to time and frequency analysis and synthesis, Fourier Transformation, Fourier Series, etc. The course demonstrates the application of elegant mathematical principles to practical engineering design concepts underlying many algorithms implemented in today's electronics and VLSI systems. Students gain hands-on skills on these topics by performing software laboratory experiments throughout the semester. The specific topics covered are continuous-time Fourier Transform, Fourier Series, effect of sampling, discrete-time Fourier Transform, DFT, Z transform, convolution and linear time/shift-invariant systems, design of digital filters, eigenanalysis and its application to OFDM signaling, etc.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

This course introduces students to the engineering knowledge (P1) of the mathematics and engineering fundamentals related to signal analysis and signal processing by linear systems. Students engage in the analysis (P2) and design (P3) of the basic modules of a linear discrete-time and continuous-time system. As part of the Lab/Tutorials, students conduct investigations of complex design and analysis problems (P4) and make use of modern tools (P5) such as MATLAB and Python. Through the Lab/Tutorial — which is team-based and requires a formal presentation — students acquire team-work (P9) and technical communication (P10) skills.
