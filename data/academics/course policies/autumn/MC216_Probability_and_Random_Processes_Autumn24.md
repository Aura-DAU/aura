## Course Description

**Course Objectives:** This course aims to provide foundational knowledge in probability, random variables, and random processes.

**Course Overview:** In the third semester of the B.Tech. (MnC) programme, students are introduced to the fundamentals of probability, random variables, and random processes through this course. These concepts are very essential to students' professional lives in the next several decades. A familiarity with these ideas is required in every branch of science and engineering, as well as management, economics, business, finance, research, and development, among other fields. For students to gain experience in problem solving, they may be required to write simple codes in a high-level programming language such as MATLAB or Python to solve real-world problems/situations as part of assignments in the course.

**Focus Areas:**
- **Employability:** Students taking the course can be employed in industries focusing on machine learning and data science.
- **Skill Development:** Students develop necessary skills to build foundational knowledge in probability and random processes.
- **Entrepreneurship:** Not specified in source document.

## Course Structure

| Topic Name | Content | No. of Lectures (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| Probability: definition and axioms | Course Introduction, Revisiting various concepts in probability, combinatorial analysis, permutations and combinations, random experiment, sample space, axioms of probability, probability space | 2 |
| Conditional probability and Bayes' theorem | Conditional probability, independence of events, multiplication rule, total probability rule, Bayes' theorem | 2 |
| Random variables | Random variable, cumulative distribution function, types of random variables, probability mass function, probability density function, distribution of function of random variables | 3 |
| Discrete random variables | Discrete random variables including Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson and other random variables | 2 |
| Functions of Random Variables, Mean and Expected Value | Mean and Expected Value of a Random Variable, Mean and Expected Value of a Function of a Random Variable, Expected Value of Sums of Random Variables | 2 |
| Continuous random variables | Continuous Random Variables like Uniform, Exponential and Normal/Gaussian distributions; Expectation and variance of a continuous random variable; Distribution of a function of a continuous random variable | 4 |
| Moment generating functions and characteristic functions | Mean and variance, higher order moments and inequalities, generating functions, characteristic functions | 3 |
| Joint distributions, covariance and correlation, independent random variables | Random vector and joint distribution, joint probability mass function, joint probability density function, independent random variables, functions of several random variables, order statistics, conditional distributions, random sum, moments and co-variance, variance-covariance matrix, multivariate normal distribution, probability generating function and moment generating function, correlation coefficient, conditional expectation, bi-variate Gaussian distribution | 6 |
| Law of large numbers, central limit theorem | Modes of convergence, Markov, Chebyshev, and other inequalities, weak and strong law of large numbers, central limit theorem | 5 |
| Random processes and their classification | Stochastic processes, classification of stochastic processes, Bernoulli process, Poisson process, Wiener process | 3 |
| Discrete-time Markov chains, simple random walk | Simple random walk, time series strict sense stationary process, wide sense stationary process, discrete-time Markov chain, Chapman-Kolmogorov equations and N-step transition matrix, classification of states, limiting and stationary distributions | 3 |
| Continuous-time Markov chains | Continuous time Markov chain, state transition diagram and Chapman Kolmogorov equation, infinitesimal generator and Kolmogorov differential equations, limiting and stationary distributions, birth-death process, Poisson process, non-homogeneous and compound Poisson process | 3 |
| Queuing processes | Introduction to queueing models and Kendall notation, M/M/1 queueing model, M/M/c queueing model, M/M/1/N model, other Markovian queueing models, transient solution of finite capacity Markovian queues | 4 |
| **Total** | | **42** |
