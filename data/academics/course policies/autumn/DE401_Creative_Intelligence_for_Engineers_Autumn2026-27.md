## Course Description

The course helps develop students' ability to think creatively and approach engineering and technology problems using structured innovation methods. It aims to foster collaborative teamwork, communication, and interdisciplinary problem-solving skills through studio-based activities and projects, preparing students to apply creative thinking methodologies in academic research, industrial product development, and societal problem-solving.

This course introduces students to structured creative thinking methods that enhance their ability to solve open-ended engineering problems. Students learn tools for developing innovative solutions through interactive studio sessions and collaborative group assignments. Emphasis is placed on applied creative thinking, teamwork, and novelty of ideas.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction to Creative Thinking (Week 1-2) | What is creative thinking; why it matters to Engineers/Technology Students; discussion of real-world case scenarios highlighting the impact in everyday contexts | 2 |
| Overview | Overview of creative thinking techniques; understanding divergent and convergent thinking; lateral and vertical thinking; understanding the thinking framework | 2 |
| Lateral Thinking Techniques (Week 3-6) | Randomized Association, De Bono Hats, Inverse Thinking, Morphological Analysis, Weighted Decision Matrices — application of structured and unstructured ideation techniques, hands-on exercises to generate, expand, and refine ideas using systematic comparison and weighted decision-making for selecting optimal engineering solutions | 12 + 2 |
| Design Thinking (Week 7-8) | Double Diamond Method and structured innovation frameworks covering problem discovery, definition, development, and delivery; application in the engineering design workflow through a classroom activity | 8 |
| Project Brief (Week 9) | Discussion and formulation of Project Brief; mentoring and design review; collaborative project planning | 6 |
| Group Innovation Project (Week 10-12) | Collaborative project and presentation — team-based application of creative problem-solving techniques, development of solutions, prototyping concepts, preparing presentations; final presentations with peer and instructor feedback; reflection on learning outcomes and areas for improvement | 4 |
| **Total** | | **36** |

*Note: "Application of ideas using multiple creativity tools" (2 lectures) is listed alongside the Lateral Thinking Techniques module in the source and has been combined into that row above.*
