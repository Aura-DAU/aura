## Course Description

This course helps students convert technical expertise into professional influence, a skill that often differentiates successful graduates from equally capable peers. In a world where AI can generate code, content, and even ideas, one human skill is becoming more valuable than ever: the ability to influence — an influence and image that is authentic and not fake, built to last.

Whether pitching a start-up, presenting a project, leading a team, interviewing for a role, or building a professional presence online, success depends not just on what one knows, but on how effectively one builds a sphere of influence. This course explores the art and practice of influence through communication, storytelling, and personal branding. Students will learn how ideas capture attention, why stories move people, how reputations are built, and how trust is earned in both physical and digital worlds. Through real-world exercises, presentations, personal reflection, and hands-on projects, students will discover their unique voice, craft their personal narrative, and strengthen their professional presence.

By the end of the course, students will have created a personal brand blueprint, a compelling professional story, and a practical toolkit for building influence throughout their careers. This course addresses a critical gap in higher education by helping students leverage technical expertise into influence and opportunity.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Topic 1: Understanding Yourself as a Brand | Why personal branding matters; discovering your unique story — clarifying values, beliefs, and identity; finding your MasterStory; crafting a personal brand statement | 4 |
| Topic 2: Ethics, Integrity & Authenticity as an Asset | In an age of filters, ghostwritten posts, and AI-polished pitches, authenticity is becoming the rarest and most valuable currency; why lasting influence is built on trust, not tricks; persuasion not manipulation | 3 |
| Topic 3: Brand Principles for Brand 'YOU' | The fundamentals of branding; brand identity, positioning, values, and differentiation; the game of perception; laying the foundation for understanding and building a compelling personal brand | 6 |
| Topic 4: Communicating with Impact and Influence | The psychology of attention and influence; communicating with clarity and impact; the art of simplification — making complex ideas easy; listening as a superpower; reading the room and tailoring communication to create influence | 6 |
| Topic 5: Storytelling for Influence | Why stories stick and facts don't; anatomy of a great story; the Hero's Journey; personal stories that build trust; business storytelling that moves people; storytelling with data; storytelling for change and leadership; crafting your signature story | 4 |
| Topic 6: Personal Branding in the Digital Age | Building a professional identity online; leveraging LinkedIn and other platforms for influence; crafting "thought leadership" in your domain; creating content that builds reputation; AI tools for personal branding; designing and cultivating your digital profile and credentials | 5 |
| Topic 7: Influence and Networking | Hofstede's Cultural Dimensions Theory; the science of influence and persuasion; building relationships that matter; networking without feeling fake; creating communities and collaborative influence | 4 |
| Capstone Project: The Personal Odyssey - The Influence Blueprint | Culmination of the course, requiring students to craft a personal brand statement & values, LinkedIn profile makeover, signature story & pitch, and a personal portfolio 90-day visibility plan; each student presents their capstone project to an invited industry jury | 8 |
