## Course Description

Speech communication is an interdisciplinary subject with different research areas ranging from electrical and communication engineering, linguistics, phonetics, psychology, computer science, etc. Speech communication is the transfer of information from one person to another or from person to machine via speech. This course equips students with different fundamental problems in speech science such as speech production process, speech perception, time-domain and frequency-domain processing, homomorphic speech processing and most popular linear prediction (LP) of speech, and finally different diverse speech communication applications such as speech analysis, speech coding, speech synthesis, speech recognition and speaker recognition are discussed. The course is designed in such a way that relationships between different parts of speech communication can be understood easily. In addition to this, cohesive discussion on speech production and speech perception will help the audience to understand the speech communication process better.

**Course Objective:** To study fundamentals of speech production (physiological, acoustical and aeroacoustic perspective), speech analysis methods — STFT, spectrograms, LP analysis, and cepstrum analysis. A case study on Automatic Speech and Speaker Recognition.

**Course Outline:** The course will first review very briefly the basic concepts of DSP and random processes, followed by a detailed discussion on speech production process from the viewpoint of physiological aspects to acoustic theory of speech production. The unique feature of the course will be a detailed discussion on aeroacoustic theory of speech production and development of the Teager Energy Operator (TEO) and the recently proposed variable length TEO (VTEO).
