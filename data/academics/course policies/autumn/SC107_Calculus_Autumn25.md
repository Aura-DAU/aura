## Course Description

Calculus is a core course for first-year B.Tech ICT program students. Course content includes: Functions of single variable — Mean value theorems and Taylor's theorem, Fundamental theorem of integral calculus; Functions of several variables — Continuity, Differentiability, maxima, minima, Multiple integrals; Complex Variables — Differentiability and analyticity, definite integrals (contour integrals, line integrals), Cauchy integral theorem and formula, Taylor and Laurent series, zeroes, singularities and residues; Ordinary differential equations — ODE of first order, linear ODE of second and higher order with constant and non-constant coefficients.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | Content | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculus of single variable | Countable, uncountable sets, sequence, series, limit, continuity, differentiability, application of differentiability, Rolle's theorem, Mean value Theorem, Riemann integral, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus | 15 |
| 2 | Multivariable calculus | Functions of several variables, Limit, Continuity, Differentiability, Partial derivative, Double integral, Triple integral | 8 |
| 3 | Complex Analysis | Complex functions, limit, continuity, C-R equation, Analytic function, Zeros of functions, singularity, Contour integral, Cauchy integral theorem, Residue | 13 |
| 4 | Ordinary differential equation | ODE of first order, Existence and uniqueness theorem, Exact differential equation, Integrating factor, Separation of variables, Homogeneous equation, 1st order linear ODE, Wronskian, Linear ODE with constant coefficients | 6 |
| **Total** | | | **42** |
