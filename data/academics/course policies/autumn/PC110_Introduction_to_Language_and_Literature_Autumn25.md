## Course Description

This course is designed to introduce students to the study of the English language and literature at the undergraduate level. It follows a two-pronged approach: first, teaching English language through literature, and second, introducing students to the world of literature and teaching them strategies of reading and interpreting texts. Literature offers perspective to readers on their own experiences and introduces them to worlds and worldviews alien to their understanding and experience. Serious engagement with literature can sensitize the reader to themselves and to the world around them, and enhance their ability to think critically and creatively. This course will harness the power of literature to launch students into debates on relationships, sexuality, family, war, media and technology, among other themes relevant to their lives. Students will be exposed to a broad variety of texts and will be asked to engage with them in multiple ways through class discussions, written analysis, presentations, etc.

**Course Objectives:** Activities will be centered around reading, speaking, listening, writing, and critical thinking. After completion of the course, students will be able to better comprehend and analyze written and audio-visual texts and respond confidently in coherent English. Rigorous group activities conducted in class will ensure that they develop good interpersonal and public communication skills.

## Evaluation / Grading Scheme

| Component | Weightage |
|---|---|
| Mid Sem Exam | 35% |
| End Sem Exam | 30% |
| Group Project | 35% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

**Attendance Policy:** Students need a minimum of 75% attendance to pass the course. Attending class means having done the reading assigned for the day and being ready to respond to questions.

**Grading Policy:** Students need to have attempted both exams and have done the assignment in order to pass the course.

**Group Project:** Each student will be assigned to a group consisting of 10 students. Together, the group will work on the assignment. Every student is expected to have participated at every stage of the assignment.
