## Course Description

The course aims to impart basics of VLSI circuit and system design. This will be covered by incorporating various practical examples. Different combinational and sequential circuit designs and their co-integration for system implementation will be taught.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

After successful completion of this course:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Understand the concept of MOS transistors, circuit design, and their applications in the field of VLSI and semiconductor technology. [P1, P2, P3, P4] |
| CO2 | Design different circuits with optimization with respect to different parameters such as speed, power, and area. [P3, P4, P12] |
| CO3 | Use EDA tools and their efficient execution to implement the system design. [P5] |

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction: Trends in VLSI/Semiconductor industry, benefits and challenges, ITRS Roadmap, Beyond CMOS, Moore's Law, Device scaling effects; Basics of MOSFETs and VLSI, inverter designs, review of MOS analytical models, SPICE models; CMOS fabrication process, VLSI circuit design and layout | Not specified |
| 2 | Combinational Logic Design: Static MOS design, Complementary MOS, Ratioed logic, Pass transistor logic, complex logic circuits, transmission gate logic designs | Not specified |
| 3 | Sequential Logic Design: Dynamic logic families and their performance analysis; Static latches, Flip flops and Registers; CMOS Schmitt trigger, Monostable sequential circuits, Astable circuits; CMOS current mirror designs, CMOS amplifiers, Comparator designs; Adder and Multiplier circuits, Memory Designs; Timing analysis (Set-up time/Max-delay constraint, Hold time/Min-delay constraint, Clock-skew budgeting, Time borrowing, simple synchronizer, FSM, introduction to pipelined system/ALU) | Not specified |
| 4 | Datapath Subsystems and Implementation: Adders, Multipliers, Division, Standard Math function implementation, Comparators, shifter-registers, Error Correcting Codes, FSM, Practical circuit and system designs | Not specified |
| 5 | Interconnects: Interconnect Parameters (Resistance, Inductance, Capacitance); Interconnect RC delays — Elmore delay calculation; Interconnect models (lumped RC model, distributed RC model, transmission line model); SPICE Wire Models (Distributed RC lines, Transmission line models); Scaling issues in interconnect (Gate and Interconnect delay); Clock distribution system; Crosstalk and noise in interconnects, mitigation techniques; Non-ideal effects in VLSI interconnects and remedial measures; Performance improvement techniques of on-chip interconnects; Advanced interconnect materials and techniques | Not specified |
| **Total** | | Not specified in source |
