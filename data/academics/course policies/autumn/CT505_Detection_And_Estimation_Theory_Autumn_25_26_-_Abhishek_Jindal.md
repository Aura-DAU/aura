## Course Description

This course covers the theory of statistical signal processing from both the estimation and detection perspectives. Topics include maximum likelihood theory, optimal estimators, Cramer-Rao bounds, Bayesian estimation, Kalman filtering, and hypothesis testing frameworks including Bayesian, Neyman-Pearson, and Minimax detection methods. The course also covers detection of random and deterministic signals in the presence of noise.
