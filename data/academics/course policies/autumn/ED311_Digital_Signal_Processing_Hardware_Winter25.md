## Course Description

The course aims to comprehend digital signal processing and realize it using hardware description language and embedded boards such as FPGA. The course further aims to incorporate project and system development.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

After successful completion of this course:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | The students shall be able to realize and comprehend digital signal processing and its system design |
| CO2 | Extensive hands-on training on HDL and FPGA will help students for practical understanding of the subject. This shall facilitate students to make state-of-the-art practical research projects and meet industrial demands |

## Course Structure

*APPENDIX-I: Detailed Course Content (Session-wise/Module-wise)*

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Concept of Signal Processing | Basics of Signal and System, Sampling Theorems, AD and DA converters | 10 |
| 2. Digital System Design | Registers, ALU, SRAM, IO ports, Instruction Decoder, microprocessor for digital system design | 20 |
| 3. System Development | FPGA concepts, system design and development | 10 |
