## Course Description

Digital Signal Processing Hardware covers digital system design and digital signal processing in the context of modern systems. The course is intended to synthesise both sides (theory and experiment) of the module content, so as to broaden and deepen understanding.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

After successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Build and debug a digital circuit, FPGA, and hardware implementations of combinational and sequential digital systems. |
| CO2 | Understand signals, quantisation, and coding of communications signals. |
