## Course Description

This course is designed to provide engineering students with a comprehensive set of management skills crucial for professional success. By blending theoretical concepts with reflective practices, students will cultivate essential abilities in self-management, interpersonal effectiveness, and strategic thinking. The course aims at the integration of key skills such as self-awareness, focus, resilience, collaboration, communication, time management, critical thinking, problem-solving, and strategic thinking. The course aims to develop well-rounded individuals who are prepared to thrive in dynamic and challenging professional environments.
