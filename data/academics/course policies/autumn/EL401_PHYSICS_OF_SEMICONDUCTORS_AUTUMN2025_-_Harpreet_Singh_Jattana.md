## Course Description

The course is designed as per industry requirements. It covers topics related to the working of semiconductor devices, the physics of these devices and device modelling. The course will cover a brief on quantum physics, wave-particle duality, silicon as a material for use in VLSI devices. This gives a better perspective to IC Design and process engineers. The course has 4 main sections:

- **Section A:** Silicon — material and properties; Structure of silicon; Energy Bands; Band structure; DoS; Intrinsic carrier Concentration; Doping effects; Fermi-Dirac Statistics; Fermi Level
- **Section B:** Transport of electrons and holes; High field transport; Drift and Diffusion; Einstein Equation; Current Continuity equation; PN Junction — under equilibrium, under bias, generation and recombination of carriers
- **Section C:** Application of PN junction; Schottky Junction; BJT transistor equation and delays; MOS Capacitor; MOSFET — working and equations; Energy bands of MOSFET
- **Section D:** Substrate bias effects; Subthreshold conduction; Compound semiconductors; Hetero Junctions; Intro to nanoscale devices; High K dielectrics; Metal gate transistors; Photodetectors; LEDs

## Course Structure

| Topic | Content | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction to Semiconductor Industry, types of semiconductors and usage, markets, products | Intro to industry, market size, key companies, products, markets, global scenario, Indian scenario; Arena of Semiconductors | 5 |
