## Course Description

This course introduces the fundamental concepts and techniques of linear algebra, which form the mathematical backbone for engineering, data science, and physical sciences. The curriculum covers matrices, vector spaces, orthogonality, determinants, eigenvalues, and singular value decomposition. Emphasis is placed on both theoretical understanding and applications, preparing students for advanced courses in machine learning, signal processing, optimization, etc.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

At the end of this course, the students will be able to:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Develop a rigorous understanding of matrices, vector spaces, subspaces, bases, and dimension |
| CO2 | Master Gaussian elimination, LU decomposition, and matrix operations for solving linear systems |
| CO3 | Understand orthogonality, projections, Gram-Schmidt process, QR decomposition, and least squares methods |
| CO4 | Analyze determinants and their applications |
| CO5 | Understand eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalization, and positive definite matrices, Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) and its applications in data science and engineering |

## Course Structure

*Note: "The contents are subject to change based on time and other factors."*

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Linear Equations | Introduction to linear systems; Gauss-Jordan elimination; reduced row-echelon form; rank of a matrix; consistency and number of solutions; matrix algebra and matrix-vector product | 3 |
| Linear Transformations | Linear transformations and their inverses; geometric transformations (projections, reflections, rotations); matrix products and composition; invertible matrices; inverse via Gauss-Jordan; determinant and area of 2×2 matrices | 5 |
| Subspaces of Rⁿ and Their Dimensions | Image and kernel of linear transformations; span, linear independence, bases, and dimension; rank-nullity theorem; finding bases of image and kernel; coordinates, change of basis, and similarity | 5 |
| Linear Spaces | Definition and examples of linear spaces (functions, matrices, sequences); subspaces; span, linear independence, basis, and dimension; linear transformations and isomorphisms; matrix of a linear transformation with respect to a basis | 4 |
| Orthogonality and Least Squares | Orthogonal projections and orthonormal bases; orthogonal complements; Gram-Schmidt process and QR factorization; orthogonal matrices and transformations; least squares solutions and normal equations; inner product spaces | 6 |
| Determinants | Definition of determinants; properties (linearity, row operations, transpose); geometrical interpretation as area/volume and expansion factor; Cramer's rule and classical adjoint | 4 |
| Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors | Diagonalization and eigenbases; characteristic polynomial; finding eigenvalues and eigenvectors; algebraic and geometric multiplicities; complex eigenvalues and rotation-scaling matrices | 6 |
| Symmetric Matrices and Quadratic Forms | Spectral theorem and orthogonal diagonalization of symmetric matrices; quadratic forms and their definiteness; singular values and singular value decomposition (SVD); applications to geometry and data compression | 4 |
| **Total** | | **37** |
