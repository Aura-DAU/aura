## Course Description

*Note: The source document states a Course Objective list rather than a narrative description; reproduced below.*

Develop students' verbal and non-verbal communication skills. Enhance confidence in speaking English in academic and professional contexts. Improve listening and comprehension abilities. Build effective writing skills for formal and informal communication. Prepare students for interviews and group discussions in workplace communication. Enhance self-awareness and apply persuasive communication technique.
