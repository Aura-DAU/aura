## Course Description

This 4-credit course is designed to equip MSc IT students with the essential skills for effective communication and comprehension of ideas that will help them in their career as IT professionals. The course includes sessions intended to improve students' writing competence, ability to articulate ideas and think critically, presentation skills, and command over the English language. Exercises like group activities, writing tasks, class presentations, and creative assignments will be combined with lectures on basic English grammar, reading skills, and protocols of communication appropriate to a modern work culture.

Students are expected to dedicate time for completing take-home readings and writing assignments. Apart from three hours of contact hours per week, the course will include two hours of practical sessions per week dedicated to group activities and language skills.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

At the end of the course, students will be equipped with essential skills for writing and speaking in English in everyday situations, and for communicating ideas as part of work, at presentations, and during interviews. During the course, students will engage in individual and group activities that will improve their writing and speaking abilities in English, as well as their ability to comprehend and appreciate ideas and cultural artefacts like literature, poetry, and cinema.
