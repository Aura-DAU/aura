## Course Description

This course covers the fundamental concepts and basic tools of data analytics with implementation in R. Topics span data summarization, probability, standard probability distributions, sampling theory, statistical inference (estimation and hypothesis testing), regression analysis, time series analysis, and resampling techniques. Course notes will be provided by the instructor for all topics. The course emphasizes data-driven decision making and systematic logical analysis of data to answer important research questions across disciplines, especially in agriculture.

## Course Structure

| Sl. No. | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data Summarization — Data types, data visualization and summarization with the help of tables and the important descriptive measures | 5 |
| 2 | Probability — Concepts of probability (both objective and subjective), statistical interpretation of probability, Bayes' theorem and its applications in solving real-life decision problems | 5 |
| 3 | Standard Probability Distributions — Binomial, Poisson, Uniform, Exponential, Gaussian; probability distributions of transforms of random variables; data generation from probability distributions using Monte Carlo method | 10 |
| 4 | Sampling — Concepts of population and sample (both random and non-random), parameter & statistic, probability distribution of a statistic (small sample & large sample), central limit theorems, illustration of sampling distribution using Monte Carlo simulation | 5 |
| 5 | Statistical Inference — Point and interval estimation, methods of estimation (method of moments & maximum likelihood method); null and alternative hypotheses, significance testing using p-value, some standard parametric and non-parametric tests | 5 |
| 6 | Analysis of Variance | 3 |
| 7 | Regression Analysis — Linear Regression & Logistic Regression | 10 |
| 8 | Time Series Analysis — Components of time series, determination of trend and seasonal components | 5 |
| 9 | Bootstrap Estimation of Bias and Standard Error (Resampling Technique) | 3 |
| **Total** | | **51** |
