## Course Description

This course provides a rigorous foundation in statistical theory, probability modeling, and computational techniques for data analysis. Students will learn to summarize and visualize data, apply standard probability models, and use Monte Carlo simulations to understand sampling distributions. The curriculum equips students to perform statistical inference via parameter estimation and hypothesis testing, build predictive models using linear and logistic regression, and analyze time series data. By integrating theoretical concepts with modern computational methods like Bootstrap resampling, students will develop the essential skills to model uncertainty and draw robust, actionable conclusions from complex datasets.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Data summarization | Data types, data visualization and summarization with the help of tables and important descriptive measures | 5 |
| 2. Probability | Concepts of probability (both objective and subjective), statistical interpretation of probability, Bayes' theorem and its applications in solving real-life decision problems | 5 |
| 3. Standard probability distributions | Standard probability models (Binomial, Poisson, Uniform, Exponential, Gaussian), probability distributions of transforms of random variables, data generation from probability distributions using Monte Carlo method | 6 |
| 4. Sampling | Concepts of population and sample (both random and nonrandom), parameter & statistic, probability distribution of a statistic (small sample & large sample), Central Limit Theorems, illustration of sampling distribution using Monte Carlo simulation | 5 |
| 5. Statistical estimation | Point and interval estimation, methods of estimation (method of moments & maximum likelihood method) | 3 |
| 6. Statistical testing of point null hypotheses | Null and alternative hypotheses, significance testing using p-value, some standard parametric and non-parametric tests | 4 |
| 7. Regression Analysis | Linear Regression & Logistic Regression, Analysis of Variance | 6 |
| 8. Time Series Analysis | Components of time series, determination of trend and seasonal components, special topics | 3 |
| 9. Bootstrap | Estimation of Bias and Standard Error | 2 |
| **Total** | | **39** |
