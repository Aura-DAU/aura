## Course Description

This course provides a rigorous introduction to probability and its applications in stochastic modelling, statistics, and data science. Many applied topics such as martingales, stochastic processes, regression, etc. will be discussed.

**Course Objectives:** This course is designed to equip students with a rigorous understanding of probability theory and its applications in modeling uncertainty across real-world phenomena. The course aims to build students' ability to formulate, analyze, and implement probabilistic and statistical models relevant to domains such as data science, finance, and engineering.

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | Content | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations of probability | Probability spaces, σ-fields, conditional probability, independence, random variables and vectors, Borel-Cantelli Lemmas | 3 |
| 2 | Discrete and continuous random variables | CDF, PMF, PDF, moments, common discrete and continuous distributions, conditional distributions and expectation, functions of random variables | 4 |
| 3 | Generating functions and applications | Different types of generating functions, branching processes, convergence and asymptotics, large deviations | 3 |
| 4 | Martingales | Martingales, sub and supermartingales, optional stopping, random walk, Wald's identity | 5 |
| 5 | Stochastic processes | Brownian motion, Gaussian processes, Stationary and ergodic processes, Stochastic calculus and applications | 6 |
| 6 | Statistical inference | Maximum likelihood estimation, Bayesian inference, Hypothesis testing, Confidence intervals | 6 |
| 7 | Regression and machine learning | Linear regression, logistic regression, generalized linear models (GLMs), supervised and unsupervised learning basics | 6 |
| 8 | Case studies and design of experiments | Inference based on t-distribution, ANOVA, Categorical data analysis and non-parametric methods | 6 |
| 9 | Sampling and bootstrap | Inverse transform, rejection sampling, importance sampling, MCMC and Gibbs sampling, parametric and non-parametric bootstrap | 5 |
| 10 | Miscellaneous topics | Advanced data analysis, reliability theory, etc. | 6 |
| **Total** | | | **50** |
