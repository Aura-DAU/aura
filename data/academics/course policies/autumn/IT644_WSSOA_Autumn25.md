## Course Description

The WSSOA course provides an in-depth exploration of modern distributed computing paradigms, focusing on the design, development, and deployment of interoperable services across heterogeneous platforms. The course covers foundational concepts such as XML, JSON, and RESTful principles, along with advanced topics including SOAP-based services, WSDL, UDDI, and security standards like WS-Security and OAuth. Students will gain hands-on experience with API design, microservices architectures, cloud-based service deployment, and integration techniques, while critically examining scalability, reliability, and performance considerations in enterprise environments. Through case studies and lab work, learners will bridge theoretical understanding with practical implementation, preparing them for research or industry roles in building robust, scalable, and secure service-oriented solutions.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

By the end of the Web Services and Service-Oriented Architecture course, postgraduate students will be able to design, implement, and deploy interoperable, scalable, and secure service-based applications that integrate seamlessly across heterogeneous systems. They will demonstrate proficiency in creating both RESTful and SOAP-based services, applying industry standards such as WSDL, UDDI, XML, JSON, WS-Security, and OAuth, and utilizing microservices and cloud platforms for distributed deployments. Students will be capable of critically evaluating service design patterns, ensuring reliability, scalability, and maintainability in enterprise environments. Furthermore, they will develop the analytical and practical skills to architect service-oriented solutions that address real-world business challenges, bridging the gap between theoretical concepts and professional implementation.

After successful completion of the course, the student will have the ability to:

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction & Foundations: Course overview, objectives, and industry relevance of Web Services & SOA; Distributed systems and the evolution towards SOA; Service-orientation principles — abstraction, contract, reusability, autonomy; Overview of Web service types — REST vs SOAP; XML and JSON as data exchange formats | 7 |
| 2 | Core Web Service Technologies: SOAP fundamentals — message structure, processing model; WSDL — service description and structure; UDDI — service registry and discovery concepts; REST fundamentals — resources, URIs, statelessness, CRUD mapping; HTTP methods, status codes, and content negotiation in REST; Comparative study: SOAP vs REST — use cases and trade-offs | 9 |
| 3 | SOA Architecture & Patterns: SOA reference architecture and components; Enterprise Service Bus (ESB) — concepts and role in integration; Service design principles and granularity decisions; Service composition and orchestration (BPEL, BPMN); SOA design patterns — inventory, composition, messaging patterns; Integration patterns — request/response, publish/subscribe, aggregator, mediator; Case studies of SOA implementations in enterprise systems | 9 |
| 4 | Security, QoS & Advanced Topics: Security challenges in web services; WS-Security — SOAP security extensions; OAuth 2.0, JWT, and API key management for REST services; Reliability and transactional integrity in services (WS-ReliableMessaging, XA); Performance optimization techniques for web services; Cloud-based SOA — deploying services to AWS/Azure/GCP | 9 |
| 5 | Microservices & Contemporary Trends: Microservices architecture vs SOA — similarities, differences, convergence; Service discovery, load balancing, and API gateways (Netflix OSS, Kong, NGINX); Containerization for service deployment — Docker fundamentals; Orchestration of services using Kubernetes | 6 |
| **Total** | | **40** |
