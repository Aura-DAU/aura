## Course Description

Core course for MDes (CD) Semester 3. This is an advanced specialised course in design, aimed to integrate inputs from previous courses into a 'multimedia' matrix, specifically focusing on understanding 'movement' over time and space.

This module introduces students to the fundamentals of animation and motion graphics, covering the principles and dynamics of motion & timing, and how to apply these concepts to both character-based animation and abstracted motion graphics. Students gain the basic concepts, methods/means, and language to conceptualize and visualize simple ideas through animated sequences and short films, as well as abstract narrative through shapes, forms, and graphic imagery as part of motion graphics. Storytelling, storyboarding, animatic, setting a stage, and kinetics are the main areas explored.

The module also delves into communicating complex ideas and hidden worlds effectively through the language of movement, animating objects and characters. Motion graphics — a subset of digital animation combining graphic design elements and composition with movement — is distinguished from traditional character-driven animation by its focus on abstract conceptual visual storytelling. The module may also touch on lip synchronization, animation workflow, the importance of sound, graphic design fundamentals, and abstract narrative for visualizing data/statistics/ideas, integrating text and icons.

Students are introduced to key software like Adobe After Effects (or associated software like Cinema 4D, Blender), and by course end are conversant with conceptualising, designing, and executing a short project/film, title sequences and introductions, UI animations, and data visualisation through movement over time.

**Knowledge outcomes:**
1. Principles of Animation
2. Understand animation scope, types, and pipeline; gain basic control of timing and movement
3. Apply core animation principles to create believable motion
4. Enhance animation expressiveness and clarity
5. Build visual storytelling skills with minimal frames
6. Navigate After Effects efficiently for 2D animation
7. Create engaging text animations for branding
8. Integrate animations with live-action footage

1. Understand team roles and project planning
2. Collaborate effectively in a team pipeline
3. Work on consistency and pacing as a team
