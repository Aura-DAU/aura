## Course Description

Notions of matrices are of fundamental importance in Mathematics as well as several other areas including Machine Learning, Data Science, Computer Vision, Networking and Optimization, Computer Science, Signal Processing, etc. This course aims to develop a strong foundation for matrix analysis, developing essential tools. The emphasis is on both analytic and algebraic aspects of Matrix Theory.

## Course Structure

*Note: "The selection of topics is variable depending on time constraints," per the source document.*

| Unit | Topic | Content | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review | Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, special types of matrices, inner product space | 2 |
| 2 | Matrix decomposition | Schur triangular form, spectral theorem, singular value decomposition, polar decomposition, generalized inverse, least squares solutions | 10 |
| 3 | Positive semidefinite matrices | Positive semidefinite and Positive definite matrices: Definition, properties and characterizations | 10 |
| 4 | Matrix norms | Definition and examples of matrix norms, spectral radius formula, relationship between matrix norms and singular values | 10 |
| 5 | Entrywise positive and non-negative matrices | Definition, Properties, Perron's theorem, irreducible matrices, Perron-Frobenius theorem | 10 |
| **Total** | | | **42** |
