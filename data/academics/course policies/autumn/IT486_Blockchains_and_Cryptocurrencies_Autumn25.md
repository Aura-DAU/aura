## Course Description

**Objectives:** This course explores the fundamentals of blockchains and cryptocurrencies.

Topics covered include blind signatures, anonymous electronic cash, decentralized cryptocurrencies, proof of work, Bitcoin blockchain, bitcoin transactions, Script language, micropayments, lotteries, cryptocurrency exchange, proof of solvency, atomic currency swap, mining, mining pools, chain splits, ECDSA/Schnorr signatures, signature aggregation, Pedersen commitment, confidential transactions, simplified payment verification, hierarchical key generation, unlinkable transactions, coin mixing, ring signatures, transaction malleability, smart contracts, and Ethereum.

Upon completion of the course, students should be able:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|

| CO3 | To understand the limitations of cryptocurrencies such as bitcoin. |
