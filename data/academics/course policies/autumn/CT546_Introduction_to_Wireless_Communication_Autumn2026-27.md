## Course Description

Wireless Communication is at the heart of most of the critical applications in real-world systems today. For a wireless system engineer, it is important to understand the fundamentals of transmission and reception, i.e., the physical layer of wireless communication. This course caters to this need at the fundamental level, dealing with the theoretical concepts of wireless communication.

For overall learning, the theory is supplemented with simulations to help the student understand how the theoretical component translates into real-time operation.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1 | Revision of fundamentals of digital communication systems: baseband and passband signals, Gaussian random variables, joint Gaussianity, random processes, noise modeling, linear operations on random processes, linear modulation | 8 |
| Module 2 | Mobile Radio Propagation: large-scale path loss (free space propagation model, basic propagation mechanisms, two-ray model, log-distance path loss model, log-normal shadowing, cell-coverage area); small-scale fading and multipath (Doppler shift, impulse response model of a multipath channel, coherence bandwidth, Doppler spread and coherence time, flat and frequency selective fading, fast and slow fading, Rayleigh and Rician distribution, envelope and power distribution, level crossing rate and average fade duration, discrete-time model) | 12 |
| Module 3 | Capacity and Diversity: capacity in AWGN, capacity of flat fading channels (channel side information known at transmitter and receiver), receiver diversity (selection combining, threshold combining, maximal-ratio combining/MRC), transmitter diversity (Alamouti scheme) | 10 |
| Module 4 | Performance over wireless channels: geometric representation of signals, receiver structure and sufficient statistics, decision regions and ML decision criterion, error probability and union bound, MPAM/MPSK/MQAM, error probability for BPSK/QPSK/MPSK/MPAM/MQAM over AWGN channels, error probability under fading | 10 |
| **Total** | | **40** |
