## Course Description

To equip postgraduate students with the theoretical foundations and advanced analytical skills required to transition from localized, conventional ground-based surveys to macro-scale, satellite-based observation systems. The course focuses on leveraging Remote Sensing (RS) and Geographic Information Systems (GIS) to analyze, model, and evaluate regional earth surface dynamics, environmental processes, and temporal landscape evolution.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Explain and Evaluate Geospatial Fundamentals — Critically analyse the physical principles of electromagnetic radiation (EMR) interactions with earth surface features and differentiate between various spatial, spectral, radiometric, and temporal resolutions of satellite sensors |
| CO2 | Process and Enhance Digital Image Data — Demonstrate proficiency in basic digital image processing techniques, including radiometric and geometric corrections, image enhancement, and supervised/unsupervised classification algorithms to extract thematic information |
| CO3 | Design and Manage Spatial Databases — Develop structural spatial databases by understanding the mechanics of vector and raster data models, coordinate systems, map projections, and topology creation within a GIS environment |
| CO4 | Execute Advanced Spatial Analysis — Apply GIS analytical functions such as buffering, overlay analysis, network analysis, and terrain modelling (DEM/DTM) to solve complex geographic and environmental problems |
| CO5 | Integrate RS and GIS for Real-World Modelling — Synthesize remote sensing derivatives (e.g., NDVI, land cover maps) with multi-layered GIS datasets to model, monitor, and evaluate regional environmental processes, urban growth, or natural hazards |

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Module 1 | Fundamentals of remote sensing with physical principles and laws involved in radiometry | 5 |
| Module 2 | Detailed study of satellite-acquisition and platforms and sensors characteristics | 8 |
| Module 3 | Detailed study of satellite image processing - Image enhancement - linear and non-linear contrast enhancement, band ratio, neighborhood raster operations, spatial filtering, principal component analysis, vegetation indices, band ratio, neighborhood raster operations, spatial filtering, principal component analysis | 6 |
