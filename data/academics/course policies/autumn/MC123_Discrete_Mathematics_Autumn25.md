## Course Description

The primary aim of this course is to introduce students to the fundamentals of Discrete Mathematics. It is a core course for first-year students of the B.Tech MnC program, with 3 hours of lecture every week and 1 tutorial per week.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

After successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Understand and create mathematical arguments for solving problems. |
| CO2 | Understand mathematical structures such as sets, graphs, trees and learn their uses in computer science. |
| CO3 | Develop skills towards mathematical modeling and analysis of engineering problems. |
| CO4 | Understand discrete probability distributions based on simple combinatorial processes, and calculate the probabilities and expectations of simple events under such discrete distributions. |

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic discrete structures: Set operations, Functions, Relations and their properties, Equivalence relations, Closure of relations, Partial orderings, Sequences and summations, Matrices | 3 |
| 2 | Mathematical logic and Proof techniques: Propositional logic, predicate logic and quantifiers, logic operators, logic proposition; Introduction to different standard proof techniques | 3 |
| 3 | Combinatorics: Combinatorial Principles and Techniques, permutations and combinations, binomial coefficients and identities, Pigeonhole principle, inclusion-exclusion principle, Recurrence relations, Solving linear recurrence relations and applications | 8 |
| 4 | Number Theory: Induction, strong induction, well-ordered property, structural induction, and generalized induction; Modular arithmetic, primes and representation of integers, linear congruences, cryptography, RSA cryptosystem | 6 |
| 5 | Probability: Basic discrete probability, Probabilistic counting, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, random variables | 6 |
| 6 | Graph Theory: Undirected graph, Representation on the computer (adjacency matrix), Handshaking lemma, Euler path and circuit, Hamiltonian cycle, Complete graph, cycles, bipartite graph, planar graphs, Euler formula, shortest path algorithm, Traveling salesman problem, trees, properties of trees, spanning trees | 8 |
| 7 | Boolean Algebra: Boolean functions, logic gates, minimization expressions, K-maps | 3 |
| 8 | Modeling Computation: DFA, NFA, languages, regular expressions, grammars, PDA, introduction to Turing machines | 3 |
| **Total** | | **40** |

## Program Structure Context

MC123 Discrete Mathematics is a core course for first-year students of the B.Tech MnC (Mathematics and Computing) program, with a 3-1-0-4 credit structure (3 lecture hours, 1 tutorial hour per week).

---

## Additional Notes

- This course covers an unusually broad range of discrete mathematics topics for a single course, including combinatorics, number theory, probability, graph theory, Boolean algebra, and theory of computation (DFA/NFA/Turing machines).
- The course includes an introduction to the RSA cryptosystem within the Number Theory unit.
