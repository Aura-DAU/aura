## Course Description

ICT in itself may not generate a vivid picture of itself, but it encompasses almost all walks of our lives. The prime objective of this course is to make students aware of the ubiquity of ICT. Therefore, this is a foundation course.

This course is designed to provide students a contextual understanding of different facets of ICT and how information and communication technologies (ICT) manifest in various existing and emerging verticals of science, technology, and engineering. Lectures are planned in a way to expose students to many of the STEAM verticals. Lectures comprise various problem statements considering real-world issues and the need to provide ICT-based solutions to abate those issues.

If the lectures are to help students "think and ideate," lab work involves "visualization" of spaces and objects and ultimately the solutions — 1D, 2D, 3D, projections, isometric views of lines, and embedding of solids will be introduced in the lab classes.

As future ICT engineers, students have to observe challenging issues around them and solve them to improve the quality of life of society at large. These solutions, within the scope of ICT, may be from the pure design part, the mechanical design aspect, and/or the electrical/electronics point of view. In the lab classes, students are given a glimpse of these three modules, as far as possible.

## Course Structure

**Lecture Plan** (Applications of ICT towards the following topics, in alphabetical order):

| Unit | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | AI/ML/Deep Learning |
| 2 | Communication Technology |
| 3 | Computational/Data Science |
| 4 | Design |
| 5 | Fashion & Music |
| 6 | Healthcare |
| 7 | IoT/Automation/Robotics |
| 8 | Landscaping |
| 9 | Mathematical Science |
| 10 | Photography & Art |
| 11 | Quantum Computing and Measurement |
| 12 | Science Education |
| 13 | Semiconductor Industry |
| 14 | Social Media |
| 15 | Some successful project demonstrations (to understand project life-cycle) |
| 16 | Technology Readiness Levels (TRL-1 to TRL-9) |

**Lab Plan:**

*Product Design part:*
1. Introduction to AutoCAD
2. AutoCAD + Video
3. Multiview drawing, Dimensions, Object Design
4. Demo — Rhino
5. 3D Printer

*Mechanical design part:*
1. Drilling & Milling Machine
2. Dremel (for finishing milling, grinding, cutting, etc.)
3. Bench Lathe Machine
4. Octagon Tabletop CNC Lathe Machine
5. Welding machine (students are not allowed to use it)
6. Grinder Machine

*Electronic design part:*
1. Recognizing electronic components
2. Understanding footprint of components and PCB and its relation with final circuit design
3. Understanding power consumption aspect of the design
4. Reading spec sheet of components
5. PCB Design on DipTrace/Eagle
6. Working with circuit boards and chips — soldering, PCB design
7. PCB fabrication and populating components
8. Final designed PCB performance testing
