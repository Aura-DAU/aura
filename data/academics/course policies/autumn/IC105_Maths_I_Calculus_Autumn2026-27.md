## Course Description

This course provides the student a solid platform to study and understand other courses in ICT such as Signals and Systems, Electromagnetic Theory, Machine Learning, etc. Through the use of the unifying themes of derivatives, integrals, limits, approximations, and applications, the content develops into a cohesive whole rather than a collection of topics in Calculus. The contents cover all the essential components of calculus, and are intended to be challenging and demanding.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Functions of a Single Variable: sequences and series, convergence of sequences and series, power series and representation of functions as power series, functions of one variable (limits, continuity, differentiability), applications of derivatives (Rolle's Theorem, Mean Value Theorem, extreme values of functions), integration and applications of integration, improper integrals, introduction to Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs) | 15 |
| 2 | Functions of Several Variables: limits and continuity of multivariable functions, partial derivatives, directional derivatives, total derivative, applications of partial derivatives, multiple integrals, applications of multiple integrals in computing area, surface area, and volume of solids | 10 |
| 3 | Functions of Complex Variables: limits and continuity of complex functions, analytic functions, sequence/series and power series of complex functions, contour integrals | 9 |
| 4 | Vector Calculus: gradient, divergence, and curl, line integrals, Green's theorem | 6 |
| **Total** | | **40** |
