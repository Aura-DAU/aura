## Course Description

This course introduces a review of semiconductor devices, the basic properties of semiconductors, and their conduction processes.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

After successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Understand the basic fundamentals of semiconductor physics |
| CO2 | Comprehend different semiconductor devices |
| CO3 | Use semiconductor devices for different applications; facilitate making state-of-the-art practical research projects and meet industrial demands |

## Course Structure

| Module | Topic |
|---|---|
| Module 1 | Introduction to Semiconductor Physics |
| Module 2 | PN Junctions |
| Module 3 | Semiconductor devices, Photodiodes, Zener diodes |
| Module 4 | Applications of semiconductor devices |
| Module 5 | Solar cell |
| Module 6 | BJT and MOSFET characteristics |
| Module 7 | LED and Laser and compound semiconductor devices |

*Lecture counts per module not stated in source document.*
