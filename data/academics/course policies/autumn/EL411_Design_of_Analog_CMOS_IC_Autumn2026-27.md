## Course Description

This is a foundation course for advanced courses in AMS Design (Data Converters, Power Management Circuits, Voltage References, PLL, DLL, Filters). The course is designed as per industry requirements. Its main aim is to give students a thorough understanding of the MOSFET as a device, and how these transistors can be used to build single-transistor configuration circuits and later more complex transistor circuits like OTA and OPAMP.

The course includes an introduction to MOSFET, their characteristics, device models, large signal and small signal models, use of models to analyze circuits, single transistor architectures, biasing techniques, and finally how to design amplifiers. This gives a better perspective on design of analog products to IC design and process engineers.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Module 0 | Introduction to Semiconductor Industry, types of semiconductors and usage, markets, products | 1 |
| Module 1: Y parameters, Incremental analysis | What is an Amplifier, amplifier at the heart of Analog products; understanding specs of Amplifier, knowing the TI Product datasheet; one-port and two-port devices, Z parameters, Y parameters; analysis of circuits having non-linear elements, incremental signal analysis | 4 |
| Module 2: MOSFET device - characteristics, MOS Models | MOSFET functionality, regions of operation, IV characteristics, small length and long length devices, DC MOS Models, AC Models, capacitances in different regions of operation | 2 |
| Module 3: MOSFET as single Tx Amp, Bias techniques, Bias stability | CS stage DC analysis, fixing operating point by Vgs, biasing by voltage, biasing by current, bias stability, different techniques of bias, NFB, fixing operating point by NFB, CS with -ve FB bias circuit DC analysis | 6 |
| Module 4: Current source, current mirror realizations, cascode current mirrors | Current source realization, current mirror with same Vgs and Vds as CS, cascode current mirrors/sources, biasing in integrated circuits | 4 |
| Module 5: Single Tx Configurations | VCVS by NMOSFET - DC and AC analysis, voltage buffers, CCCS - biasing, DC, incremental analysis, current buffers, VCCS, CSVS | 6 |
| Module 6: Frequency response | Frequency response of controlled sources/voltage and current buffers | 3 |
| Module 7: Differential circuits, OTA | Differential circuits advantages, Diff Amp, biasing of Diff Amp, Diff Amp with active loads, half circuit analysis (differential and common mode circuit), AC and DC analysis, calculating CMRR, OTA, swing limits | 7 |
| Module 8: Design of OTA/OPAMP | Design of operational amplifiers, 1-stage, gain boosting by cascode, 2-stage amp, interfacing stages in amplifier design, stability analysis, swing limits, calculation of CMRR, frequency response (Bode plots, dominant pole), noise analysis, mismatch analysis in TSPICE | 8 |
| Module 9: Industrial specs of amplifiers, Layout Intro | Understanding data sheet, specifications of COTS amplifier, showing and discussing the layout of amplifier, GDSII shot, wafer/die image | 3 |
| **Total** | | **44** |
