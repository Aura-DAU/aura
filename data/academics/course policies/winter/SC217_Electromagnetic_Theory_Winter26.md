## Course Description
Electromagnetic Theory (SC217) is a compulsory course for BTech (ICT and ICT-CS) students. The course will consist of 3 hours of lectures each week, and one tutorial of duration 1 hour.

This course is targeted at engineering students and will teach basic electrodynamics which governs the response of a medium to a signal. The course will mainly concentrate on electromagnetic waves and their behaviour in different media.

## Course Structure
The source document lists a detailed 39-item "Syllabus and lecture sequence" (topic-by-topic, not grouped into numbered units with lecture-hour counts). Key groupings, summarized from the full sequence:

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Vector Calculus Foundations | Electromagnetism/electrodynamics basics; scales of interaction (Coulomb, Oersted, Ampere, Faraday); fields (scalar/vector); flux and field strength; gradient, divergence, curl; second-derivative identities; potentials; vector integrals; Gradient/Gauss divergence/Stokes curl theorems; curvilinear coordinates (cylindrical, spherical) and vector differential operators in these systems (items 1–13) | Not stated in source document |
| Electrostatics | Coulomb's law and superposition; integral/differential forms of Gauss's law; Gauss's law in spherical, cylindrical, and plane symmetry; boundary conditions; Poisson's and Laplace's equations and their symmetry-specific solutions; uniqueness theorems; method of images; dipole moment; dielectrics, polarisation, bound charges; electric displacement, susceptibility, permittivity (items 14–25) | Not stated in source document |
| Magnetostatics | Lorentz force; Biot-Savart law; Ampere's law (integral/differential) and applications; magnetic flux/divergence; magnetostatic boundary conditions; Maxwell's equations for statics (items 26–29) | Not stated in source document |
| Electrodynamics and Electromagnetic Waves | EMF, Faraday's law; equation of continuity, displacement current, Maxwell's equations of electrodynamics; magnetic fields from current loops; polarisation current; wave equation and light as an EM wave; EM waves in matter, refractive index; plane EM waves; energy density, Poynting vector, irradiance; momentum density, radiation pressure; boundary conditions at interfaces; reflection/transmission at normal and oblique incidence; geometrical optics; total internal reflection (items 30–39) | Not stated in source document |
