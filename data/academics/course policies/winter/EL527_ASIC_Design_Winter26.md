## Course Description

VLSI (Very Large Scale Integration) technology has emerged as a very important technology in modern electronics, featuring deep sub-micron manufacturing processes, low voltage operations, exploding speeds, and smart programmable devices sufficient to digest ambient conditions to extremes. The electronics industry worldwide is rapidly approaching another revolutionary leap in the global market scenario. Semiconductor technology has crossed the quarter-micron threshold, making tens of millions of transistors available on a single chip equipped with the powerful arm of VLSI design. This gives the electronics industry the potential to create designs of incredible densities and lightning speeds while utilizing batteries to power them. This has had a phenomenal impact on widespread applications ranging from consumer electronics, communications, and defense to just about everything.

As part of the Electronics India program by the Govt. of India, most electronic products and semiconductor ICs are planned to be made in India. This skill development program in VLSI Physical Design will help generate skilled manpower in IC design and manufacturing.

**Course Content:** In this course, individuals shall learn how to implement a design from RTL-to-GDSII. It will start by coding a design in VHDL or Verilog, then simulating the coded design, followed by design synthesis and optimization. Further, equivalency checks are run at different stages of the flow. After synthesizing the design, it will be followed by floorplan, and place-and-route of the synthesized netlist while meeting timing. Gate-level simulation shall be run throughout the flow. Finally, a GDSII file is written out.

## Course Outcomes (COs)

Learn about the various ASIC architectures, ASIC design flow, issues in ASIC design and verification, and its fix. The following are the highlights:

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Describe architecture based on application specifications. Identify circuit topology. [P1–P5] |
| CO2 | Derive, design and define circuit parameters. [P1–P5] |
| CO3 | Write synthesizable RTL code. [P1-P3, P5] |
| CO4 | Perform synthesis, process design flow, CTS, routing etc. [P1-P5, P9, P12] |
| CO5 | Verify and validate the integrated circuit at various stages of design. [P1-P5, P9, P12] |
| CO6 | The final step is generating the GDSII file of the design that is sent to the foundry for fabrication. [P5] |

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Frontend design | 3 |
| 2 | Introduction to backend flow, Inputs and outputs of synthesis, Synthesis | 5 |
| 3 | Types of synthesis, Command flow of synthesis | 4 |
| 4 | Static timing analysis, Timing paths, Timing slack calculations | 4 |
| 5 | Constraint designing, Report analysis of timing paths, MMMC, OCV | 4 |
| 6 | Introduction to PD flow, Floor plan, Power plan | 5 |
| 7 | Placement, Types of Placement, Congestion | 5 |
| 8 | Clock tree synthesis, Clock skew, Clock tree optimization | 4 |
| 9 | Routing, Types of routing, Crosstalk | 4 |
| 10 | Physical Verification, Timing fixes | 4 |
| 11 | Project | Throughout the semester as lab component |
| **Total** | | **42 (lecture units) + semester-long project/lab** |

## Program Structure Context

EL527 ASIC Design is an Elective course offered to M.Tech ICT (VLSI and Embedded Systems) Semester II students and B.Tech (ICT and CS) Semester VI/VIII students, with a 3-0-2-4 credit structure. It requires Basics of VLSI as a prerequisite.

## Additional Notes

- The course covers the complete RTL-to-GDSII ASIC design flow: RTL coding, simulation, synthesis, equivalence checking, floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, physical verification, and final GDSII generation.
- The course explicitly connects to India's national semiconductor manufacturing initiatives (Electronics India program).
- No textbook is used; course materials are instructor-prepared and supplemented with online references.
