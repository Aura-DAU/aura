## Course Description

At the end of the course, students will be able to:
1. Mathematically formulate and solve different types of optimization problems.
2. Define constraint functions on a problem.
3. Check the feasibility and optimality of a solution.
4. Apply different types of classical and modern optimization methods to solve real-world engineering problems.
