## Course Description

After a few minutes in a restaurant, we cease to notice the annoying sound of surrounding conversations, but a sudden silence reminds us of the presence of neighbors. Our attention is clearly attracted by transients and movements as opposed to stationary stimuli, which we beautifully ignore in our auditory perception. Concentrating on transients is probably a strategy for selecting important information from the overwhelming amount of data recorded by our senses.

This course will prepare students in wavelet signal processing algorithms such as signal (image) compression, signal denoising, edge detection, etc.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fourier Analysis (Kingdom): Basic review of Signals and Systems and Related Calculus — LTI Operator (System) and Impulse Response, Fourier transform in L1(R) and its properties, Defining Fourier Transform in L2(R) via Density Extension, Global regularity of signal and spectrum decay, 2-D Fourier transform and its properties, Gibbs Oscillations |
| 2 | Sampling and Reconstruction: Shannon's three-step sampling paradigms and its limitations, Removal of Aliasing, Generalized Sampling Theorem in Hilbert Space Framework, Compressive sensing |
| 3 | Wavelet vs. STFT Frames and Applications: Concept of frames (linearly dependent vectors) and application to noise reduction, Design of Sigma-Delta Analog to Digital Converters, Communications, etc. |
| 4 | DSP Hardware Architectures: Radix-2 FFT, and DWT |
| 5 | Review of Basic Linear Algebra and Functional Analysis: Metric Space, Vector Space, Linear Combination, Basis, Frames, Normed Space, Banach Space, Inner Product Space, Hilbert Space — Introduction to Hilbert spaces, L2 norm, inner product, L2(R) |
| 6 | Time-Frequency analysis and wavelet: Spectrogram vs. Scalograms, Wavelets — CWT, Admissibility condition, real vs. analytic wavelets, Application of Wavelets to Instantaneous Frequency (IF) Estimation |
| 7 | Wavelet Zoom: Lipschitz Uniform vs. Pointwise Regularity, Vanishing Moments of Wavelets, Detection of Singularities, Multiscale Edge Detection, Multifractals, Fractal Dimension, Singularity Spectrum, Modeling of Hydrodynamic Turbulence |
| 8 | Multirate DSP: Upsampling, Downsampling, Quadrature Mirror Filterbank (QMF), Paraunitary Filterbanks, Wavelets and Filterbanks |
| 9 | Feature Vector Dimensionality Reduction for Machine Learning: Binning, Log-transform, Principal Components Analysis (PCA), Eigenvalue decomposition, Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT), DWT |
| 10 | Wavelets in Machine Learning: Wav-KAN (Wavelet Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks), Wavelet Scattering, Designing Learnable Wavelet Filters, etc. |
