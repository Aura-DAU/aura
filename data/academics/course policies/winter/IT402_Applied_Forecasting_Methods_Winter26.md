## Course Description

This course aims to develop a systematic understanding of forecasting models from a machine learning perspective. The course offers a smooth journey from the basic Least Square Regression model developed around the year 1805 to modern complex forecasting methods after 2020. The content of the course covers variants of point and probabilistic forecasting methods with detailed statistical and mathematical analysis for panel and time-series data.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

**Outcomes and Objectives:**
- Systematically develop a deep understanding of the main principles of forecasting.
- Develop the machine learning perspective for forecasting and uncertainty modeling.
- Develop the understanding of statistical and modern time series models for point and probabilistic forecasting tasks.
- Develop the implementation and practical skills for modern and state-of-art forecasting methods used in research and industry after COVID-19.
- Gain hands-on experience for different forecasting tasks in Finance, Energy, and Sustainability verticals.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | Content | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Forecasting Methods | Application of forecasting in different verticals like energy, finance, environment and health; Motivation, Definition, objectives and approaches in Forecasting | 2 |
| 2 | Regression from ML perspective | Linear regression models, Least Square Methods, Non-linear regression models, Kernel Regression, Gradient Descent Methods, Evaluation Metrics | 6 |
| 3 | Quantile Regression & Uncertainty Quantification | Quantile Regression model, Pinball loss function, Quantile Regression with Kernel Machine, Quantile Regression Neural Network, Prediction Interval Estimation, Evaluation Metrics, Uncertainty Modeling with Kernel Machine and Neural Network | 12 |
| 4 | Statistical Time-series Models | Introduction to Time Series Forecasting, AR, ARMA and ARIMA models | 8 |
| 5 | Time Series Forecasting with Deep Learning | Time Series Forecasting with Recurrent Neural Network (RNN), Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU), Long Short Term Memory (LSTM), Deep Auto Regressive (Deep AR) model, Time GPT model | 8 |
| 6 | Probabilistic Forecasting | Probabilistic Forecasting in Neural Network, RNN, LSTM and Deep AR models | 4 |
| **Total** | | | **40** |
