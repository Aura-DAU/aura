## Course Description

Designing a software or a hardware system is a nontrivial task. The design of the system is formally modelled and then verified to gain confidence. Model checking is one of the predominant techniques for verification of systems. This course aims to introduce students to the mathematical and logical foundations of model checking.

**Course Contents:** Modelling hardware and software systems through transition systems; modelling concurrency and interleaving in systems; linear time properties; safety properties; liveness properties; Buchi automata, omega regular properties; syntax and semantics of LTL (linear temporal logic); syntax and semantics of CTL (computation tree logic); introduction to model checking tool SPIN.
