## Course Description
**Objective:** This course is an introduction to the emerging field of Quantum Computers. Students are expected to know Quantum Mechanics but it is not mandatory. The objective of this course is to introduce the students to a different kind of logical system, namely, the quantum logic, based on the superposition of the states 0 and 1. The phenomenon of entangled states and its use in controlled operations are introduced. With these basic tools from the physics of Quantum Mechanics, the course intends to prepare the students to understand some of the important algorithms in quantum computation, quantum Information and Cryptography.

## Course Outcomes (COs)
The students learn the idea of qubits which implements parallel processing in quantum mechanics. The introduction of gates as Unitary operators acting on qubits helps them look at quantum computers as intrinsically reversible machines. The student learns a completely new algebra of logical system based on subspaces of a vector space as opposed to the boolean algebra based on subsets of a set. Controlled gates provide a concrete application of the complex phenomena of entanglement and measurement in quantum mechanics. The students learn Deutsch's Algorithm, the search algorithm and algorithms based on Quantum Fourier Transform, including Shor's probabilistic algorithm for factoring an integer. The students learn the interesting application of generating a private key between parties using quantum phenomena, rather than transmitting a predetermined key, in Quantum Cryptography. The course prepares the student to do further reading or get into research in the field of Quantum Computation and Quantum Information.

| CO# | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Understand qubits and superposition as the basis of parallel processing in quantum computation. |
| 2 | Understand quantum gates as unitary operators and quantum computers as intrinsically reversible machines. |
| 3 | Understand the logical algebra based on subspaces of a vector space (as opposed to Boolean algebra based on subsets of a set). |
| 4 | Understand entanglement and measurement through controlled gates. |
| 5 | Learn Deutsch's Algorithm, quantum search algorithms, and Quantum Fourier Transform-based algorithms including Shor's algorithm for factoring. |
| 6 | Understand Quantum Cryptography, including private-key generation between parties using quantum phenomena. |
