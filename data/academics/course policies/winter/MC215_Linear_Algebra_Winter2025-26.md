## Course Description
Vectors are an essential idea to understand numerous natural phenomena. Their applications start right from mechanics and electrostatics and magnetostatics. These applications use equations involving vectors. Such ideas are abstracted to study solutions of a system of linear equations in several variables.

## Course Outcomes (COs)
| CO# | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Interpret existence and uniqueness of solutions geometrically. Understand algebraic and geometric representations of vectors in Rⁿ and their operations, including addition, scalar multiplication and dot product. Solve systems of linear equations using Gauss-Jordan elimination to reduce to echelon form. Solve systems of linear equations using the inverse of the coefficient matrix when possible. Understand the nature of the solutions of systems of linear equations. (PO1, PO2, PO12) |
| CO2 | Determine a basis and the dimension of a finite-dimensional space. Discuss spanning sets for vectors in Rⁿ. Discuss linear independence for vectors in Rⁿ. Define the dimension of a vector space. Prove all bases have the same number of elements. Prove elementary theorems concerning rank of a matrix. State and prove the rank-nullity theorem. Demonstrate matrix representation of linear operators and understand the concepts through linear operators and matrices. (PO1, PO2, PO12) |
| CO3 | Find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a matrix. Define eigenvalues and eigenvectors geometrically. Use characteristic polynomials to compute eigenvalues and eigenvectors. Use eigenspaces of matrices, when possible, to diagonalize a matrix. Perform diagonalization of matrices. Explain the significance of eigenvectors and eigenvalues. (PO1, PO2, PO12) |

## Course Structure
| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | System of linear equations: System of linear equations, Elementary row operations, Row reduced echelon form | 3 |
| 2 | Vector spaces: Definition, Example, Basis and dimension, Subspace, Direct sums | 7 |
| 3 | Linear transformations: Definition, Example, Rank-nullity theorem, Algebra of linear transformations, Representation of linear transformations by matrices, Change of bases, Linear functionals and annihilators, Dual spaces | 10 |
| 4 | Eigenvalues and eigenvectors: Definition, the characteristic polynomial, the Cayley-Hamilton Theorem, the minimal polynomial, algebraic and geometric multiplicities, Diagonalization, Jordan canonical form | 10 |
| 5 | Inner product spaces: Definition, orthonormal bases, Gram-Schmidt process; Adjoint of operators, normal, unitary, and self-adjoint operators, spectral theorem for self-adjoint and normal operators | 10 |
