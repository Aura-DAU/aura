## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to optimization methods: Introduction, Formulation of an LPP, Graphical Method to solve an LPP, Nature of a solution | 4 |
| 2 | Linear programming problem: Algebraic method, Simplex method, Revised simplex method, Duality, Dual simplex method, Sensitivity analysis | 10 |
| 3 | Transportation Problem: North-west corner Rule, Row-minimum method, Vogel's Approximation, u-v method | 6 |
| 4 | Integer Programming: NP-hardness, Totally Unimodular Matrix, Heuristic Algorithm - Cutting Plane Method | Not specified |
| 5 | Game Theory: Introduction to Game Theory, Game with pure and mixed strategies, 2×n and m×2 Games, LPP Formulation of m×n Games | 6 |
| 6 | Numerical Solution of Convex Optimization: Gradient Descent, Steepest Descent, Newton's Method, Conjugate Direction and Conjugate Gradient Methods | 8 |
| **Total** | | **34+ (Integer Programming unit lecture count not specified)** |
