## Course Description

**Course Objectives:**
- Understand the fundamentals of sensors, sensing measurands, and sensor characteristics.
- Learn sensor instrumentation and interface electronics for system-level design.
- Explore fabrication, advanced sensors and IoT-enabled applications.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

| CO | Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Identify and analyse appropriate sensors for different measurands and applications. |
| CO2 | Design and implement sensor systems with appropriate interfacing and measurement techniques. |

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Sensors: Sensor measurements, Sensing measurand, Sensor transfer characteristics, Sensor characteristics (Sensitivity, Linearity, Resolution, Hysteresis, Repeatability, Accuracy), Principle working of various sensors technology, Sensor design optimization methods | 10 |
| 2 | Sensor's Instrumentation: Interface electronics for various sensors technology, Understanding of sensor transfer characteristics and Instrumentation design, System design for the sensors | 8 |
| 3 | Sensor Fabrication Technology and Advanced Sensors: Fundamentals of fabrication processes, Contamination and clean room concepts, Wafer cleaning, Oxidation, Photolithography, Mask Design, Wet and Dry Etching, Thin Film Deposition Techniques, Advanced Sensor Concepts (Introductory Level) — Chemical, MEMS, and Flexible sensors | 14 |
| 4 | Intelligent Sensing and Applications: Sensor interfacing (Arduino/ESP32), IoT-enabled sensor systems, Applications — Healthcare, Agriculture & Environmental monitoring, Consumer electronics, Automotive & Defence | 10 |
| **Total** | | **42** |

## Additional Notes

- The course covers both classical sensor theory/instrumentation and modern IoT-enabled intelligent sensing applications (Arduino/ESP32 interfacing).
- The Sensor Fabrication Technology unit shares substantial content overlap with EL469 (VLSI Technology) in terms of fabrication process topics (photolithography, etching, thin film deposition).
