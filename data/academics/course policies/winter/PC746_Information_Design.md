## Course Description
Information Design module focuses on transforming complex data into clear, usable information. By utilizing various techniques such as writing, analysis, and design, information designers aim to create meaningful and accessible content that effectively communicates intended messages. This module explores the process of defining, planning, and shaping information to achieve specific objectives and meet the needs of users. Key areas of focus include understanding the target audience, designing intuitive navigation and hierarchy, and using visual elements to enhance engagement and comprehension.

Through case studies, information collection, data analysis, mapping, visualization, and user research, students will develop the skills necessary to create concise, clear, and visually sophisticated communication materials. Ultimately, this module equips students with the ability to structure and present information in a way that promotes understanding, engagement, and effective use.
