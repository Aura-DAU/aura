## Course Description
It is a core course for the BTech MnC program. Course format: 3 hours lecture every week and 1 hour tutorial.

Course content: Mathematical notations, different types of proofs, regular languages, DFA, NFA, regular expressions, context-free grammar, pushdown automaton, Turing machines and un-decidability.

This subject forms the theoretical foundation of computer science. While the contemporary languages and architectures would become obsolete someday, the foundational matter in computer science would always remain relevant. Studying this subject will make students aware of the capabilities and limitations of computers. It also tells the students about the kind of problems that are realistically tractable.
