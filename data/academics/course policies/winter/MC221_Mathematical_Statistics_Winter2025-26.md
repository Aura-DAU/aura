## Course Description
This course provides a rigorous introduction to Probability, Sampling Distributions, Estimation, Hypothesis Testing, Regression, and Correlation. The emphasis is on both theoretical foundations and practical applications necessary for undergraduate students in statistics and related fields.

## Course Outcomes (COs)

## Course Structure
| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic Probability: Review of Probability; Probability distributions; Moment generating functions | 05 |
| 2 | Sampling distribution: Sample, Population and sampling; Probability and the distribution of sample means; Central limit theorem and the law of large numbers; Methods of sampling; Looking ahead to inferential Statistics | 07 |
| 3 | Statistical Inference: Parameter estimation; Types and Criteria for a good estimator; Point estimator; Interval estimator; Testing of Hypothesis; Parametric tests | 13 |
| 4 | Non-parametric test: Introduction; Chi-square tests; Tests for goodness of fit; Tests for independence | 05 |
| 5 | Regression analysis: Introduction; Significance of the Regression equation; Linear and multiple regression | 05 |
| 6 | Correlation: Introduction; Pearson correlation; Hypothesis tests using Pearson correlation; Pearson correlation alternatives | 05 |
