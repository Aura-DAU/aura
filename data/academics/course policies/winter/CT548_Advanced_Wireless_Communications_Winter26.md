## Course Description

The course primarily starts with the evolution of wireless technologies, followed by wireless channel modeling and representation. Later, important concepts like MIMO and OFDM, which emerged as key technologies for 4G and 5G wireless communications, are discussed. Important aspects of channel estimation for wireless communication systems will be discussed in detail. Subsequently, Massive MIMO and millimeter-wave systems will be discussed for next-generation wireless communication systems. The prime motive of the course is to provide an understanding and background in the domain of wireless communication, which could pave the way forward to academic research in various areas of wireless communication. Certain modules of the course could be helpful and serve as an important prerequisite in relevant companies working in the domain of wireless communication.

**Course Objectives:**
- Lays the foundation towards working on further academic research problems and assignments in the broad domain of wireless communication and aligned research areas.
- Candidates gain important theoretical concepts mandatory for fresh graduates working in relevant wireless communication and associated industries. MATLAB programming learnt during practical sessions helps correlate and better understand the fundamentals of wireless communications.
- Problem Analysis and Design of Systems: Candidates will be able to utilize the concepts learnt in various principles of wireless communications, such as algorithm design, performance evaluation, and coding assignments in industry, which require theoretical understanding of the overall wireless transceiver architecture.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | WSSUS Channel Model, Diversity & Capacity Analysis of Fading Channels: WSSUS Model (Doppler, Temporal Characteristics of Fading Channel); Classification of Fading Channels (Coherence time and Bandwidth); Effect of Mobility and Doppler Spectrum; Introduction to Principles of Diversity; Antenna Diversity and Maximal Ratio Combining; BER Analysis of Multi Antenna Systems; Deep Fade in Multi Antenna System, Diversity Order; Capacity of Fading Channels | 10 |
| 2 | MIMO Systems: Introduction — MIMO System Model; MIMO Receivers and Performance Analysis; Beamforming and Space Time Block Codes; Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) in MIMO Systems; SVD Based MIMO Transmission; Capacity of MIMO Systems | 8 |
| 3 | OFDM Systems: Multicarrier Modulation and Transmission; Introduction to OFDM; Cyclic Prefix and Signal Model; OFDM Transmitter and Receiver Models; Performance Evaluation of OFDM Systems | 6 |
| 4 | Channel Estimation Approaches in Wireless Communication Systems: Maximum Likelihood (ML) and Bayesian Channel Estimation for SISO systems; ML and Bayesian Channel Estimation for MISO systems; ML and Bayesian Channel Estimation for MIMO systems | 8 |
| 5 | Introduction to Massive MIMO and mmWave Technologies: Massive MIMO System Architecture-I; Massive MIMO System Architecture-II; mmWave System Architecture-I; mmWave System Architecture-II; Discussions on Relevant 5G Related 3GPP Technical Specification & usage in Industry | 10 |

## Evaluation / Grading Scheme

**Assessment Method:** Continuous evaluation will be carried out throughout the semester with a Mid-Semester Examination, End-Semester Examination, and some weightage given to assignments. There might be a small change in this assessment method, but in that case it would be duly notified well in advance.

*Note: No specific percentage weightages are stated in the source document.*

**Laboratory Component:** Students will be given weekly problems which need to be simulated in MATLAB. Focus will be on the different components of the system under study. A report containing the results needs to be prepared for evaluation. Students can either use the institute's MATLAB license or a personal license. The idea is to enhance coding expertise and understanding of wireless and digital communication systems and their modeling.
