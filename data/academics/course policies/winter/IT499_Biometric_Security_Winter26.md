## Course Description

Introduction to biometrics: basic processing pipeline, biometric recognition including identification and verification, biometric traits or modalities, specific characteristics of biometric traits, different biometric modes: face, speech, iris, palmprint, finger-vein, palm-vein, fingerprint. Evaluation metrics: impostor scores, genuine scores, FAR, FRR, EER, GAR, DET curve, CMC curve, Receiver operating characteristics. Face recognition: state-of-the-art in face biometrics, deep learning architecture pipeline, hand-crafted features, vascular biometrics: acquisition, vascular biometric traits (palm-vein, finger-vein, dorsal hand-vein, iris). Attacks: presentation attacks on biometric systems, real-world attack instances, different defense measures, adversarial attacks using physical accessories, morphing attacks, multi-modal biometric systems (fusion at different levels: image-level fusion, score-level fusion, decision-level fusion). Cancelable biometrics, secure and privacy-preserving biometrics, face de-identification, cross-domain/heterogeneous recognition, person re-identification.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Biometrics: Biometric traits (face, iris, fingerprint, palm, ear, gait, vascular traits, speech); biometric modes — verification and identification; biometric processing pipeline; evaluation metrics — impostor and genuine scores, equal error rate, DET curve, cumulative match characteristics curve, ROC curve | 4 |
| 2 | Relevant Image Processing Basics | 4 |
| 3 | Face Recognition: Overview and Challenges — Face verification and identification, challenges such as pose, illumination, occlusion; Handcrafted FR features, deep learning-based approaches | 6 |
| 4 | Fingerprint Biometrics: Overview and Challenges — Biometric acquisition, features (ridges, minutiae, pore); seminal fingerprint recognition techniques; research on partial fingerprint matching | 5 |
| 5 | Vascular Biometrics: Overview and Challenges — Acquisition, vascular biometric traits (palm-vein, finger-vein, dorsal hand-vein, iris) | 4 |
| 6 | Multi-modal Biometric Systems: Fusion at different levels — image-level fusion, score-level fusion, decision-level fusion | 2 |
| 7 | Research Presentations I: Presentation attacks (real-world attack instances, different defense measures); adversarial attacks using digital/physical accessories, poisoning attacks, morphing attacks | 7 |
| 8 | Research Presentations II: Cancelable biometrics, secure and privacy-preserving biometrics, face de-identification; cross-domain recognition (heterogeneous); person re-identification | 8 |
