## Course Description

This course introduces students to the robot arm, autonomous car, and ROS (Robot Operating System), as well as numerous available tools — VSCode, Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow, and GitHub. The course focuses on theory and hands-on work, whereas hands-on work will focus on applications and implementations involving motor, camera, GPS, computer vision, and artificial intelligence. Students learn how to create robot models, interface to sensors and actuators, and integrate AI/ML algorithms for autonomous robots. The course works through exercises involving a number of autonomous robots (ground-based) that students could eventually use in their future robotics research and innovation. The course consists of two parts: (i) an interactive lecture section, and (ii) lab exercises on ROS, which will provide a platform to understand a real robot from scratch.

## Course Outcomes (COs)

This course will give students an opportunity to enhance their skills in the basics of a custom robotic arm and autonomous car (PE1, PE2). Students will gain understanding of a 7-degree-of-freedom robotic arm, self-drive car, lane assist, cruise control, local navigation, and global navigation, which are required for future automation and transportation. Students will acquire substantial skills in Ubuntu, ROS, Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow, and GitHub (PSO1, PSO2, PSO3, PE3).

Today, world-leading companies such as Google, TCS, Amazon, Boston Dynamics, Softbank, DENSO, Disney, HONDA, Hyundai, etc., are looking for people with expertise in computer vision, artificial intelligence, and robotics. This program will provide a platform to develop skills for which companies are interested in hiring professionals (PEO3).

After successful completion of the course, students will have the ability to apply engineering knowledge to identify problems using modern tools for social development (PO1, PO2, PO5, PO6). Robotics, which is interdisciplinary in nature and has complexity in development requiring long-term goals, requires learning ethics for use in society (PO4, PO8, PO12, PE4). Students will develop the ability to work in a group project of 3-4 students in a team (PO9, PO11, PE5).

## Program Structure Context

IE416 Robot Programming is an ICT and Technical Elective course for 3rd year BTech ICT and RAS (Robotics and Automation Systems) minor program students, also offered as an elective for M.Tech students, with 3 hours lecture and 2 hours lab every week.

## Additional Notes

- The course places heavy emphasis on ROS (Robot Operating System) and autonomous vehicle programming (lane detection, sign classification, GPS navigation, path/motion planning).
- Group projects are conducted in teams of 3-4 students.
- This course shares the same instructor (Tapas Kumar Maiti) as EL203 (Embedded Hardware Design) from this Winter 2025-26 batch, and IE420/EL434/EL518 (Real Time Embedded Software) from the Autumn 2025-26 batch.
