## Course Description

The course is designed as per Industry requirements, covering details of various processes, techniques, and methods employed in the manufacturing of semiconductors, based on silicon or compound semiconductors like GaN, GaAs, AlGaN, and MEMS devices. The course deals with the manufacturing process cycle for semiconductors. The focus is on understanding the Global semiconductor Industry and its impact on society. The details of various microfabrication techniques are covered from substrate preparation to wafer fabrication.

Topics include: substrate material properties, Silicon as a material, Wafer Growth techniques like CZ, FZ, etc., Clean room concepts, wafer cleaning techniques. Various fabrication techniques covered include: Diffusion process and kinetics of diffusion, CVD and PVD, Deposition techniques and Furnace used, Ion Implantation techniques and equipment, Dry and wet etching, etch profile, etc., wafer bonding.

Additionally, the course covers wafer sort, device packaging, different types of packages, bonding diagrams, device testing, QA, failure mode, and device reliability in brief.

## Course Outcomes (COs)

**Outcomes and Objectives:**
1. Spark an interest in Semiconductors/VLSI.
2. Understand the complete manufacturing cycle of Semiconductors.
3. Understand Product development cycle — specifications to qualified product.
4. Learn techniques of Microfabrication.
5. Learn methods of Silicon Ingot making.
6. Understand mechanics of Oxidation and models representing Oxidation.
7. Learn concepts of Lithography and Mask.
8. Learn thin film Deposition techniques.
9. Learn device package types and process of packaging.

At the end of the course, students will be able to understand the various techniques of Microfabrication used in manufacturing of Integrated circuits and MEMS devices.
