## Course Description
Interaction Design can be understood as the design of interactions between users and their artefacts. While it is often thought of in terms of software products, interaction design is a broad field that includes elements such as motion, space, sound, image and more.

Thinking past the boundaries of mobile or desktop interactions, this course encourages students to look at the physical world and the possibilities of creating new immersive experiences and environments.

Introducing technologies such as gesture and voice controls, hologram projections, 360 video, augmented, virtual and mixed realities, the course will challenge students to understand how designing for interactive spaces diverges from small-scale interactions of mobile or web design, to encompass constraints of scale, medium, environment, and whole body utilization.

The course has been structured as a mix of lectures, short exercises and a semester project that will enable students to engage with design issues involving interaction through practical application.

In the semester project, students will undertake the creation of an interactive space. They will work individually and at times in groups, to investigate this area in the context of interaction design. Each student will by the end of the semester be expected to frame his/her individual proposal and develop an innovative design prototype.
