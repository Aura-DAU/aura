## Course Description

This course provides a rigorous introduction to resampling techniques and Bayesian computation. The resampling module covers jackknife, bootstrap, and variance estimation. The Bayesian module covers priors, posteriors, conjugacy, credible intervals, and MCMC methods including Metropolis-Hastings and Gibbs sampling.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | Content | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic Probability | Quick refresh of probability basics. Joint and conditional probabilities, common distributions, expectation and variance. Conditional expectation, law of total expectation, simple examples to warm up | 4 |
| 2 | Resampling Techniques | Brief introduction to resampling, examples of calculating variance, sampling distributions. Different resampling methods: Jackknife, Bootstrap, Half sampling, etc. | 4 |
| 3 | Bootstrap and Jackknife for i.i.d. cases | Bootstrap in parametric and non-parametric setups. Consistency properties, comparison of bootstrap with normal approximation. Jackknife for iid case, consistency, comparison with non-parametric bootstrap | 8 |
| 4 | Bootstrapping on linear models | Residual Bootstrap, weighted bootstrap, discussion about robust and efficient bootstrap techniques | 4 |
| 5 | Brief introduction to Bayesian Inference | Prior and posterior distributions, Conjugacy, Non-informative priors, Posterior mean, variance, credible intervals and high-posterior density intervals, comparison between Bayesian and frequentist techniques | 8 |
| 6 | Bayesian Bootstrap and MCMC | Comparison of Bayesian credible interval with bootstrap confidence intervals. Bayesian Bootstrap, Introduction to MCMC, Metropolis-Hastings and Gibbs sampling | 8 |
| 7 | Applications | Case studies linking theory with practice. Examples from physics, biology, and statistics. Discussions and problem-solving sessions | 4 |
| **Total** | | | **40** |
