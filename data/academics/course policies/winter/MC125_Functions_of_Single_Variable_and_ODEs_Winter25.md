## Course Description
Functions of Single Variable and ODEs is a core course for first year students of the B.Tech MnC program. It is 3 hours' lecture and 1-hour tutorial every week.

This course aims at building an advanced understanding of calculus in single variable and ordinary differential equations. After successful completion of the course the student will have the ability to formulate mathematical models of real-world situations by applying abstract concepts to complex problems and solving the model either analytically or by adopting numerical methods.

## Course Structure
| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real Number Systems: Properties of Real Numbers; Sequence and Series; Limit, Continuity and Differentiability of Functions of Single Variable | 8 |
| 2 | Differential Calculus: Implicit Differentiation; Mean Value Theorems and Applications; Linear Approximations; Power Series and Taylor Series; Maxima and Minima, Orthogonal Trajectories | 7 |
| 3 | Integral Calculus: Definite Integrals and Their Properties; Riemann Integral; Improper Integrals; Application of Integration | 7 |
| 4 | Ordinary Differential Equations: Order and Degree of ODEs; Picard's Theorem for Initial Value Problems (IVPs); Methods for Solving First Order Differential Equations; Euler's Methods for IVPs; Higher Order Linear ODEs with constant coefficients; Power Series Solutions for 2nd Order ODEs; Methods of Variation of Parameter; Methods of Undetermined Coefficients | 12 |
| 5 | Important Results and Laplace Transform: Sturm Comparison Theorem; Sturm–Liouville Boundary Value Problem; Fourier Series and Integrals; Laplace Transform: Existence and its applications | 8 |
