## Course Description

**Course Content areas:**
1. Entrepreneurship Fundamentals
2. Business Management Fundamentals
3. Product Design

The course covers the essential elements of entrepreneurship in a manner that sensitizes students to the prospects, opportunities, and challenges in entrepreneurship, and the potential for value creation from prospective ideas. In addition, there will be multiple modules on different functions of a company — Finance, HR, IT, and Marketing — delivered by industry experts. There will be an extended module focusing on 'Product Design,' encompassing aspects of design thinking and affordable innovation.

In addition to classroom teaching of essential concepts and theories, there will be a project-based learning approach, including practical exposure to industry and incubation centres. Cases will be used to narrate the actual journeys of entrepreneurs and their enterprises (both successes and failures), covering a mix of start-ups and corporates.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

Students will become aware of the importance of "Entrepreneurial Capital" as a vital contributor to the growth of a nation's economy and well-being. Given that they are essentially being trained as "Engineers," the course will enable them to appreciate how to make/leverage technology- and process-driven innovations for solving problems, and how to convert their ideas into marketable solutions.

In addition, topics ranging from Vision and Purpose to the creation and leading of larger established corporates will be discussed, so students become knowledgeable about the industry ecosystem too.

The emphasis in this course is on project-based learning, leading to identification of problems worthy of solving and proposing techno-economically viable ideas that could potentially solve them.

By the end of this course, students are likely to be inspired to explore entrepreneurship as a viable career goal.
