## Course Description

The objective of this course is to introduce basic to advanced levels of techniques for processing images. The techniques include various spatial domain and transformed domain techniques to enhance, restore, and segment images for higher-level processing.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)

After completion of this course, students should be able to:

| CO | Description |

## Course Structure

| Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basics of Digital Image and Processing Steps: Concepts of Signal, Image, History, Applications; Fundamental processing steps, Human visual system, Formation model, Sampling, Quantization; Pixel Relationship, Operations on Images (pixels, spatial), Affine transformations; Image Registration, Image Interpolation | 9 |
| 2 | Intensity Transformations: Contrast, Image negative, Log transform, Gamma Transform, Contrast Stretching, Intensity level slicing, Bit-plane slicing; Histogram, Histogram Equalization, Histogram Specification, Local Histogram Processing; Advanced Application | 3 |
| 3 | Spatial Filtering: Concept of filtering, Mechanics of spatial filtering, spatial correlation and convolution; Filtering for image smoothing and sharpening; Convolution by matrix vector multiplications; Advanced spatial domain filter | 5 |
| 4 | Frequency Domain Filtering: Fourier Series, Impulses and their sifting properties; Fourier transform of one continuous variable, Fourier transform of convolution, Fourier transform of sampled functions, Sampling theorem; Signal Recovery, Aliasing, DFT, 2D DFT; Fourier magnitude spectrum and phase, 2D convolution theorem, Fundamental steps of filtering in frequency domain; LPF with Ideal, Butterworth, Gaussian; HPF with Ideal, Butterworth, Gaussian, Laplacian, Homomorphic filtering, FFT | 7 |
| 5 | Image Restoration: Degradation model, Noise models, Estimation of noise parameters; Noise-only restoration by mean filters, order statistic filters, frequency domain filtering; Estimation of degradation function, and restoration; Advanced topics | 6 |
| 6 | Image Segmentation: Point, line and edge detection; Edge linking; Thresholding | 6 |
| 7 | Representation and Description: Representation; Boundary Descriptors, Regional Descriptors; PCA, SIFT | 3 |
| 8 | Object Recognition: Bayes Classifier, NN Classifier, CNN Classifier | 3 |
| **Total** | | **42** |

## Program Structure Context

IT507 Advanced Image Processing is an elective course open to UG and PG students, with a 3-0-2-4 credit structure (3 hours lecture and 2 hours lab every week).

---

## Additional Notes

- The course covers a complete image processing pipeline: spatial and frequency domain filtering, image restoration, segmentation, representation/description (including SIFT), and object recognition (Bayes/NN/CNN classifiers).
- Mini-projects (15% of grade) supplement lab assignments (also 15%) as hands-on components.
