## Course Description
**Course Objective:**
- Students will integrate knowledge from multiple disciplines representing physical and life sciences perspectives, political and economic perspectives, social and cultural perspectives on humans' interactions with environments.
- Students will contribute to and facilitate interdisciplinary research and problem solving, through independent and collaborative work.

**Course Methodology:** Lectures; Case Studies; Article Reviews and Presentations; Project/Poster presentation; Movies/Videos.

## Course Outcomes (COs)
After completing Environmental Studies, students will be able to:

| CO# | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Articulate the interconnected and interdisciplinary nature of environmental studies. |
| 2 | Demonstrate an integrative approach to environmental issues with a focus on sustainability. |
| 3 | Understand and evaluate the global scale of environmental problems. |
| 4 | Reflect critically on their roles, responsibilities, and identities as citizens, consumers and environmental actors in a complex, interconnected world. |
