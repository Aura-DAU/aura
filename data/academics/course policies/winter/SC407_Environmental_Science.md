## Course Description
How do nature and environment figure in both our ordinary and scientific discourses? The term environment mostly refers to the things which remain outside the human and social sphere, often hiding the complex relations of dependence and contiguity that define the human-environment nexus. Ecology is the field which investigates this nexus. In this course, we will study the main themes and ideas of the ecological debate, which intersects with hard sciences, technology and social sciences at the same time. This debate is roughly 150 years old and has contributions from across the board, from zoologists and geologists to poets and activists.

This course is divided into four parts:
- Lectures
- Thought Experiments and Case Studies
- Films/Documentaries
- Research Report along with the viva
