## Main Content

MC216: Probability and Random Processes – Autumn 2024

The course is placed in the Third Semester for the B.Tech. MnC program, and is a core course.

Course Format
For the MC216: Probability and Random Processes course, we have (L-T-P-C) = (3-1- 0-4), meaning that it is a four-credit
course having three lectures, and one tutorial weekly.

Course Overview
In the third semester of the B.Tech. (MnC) programme, students are introduced the fundamentals of probability, random
variables, and random processes through this course. These concepts are very essential to the students’ professional lives
in the next several decades. A familiarity with these ideas is required in every branch of science and engineering, as well as
management, economics, business, finance, research, and development, among other fields. For students to gain
experience in problem solving, they may be required to write simple codes in a high-level programming language such as
MATLAB, Python to solve real-world problems/situations as a part of assignments in the course.

A list of important topics from the course is as follows:
1. Probability: definition and axioms
2. Conditional probability and Bayes’ theorem
3. Random variables
4. Discrete random variables, Bernoulli, binomial, and Poisson distributions
5. Continuous random variables, exponential and Gaussian distributions
6. Functions of random variables
7. Mean, variance, moments of random variables
8. Moment generating functions and characteristic functions
9. Joint distributions, covariance and correlation, independent random variables
10. Bi-variate Gaussian distribution
11. Law of large numbers, central limit theorem
12. Random processes and their classification
13. Markov chains, simple random walk
14. Poisson process, Wiener process, white noise, power spectral density
15. Queuing processes
Course Contents⋆
“Random experiment, sample space, axioms of probability, probability space, conditional probability, independence of events,
multiplication rule, total probability rule, Bayes’ theorem, random variable, cumulative distribution function, types of random
variables, probability mass function, probability density function, distribution of function of random variables, mean and
variance, higher order moments and inequalities, generating functions, common discrete distributions, applications of random
variables, random vector and joint distribution, joint probability mass function, joint probability density function, independent
random variables, functions of several random variables, order statistics, conditional distributions, random sum, moments
and co variance, variance- covariance matrix, multivariate normal distribution, probability generating function and moment
generating function, correlation coefficient, conditional expectation, modes of convergence, law of large numbers, central limit
theorem, stochastic processes, classification of stochastic processes, Bernouli process, Poisson process, simple random walk,
time series, strict sense stationary process, wide sense stationary process, discrete time Markov chain, Chapman Kolmogrov
equations and N-step transition matrix, classification of states, limiting and stationary distributions, continuous time Markov
chain, state transition diagram and Chapman Kolmogrov equation, infinitesimal generator and Kolmogrov differential
equations, limiting and stationary distributions, birth-death process, Poisson process, non-homogeneous and compound
Poisson process, introduction to queueing models and Kendall notation, M/M/I queueing model, M/M/c queueing model,
M/M/I/N model, other Markovian queueing models, transient solution of finite capacity Markovian queues.”
