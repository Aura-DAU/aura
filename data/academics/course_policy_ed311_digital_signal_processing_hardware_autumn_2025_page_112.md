## Course Description

Digital Signal Processing Hardware Digital system design and of digital signal processing in the context of modern systems. The course is intended to synthesise both sides (theory and experiment) of the module content, so as to broaden and deepen understanding. Syllabus  Introduction to DSP and DSP Hardware  Introduction to Stochastic Signals, Sampling and Quantisation  ADC, DAC Architectures  Registers, ALU, SRAM, IO ports, Instruction Decoder, Synthesis of a simplified MIPS microprocessor on FPGA  Testing combinational and sequential digital systems, Boundary Scan, Build-in self-test 
