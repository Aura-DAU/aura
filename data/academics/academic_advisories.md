# Academic Advisories

## Overview

Academic advisories and circulars are official communications issued by the **Dean's Office (Academic Programs)** and the **Academic Council** of Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT).

These advisories may include:
- Announcements about academic policy changes
- Circulars on examination procedures
- Dean's office notices affecting registered students and faculty
- Academic Council resolutions and guidelines

> Advisories and circulars are published on the Intranet portal. Students and faculty should check both the DAU Intranet and their institutional email regularly for new advisories.
