# Examination Guidelines

## Overview

The Examination Department at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT) manages all aspects of in-semester and end-semester examinations, including seating arrangements, malpractice guidelines, and special accommodations.

| Resource | Description |

| Examination Department Page | Official department page on DAU website |

| Guidelines for Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PDF) | Special accommodation procedures and eligibility |
| Malpractice Guidelines (PDF) | Rules and consequences related to academic dishonesty |
| Exam Seat Arrangement Portal | Check seat allotments for upcoming examinations |

## Additional Notes

- Students with benchmark disabilities must submit relevant documentation in advance to the CoE office for accommodation arrangements.
- Malpractice cases are handled as per the official Malpractice Guidelines. Students found in violation face penalties as specified therein.
- Seat arrangements are published on the Exam Seat Arrangement Portal before each examination.
