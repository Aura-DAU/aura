# Course Catalog

This document provides a comprehensive list of courses offered, including credits, prerequisites, and brief descriptions.

## 1. Core Engineering and Technology Courses
- **Introduction to Programming (IT112/IT603)**: Foundational programming concepts.
- **Data Structures and Algorithms (IT205/IT623)**: Fundamental data organization and algorithmic efficiency.
- **Computer Organization and Programming**: Hardware-software interface and assembly programming.
- **Design and Analysis of Algorithms (IT216)**: Advanced algorithm design, dynamic programming, and greedy methods.
- **Operating Systems**: OS architectures, concurrency, and memory management.
- **Database Management Systems**: Relational models, SQL, and transaction management.
- **Software Engineering**: Software development lifecycles, Agile, and design patterns.
- **Computer Networks**: OSI/TCP-IP models, routing, and network protocols.
- **Machine Learning**: Foundational ML techniques, classification, regression, and neural networks.

## 2. Core Mathematics and Sciences Courses
- **Calculus / Functions of Single Variable and ODEs**: Limits, continuity, and differential equations.
- **Discrete Mathematics**: Logic, set theory, combinatorics, and graph theory.
- **Linear Algebra / Groups and Linear Algebra**: Vector spaces, matrices, and eigenvalues.
- **Probability and Random Processes**: Probability theory, random variables, and stochastic processes.
- **Numerical and Computational Methods**: Numerical integration, differentiation, and root finding.
- **Introductory Computational Physics**: Physics principles using computational techniques.
- **High Performance Computing**: Parallel processing architectures and algorithms.
- **Modelling and Simulation**: Simulating complex systems using computational models.

## 3. Humanities, Social Sciences, and Management Courses
- **Language and Literature**: Analytical reading and academic writing.
- **Approaches to Indian Society**: Sociological perspectives on Indian culture and society.
- **Science, Technology, Society**: The intersection of technological advancement and societal impact.
- **Principles of Economics**: Micro and macroeconomic principles.
- **Environmental Studies**: Ecological concepts and environmental conservation.

## 4. Electronics and Communications Courses
- **Basic Electronic Circuits**: Fundamental circuit analysis and components.
- **Digital Logic Design**: Boolean algebra, combinational, and sequential logic.
- **Signals and Systems**: Continuous and discrete-time signal analysis.
- **Introduction to Communication Systems**: Analog and digital modulation techniques.
- **Embedded Hardware Design**: Microcontrollers, interfacing, and embedded systems programming.

## 5. Electives and Projects
- **ICT / Technical Electives**: Advanced topics such as Computer Vision, Information Retrieval, Blockchain, and Nanoelectronics.
- **Science Electives**: Advanced topics like Nonlinear Science, Optimization, and Computational Biology.
- **B.Tech Project (BTP)**: A capstone research or engineering project spanning 1 or 2 semesters.
- **Independent Projects (IP)**: Faculty-supervised individual research projects.
