## Course Description

At the end of the course, students are expected (i) To obtain basic understanding of most known modal logic s and their applications (ii) To know the nature of tableaux method for decidability (iii) To understand different properties of models in modal logic (iv) To learn various systems of modal logic and how to apply them to solve problems. (v) To write a scientific report about an applica tion of modal logic in AI, distributed systems

| 2 | Intuitionistic logic, Kripke frames and models, Truth preserving operations, Hintikka systems, Intitutionistic frames and formulas,, Intitutionistic calculus, Basic properties of intuitionistic logic, | 10 |
| 3 | Modal logic, Possible world semantics, Modal frames and models, Truth preserving operations, Hintikka systems, Modal frames and formulas, Calculus K, Basic properties of K, A few more modal logics, From logics to classes of logics, | 12 |
