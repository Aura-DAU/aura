## Course Description

The activities will be centered around reading, speaking, listening, writing and critical thinking. After completion of the course, students will be able to better comprehend and analyze written and audio-visual texts and to respond confidently in coherent English. Rigorous group activities conducted in the class will ensure that they develop good interpersonal and public communication skills. Texts: The instructor will circulate readings that students are supposed to get photocopied. Students will be expected to bring these to the classroom.
