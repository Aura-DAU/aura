# IT443 ResamplingTechniquesandBayesianComputation Winter26

## Main Content

IT443 Resampling Techniques and Bayesian Computation (Winter ’2026)

Instructor: Subhasish Basak, 3206, Faculty Block 3

Course Placement: B.Tech. (ICT), Semester-VI

Course Format: 3 Lecture hours + 2 Lab hours (3-0-2)

Course Content: This course provides a rigorous introduction to resampling techniques
and Bayesian computation. The resampling module covers jackknife, bootstrap, and
variance estimation. The Bayesian module covers priors, posteriors, conjugacy, credible
intervals, and MCMC methods including Metropolis–Hastings and Gibbs sampling.

Textbooks:

1. An Introduction to the Bootstrap — Efron & Tibshirani

2. Computer Age Statistical Inference — Efron & Hastie

3. Bayesian Data Analysis — Gelman et al.

4. Monte Carlo Statistical Methods — Robert & Casella

Evaluation Plan:

1. Two Assignments – 20%

2. In-Semester Exam I – 20%

3. In-Semester Exam II – 20%

4. End-Semester Examination – 40%

Tentative Lecture Distribution:


| Topic Name | Content (2 -3 lines per 4 – 6 lectures) | No. of lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Probability | Quick refresh of probability basics. Joint and conditional probabilities, common distributions, expectation and variance. Conditional expectation, law of total expectation, simple examples to warm up. | 4 |
| Resampling techniques | Brief introduction to resampling, Examples of calculating variance, sampling distributions. Different resampling methods: Jackknife, Bootstrap, Half sampling etc. | 4 |
| Bootstrap and Jackknife for i.i.d cases | Bootstrap in parametric and non-parametric setups. Consistency properties, comparison of bootstrap with normal approximation. Jackknife for iid case, consistency, comparison with non- parametric boot strap | 8 |
| Bootstrapping on linear models | Residual Bootstrap, weighted bootstrap, discussion about robust and efficient bootstrap techniques. | 4 |
| Brief introduction to Bayesian Inference | Prior and posterior distributions, Conjugacy, Non- informative priors, Posterior mean, variance, credible intervals and high-posterior density intervals, comparison between Bayesian and frequentist techniques | 8 |
