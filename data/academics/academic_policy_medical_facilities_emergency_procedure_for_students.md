Procedure for Medical Emergency 
 
An Institute car along with a driver is always on standby duty including weekends/ 
holidays and beyond office hours on all weekdays to take care of emergencies on campus.

Security Supervisors are authorized to provide car, in acute emergencies, without prior 
approval of the authorities.

However, Security Supervisor will immediately inform the concerned on phone about the 
incident and the help provided, who in turn may advise further action, if needed.

In case the patient is to be taken to a panel doctor, availability of the doctor should be 
ascertained on telephone before starting. Pre-checking may not be required in case of 
hospitals.
