## Course Description
This course introduces the characteristics and applications of semiconductor devices and circuits. Emphasis is placed on analysis, selection, biasing, and applications. Upon completion, students should be able to construct, analyse, verify, and troubleshoot analog circuits using appropriate techniques and test equipment.

## Course Structure

| ED222 Unit | Topic | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | **Introduction to Semiconductor Physics**: N-type and P-type materials, electron and hole currents, PN junction and biasing | 1 |
| Unit 2 | **Diodes**: Operation, Diode models, Rectifier circuits, Clipper and clamper circuits, voltage multiplier ckt, Zener diodes and LED | 5 |
| Unit 3 | **Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs)**: Transistor operation, Biasing & bias stability, Small-signal analysis, AC Equivalent circuit, Capacitive coupling, Single-stage CE amplifiers, Emitter degeneration, CB & CC amplifiers, Multi-stage amplifiers, High-frequency transistor model, Open-circuit time constant method, Bode plot, Basic feedback concepts, Stability analysis, Unity-gain frequency & phase-margin, Compensation | 29 |
| Unit 4 | **Feedback amplifiers**: Designing of negative feedback amplifiers, voltage series, voltage shunt, current series and current shunt | 4 |
| Unit 5 | **Differential Amplifiers**: Differential Amplifiers | 2 |
| Unit 6 | **Mutivibrators**: Astable, Monostable and Bistable Multivibrators | 1 |
