# M.Sc. Agriculture Analytics – Year of Current Curriculum / Structure Adoption

## Key Fact

The M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics) joint program adopted its **current curriculum and program structure effective from the Autumn semester of 2022-23**.

## Program Structure Summary (Adopted 2022-23)

The current structure adopted in 2022-23 consists of:
- **Semester 1** – DA-IICT, Gandhinagar (Python, Statistics, EO Systems, Mathematics/Agri Science)
- **Semester 2** – IIRS, Dehradun (Geodata Processing, Big Data Analytics, Machine Learning, Spatial Modeling)
- **Summer Semester** – Value Added Courses (6 credits, industry-delivered at DA-IICT)
- **Semester 3** – AAU, Anand (Crop & Soil Analytics, Weather & Water Analytics, Agricultural Market Analytics, Risk Analysis)
- **Semester 4** – Industry Internship / Faculty Research Project (16 credits)

## Summary Answer

**The M.Sc. Agriculture Analytics joint program adopted its current curriculum and structure in 2022, with the program effective from the Autumn 2022-23 academic year.**
