# Course Registration Procedure

## Overview

Course registration at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT) is conducted through the **ERP/eCampus portal** within the designated registration window. The Dean (Academic Programs) is responsible for organising semester-wise academic offerings and designating instructors for all courses, projects, and internships.

## Key Portals

| Portal | Purpose | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Moodle | Course portal — syllabus, lecture notes, resources | [Moodle — DAU](https://moodle.daiict.ac.in) |
| eCampus / ERP Portal | Course registration and academic management | [eCampus — DAU](https://ecampus.daiict.ac.in) |

## Process Summary

1. Log in to the eCampus / ERP portal during the open registration window (refer to the Academic Calendar).
2. Select courses for the upcoming semester through the portal.
3. Verify your registration on the Academic page of the ERP portal.
4. Ensure all required and elective courses are selected as per program requirements.

## Important Notes

- The registration window opens separately for each semester (Autumn, Winter, Summer). Dates are published on the Academic Calendar.
- Fresh UG students and Fresh PG students have separate registration dates — check the Academic Calendar.
- Courses dropped after the Add/Drop window require special permission from the Dean (AP).
