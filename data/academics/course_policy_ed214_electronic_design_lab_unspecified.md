## Course Description
This module equips the students to learn different CAD, EDA tools & HDL for hardware and be conversant with the measuring instruments.

---

## Course Outcomes (COs)
After successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

| CO | ED214 Description |
|---|---|
| CO1 | Use function generators, oscilloscopes and complex devices such as logic analysers, spectrum analysers, understand data sheets, debug HW and SW systems, design simple digital ICs and perform synthesis of simple electronic circuits and systems. |

## Program Structure Context
The Electronic Design Lab provides essential hands-on experience with hardware design tools and measuring instruments, serving as a core practical foundation for students in hardware engineering.

---

## Additional Notes
**Experiments Included:**
1. Build a part of an 8-bit Microprocessor using Discrete components and verify them.
2. Verify Various Analog Circuits using LM741.
