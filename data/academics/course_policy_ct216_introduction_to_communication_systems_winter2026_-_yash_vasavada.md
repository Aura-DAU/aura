# CT216 Introduction to Communication Systems Winter2026

## Main Content

CT216: Introduction to Communication Systems
Course Placement a core course for the second year BTech ICT and ICT+CS students
Course Format three Lecture hours and two Lab hours per week
Course Content The course CT216 introduces the students to the various engineering tech-
niques that empower the modern communication systems. The course demonstrates ap-
plication of elegant mathematical principles to practical engineering design of the com-
munication systems. The students gain hands-on skills on these topics by performing
software laboratory experiments throughout the semester and by completing one project
in the semester. The specific topics covered are information theoretic limits and power
versus bandwidth trade-off in a communication system, introduction to signals and sys-
tems, digital and analog modulation schemes, design of receivers and demodulators,
channel coding schemes, seven layer OSI model, etc.
Textbook U.Madhow,IntroductiontoCommunicationSystems,CambridgeUniversityPress,
2018
Reference Books The following books are used as the reference:
1. L. Couch, Digital and Analog Communication Systems, Pearson, 2012
2. D. J C MacKay, Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms, Cam-
bridge University Press, 2003
3. JohnG.ProakisandMasoudSalehi,DigitalCommunications,FifthEdition,McGraw-
Hill, 2014
4. Stephen Wicker, Error Control Systems for Digital Communication and Storage,
Prentice-Hall, 1994
