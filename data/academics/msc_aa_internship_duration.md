# M.Sc. Agriculture Analytics – Semester 4 Internship Duration

## Key Fact

The **Semester 4** of the M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics) program is dedicated entirely to an **Industry Internship (or Research Project with a faculty supervisor)**. This semester constitutes **one full semester of approximately 16 weeks**, equivalent to roughly **4 to 6 months** in practice.

The internship is registered as course **PC650 – Project Internship (16 credits)** with a credit formula of 0-0-32-16 (no lectures or tutorials; 32 hours per week of project/lab work; 16 credits total).

## Internship Details

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Semester | Semester 4 (final semester) |
| Duration | One full semester ≈ **4–6 months** (aligned with DA-IICT academic calendar, typically January–May or July–November) |
| Credit Load | 16 credits (PC650 – Project Internship) |
| Format | Industry internship OR faculty-supervised research project |
| Location | Industry / research organisation or DA-IICT |

## Program Context

The M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics) is a two-year (four-semester) joint program offered by DA-IICT, Anand Agriculture University (AAU), and the Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS, Dehradun):

- **Semester 1** – Coursework at DA-IICT
- **Semester 2** – Coursework at IIRS, Dehradun
- **Semester 3** – Coursework at AAU
- **Summer Semester (between Sem 2 and 3)** – Value Added Courses (6 credits, industry-delivered)
- **Semester 4** – Project Internship at industry or with faculty (**16 credits, ≈ 4–6 months**)

## Source Reference

Academic Requirements for the M.Sc. (AA) Program (effective Autumn 2022-23), Section 2.1:

> "…the fourth semester will be internship (industry) or research (with faculty) project."

The program structure document confirms:
> "Semester-IV (Total Credits: 16): Projects / Industrial Internship"

## Summary Answer

**The M.Sc. Agriculture Analytics internship (Semester 4) lasts one full academic semester, which is approximately 4 to 6 months (16 weeks) in duration.**
