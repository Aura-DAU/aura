## Detailed Course Content

Relevant portions from Indian and western philosophy will be covered to explain democracy, secularism, socialism and role of religion in the Indian society. The course will comprise of topics related to making of the post -independence India such as general elections, five year planning, ethnic a nd religious conflicts along with other social and national issues. Important issues such as Communalism and Left Wing Extremism will be included which are related to governance and security problems facing India. Course will further include structure and functioning of Central and State governments, Panchayat system and local self -government. There will be exposure to governance at grass root and district level. Course will contain exposure to State police organization, its functioning and Central Police Organizations (CPO s). It will have introduction to National Security Structure and its components. Serious social problems like Communalism and Corruption will be covered which are related to day to day governance. There will be brief exposure to Co astal Security. Course Objectives:

1. To understand the evolution of India as a nation with reference to Democracy, Socialism, Equality and Secularism mentioned in Indian Constitution. To give social, philosophical and cultural context in which Indian political system has evolved.

2. To help students to understand the functioning of union, state and local self-government and the associated issues.

3. To provide understanding of National Security Structure, response mechanism and working of security agencies.

4. To give insight into some contemporary social and security problems such as communalism, naxalism and corruption Course Structure: Classes will be structured in the form of lectures along with seminar format. There will be continuous and in -depth discussion between teacher and students during the class sessions. Presentation by the students will be encouraged to make them sharpen their articulation skills. Experience sharing by the instructor and students will be integral part of the course structure. The students will be motivated to understand and critically analyse multi -dimensional issues relating to Indian state and equip themselves with a liberal, broad -based understanding of Indian society and polity. This will not only help them to gain knowledge but make them more integral to the society which is continuous evolving and transforming itself through volati le democracy and technology. Lecture Themes: The lectures have been designed to give an insight into the characteristics of Indian society, it’s historical, cultural and philosophical evolution into a modern, secular and democr atic nation. India holds unique position in respect of its diversity and composite culture which has deeply impacted on our freedom struggle, making of our constitution and post

- independence governance. The lecture’s theme will incorporate the socio

- politi cal dynamics and government apparatus responsible for realizing the principles enshrined in our constitution. Theme will include the dynamics behind growing communalism facing the nation and ideological resistance movement such as left long extremism in ce rtain parts of the country. Some national security issues will be included in the theme. Evaluation : Evaluation will be continuous , based on presentations, panel discussions, class tests and assignment. It is proposed to have module -wise evalua tion of 4 modules (30%). Term paper writing based on in -depth study/discussions and data collection/survey will be 30%. End term will be of 40% weightage which will have written component. Learning Outcomes : After completion of the course the student will be able to 1 Get a broad -based understanding of Indian society and enable students to relate their theoretical knowledge with social realities on the ground.

2. Understand the evolution of India into modern, secular and democratic republic.

3. Understand the functioning of the government.

4. Have understanding of National Security structure to deal with the security problems being faced by India.

5. Initiate research work relating to governance issues Reading:
