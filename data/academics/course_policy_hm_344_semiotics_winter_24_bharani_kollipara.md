HM 344: SEMIOTICS

Semiotics is the study of signs , codes  and symbol systems. These systems enable human beings to
perceive certain events and entities as signs, bearing meaning. Signs are an essential part of human
culture, though constrained by biological and physical factors as well. The two great founding
fathers of semiotics ar e Ferdinand de Saussure and Charles Sanders Peirce. We’ll try to study how
the ideas of these two figures have shaped the destiny of this discipline.

Semiotics situates itself on the border between the humanities and social sciences. Hence, it has
interf ace with several lines of inquiry like anthropology, literature, linguistics etc. This course
assumes some basic knowledge of the social sciences that you have  already acquired studying AIS and
STS and a willingness to learn new ideas and apply them to the  politics, culture and society of the
contemporary  sphere.

A good deal of emphasis will on practical demonstrations and applications of what you have learnt.

You can expect q uizzes , and  class performances , projects  and presentations . Students will be
exp ected to write a research paper and face a viva around the course and their research work
towards the end .

The course will prepare you to acquire and utilize critical thinking skills.

Topics:

1) Semiotics : An Introduction (Saussure  & Pierce) 2) Myth, Totemism (Levi Strauss) 3) Semiotics
of sign and symptom  (body and body politic) 4) Narrative 5) Ritual and symbols 6) Semiotics of
popular culture  (fashion &theatre) . 7) Semiotics & pictorial representation 8) Architecture and
the city

The course will hover around the ideas of Roland Barthes,  Julia Kristeva  Umberto Eco, Ernst
Gombrich , Victor Turner, Michel Foucault , Mikhail Bakhtin, Jacques Lacan, Gregory Bateson, and
Roman  Jakobson.
