## Course Structure

| Sl. No. | Description | No. of Lectures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Calculus Limit, continuity, differentiation, application of derivatives, integration, definite integrals, method of substitution, integration by parts, application to finding area and volume, double and triple integrals, improper integrals. | 10 |
| 2 | Linear Algebra System of linear equations, matrix, matrix addition, subtraction, multiplication, determinants, rank of a matrix, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and applications. | 6 |
| 3 | Vectors: Vector algebra, products of vectors, vector triple product, vector equations, differentiation and integration of vectors, vector calculus. | 7 |
| 4 | Differential Equations: Formation and solution of ODE, solution of first order ODEs, Linear inhomogeneous second order ODEs, free and forced oscillators and applications. Formation and solution of PDEs, Second order linear PDE, Diffusion equation and applications. | 8 |
| 5 | Complex Numbers: Complex numbers and its properties. polar representation, Euler’s formula, exponential form. | 6 |
| 6 | Numerical methods: Newton’s method for finding roots, numerical integration methods, finite element method. | 4 |
