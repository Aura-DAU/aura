## Detailed Course Content


- This course introduces the speculative fiction supergenre by focusing on its five representative subgenres: fantasy, science fiction, horror, dystopian and post-apocalyptic fiction. The objective is to examine the supergenre’s historical, cultural, and philosophical underpinnings while exploring the themes of wonder, dread, technological hubris, tyranny and human resilience. By the end of this course, learners will be able to recognize and appreciate works of speculative fiction while engaging in critical discourse and analysis of representative texts and films. Note: Films only constitute 25% of the course content, the remaining 75% includes intensive reading of short stories, novels, essays and research papers. Course Objectives:

- To recognize and appreciate works of horror, science fiction, fantasy, dystopian fiction and post-apocalyptic fiction.

- To position and understand works of speculative fiction within a larger cultural, historical, and socio-political context.

- To encourage learners to engage in discourse related to speculative fiction. Course Material (Selected): Primary Readings: Tolkien, J. R. R. “On Fairy-Stories.” Tolkien on Fairy-Stories .
