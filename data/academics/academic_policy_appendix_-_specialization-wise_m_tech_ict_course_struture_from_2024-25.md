Appendix I
Academic Requirements for the M Tech (ICT) Program
(Effective from 2022-23 batch)
The Rules governing the M Tech (Master of Technology) program of DA-IICT, which is a two-
year full-time postgraduate program, are defined in a document referred as “Academic
Requirement for M Tech (ICT) Program.” This document contains course structure of all the
specialization including new specialization – WCSP introduced from the year 2024. All other
rules apply uniformly to M Tech (ICT) program as mentioned in a document referred to as
“Academic Requirement for M Tech (ICT) Program.”
