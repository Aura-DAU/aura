# Introduction to Speculative Fiction and Films (HMXXX)

## Main Content

HMXXX, Introduction to Speculative Fiction and Films (Autumn 2025)
Course Placement: B.Tech. (ICT, CS and MnC), Open Elective, Semester – VII
Course format: 3 hours of lectures each week.
Course Instructor: Satvik Gupta, 3204, Faculty Block 3
Course Content:
This course introduces the speculative fiction supergenre by focusing on its five
representative subgenres: fantasy, science fiction, horror, dystopian and post-apocalyptic
fiction. The objective is to examine the supergenre’s historical, cultural, and
philosophical underpinnings while exploring the themes of wonder, dread, technological
hubris, tyranny and human resilience. By the end of this course, learners will be able to
recognize and appreciate works of speculative fiction while engaging in critical discourse
and analysis of representative texts and films.
Note: Films only constitute 25% of the course content, the remaining 75% includes
intensive reading of short stories, novels, essays and research papers.
Course Objectives:
● To recognize and appreciate works of horror, science fiction, fantasy, dystopian
fiction and post-apocalyptic fiction.
● To position and understand works of speculative fiction within a larger cultural,
historical, and socio-political context.
● To encourage learners to engage in discourse related to speculative fiction.

Vintage Classic, 2011, pp. 61–98.

Course Outcomes:

Upon the completion of the course, it is expected that the students will be able to:
● Recognize and analyze recurrent speculative fiction motifs, themes and modes of
expression.
● Demonstrate a critical understanding of the supergenre’s impact on contemporary
and popular culture.
● Develop informed arguments on the intersections of literature and cinema within
the sphere of speculative fiction.
