# Overview

Starter reference for engineering students considering an MBA via CAT
(Common Admission Test) after (or instead of) a core-engineering role.
General content only — no DAU-specific placement/MBA-admit data included.

# What CAT is

CAT is the primary entrance exam for the IIMs and is also accepted by many
other top Indian B-schools (FMS Delhi, MDI, SPJIMR, and others, each with
their own additional criteria). It tests Verbal Ability & Reading
Comprehension, Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative
Ability.

# Why an engineering graduate might pursue it

- Career pivot into consulting, product management, or general management
  roles that value a technical + business combination.
- Some students target it directly after their undergraduate degree;
  others build 1–3 years of work experience first — both are common paths,
  and which is "better" genuinely depends on the target school and the
  student's goals, not a universal rule.

# Typical timeline (general — verify each year)

- CAT registration usually opens around August, exam is held in late
  November.
- Results typically release in early January, followed by WAT/PI
  (Written Ability Test / Personal Interview) rounds at individual
  institutes through February–March.

# Preparation approach (general)

1. Diagnostic test to identify weak sections early.
2. QA/DILR sections usually need the most sustained practice for
   engineering students coming from a technical background — VARC is often
   the section that needs the most deliberate work instead.
3. Many aspirants use a 6–12 month prep window, often starting a year or
   more before their target exam sitting if combining prep with a full-time
   job.
