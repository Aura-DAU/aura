# What GATE is

GATE is a national-level exam (jointly conducted by the IITs/IISc on
rotation) used as the primary admission gateway for M.Tech/MS programs at
IITs, NITs, IIITs, and IISc, and also used by many PSUs (Public Sector
Undertakings) for direct recruitment into engineering roles.

# Why a DAU student might pursue it

- M.Tech/MS admission at IITs/NITs/IISc typically requires a valid GATE score.
- PSU recruitment (ONGC, BHEL, NTPC, ISRO, and others) frequently uses GATE
  scores directly in place of a separate written exam.
- Some GATE-qualified candidates are also eligible for a MHRD scholarship/
  stipend during an M.Tech program.

# Typical timeline (general, verify current-year dates)

- Applications typically open in late August/early September.
- The exam is usually held in early-to-mid February.
- Results are typically announced in mid-March.
*(Exact dates change every year — always confirm against the official GATE
website for the current cycle rather than relying on this document.)*

# How to prepare (general guidance)

1. Identify your GATE paper code (matches your engineering discipline).
2. Review the official syllabus PDF for that paper code.
3. Standard reference materials: NPTEL lecture series for conceptual gaps,
   previous 10–15 years of GATE papers for practice, and a formula/notes
   sheet built incrementally through the prep cycle.
4. Most successful candidates report a 6–10 month structured prep window
   alongside coursework, not a last-minute cram — but this varies a lot by
   student and discipline.
