# Main Content

## Food Court

[Image Present: Food Court inner]

DA-IICT campus has 7 food courts offering wide variety of food covering almost all the cuisine and taste from North India to South India.

The food courts are outsourced to run on a yearly basis to experienced caterers. The contractors are provided with modern infrastructure facilities at low cost in order to work out affordable price for the food items served.

All the food courts serve North and South Indian Food and in addition, two food courts serve South Indian food, one special breakfast, snacks and pastries and one fruit and fruit juices.

There is a special arrangement with Amul to make Amul products available at the food court and a separate food court which serves non-vegetarian food including egg dishes.
