# Main Content

## Cybage Khushboo Scholarships

#### for Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)

**Khushboo Charitable Trust** is a self-sustaining NGO and the philanthropic arm (CSR wing) of Cybage Software Private Limited, Pune. The Trust has instituted Merit-cum-Means Scholarships at DAU for economically backward but promising students to help them fulfil their educational aspirations. The terms and conditions of the Scholarships are as follows:

### I. Scholarships

* Ten Scholarships per academic year starting the academic year 2025-26 to the students of ICT Domain and LAW domain-related courses.
* The amount of Scholarship shall be limited to 80% of the tuition fee or ₹ 1,50,000 per annum (whichever is lower). Hostel fee and any other fees or expenses will not be covered under the scholarship.
* The Scholarship period is 4(four) years starting August 2025-26. Continuation of the Scholarship beyond 4(four) years will be decided by the Trustees of Cybage Khushboo Trust, and their decision shall be final and binding on the recipient of the Scholarship and the Institute.

### II. Eligibility

* **For B.Tech Program:** The student should have obtained **minimum 60% aggregate percentage** in Class X and Class XII Board Examinations
* **For other programs:** The applicant should have obtained **60% aggregate marks from Class X to Bachelor’s Degree** final examinations.
* The total annual income of the student’s family should be **less than 5.00 lakh per annum**.
* The students residing in **Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Anand, Kheda, and Vadodara districts** are eligible to apply for this scholarship
* The scholarship is granted on the student clearing all courses in a semester. The scholarship shall stand discontinued in case the student fails in a subject in a given semester
* The shortlisted student will be interviewed by Cybage Khushboo Trust Representatives. The Representatives may also visit the home of the applicant.

### III. How to apply?

Please visit **<http://cybagekhushboo.org/scholarship/>** and refer ‘Students Guide’ available at the site for details. The student has to submit the application form along with the following documents to the University.

The following are the supporting documents to be submitted:

* Scholarship Form
* Board Mark sheet of Class X and XII.
* Final Year Degree Mark sheet issued by the University*(Applicable to PG Students).*
* CET/JEE/NEET result
* School leaving certificate
* Parents’ Income Certificate
* Aadhar card – Student & Parents’
* Ration card
* Cast Certificate and Creamy Layer Certificate, if any
* Photocopy of all bank accounts’ passbook of the student & parents’ (last financial year)
* The Grade Sheet issued by the Institute (Registrar’s office will directly provide the grade sheet to the Trust in each semester).
