## Biography

Abhishek Tilva currently serves as an Assistant Professor at DAU. He holds M.A., M.Phil. and Ph.D. degrees in Statistics from Columbia University, New York and B.S. and M.S. degrees in Mathematics from Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune.

## Specialization
