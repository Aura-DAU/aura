# Main Content

### DAU offers five programs at the postgraduate level:

**(1)** A two-year M.Tech. program in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) with specializations in (a) Machine Learning, (b) Software Systems and (c) VLSI and Embedded systems.

**(2)** a two-year M.Sc. program in Information Technology (IT).

**(3)** a two-year M.Sc. program in Agriculture Analytics (AA).

**(4)** a two-year M.Sc. program in Data Science (DS).

**(5)** a two-year M.Des. program in Specialization 1: Communication Design (CD) & Specialization 2: Intelligent User Experience Design (IUxD).

**Please note that in the academic year AY-2026-27, admissions in the M.Des. Programme are being offered only for the IUxD specialization.**

The details of the admissions process for the above stated programs can be found  [**here**](https://www.daiict.ac.in/admissions#tab-2)

Financial assistance/Stipend is provided to a majority of the M.Tech. (ICT) students in the form of Teaching Assistants. The details of pedagogy, curriculum, courses and related things can be found in the respective program pages.
