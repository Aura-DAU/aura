# Admissions - M.Sc. (Data Science)

## Overview

Data science integrates Mathematics, Statistics, and Machine Learning to comprehend and analyze real-world phenomena using data. It involves extracting meaningful insights from structured and unstructured organizational data through analytical study. In today's digital age, organizations handle vast amounts of data daily, and evolving technologies offer cost-effective data storage and analysis solutions. Proficiency in data analysis is essential for career advancement, spanning various domains such as banking, finance, healthcare, e-commerce, and more. With nearly 100,000 data analytics job vacancies, India ranks as the second-largest data analytics jobs hub globally, indicating a rapidly increasing demand for data science skills.

## Program Structure

### Degree Name
**MSc. in Data Science**

### Duration
**2 Years (Four Semesters)**

### Program Characterization
Intersection of Mathematics, Statistics, Machine Learning, Programming, and System Development

### Program Uniqueness
Hands-on and Case Study-based Program

### Target Audience
* Traditional Science/ Economics/ Engineering Graduates with good mathematical aptitude, basic programming skills, and an inclination toward data science.
* Professionals in the workplace who wish to improve their skills for emerging jobs in data-science-related fields.

## Program Objectives

The primary goal of the M.Sc. in Data Science program is to cultivate a competent workforce capable of meeting the growing demands of big data analytics. The program focuses on imparting skills in quantitative data analysis, data mining, modeling, prediction, storage, management, and big data processing. It also emphasizes expertise in data visualization, handling multimedia big data, programming, and communication. Additionally, the program incorporates value-added courses, practical case studies, and training sessions to enhance learner confidence and market readiness.

## Eligibility Criteria

* A Bachelor’s degree in Science from a recognized university with a minimum aggregate of 60% or its equivalent in disciplines such as Statistics, Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, Economics, Econometrics, and Operational Research.
* A Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or Technology from a recognized University with a minimum aggregate of 60% or its equivalent in disciplines such as Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics, Electronics and Communication, or Electrical Engineering.
* Candidates holding BCA, BSC (IT), BSC (AI/ML), and B.Sc. (Data Science) degrees with a minimum aggregate of 60% or its equivalent are also eligible.
* Candidates must have passed the 12th standard with Mathematics as a main subject.

**Final Year Students:** Candidates appearing in their final degree examination and expecting to complete it by July 2026 may apply, subject to obtaining a minimum aggregate of 60% marks by 30 October 2026.

**Age:** No age limit applies.

## Selection Process

### Entrance Test Details

**Exam Pattern:**  
Multiple choice questions (MCQ) from four sections: Mathematics, Statistics, Basic Computer Science (programming), and Quantitative Aptitude.

**Duration:**  
90 minutes

**Scoring:**  
+4 for correct answers, -1 for incorrect answers (no section-wise cutoffs)

**Tie Breaker:**  
1. Mathematics and Statistics section scores  
2. Grade 12th and 10th examination marks

**Test Centers:**  
DAU (Formerly DA-IICT) Gandhinagar, Ahmedabad, Bhopal, Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Patna, Jaipur, Kolkata, New Delhi, Pune, Rajkot, Surat, Udaipur, Bhavnagar, Bhilai, Bhubaneswar, Chandigarh, Guwahati, Jammu, Kochi, Lucknow, Pant Nagar, Porbandar, Ranchi, and Vijayawada.
