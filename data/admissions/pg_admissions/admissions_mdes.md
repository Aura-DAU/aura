## Program Overview

## Master of Design (Intelligent User Experience Design) – M.Des. (IUxD)

The M.Des. in Intelligent User Experience Design (IUxD) at Dhirubhai Ambani University is India’s first M.Des program that fuses Intelligent Technology with Human-Centered Design. This two-year, full-time program is built for the next generation of Designers who will shape how people interact with smart technologies like AI, AR/VR, and connected devices. This program prepares the future Designers to be the bridge between complex systems and real human needs—making innovation accessible, inclusive, and impactful.

Graduates will be equipped to work across industries like Technology, EVs, Retail, & Smart products, driving the future of intelligent experiences.

* India's one of the first M.Des with focus on Technology and AI in Design.
* Interdisciplinary curriculum blending UX, AI, HCI, AR/XR and Technology
* Studio-based, hands-on learning with real-world design challenges.
* Career paths in Interaction Design, UX Research, phygital and immersive design.
* Small batch of 25-seats ensures personalized mentorship and guidance.

## Program Structure

## M.Des. (IUxD)

The MDes (IUxD) is a full-time two-year program, which is divided into four semesters. This Program extends traditional UX, UI, and interaction design principles to enable intuitive interactions with smart and autonomous systems, ensuring that the user experience remains meaningful and effective in an increasingly intelligent technological landscape. The program also includes conversations and debates on the ethics, challenges and uses of ‘intelligent technologies’ to create sensitive designers who can visualize, leverage and integrate products and services in a socially and culturally sensitive manner.

## Eligibility

### Educational Qualification

A Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent from a statutory university in the fields of Design, Science, Engineering, Technology, Humanities, Social Sciences, Fine Arts, Applied Arts, Architecture and Performing Arts. Professional Diplomas from nationally recognized institutions such as NID, NIFT, CEPT, IDC will also be considered for eligibility. Candidates must have 55% marks or equivalent in the last qualifying degree/examination and are required to submit the provisional Degree certificate or proof of completion of Degree before 30 October 2026 failing which their admission is liable to cancellation.

The decision of the concerned authorities of DAU (Formerly DA-IICT) regarding eligibility of any candidate shall be final.

#### **Age**:

There is no age limit applicable to this program.

## Selection Process

## M.Des. Program follows a 3-step system for admission

* **Step 1**: Admission form and Statement of Purpose
* **Step 2**: Design Aptitude Test (DAT) conducted by DAU MDes Program. Currently students who have qualified for CEED is exempt from taking DAT.
* **Step 3**: Interview

All candidates for this programme must appear for a Design Aptitude Test (DAT) to be conducted by DAU and on securing minimum 40% score will be selected for interview. Design Aptitude Test (DAT) may be conducted at selected centers all over the country or online. The tentative list of centers is: DAU (Formerly DA-IICT) Gandhinagar, Ahmedabad, Bhopal, Benguluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Patna, Jaipur, Kolkata, New Delhi, Pune, Rajkot, Surat, Udaipur, Bhavnagar, Bhilai, Bhubaneswar, Chandigarh, Guwahati, Jammu, Kochi, Lucknow, Pant Nagar, Porbandar, Ranchi and Vijayawada.

The final selection will be based on the combined scores attained in the DAT and the interview. The DAT will constitute 60%, and the interview 40% of the overall marks assigned to the admission test.

The candidate should secure a minimum of 40% in the exam to be considered for the interview process.

Currently students who have qualified for CEED is exempt from taking DAT. Their CEED score will considered in equivalence to DAT score.

**Note**:

* The decision of the Competent Authorities of DAU regarding eligibility and selection of any candidate shall be final.

### Application form and Statement of Purpose

All candidates will have to submit their Statements of Purpose online, along with their applications. Applications without an SOP will not be considered. Candidates will have to relate the curricular content of MDes (IUxD) specialization to their own interests and aptitudes and explain how they can learn and contribute to the specialization in their own way.

### Design Aptitude Test (DAT):

#### ***M.Des. (IUxD) - Design Aptitude Test (DAT):***

The Design Aptitude Test for MDes Intelligent User Experience Design (MDes IUxD) will have the following 3 parts, each bearing 20 marks each

* **M.Des. (IUxD) Part 1:** This test will evaluate the candidate’s ability to think creatively and test problem-solving skills in the context of human centric design.
* **M.Des. (IUxD) Part 2:** Will assess the candidate’s understanding of digital product design and human-computer interaction related challenges.
* **M.Des. (IUxD) Part 3:** The candidates will be asked to write a subjective response to a user experience design brief, demonstrating their design thinking skills.

[A sample question paper can be found here (pdf)](https://www.daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/MDes_IUxD_Sample_QP_25.pdf)

Final merit list of confirmed and wait-list candidates based on entrance test and interview performance will be posted on the website of the Institute.

The offer letter for admission will be sent by the Admission committee through post or email to candidates.

In case of a tie in the final merit list, the tie will be broken by applying the following criteria in this exact order:
1. Years of experience (work experience)
2. Marks obtained in the 10+2 / Higher Secondary examination
3. Marks obtained in the 10th standard / Secondary examination
4. Age of the candidate (older candidate preferred)
