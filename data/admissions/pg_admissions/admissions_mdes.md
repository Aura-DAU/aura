## Program Overview

### Master of Design (Intelligent User Experience Design) – M.Des. (IUxD)

The M.Des. in Intelligent User Experience Design (IUxD) at Dhirubhai Ambani University is India’s first M.Des program that fuses Intelligent Technology with Human-Centered Design. This two-year, full-time program is built for the next generation of Designers who will shape how people interact with smart technologies like AI, AR/VR, and connected devices. This program prepares the future Designers to be the bridge between complex systems and real human needs—making innovation accessible, inclusive, and impactful.

Graduates will be equipped to work across industries like Technology, EVs, Retail, & Smart products, driving the future of intelligent experiences.

- India's one of the first M.Des with focus on Technology and AI in Design.
- Interdisciplinary curriculum blending UX, AI, HCI, AR/XR and Technology
- Studio-based, hands-on learning with real-world design challenges.
- Career paths in Interaction Design, UX Research, phygital and immersive design.
- Small batch of 25-seats ensures personalized mentorship and guidance.

## Program Structure

### M.Des. (IUxD)

The MDes (IUxD) is a full-time two-year program, which is divided into four semesters. This Program extends traditional UX, UI, and interaction design principles to enable intuitive interactions with smart and autonomous systems, ensuring that the user experience remains meaningful and effective in an increasingly intelligent technological landscape. The program also includes conversations and debates on the ethics, challenges and uses of ‘intelligent technologies’ to create sensitive designers who can visualize, leverage and integrate products and services in a socially and culturally sensitive manner.

## Eligibility

### Educational Qualification

A Bachelor’s Degree or equivalent from a statutory university in the fields of Design, Science, Engineering, Technology, Humanities, Social Sciences, Fine Arts, Applied Arts, Architecture and Performing Arts. Professional Diplomas from nationally recognized institutions such as NID, NIFT, CEPT, IDC will also be considered for eligibility. Candidates must have 55% marks or equivalent in the last qualifying degree/examination and are required to submit the provisional Degree certificate or proof of completion of Degree before 30 October 2026 failing which their admission is liable to cancellation.

The decision of the concerned authorities of DAU (Formerly DA-IICT) regarding eligibility of any candidate shall be final.

### Age

There is no age limit applicable to this program.
