## Program Overview

### Program Structure

The M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics) is a two-year postgraduate program jointly offered by DAU (Formerly DA-IICT), Anand Agriculture University, and Indian Institute of Remote Sensing. The program follows a four-semester structure: three semesters of coursework followed by one semester of project/research work. Summer semesters include Value Added Courses (VAC) organized by industries/academia with certification upon completion.

### Multidisciplinary Focus

The program integrates agriculture and data analytics, focusing on descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics to optimize decision-making in agriculture. It addresses challenges like land fragmentation, climate change, and high input costs through technologies such as Big Data Analytics, IoT, and AI.

### Value Added Courses

Industry-partnered certifications include:
- **Domain Specific**: Crop Yield Modeling (SATSURE Analytics), Enhanced Weather Forecasting (IBM India), Spatial Analysis (ESRI India), AI in Agritechnology (AMNEX Info Technologies)
- **Technology Specific**: Visualization & Storytelling (Analytics Vidhya), Microwave Data Processing (SATSURE Analytics)

## Eligibility

### Academic Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Agriculture, Environmental Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or Physics with **60% aggregate** (or equivalent).
- Final year students expected to complete degrees by July 2026 may apply, subject to 60% requirement.
- Degree certificates must be submitted by **30 October 2026**.

### Age Limit
- Maximum age: **45 years** as of application deadline.

### Professional Eligibility
- Professionals in agriculture domain with qualifying education are eligible.

## Selection Process

### Evaluation Components
1. **Entrance Test (50%)**: 90-minute MCQ test covering Mathematics, Science, and Logical Reasoning (45 questions total, 15 per section)
2. **Interview (30%)**: For shortlisted candidates
3. **Academic Credentials (20%)**: 10th, 12th, and graduation marks

### Entrance Test Details
- **Pattern**: 45 MCQs (15 per section)
