# M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics)

## Overview

The M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics) is a two-year postgraduate program jointly offered by DAU (Formerly DA-IICT), Anand Agriculture University, and Indian Institute of Remote Sensing with active participation of Industries/Academia by offering value-added courses.

Indian agriculture contributes 16% of the overall GDP and 60% of the total employment in the country. However, it suffers from various crises, such as fragmentation of land holdings, high input costs, and climate change, which have reduced farmers' income substantially over time.

## Program Overview

The M.Sc. (Agriculture Analytics) program is a multidisciplinary program of two major disciplines: Agriculture and Data Analytics. It is a two-year postgraduate program that aims to nurture students with insights and knowledge to take the agriculture sector into the future.

The program will introduce students to concepts of data analytics, such as descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive, in agriculture. It will empower students to eliminate speculative farming and usher in the age of predictive agriculture.

## Program Structure

The M.Sc. Agriculture Analytics Program follows a full-time four-semester pattern. The curriculum consists of three semesters of coursework followed by a one-semester project/research work on a specified topic.

During the summer semester, Value-Added Courses (VAC) will be organized at DAU (Formerly DA-IICT) by the Industries/Academia. The VAC comes with certification after successful completion.

## Entrance Test

The test will evaluate the candidates on their mathematical and science aptitude, and logical reasoning.

**Pattern:**

* Multiple choice questions (MCQ) from three sections – Mathematics, Science, and Logical Reasoning
* Exam Duration: 90 minutes
* Weightage:
	+ Mathematics, Science, and Logical Reasoning: 15 questions each
* Difficulty Level: Questions from Mathematics and Science will be of standard 10th difficulty level of CBSE board

**Exam Syllabus:**

* Basic Mathematics and Science Syllabus For Entrance Test
	+ Chemistry: Matter, elements, compounds, and mixtures; atomic and molecular masses; valency; chemical formulae; chemical equations; acids, bases, and salts; pH scale
	+ Physics: Distance, displacement, velocity, acceleration; force and motion; Newton’s laws of motion; inertia; momentum; gravitation; universal law of gravitation; acceleration due to gravity; mass and weight; free fall; thrust and pressure; archimedes' principle; buoyancy; elementary idea of relative density
	+ Biology: Life processes; nutrition; autotrophic and heterotrophic nutrition; respiration in human beings and plants; transportation in human beings and plants; excretion in human beings and plants; our environment; ecosystem; food chains and webs; management of natural resources; forests and wildlife; stakeholders; sustainable management; water for all; water harvesting; coal and petroleum
