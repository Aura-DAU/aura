# B. Tech DAFS Merit Scholarships at DA-IICT

## Overview

The Institute awards Merit scholarships to students admitted through Direct Admission of Foreign and NRI Students (DAFS) category in the B Tech programs.

## Scholarships at DA-IICT for Students Admitted Under Direct Admission of Foreign and NRI Students (DAFS) Category

### Introduction

The Institute awards Merit scholarships to students admitted through Direct Admission of Foreign and NRI Students (DAFS) category in the B Tech programs.

Scholarships of the value equal to Rs. 70,000/- per semester for the duration of the program (maximum eight semesters only) are awarded from the year 2019-20.

### Selection of Scholarship Awardees

Scholarships would be offered to the five students with the highest SPI (Semester Performance Index) for the students admitted through Direct Admission of Foreign and NRI Students (DAFS) category.

### Eligibility Criteria

#### Minimum SPI

The SPI (Semester Performance Index) obtained by the student should be 7.00/10.00 or above in every semester.

#### Pass in All Courses

The student should not obtain a grade other than a passing grade in all the courses taken in a semester.

#### Duration of Study

The student should complete all the degree requirements in the normal duration of study for the program.

#### Probation or Disciplinary Action

The student should at no time be placed on academic probation or be subjected to any disciplinary action by the Institute.

#### Scholarship other than DA-IICT

The awardee should not be in the receipt of any other scholarship of Govt. of India / State Government / any other agency.
