## Program Structures and Brochures  

### B.Tech. Information and Communication Technology (ICT)  

Information & Communication Technology (ICT) embodies the convergence of Computer and Communication systems and has obtained wide acceptance as a distinct discipline. ICT is considered to be a discipline dealing with accessing, storage, processing, transmission, reception and display of information, primarily using digital systems and techniques. The curriculum in the B.Tech. (ICT) program tries to address the optimized convergence of existing Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) as well as Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) disciplines by identifying the broad areas of performance capabilities. For details of the program click **here**.  

### B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with minor in Computational Science  

Computational Science is concerned with constructing mathematical models, simulating and quantitative analysis techniques, and using computers to analyze and solve scientific problems with applications in engineering and social sciences. The curriculum in **B.Tech. (Honours) in ICT with minor in Computational Science** aims to provide expertise in modeling and simulation of complex systems, implementation on computers through parallel computing techniques, and analysis of large datasets. Further details of the program are available **here**.  

To complete the program, students must take additional 18-24 credits and maintain a minimum CPI of 6.5 after the third semester. Students with a CPI of 7.5+ may apply for transfer to the B.Tech. (Computational Science) Program, subject to seat availability.  

### B.Tech. Mathematics and Computing (MnC)  

Mathematics and Computing (MnC) is a fusion of Mathematics and Computer Science. The program aims to expand mathematical, algorithmic, and computational thinking while providing a foundation for skill development in MnC. Graduates will gain analytical, logical, and problem-solving skills for real-world applications. For details of the program click **here**.  

### B.Tech. Electronics and VLSI Design (EVD)  

The B.Tech. in Electronics and VLSI Design (EVD) focuses on semiconductor and VLSI design, embedded systems, and high-performance computing. Students gain hands-on training with EDA tools and hardware boards. The program includes pathways in **Electronic System Design** and **VLSI Design**. For more details, **click**.  

### B.Tech. Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (CS and AI)  

DAU’s B.Tech. in CS and AI combines foundational computing with AI concepts. Graduates will be equipped for research and industry roles in intelligent systems. For more details, **click**.  

### B.Tech. Electronics and Communication Engineering – Artificial Intelligence (ECE-AI)  

The ECE-AI program integrates core ECE disciplines with AI, machine learning, and data-driven techniques. Graduates will design intelligent electronic systems. For more details, **click**.

## Fee Structure  

An amount of Rs.2,17,500 (Rs.1,92,500 tuition fee for the first semester + Rs.25,000 caution deposit) is to be paid at counselling. Subsequent fees are charged semester-wise.  

| Item | Amount |  
| --- | --- |  
| Tuition fee**\*** | Rs.1,92,500 per Semester |  
| Registration Fee | Rs. 2,500 per Semester |  
| Caution Deposit | Rs.25,000 (Refundable) |  
| Hostel Rent | Rs. 37,800 per semester |  
| Food | On actuals (~Rs. 5,500/month) |  

***\* This Fee Structure is submitted to the Appellate Committee of the State Government for consideration.***  
**\*** Subject to revision every Academic Year from 8 to 10%.
