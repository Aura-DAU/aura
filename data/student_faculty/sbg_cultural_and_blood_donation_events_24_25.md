# Cultural and blood donation events 24 25

Dhirubhai Ambani University

Student Body Government (SBG)

Report on Cultural Activities & Blood Donation Camps

(2024–25)

## Cultural Activities

### 1. Janmashtami (Dahi Handi Competition)

The cultural committee organized a dahi handi competition on the occasion of Janmashtami. The event began with an Aarti at the SAC-1 foyer, followed by a mud-holi-cum-matki-phod competition at the SAC Ground. The celebration saw enthusiastic participation and created a spirit of unity across the campus.

Date of event : 26/08/2024

### 2. Ganesh Chaturthi

The cultural committee organized Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations on campus from 7th September 2024 for five days. The festival began with the Sthapna Ceremony at the main gate, followed by daily morning and evening aartis. Students participated actively, creating a devotional and festive atmosphere on campus.

Date of event : 07/09/2024

### 3. Tarang (Navratri of DAU)

The cultural committee organized Tarang, the annual Navratri celebration, on 4th and 5th October 2024. The two-day event featured vibrant Garba and Dandiya Raas performances. Renowned artists Mihir Jani and Rishikesh Gadhvi performed on the respective days, adding to the excitement.

Date of event: 04/10/2024 and 05/10/2024

### 4. Dussehra

The cultural committee celebrated Dussehra by distributing Jalebi and Fafda at the Padma Kamal Canteen.

Date of event : 12/10/2024

### 5. Diwali

The cultural committee organized Diwali celebrations on campus with a Pooja ceremony, sweets distribution. Students who couldn’t go home for the break joined the event, making the evening festive and enjoyable for all.

### 6. Uttarayan (Makarsankranti)

The cultural committee organized Uttarayan celebrations on 14th January at the SAC Ground from 9:00 AM onwards. Kites and manjha were provided to participants, and students enjoyed kite flying along with a fun activity of writing messages on their kites. Sweets were also distributed on a first-come, first-serve basis. The event saw enthusiastic participation from students and was conducted with safety measures in place.

Date of event: 14/01/2025

### 7. Holi

The cultural committee organized Holi celebrations on 8th March 2025 at the SAC Ground from 9:30 AM onwards. Students participated enthusiastically in the festivities, enjoying colors, music, and fun activities. The event created a lively and joyful atmosphere on campus.

## Blood Donation camps

The blood donation camps were held twice in the year 2024-25, one in autumn semester and one in winter semester. For the autumn semester the blood donation camp was held on 06/10/2024 while the camp of the winter semester was held on 12/02/2025.
