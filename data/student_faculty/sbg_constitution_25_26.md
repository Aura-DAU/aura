### 1 Preamble

We, the students of DA-IICT, have solemnly resolved to constitute a self-governing democratic organization, the Student Body Government, aimed to:

• Monitor and regulate all the student activities.

• Ensure justice and equality in all aspects of student life.

• Enhance the overall development of all the students.

### 2.2 Elections

### 2.2.1 Election Commission

• The Election Commission (EC) is an independent and autonomous body, constituted by a set of students, responsible for organizing and conducting periodic, free and fair elections of the student representatives to be a part of the DA-IICT Student Body Government as and when it is required to do so. The EC is mentored by and reports directly to the Dean (Students).

• The Election Commission comprises of a total of four members:

1. The Chief Election Commissioner, the senior-most member of the EC.

2. Election Commission representatives (Election Commissioners) from every participating batch — one representative from each UG batch and one representative from the collective PG batches.

• The Chief Election Commissioner may be impeached by the SBG through a two-thirds majority of the present members and a ratification by Dean

(Students).

• A student either on academic or disciplinary probation will not be considered eligible for contesting in the elections.

### 2.2.4 Retention in Committees

• A Committee may wish to nominate floor(n/10) members for retention before the General Elections.

• If some/all of the floor(n/10) past Committee members are not elected, then the nominated candidates would automatically become a part of the

Committee and occupy the selection seats.

• The Committee will choose which selection seat to remove from another batch if the remaining number is greater than the number of selected seats for that particular batch.

• The retention process excludes the Hostel Management Committee.

• Note: ’n’ is the total number of seats in the Committee.

### 3 Structures and Functions

### 3.1 Structure

• As stated in the Section 2.1 (Point 1), the student members of 8 commit-tees, 21 clubs and 3 organizations constitute the Student Body Government.

• The Student Body Government Convener, Deputy Convener, Secretary, and

Treasurer are elected to form the Core Team.

• Dean (Students) is the ex-officio mentor of the Student Body Government.

### 3.2 Functions

• The Student Body Government is the primary forum for discussions of student ideas, concerns, plans, and activities.

### 3.2.1 SBG Core Team

• The Core Team acts as a judiciary body responsible for smooth functioning of the student activities and resolving conflicts among the community.

• Roles and Responsibilities of the SBG Convener:

1. To convene and conduct the meetings of the Student Body

Government.

2. To act as a spokesperson of the Student Body Government.

3. Member, by position, of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC).

4. To oversee and ensure smooth functioning of the SBG operations.

5. To check and verify the amendments made to the SBG Constitution (after being passed in the general meetings) and act as a signing authority.

• Roles and Responsibilities of the SBG Deputy Convener:

1. In the event that the SBG Convener is not present, the Deputy

Convener takes over their duties.

2. To monitor all Club and Committee operations, functions and responsibilities and recommend changes as necessary.

3. To arrange a suitable venue for the meetings of the Student Body

Government (taking into consideration disabled access).

4. To aid the Convener in setting up the agenda for the meeting.

5. To manage the SBG calendar for various events held throughout the year.

6. To ensure that all the student Clubs and Committees get equal opportunity for conducting their events.

• Roles and Responsibilities of the SBG Secretary:

1. To complete the necessary paperwork prior to the implementation of any new decision or policy.

2. To maintain and draft the Constitution of the Student Body

Government.

3. To prepare the minutes of the meeting and keep a record of meeting attendance.

4. To ensure that all the student Clubs and Committees submit the event reports.

5. To issue certificates to SBG members and Sports team captains as a form of recognition of their contribution to the student community.

6. To maintain the inventory reports and make sure they are submitted to the next SBG Secretary each year with a copy going to the Dean

(Students).

• Roles and Responsibilities of the SBG Treasurer:

1. To organize the budget session for funds allocation.

2. To maintain up-to-date record of financial transactions.

3. To ensure that funds are spent judiciously.

4. To present an end-of-the-year financial report to the Dean (Students).

5. To ensure the following procedure is followed for advance indents:

(a) The student convener of the respective Club or Committee will create the proposal for advance as per the requirement and amount sanctioned in the budget session for that particular event.

(b) The student convener/member of the respective Club or

Committee will get it approved (i.e. signed) from the faculty mentor of the particular Club or Committee.

(c) The student convener/member of the respective Club or

Committee will give it to the SBG Treasurer for approval, at least

### 3 days prior to the event.

(d) The SBG Treasurer will get it certified from the Chief Accounts

Officer and get it approved from the Executive Registrar to get the amount sanctioned from the Accounts Section.

(e) The SBG Treasurer will hand over the amount only to the student convener of the respective Club or Committee.

(f) A Club or Committee is not permitted to create advance for prize money.

(g) No further advance will not be sanctioned for a Club or Commit-tee if the previous advance of the respective Club or Committee remains unsettled.

6. To ensure the following procedure is followed for clearance indents:

(a) The student convener/member of the respective Club or

Committee will collect all the bills after the event.

(b) The student convener/member of the respective Club or

Committee will get the bills approved from the faculty mentor of the particular Club or Committee.

(c) The student convener/member of the respective Club or

Committee will submit the indent to the SBG Treasurer for approval.

(d) The SBG Treasurer will get the clearance or settlement approved by the Chief Accounts Officer and the Executive Registrar.

### 3.2.2 Impeachment of the SBG Core Team

The procedure to impeach the SBG Deputy Convener/Secretary/Treasurer:

1. Any student can raise a motion of no confidence by expressing their concerns to the SBG Convener. An impeachment meeting is to be called if the motion is supported by more than one-thirds of the members in-attendance.

2. In the impeachment meeting, the resolution will be discussed, followed by voting. The office bearer is required to resign from the position if the resolution receives the support of more than two-thirds of the current total members.

3. The SBG Convener shall assume the duties of the vacant position until the Election Commission conducts by-elections for the same.

The procedure to impeach the SBG Convener:

1. The procedure for the impeachment will be the same as the procedure for the impeachment of the SBG Core Team except that the concerns shall be expressed to the SBG Deputy Convener and the Deputy

Convener shall convene the meetings.

2. The SBG Deputy Convener shall assume the duties of the SBG

Convener until the Election Commission conducts by-elections for the

Convener position.

### 3.2.3 Faculty Mentor

Roles and Responsibilities of the Faculty Mentor:

1. To provide advice on all issues pertaining to the functioning of the respective Club or Committee.

2. To prepare budgetary requirements in consultation with the respective Club or Committee.

3. To request the administration for requirements of space, manpower or other institute facilities for the respective Club or Committee activities.

4. To control and supervise all the purchases and expenditures without breaching the rules and procedures laid down by the Institute and the

Student Body Government.

5. To act as the reporting authority for the student activities pertaining to the respective Club or Committee’s domain.

6. To report any violation of the rules and guidelines to the Dean (Stu-dents).

7. Endeavour to bring about any improvements needed in the facilities and the support system provided by the institute.

### 3.2.4 Amendments

To enact amendments that alter the existing Constitution:

1. Any student or faculty member may propose changes to the Student

Body Government Constitution, with a copy to the Dean (Students).

2. The Student Body Government must take into consideration every idea or submission, but they are not required to enact every-one.

3. A two-thirds majority of those present at the meeting is necessary to enact amendments.

4. In an emergency, the Dean (Students) may make any necessary temporary changes after consulting with the SBG Core Team. Until the next SBG amendment meeting, when a definitive decision can be made about the matter, the temporary change is in effect.

5. The SBG Core Team may make minor amendments to the constitution after consulting with the Dean (Students).

### 4 Meetings

### 4.1 Attendance and Quorum

• A Student Body Government meeting requires fifty-percent of the total club and committee members to be in attendance.

• Each Committee’s elected and selected members, as well as the core members of each Club, are required to attend Student Body Government meetings.

• If a member is unable to attend a meeting, they should notify the SBG

Secretary of their absence.

• If a member misses two consecutive meetings without an acceptable explanation, the SBG Convener may issue a show cause notice to the member.

• The SBG Convener may suggest removing a member if they routinely miss meetings without providing justification. A simple majority of the members of Student Body Government must agree to the Convener’s request for such a removal in order for it to proceed.

### 4.2 Calling Meetings

• All the Student Body Government meetings will be convened by the SBG

Convener (or the SBG Deputy Convener in the absence of the Convener).

• The meetings can be convened with at least a 24-hour prior notice to the members.

• During the regular semesters, it is advised that meetings be called at least once per month.

• Any student of DA-IICT may call a meeting by sending a request to the

SBG Convener with justification.

1. When such a request is submitted, the Convener is required to call a meeting within two weeks of the request.

2. The Convener shall communicate the concerns to the appropriate member or Committee at least one week before the meeting if they are addressed at a specific Student Body Government member or

Committee.

3. The member or Committee in question must respond to the concerns raised, in the meeting.

• The Student Body Government meeting minutes are available to all students upon request as public records.

### 4.3 Decision making procedures

• Decisions will be taken via a formal consultation procedure.

• Members must be courteous, respectful, and open-minded in order for consultation to take place.

• The purpose of consultation will be to arrive at the most equitable and fair decision possible, not to impose one’s personal beliefs.

• The SBG Convener must ensure:

1. The process of consultation is well-ordered.

2. Members always address the Convener, and not each other, while expressing their opinions.

3. Comments are made to aid in decision-making and not to criticize other members’ opinions.

4. An atmosphere of cordiality is maintained at all times.

• When there has been sufficient discussion on a particular subject, the

Convener will list the potential options available for making a decision and request votes from the elected and selected committee members for each option. The option receiving the most votes will be considered as the final decision.

### 4.4 Budget Sessions

• The SBG Treasurer will send out requests to all Committees and Clubs, at least one week before the budget session, asking them to submit their budget requests.

• The Institute’s Finance Committee decides the overall student activities budget (operational expenditure and capital expenditure) based on the funding proposals submitted by the previous Student Body Government.

• All Committees and Clubs will present their budgets during the budget session.

• The Director will receive the approved budget after it has been forwarded to the Dean (Students) by the SBG Treasurer. The Director will serve as the final decision-maker on the Student Activities budget.

### 5 Student Clubs

### 5.1 Definition

• A Student Club is a group of students who come together voluntarily to engage in an extracurricular or co-curricular activity with some broadly stated objectives.

• The club is entitled to one vote on behalf of the whole core for the SBG

general meetings.

### 5.2 Regulations

• A club must have a Convener and is decided by the Faculty Mentor in consultation with the core members.

• The core team shall be constituted of a minimum of two members

(Convener and Deputy Convener) and a maximum of six members depending on the club needs (except PMMC which has seven members).

• Clubs are entitled to the following:

1. An email ID (if possible, and with group mailing permissions)

2. Use of institute facilities (such as classrooms, lecture theatres, labs, etc.) with prior permissions.

3. Budgetary allocation (if required) from the student activities budget, for activities that would be of broad interest to the entire student community.

• Faculty Mentor:

1. A Club must have a Faculty Mentor.

2. A Club chooses its own Faculty Mentor.

3. The functions of the Faculty Mentor will be the same as those de-scribed in Sec. 3.2.3.

• The clubs are answerable to SBG and receive part from the SBG budget for conducting activities. They are welcome to make proposals and suggestions but do not have the right to vote in Student Body Government meetings because there are no elections.

• The SBG Core Team holds the right to review a Club’s work and utilization of budget. The Club is bound to answer within twenty-four hours.

### 5.3 Dormant Clubs

• Clubs that fail to attend two consecutive SBG general meetings, have not mailed any batch in an academic year and have not performed any club activity. Failure to do any of the above-mentioned activities will result in the club being classified as dormant, in which case the SBG will no longer assume responsibility for its activities.

• The club can be reinstated when the club core is restored and the club activities starts as usual then the club would not be classified as dormant.

### 6 Student Committees

### 6.1 Seat Increment Policy

Three requirements must be met in order to increase a seat on a committee:

1. Approved SBG mandate by two-thirds majority.

2. Approved by Faculty Mentor.

3. Approved by Dean (Students).

The request for seat increment can only be proposed once in an academic year.

### 6.2 Meetings

• The first meeting of a newly elected committee shall be convened either by the

Convener of the previous committee, or by the newly elected members themselves.

• The Convener and the Deputy Convener shall be chosen by a simple majority vote in a secret ballot process. At least one SBG Core Team member, not belonging to the Committee, must be present for the voting to take place. The Committee Convener and Deputy Convener are chosen by both elected and selected members. The elections for the position of the Convener will happen first, and then the elections for deputy convener.

Candidates losing in the Convener elections are eligible to contest for

Deputy Convener elections.

• The timings of the Committee meetings shall be conveyed during the previous meeting, or communicated to the members by email or other means of communication at least 24 hours in advance.

• If a majority of the members are available, an emergency meeting may be convened at any moment.

• Any committee member may write to the Convener to request a meeting of the Committee.

• Fifty-percent of the Committee’s total strength will constitute a quorum for any decision-making.

• The members must be informed via email or other means of communication of any issues that require a vote at least 24 hours before the meeting.

### 6.3 Absentees at meetings

• A member should notify the Convener in advance if (s)he will not be able to attend a meeting.

• If a member consistently misses meetings, the Convener or another

Committee member shall bring up the matter with the Faculty Mentor or the SBG Core Team.

• If any member is absent for 3 consecutive SBG meetings or a general meet without prior notice, (s)he can be directly expelled.

### 6.4 Functions of Convener and Deputy Convener

• To convene and conduct meetings.

• To send the agenda of the meeting at least twenty-four hours in advance.

• To act as an interface between the Committee, the SBG Core Team and the Administration.

• To act as the official spokesperson of the Committee.

6.5 Procedure for withdrawal of the Convener from their post

• In case the Convener of a Committee resigns from their position, then the vacant seat shall be filled by the Deputy Convener, i.e., the Deputy

Convener shall become the new Convener. Furthermore, there should be elections for the position of the Deputy Convener within four days of the resignation of the Convener following the usual procedure.

### 6.6 Impeachment of the convener

• Any member can raise a motion of no confidence. A special meeting is called for the purpose of impeachment if the motion is supported by more than one-thirds of the members in-attendance.

• In the impeachment meeting, the resolution will be discussed, followed by voting. The Convener is required to resign from the position if the resolution receives the support of more than two-thirds of the current total members.

• The current Deputy Convener will take over as the new Convener of the

Committee upon the Convener’s impeachment, and the Committee can then elect the new Deputy Convener from within itself following the usual procedure.

### 6.7 Inactivity/Misuse of position and expulsion

Inactivity or misuse of position of a committee member is defined as:

• Not participating in Committee meetings and displaying a lack of enthusiasm in carrying out the tasks assigned to them.

• Not abiding by the Constitution.

• Threatening actions of any type using committee membership as leverage.

A member is subject to expulsion by the Committee on grounds of any of the aforementioned reasons.

Any Committee member may start the expulsion process by writing to the

Committee Convener stating appropriate reasons. Expulsion proceedings must take place in a meeting that includes at least one SBG Core Team member. A two-thirds majority is required to expel a member, with the cause of expulsion being shared with the Faculty Mentor.

### 6.8 Conflicts

• Any complaint/grievance/conflict against or with any member of a

Committee shall be addressed and reported to the Convener of the respective Committee.

• Any complaint/grievance/conviction against or with a committee shall be addressed to the Convener of the Student Body Government, who will then bring the issue up in a Student Body Government meeting.

### 6.9 Academic Committee

### 6.9.1 Preamble

• The Academic Committee works as an interface between the student com-munity and the administration of the institute (including Dean Academics,

Registrar, Institute Committees, and the Director) for the development of the academic environment, and enhancement of academic life in DA-IICT.

### 6.9.2 Functions

• To convey the views, concerns, and problems, related to academics, of the student community to the institute administration (specifically, the various academic related committees of the institute) so as to enhance the quality of academic life and programs, and foster a conducive academic environment in the institute.

• To organize activities that will enhance the academic aspects of student life in any and every possible way.

### 6.9.3 Roles and Responsibilities of a member

• To represent the views and decisions of the Academic Committee to their respective institute committees. The representatives will be responsible to convey the decisions of the whole committee, and not their own views.

6.10 Annual Festival Committee

6.10.1 Functions

• To organize and manage DA-IICT’s Annual Festival.

6.10.2 Activities

The activities within the paradigm of organizing the Annual Festival comprises of:

• Event Management:

1. Management of the events being organized during the festival.

2. Hospitality Management relating to accommodation and stay of guests and participants.

3. Volunteer Management relating to identification of volunteers for the events and the festival in general.

• Sponsorship Management: This includes interacting with the companies for obtaining financial support for the organization of the festival. The Annual

Festival Committee will never receive funding from the SBG bud- get. The money received from sponsorships must be used only to fund and plan the festival.

• Public Relations Management: This includes being in charge of interacting with other colleges and the media, to promote the festival.

• Design Management: This includes being in charge of designing brochures, posters, certificates and mementos.

• Finance Management: This includes being in charge of resource allocation and monetary sanctions.

• Inventory Management: This includes being in charge of managing the inventory procured for the festival.

• Quality Control: The Convener will be in charge of ensuring proper coordination among the Committee members and handling disagreements.

Issues pertaining or concerning any of the above-mentioned departments must be conveyed to the Convener and the Deputy Convener of the Committee.

6.11 Cafeteria Management Committee

6.11.1 Preamble

• The Cafeteria Management Committee (CMC) addresses issues related to the cafeteria and mess at DA-IICT. In collaboration with the CMC Faculty

Convenor, SBG Core Team and the administration, the CMC plays an active role in determining food prices, ensuring a fair balance between student affordability and vendor sustainability.

6.11.2 Regulations

• Students should contact the CMC for any complaints related to food quality, quantity, hygiene, or other cafeteria-related issues.

• The CMC is responsible for addressing concerns and reporting them to the administration when necessary.

6.11.3 Functions

• The CMC, in coordination with the Faculty Convenor, negotiates and finalizes cafeteria vendor contracts. The administration facilitates the formalization of contracts based on CMC’s decisions.

• Regular and random inspections of food counters are conducted to assess the food quality, quantity, hygiene, and compliance with contractual agreements.

• Inspection reports are submitted to the administration for necessary action.

• The CMC addresses hygiene, cleanliness, and general complaints and reports them to the administration.

• Bi-monthly feedback from students is gathered to improve quality standards.

• The committee is responsible for deciding and regularly updating the mess menu based on student feedback.

• The CMC, along with the SBG, represents students in discussions related to the formation and updates of the mess policy.

6.12 Cultural Committee

6.12.1 Preamble

• Cultural Committee is responsible for promoting various cultural activities like music, drama and dance amongst the student community. It organizes various big events all year round the academic calendar, thus creating an aura of excitement and enjoyment along with providing a platform for students to showcase their talent in front of the college. Apart from these, the Cultural Committee also takes upon its onus, the celebrations of various festivals throughout the year to promote the significance of these festivals as well as generate a homely feeling for the students.

6.12.2 Functions

• To ensure a balance of events catering to the interests of the entire student community.

6.13 Hostel Management Committee

6.13.1 Preamble

The Hostel Management Committee is in charge of:

• Developing a healthier environment in the hostel lives of all students.

• Ensuring proper residential facilities in the hostel, reporting all maintenance-related complaints to appropriate personnel, and following up on these complaints until the work is satisfactorily completed.

• Making decisions related to hostel life, and ensure their implementation on behalf of the residents of the hostel.

6.13.2 Functions

### 7 Budget and Finance

### 7.1 Preamble

• The Student Body Government’s activities are funded by the institute. All expenditures made by the Student Body Government will adhere closely to the financial and accounting standards of the institution.

### 7.2 The Budgetary Cycle

• At the beginning of each academic session or at the conclusion of the previous session, Clubs and Committees requiring budget for the subsequent year will submit their budget requests to the SBG Treasurer.

• The SBG Treasurer will aggregate these requests and present a comprehensive Student Activities budget to the Dean (Students), who will then forward it on to the Institute’s Finance Committee (for both operational and capital expenditures).

• The SBG Core Team will be informed of the budget provided by the Finance

Committee for Student Activities either in April or at the start of the

Autumn term (August).

• The Student Body Government will discuss and debate on the budgetary requests presented by different Clubs and Committees during the budget session.

• Dean (Students) will forward the Student Body Government’s precise bud-get allocation to the Director for final approval.

• No changes can be made to the allocation of funds for different activities once the Director has approved the budget. Any requests for change of allocations must get the Director’s approval.

### 7.3 Finance policies

• Clubs and Committees are permitted to use the allocated amount designated for specific activities after the Director has approved the budget.

• When using funds from the Student Activities Budget, each Club and

Committee shall adhere to the following purchasing policy:

1. The Commercial Officer should work with student members to make any purchases that can be made using a purchase order and a bill payment.

2. Ad-hoc committees made up of the Commercial Officer and two or more student members from the relevant Committees must acquire quotation, place purchase orders, and handle any major purchases

(where “major” is defined as any individual item costing more than Rs.

1000).

3. Any purchases shall be made with at least one week’s notice to the

Administration.

4. Indent forms must be signed by both the SBG Treasurer and the

Committee’s faculty mentor.

5. Up to Rs. 10,000 can be withdrawn by faculty mentors for a variety of short-term expenditures.

6. The Faculty Mentor may give permission for students to make direct small-item purchases (where “small” is defined as any one item costing less than Rs. 1000).

7. All expenditures must be supported by genuine bills, which must be delivered to the institute’s accounts department for proper accounting and auditing.

### 8 Breach of the Constitution by Club/Committee

The violation of the rules and regulations mentioned in the SBG Constitution is categorized in three levels. The severity level will be decided solely by the SBG

Core Team when the issue is brought up to them with substantial proof.

### 8.1 Severity Level 1

The Level 1 severity includes the breach of constitution that affects the working of a Club/Committee.

In such scenarios, the SBG Core Team can call for a meeting with the

Conveners and the Deputy Conveners of all the Club/Committee. The discussions must be held to penalize the concerned Club/Committee.

### 8.2 Severity Level 2

The Level 2 severity includes the breach of constitution that affects the entire

Student Body Government.

In such scenarios, the discussions will be held between the SBG Core Team and the concerned Club/Committee. The discussions will be kept internal. The punishment will be decided by the SBG Core Team.

After the issue is resolved, it is the responsibility of the SBG Core Team to inform the Conveners and the Deputy Conveners of all the Clubs/Committees.

The information to be provided can be in a detailed or general manner depending upon the seriousness of the issue. The information must be kept confidential.

### 8.3 Severity Level 3

The Level 3 severity includes the breach of constitution that can lead to the suspension of the student activities by the administration.

In such scenarios, the SBG Core Team can call for a general meeting to dissolve the entire Club/Committee.

To dissolve a Club/Committee:

• The concerned Club/Committee must address the issue created in a de-tailed manner.

• Following the discussions, voting for dissolution takes place.

• If the Club/Committee gets three-fourths majority, it will be dissolved.

• If the entity to be dissolved is a committee, the members of the committee don’t get the voting rights.

• If a committee is dissolved, the SBG Core Team takes over the functioning of the Committee till new elections by the Election Commission are conducted. All the members of the Committee face a one-year ban for contesting in the elections.

• If a Club is dissolved, the Club activities stand suspended for one year.

### 9 Involvement of SBG and Administration

For the administrative decisions affecting the student community such as the academic calendar, canteen prices, etc. the SBG Core Team must be kept in the loop during the discussions (discussions must not be held during the examinations) and must be notified at least two weeks before the change in rules.

The Dean (Students) will intimate the College Administration with the SBG

Constitution after their official approval of the same. Post this, the Constitution shall also be applicable in the decisions including the Administration.
