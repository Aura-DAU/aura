# Synapse '26 — Event Descriptions

This document outlines the descriptions of all events organized under different categories during the Synapse '26 cultural and technical festival.

---

## Dance Events

### Footloose
- **Description:** From hip-hop to contemporary, salsa to breakdance, Footloose brings together a vibrant mix of western dance styles. Watch talented performers light up the stage with energy, rhythm, and powerful expressions.

### Naach
- **Description:** Dancers celebrate the beauty of our rich cultural heritage. Through classical and folk dance forms, performers bring grace, elegance, and tradition to life on stage.

### Showdown
- **Description:** A high-energy dance battle face-off. Dancers from different styles and backgrounds battle it out, showcasing raw talent, creativity, and confidence. Only the best takes the crown.

---

## Music Events

### Battle of Bands
- **Description:** Experience the thrill as bands take over the stage with their passion for music. From soulful melodies to electrifying rock, this is where bands compete to win hearts and own the spotlight.

### Raaga & Rhapsody
- **Description:** Step into a world of soothing classical music and timeless melodies. Let the magic of ragas and surs captivate you as vocalists and instrumentalists create a truly soulful experience.

### Rave Knight
- **Description:** Turn up the volume and let the beats drop. Get ready for a night filled with powerful mixes, electrifying rhythms, and nonstop dancing as DJs take control of the console.

### Rap Battle
- **Description:** Spit your truth, drop your bars, and own the mic. This open rap battle is all about expressing thoughts, emotions, and stories through powerful words and sharp flows.

---

## Theater Events

### Mime-Out-Loud
- **Description:** When words fall silent, expressions take over. Experience powerful stories told through actions, emotions, and gestures in this captivating mime event.

### Nukkad Natak
- **Description:** Watch street theatre come alive with bold performances that highlight social issues. With strong messages and impactful acting, this event sparks awareness and reflection.

### Stage Play
- **Description:** Get immersed in gripping stories as actors bring drama, emotions, and narratives to life. A perfect blend of storytelling and performance awaits you on stage.

### Mono Acte
- **Description:** A solo performer, countless emotions. Witness an engaging mix of drama, movement, and expression as artists captivate the audience all by themselves.

---

## Fashion & Cosplay Events

### Rampage
- **Description:** Lights, camera, fashion. Watch budding designers express their creativity, independence, and boldness as they illustrate their stories while the most glamorous models walk to the pumping beats.

### CosCon
- **Description:** Dive into CosCon, where cosplay magic happens with anime, Bollywood, Hollywood, or any character you fancy. Join the lively character parade, celebrate cross-cultural creativity, and revel in a day of diverse, character-driven festivities.

---

## Photo and Art Events

### Stroke of Genius
- **Description:** Grab your brushes and paint down your thoughts and imaginations on some piece of merchandise.

### SynapShot
- **Description:** Capture the best moments of Synapse'26 with your phone and let your creativity run wild. Whether it is a reel, time-lapse, vlog, or anything else — show off your talent and unique perspective.

---

## Gaming Events

### Battledrome
- **Description:** You are never too old for video games. Bring out the controllers, keep your thumbs rested, and join the showdown for ultimate glory. Display great use of strategy, tactics, and planning in engaging games including Counter-Strike, Brawl Stars, Valorant, Clash Royale, and Rocket League.

---

## Fun Events

### Hunger Hikes
- **Description:** Foodies Alert. A treasure hunt, but full of food. Showcase your knowledge about food, get subsequent clues, and find the treasure in teams.

### Synapse Got Latent
- **Description:** The show where any talent is fair game. Sing, dance, juggle, or do something totally wild. The judges score your performance, and the weirder it is, the better.

### Crimes & Clues
- **Description:** Borrow Sherlock Holmes' hat and solve some of the trickiest riddles with Google by your side.

---

## Technical Events

### Code Mutants
- **Description:** Show off your programming skills, work as quickly as you can, beat the clock, and solve as many problems as you can. This competitive coding contest will challenge your speed and accuracy.

### Tech Hunt
- **Description:** Embark on a coding adventure where solving challenges unlocks the next code quest. Maneuver through a series of challenges, strategize your moves, and race to crack the final coding treasure.

### Anweshan
- **Description:** Present research on how to use the latest technology to solve current societal issues and create a huge breakthrough.

---

## Informal Events

### Prom Night
- **Description:** Get those dashing shirts and elegant dresses out and groove to your favorite romances with your partners. An iconic experience you have to try at least once.

### Shailee
- **Description:** A golden chance to prove that the pen is mightier than the sword by participating in various online competitions like spelling bee, creative writing, crosswords, shayris, and innovative workshops.

### Quiz-a-Palooza
- **Description:** Work up those gray cells and test your logic and knowledge with quizzes that satisfy your interests, drawing participants from some of the best universities in India.
