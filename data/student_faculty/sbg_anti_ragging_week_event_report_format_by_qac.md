## Event Details

- **Event Title:** Anti-Ragging Day & Week 2025

- **Event Type (Sports/Cultural/Technical etc.)  (Level: Inter/Intra University/State/National etc.):** Awareness Event (Intra Level)

- **Short Event Description:** In accordance with the guidelines of the University Grants Commission (UGC) and as part of the ongoing commitment to maintain a safe, inclusive, and ragging‐free environment, DAU celebrated Anti-Ragging Day & Week with the following: a) Orientation for Undergraduate Students The day began with a dedicated Orientation Session for newly admitted undergraduate students. The session was addressed by the Dean (Students), who emphasized the university’s strict zero‐tolerance policy towards ragging and highlighted the importance of maintaining mutual respect, harmony, and inclusivity among students. b) Screening of Informative Videos Following the address, informative videos on the theme of anti‐ragging were screened. These videos sensitized students about: The legal provisions and punishments related to ragging. The rights and responsibilities of students. Support mechanisms and grievance redressal systems are available in the university. c) Awareness through Selfie‐Stand Activity To further promote awareness in an interactive and engaging manner, a themed selfie‐stand was installed. Students enthusiastically participated by clicking photographs and sharing them, reinforcing the message of staying vigilant and committed to a ragging‐free campus. d) Information on Committees & Vigilance Squads Students were informed about the constitution of: Anti‐Ragging Committees Anti‐Ragging Vigilance Squads These bodies, constituted by the competent authorities, comprise faculty members, administrative staff, and student representatives. Their role is to monitor studentinteractions, handle complaints promptly, and ensure the safety and well‐being of all.

- **Event Date(s) and Time  (for multiday events, mention  From and To):** Anti‐Ragging Day (12th August 2025) Anti‐Ragging Week (12th – 18th August 2025)

- **Venue:** DAU Campus

- **Name of DAU Host / Coordinator(s)/Organizer(s):** Dhirubhai Ambani University

- **Participant’s Profile  (Students, Senior Students, Faculty  Members, Researchers, Industry experts – internal and  external  etc.):** Students, faculty members & administrative staff.
