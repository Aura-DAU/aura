# Sports Committee Event Reports (2025-26)

This document contains the official event reports for cultural, sports, and other activities organized by the Sports Committee of Dhirubhai Ambani University (formerly DA-IICT) for the academic year 2025-26.

---

## 1. DVL (DAU Volleyball League)
* **Event Type**: Sports Event
* **Level**: Intra University
* **Description**: The tournament began with prospective club owners placing bids to secure ownership of teams. The highest bidders were awarded their respective teams and proceeded to the player auction. During the auction, team owners used their allocated points to bid on players from a pool. The eight teams were then divided into two groups of four teams each. Within each group, teams competed against one another in a round-robin format, ensuring that every team had the opportunity to play multiple matches.
* **Dates**: From 1st February 2026 to 22nd February 2026
* **Venue**: Volleyball court, SAC ground
* **Organizer / Host**: Sports Committee
* **Participant Profile**: Students from B.Tech and Masters programs
* **Total Number of Participants**: 80
* **Organized & Funded By**: Sports Committee
* **Report Prepared By**: Sports Committee

## 2. DCL (DAU Cricket League)
* **Event Type**: Sports Event
* **Level**: Intra University
* **Description**: The tournament commenced with a team ownership bidding process, where interested club owners placed bids to acquire teams. The highest bidders secured ownership and moved forward to the player auction. During the auction, team owners were allocated a fixed number of points to bid on players from a pool consisting of students from across the university. The eight teams were then divided into two groups of four teams each. Within each group, teams competed against one another in a round-robin format, ensuring that every team played multiple matches. Throughout the tournament, both participants and spectators contributed to an energetic and lively atmosphere.
* **Dates**: From 31st January 2026 to 22nd February 2026
* **Venue**: SAC ground
* **Organizer / Host**: Sports Committee
* **Participant Profile**: Students from B.Tech, Masters, and PhD programs
* **Total Number of Participants**: Not Specified (Blank in report)
* **Organized & Funded By**: Sports Committee
* **Report Prepared By**: Sports Committee

## 3. DAU Badminton League (DBL)
* **Event Type**: Sports Event
* **Level**: Intra-University
* **Description**: The DAU Badminton League (DBL) was conducted with a total of 12 teams. The tournament followed a round-robin format in four groups of three teams each, after which the top teams advanced to the knockout stage. The event ensured competitive matches, fair play, and active participation from students across programs, promoting sportsmanship and teamwork. The tournament witnessed enthusiastic participation from students across B.Tech, Masters, and PhD programs.

## 4. CONCOURS 2025
* **Event Type**: Sports Event
* **Level**: Inter-College / National Level
* **Description**: CONCOURS 2025 was a four-day national-level inter-college sports event hosted by Dhirubhai Ambani University. More than 10+ sports tournaments were conducted, promoting athletic excellence, team spirit, and inter-institutional engagement. Colleges from across India participated, making it one of the largest student-driven sports events of the year.
* **Dates**: From 6th November 2025 to 9th November 2025 (Daily 9:00 AM – 2:00 AM). Note: Cricket tournament matches started earlier from 3rd of November.
* **Venue**: Dhirubhai Ambani University
* **Organizer / Host**: Sports Committee 25-26, DAU
* **Participant Profile**: Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) students from multiple colleges across India
* **Total Number of Participants**: Approx. 3000 Students from Multiple Colleges + all DAU Students
* **Organized & Funded By**: Sports Committee 25-26, DAU
* **Report Prepared By**: Sports Committee 25-26, DAU
* **Report Copied To**:
  * Dean (Students) (Mandatory)
  * IQAC Director (Mandatory)
