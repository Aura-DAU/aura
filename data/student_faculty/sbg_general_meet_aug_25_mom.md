# SBG General Meet Aug 25 MoM

SBG General Meeting [August 2025]

Minutes of the Meeting

Date: 04/08/2025

Time: 6:30 PM onwards

Location: LT-3

Attendees:

● SBG Core

● All committee members, IEEE SB, Sambhav, DCEI and all club cores

● General students as audience

Agendas:

### 1. MSTC :
 To increase 1 core seat of the club.

### 2. Formation of new club :

 Reader’s society.

### 3. To increase 3 open selection seats in Annual Festival Committee

### 1. MSTC :
 To increase 1 core seat of the club.

A proposal was put forth to increase the number of core committee seats for MSTC from 6 to 7,

citing increased workload and uneven work distribution during flagship events.The members

discussed whether regular club members could take on some of these responsibilities instead of

adding an additional core member. The club representatives clarified that while general

members are capable of handling the tasks, core members are selected for their higher level of

responsibility and commitment, which is crucial for the successful execution of major events.

### Conclusion:
 The agenda was not approved as it failed to reach a passing majority (two thirds

for seat-increasing agendas).

### 2. Formation of new club :

 Reader’s society.

The Reader's Society expressed interest in coming under the Student Body Governance (SBG)

to gain formal recognition, which would help them reach a broader student base.It was

discussed that, upon recognition, the club would operate independently of the Resource Center

(RC) and would be required to adhere to SBG rules regarding room bookings, event approvals,

and other protocols.The club also mentioned that, if formed, they would not request a budget

allocation or voting rights during the first year of its formation.

### Conclusion:
 The agenda was approved, as it reached a passing majority (50%).

### 3. To increase 3 open selection seats in the Annual Festival Committee.

A proposal was presented highlighting the growing workload within the committee, which is

disproportionate to the current number of core members.It was noted that although associate

members are part of the committee, they face challenges such as limited responsibilities and a

lack of formal recognition within the administration.The committee acknowledged that several

associate members have contributed at a level comparable to core members. As a result, it was

proposed that the number of core seats be increased to better reflect the current workload and

contributions.

### Conclusion:
 The agenda was approved, as it reached a passing majority (two thirds for

seat-increasing agendas).

### 4. General Student Concerns (Non Voting Agenda)

Students raised various student concerns.

### The major ones were as follows:

### 1. ACs in Classrooms:

 It was discussed that classrooms of big size, especially CEP 110,

CEP 102, LT 2 and LT 3 require Air Conditioning, as the atmosphere, especially during

humid days year round, gets very uncomfortable, and negatively impacts the

teaching-learning environment.

### 2. Lab Infrastructure:

 It was discussed that the lab infrastructure has become really

outdated, with a lot of equipment not working properly. Moreover, the state of the

General Lab only has 1 or 2 PCs working. EVD Second Year Students also do not have

access to sufficient soldering equipment which needs to be looked upon immediately.

Moreover, more licenses of Cadence software also need to be made available.

### 3. Speakers in Classrooms:

 It was briefly discussed that a lot of classrooms do not have

good quality speakers and this hampers with the teaching learning process, as well.

### 4. Club Rooms:

 It was discussed that clubs are facing significant challenges without club

rooms. This includes but isn’t limited to: no space to practice, no space or limited shared

space to store inventory, problems sharing rooms (eg. Radio and EHC), etc.

### 5. Gym Infrastructure:

 It was raised that the Gym Infrastructure is really outdated, with a

majority of the machines not working, and the Gym requires a complete rehaul to be

usable.

### 6. Hostel Renovations:

 The top floors of Boys Hostel, as well as the entirety of Girls Hostel

are in the need of a major refurbishment. There are leakage problems with top floors, as

well as cracks, old tiles, etc.

The SBG Representatives ensured that these points will be raised with relevant authorities. The

SBG also suggested the students to be more proactive in raising their concerns. Students were

made aware of the Grievance Redressal Mechanism and the levels of Grievance Redressal. It

was suggested to the students that they make use of this mechanism as and when necessary.
