## CTF Training Session by Cyberpedia

A CTF Training Session was conducted in collaboration with Cyberpedia to help students learn the basics of Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions and improve their cybersecurity skills. The session was led by experts who shared real-world insights and guided participants through hands-on challenges using Kali Linux. It served as great preparation for the upcoming i.Fest CTF competition.

Club/Committee: CINS Club

Participation: 75

Date: 13/10/2025

## Mosaic for Early Diwali Celebration

A vibrant Rangoli-themed peacock mosaic was created by the Cubing Club and DAU students to celebrate the spirit of Diwali in a unique and creative way. Made entirely using Rubik’s Cubes, this colorful artwork beautifully blended tradition with innovation, marking an early festive celebration filled with teamwork, art, and cubing enthusiasm.

Club/Committee: Cubing Club

Participation: 40

Date: 11/10/2025

## DAU Gandhinagar Cube Open 2025

Cubing Club organized DAU Gandhinagar Cube Open 2025, a WCA official speedcubing competition held at DAU. Sponsored by Cubelelo, this flagship event featured five events - 3x3, 2x2, Pyraminx, Megaminx, and 4x4. The full-day competition brought together cubers of all ages, including several young participants, making it a vibrant and inclusive celebration of cubing. As this was a non-institutional event (not under SBG budget), all competition expenses and budget were managed entirely through sponsor support and participant registration fees. Winners received certificates and prizes sponsored by Cubelelo, while all participants were awarded digital participation certificates for their enthusiasm and performance.

Club/Committee: Cubing Club

Participation: 71

Date: 12/10/2025

## A Kafkaesque Evening

The Readers’ Society organized A Kafkaesque Evening, a literary discussion on Franz Kafka’s short stories The Judgment and A Hunger Artist. The session opened with a brief look at Kafka’s life and the anxieties that shaped his writing, followed by an engaging discussion on themes of guilt, isolation, and absurdity. Welcoming both avid readers and newcomers, the event explored what it truly means to call something Kafkaesque.

Club/Committee: Readers’ Society

Participation: 26

Date: 30/10/2025
