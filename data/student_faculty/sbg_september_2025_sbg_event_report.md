# September 2025 SBG event report

Dhirubhai Ambani University

Student Body Government (SBG)

SBG activities report

SEPTEMBER 2025

## Introduction to Competitive Programming

The session introduced students to the world of Competitive Programming (CP), how to start the CP journey, and the fundamental concepts required. The event included interactive discussions and basic problem-solving activities to engage participants and build their interest in CP.

Club/Committee: Programming Club

Participation: 150+

Date: 01/09/2025

## Garba Night

Garba Night brought together enthusiastic participants, some seeking relaxation after first in semester exams and others dancing with full energy, making it a joyful and memorable evening for all.

Club/Committee: Khelaiya Club

Participation: 200-230

Date: 02/09/2025

## Cube Solving Workshop

In this workshop, club members taught the participants how to solve 3x3, 4x4, and 3x3 (One Handed) from scratch. For those who already knew the basics, advanced methods were taught to them so that they can get faster at solving cube.

Club/Committee: Cubing Club

Participation: 200

Date: 08/09/2025

## Freshers’ GD

The event was a group discussion event for btech 1st year (2025 batch) as an introduction to the debating society.

Club/Committee: The Debating Society

Participation: 35

Date: 02/09/2025

## Integration Bee

An integration bee event where participants go through multiple rounds of integration questions of increasing difficulty with the final round being a 1v1 battle where they solve the same integral side by side to see who's fastest.

Club/Committee: AI Club

Participation: 124

Date: 03/09/2025

## Gamthi Mela & Swar Sandhya

Gamthi Mela: The event featured vibrant food stalls and a handmade jewellery shop, showcasing local flavours and craftsmanship.

Swar Sandhya: A soulful musical evening presented by three talented local singers, creating an engaging and melodious experience for the audience.

Club/Committee: Heritage Club

Date: 03/09/2025

## Vaani-The Open Mic

An Open Mic Event where contestants were encouraged and invited to present their own poetries,shayaris, monologues, good content or stories. This was an attempt to increase creative skills of writing and presenting.

Club/Committee: Radio Club

Participation: 10 and approximately 50 as audience members

Date: 09/09/2025

## Bug Bounty Webinar

The Bug Bounty Workshop was a 2-hour interactive session featuring guest speaker Jai Niresh J, a renowned Bug Bounty Hunter. The workshop was divided into two insightful phases. In the first phase, attendees were introduced to the fundamentals of bug bounty hunting, including how to effectively choose and analyze a target. The second phase offered a hands-on experience, where participants gained practical exposure to real-world bug bounty techniques, guided by Jai Niresh J using his specialized tools and methodologies. The session not only provided valuable knowledge but also gave participants a practical understanding of how bug bounty hunting works in

real-life scenarios.

Club/Committee: Cyber Information and Network Security Club

Participation: 22

Date: 11/09/2025

## ARTBIZZ 2025

Artbizz is an annual doodling competition aimed towards freshers, to introduce the MUSE club. There is no fixed format of the submission – the participant can submit either a digital or a physical doodle.

Club/Committee: MUSE-The Design Club

Participation: 12

Event launch : 29/08/2025

Submissions closed: 5/09/2025

Results announced: 6/09/2025

## IPC 1

These short-duration contests, each spanning 2 hours and comprising 5 problems, are designed to foster a healthy and competitive coding environment within the college. The primary objectives are to enhance students’ problem-solving abilities and strengthen their knowledge of Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), while also preparing them for global online competitions on popular platforms such as Codeforces and CodeChef.

Club/Committee: Programming Club

Participation: 90+

Date: 11/09/2025

## Freshers Debate

Introduction to Asian Parliamentary Debates: A special session was conducted for first-year students to familiarize them with the Asian Parliamentary Debate format. This was followed by four debates organized in the same format, allowing students to gain practical exposure and develop confidence in structured debating.

Club/Committee: The Debating Society

Participation: 28

Date: 11/09/2025

## Garba Workshop

A two-day Garba Workshop was organized around ten days prior to Navratri. Professional instructors were invited to guide participants through a variety of trendy and engaging steps, starting from the basics. The workshop was marked by high energy, enthusiasm, and active participation, leaving students well-prepared and excited to celebrate the upcoming Navratri festivities.

Club/Committee: Khelaiya Club

Participation: Around 90 on both the days.

Date: 11/09/2025 and 12/09/2025

## Intra-DA Cubing & Sudoku Competition

Many students enthusiastically participated in the Intra-Institute Cubing and Sudoku Competition, which comprised five events: 3x3x3 (Freshers Only), 3x3x3 (Common), Pyraminx, 2x2x2, and Sudoku. Among these, the 3x3x3 Freshers, 3x3x3 Common, Pyraminx, and Sudoku events were prize-winning categories, while the 2x2x2 event was conducted as a non-prized competition.

Club/Committee: Cubing Club

Participation: 77

Date: 16/09/2025

## The Vices Quiz

A quiz competition featuring creative and thought-provoking questions was organized, receiving enthusiastic participation from students and concluding with prizes for the winners.

Club/Committee: Headrush (The quizzing club)

Participation: 70-80

Date: 15/09/2025

## An evening of Thrillers

A session on the thriller genre was recently hosted. In preparation for the event, PDFs of five short stories were shared with participants a week in advance. During the session, a literary analysis of all five stories was conducted, covering elements such as the plot, characters, themes, writing style, and the era in which each story was written.

Club/Committee: Readers’ Society

Participation: 29

Date: 15/09/2025.

## Introduction to C Programming

A friendly “Introduction to C Programming” session for the B.tech 2025 batch was held. The session covered core topics such as data types, loops, conditional statements, operator precedence, arrays, and functions. It aimed to build strong foundational programming skills in a relaxed and supportive environment, help students prepare for upcoming exams, and inspire interest in competitive coding and problem-solving.

Club/Committee: Programming Club

Participation: 90+

Date: 17/09/2025

## FujiFilm Workshop

The Fujifilm Workshop, led by Shadab Sir and his team, offered photography enthusiasts an immersive learning experience. Participants explored photography and videography fundamentals through presentations, demonstrations, and hands-on practice. They got to try a wide range of Fujifilm cameras and lenses, including the Fujinon 150–600mm and the professional GFX series. The workshop combined conceptual learning with practical exercises, giving attendees a well-rounded understanding of creative techniques and advanced imaging technologies.

Club/Committee: Photography and Movie Making Club

Participation: 113

Date: 17/09/2025

## Arduino Session + Workshop

An Introduction to Arduino session followed by a live hands-on workshop was conducted, where students were introduced to Arduino basics, its real-world applications, and practiced building simple projects.

Club/Committee: Electronics Hobby Club

Participation: 147

Date: 18/09/2025 (Session) and 19/09/2025 (Workshop)

## Fanfiction Writing Competition

An online Fanfiction Writing Competition was organized by the Press Club in collaboration with the Readers’ Society. Submissions consisted of fanfiction based on participants’ favorite media and were evaluated and ranked according to various criteria.

Club/Committee: Press Club in collaboration with Readers’ Society

Date: 08/09/2025 to 28/09/2025 (online)

Club/Committee: Chess Club

## INDGenius AI Summit

The INDGenius AI Summit 2025 was a one-day event that explored the transformative impact of Artificial Intelligence across fields such as Generative AI and Cybersecurity. Featuring expert talks, panel discussions, and interactive sessions, the summit aimed to spark meaningful conversations around India’s AI roadmap and its global relevance.

Club/Committee: IEEE Student Branch

Participation: Approximately 250

Date: 14/09/2025

## Bootcamps : ML edition

The Bootcamps: Machine Learning Edition provided students with a practical, hands-on learning experience in AI and Machine Learning. Over two weekends, participants attended sessions on MATLAB fundamentals, AI, and deep learning. The first weekend featured a speaker from MATLAB, while the second had a speaker from L&T Technology Services, giving students exposure to real-world tools, workflows, and industry insights.

Club/Committee: IEEE Student Branch

Participation: Approximately 200

Date: 06/09/2025 - 07/09/2025 and 13/09/2025 - 14/09/2025

## Hackout’25

HackOut’25 was a 48-hour innovation hackathon that brought together students from across the disciplines to design, develop, and demonstrate solutions under the theme: “Green Hydrogen and Blue Carbon Ecosystem.” The event became one of Gujarat’s largest sustainability hackathons,showcasing how young innovators can apply emerging technologies - AI, blockchain,IoT, DevOps, and geospatial systems -  to pressing climate and energy challenges.

Club/Committee: Annual Festival Committee

Participation:400+ student teams (1000+ participants)

Date: 29/08/2025 to 31/08/2025

## Tarang 2025

Tarang 2025 featured vibrant performances over two days. On the first day, Rishikesh Gadhvi performed, followed by DA no Dayro, a cultural session of music and storytelling capturing the essence of Gujarati tradition. On the second day, Ishani Dave took the stage, and Mandli Garba continued the Garba and Raas celebrations throughout the night.

Club/Committee:Cultural Committee

Participation: Approximately 3300

Date: 30/09/2025 and 01/10/2025
