## Event Details

- **Event Title:** Internal Hackathon  (Under Smart India Hackathon – SIH’25)

- **Event Type (Sports/Cultural/Technical etc.)  (Level: Inter/Intra University/State/National etc.):** Hackathon (Intra Level)

- **Short Event Description:** An Internal Hackathon for SIH 2025 was successfully organized at Dhirubhai Ambani University formerly known as DA-IICT by the Office of Dean (Students) with the assistance of Student Body Government (SBG) on 20th September 2025 at CEP Block. The event brought together bright young minds from B. Tech and M. Tech programs, working passionately to design solutions for real-world challenges sourced from the official SIH portal. Students explored both software and hardware tracks, showcasing their creativity, technical acumen, and teamwork.

- **Event Date(s) and Time  (for multiday events, mention  From and To):** 20th September 2025  From 10:00 am. to 1:00 pm.

- **Venue:** CEP Block – Room No. 106

- **Name of DAU Host / Coordinator(s)/Organizer(s):** Dhirubhai Ambani University

- **Participant’s Profile  (Students, Senior Students, Faculty  Members, Researchers, Industry experts – internal and  external  etc.):** Students

- **Total number of participants:** 90

- **Organized/Funded by:** Under the supervision of the Dean (Students) and with active participation from the Student Body Government (SBG)

- **Report Prepared by:** Dean (Students) Office
