# Guidelines for Indents

Guidelines for Indents

Procedure for Clearance Indents as per the SBG Constitution:

(a) The student convener/member of the respective Club or Committee will collect all the bills

after the event.

(b) The student convener/member of the respective Club or Committee will get the bills

approved from the faculty mentor of the particular Club or Committee.

(c) The student convener/member of the respective Club or Committee will submit the indent to

the SBG Treasurer for approval.

(d) The SBG Treasurer will get the clearance or settlement approved by the Chief Accounts

Officer and the Executive Registrar.

General Guidelines:

(a) The indent for an event needs to be submitted within 7 days after the event ends, any delays

should be addressed to the treasurer via mail with valid reason, or else there might be

consequences for late submission.

(b) Attach the original bills/payment screenshot in the sequence in which they are mentioned in

the table (in the indent form).

(c) In case of cash dealings, DO NOT forget to take the bill or receipt from the vendor that

proves the purchase, the money won’t be reimbursed without the bills/receipts.

(d) Print the pages on BOTH sides in hard copy to avoid wastage of the paper.

(e) Do not forget to attach the cancelled cheque of the bank account of the person in which the

amount will be deposited.

(f) Attach the printed pages in a neat stack with a single stapler pin.
