# Sports Committee

Dear Students,

The Sports Committee is a student-led body dedicated to promoting sports, fitness, and a healthy lifestyle across the Dhirubhai Ambani University campus. We organize sporting events, maintain sports facilities, manage equipment, and assist in the selection of university teams. Whether you are an experienced athlete or a beginner looking to stay active, the Sports Committee provides opportunities for everyone to participate and grow.

---

## Sports Facilities

The university provides modern sports infrastructure to encourage students to participate in various sports and recreational activities. Our facilities include:

* Badminton Courts
* Basketball Courts
* Table Tennis Courts
* Volleyball Courts
* Football Ground
* Cricket Ground with Two Practice Nets
* Fully Equipped Gymnasium

Students are encouraged to make full use of these facilities while following the university's rules and maintaining the equipment responsibly.

## Sports Events & Tournaments

The Sports Committee organizes various sporting events throughout the academic year, including:

### Concours
Concours is Dhirubhai Ambani University's flagship national-level sports festival, bringing together students from universities across the country. The event features competitions in multiple sports, promoting excellence, teamwork, and sportsmanship while providing a platform for athletes to showcase their talent.

### DAU Cricket League (DCL)
The DAU Cricket League is the university's premier intra-campus cricket tournament. Teams are formed through an auction, and players compete in a league format followed by knockout rounds, encouraging competitive cricket and team spirit.

### DAU Volleyball League (DVL)
The DAU Volleyball League is an exciting campus volleyball tournament where mixed teams compete in a league-cum-knockout format. The event promotes teamwork, strategy, and healthy competition among students.

### DAU Badminton League (DBL)
The DAU Badminton League provides students with an opportunity to compete in singles and doubles badminton matches. Teams are formed through an auction, making the tournament competitive and engaging for participants of all skill levels.

### Inter-Wing Tournament
The Inter-Wing Tournament is a hostel-based sporting competition where different hostel wings compete across various sports. The event strengthens friendships, teamwork, and healthy competition among residents.

### Freshers' Weekend
Freshers' Weekend includes a variety of fun sporting activities and friendly matches designed especially for first-year students. The event helps new students interact with their peers, explore different sports, and become part of the university's sporting culture.

### Faculty & Staff Sports Day
Faculty & Staff Sports Day is an annual event organized exclusively for university faculty and staff. It encourages physical fitness, teamwork, and interaction through friendly competitions in multiple indoor and outdoor sports.

### Inter-University Tournaments
Dhirubhai Ambani University regularly participates in inter-university tournaments organized by other institutions.

## Practice Sessions

Regular practice sessions are conducted for all university sports teams to help athletes improve their skills, maintain fitness, and prepare for inter-university competitions. Practice schedules vary depending on the sport, availability of facilities, and upcoming tournaments.

Each team's practice sessions are planned and coordinated by the respective **team captain** in consultation with the **Sports Committee** and coach (where applicable). Team members are informed about practice timings and venue through official communication channels such as WhatsApp groups and email.

Students selected for university teams are expected to attend practice sessions regularly and maintain discipline, punctuality, and commitment throughout the training period. Regular participation in practice is essential for team selection and tournament preparation.

---

## Coaches & Support Staff

The Sports Committee is committed to providing the necessary guidance and support to all university sports teams.

### Coaches
Qualified coaches are appointed during major tournaments and important competitions to train, mentor, and prepare university teams. They assist athletes in improving their technical skills, fitness, game strategies, and overall performance, ensuring they are well-prepared to represent Dhirubhai Ambani University.

### Support Staff
A dedicated support staff works throughout the year to ensure that sports facilities and equipment are maintained in excellent condition. The support team includes:
* **Sports Officer**: Oversees and manages all sports-related activities, including sports facilities, tournaments, team selections, equipment management, and coordination with coaches and the Sports Committee.
* **Sports Inventory Officer**: Manages the issue, return, maintenance, and inventory of all sports equipment.
* **Ground Maintenance Staff**: Responsible for the upkeep and maintenance of sports grounds, courts, and playing fields.
* **Badminton Court Maintenance Staff**: Ensures that the badminton courts are cleaned, maintained, and ready for regular practice sessions and tournaments.

Together, the coaches and support staff play a vital role in providing a safe, well-maintained, and professional sporting environment for all students.
