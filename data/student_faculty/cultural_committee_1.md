# Cultural Committee — DAU Overview

## About Cultural Committee

The Cultural Committee organizes different festivals and cultural events in DAU, bringing life and energy to the campus throughout the year. We aim to create a dynamic and welcoming atmosphere where every student feels included and encouraged to participate. We provide opportunities for creativity and expression, enriching the student's experiences at DAU.

---

## Our Role

### Major OAT Events

#### Teachers' Day Celebration
A heartfelt celebration dedicated to our teachers, featuring a range of student performances including dance, music, band performances, drama, and other cultural acts to express gratitude and appreciation.

#### Waltz
An exciting intra-college dance competition that celebrates grace, creativity, and talent. Participants can showcase their skills across multiple categories, including solo performances, duets, group performances, and thrilling face-offs.

#### Raaga & Rhyme
A vibrant intra-college cultural competition that brings together music, dance, and literary arts. The event features a variety of categories such as solo singing, duets, band performances, and poetry, providing a platform for every artistic talent to shine.

#### Bollywood Day
Step into the shoes of your favorite Bollywood character and light up the stage with style and confidence. Participants dress up as iconic characters and take part in a ramp walk competition, with awards presented for the most creative and captivating costumes.

#### Manière
A dynamic group fashion show competition that emphasizes creativity, coordination, and teamwork. Teams present thematic performances through fashion and choreography, creating a spectacular showcase of style and storytelling.

## Traditions & Festivities

### Ganesh Chaturthi
One of the most celebrated festivals on campus, Ganesh Chaturthi is observed with immense devotion and enthusiasm. From the grand and energetic arrival of Lord Ganesha to the equally spectacular visarjan celebrations, the festival beautifully blends spirituality with festivity.

### Navratri
A vibrant two-day celebration filled with music, dance, and festive spirit. With renowned artists performing on campus and students coming together to celebrate, Navratri creates an unforgettable atmosphere of energy and excitement.

### Makar Sankranti
A festival that fills both the skies and hearts with joy. The campus comes alive with colorful kites soaring high, while traditional sweets add to the warmth and celebration of the occasion.

### Holi
Celebrate the festival of colours by spreading joy, laughter, and togetherness. Holi transforms the campus into a vibrant canvas, creating memories filled with colour, music, and friendship.

### Janmashtami
Experience the spirit of Janmashtami through exciting celebrations such as the traditional Matki Phod competition and the much-awaited energetic Mud Holi, making it one of the most thrilling festivals on campus.

### And Many More...
The festive spirit continues throughout the year with celebrations of various festivals and occasions, including Diwali, Dussehra, Halloween, Eid, and many more, ensuring that there is always something to look forward to on campus.

## The Freshers' Experience

### Cultural Weekend
A fun-filled and exciting weekend designed especially for freshers to help them bond with their batchmates and interact with seniors. Packed with engaging activities, interactive sessions, and entertaining games, Cultural Weekend creates the perfect opportunity to build friendships, create memories, and win exciting prizes.

### Treasure Hunt
One of the most thrilling and memorable experiences for freshers, the Treasure Hunt keeps participants engaged throughout the night with clues hidden across different parts of the campus. Conducted in teams, this event encourages teamwork, problem-solving, and exploration, while giving students the chance to connect with their batchmates, interact with seniors, and become familiar with the campus in a fun and adventurous way.

## Our Biggest and Brightest Celebration

### TARANG — The Spirit of Navratri

Tarang, our grand two-day Navratri festival, is one of the most awaited and celebrated events of the academic year. A vibrant celebration of Gujarat's rich culture, heritage, and rhythmic traditions, Tarang brings together students, faculty members, and participants from colleges across the region to celebrate the spirit of unity, friendship, and festivity.

The campus transforms into a spectacular cultural arena, filled with traditional attire, energetic Garba and Dandiya performances, music, and unforgettable memories. Beyond being a festival, Tarang serves as a platform for students to connect, showcase their talent, and experience the true essence of community and celebration.

One of the major highlights of Tarang is the presence of renowned artists who elevate the festive atmosphere with their electrifying performances, making each edition a memorable experience for everyone involved.

- **Tarang '25** featured celebrated artists **Rishikesh Gadhvi** and **Ishani Dave**, creating an unforgettable musical experience for the audience.
- **Tarang '24** witnessed mesmerizing performances by **Mihir Jani** and **Rishikesh Gadhvi**, further adding to the legacy of this iconic festival.

With thousands of students coming together to celebrate tradition, culture, and togetherness, Tarang continues to be more than just a festival — it is an experience, a tradition, and a memory that every student cherishes.
