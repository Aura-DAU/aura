# ITSS Workshop at DA-IICT

# We welcome the eminent Speakers and the guests to the inaugural event of the IEEE ITS Society Chapter jointly organized by ITSS Gujarat Chapter and DA-IICT.

# Theme : Innovation in Motion - Shaping the future of Transportation in Developing Countries

# Date : 16 March 2024

# Time : 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Join us for a day filled with insightful discussions and networking opportunities, bringing together academia, industries, and professionals in the field of Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)

# Our distinguished speakers include :

Prof. Digvijay Sampatrao Pawar , IIT Hyderabad
