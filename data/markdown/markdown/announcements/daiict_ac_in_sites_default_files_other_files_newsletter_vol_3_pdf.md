Obituary- Professor Suman Mitra

DA-IICT community condole the sad demise of Professor Suman Mitra, who passed
away on April 18, 2021, after a brief illness. Professor Mitra has been with the
Institute almost since its inception. Professor Suman Mitra was the Dean of the
Academic Program from 2013 to 2020. He was a brilliant teacher, an outstanding
researcher and an epitome of kindness. He was always compassionate and gentle.

Professor Mitra will forever be a part of the countless futures he helped create at
DA-IICT. His students and his colleagues at the Institute will forever miss his guidance

and reassuring presence.

CENTRE OF EXCELLENCE AWARD

The Government of Gujarat has chosen DA-IICT for Centre of Excellence (CoE) award. The selection
was made after a series of verification and assessment of overall achievements of the Institute by the
government appointed expert committees. The process of awarding the status is expected to be
completed within the next few months.

CONFERENCE

IEEE Gujarat Section, IEEE Signal Processing Society (IEEE SPS), Gujarat Chapter along with DA-IICT
organised the Fourth Edition of the three day National Seminar on ‘New Trends in Signal Processing
(NeTSiIP 2021) online during March 19-21, 2021. The seminar featured talks by eminent researchers
in Signal Processing and Artificial Intelligence covering the sub-fields of speech, image, audio and
video processing, biometrics, media security, bioinformatics, language processing and
communications. The seminar provided a forum for participants to learn new developments in Signal
Processing and Artificial Intelligence.

AILP LECTURES

The faculty research group on Artificial Intelligence and Language Processing (AILP) is involved in
collaborative research in the areas of artificial intelligence, machine learning, image processing,
pattern recognition, computer vision, information retrieval and natural language processing. The
objective is to create a vibrant research environment in DA-IICT and the AILP community at large and
to undertake various research and dissemination activities and provide research opportunities to
young Scholars. The AILP Lectures are part of this initiative.
