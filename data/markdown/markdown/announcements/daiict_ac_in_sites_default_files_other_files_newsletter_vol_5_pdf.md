NEWSLETTER
JULY 22 - DECEMBER 22
Dear Friends & Well-wishers of DA-IICT Gandhinagar, 
It is my pleasure to present you with the December 
2022 edition of the Institute newsletter. Periodic dose 
and dollops of news and updates featuring everything 
that happened at DA-IICT and with DA-IICTians during 
the 2nd Quarter of the academic year 2022-23 covering 
the months of September, October, November and 
December. A lot has happened during the past four 
months, from Placements to Immersions, from Centre 
activities to festival Celebrations. 
Everything was in overdrive as if we were trying 
hard to make up for the over two years lost being 
away from our beautiful campus. The energy, the 
excitement, and the enthusiasm was palpable and 
infectious. This issue will give you glimpses of the 
achievements in the academic and research areas, 
alumni contribution, international and corporate 
relations.
Your comments and suggestions are welcome 
to make the next issue of the newsletter more 
interactive. 
Warm regards,
Prof K. S. Dasgupta
Director DA-IICT Gandhinagar

NEW FACULTY RECRUITMENTS
ALUMNI TALK 
The Alumni Association organized an alumni talk 
on 29th November, 2022. The title of the talk is 
“Revealing unseen opportunities with our alumni” 
Gauravi Dubey from (2001-2005 BTech) batch 
Currently working at Indian Revenue Service- 
Customs and Indirect Taxes., Irukulapati Naga from 
(2005-2009 BTech) batch currently working in at 
Ericsson Research and 5G patent holder, Indira Negi 
from (2003-2007 BTech) batch currently working at 
Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation and Kunal Gupta 
from (2015-2017 MDes) batch currently working at 
DX/UX Research at Intel Corporation shared their 
experiences. The talk was organized in hybrid mode. 
In the technological field, all of us are so focused 
on getting high packages that we have neglected 
to explore other opportunities available to us after 
college graduation. At this point, many of us have 
already been hired, while others are on the hunt for 
jobs. In this talk students are enlightened with the 
knowledge and opportunities after graduation. What 
career paths they took after graduation.
Dr. Jenson Joseph  joined the institute as an 
Assistant Professor in July 2022. He completed a PhD 
(Communication), from the University of Hyderabad. 
He has specialized in Media Theory, Film Studies, 
Cultural Studies, Malayalam Cinema, Popular Culture. 
Dr. Joseph has worked at Alexander von Humboldt as 
a Research Fellow. (Department of Gender and Media 
Studies for the South Asian Region, Institute for Asian 
and African Studies (IAAW)) Employment
Mr. Cyril Jos joined the institute as an Adjunct 
Faculty. He is pursuing his PhD at the Centre for 
Development Studies, Trivandrum, which is affiliated 
to the Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi. He has 
previously taught at St Xavier’s College, Thumba and 
The Asian School of Business in Trivandrum, Kerala. 
Mr. Jos has obtained his B.A Economics from St 
Xavier’s College, Mumbai and M.A and M.Phil Applied 
Economics degrees from the Centre for Development 
Studies. He is also the recipient of a UGC Junior 
Research Fellowship.
Dr. Gopinath Panda  joined the institute as an 
Assistant Professor in July 2022. He completed his 
PhD in (Mathematics) from IIT Bhubaneswar. He 
was an Adjunct Faculty in the College of Aeronautics 
at Florida Tech, Melbourne, USA, and a Voluntary 
Researcher in the Department of Electrical and 
Computer Engineering at The University of Central 
Florida. He has four years of Postdoctoral Research 
experience from various reputed institutions.

CONCOURS
i.FEST
Concours is the annual national Sports Fest of the 
Institute, and entered its 12th edition this year. The 
central idea of the fest is to ingrain vigour and honour 
in the students - the most important characteristics 
of a winner. As always, Concours facilitated healthy 
competition among the students in nine different 
sports – Badminton, Basketball, Chess, Carroms, 
Cricket, Football, Lawn Tennis, Table Tennis and 
Volleyball – and also gave the students an opportunity 
to explore other interests like music, quizzing and 
online gaming. It is a 4-day event, which was held 
on the 3rd—6th November. This year we witnessed 
participation from 35+ colleges from all over India. 
Here are some glimpses of it:
On the second weekend of November ‘22, with a 
footfall of more than 7000 participants across Gujarat, 
IEEE SB DAIICT organized one of Gujarat’s largest 
technical carnivals - i.Fest ‘22 Reality Reloaded ! With 
the energy of resurgence.
Team i.Fest ‘22 came forward to celebrate the revival 
of the real world with more than 20 technical and 
cultural events. With a hilarious Stand-Up Night 
with Chirayu Mistry, and quiz events like ‘iHunt’ and 
‘Roboclash’, the fest commenced with a great deal of 
enthusiasm on the first day. 
On the second day, with many other cultural events 
such as i.Clash and SellOut going on in parallel, 
coding events like ‘RepoReboot’, ‘i.Relay’ and 
‘Catch the flag’ (fixing bugs in websites) were held. 
‘CryptoTrade’,  an online trading event using dummy 
currencies, attracted participants in huge numbers. 
On the third day of the fest, various quiz events like 
i.Quiz, i.Ganith, Chess64, and ‘i.Bot’ took place. i.Ride 
(a slow-cycling event), Paintball and Treasure Hunt 
were some of the prominent fun events. Apart from 
all this, we had with us in The Open Box, Mr.Sandeep 
Jain (founder of ‘GeeksforGeeks’), Ms.Roopa 
Venkatakrishnan (Director at Sapient Wealth) and 
Mr.Ashris Choudhury (Creator, India in Pixels), who 
inspired folk to think about the future of technology, 
the importance of finances and the power of creativity. 
Mr. Rajeev Jyoti (Director, INSPACE Technical) also 
came to talk to us about the future of potential start-
ups in Space Science and Technology. 
A musical event by ‘When Chai Met Toast’ added 
glory to the fun-filled fest. The carnival ended with a 
joyful and heartwarming event – the ‘Drum Circle’ by 
Rujul Vora.

YOUTH RUN’22
FIGMA 101
BLOOD DONATION CAMP
Youth Run’22 was a 7km long marathon organized 
by the Synapse Committee as a pre-fest event to 
Synapse’23, aiming to create awareness about a 
suitable ‘Lifestyle for Environment’. We were glad to 
receive participation from the CRPF, SAI and health 
enthusiasts from Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar in 
large numbers. Here are some glimpses of 
Youth Run ‘22:
MUSE (the Design Club) hosted a session called 
Figma 101. The session was led by members of 
MUSE and aimed to introduce attendees to the basics 
of Figma, a popular design and prototyping tool. 
There were approximately 40 participants in 
attendance, all eager to learn more about Figma 
and how it could be used in their design work. The 
speakers began by providing a general overview of 
Figma and its capabilities, including its collaboration 
features, design tools, and prototyping capabilities. 
Throughout the session, attendees were given the 
opportunity to ask questions and try out Figma for 
themselves, with the speakers providing guidance 
and assistance as needed. The speakers also shared 
their own experiences using Figma in professional and 
personal projects, providing valuable insights and tips 
for attendees.
“Give the gift of life”: Sambhav club organized a 
Blood Donation Camp in association with the Civil 
Hospital, Ahmedabad on 17th November 2022. The 
donation was held in the Faculty mess, and donors 
were provided food and all the necessary care. A 
team from the Blood Bank was accompanied by two 
air-conditioned Mobile Vans to help with the donation.
