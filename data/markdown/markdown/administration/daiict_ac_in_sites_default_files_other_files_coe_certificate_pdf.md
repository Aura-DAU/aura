the High Level Committee, notifies the following Universities, as
“Centre of Excellence” for the period of three years from the date of
publication of this notification in the Official Gazette.

The status of the Center of Excellence shall be applicable only for the
courses as specified in clause (k) of section 2 of the Gujarat Professional
Technical Educational Colleges of Institutions (Regulation of Admission
and Fixation of Fees) Act, 2007 (Hereinafter referred to as “the said Act”)
and run by the constituent Institutions of the Universities within the State
of Gujarat.
