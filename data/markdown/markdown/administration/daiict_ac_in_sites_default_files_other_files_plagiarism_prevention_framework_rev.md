Policy Title
Academic Integrity Policy
Background

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology — DAIICT was established
in 2001 and was conferred university status in 2003. The DAIICT is included in the list of Universities
maintained by the University Grants Commission.

DAIICT is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

The National Assessment and Accreditation Council, Government of India has accredited DA-IICT with
an ‘A’ Grade in 2017.

DAIICT being a research-led University, the faculty, students, and research staff are active in research
in several academic disciplines. As a part of their research and publications, the faculty, students and
the research staff access and make use of the significant amount of information available in online
and offline mode. The ease of online access also poses a serious concern of unacknowledged use of
others’ intellectual work — plagiarism- which must be curbed.

DAIICT firmly believes that Academic integrity and ethical research are pivotal in ensuring fair play in
new knowledge creation and supports a policy as a means to achieve compliance.

This policy is framed in pursuance of the University Grants Commission’s (Promotion of Academic
Integrity and Prevention of Plagiarism in Higher Educational Institutions) Regulations, 2018 published
in the Gazette of India.

Policy Owner

This policy is owned by the Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology
referred to as ‘DAIICT’ hereafter.

Responsible Office

The Dean (Academic Program) will have the overall responsibility for creating, implementing and
updating the policy.

Approved By

This policy has been approved by the Board of Governors of DAIICT.

Scope of the Policy

This policy applies to all DAIICT faculty (full-time, adjunct, contractual), students, and research staff,
involved in research and publishing their respective work including external collaborators (institutions
and individuals) supporting DAIICT in its various research projects, hereafter referred to as ‘Authors’.

This policy also applies to all the students and research staff submitting their respective project work,
theses and dissertation as a part of their degree submission.

This policy provides supportive documentation subject to regular updates in compliance with the UGC
guidelines on plagiarism and academic integrity.

Hence, it is recommended that the Author completely and fully understand this policy to ensure that
academic integrity and research ethics are adhered to. It is expected that this policy will help the users
to follow the procedure to check and control the level of plagiarism and submit the plagiarism check
certificate (similarity report) wherever applicable.

Definitions
Under this policy, unless the context otherwise requires—

a) “Academic Integrity” is the intellectual honesty in proposing, performing, and reporting any
activity, which leads to the creation of intellectual property;

b) “Author” includes a student or a faculty or a researcher or staff of DAIICT who claims to be the
creator of the work under consideration;

c) “UGC” means the University Grants Commission as defined in the University Grants Commission
Act, 1956;

d) “Information" includes data, messages, text, images, sound, voice, codes, computer programs,
software, and databases or microfilm or computer-generated microfiche;

e) “Institutional Academic Integrity Panel” shall mean the body constituted at the Institutional level
to consider recommendations of the departmental academic integrity panel and take appropriate
decisions in respect of allegations of plagiarism and decide on penalties to be imposed. In
exceptional cases, it shall investigate allegations of plagiarism at the institutional level;

f) “Plagiarism” means the practice of taking someone else’s work or idea and passing them as one’s
own.

g) “Programme” means a programme of study leading to the award of a master’s and research-level
degree;

h) “Researcher” refers to a person conducting academic/scientific research in DAIICT;

i) “Script” includes a research paper, thesis, dissertation, chapters in books, full-fledged books, and
any other similar work, submitted for assessment/opinion leading to the award of master and
research level degrees or publication in print or electronic media by students or faculty or
researcher or staff of DAIICT; however, this shall exclude assignments/term papers/project
reports/course work/essays and answer scripts, etc.;

j) “Source” means the published primary and secondary material from any source whatsoever and

Policy Objectives

1. To create awareness about the significance of academic integrity in the conduct of research
and submission of the thesis, dissertation, and other research publications.

2. Toprevent academic misconduct including plagiarism in academic writing among students,
faculty, and research staff by providing timely information, technical support, and guidance.

3, Tostrengthen all existing institutional mechanisms to ensure responsible conduct of research.
To develop systems to check and deter plagiarism that is in compliance with the UGC
regulation.

Plagiarism prevention

a) DAIICT has implemented and enforced the technology-based mechanism using TURNITIN software
to ensure that documents such as a thesis, dissertation, publications, or any other such documents
are free of plagiarism at the time of their submission. All faculty have access to this software and
would make it available to their students whenever required.

b) The mechanism as defined in (a) above will make accessible to all engaged in research work
including M Tech, M Des, and PhD students, and research staff as suggested by the AIC.

c) Every student submitting a thesis, dissertation, or any other such documents to the DAIICT shall
submit an undertaking indicating that he or she has prepared the document that is his/her original
work and free of any plagiarism.

d) Such document/s must be uploaded on TURNITIN and get a similarity report

f) Each supervisor shall maintain a record of these similarity reports for each dissertation/thesis of
the students registered under him/her.

e) The Resource Centre will accept the final report which complies with AIC guidelines.

Similarity checks for exclusion from Plagiarism ™

The similarity checks for plagiarism shall exclude the following:

i. All quoted work reproduced with all necessary permission and/or attribution.
ii, Allreferences, bibliography, table of content, preface, and acknowledgments.
iii. All generic terms, laws, standard symbols, and standards equations.

Levels of Plagiarism ™

Plagiarism would be quantified into the following levels in ascending order of severity for the purpose
of its definition:

Level 0: Similarities upto 10% - Minor similarities, no penalty (Zero level)
Level 1: Similarities above 10% to 40% (Low level)

Level 2: Similarities above 40% to 60% (Mid-level)

Redress procedure upon plagiarism detection
Pre-submission phase

The supervisor will examine the similarity report of the to-be-submitted publication and if the level of
plagiarism is above level 1, the student will be counseled for taking appropriate measures including
re-writing and re-phrasing the contents which are shown to be similar to the existing document/s. The
process will be continued till the similarity report clears the to-be-submitted publication.
Post-submission phase

If any allegation of plagiarism is brought against a student post-submission by either examiners or

publishers or any other individual or agency, the matter will be referred to the AIC by the supervisor.

The AIC shall follow the principles of natural justice while deciding about the allegation of plagiarism
against the Author/s. The AIC shall submit its recommendations post the hearing keeping in view of
exclusions mentioned under the similarities check.

The redress process and the penalty will be dealt with on a case-to-case basis based on the merits of
the complaints. The AIC will look into the merits of the complaint and hear out both sides to resolve
the issues as and when required.

Penalties

Penalties in the cases of plagiarism shall be imposed on students pursuing studies at the level of
Masters and Research programs and on faculty & research staff of the DAIICT only after academic
misconduct on the part of the individual has been established without doubt, when all avenues of

appeal have been exhausted and individual in question has been provided with enough opportunity
to defend himself or herself in a fair or transparent manner before the AIC.

Penalties in case of plagiarism in the submission of thesis and dissertations
The Academic Integrity Committee (AIC) shall impose a penalty after due process and in accordance
