Policy Document On Environment and Energy Usage.

The Environment and Energy Usage Policy of “Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of
Infromation and Communication Technology, Gandhinagar” is to manage

energy usage in systematic way to minimize its impact on the environment.

The policy implies to explore the renewable energy resources like Solar Power
to find out substitute natural resources as solutions to the energy crisis. This
environment and energy policy is binding for all the components of the
institution and applies to all its users and to the various activities undertaken by
the institution. It will help us to ensure that the efficiency and environmental
awareness into our everyday activities, thus helping us to realize our
responsibilities and commitment to conservation of natural resources and to
limit its usage. The concern department an official platform devoted to the

cause of environmental awareness.

Policies:

e To reduce local air pollution emissions using environment-friendly vehicles

including E-Loaders, bicycles and use of pedestrian-friendly roads.

¢ To Install Solar Power Plant for the generation of alternate energy.

¢ Replacements of existing old bulbs with new energy efficient LED bulbs in the

phase manner and try to achieve complete campus to save energy.

* To Develop the systematic waste management mechanism for horticulture

waste and liquid waste.
¢ To Develop recharge wells in phase manner for rain water harvesting.
¢ Tree plantation drives.

¢ To take additional measures to continuously improve our energy consumption
by using of energy savings equipment’s, sensor based water supply system,

timer base general area lighting etc.
¢ To ensure the avaibility of necessary resources to achieve our objectives.

¢ to undertake green initiatives, and to conduct various programmes to save

energy and to protect the environment.

e To provide information and training opportunities on energy saving measures.

* To count CO2 emissions generated by our means of transportations- vehicles.

This policy will be communicated to the students and employees via internal
communication channels. The Environment and Energy Policy, objectives and

targets will be reviewed on a regular basis by n department.
