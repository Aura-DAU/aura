DISCIPLINARY GUIDELINES FOR STUDENTS 
 
 
The breach of Discipline 
 
1) 
The  Students  are  expected  to  behave,  both  within  and  outside  the 
Institute’s campus, in a manner befitting a sincere student of a reputed 
Institute. Any instance of unseemly behaviour or of activities indicating 
lack of integrity and honesty on the part of the students will be regarded 
as a breach of discipline. 
 
2) 
The breach of discipline will encompass: 
 
• Physical Assault 
• Property damage; attempts to damage Institute property 
• Theft, including theft of Institute property such as components, street 
signs, furniture, and library books 
• Forgery, including forging signatures 
• Sexual harassment or other sexual misconduct 
• Misrepresentation in seeking financial aid or other Institute benefit. 
• Misuse of Institute computer equipment or e-mail/internet 
• Sending  threatening  and  obscene  messages  via  e-mail,  phone  or 
voice-mail. 
• Rough driving on campus 
• Storing/consumption of alcohol/drugs on the campus 
• Violating Institute’s rules and Regulations 
• Any such other act of unseemly behaviour. 
 
The Disciplinary Committee 
 
3) 
The Disciplinary Committee for students will be the relevant authority 
for dealing with the disciplinary Infractions arising out of general 
behaviour/conduct of students. 
 
4) 
The  Disciplinary Committee will  be constituted/reconstituted by  the 
Director from time to time. 
The Process 
 
5) 
Any instance of the breach of discipline can be referred, in writing only, 
to the Disciplinary Committee by any faculty/staff member through the 
incharge of that function/activity. 
 
 
6) 
The Disciplinary Committee then will start enquiries/investigations. The 
Committee will hold hearing(s) to give opportunity for making any 
representation to the concerned parties.

7) 
The Committee shall document the entire process and should 
issue a written order on the actions/penalty to be imposed. 
 
 
The Penalty 
 
8) 
The Disciplinary Committee shall decide upon the actions/penalty to be 
taken against students who violate any of the above disciplinary norms 
and shall forward to the Executive Registrar for execution. 
 
9) 
There is no standard penalty that applies to violations/breach of the 
disciplinary norms. Infractions may lead to penalties ranging from: 
 
o warning, imposition of fines and disciplinary probation for certain 
period 
 
to 
 
o suspension from hostel/Institute and permanent dismissal from 
the Institute. 
 
In each case, the nature and seriousness of the offence, the motivation 
underlying the offence, and precedence in similar cases are to be 
considered. 
 
10) 
An  infraction during  the  disciplinary probation period  shall  lead  to 
stricter penalties   ranging   from   suspension   for   few   semesters   to 
permanent dismissal from the Institute. 
 
11) 
Any penalty involving temporary suspension or permanent dismissal 
from the Institute shall be executed only after the approval of the 
Director. 
 
 
The Appeal 
 
12) 
Any  student  who  is  aggrieved  about  such  actions  taken  by  the 
Disciplinary Committee may appeal to the Director of the Institute, who 
is the final appellate authority. 
 
13) 
No appeal under this shall be entertained unless it is submitted within a 
period of two weeks from the date on which the appellant receives a 
copy of the order. The Appellate Authority may entertain an Appeal 
after the expiry of the said period, if (s)he is satisfied that the Appellant 
has sufficient cause for not submitting the appeal in time. 
 
14) 
Notwithstanding anything contained in these Guidelines, the Director 
may, on appeal or on her/his own motion after calling for the records of 
the case, review any order which is made under these Guidelines, and 
 
i) 
confirm, modify or set aside, the order, 
ii) 
impose any penalty or set aside, reduce, confirm or enhance 
the penalty imposed by the order, 
iii)  
remit the case for review to the Disciplinary Committee or

to any other authority directing such further action or     
enquiry as he considers proper in the circumstances of the 
case, or 
iv) 
pass such other orders as (s)he deems fit; 
provided that an order imposing or enhancing a penalty 
shall not be passed unless 
the    student    concerned   
has    been    given    an opportunity of making any 
representation which (s)he may wish to make against such 
enhanced penalty. 
 
15) 
The decision of  the Director in  all  such instances shall be  final and 
binding.
