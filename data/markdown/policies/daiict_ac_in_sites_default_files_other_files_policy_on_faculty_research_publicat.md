A Policy for Faculty Research Publication Incentives

Objective: To recognize and incentivize the faculty-led original research that has resulted in
publication at a reputed journal or a leading conference.

Award: The incentive comprises a financial award to the faculty members who have published
in top-quality journals and conferences.

Criteria:
1. All full-time faculty members of Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU) are eligible to
receive this award.
2. The faculty member will receive this award for each journal and conference article
published at a high-quality venue in a calendar year, i.e., from January to December.
3. The financial award (Pmax) per publication is as follows:

Procedure:

Only those conference papers published in the main technical track of the

conference (excluding the workshops, tutorials, invited talks, etc.) are eligible.

For the journal papers, only the research articles are eligible; book reviews,

editorials, etc., are not eligible.

The papers published in the QI or Q2 Open Access journals are eligible if the

Institute has not paid any type of article processing/publishing charges (APCs).

The faculty affiliation should be listed as DAU/DA-IICT in the publication for

it to be considered for this award.

The faculty members should be affiliated with DAU on a full-time basis at the

time of the initial submission of the article.

i. Faculty members on lien or extraordinary leave will receive their

incentive award when they re-join the Institute.

The date of publication will be considered as per the Digital Object Identifier

(DOI).

Multiple authors from the Institute/University: an article may be published

collaboratively by multiple faculty members of the DAU. If there are N >1

faculty members who have authored a paper, each co-author shall receive a

financial award equal to [Png,/N].

A faculty member can receive a maximum financial award of Rs. 4 lakhs per

calendar year.

i. Ifa faculty member qualifies for an award (as per the above criteria) that

exceeds this limit, the excess amount shall be credited to the faculty’s
CPDA.

1. For Category II list (except Q2 journal category in Scimago Journal Ranker, and B

category in Core-edu and Aminer), each area coordinator, in consultation with the
faculty members of that area, will prepare a list of conferences that they propose to
include in Category II and communicate this list to the Dean of Research by the end of
september of each year. Dean Research will get this list reviewed and approved as

recommended by a panel of external experts before the end of the year. The area
coordinators will provide a list of external experts in their respective areas to Dean
Research to facilitate this process.

2. Faculty whose research publications meet the criteria stated above will be required to
send the information to the Dean of Research to claim the financial incentives for the
same.

3. The Dean's Research Office, in consultation with the Resource Centre, will verify the
claim. After confirming the DOI of the publication (that is, the electronic version of the
paper appeared in the conference proceedings or the journal volume), Dean Research
will obtain the Director’s approval for granting the award to the faculty member(s) who
authored/co-authored the publication.

4. The list of faculty research incentives recipients will be shared with the
Communications Office. The names of the recipients, along with the publication details,
will be displayed on the Institute's website.
