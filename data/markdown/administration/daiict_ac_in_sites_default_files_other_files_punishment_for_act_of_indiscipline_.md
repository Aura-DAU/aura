Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC): Act of indiscipline and indicative list of punishment    
This has reference to: 
1. Disciplinary Guidelines for Students (March 01, 2024) and 
2. Student Code of Conduct and DAC Guidelines  
In this connection, the disciplinary procedure is redefined as under: 
a. Primary investigation and notice to be issued by the Deputy Registrar (Academic) to the 
culprit to make himself/herself present for DAC meeting. 
b. Post DAC Meeting, the culprit ((if found proven guilty) to be placed on Disciplinary Probation 
and awarded appropriate punishment as per policy 
c. To inform the Parent(s) about incident and decision of the DAC in writing  
d. To give opportunity to the parent(s) to meet the DAC, if required 
The punishment for violating discipline in the institute can vary depending on the severity of the 
offense, the institute's policies, and the discretion of the disciplinary committee or authorities. Some 
indicative but not limited punishments are defined in this document.
