Dear Abhi Gupta, 
 
Congratulations! We are pleased to inform you that you have been selected for Reliance 
Foundation Postgraduate Scholarship (the “Scholarship”) for the year 2022-23. 
 
You have been selected for the Scholarship in recognition of your academic achievements, 
demonstration of leadership, and potential for success in your future career. The expert 
panelists were impressed by your values of excellence, growth mindset, leadership 
potential, community commitment, integrity, and courage, each being aligned with the 
values of Reliance Foundation Scholars. 
 
You will receive a scholarship of up to Rs. 6 lakhs (INR Six lakhs) over the duration 
of your postgraduate degree programme. 
 
We are excited for you to join the community of Reliance Foundation Postgraduate 
Scholars who are uniquely positioned to be at the forefront of India’s technology driven 
growth. As a Reliance Foundation Postgraduate Scholar, you will join a community of 
India’s brightest youth with the potential to become India’s global leaders of tomorrow. We 
hope that you will embrace this opportunity to nurture your talent, and that this scholarship 
will enable you reach your full potential in building next-generation technology solutions for 
India and for the world.  
 
To indicate your acceptance of this award, please review all the documents and complete 
the formalities to expedite your enrolment as Reliance Foundation Postgraduate Scholar. 
For any queries, please contact us at RF.PGScholarships@reliancefoundation.org. 
 
Congratulations once again on your selection as a 2022-2023 Reliance Foundation 
Postgraduate Scholar!
