## Biography

Dr. Anupam Rana is an académicien with over 22 years of experience, including 20 years in design education. He holds a B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering and a Ph.D. in Experience Design. He spent 17 years at NIFT Gandhinagar & Shillong, where he was a founding member of Accessory Design and Experience Design program. Dr. Rana’s expertise Spans, Product Design Human-Centric Design, Exhibition Design, and Multidisciplinary Research. He also serves as an expert consultant to the industry & govt. organisation, bringing practical insights to academic and professional design challenges.
