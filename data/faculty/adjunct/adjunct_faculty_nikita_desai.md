## Biography

I received my M.Des and Ph.D from DAIICT, and have been teaching courses as an adjunct faculty with the M.Des program since 2017. I am also the Co-Founder and Creative Technology Director of Myth Interactives, a design agency specializing in the creation of Interactive and Immersive Experiences for Cultural Spaces since 2009.
