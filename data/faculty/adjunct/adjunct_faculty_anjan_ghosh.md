## Biography

Dr. Anjan K Ghosh has 35 years of research and teaching experience in the areas of optical information processing, optical communications and photonic sensors. He obtained his PhD in Electrical Eng. in 1984 from Carnegie-Mellon Univ., Pittsburgh and his MS from the SUNY at Stony Brook. He is an alumnus of IIT Kharagpur, India. At present, he is a Professor in DA-IICT, Gandhinagar, India. Dr. Ghosh was a member of technical staff in AT&T Bell Laboratories for 2 years. Then he served as a faculty member in the Univ. of Iowa, Iowa City, USA, IIT Kanpur, India, Nanyang Tech. Univ., Singapore, the University of Oklahoma, Tulsa, USA and DA-IICT, Gandhinagar, India. Dr. Ghosh has served as the Vice Chancellor of Tripura (Central) University in Agartala, Tripura, India for 5.5 years. He was the Head of the Dept. of Electrical Eng., Adv. Center for Electronic Sciences, Laser Tech. Program and the Center for Laser Tech., all at IIT Kanpur. He published over 160 papers in journals and conference proceedings and delivered several invited talks. He is a Senior Member of IEEE, a fellow of the IETE(India), IE (India), and the Optical Soc. of India and a member of SPIE, and OSA. He is also a member of the honor societies: Eta-Kappa-Nu and Sigma-Xi in USA.
