# Jayanth Varma

## Biography

Prof. Jayanth R. Varma has extensive experience in financial markets, regulation, and academia; he has served on the RBI Monetary Policy Committee, been a member of SEBI, chaired multiple regulatory committees, held board positions in major organizations, and taught courses on capital markets, risk management, and corporate finance.

## Specialization

Financial Markets and Pricing Models, The Financial Sector, International Finance, and Quantitative Modeling.

## Publications

He is the author of several books including Derivatives and Risk Management, Portfolio Management, and The Great Indian Scam, and has published extensively in leading journals and edited volumes on financial markets, regulation, and corporate finance. Recent papers are listed at HTTPS://WWW.JRVARMA.IN/RECENT.HTML

## Teaching

Courses on capital markets, fixed income, alternative investments, risk management, and corporate finance during my tenure at IIMA. List of these courses is available at HTTPS://WWW.JRVARMA.IN/COURSES.HTML
