## Biography

I have joined Dhirubhai Ambani University (DA-IICT), Gandhinagar as an Assistant Professor in May 2025. Previously, I worked at Institute of Infrastructure Technology Research And Management (IITRAM), Pandit Deendayal Petroleum University, and Nirma University.

I did M.Tech. (ICT) and Ph.D. (ICT) from Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of ICT (DA-IICT) in year 2009 and 2016, respectively.

My Research interests include the design of Intelligent Transportation Systems, Embedded Systems and IoT, Scalable protocol design for large distributed systems, and the ICT system design in general. I am also interested in studying distributed data structures, peer to peer content sharing, and the use cases of Blockchain framework.

## In-Depth Profile / Student Guide

### Research Identity
His work is centered around Embedded Systems + Internet of Things (IoT) + Intelligent Transportation Systems + Real-Time Systems + Cyber-Physical Systems. This is a hardware + software + real-world systems integration profile. He is part of DAU's in-house systems + IoT + embedded computing research group.

### Core Research Areas
1. **Embedded Systems (Core Area):** Microcontroller-based systems, Real-time embedded applications, Sensor integration systems, Low-power computing systems. (Applications: Smart devices, Automotive electronics, Industrial automation).
2. **Internet of Things (IoT):** Sensor networks, Device-to-device communication, Smart environment systems, IoT architecture design. (Examples: Smart home systems, Smart city infrastructure, Environmental monitoring systems).
3. **Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS):** Traffic monitoring systems, Smart traffic control, Vehicle tracking systems, Transportation optimization systems. (Real-world relevance: Smart traffic lights, GPS-based traffic prediction, Urban mobility systems).
4. **Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS):** Physical World (Sensors/Devices) -> Computation Layer (Embedded Systems) -> Decision System (Control/AI/Logic) -> Physical Action (Actuators). Used in Smart cities, Autonomous systems, Industrial automation.
5. **Real-Time Systems:** Time-critical computing systems, Scheduling algorithms, Latency optimization, System reliability.

### Research Style
His approach: Sensors / Hardware -> Embedded Controller -> Real-Time Processing -> Decision Output / Actuation. This is engineering-heavy systems design, not ML-heavy AI.
