## Biography  
Completed Bachelor's in Statistics from Presidency University, Kolkata (2017), and Master’s in Statistics from IIT Kanpur (2019). Joined ISI as a research scholar in 2019, focusing on high-dimensional inference with sparsity. Appointed Assistant Professor at Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology in 2025.
