## Biography

I am Rachit Chhaya. I have recently joined as An Assistant Professor at DA-IICT. Prior to that I was a Ph.D. scholar in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar, working with my advisor Prof. Anirban Dasgupta. I am currently waiting for my thesis defense. I work in areas of Machine Learning and Data Science. My chief research interests lie constructing coresets for Machine Learning Problems Involving Regularization, Fairness Constraints etc. I am fortunate to collaborate with my advisor and friends, Jayesh Choudhari and Supratim Shit.

## In-Depth Profile / Student Guide

### Research Identity
His work is centered around Machine Learning + Algorithms + Data Summarization + Optimization + Coreset Theory. He focuses on making ML efficient, scalable, and mathematically grounded. He belongs to the theoretical ML + algorithms research group, not applied AI.

### Core Research Areas
1. **Machine Learning Theory:** Foundations of learning algorithms, Generalization bounds, Efficient learning methods. (Why models work, how to make them efficient).
2. **Coresets (Key Specialty):** A small representative subset of a large dataset that preserves properties of the full dataset. Enables faster training, less memory usage, scalable ML. (Applications: k-means clustering, regression, deep learning acceleration).
3. **Large-Scale Data Algorithms:** Efficient clustering, Streaming algorithms, Big data summarization, Distributed learning methods.
4. **Optimization:** Convex optimization, Approximation algorithms, Efficient computation techniques.
5. **Data Science Foundations:** Statistical learning, Scalable analytics, Algorithmic data analysis.

### Research Style
His approach: Massive Data -> Mathematical Reduction (Coresets / Sampling) -> Efficient Algorithm -> Fast ML Model. This is scalable ML design, not model tuning.
