## In-Depth Profile / Student Guide

### Research Identity
His work is centered around VLSI Design + Analog & Digital Circuits + Semiconductor Devices + IC Design Methodologies + Hardware System Optimization. This is deep chip + circuit-level engineering, more detailed than system-level hardware. He belongs to DAU's advanced hardware + semiconductor research group.

### Core Research Areas
1. **VLSI Design (Core Area):** Chip design architecture, Circuit optimization, Layout design, Transistor-level logic implementation.
2. **Semiconductor Devices:** Behavior of semiconductor materials, Device scaling (MOSFET, CMOS), Leakage current control, Device physics for modern chips. (Applications: CPUs, GPUs, Mobile processors, Embedded chips).
3. **Analog & Digital Circuits:** Analog (Signal amplification, Noise control, Analog signal conditioning), Digital (Logic circuits, Sequential systems, Digital architecture).
4. **IC Design Methodology:** Chip design flow, Design verification, Hardware modeling, Performance testing.

### Research Style
His pipeline: Device Physics -> Circuit Design -> IC Architecture -> Fabrication Optimization. This is hardware-first engineering from physics -> chip level.
