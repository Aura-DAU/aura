## In-Depth Profile / Student Guide

### Research Identity
His work is centered around networked systems + IoT + modeling of large distributed systems. Strong research background in physics -> systems -> networks -> IoT modeling. Think: "Mathematical + simulation-based design of large networked systems".

### Core Research Areas
1. **Internet of Things (IoT)**
2. **Wireless Sensor Networks**
3. **Network protocol modeling & analysis**
4. **Simulation of large-scale distributed systems**
5. **Mobile and networked communication systems**

### Research Style
He works on problems like: How thousands of sensors communicate efficiently, How data travels in large wireless networks, How to simulate internet-scale systems before building them, How protocols behave under congestion or load, Designing efficient routing strategies for IoT networks. His publications include IEEE and applied systems work such as: Intelligent transportation systems using multi-modal data, Human mobility modeling in networks, Wireless sensor network routing protocols, Interplanetary ad-hoc network routing concepts, Air pollution monitoring using sensor networks.
