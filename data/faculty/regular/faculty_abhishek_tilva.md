## In-Depth Profile / Student Guide

### Research Identity
His work is very math-heavy and finance-theory oriented, focusing on advanced probability & statistics. He has one of the strongest mathematical-statistics backgrounds in the institute.

### Core Research Areas
1. **Stochastic Portfolio Theory:** Arbitrage Theory in Finance, Stochastic Analysis.
2. **Mathematical Finance:** High-dimensional financial systems (markets with changing number of assets).
3. **Advanced Probability Theory:** Focus on rigorous probability theory + stochastic calculus.

### Research Style
Based on his published papers (e.g., work with Erhan Bayraktar, published in Mathematical Finance), he works on advanced financial mathematics models, studies markets where the number of stocks changes dynamically, and focuses on rigorous probability theory + stochastic calculus. This means more theory + proofs + equations, less "programming projects," and heavy on math intuition + derivations.
