## Biography

Dr. Abhishek Gupta received his PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Toronto Metropolitan University, Canada. During his PhD, he worked on the application of Machine Learning techniques for performance optimization in Wireless Communications. He also holds an MS in Electrical, Computer from Ryerson University, Canada where he worked on Computer Vision in Autonomous Vehicles using machine learning techniques. Prior to this, he completed an MS in Intelligent Systems from De Montfort University, UK and BE in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Savitribai Phule Pune University, Pune, India. His research interests are machine learning, signal processing, wireless communications and computer vision.
