## Biography

Myself Dr. Arnab Bhabak. I obtained my PhD degree in Mathematics from Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, India. During my PhD, I worked on the Stochastic Control and Stochastic Game Theory. I did my Master’s from Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, India. Prior to this, I completed my Graduation in Mathematics as a Major from St. Xavier’s College Kolkata, India. My primary work includes zero sum and non zero sum game problems for different dynamics viz., Markov chains, semi Markov processes, piecewise deterministic Markov chains.
