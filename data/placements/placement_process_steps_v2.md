# The Placement Process Pipeline

The standard recruitment cycle for a visiting company follows a strict 8-step chronological process coordinated by the Placement Office:

### Step 1: Invitation and JAF
The Placement Office designates a specific Point of Contact for each target company and sends an official invitation alongside the Job Announcement Form (JAF).

### Step 2: Company Response

Interested companies respond to the invitation by submitting the fully completed Job Announcement Form (JAF), detailing the role, CTC, and eligibility criteria.

### Step 3: Verification and Publication

Upon internal verification of the JAF, the job opportunity is officially published and made accessible to students according to the schedule set by the Placement Office.

### Step 4: Student Registration

Interested and eligible students register for the published opportunity. The finalized list of applicants is then shared directly with the company.

### Step 5: The Recruitment Drive

The company conducts its recruitment process (tests, GDs, interviews) after finalizing the schedule in strict coordination with the Placement Office.

### Step 6: Final Selection

Following the conclusion of the drive, the company provides the final, official list of selected candidates.

### Step 7: Acceptance Confirmation

The Placement Office confirms acceptance from the selected candidates and notifies the company accordingly.

### Step 8: Offer Distribution

Official offer letters are sent directly to the Placement Office, which are then distributed to the respective candidates.
