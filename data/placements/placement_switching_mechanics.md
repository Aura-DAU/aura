# Offer Switching Mechanics

### The Core Rule
If a student receives a Job (J) or Internship + Job (I+J) offer from a **Category I** company, this is the final offer. The student is immediately out of the placement process and **cannot switch** to another company.

### The 1.5x Switch Option (Category II Only)

If a student receives an **Internship (I) only** offer from a **Category II** company, they are granted **one chance to switch**, provided the following mathematical condition is met:

* **The Multiplier:** The next company must offer a CTC that is at least **1.5 times** the CTC of the initial Category II offer.
* **Example Calculation:** If a student's first offer has a CTC equivalent of 5 LPA, the next eligible company they apply for must offer a minimum CTC of 7.5 LPA.

### Multiple Offer Handling

If the student successfully secures a new Job (J) offer that allows them to complete internships elsewhere, the student is permitted to keep both offers. However, upon upgrading to a higher category company via a successful switch, the previous offer stands rejected automatically.
