# Dream Category Companies

### Definition and Authority
At the sole discretion of the Placement Cell, certain high-value or highly sought-after companies may be classified as "Dream" companies. 

### Application Exceptions

The standard switching restrictions are temporarily lifted for Dream companies. **All students**, including those who have already exhausted their single switch option or those who are already placed, are eligible to apply to a Dream company when it visits campus.

### Automated Rejection Protocol

If a student successfully secures an offer from a Dream company:
1. All previously secured offers (regardless of category) are automatically rejected.
2. The student immediately exits the placement process entirely.
