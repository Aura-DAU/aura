## Placement Disciplinary Policies (PIAAC & DAC)

### Overview

To maintain the reputation of Dhirubhai Ambani University and ensure fair opportunities for all, the university enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy regarding student misconduct during the placement and internship process.

### The Committees

Disciplinary issues are handled by two distinct administrative bodies:
1. **DAC (Disciplinary Action Committee):** Handles general university-wide disciplinary infractions and academic misconduct.
2. **PIAAC (Placement and Internship Affairs Action Committee):** A specialized committee dedicated exclusively to investigating and penalizing misconduct related to corporate recruitment drives, interviews, and internships.

### Scope of PIAAC Violations

Students will be referred to PIAAC for actions including, but not limited to:
* Abandoning a company's recruitment process midway without prior written authorization from the Placement Office.
* Falsifying resumes, portfolios, or academic records (e.g., misreporting CGPA or exaggerating project contributions).
* Engaging in unprofessional conduct during interviews, group discussions, or pre-placement talks (PPTs).
* Reneging on an accepted offer (Job or Internship) to pursue off-campus opportunities without utilizing the official "Switching Rules" outlined in the Placement Policy.
* Any formal complaint lodged by a visiting recruiter regarding a student's behavior that damages the university's corporate relationship.

### Zero-Tolerance Penalties

If PIAAC finds a student guilty of a violation, penalties may include:
* Immediate expulsion from the current placement drive.
* Permanent blacklisting from all future university-sponsored recruitment events.
* Revocation of previously secured offers.
* Referral to the general DAC for further academic penalties, including suspension or marks deduction, depending on the severity of the offense.

## Independent Placement Reporting Standards (IPRS)

### Placement Verification and Audits

Dhirubhai Ambani University is committed to transparency in its placement figures. All placement data, including offers, CTCs, and company categories, are independently audited and formatted according to the Independent Placement Reporting Standards (IPRS). 

### 2023-2024 Audit Reports

The official, audited UG and PG Placement Reports for the previous academic year (2023-2024) are stored in the following dedicated repositories:
* For Undergraduate data (B.Tech programs): Refer to `iprs_ug_report_2023_24`
* For Postgraduate data (M.Tech, M.Sc, M.Des programs): Refer to `iprs_pg_report_2023_24`
