# DTG — 2nd Prize at Aahvaan Street Play Competition, IIT Gandhinagar

## Overview

DTG (DA-IICT Theatres Group) bagged the **2nd Prize** in the street play competition **Aahvaan** held at IIT Gandhinagar.

## Main Content

Congratulations to **DTG (DA-IICT Theatres Group)** on winning the **2nd Prize** in the street play competition **Aahvaan**, held at **IIT Gandhinagar**.

This achievement showcases the theatrical talent and creative expression of DA-IICT students, demonstrating excellence in the performing arts alongside their technical education.

- **Venue:** IIT Gandhinagar
- **Team:** DTG (DA-IICT Theatres Group)
