# Rajat Garg — High Commendation at Thar MUN, Jodhpur

## Overview

Rajat Garg (B.Tech 3rd Year) won the **High Commendation** at the **Thar MUN** (Model United Nations) held in Jodhpur. He represented the delegate of Iran in the UNSC committee.

## Main Content

Congratulations to **Rajat Garg** (B.Tech 3rd Year) on winning the **High Commendation** at the **Thar MUN** (Model United Nations) in Jodhpur. He was a part of the **UNSC (United Nations Security Council)** committee and represented the **delegate of Iran** in the committee.

This achievement demonstrates Rajat's exceptional skills in diplomacy, international relations, public speaking, and research.
