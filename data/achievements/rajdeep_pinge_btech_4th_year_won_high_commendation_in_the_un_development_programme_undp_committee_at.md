# Rajdeep Pinge — High Commendation at PDPU MUN (UNDP Committee)

## Overview

Rajdeep Pinge (B.Tech 4th Year) won the **High Commendation** in the **UN Development Programme (UNDP)** committee at the **PDPU MUN** (Model United Nations).

Congratulations to **Rajdeep Pinge** (B.Tech 4th Year) on winning the **High Commendation** in the **UN Development Programme (UNDP)** committee at the **PDPU MUN** (Model United Nations organized by Pandit Deendayal Energy University).

This recognition reflects Rajdeep's strong command of international development issues, diplomacy, and public speaking at one of Gujarat's prominent MUN conferences.
