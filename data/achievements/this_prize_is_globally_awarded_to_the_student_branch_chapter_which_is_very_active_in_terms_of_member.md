# IEEE IAS Outstanding Student Branch Chapter Award — DA-IICT

## Overview

The IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter at DA-IICT was awarded the prestigious **Outstanding Large Chapter Award** — a prize globally awarded to the Student Branch Chapter which is very active in terms of member activities, education/technical events, and membership.

## Main Content

The **IEEE IAS (Industry Applications Society) Student Branch Chapter at DA-IICT** received the **Outstanding Large Chapter Award** — a prize globally awarded to the Student Branch Chapter which is very active in terms of member activities, education/technical events, and membership.

This globally competitive prize recognizes chapters that demonstrate exceptional leadership, event organization, technical education, and active member engagement throughout the year. The achievement places DA-IICT's IEEE IAS chapter among the most recognized student chapters worldwide.

## Important Information

- **Award:** Outstanding Large Chapter Award (IEEE IAS)
- **Recipient:** IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter, DA-IICT
- **Criteria:** High activity in member events, education/technical programs, and membership growth
