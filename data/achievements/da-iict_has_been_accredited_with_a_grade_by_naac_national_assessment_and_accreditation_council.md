# DA-IICT Accredited with A Grade by NAAC

## Overview

DA-IICT has been accredited with an **A Grade** by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council), reflecting the institution's high standards of academic quality, research, and governance.

## Main Content

**DA-IICT (Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology)** has been accredited with the **A Grade** by **NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council)** following the Cycle 1 assessment. This accreditation underscores the university's commitment to providing quality education, fostering research, and maintaining institutional governance standards.

DA-IICT subsequently achieved **A+ Grade** in NAAC Cycle 2 (2023), with a score of 3.32, further cementing its standing as one of India's premier private technical universities.

## Important Information

- **NAAC Grade (Cycle 1):** A
- **NAAC Grade (Cycle 2, 2023):** A+ (Score: 3.32, Date: 07/09/2023)
- **Institution:** DA-IICT (Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology), Gandhinagar
