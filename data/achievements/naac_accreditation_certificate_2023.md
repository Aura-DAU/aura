# NAAC Accreditation Certificate 2023

## Overview

This is the official NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council) Accreditation Certificate issued to DA-IICT / Dhirubhai Ambani University in 2023.

## Main Content

DA-IICT has been awarded the **A+ Grade** by NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council) following the Cycle 2 assessment conducted in 2023. The accreditation certificate validates the university's commitment to academic excellence, quality research, and institutional governance.

## PDF Source

The accreditation certificate is available as a PDF at: [NAAC Accreditation Certificate 2023](https://daiict.ac.in/sites/default/files/other-files/NAAC_Accreditation_2023.pdf)

## Important Information

- NAAC Grade: A+
- Cycle: Cycle 2
- Year: 2023
