# Research / DAU Student Research Excellence Award
## Overview
The DAU Student Research Excellence Award is presented to students who have published in top-quality journals/conferences. The award comprises a certificate and plaque/medal, presented annually by the Director.

## Award
The award is given to students who have published in Q1 category journals or A*, A, or B category conferences, with DA-IICT as their primary affiliation.

## Eligibility Criteria
### Eligible Students
All currently enrolled DA-IICT students (UG and PG) and graduated students who received their degrees in the previous academic year are eligible.
#### UG Students
Eligible up to one year after completing their program (e.g., for the 2025 award, batches 2019-2023 to 2024-2028 are eligible).
#### PG Students
Eligible up to one year after completing their program or award of degree (e.g., for the 2025 award, batches 2021-2023 to 2024-2026 are eligible).
#### PhD Students
Eligible up to one year after completing their program or award of degree.

## Selection Criteria and Process
### Application Process
Students provide information about their eligible research publications in a Google Form circulated by the Dean of Research.
### Verification Process
The Resource Center verifies the eligibility criteria, including DA-IICT affiliation, publication venue, and date.
### Selection
All students meeting the eligibility criteria are selected for the award.
### Award Ceremony
The award ceremony is held annually, and selected candidates are informed via email.
