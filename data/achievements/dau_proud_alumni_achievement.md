# Overview

We are proud to share that NiyamSan Chhaya, Vice President of Orbrick Consulting and an esteemed DAU alumnus, has been named Joint Winner of the UK Oracle User Group (UKOUG) “Leader of the Year” Community Award 2025! The award celebrates outstanding leadership, vision, and impact within the Oracle community.
