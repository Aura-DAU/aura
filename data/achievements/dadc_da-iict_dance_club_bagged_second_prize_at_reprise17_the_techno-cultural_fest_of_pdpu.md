# DADC — Second Prize at Reprise'17

## Overview

DADC (DA-IICT Dance Club) bagged the **Second Prize** at **Reprise'17**, the techno-cultural festival of PDPU (Pandit Deendayal Energy University).

## Main Content

Congratulations to the **DADC (DA-IICT Dance Club)** on winning the **Second Prize** at **Reprise'17**, the prestigious techno-cultural festival organized by PDPU (Pandit Deendayal Energy University).

This achievement reflects the talented dance performers of DA-IICT and their dedication to excellence in the performing arts alongside their academic pursuits.

## Important Information

- **Award:** Second Prize
- **Event:** Reprise'17 — Techno-Cultural Fest
- **Host Institution:** PDPU (Pandit Deendayal Energy University)
- **Team:** DADC (DA-IICT Dance Club)
