# Main Content

Sarvagn Pathak (B.Tech. 2020-24) has earned appreciation from top global organizations for identifying gaps in their cybersecurity and enabling them to hack-proof/intensify their cybersecurity systems.
He has since been featured in the Halls of Fame of NASA, UNESCO, Princeton University, BU-CERT, US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, the California Department of Technology, New Jersey Cybersecurity & Communications Integration Cell, the U.S. Department of Energy, the World Health Organization, National Cyber Security Centrum (NCSC-NL) of the Dutch Govt, and others. 
He has also been awarded Certificates of Appreciation from California Cyber Security Integration Center, the U.S. Department of Education,  US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, and the Australian Signals Directorate, as well as a LoR from NASA.
Congratulations, Sarvagn!  We are incredibly proud of your accomplishments!
Sarvagn’s passion for cybersecurity took shape during his student days, where he founded the Cyber Information and Network Security Club in addition to being a member of the Student Body Governance and the Convener - Music Club; winning hackathons and being active in research. He also interned with the DRDO - Ministry of Defence, Govt. of India. Currently, he is pursuing an MS in Cybersecurity in the U.S.
