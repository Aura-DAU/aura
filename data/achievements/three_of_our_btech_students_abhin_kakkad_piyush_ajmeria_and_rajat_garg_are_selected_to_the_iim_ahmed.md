# Three DA-IICT Students Selected for IIM Ahmedabad Summer School in Management

## Overview

Three B.Tech students — **Abhin Kakkad, Piyush Ajmeria, and Rajat Garg** — have been selected for the prestigious **IIM Ahmedabad Summer School in Management**.

## Main Content

Congratulations to three B.Tech students — **Abhin Kakkad**, **Piyush Ajmeria**, and **Rajat Garg** — on being selected for the prestigious **IIM Ahmedabad Summer School in Management** (also known as the IIM-A Summer School).

The IIM Ahmedabad Summer School in Management is a highly competitive program open to undergraduate students from premier technical and scientific institutions. Selection to this program reflects strong academic achievement, leadership potential, and multidisciplinary thinking — combining technical education with management insights.

This achievement underscores DA-IICT's commitment to producing well-rounded graduates who excel across both technology and management domains.

## Important Information

- **Students:** Abhin Kakkad, Piyush Ajmeria, and Rajat Garg (B.Tech)
- **Achievement:** Selected for IIM Ahmedabad Summer School in Management
- **Institution:** IIM Ahmedabad (Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad)
