# Divyakumar Solanki — IEEE Student Branch Leadership Training

## Overview

Divyakumar Solanki, Chair of IEEE Student Branch DA-IICT (BTech 4th Year), was selected to attend the IEEE Student Branch Leadership Training program, recognizing his leadership and contributions to the IEEE student community.

## Main Content

Congratulations to **Divyakumar Solanki**, Chair of the IEEE Student Branch DA-IICT (BTech 4th Year), on being selected to attend the **IEEE Student Branch Leadership Training**. This selection recognizes his exceptional leadership qualities, contributions to the IEEE Student Branch DA-IICT, and his dedication to advancing the IEEE mission among students.

The IEEE Student Branch Leadership Training is a competitive program open to outstanding student branch leaders from across the region, focusing on leadership development, collaboration, and advancing the IEEE's mission in engineering education.

- **Student:** Divyakumar Solanki (Chair, IEEE Student Branch DA-IICT, BTech 4th Year)
