# Overview

Our Director General, Prof Tathagata Bandyopadhyay, shares his views with Indian Express about how this year marks two historic milestones-the transition of the DA-IICT into Dhirubhai Ambani University and the completion of 25 successful years dotted with several milestones. As we embark on this new phase of growth, he reiterates the DAU's commitment to redefining education and research, foster innovation and contribute to a sustainable and equitable future.
