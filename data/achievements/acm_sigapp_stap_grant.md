# Overview

Two B.Tech 2015 batch students, Rishab Arora and Ashray Bharambe, got the grant of $1000 individually from ACM SIGAPP STAP to attend and present their work (carried out as a part of their BTech Project) in 35th SAC 2020, Brno, Czech Republic, March 30-April 3, 2020. The title of the work is “UC2Map: Automatic Translation of Use Case Maps from Specification”.
