# SPCOM 2022 Best Student Paper Finalist Certificate

## Overview

This is the Best Student Paper Finalist Certificate awarded at IEEE SPCOM 2022, held at IISc Bangalore.

## Main Content

**SPCOM 2022 Best Student Paper Finalist**

Paper titled "Morse Wavelet Features for Pop Noise Detection" by Priyanka Gupta, Piyushkumar K. Chodingala, and Hemant Patil was a finalist in the Best Student Paper Contest at IEEE SPCOM 2022, held at IISc Bangalore (July 11–15, 2022).
