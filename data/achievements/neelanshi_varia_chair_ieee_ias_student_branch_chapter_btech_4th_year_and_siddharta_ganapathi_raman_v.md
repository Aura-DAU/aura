# Neelanshi Varia and Siddharta Ganapathi Raman — IEEE IAS Travel Grant

## Overview

Neelanshi Varia (Chair, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter, BTech 4th Year) and Siddharta Ganapathi Raman (Vice Chair, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter) received the **IEEE IAS Travel Grant** to attend a major IEEE IAS event.

## Main Content

Congratulations to **Neelanshi Varia** (Chair, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter, BTech 4th Year) and **Siddharta Ganapathi Raman** (Vice Chair, IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter) on receiving the **IEEE IAS Travel Grant**.

The IEEE Industry Applications Society (IAS) Travel Grant is a competitive award presented to outstanding student members, enabling them to attend IEEE IAS conferences and events. This recognition reflects their leadership in the IEEE IAS Student Branch Chapter at DA-IICT.

## Important Information

- **Award:** IEEE IAS Travel Grant
- **Recipients:** Neelanshi Varia (Chair, IEEE IAS SBC, BTech 4th Year) and Siddharta Ganapathi Raman (Vice Chair, IEEE IAS SBC)
