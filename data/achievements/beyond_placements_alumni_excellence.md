# Overview

A comprehensive curated dataset of DA-IICT (now Dhirubhai Ambani University - DAU) alumni who have pursued impactful career paths outside standard campus placements. This document serves as an inspiring repository for students to showcase that placements are just one of many outcomes, highlighting alumni excellence in Entrepreneurship/Startups, Civil Services & Government/Defense, and Higher Education & Global Research (Ivy League/Top Universities).
