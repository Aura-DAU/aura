# Overview

A comprehensive curated dataset of DA-IICT (now Dhirubhai Ambani University - DAU) alumni who have pursued impactful, prestigious, and unconventional career paths outside standard IT campus placements. This document serves as an inspiring repository for students to showcase that placements are just one of many elite outcomes. It highlights alumni excellence across Entrepreneurship (Unicorn founders), High-Frequency Trading & Quant Finance, Management Consulting & Investment Banking, Frontier AI & Think Tanks, Civil Services & Government/Defense, and Higher Education & Global Research (Ivy League, IIMs, European/Asian Research Institutes).
