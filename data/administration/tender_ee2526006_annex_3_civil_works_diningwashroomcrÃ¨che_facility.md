# Tender EE2526006 — Annex 3 Civil Works (Dining/Washroom/Crèche Facility)

## Overview

Bill of Quantities for civil works — Washroom Facility in Faculty & Staff Dining Hall, Crèche & Admin Store, DAU Gandhinagar (September 2025).

## Main Content

Key BOQ items:
- Dismantling of existing exhaust duct, gas lines
- Demolition of brick masonry, Kotastone floor, wall tiles, tar road, pavers
- New paver road/parking laying — 200 sqft
- New 4½" brick masonry with copping — 560 sqft
- Rough coat plaster with GI chicken mesh — 1,900 sqft
- Chemical waterproofing (2mm, 10-year guarantee) — 400 sqft
- Granite door/window frames — 200 sqft
- Aluminium sliding windows with MS safety grill — 100 sqft
- Interior/exterior paint (Tractor Aqualock, Royal, Apex Ultima) — 2,300 sqft total
- False ceiling (gypsum board) — 500 sqft
- Waterproof flush doors — 5 nos.
- Granite kitchen platform with CERA single-bowl sink
- PVC cabinet under platform
- 600x600 vitrified flooring — 260 sqft; 600x300 wall dado — 240 sqft
- **Toilets:** CERA Single Flush Cistern, EWC CORDO — 3 units
- **Washbasins:** CERA CONCORD — 2 units
- **Urinal:** CERA CARAVAN PRO — 1 unit
- Antiskid floor tiles — 250 sqft; glossy wall tiles — 1,000 sqft
- Mirrors, exhaust fan frames, floor drain points — multiple
- Excavation, UPVC water supply & PVC SWR drainage piping
- Masonry drainage chambers — 12 nos.
- MS fabrication, RCC foundation, partition work
