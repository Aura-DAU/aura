## CEP Financial & Administrative Regulations (May 2024 Policy)

### 1. Revenue & Surplus Calculation
The net program surplus is calculated using the standard institutional formula:
$$\text{Net Surplus } (P) = A - (B + C + D)$$

Where:
- **$A$ (Total Revenue):** Total gross fees collected from participants / client organizations.
- **$B$ (Institute Overhead):** Flat **10% of total revenue ($A$)** retained by the university.
- **$C$ (Faculty Anchor / Coordinator Honorarium):** Flat **10% of total revenue ($A$)** paid to the Program Director(s).
- **$D$ (Direct Operational Expenses):** Actual direct costs incurred (course material, external honoraria, travel/boarding, catering, laboratory consumables, and certificates).

### 2. Net Surplus Sharing Tiers
Once direct expenses and overheads are deducted, the remaining net surplus ($P$) is shared between the University and the Course Resource Team according to progressive financial slabs:

| Net Surplus ($P$) Range | Institute Share | Course Team / Faculty Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Up to ₹3.00 Lakhs** | 20% | **80%** |
| **₹3.00 Lakhs to ₹5.00 Lakhs** | 30% | **70%** |
| **Above ₹5.00 Lakhs** | 50% | **50%** |

### 3. Program Approval & Governance
- All Continuing Education Programs must receive prior formal approval from the Dean (Academic) and Executive Registrar.
- Certificate issuance requires a minimum qualifying attendance (typically 80%) and satisfactory completion of program assessments.
