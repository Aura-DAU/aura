# Tender EE2526009 — 180 KW Solar Roof Top Plant (Technical Bid / BOQ)

## Overview

Technical Bid (Bill of Quantities / Annexure 3) for the 180 KWp Solar Roof Top Power Plant tender (CAPEX model) — Design, Supply, Installation, Commissioning & Testing.

## Main Content

Key items in the Bill of Quantities:

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Solar Module | N-Type TOPCon PV Module (Make: WAAREE/ADANI/HAVELLS/TATA) |
| Solar String Inverter | 150 KWp (Make: HAVELLS/GROWATT/DELTA/SOLIS/SUN GROW) |
| Mounting Structure | Elevated GI structure, 80-micron hot-dip GI pipe, RCC foundation |
| DCDB, Fuse Link & MCBs | Make: HAVELLS/C&S/SCHNEIDER/L&T |
| Earthing | Chemical earthing per GEDA & DISCOM guidelines (8 pits: 2 LA, 2 AC, 2 DC, 2 Structure) |
| Cabling | 3.5 Core Aluminium Armoured XLPE cable (Make: POLYCAB/HAVELLS/FINOLEX) |
| ACDB | 320 Amp MCCB/Contactor, SPP & SPD (Make: L&T/Elmer) |
| Lightning Arrester | ESE Type, 5m height, min. 107m radius protection |
| Cleaning System | Automatic timer-based water sprinkler system, 1 HP LUBI pump |

Note in the document: *"The plant capacity may vary according to design and space available."*
