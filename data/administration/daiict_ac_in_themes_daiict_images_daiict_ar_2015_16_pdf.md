## About the Institute  
### Founding and Vision  
Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT), established in 2001 by Shri Dhirubhai Hirachand Ambani, aims to build a knowledge-led society through intellectual competitiveness. Recognized as a University in 2003, DA-IICT is UGC-accredited and a member of AIU. Managed by a Board of Governors chaired by Shri Anil Ambani, it focuses on ICT education and research.  

### Academic Programs  
- **Undergraduate**:  
  - BTech (Information and Communication Technology)  
  - BTech (Hons.) in ICT with Computational Science (minor)  
- **Postgraduate**: Five programs including a doctoral program.  
- **Continuing Education**: Industry-focused training for professionals, government officials, and defense personnel.  

### Faculty and Research  
DA-IICT employs highly qualified, research-oriented faculty with international publications and sponsored projects. Research collaborations span DST, DIT, and global institutions. The government-supported Incubation Center nurtures tech-based startups.  

### Campus and Infrastructure  
- **Location**: 50-acre campus in Gandhinagar, Gujarat (30 km from Ahmedabad).  
- **Facilities**:  
  - 1500+ students  
  - 3 lecture theatres (350/250 seating)  
  - Modern labs, libraries, and computer studios  
  - Sports complexes, gymnasium, and cultural facilities  
  - Eco-friendly design with 80% green cover  

### Strategic Partnerships  
DA-IICT mentors the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Vadodara (IIIT-V) under Public-Private Partnership. The campus connects to Ahmedabad’s Sardar Vallabhai Patel International Airport (30-minute drive).
