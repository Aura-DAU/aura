# Travel Policy for Dhirubhai Ambani University Employees  

## Sanctioning Authorities and Prior Approval  
### Sanctioning Authorities Table  
| ID | Employee Category | Purpose of Travel | Approving Authority |  
|----|-------------------|-------------------|---------------------|  
| 1 | Faculty | Research related | Dean Research |  
| 2 | Research Associates | Sponsored research related | Principal Investigator, followed by Dean Research |  
| 3 | Teaching Assistants | Academics related | Dean AP |  
| 4 | Administrative Staff | Institute related | Reporting Manager/Head – HR & Admin, followed by Executive Registrar |  

Prior approval via email from the sanctioning authority is mandatory for all official travel. Reimbursement requests without prior approval will not be processed.  

---

## Allowed Types of Travel  
### Mode of Travel by Designation  
| Designation | Air | Rail | Road |  
|-------------|-----|------|------|  
| Faculty | Economy Class AC | First Class AC | Taxi |  
| Staff (Group-A) | Economy Class AC | First Class AC | Taxi |  
| Staff (Group-B) | Economy Class AC | 2-tier Class AC | Taxi |  
| Research Associates, Project Personnel, Teaching Assistants | AC 3-tier class | AC Taxi / AC Bus | |  
| Staff: Group C or D | 2nd AC / AC Chair Car | Taxi / Bus | |  

1. Local conveyance (airport/hotel/venue) is reimbursed up to **₹5,000 per trip**.  
2. Air/Train/Taxi reimbursements require submitted fare receipts.  
3. Private vehicle travel requires prior approval and reimbursement at **₹20/km** (no receipts needed).  

---

## Daily Allowance Limits  
### Daily Allowance Rates  
| Designation | Hotel Accommodation per Day (Metro Cities) | Hotel Accommodation per Day (Non-Metro Cities) | Food per Day |  
|-------------|--------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|--------------|  
| Faculty & Staff (Group-A) | ₹1,200 | ₹3,000 | ₹10,000 |  
| Staff (Group-B) | ₹7,000 | ₹4,500 | ₹2,000 |  
| Research Associates / Project Personnel / Teaching Assistants | ₹5,500 | ₹3,000 | ₹1,500 |  
| Staff (Group-C/D) | ₹5,500 | ₹3,000 | ₹1,500 |  

**Notes:**  
- Hotel accommodation includes incidentals (laundry, internet, etc.).  
- Tips and hard drinks are non-reimbursable.  
- Exceeding approved limits requires special approval from the Director.  

---

## Reimbursement Claims  
### Required Documents for Reimbursement  
| Mode of Travel | Requirements |  
|----------------|--------------|  
| By air | Boarding pass (if self-purchased) and air-ticket |  
| By train | Train ticket/ticket number, train number, class of travel |  
| By public transport/taxi | Ticket/Invoice |  

**Notes:**  
- Claims must be submitted within **3 months** of return; otherwise, advances are recovered from salary.  
- International travel bookings via Institute agents must be routed through the Administrative Section.  
- Reimbursable charges include reservation fees, Tatkal charges, e-ticketing fees, and cancellation charges for official reasons.  

---

## International Travel  
### Daily Allowance for International Travel  
1. **Faculty/Staff Cadre A:**  
   - Indian subcontinent/South-East Asia: **$175/day**  
   - Far East/Middle East/Australia: **$200/day**  
   - Americas: **$225/day**  
   - Western Europe/Japan: **$250/day**  
2. **Staff below Cadre A/Students/Research Associates:** **$140/day** globally.  
3. **Training/Functional Visits (self-arranged):** **$80/day** for all employees.  

**Notes:**  
- Includes lodging/food/local conveyance; excludes hard drinks/entertainment.  
- All air
