# Student Entrepreneurship and Incubation Policy — DCEI

## Overview

DAU's **Dhirubhai Ambani Centre for Entrepreneurship and Incubation (DCEI)** is the dedicated body that fosters entrepreneurship among students and faculty. Students are permitted and encouraged to develop startups while studying at DAU.

## Main Content

### About DCEI

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Full Name | Dhirubhai Ambani Centre for Entrepreneurship and Incubation |
| Legal Status | Independent Section 8 company |
| Mission | Encourage ICT-focused innovation and new venture creation |
| Funding | MeitY (Govt. of India, TIDE scheme), Govt. of Gujarat |
| Policy Framework | SSIP (Student Startup and Innovation Policy), Gujarat Govt. |

### Can Students Start a Company at DAU?

**Yes.** DAU actively supports students in starting companies through:

- DCEI incubation support
- Access to research labs and mentorship
- Seed funding through SSIP and TIDE schemes
- Networking with investors and industry experts
- Annual events: Startup Expo, Entrepreneurship Awareness Drives

### Incubation Process

| Step | Details |
|---|---|
| 1. Submit Proposal | Business plan + executive summary |
| 2. Evaluation | Based on: innovation, market potential, IP viability, team strength, scalability |
| 3. Incubation | Access to labs, mentors, funding support |
| 4. Growth | Networking, investor connect, collaboration with other incubators |

### IP and Conflict of Interest

- Faculty members engaging in startup activities are governed by the **Faculty Entrepreneurship Policy**.
- Students working on IP-generating projects should refer to the **Patent Filing Policy** for IP ownership guidelines.
- Potential conflicts of interest between studies and startup activities must be disclosed to the Dean of Students.

## Important Information

- DCEI is aligned with **AICTE and SSIP guidelines** for structured startup support.
- Students can participate at **pre-incubation stage** without formally registering a company.
- Contact DCEI during orientation events or Startup Expo to get started.
