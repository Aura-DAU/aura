## About the Institute  
### Founding and Vision  
DA-IICT was founded in 2001 by Shri Dhirubhai Hirachand Ambani with a vision to build a knowledge-led society. It operates under a Board of Governors chaired by Shri Anil Ambani.  

### Academic Programs  
DA-IICT offers:  
- **Undergraduate**: B.Tech in ICT, B.Tech (Hons.) in ICT with Computational Science minor.  
- **Postgraduate**: M.Tech (ICT), M.Tech (CSE) with specializations in Information Security/Data Science, M.Tech (E&CE) with Wireless Communication/Embedded Systems, M.Sc (IT), M.Des (Communication Design), and PhD.  

### Collaborations and Alumni  
- Partnerships with global institutions for faculty exchange and joint research.  
- Over 5,500 alumni working in technical, managerial, and research roles globally.  

### Campus and Sustainability  
- 50-acre campus in Gandhinagar with eco-friendly design (rainwater harvesting, recycled water irrigation).  
- Home to diverse bird species and green spaces.
