# One Time Purchase Open Inquiry — Electrical Items (Oct 2025)

## Overview

Open Inquiry for One Time Purchase of electrical items at DA-IICT Road, Gandhinagar. Inquiry No. EE2425004.

## Main Content

- **Contact:** estate_engineer@dau.ac.in and registrar@dau.ac.in
- **Submission deadline:** 14 November 2025, 3:00 PM
- **Rate validity:** 3 months from submission deadline
- **Purchase orders:** May be awarded in parts over up to 6 months, to one or more bidders as recommended by the Purchase Committee
- **Delivery period:** 15 working days normally (up to 7 in emergencies), delivered to general store 2:00–5:00 PM on working days
- **Penalty:** Up to 3% of order value for delayed supply, plus possible blacklisting from future inquiries
- **Payment:** Within 20 working days of correct invoice; no advance payment
- **Warranty:** Manufacturer warranty cards to be attached
- **Disputes:** Referred to Executive Registrar; Gandhinagar jurisdiction

## Important Information

- **Inquiry No.:** EE2425004
- Contains 2 Annexures: General Terms & Conditions, Technical & Financial Bid (Electrical Items list)
