## About S4P 2025
### Overview
The 7th edition of the Summer School on Speech Signal Processing (S4P) was organized by the Speech Research Lab at DAU. Building on the success of previous editions (S4P 2024, S4P 2019–2018, ASAP 2016), the 2025 edition focused on **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)**, a critical technology for intelligent personal assistants (e.g., Siri, Alexa).  

### Objectives
- Enhance fundamentals in speech signal processing  
- Expose participants to cutting-edge research  
- Targeted at graduate/postgraduate students, faculty, and industry researchers
