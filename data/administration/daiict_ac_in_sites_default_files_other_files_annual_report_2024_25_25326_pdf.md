## Director General’s Message  
### Academic Growth and Programme Development  
- **Accreditations**:  
  - NAAC A+ accreditation  
  - Five-Star rating under Gujarat State Institutional Rating Framework  
  - Centre of Excellence recognition (2025–2031)  
- **Institutional Transition**: Formal transition from DA-IICT to DAU via Gujarat Government Gazette (2024).  
- **New Programs**:  
  - School of Law (SoL) launched in 2024.  
  - Proposed Integrated Master’s in Data Science, Information Technology (2026–27).  
  - New undergraduate programs in Electronics & AI, Computer Science & AI.  
- **Curriculum Updates**: Alignments with NEP 2020, interdisciplinary learning, and industry relevance.  

### Establishment of a Centre for Teaching and Learning  
- **SPARK Initiative**:  
  - "Supporting Pedagogy, AI, Resources & Knowledge" (SPARK) launched.  
  - Focus: Global best practices, pedagogical capacity-building, AI integration in education.
