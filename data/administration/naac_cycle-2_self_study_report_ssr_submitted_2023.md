# NAAC Cycle-2 Self Study Report (SSR), Submitted 2023

## Overview

DA-IICT's Self Study Report (SSR) for the 2nd Cycle of NAAC Accreditation, submitted to the National Assessment and Accreditation Council, Bangalore in February 2023. 107 pages.

## Main Content

### Basic Institutional Info
- **Founded:** 6 August 2001, by Shri Dhirubhai Ambani
- **University status:** Conferred 2003 by Gujarat Legislature Act
- **UGC recognition:** Section 2(f), 30 November 2004
- **Director (at time of SSR):** Dr. K.S. Dasgupta
- **IQAC Coordinator:** Anil Roy
- **Nature:** State Private University, Unitary type
- **Campus:** 50 acres, urban, 202,350 sq.mts built-up area

### Cycle 1 Result
NAAC Cycle 1 (2017): **A Grade**, CGPA 3.11

### SWOC Analysis
**Strengths:** Centre-of-Excellence status (Gujarat), strong student/faculty attraction, no departmental silos, excellent placement, adjunct/practice professorships, green campus, ERP automation, mandatory rural internship, Anchor Institute status.
**Weaknesses:** Hostel/building infrastructure limits, faculty retention, few international students, low regional diversity, limited IPR/incubation output.
**Opportunities:** Expand industry-aligned programs, leverage alumni/industry funding, online content (MOOCs), international partnerships.
**Challenges:** Attracting top faculty/students, revenue generation, alumni engagement, NEP 2020 implementation, international research collaboration.

### Governance
Board of Governors → Finance Committee → Academic Council → Board of Studies, with the Director as Chief Executive.

### Research & Innovation
DA-IICT Center for Entrepreneurship and Incubation (DCEI) received: ₹150 lakh (TIDE scheme, MeitY), ₹15 lakh (Industries Commissionerate), ₹10 lakh installment of ₹100 lakh (SSIP). Consultancy revenue (5 yrs): ₹91.15 lakh. Consultancy policy: faculty limited to 52 working days/year; fee split 70% faculty salary / 10% CPDA / 20% institute overhead; minimum ₹5,000/day.

### Infrastructure
- Data centre: 30 servers, 10G switch
- HPC cluster: 40 Gbps SAN switch, Scientific Linux 7.1
- Library (KOHA ILMS): 30,000+ bibliographic records; subscriptions to IEEE Xplore, ACM, ScienceDirect, Springer, Nature
- Sports: 3 badminton courts, gym, yoga hall, cricket/football ground, 2 volleyball courts, 2 basketball courts
- Utilities: 20 KW solar plant, sensor-based water systems, sewage treatment plant (3.5 lakh L/day), FSSAI 5-star "Eat Right Campus"
- 25+ student clubs (Cultural Committee runs 25-30 events/year)

### Extension Activities
"Sambhav" (community service group) runs blood donation camps, old-age home visits, orphanage visits, plastic-free campus drives. Rural Internship is a mandatory 4-week B.Tech component, cited as one of DA-IICT's best practices.

### Institutional Values
Tagline: **"ENGINEERS WITH SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY."** Zero tolerance towards ragging; 24x7 CCTV-monitored women's hostel.
