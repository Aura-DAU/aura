# Academic Integrity Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the Academic Integrity Policy of Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DAIICT), a research-led university that emphasizes the importance of academic integrity and ethical research.

## Main Content

### Background

DAIICT was established in 2001 and was conferred university status in 2003. The university is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) and has been accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council with an 'A' Grade in 2017.

### Policy Objectives

The policy objectives are:

1. To create awareness about the significance of academic integrity in the conduct of research and submission of the thesis, dissertation, and other research publications.
2. To prevent academic misconduct including plagiarism in academic writing among students, faculty, and research staff by providing timely information, technical support, and guidance.
3. To strengthen all existing institutional mechanisms to ensure responsible conduct of research and develop systems to check and deter plagiarism that is in compliance with the UGC regulation.

### Definitions

The following definitions are used in this policy:

* Academic Integrity: The intellectual honesty in proposing, performing, and reporting any activity, which leads to the creation of intellectual property.
* Author: A student or a faculty or a researcher or staff of DAIICT who claims to be the creator of the work under consideration.
* UGC: The University Grants Commission as defined in the University Grants Commission Act, 1956.
* Information: Data, messages, text, images, sound, voice, codes, computer programs, software, and databases or microfilm or computer-generated microfiche.
* Institutional Academic Integrity Panel: A body constituted at the institutional level to consider recommendations of the departmental academic integrity panel and take appropriate decisions in respect of allegations of plagiarism and decide on penalties to be imposed.

### Policy Scope

This policy applies to all DAIICT faculty (full-time, adjunct, contractual), students, and research staff involved in research and publishing their respective work, including external collaborators (institutions and individuals) supporting DAIICT in its various research projects.

### Formation of Academic Integrity Committee (AIC)

The AIC will have the following constitution:

* Chairperson: Dean (AP)
* Members:
	+ Dean (Research)
	+ Executive Registrar (ex officio)
	+ RCC Convener (ex officio)
	+ IQAC Director (ex officio)
	+ External faculty member from other Higher Education Institute nominated by the Director
	+ Member Secretary: Librarian (ex officio)

### Terms of Reference of the Academic Integrity Committee (AIC)

The AIC will:

1. Adopt and implement the Academic Integrity Regulation of UGC (31 July 2018)
2. Evaluate and strengthen the existing processes of theses/dissertation submission including the plagiarism check
3. Examine the grievance of any misconduct pertaining to academic integrity and honesty
4. Conduct a fair and free investigation
5. Recommend suitable remedial action

### Plagiarism Prevention

DAIICT has implemented and enforced the technology-based mechanism using TURNITIN software to ensure that documents such as a thesis, dissertation, publications, or any other such documents are free of plagiarism at the time of their submission.

### Plagiarism Awareness

The AIC recommends that there will be awareness creation sessions for students at regular intervals for prevention, detection, and for taking remedial action.

### Redress Procedure upon Plagiarism Detection

The supervisor will examine the similarity report of the to-be-submitted publication and if the level of plagiarism is above level 1, the student will be counseled for taking appropriate measures including re-writing and re-phrasing the contents which are shown to be similar to the existing document/s.

### Penalties

Penalties in the cases of plagiarism shall be imposed on students pursuing studies at the level of Masters and Research programs and on faculty & research staff of the DAIICT only after academic misconduct on the part of the individual has been established without doubt, when all avenues of appeal have been exhausted and individual in question has been provided with enough opportunity to defend himself or herself in a fair or transparent manner before the AIC.
