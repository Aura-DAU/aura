# Reliance Foundation Abhi Scholarship 2023

## Overview

Documentation related to the Reliance Foundation Undergraduate & Postgraduate Scholarships (also known as "Reliance Foundation Scholarships" or informally "Abhi" scholarship program) awarded to students, including DA-IICT students, in 2023.

## Main Content

Reliance Foundation offers merit-based scholarships to talented undergraduate and postgraduate students across India, with an initial focus on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Computer Sciences. The inaugural scholarship cycle in 2020-21 awarded scholarships to 76 first-year undergraduate and postgraduate students nationally.

Scholars benefit from:
- Financial support for their studies
- Access to talks and workshops with leading global experts
- Opportunities to apply for mentoring and internships
- Volunteering opportunities
- Membership in a strong alumni network of scholars

Students are selected purely on merit, with applications invited from various socioeconomic backgrounds, aiming to build "a vibrant community of scholars that will be at the forefront of creating and building a new India."

DA-IICT students have been recipients of Reliance Foundation Scholarships as part of the broader scholarship ecosystem the university facilitates for its student body, alongside institutional scholarships (Merit-cum-Means, Cybage Khushboo, Jai Jhulelal, Satnaam WaheGuruji, etc.).

## Important Information

- **Scholarship name:** Reliance Foundation Undergraduate & Postgraduate Scholarship (Abhi cycle/batch reference)
- **Focus areas:** AI, Computer Sciences (initial cycles), later expanded
- **Sponsor:** Reliance Foundation
