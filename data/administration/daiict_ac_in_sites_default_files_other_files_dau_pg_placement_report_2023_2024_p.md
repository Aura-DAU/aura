Fixed Yearly Cash Component: This is a total of the annual basic salary and additional
guaranteed cash components. These additional components include cash payments and
allowances that are part of the annual package. The term guaranteed signifies that the amount
is certain unless there is an overall pay revision. The components falling under this salary head
are final and are not related to performance.

One-time Cash Payment : This head indicates the value of the remuneration given to a
candidate as one-time cash benefit mostly at the time of joining.

Total guaranteed Cash Component: This is the sum of fixed yearly cash component and
one-time cash payment.

Maximum Earning Potential: This is the sum of total guaranteed cash component, maximum
performance-linked variable and all other components of salary that are part of the offer. This
can include ESOPs, relocation allowances, long term compensation such as PF and other perks
as well.
