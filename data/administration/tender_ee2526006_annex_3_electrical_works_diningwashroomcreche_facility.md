# Tender EE2526006 — Annex 3 Electrical Works (Dining/Washroom/Crèche Facility)

## Overview

Bill of Quantities for electrical works — Washroom Facility in Faculty & Staff Dining Hall, Crèche & Admin Store, DAU Gandhinagar (September 2025).

## Main Content

Key BOQ items:
- Copper flexible circuit wiring (various gauges) — 140 rmtr total
- Single/loop light control points — 10 + 12 nos.
- Fan control point — 1 no.
- AC power point (16 Amp) — 1 no.; general power points (6 Amp) — 10 nos.
- 22W and 15W LED down-lights (2-year warranty) — 14 + 12 nos.
- 300mm Crompton exhaust fans — 3 nos.
- PVC conduit pipe laying — 100 rft
- Module MS back boxes — 24 nos.
