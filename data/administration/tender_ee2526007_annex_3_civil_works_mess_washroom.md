# Tender EE2526007 — Annex 3 Civil Works (Mess Washroom)

## Overview

Bill of Quantities for civil works — Washroom Renovation of Student's Mess, DAU, Gandhinagar (September 2025).

## Main Content

Key BOQ items:
- Demolition of existing brick masonry, floor tiles, and wall tiles
- Chemical waterproofing (2mm, 10-year leakage guarantee) — 460 sqft
- New 4½" brick masonry with copping — 100 sqft
- Rough coat plaster with GI chicken mesh — 1,600 sqft
- Granite door/window frames (₹120/sqft) — 200 sqft
- Interior/exterior paint — 600 + 1,000 sqft
- False ceiling (600x600 waterproof tiles) — 300 sqft
- Waterproof/termite-proof flush doors — 6 nos.
- **Toilets:** CERA Single Flush Cistern, EWC CORDO — 4 units
- **Washbasins:** CERA CLORA half-pedestal — 3 units
- **Urinals:** CERA CAPRIANA — 2 units
- Antiskid floor tiles (₹40/sqft) — 460 sqft; glossy wall tiles (₹30/sqft) — 1,600 sqft
- Exhaust fan frames — 4 nos.; Mirrors (24"x18" and 60"x24") — 4 nos.
- Floor GT/clean-out points — 12 nos.
- UPVC water supply lines and PVC SWR drainage piping
- Masonry drainage chambers (2 sizes) — 7 nos.
- MS fabrication, aluminium partition work, FRP weather shed
