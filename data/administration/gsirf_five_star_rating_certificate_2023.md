# GSIRF Five Star Rating Certificate — 2023

## Overview

Official certificate rating DA-IICT (Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology) as a Five Star Institution in the "University" category under the Gujarat State Institutional Ratings Framework (GSIRF).

## Main Content

The Executive Committee of the Knowledge Consortium of Gujarat, Department of Education, Government of Gujarat — on the recommendation of the Indian Centre for Academic Rankings and Excellence (ICARE) — rated Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology as a **Star Institution** in the category of 'University' based on comprehensive performance metrics under GSIRF, on **28 February 2023**.

## Important Information

- **Rating:** Five Star Institution (University category)
- **Date:** 28 February 2023
- **Framework:** Gujarat State Institutional Ratings Framework (GSIRF)
- **Assessment agency:** Indian Centre for Academic Rankings and Excellence (ICARE)
