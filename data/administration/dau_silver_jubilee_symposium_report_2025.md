# DAU Silver Jubilee Symposium Report 2025

## Overview

Report on the Symposium on Pedagogies in Engineering Education, held on 15 November 2025 as part of Dhirubhai Ambani University's (DAU) Silver Jubilee celebrations marking 25 years of academic excellence.

## Main Content

### Event Details
Dhirubhai Ambani University (formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar, organized a **Symposium on Pedagogies in Engineering Education** on **15 November 2025** at its campus, as part of the year-long Silver Jubilee (25th anniversary) celebrations.

### Background — SPARK Centre
DAU recently created a **Centre for Teaching and Learning called "SPARK"**, with a mandate to strengthen, modernise, and enrich the university's teaching-learning ecosystem. This symposium was the **first extension event** organized under the SPARK umbrella.

### Organizing Team
Conceived and organized by a team of faculty including:
- Prof. Rachit Chhaya
- Prof. Sunitha Vadivel Murugan
- Prof. Sanjay Srivastava
- (and additional faculty members)

### Context — Silver Jubilee Year
2025 marked DAU's Silver Jubilee (25 years since founding in 2001). The university ran a series of Silver Jubilee events throughout the year, including:
- Silver Jubilee Alumni Meet/Reunion (27–28 December 2025) — for founding batches (2001 onward)
- Silver Jubilee Sports Day for employees (23 November 2025)
- Workshop on Smart Energy and Sustainability by SELC (19–20 May 2026)
