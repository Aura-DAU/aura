## About the Institute  
DA-IICT, established as a premier institution for information and communication technology, focuses on:  
- Academic excellence with NAAC ‘A+’ accreditation  
- Research in emerging technologies (e.g., VLSI, cybersecurity)  
- Industry-aligned programs to produce skilled engineers
