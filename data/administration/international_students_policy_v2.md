# International Students Policy — DAFS Category

## Overview

DAU (formerly DA-IICT) has a dedicated admission pathway for international and NRI students under the **Direct Admission of Foreign and NRI Students (DAFS)** category, managed by the International Student Cell (ISC).

## Main Content

### Admission Overview

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Managing Body | International Student Cell (ISC) |
| Seats Reserved | ~10% of total seats for NRI/Foreign nationals |
| Application | Through official university portal |
| Accepted Tests | SAT (for BTech), AIU-recognized qualifications |

### Eligibility Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Class 12 qualification | Must include Physics + Mathematics + (Chemistry / CS / Biotech / Biology) |
| Board recognition | Must be recognized by Association of Indian Universities (AIU) |
| Minimum marks | 60% aggregate or GPA 6/10 |
| Age | Born on or after October 1, 2001 (or as per current year's notification) |

### Key Policies for International Students

| Policy | Details |
|---|---|
| Category stability | Students admitted under DAFS must remain in this category throughout — switching not permitted |
| FRRO Registration | Foreign passport holders must register with the Foreign Regional Registration Office (FRRO), Govt. of India |
| On-campus residence | BTech students are required to reside on campus |

### Fee Structure for DAFS Category

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tuition Fee | ~$6,000 USD per semester |
| Registration Fee | As applicable |
| Hostel Fee | As applicable |
| Caution Deposit | As applicable |

## Important Information

- International students get dedicated support from the ISC throughout their studies.
- FRRO registration must be completed **immediately upon admission**.
- International students must maintain valid visa status throughout their studies.
- All policies regarding conduct, attendance, and academics apply equally to international students.
