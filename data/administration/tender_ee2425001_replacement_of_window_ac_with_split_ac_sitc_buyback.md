# Tender EE2425001 — Replacement of Window AC with Split AC (SITC + Buyback)

## Overview

Open Tender for Supply, Installation, Testing, and Commissioning (SITC) of Split AC units with buyback of old Window AC units at DAU, Gandhinagar.

## Main Content

- **Contact:** Mr. Rajesh Patel, Sr. Estate Engineer — estate_engineer@daiict.ac.in, +91 93287 21608
- **Site visit deadline:** 9 April 2025
- **Submission deadline:** 16 April 2025, 3:00 PM
- **Tender Fee:** ₹5,900 (non-refundable), via DD or NEFT/RTGS
- **Security Deposit:** 5% of order value, refunded 30 days after 1-year work completion period
- **Award Criteria:** Lowest cost (L1); if L1 declines, L2 awarded
- **Supply payment:** 75% released in 30 days, balance 30 days after installation
