# Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC): Act of Indiscipline and Indicative List of Punishment

## Overview

DAU's Disciplinary Action Committee (DAC) document defining categories of student indiscipline and indicative (non-exhaustive) punishments, dated 1 March 2024, signed by Dean (Students). References the Disciplinary Guidelines for Students (March 2024) and Student Code of Conduct.

## Main Content

### Disciplinary Procedure
1. Deputy Registrar (Academic) issues notice for the student to appear before DAC
2. If found guilty, student is placed on Disciplinary Probation with appropriate punishment
3. Parents informed in writing
4. Parents may request to meet the DAC

### Indicative Punishment Table
| Offense Category | Minimum Punishment |
|---|---|
| Ragging | Disciplinary probation until graduation; academic suspension 1 semester |
| Possession/consumption of intoxicants (drugs, alcohol) | — |
| Criminal activity | Major |
| Bullying, physical assault/violence, threatening | — |
| Firearms/weapons possession | — |
| Gambling/betting | — |
| Smoking (first time) | Probation 1 semester; hostel suspension |
| Improper behaviour with student/faculty/staff | Probation until graduation; academic suspension 1 semester |
| Violation of Halls of Residence (HoR) rules | Probation until graduation; hostel suspension |
| Damage/destruction of property | Probation until graduation; cost recovery + fine + academic suspension 1 semester |
| Theft (incl. Institute property) | — |
| Traffic rule violation on campus | Probation until graduation; cost recovery + fine |
| Causing disturbance to co-residents | Hostel suspension and/or social service (min. 1 semester) |
| Visiting HoR of opposite gender | Probation until graduation; hostel suspension |
| Misuse of DA-IICT computing/network | Probation until graduation; cost recovery + fine + academic suspension 1 semester |
| Defamation of DA-IICT/staff/faculty/students | — |
| Disruption of teaching/research/admin activities | Probation until graduation; academic suspension 1 semester |
| Failure to appear before DAC | — |
| Forgery (incl. forging signatures) | — |
| Misrepresentation for financial aid | — |
| Any other unseemly behaviour | — |
| Violation during DAC proceedings | Probation until graduation; academic suspension 1 semester |
| Passive participation (e.g. sheltering hostel-out student) | Probation for 1 semester; hostel suspension |

Note: finding liquor bottles, smoking materials, or strong smell of smoke/alcohol in a room is adequate reason for hostel authorities to initiate action against room residents.

### Appeal
The Appellate Mechanism defined in the Disciplinary Guidelines for Students remains unchanged.

## Important Information

- **Issued by:** Dean (Students)
- **Related documents:** Disciplinary Guidelines for Students (March 2024), Student Code of Conduct
