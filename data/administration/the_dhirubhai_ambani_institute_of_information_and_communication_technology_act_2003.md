# The Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology Act, 2003

## Overview

Gujarat Act No. 6 of 2003 — the founding legislation establishing DA-IICT as a University in Gujarat, as modified up to 31 May 2012. Assented to by the Governor on 6 March 2003.

## Main Content

### Purpose
An Act to establish the Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology, Gujarat, and confer university status upon it.

### Key Provisions
- **Establishment (Sec. 3):** Non-affiliating University at Gandhinagar; does not affiliate other colleges; receives no grant-in-aid from Central/State Government, UGC, or AICTE.
- **Objects (Sec. 4):** Create centres of excellence in ICT education/training/research; develop multimedia content capabilities; upgrade ICT infrastructure to global standards; function as learning resource centre for ICT entrepreneurship; establish industry linkages.
- **Non-discrimination (Sec. 5):** No exclusion based on sex, race, creed, caste, class, birthplace, or religious/political opinion.
- **Powers (Sec. 6):** Confer degrees/diplomas, conduct research, establish hostels, fix fees, award fellowships/scholarships, acquire property, borrow money, create academic/administrative posts.

### Governance Structure
- **President (Sec. 7):** Patron Trustee of the Dhirubhai Ambani Memorial Trust, Mumbai, serves for life
- **Authorities (Sec. 8):** Board of Governors, Academic Council, Finance Committee
- **Officers (Sec. 9):** Director, Deans, Executive Registrar

### Board of Governors (Sec. 10)
Includes: President (Chairman), 2 Trust representatives, Director, 2 Deans (rotating), Secretary (Higher & Technical Education, Govt. of Gujarat), Secretary (Science & Technology, Govt. of Gujarat), 3 academician experts, 3 discipline experts (finance/legal/management/humanities), 2 industry representatives. Term: 3 years.

### Academic Council (Sec. 14)
Director (Chairman, ex officio), 2 nominated academicians, 2 external academicians/professionals, 2 rotating Deans, 1 Professor per discipline (rotating), Executive Registrar (non-member Secretary).

### Finance Committee (Sec. 16)
Director (Chairman), 1 Board member, 1 rotating Dean, 1 expert, Executive Registrar (non-member Secretary).

### Director (Sec. 18-19)
Appointed by the Board from a panel; term not exceeding 5 years; serves as Chief Executive and Academic Officer.

### Financial Provisions
- **Permanent Endowment Fund (Sec. 22):** ₹5 crore or 3 years' operational expenditure, placed by the Trust in government-backed securities
- **Accounts (Sec. 25):** Audited annually by a statutory Chartered Accountant; internal audit also required

### Regulations (Sec. 32)
The Board may make Regulations covering meetings, committee constitution, admissions, discipline, degrees/diplomas, examinations, staff appointments, fees, pension/provident schemes, budgets, and hostels.

## Important Information

- **Act:** Gujarat Act No. 6 of 2003
- **Assented by Governor:** 6 March 2003
- **As modified up to:** 31 May 2012
- **Total sections:** 35
- Later amended by the DA-IICT (Amendment) Act, 2024 (Gujarat Government Gazette Vol. LXV, 13 May 2024)
