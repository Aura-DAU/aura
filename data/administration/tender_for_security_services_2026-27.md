# Tender for Security Services 2026-27

## Overview

Tender for Security Services at DAU, Gandhinagar for 2026-27. Tender No. Tender/Admin/Security/26-27/001.

## Main Content

- **Contact:** Mr. Kirit Pandya, Manager Administration — +91 93270 43616
- **Submission deadline:** 2 April 2026, 11:00 AM
- **Tender Fee:** ₹5,900 (non-refundable)
- **Security Deposit:** 5% of order value
- **Award Criteria:** Past/current experience in higher education institutions + competitive financial bid (not lowest-cost only)
- **Required licenses:** PSARA License, Certificate of Incorporation, Labour License, EPF/ESIC Registration, Professional Tax Registration, Workmen's Compensation Policy, Police Clearance Certificates

### Personnel Requirements
- **Security Officer/Supervisor:** Must be Ex-Servicemen (Officer rank not below JCO), computer literate, fluent in Hindi & English
- **Security Guard:** 12th pass, age 25–40 years
- Deployment example: 1 Officer, 2 Supervisors, 28 Guards (26 male + 2 female)
- Uniform: 2 sets with badges, cap, safety shoes, raincoat, torches, batons, whistles
- Emergency response within 1 hour required

### Penalties
| Violation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Inadequate deployment | ₹1,000/instance |
| Smoking/tobacco while on duty | ₹500/instance |
| Unauthorized changes/mobile use on duty | ₹500/instance |
| Misbehavior | ₹1,000/instance |
| Improper uniform/PPE | ₹1,000/instance |

### Payment
Monthly billing, payment within 10 working days after TDS deduction. Wages follow Gujarat State Minimum Wages. Gratuity paid only at actuals on valid claim.
