# Policies/Conduct Rules

## Overview

This document outlines the policies and conduct rules for employees at Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT).

## Main Content

### Personnel Policy

DA-IICT is committed to building a world-class educational institution with the best talents from around the world. The personnel policy aims to provide opportunities for employees to achieve excellence and promote a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship.

* DA-IICT believes in providing equal opportunities to all employees, regardless of caste, creed, religion, nationality, color, or sex.
* Merit and performance are the sole criteria for selection and recognition of employees at all levels.
* The Institute encourages faculty members to undertake entrepreneurial activities while working, with a scheme of dual functions in the classroom and outside.

### Classification of Staff

The Institute has classified its staff into different categories:

* Academic and Teaching:
	+ Director
	+ Chair Professors
	+ Research Professors
	+ Professors
	+ Associate Professors
	+ Assistant Professors
	+ Distinguished Professors
	+ Adjunct Professors
	+ Visiting Professors
* Other Academic:
	+ Research Associates
	+ Research Assistants
	+ Technical Assistants
	+ Other posts created to assist in academic activities
* Academic Related:
	+ Registrar, Deputy Registrar, Assistant Registrar
	+ Controller of Examination, Joint Controller of Examination
	+ Librarian, Deputy Librarian, Assistant Librarian
	+ Data Processing Staff
	+ System Manager/System Analyst/System Engineer
* Administrative and Technical:
	+ Estate Manager/Engineers and other Engineers
	+ Other staff members, including Secretarial staff, Laboratory and Workshop staff

### Opportunities for Entrepreneurial Initiative

The Institute encourages faculty members to undertake entrepreneurial activities, with a scheme of dual functions in the classroom and outside.

### Age of Retirement

* Teaching staff: 65 years
* Non-teaching staff: 60 years

### Service Conditions, Conduct Rules, and Disciplinary Provisions

* All employees are subject to the Service Conditions, Conduct Rules, and Disciplinary Provisions laid down in this document.
* Employees are required to devote their whole time to the service of the Institute and not engage in any trade or business that may interfere with their duties.
* The appointing authority has the power to terminate the services of any member of the staff by giving 3 months' notice or on payment of 3 months' salary in lieu thereof.
* Employees may terminate their engagement by giving 3 months' notice, subject to the appointing authority's discretion.

### Conduct Rules

* Employees are required to maintain absolute integrity and devotion to duty and be strictly honest and impartial in their official dealings.
* Employees are required to be courteous in their dealings with other members of the staff, students, and members of the public.
* Employees are required to observe the scheduled hours of work and be present at their place of duty.
* Employees are not allowed to be absent from duty without prior permission, except for valid reasons or unforeseen contingencies.
* Employees are not allowed to leave the station without prior permission, even during leave or vacation.
* Employees are required to inform the Head of the Department or Director of their address during their absence from station.

### Connection with Press or Radio/TV or Patents

* Employees are not allowed to own wholly or in part any newspaper or other periodical/publication without prior sanction from the competent authority.
* Employees are allowed to broadcast/telecast or contribute to any purely literary, artistic, scientific, or academic character without prior approval.
* Employees are at liberty to publish their original scientific, academic, literary work in journals of repute in India and abroad without sanction.

### Criticism of the Institute

* Employees are not allowed to make any statement of fact or opinion that has the effect of an adverse criticism of any current or recent policy or action of the Institute.
* Employees are not allowed to make any statement that is capable of embarrassing the relations between the Institute and the Central Government or any State Government or any other institution or organization or members of the public.

### Evidence before Committee or any other authority

* Employees are not allowed to give evidence in connection with any inquiry conducted by any person, committee, or authority without prior sanction from the competent authority.
* Employees giving evidence are not allowed to criticize the policy or any action of the Institute or the Central Government or any State Government.

### Unauthorized communication of information

* Employees are not allowed to communicate any official documents or information to any person to whom they are not authorized to communicate such document or information.

### Private Trade or employment

* Employees are not allowed to engage directly or indirectly in any trade or business or any private tuition or undertake any employment outside their official arrangement without prior permission from the competent authority.
* The above restrictions do not apply to academic work and consultative practice undertaken with prior permission from the competent authority.
