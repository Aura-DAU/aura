## About the Institute  
Founded in 2001 by Shri Dhirubhai Ambani, DA-IICT aims to build a knowledge-led society through ICT education. Key facts:  
- **Status**: University under Gujarat State Legislature (2003), UGC-recognized.  
- **Programs**:  
  - Undergraduate: B.Tech (ICT), B.Tech (ICT) Hons. with Computational Science minor.  
  - Postgraduate: M.Tech (ICT), M.Sc (IT), M.Des (Communication Design), PhD.  
- **Alumni**: Over 4,500 graduates globally.  
- **Research**: Government-funded Incubation Center, industry-funded doctoral programs.  
- **Infrastructure**: 50-acre eco-friendly campus with rainwater harvesting and biodiversity.

## Admissions 2017-18  
### Process  
- **50% seats**: Managed by Gujarat’s ACPC.  
- **50% seats**: Direct admission via JEE Main 2017 ranks.  
- **NRI/Foreign students**: Based on SAT/JEE Main scores.  

### Undergraduate Programs  
- **B.Tech (ICT)**: 360 seats (revised from 300), including 54 for NRI/Foreign students.  
- **B.Tech (ICT) Hons.**: 70 seats with Computational Science minor.  

### Eligibility & Guidelines  
- Reserved category admissions follow state guidelines.  
- Counseling conducted via online system.
