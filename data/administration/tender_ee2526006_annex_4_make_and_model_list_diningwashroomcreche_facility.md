# Tender EE2526006 — Annex 4 Make and Model List (Dining/Washroom/Crèche Facility)

## Overview

Approved make/model list for civil and electrical materials — Faculty & Staff Dining Hall, Crèche & Admin Store Washroom Facility, DAU Gandhinagar.

## Main Content

**Civil materials:** Century Ply (plywood/block board, waterproof), 1mm ISI Laminate (Shumard Walnut), Jindal Aluminium, Saint Gobain/Modi Guard Glass, Ambuja/Ultratech Cement, Roff Tile Adhesive, ROSSA Washroom Dado, Supreme UPVC/PVC pipes, Prayag/Goldline PTMT fixtures, Gyproc Saint Gobain Gypsum Board, Asian Paint.

**Electrical materials:** Hi-Fi Modular switches/sockets, Finolex/Havells/Popper FRLS copper wires, Vraj PVC conduit pipes, Astha/Amex/Astro LED lights (2-year warranty).

## Important Information

- **Tender No.:** EE2526006, Annexure 4
