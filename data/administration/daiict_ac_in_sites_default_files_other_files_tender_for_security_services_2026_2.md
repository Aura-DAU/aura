- Compliance with Labour Laws and Workmen Compensation Act is mandatory.  
- All statutory liabilities for contractor’s employees fall on the contractor.  

---

## Scope of Contractor  
### Security Personnel Deployment  
- Provide replacement guards and deploy competent personnel during leave.  
- Emergency response system must activate within 1 hour.  
- Conduct surprise checks to ensure service quality.  

### Surveillance & Reporting  
- Monitor all incoming/outgoing persons, materials, and vehicles.  
- Maintain daily logs of major/minor events and submit reports to DAU.  
- Investigate theft, fire, or criminal activities and report to DAU.  

### Pre-Employment Requirements  
- Conduct police verification for all candidates.  
- Rotate security personnel as advised by DAU.  
- Submit written reports of incidents to administrative officers daily.  

---

## Minimum Requirements  
### Security Officer & Supervisors  
- Ex-Servicemen with rank not below JCO.  
- Proficient in Hindi/English, computer literacy, and security systems.  

### Security Guards  
- 12th Pass, age 25–40 years.  
- Knowledge of security procedures and systems.
