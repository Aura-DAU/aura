# UGC Notification — Change of Name to Dhirubhai Ambani University

## Overview

Official University Grants Commission (UGC) notification (No. F.9-18/2004(CPP-I/PU), June 2024) confirming the change of name from "Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology" to "**Dhirubhai Ambani University**."

## Main Content

The UGC (Ministry of Education, Government of India) notified that the name of "Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology," Near Indroda Circle, Gandhinagar — already included in the list of State Private Universities under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act 1956 — has been changed to **"Dhirubhai Ambani University," Gandhinagar, Gujarat**, per Gujarat Act No. 6 of 2024, passed by the Gujarat Legislature and notified by the Gujarat Government.

Copies sent to:
- The Registrar, Dhirubhai Ambani University
- Principal Secretary, Higher & Technical Education, Government of Gujarat
- Secretary (Higher Education), Ministry of Education, Government of India
- UGC Website

## Important Information

- **Notification No.:** F.9-18/2004(CPP-I/PU)
- **Date:** June 2024
- **Legal basis:** Gujarat Act No. 6 of 2024
- **Issuing authority:** University Grants Commission (UGC), Ministry of Education, Government of India
