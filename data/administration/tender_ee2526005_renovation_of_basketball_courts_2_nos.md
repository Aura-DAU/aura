# Tender EE2526005 — Renovation of Basketball Courts (2 Nos.)

## Overview

Tender for renovation of 2 basketball courts at DAU, Gandhinagar. Tender No. EE2526005.

## Main Content

- **Contact:** Mr. Rajesh Patel, Sr. Estate Engineer — estate_engineer@daiict.ac.in, +91 93287 21608
- **Site visit:** Friday, 8 August 2025, 10:00–12:00 or 15:00–17:00
- **Submission deadline:** 11 August 2025, 11:00 AM
- **Security Deposit:** 5% of order value
- **Advance Payment:** 30% of order value against equivalent guarantee cheque
- **Completion timeline:** 1 month from possession of site/work order
- **Award Criteria:** Lowest cost among technically qualified bidders (L1)

### Basketball Court Specifications
- **Surface:** 5-layer synthetic acrylic system (resurface, cushion, 2 anti-slip color coats) — 13,500 sqft
- **Base:** Asphalt — WMM (100mm) + BM (40mm) + BC (20mm) + seal coat — 13,500 sqft
- **Backboard:** Acrylic, 183cm x 107cm, 20mm transparent, with dunking ring and nylon net — 2 pairs
- **Standard:** FIBA (International Basketball Federation) norms
- **Pole:** Mild Steel, 8-inch round, 3–5mm thickness; rim height 3.05m (10 ft) per FIBA standard; total pole height 12–14 ft
- **Drainage:** Precast cement drain line, ~650 RFT for both courts
- Court separation via net curtain with guide wire and poles
