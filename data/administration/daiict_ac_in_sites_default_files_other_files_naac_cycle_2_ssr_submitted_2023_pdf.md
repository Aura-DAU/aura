# Self Study Report of DHIRUBHAI AMBANI INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGY (DA-IICT)  
## Executive Summary  

### Introduction  
Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT), established in 2001 by Shri Dhirubhai Ambani, aims to build a knowledge-led society in the fourth industrial revolution era. Legislated as a university in 2003 under Gujarat's act, DA-IICT operates as a society under the Societies Registration Act 1860 and Bombay Public Trust Act 1950. It became a UGC-recognized university in 2003 and joined the Association of Indian Universities (AIU) in 2009.  

DA-IICT focuses on interdisciplinary ICT education and research, emphasizing quality education through state-of-the-art infrastructure, visionary advisory bodies, distinguished faculty, and a vibrant student community. Key achievements include:  
1. High-quality graduates  
2. Research output  
3. Sponsored/consortium projects  
4. Strong placement statistics  
5. Entrepreneurship initiatives  
6. Mentorship of an Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT)  
7. Teacher training programs  
8. International seminars/workshops  
9. National/international collaborations  
10. Social outreach  
11. Environmental consciousness  
12. Innovations  

### Vision and Mission  
**Vision**: To build a knowledge-led society founded on intellectual competitiveness for global leadership.  
**Mission**: To become a first-choice academic institute with high-caliber students, dynamic faculty, and innovative research, fostering global collaboration and civic engagement.  

### Quality Policy  
DA-IICT adheres to global excellence standards in teaching, research, consultancy, and continuing education in ICT. It emphasizes self-evaluation and continuous improvement. Notable recognitions include an A grade from NAAC (2017) and a NIRF ranking around 100.  

## Institutional Strength, Weakness, Opportunity, and Challenges (SWOC)  

### Strengths  
- Recognized as a Center of Excellence by Gujarat (2021)  
- Attracts top students nationally  
- Faculty with strong research records  
- Academic reputation and novel collaborative environment  
- Well-placed alumni and excellent placements  
- Adjunct professorships for international faculty  
- Professor for Practice roles for industry experts  
- Joint programs with IIT Jammu, CR Rao Institute, IIRS Dehradun, and Anand Agricultural University  
- Campus entrepreneurship and incubation center  
- Eco-friendly campus  
- Active student governance in cultural/sports activities  
- Seed grants for new faculty research  
- Professional development funds (CPDA)  
- ERP-driven automation  
- Mandatory rural internships  
- Research-led teaching in ICT  
- Faculty-started startups  
- Anchor institute status for Gujarat faculty mentoring  

### Weaknesses  
- Limited infrastructure for student residences and academic buildings  
- Challenges in retaining top faculty against peer institutions  
- Low international student/faculty diversity  
- Regional diversity gaps in student population  
- Untapped alumni engagement potential  
- Low IPR generation per faculty/research scholar  
- Few incubations/startups from faculty/students  
- Lack of high-visibility research projects  

### Opportunities  
- Expand programs to meet local industry/government needs  
- Leverage credibility for new academic/research initiatives  
- Enhance alumni/industry fundraising  
- Deliver advanced online education and MOOCs  
- Expand interdisciplinary executive training  
- Strengthen domestic/international university ties  
- Attract more international students  
- Boost entrepreneurship opportunities  

### Challenges  
- Attracting high-quality faculty/students  
- Revenue generation for operational scaling  
- Alumni engagement  
- Implementing NEP 2020 reforms  
- Enhancing international research collaborations  

## Curricular Aspects  

DA-IICT specializes in ICT education and research, emphasizing Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS). Curricula focus on foundational knowledge, electives, projects, and industry-readiness. Feedback mechanisms include semester-end student evaluations and exit surveys.  

### Teaching-Learning and Evaluation  
- **Undergraduate admissions**: Based on JEE Main (All India) or GUJCET (Gujarat).  
- **Postgraduate admissions**: MTech via GATE scores; other programs via entrance tests and interviews.  
- Committees monitor student performance, identifying advanced/slow learners for tailored support (e.g., teaching assistantships for advanced learners).
