# Table 3 - Grievance Redressal Authority for Staff

## Overview

This document lists the Grievance Redressal Authority levels for staff members at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), categorized by the nature of the grievances.

## Main Content

## **Table 3:** Grievance Redressal Authority for Staff

| Nature of Grievances | Level-I<br> Grievance Handling Authority | Level-II<br> Grievance Handling Authority | Level-III<br> Grievance Handling Authority |
| :-: | --- | --- | --- |
| Matters related to Human Resources and Administration | Head-HR | Executive Registrar | GRHC |
| Matters related to an action of any member of the faculty | Dean-Faculty | GRHC | Director |
| Matters related to an action of any member of the staff | Executive Registrar | GRHC | Director |
| Matters related to service conditions | Head-HR | Executive Registrar | Director |
