# S4P 2025 Closure Report

## Overview

This is the closure report for the **7th Edition of the ISCA-Supported Summer School on Speech Signal Processing (S4P 2025)**, held July 5–9, 2025 at the Speech Research Lab, Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), Gandhinagar. The theme of the school was **Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)**.

## Main Content

### Organizing Chair
Prof. (Dr.) Hemant A. Patil, Speech Research Lab, DAU Gandhinagar (ISCA Distinguished Lecturer 2020–2022, APSIPA Distinguished Lecturer 2018–2019).

### About S4P 2025
S4P is an educational outreach initiative of the Speech Research Lab at DAU. This was the 7th edition, following S4P 2024, S4P 2019, S4P 2018, S4P 2017, S4P 2016, and ASAP 2016. The 2025 theme, Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), covered near-field/far-field speech, acoustic and language models, and signal degradation challenges relevant to Intelligent Personal Assistants (Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant, Bixby, etc.).

### Patron
Prof. Tathagata Bandyopadhyay, Director General, DAU Gandhinagar.

### Deepfake Challenge — 1st Edition
Introduced the **MLADDC (Multi-Lingual Audio DeepFake Detection Corpus)** — 20 languages, 400K+ files (1,125+ hours), one of the largest datasets of its kind. 9 teams (7 submissions) from India and the USA competed. Winners: Priyanshi K. Patel (1st, M.Tech ML, DAU), Sneha Vijay Ilame (2nd, NFSU Gandhinagar), Patel Parth Rajeshbhai (3rd, M.Tech ML, DAU).

### Technical Program
40 lectures (L1–L40) covering ASR fundamentals, self-supervised learning, phase processing, multilingual speech tokenization, quantization of neural models, speech LLMs, and audio deepfake detection.
