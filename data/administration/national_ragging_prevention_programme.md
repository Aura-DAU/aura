# National Ragging Prevention Programme

## Overview

DA-IICT's poster/notice publicizing the National Ragging Prevention Programme, UGC's national anti-ragging helpline, and monitoring agency contacts. Content sourced from a screenshot of the document (single-page notice).

## Main Content

**National Anti-Ragging Helpline**
- 24x7 Toll Free: **1800-180-5522**
- Email: helpline@antiragging.in
- Website: www.antiragging.in

**UGC Monitoring Agency**
- Centre for Youth (C4Y)
- Email: antiragging@c4yindia.org
- Website: www.c4yindia.org

**Contact Details of the Anti-Ragging Committee and Squad**
- Links provided to Anti-Ragging Committee (ARC) and Anti-Ragging Squad (ARS) contact details (institution-specific listing, not captured in the screenshot).

**Warning Notice**
"RAGGING IS A CRIMINAL OFFENCE AND THE CULPRITS WILL ATTRACT PUNITIVE ACTION AS MENTIONED IN THE UGC REGULATIONS" — with reference to UGC Annexure-I (www.antiragging.in/assets/pdf/annexure/Annexure-I.pdf).

Footer reference: www.ugc.ac.in

## Important Information

- **Type:** Single-page notice/poster (standard UGC-mandated anti-ragging disclosure)
- **Legal basis:** UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging
- **Monitoring agency:** Centre for Youth (C4Y), on behalf of UGC
- Note: This content was extracted from a screenshot image of the notice rather than the full source PDF; institution-specific Anti-Ragging Committee/Squad contact names are not included here — see the related documents `Anti_Ragging_Vigilance_Committee_2024-25.pdf` or `Anti-RaggingCommittee.pdf` on daiict.ac.in for named contacts.
