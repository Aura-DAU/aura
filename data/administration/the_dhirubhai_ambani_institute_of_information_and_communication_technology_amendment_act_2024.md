# DA-IICT (Amendment) Act, 2024

## Overview

Gujarat Act No. 6 of 2024, published in the Gujarat Government Gazette (Vol. LXV) on 13 May 2024, assented to by the Governor on 22 March 2024. This Act transforms DA-IICT (Institute) into **Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU)** and dramatically expands its scope beyond ICT.

## Main Content

### Key Changes
- **Renaming:** "Institute of Information and Communication Technology" → "**University**" throughout the Act. The institution becomes **Dhirubhai Ambani University**.
- **Expanded Objects (New Section 4):** Beyond ICT, the University's objects now cover Science, Technology, Engineering, Gas, Oil, Mining, Energy, Environment, Sustainability, Medical Science, Healthcare, Dental, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Paramedical, Pharmacy, Commerce, Management, Law, Humanities, Literature, Social Sciences, Political Science, Economics, Education, Architecture, Urban Planning, Design, Fashion, Arts & Crafts, Performing Arts, Mass Media, Film, Drama, Journalism, Sports, Dairy, Animal Husbandry, Agriculture, Farming, Horticulture, Forestry, Fisheries, Skill Development, and interdisciplinary areas — consistent with **NEP 2020**.
- **New Leadership Structure:** "Director" → "**Director General**"; introduces "**Directors**" (new Section 19A) to head individual faculties/departments/institutes/centres.
- **Board of Governors:** Now includes 2 rotating Directors nominated by the Director General.
- **Academic Council:** Includes 2 rotating Directors; scope expanded across all new disciplines.
- **Finance Committee:** Includes 1 rotating Director.
- **Deans (New Section 21):** Appointed by Director General in consultation with the relevant Director, with President's approval.
- **New capabilities:** Online/distance learning, e-learning, study/examination centres within and outside Gujarat (including overseas, subject to regulatory approval), Choice Based Credit System (CBCS).

### Transitory Provisions
Existing Board, Academic Council, Finance Committee, and Regulations continue until reconstituted under the new Act.

## Important Information

- **Act:** Gujarat Act No. 6 of 2024
- **Assented by Governor:** 22 March 2024
- **Published:** 13 May 2024 (Gujarat Government Gazette, Vol. LXV)
- **Effect:** Converts DA-IICT (Institute) into Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), a multidisciplinary university aligned with NEP 2020
