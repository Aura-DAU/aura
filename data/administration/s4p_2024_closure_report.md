# S4P 2024 Closure Report

## Overview

Closure report for the 6th Edition of the ISCA-Supported Summer School on Speech Signal Processing (S4P 2024), held 6–10 July 2024 at the Speech Research Lab, DA-IICT, Gandhinagar. Theme: Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR).

## Main Content

### About S4P 2024
S4P 2024 was the sixth edition following S4P 2019, S4P 2018, S4P 2017, S4P 2016, and ASAP 2016. Aimed at graduate/postgraduate students, faculty, and industry researchers interested in speech signal processing, particularly Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) — the technology behind Intelligent Personal Assistants like Siri, Cortana, Google Assistant, Alexa, Bixby, and Watson.

### Key Features
- **5 Minutes PhD Thesis Contest (5MPT):** 5th edition, four cash prizes for Ph.D. scholars
- **Industry Perspective Talks:** 2nd edition — special sessions with speech technology industry experts
- **On-spot Poster Session:** held during tea/lunch breaks
- International program committee with world-renowned researchers from academia and industry

### Organizing Body
Speech Research Lab, DA-IICT, Gandhinagar
