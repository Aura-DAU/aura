# GSIRF Five Star Rating Certificate — 2024-25

## Overview

Official certificate rating Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology as a Five Star Institution in the "University" category under GSIRF, valid until 31 May 2025.

## Main Content

The Executive Committee of the Knowledge Consortium of Gujarat, Department of Education, Government of Gujarat — on the recommendation of ICARE (Indian Centre for Academic Rankings and Excellence) — rated the university as a **Star Institution** based on comprehensive performance metrics under GSIRF, on **2 December 2024**. Valid up to 31 May 2025.

**Signatories:**
- Sunaina Tomar, IAS — Additional Chief Secretary (Higher and Technical Education), Government of Gujarat
- Gaurav Dinesh Ramesh, IAS — Director (Higher Education), Government of Gujarat
- Dr. Karthick Sridhar — Vice Chairman, ICARE
- Prof. A.U. Patel — Advisor, Knowledge Consortium of Gujarat

## Important Information

- **Rating:** Five Star Institution (University category)
- **Date:** 2 December 2024
- **Valid until:** 31 May 2025
- **Framework:** Gujarat State Institutional Ratings Framework (GSIRF)
