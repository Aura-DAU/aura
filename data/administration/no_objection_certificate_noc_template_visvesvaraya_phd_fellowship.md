# No Objection Certificate (NOC) Template — Visvesvaraya PhD Fellowship

## Overview

Annexure-I template for the "No Objection Certificate" (NOC) required from candidates applying to DAU's Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme in the External Part-Time category.

## Main Content

### Context — Visvesvaraya Ph.D. Scheme at DAU
The Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme for Electronics and IT is a flagship Government of India (MeitY) program under Digital India, aimed at enhancing the number of PhDs in Electronics System Design & Manufacturing (ESDM) and IT/IT-enabled Services (IT/ITES). Priority areas include ESDM, IT/ITES, VLSI, and ASIC Design.

### NOC Requirement
A candidate applying under the **External Part-Time category** must:
- Be currently employed at an R&D Organization or University/Institution
- Have at least 3 years of relevant experience
- Submit a 'No Objection Certificate' (NOC) issued by the head of their employing organization/institution (this document is the required template, Annexure-I)

### Scheme Highlights (Phase II, Round II — MeitY funded)
- Monthly scholarship: ₹38,750 (first 2 years), ₹43,750 (remaining 3 years) — support up to 5 years
- Reimbursement of Rent (ROR) if accommodation not provided by Institute
- Annual contingency grant: up to ₹1,20,000/year
- One-time international conference support: ₹1.5 lakh/full-time PhD
- One-time support for visits to labs abroad
- One-time completion incentive: ₹3.00 lakh (via DBT) upon successful PhD completion
- Funds disbursed directly to the student's verified bank account by the Ministry

## Important Information

- **Applicable to:** External Part-Time PhD candidates under the Visvesvaraya PhD Scheme
- **Funding body:** Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India
