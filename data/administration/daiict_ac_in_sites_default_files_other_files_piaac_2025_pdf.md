Note:
The Committee will be responsible for policy guidelines and implementation strategies for
placements and academic internships. The committee will deal with:
I.
All disciplinary matters related to a students' placement, job, and academic
internship come under the purview of the committee, including (but not limited to)
misbehavior of a student(s) in a company, bad-mouthing about DAU at a company,
bad-mouthing about a company at the DAU, doing an industrial internship along
with doing Academic Internship under a faculty member (either from DAU and/or
from outside University/College/Institute) without informing both the parties, etc.

1. PIAAC may impose a monetary fine based on the severity of the offence. Such fines
may be deducted from the student’s caution deposit.

2. A dedicated ledger titled “PJAAC Fine” will be maintained under DAU’s bank account
and all collections will be utilized solely for DAU placement activities.

3. Appeals: Students may submit a single appeal to the Director General within two weeks
of receiving a decision from PIAAC, supported by documentary evidence. The Director
General’s decision shall be final and binding.

4. Any other offence not listed above shall be equated by PIAAC to the closest relevant

category (Offense/Breach/Violation) based on its nature and circumstances.
