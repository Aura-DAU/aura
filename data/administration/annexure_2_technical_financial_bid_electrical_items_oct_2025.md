# Annexure 2 — Technical & Financial Bid: Electrical Items (Oct 2025)

## Overview

Detailed electrical items list (Annexure 2) for the One Time Purchase Open Inquiry EE2425004, DAU Gandhinagar — 92 line items.

## Main Content

Item categories in the 92-item list:
- **Copper wires/cables** (1–6 Sqmm, FR/FRLS grade) — Finolex/Havells/Polycab, various coil quantities
- **LED flood lights** (60W–200W, various color temperatures) — Phinix Technology, 2-year warranty, up to 24 units per spec
- **LED surface/panel lights** (15W–22W) — Astha/Astro/Amex, 2-year warranty, up to 300 units
- **LED pathway/street lights** (12W–100W) — Phinix Technology
- **MCBs** (10A–63A, SP/DP/FP) — MDS Legrand/L&T Hager, 12–20 units each
- **Cable ties, clamps** — 200–400 units
- **Fan regulators & modular switches/sockets/plates** — Hi-Fi brand, various sizes, up to 200 units
- **Ceiling fans** (Crompton HS Plus, 1200mm) — 30 units; **Exhaust fans** (Almonard/Crompton) — 46 units total
- **Tube lights/LED battens** (2'/4', various color temps) — Philips/Magik Plus/Phinix/Maxima
- **PVC pipes, bends, junctions, casing capping** — Nihir/Vraj brand
- **Digital timers** (Frontier/Legrand) — 24 units
- **Marshal boxes, switch back boxes** — various sizes
