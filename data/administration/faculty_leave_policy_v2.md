# Faculty and Staff Leave Policy

## Overview

This document outlines the leave entitlements and procedures for faculty and staff members at DAU (formerly DA-IICT).

## Main Content

### Leave Entitlements — Faculty

| Leave Type | Entitlement | Key Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave | **8 days per calendar year** | Cannot be combined with other leave types |
| Earned Leave | As per service rules | Applied via HOD / Dean approval |
| Medical Leave | As per service rules | Medical certificate required |
| Maternity Leave | As per Government of India rules (26 weeks) | For female employees |
| Paternity Leave | As per applicable rules | For male employees |
| Academic Leave / Study Leave | Subject to Director approval | For higher studies or research |
| CPDA (Conference / Professional Development) | As per CPDA Policy | For attending conferences, workshops |

### Casual Leave — Specific Rules

- **8 days per calendar year** with full pay.
- Cannot be combined with any other category of leave.
- **May be combined** with Sundays and/or holidays, but total combined absence must not exceed **7 days at a stretch**.
- Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays are **not counted** as casual leave.
- Prior formal application and sanction are **generally not required** for casual leave.

### Leave Application Process

- **Casual Leave:** Informal — inform HOD.
- **Other Leave (less than 10 days):** Apply via designated Google Form; approved by **HOD**.
- **Other Leave (10 days or more):** Apply via designated Google Form; approved by **Dean (Faculty)**.

### Important Notes

- Leave is **not a matter of right** — it may be refused or revoked during exigencies of service.
- Faculty and staff on long leave or deputation must **return all borrowed library materials** before leaving.
- All leave records are maintained by the HR / Administration Department.

## Important Information

- Maternity leave: 26 weeks as per the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act 2017.
- Paternity leave: Subject to institute-specific rules; consult HR office.
- Study leave and academic leave require advance planning and Director approval.
- CPDA entitlements are governed by the separate CPDA Policy document.

## Contact Information

- **Office:** HR / Administration Office, DAU, Gandhinagar
- **Sanctioning Authority:** HOD (< 10 days), Dean Faculty (≥ 10 days)
