## New Academic Programs Launched

**MSc in Data Science:** A new degree program focused on solving real-world 
problems in big data, covering Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, 
multimedia and big data analysis. Launched in collaboration with SAS 
(American multinational analytics software company with 40+ years of 
experience) from 2020.

**BTech in Mathematics & Computing (MnC):** A new degree program fusing 
Mathematics and Computer Science, aimed at expanding mathematical, 
algorithmic and computational thinking for careers at the cutting edge of 
technology and research.

**MTech in CSE (Joint with IIT Jammu):** Program offered jointly by IIT Jammu 
and DA-IICT with specialization tracks in Data Science and Information Security.

## Institutional Achievements

- **MoU with University of Auckland, New Zealand** — Academic exchange 
  and cooperation: collaborative research, faculty/staff exchange, and 
  Visiting Scholar programs.

- **DST-FIST Grant (Level 1):** DA-IICT awarded INR 57 lakh. One of 16 
  proposals shortlisted out of 218 nationwide under FIST 2019 
  (Engineering Sciences).

- **SAS Collaboration:** DA-IICT partnered with SAS to launch the 2-year 
  full-time MSc in Data Science from 2020, addressing fact-based 
  decision making in the Big Data era.

## Faculty Achievements

**Prof. Hemant Patil** — Elected ISCA (International Speech Communication 
Association) Distinguished Lecturer for 2020-21. First Indian scientist 
working in an Indian university to receive this recognition. Also held 
APSIPA Distinguished Lecturership during 2018-19.

**Prof. Sanjeev Gupta & Prof. Yash Vasavada** — SAC-ISRO sanctioned a new 
research project: "Adaptive Beam Forming for Mitigation of Interference 
and Jamming at the Ground Terminal of Global Navigational Satellite 
Systems (GNSS)."

**Prof. Bhaskar Chaudhury** — Co-edited a special issue "Finite Difference 
Methods: Recent Developments and Applications in Computational Science" 
in Elsevier Journal of Computational Science (2020), with Prof. Matthias 
Ehrhardt (University of Wuppertal, Germany) and Prof. Vrushali Bokil 
(Oregon State University, USA).

**Prof. Yash Vasavada** — Launched CSPMI (Communication, Signal Processing, 
and Machine Intelligence) Lab with ISRO funding of INR 1.25 Crore; 5 
ongoing research projects with 5 faculty PIs/Co-PIs and 8 JRFs.

## Notable Faculty Appointment

**Prof. Nicholas J. Belkin** — Appointed Adjunct Chair Professor. Distinguished 
Professor Emeritus of Library and Information Science, School of 
Communication and Information, Rutgers University, USA. Known for 
human-centred information retrieval and cognitive viewpoint in information 
science. Recipient of ACM SIGIR Salton Award and ASIS&T Award of Merit.
