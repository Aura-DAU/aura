## Faculty Achievements

**Prof. Manish Khare** — Received Best Special Session Award at the 13th 
Asian Conference on Intelligent Information and Database Systems (ACIIDS 
2021), April 7–9, 2021, Phuket, Thailand for the session 'Computational 
Imaging and Vision (CIV 2021).'

**Prof. Hemant Patil** — Appointed Associate Editor of IEEE Signal Processing 
Magazine (Columns and Forum), part of a select group of Associate Editors 
from reputed universities and research institutions worldwide.

**Prof. Bhaskar Chaudhury** — National Supercomputing Mission sanctioned 
a 2-year research project: 'Computational Investigations of Instability-driven 
Transport in Low Temperature Magnetized Plasma Discharges using Massively 
Parallel 2D-3v PIC-MCC Simulations.'

**Prof. Ahlad Kumar** — GUJCOST sanctioned a 2-year project: 'Design and 
Implementation of a Visibility Improvement Model for Haze Removal in Images 
Acquired from Real Time CCTV Cameras.'

**Prof. Priyanka Singh** — GUJCOST sanctioned a 2-year project: 'A Forensic 
Tool for Land Revenue Documents Forgery Detection.'

**Prof. Avik Hati** — Azure Knowledge Corporation (Ahmedabad, MoU partner) 
awarded a consultancy project: 'Duplicate Object Identification from Video Data.'
