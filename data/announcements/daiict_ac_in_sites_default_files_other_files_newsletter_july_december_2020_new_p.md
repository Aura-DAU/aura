## Faculty Achievements

Prof Hemant Patil delivered ISCA (International Speech Communication 
Association) Distinguished Lecture 2020-21 on 'Generative Adversarial 
Networks (GANs)' on September 22, 2020. He is the first Indian Scientist 
working in an Indian University to receive this international recognition, 
in recognition of his joint research work in Speech and Speaker Recognition 
and Anti-Spoofing Methods for Automatic Speaker Recognition.

## Infrastructure

**PARAM Shavak Supercomputer:** The Gujarat Council on Science and 
Technology, Government of Gujarat, established the Supercomputer facility 
'PARAM Shavak' (developed by C-DAC, Government of India) at DA-IICT. The 
facility provides faculty, researchers and students with advanced technologies 
for high-end computations for scientific and engineering research, High-Performance 
Computing (HPC) and Deep Learning (DL).

## Sponsored Research Projects

- **Dew Water Harvesting (Govt. of Gujarat):** Prof Anil Roy proposed and 
received a grant from the Climate Change Department, Government of Gujarat 
(2 years, starting July 2020) to draw a dew map of Gujarat State to estimate 
annual water production from dew.

- **Indoor Navigation System (DST):** Prof Kalyan Sasidhar (PI) and 
Prof Bhaskar Chaudhury (Co-PI) received a DST grant for 
'Development of GeoMagnetism based Indoor Navigation System' under the 
NRDMS framework.

- **Dynamical Sampling (SERB):** Prof Nabin Kumar Sahu received a Core 
Research Grant from SERB, Government of India for 'Dynamical Sampling and 
Representation of Frames via Iterated Operator Systems'.
