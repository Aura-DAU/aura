# IQAC Member Appointment Authority at DAU

## Key Fact

At Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT), the **Director** is the Chairperson of the IQAC (ex-officio), and by institutional convention and statute, the **Director** holds the authority to constitute and appoint the members of the IQAC.

## DAU-Specific IQAC Appointment Structure

| Role / Category | Appointment Basis |
|---|---|
| Chairperson (Director, DAU) | Ex-officio (automatic by position) |
| Dean (Faculty), Dean (Academic Programs), Dean (Research), Dean (Students), Dean (Alumni & External Relations) | By position (ex-officio members) |
| Convenor, Internal Complaints Committee | By position |
| NEP Co-ordinator | By position |
| Teacher representatives (by name) | **Appointed by the Director** |
| Management representative (Reliance Management) | Nominated by management / Board |
| Executive Registrar | By position |
| Alumni Association President | By position |
| SBG-Convenor (student representative) | By position |
| Student member (by name) | Nominated / appointed |
| NGO representative | Appointed |
| Employer / Industry nominees | Nominated by institution |
| IQAC Director / Coordinator | **Appointed by the Director** |

## Governance Basis

The IQAC was established per NAAC guidelines (post-accreditation quality sustenance measure). Within DAU's statutory framework:

- The **Director** is the head of the institution and chairs the Academic Council.
- The Director's office has the authority to constitute committees and appoint members to institutional bodies, including the IQAC.
- Members appointed "by name" (teacher representatives, student representatives, NGO representatives) are **appointed by the Director** in consultation with relevant stakeholders.
- Members appointed "by position" (ex-officio members) automatically hold IQAC membership by virtue of their designated role.

## Current IQAC Chairperson (as of 2024)

**Prof. Tathagata Bandyopadhyay** – Director, DA-IICT (Chairperson, IQAC)

## Summary Answer

**At DAU, the Director appoints IQAC members (for named/nominated positions). Ex-officio members serve by virtue of their designated positions. The Director also chairs the IQAC. The current composition is effective from 1 August 2024.**

## Note on NAAC vs DAU-Specific

While NAAC guidelines specify minimum composition requirements (management representative, senior administrator, teacher representatives, student representative, employer/industrialist nominee, alumni representative, and coordinator), DAU's actual implementation is through the Director's authority as head of institution and Chairman of the Academic Council.
