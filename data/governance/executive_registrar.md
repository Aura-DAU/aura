# Overview

Profile of Shri Prem Kumar Chopra (P. K. Chopra), the Executive Registrar of Dhirubhai Ambani University (formerly DA-IICT), highlighting his extensive experience in higher education administration, institutional governance, and organizational development.

# Main Content

### Shri Prem Kumar Chopra

Shri P. K. Chopra is a seasoned administrator with over **35 years** of professional experience across private, public, and government organizations of national repute. For nearly 25 years, he has served in the higher education sector, making significant contributions to institutional governance, administration, and organizational development.

### Education

- **B.Sc. (Hons) in Agriculture** — Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar
- **M.Sc. in Horticulture** — Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar
- **Post Graduate Diploma in Rural Management (PGDRM)** — Institute of Rural Management Anand (IRMA)

### Institutional Experience

He has played a pivotal role in strengthening institutional systems and governance frameworks at premier institutions:

- **Registrar**, Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Gandhinagar (approx. 12 years)
- **Registrar**, Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology (DA-IICT) (approx. 10 years)

### Core Areas of Expertise

- Institutional governance
- Strategic planning
- Financial management
- Organizational development
- Human resource management
- Team building

# Important Information

- Over 35 years of professional experience, including 25 years in higher education.
- Previously served as Registrar at IIT Gandhinagar and DA-IICT.
- Recognized for his commitment to good governance, operational excellence, and the development of higher education institutions.
- Currently serves as Executive Registrar at Dhirubhai Ambani University.
