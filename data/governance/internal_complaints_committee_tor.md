> Source: Official DA-IICT Committees T&R document (15 June 2024)

## Terms of Reference

1. Provide guidelines for protection from sexual or any other harassment
2. Advise DA-IICT from time to time in matters of harassment
3. Prepare detailed guidelines on what constitutes harassment and acts that affect dignity of a person
4. Deal with complaints received or referred to the cell in respect of all kinds of harassment
5. Recommend measures to deter any kind of harassment to the Director
6. Deal with other matters connected with harassment to a person
7. Arrange orientation sessions for students and staff on gender sensitization

## How to File a Complaint

- Submit a written complaint to the Convenor (Prof. Minal Bhise) or Co-Convenor (Prof. Shefali Jha)
- Contact through Secretary: Ms. Geeta Nair
- All complaints are treated as strictly confidential
