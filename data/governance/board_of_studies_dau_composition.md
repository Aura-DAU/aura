# DAU Board of Studies – Official Composition and Total Member Count

## Overview

The Board of Studies (BoS) at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU, formerly DA-IICT) is a statutory academic body responsible for overseeing the design, review, and approval of curricula and syllabi for all academic programs. This document provides the DAU-specific composition, which is distinct from generic NAAC guidelines.

## Total Member Count

The BoS at DAU consists of:

| Category | Count |
|----------|-------|
| Chairman (ex-officio) | 1 |
| Core Members – UG Program | 6 |
| Core Members – PG Program | 4 |
| Common Domain External Experts (invited as needed) | 21 |
| Common Industry Representatives (invited as needed) | 8 |
| **Total Permanent/Core Members (excluding invitees)** | **11** |
| **Total including all external and industry members** | **40** |

> **Note:** External experts and industry representatives (marked with *) are invited as subject experts when relevant subjects are taken up for discussion. They are not permanent attendees of every BoS meeting.

## External Domain Experts (invited as needed)

21 external domain experts from institutions such as ISRO, IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Gandhinagar, IIT Guwahati, IIT Kharagpur, Ahmedabad University, Nirma University, and CR Rao Institute are on the BoS panel and are invited when their subject areas are under discussion.

## Industry Representatives (invited as needed)

8 industry representatives from organisations including IBM Research, eInfochips, Aureus Analytics, Evive Software Analytics, Texas Instruments, and Menstrupedia are on the panel and invited as relevant.

## Key Governance Facts (DAU-Specific)

- The BoS is chaired by the **Dean (Academic Programs)** in an ex-officio capacity.
- The Convenors for UG and PG Programs are permanent ex-officio members.
- The **Dean (Research)** and **Dean (Faculty)** are represented on the BoS.
- External experts and industry representatives serve in an advisory capacity and are not required to attend every meeting.
- The total number of **standing core internal members** (Chairman + 6 UG core + 4 PG core, accounting for overlaps) is **approximately 9–11 distinct faculty members**.
