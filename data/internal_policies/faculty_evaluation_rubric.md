# Faculty Evaluation Rubric

This document outlines the evaluation criteria for faculty members at DA-IICT.

## Research Output
Faculty are expected to publish in high-impact journals and conferences.

## Teaching Quality
Student feedback and peer reviews contribute to the teaching quality score.
