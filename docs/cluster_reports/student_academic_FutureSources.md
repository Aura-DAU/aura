# Future Sources: Academics

**Purpose:** Plan future knowledge acquisition to fill the gaps identified in the knowledge base.

### 1. Upcoming Semester Elective Offerings & Syllabi
* **Source Type:** Course Catalog / Timetable
* **Person/Committee:** Dean (Academics) / Academic Administration Office
* **Information Required:** The finalized list of elective courses for the upcoming semester, complete with prerequisites, instructor assignments, and updated syllabi.
* **Expected Outcome:** Allow the RAG system to help students plan their semester effectively by answering queries about upcoming electives.

### 2. Comprehensive B.Tech Honours Guidelines
* **Source Type:** Academic Policy
* **Person/Committee:** Academic Programs Committee
* **Information Required:** The precise CGPA requirements, additional credit hours required, and specific courses eligible for achieving an Honours degree.
* **Expected Outcome:** Accurate guidance for undergraduate students aiming for academic distinction.

### 3. M.Tech / Ph.D. Thesis Submission Timelines
* **Source Type:** Graduate Program Guidelines
* **Person/Committee:** Post-Graduate Committees
* **Information Required:** Exact deadlines for thesis submission, defense scheduling procedures, and formatting guidelines.
* **Expected Outcome:** Resolution of specific queries from graduate students regarding their final deliverables.

### 4. Summer Term Registration Rules
* **Source Type:** Registration Policy
* **Person/Committee:** Academic Administration Office
* **Information Required:** Eligibility criteria for summer term, maximum number of credits allowed, and the fee structure for summer courses.
* **Expected Outcome:** Ability to answer queries for students looking to clear backlogs or accelerate their coursework.

### 5. Detailed Grading Policy Exceptions
* **Source Type:** Examination Guidelines
* **Person/Committee:** Evaluation Committee / Instructors
* **Information Required:** Specific edge cases in grading, such as rules for "I" (Incomplete) grades, supplementary exam caps, and specific course curve policies.
* **Expected Outcome:** Clear answers for students querying unusual grading situations or supplementary requirements.
