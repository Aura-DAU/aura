# Future Sources: Alumni Cluster

**Team C – Alumni Cluster**  
**Done By:** Bhagyashree Khemwani  
**Date:** 2026-06-08  

---

## Purpose
Plan future knowledge acquisition to improve completeness, accuracy, and real-time relevance of alumni-related information in the knowledge base.

---

## 1. Centralized Alumni Database / Directory System
- **Source Type:** Institutional Alumni Portal / Database  
- **Person/Committee:** Alumni Relations Office / IT Services  
- **Information Required:** Structured searchable database of alumni including graduation year, branch, current role, company, and location.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Instant retrieval of alumni profiles and professional details.

---

## 2. Real-Time Alumni Career Tracking System
- **Source Type:** Career Tracking Platform / LinkedIn Integration  
- **Person/Committee:** Alumni Relations Team / Career Services Cell  
- **Information Required:** Updated career progression data such as job changes, promotions, startup ventures, and higher education updates.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Live tracking of alumni professional growth.

---

## 3. Alumni Mentorship Program Platform
- **Source Type:** Mentorship Portal / Engagement System  
- **Person/Committee:** Alumni Association / Student Affairs Office  
- **Information Required:** Structured system connecting students with alumni mentors, including domain mapping, availability, and session tracking.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Strong student–alumni mentorship ecosystem.

---

## 4. Alumni Event Management System
- **Source Type:** Event Management System  
- **Person/Committee:** Alumni Relations Office  
- **Information Required:** Details of alumni meets, webinars, guest lectures, reunions, and participation tracking system.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Better visibility and coordination of alumni engagement events.

---

## 5. Alumni Contribution & Donation Tracking System
- **Source Type:** Finance / Alumni Relations Records  
- **Person/Committee:** Development Office / Alumni Cell  
- **Information Required:** Records of financial contributions, scholarships funded, infrastructure donations, and CSR activities by alumni.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Transparent alumni contribution ecosystem.

---

## 6. Alumni Startup & Entrepreneurship Registry
- **Source Type:** Incubation Cell / Innovation Hub Records  
- **Person/Committee:** E-Cell / Startup & Innovation Office  
- **Information Required:** Database of startups founded by alumni, funding stages, domains, and success metrics.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Visibility of entrepreneurial impact of alumni.

---

## 7. Distinguished Alumni Recognition System
- **Source Type:** Awards & Recognition Committee  
- **Person/Committee:** Director Office / Alumni Relations Committee  
- **Information Required:** Criteria, nomination process, and list of distinguished alumni with achievements and contributions.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Structured recognition and showcase of top alumni.

---

## 8. Alumni Networking & Community Platform
- **Source Type:** Social / Professional Networking Platform  
- **Person/Committee:** Alumni Association / IT Department  
- **Information Required:** Online community space for alumni interaction, group discussions, job postings, and networking opportunities.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Strengthened global alumni network.

---

## 9. Alumni Feedback & Engagement Analytics
- **Source Type:** Survey System / Analytics Dashboard  
- **Person/Committee:** Alumni Relations Office  
- **Information Required:** Feedback on institute experience, engagement levels, participation rates, and satisfaction metrics.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Data-driven improvement in alumni relations.

---

## 10. LinkedIn & External Profile Integration System
- **Source Type:** External API Integration  
- **Person/Committee:** IT Services / Alumni Portal Development Team  
- **Information Required:** Integration of LinkedIn profiles and professional updates to automatically sync alumni career data.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Reduced manual updates and improved data accuracy.
