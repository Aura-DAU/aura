# Knowledge Gaps: Alumni Cluster

**Purpose:** Identify missing knowledge based on failed evaluation questions and system audit.

## Level 1 Gaps (Static Knowledge Missing)

### Gap 1
* **Question:** Is there an official DA-IICT Alumni Association and who are its office bearers?
* **Failure Reason:** Alumni-related pages mention alumni activities and engagement but do not clearly provide details of the alumni association structure.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Alumni association hierarchy, committee members, and contact details.
* **Required Source:** Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 2
* **Question:** How can alumni update their personal and professional information in the alumni database?
* **Failure Reason:** Privacy policies exist, but the update process is not clearly documented.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Procedure for profile updates and account management.
* **Required Source:** Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 3
* **Question:** What benefits are available exclusively to DA-IICT alumni?
* **Failure Reason:** Existing sources highlight engagement activities but do not comprehensively list alumni benefits.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Alumni privileges, services, and institutional support.
* **Required Source:** Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 4
* **Question:** Is there an official alumni directory available for networking?
* **Failure Reason:** Public sources do not provide information regarding alumni directories or networking platforms.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Alumni directory access and networking resources.
* **Required Source:** Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** High

## Level 2 Gaps (Dynamic Information Missing)

### Gap 5
* **Question:** What are the latest achievements of DA-IICT alumni this year?
* **Failure Reason:** Alumni achievements are continuously updated and may not be reflected immediately in existing sources.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Recent awards, promotions, startup launches, and professional accomplishments.
* **Required Source:** Alumni News Portal / Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 6
* **Question:** What upcoming alumni events are scheduled?
* **Failure Reason:** Event information changes frequently and requires regular updates.

* **Required Source:** Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 7

* **Failure Reason:** Only selected events are documented, and newer visits may not yet be published.

* **Required Source:** Event Management Team / Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 8

* **Failure Reason:** Mentorship participation changes over time and is not publicly maintained.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Active alumni mentors and mentorship programs.
* **Required Source:** Alumni Relations Office.
* **Priority:** High
