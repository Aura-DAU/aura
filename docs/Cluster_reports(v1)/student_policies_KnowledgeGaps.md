# Knowledge Gaps: Policies & Administration

**Purpose:** Identify missing knowledge based on failed evaluation questions and system audit.

## Level 1 Gaps (Static Knowledge Missing)

### Gap 1
* **Question:** What are the rules regarding noise levels and playing music on speakers?
* **Failure Reason:** The hostel document says "loud music disrupting the peace of others will not be allowed" but does not specify quiet hours or exact decibel rules.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Explicit quiet hours, speaker-specific rules, and exact fines for noise violations.
* **Required Source:** Chief Warden / Hostel Management Committee.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 2
* **Question:** How do the laundry facilities work? Is it paid per load or included in the fee?
* **Failure Reason:** Laundry details are entirely absent from `hostel_rules_and_regulations.md`.
* **Missing Knowledge:** The operational details of the campus laundry service — pricing, turnaround time, and vendor contact.
* **Required Source:** Hostel Management Committee.
* **Priority:** Low

### Gap 3
* **Question:** General gap identified during system audit — Mess & Canteen Policies.
* **Failure Reason:** The entire `data/` directory lacks any documents regarding the campus mess, cafeteria, or canteen policies.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Official mess timings, mandatory/optional mess subscription, rebate policies, canteen hours, and food wastage rules.
* **Required Source:** Food & Beverage Services / Hostel Management Committee.
* **Priority:** High

## Level 2 Gaps (Dynamic Information Missing)

### Gap 4
* **Question:** What is the latest attendance policy for this semester?
* **Failure Reason:** Attendance policies change frequently and the current data may be outdated.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Current semester's attendance thresholds and tracking mechanisms.
* **Required Source:** Dean (Academics) Office — needs periodic refresh.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 5
* **Question:** What are the current fee structures and due dates?
* **Failure Reason:** Fee information is dynamic and updates every academic year.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Current year's fee breakdown, payment deadlines, and late fee penalties.
* **Required Source:** Accounts Office — needs annual refresh.
* **Priority:** High

## Level 3 Gaps (Human-Provided Knowledge Missing)

### Gap 6
* **Question:** What documents are required to support a leave application for a hackathon or conference?
* **Failure Reason:** The leave policy only covers illness, accident, or death in the family — not co-curricular events.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Policy on attendance waivers and document proof for external events.
* **Required Source:** Dean of Students Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 7
* **Question:** What are the rules for extending an already approved leave if I haven't recovered yet?
* **Failure Reason:** The leave policy does not address extending an absence beyond the initial 3-day window.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Procedure for submitting extension medical certificates.
* **Required Source:** Academic Administration Office.
* **Priority:** High
