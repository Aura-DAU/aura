# Future Sources: Campus Life & Co-curricular

**Purpose:** Plan future knowledge acquisition to fill the gaps identified in the Campus Life & Co-curricular knowledge base.

### 1. Mess & Canteen Policies
* **Source Type:** Operational Document / Guideline
* **Person/Committee:** Cafeteria Management Committee (CMC) / Food & Beverage Services
* **Information Required:** Daily mess menus, mess rebate application forms, food court vendor contracts, pricing rules, and canteen timings.
* **Expected Outcome:** Complete coverage of food-related operations for students.

### 2. Hostel Room Inventories & Maintenance details
* **Source Type:** Asset Inventory / FAQ update
* **Person/Committee:** Hostel Management Committee / Warden Office
* **Information Required:** Explicit policies on room furniture provisions (including mattresses/linen), personal appliances allowances (heaters, kettles, irons), and direct maintenance helpline extensions.
* **Expected Outcome:** Clear guidance for hostellers on what to bring and how to resolve facilities complaints.

### 3. Student Club and Committee Activities & Archives
* **Source Type:** Activities Calendar / Event Archives
* **Person/Committee:** Student Body Government (SBG) Convenor / Club Faculty Mentors
* **Information Required:** Official roster of club achievements, lists of publications (e.g. News & Nonsense editions, Entelechy magazine copies), and calendars of events (HackOut, YouthRun, Concours, Synapse).
* **Expected Outcome:** Comprehensive historical knowledge on co-curricular achievements and schedules.

### 4. Emergency Healthcare & Empanelled Hospitals Contacts
* **Source Type:** Emergency Protocols SOP
* **Person/Committee:** Head of HR & Administration / Medical Centre Admin
* **Information Required:** Direct emergency numbers for empanelled hospitals (such as SGVNS Swaminarayan, Apollo Gandhinagar, and Aashka Hospital), ambulance dispatcher number, and weekend duty registers.
* **Expected Outcome:** Accurate guidance during student medical emergencies.

### 5. Official University Brand & Location Registries
* **Source Type:** Legal Registry / Brand Manual
* **Person/Committee:** Executive Registrar's Office
* **Information Required:** Official university naming protocols (resolving name change from DA-IICT to DAU), contact details page updates, and campus geographic coordinates.
* **Expected Outcome:** Accurate answers for geographic location and legal branding queries.
