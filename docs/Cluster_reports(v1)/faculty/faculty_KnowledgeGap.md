# Knowledge Gaps: Faculty Cluster

**Purpose:** Identify missing knowledge based on failed evaluation questions and system audit.

## Level 1 Gaps (Static Knowledge Missing)

### Gap 1
* **Question:** What are the office hours of Professor X?
* **Failure Reason:** Faculty profile pages provide contact information and research details but do not consistently provide office hours.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Faculty-wise consultation timings and availability schedule.
* **Required Source:** Individual Faculty Members / Department Offices.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 2
* **Question:** Which faculty members are currently accepting students for projects or independent study?
* **Failure Reason:** Faculty research interests are available, but project availability is not published.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Current project openings and student recruitment information.
* **Required Source:** Individual Faculty Members.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 3
* **Question:** Which faculty members are available as mentors for startups and entrepreneurship activities?
* **Failure Reason:** Faculty expertise is available, but mentorship availability is not documented.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Faculty mentorship preferences and startup guidance opportunities.
* **Required Source:** Faculty Members / I-Hub / Incubation Cell.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 4
* **Question:** What courses has Professor X taught in previous semesters?
* **Failure Reason:** Faculty profiles do not consistently contain teaching history.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Course-wise teaching records.
* **Required Source:** Academic Administration Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 5
* **Question:** Which faculty members supervise PhD students in a specific research area?
* **Failure Reason:** Research interests are listed, but supervision information is not consistently available.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Faculty-wise PhD supervision details.
* **Required Source:** Research & Development Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

## Level 2 Gaps (Dynamic Information Missing)

### Gap 6
* **Question:** Has Professor X recently changed research areas or begun working on a new topic?
* **Failure Reason:** Research interests and profiles may not be updated immediately.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Latest research activities and collaborations.
* **Required Source:** Faculty Profile Updates.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 7
* **Question:** What are the latest faculty publications this year?
* **Failure Reason:** Publication lists require continuous updates.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Newly published papers, journals, and conference publications.
* **Required Source:** Faculty Publications Database.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 8
* **Question:** Have any new faculty members joined recently?
* **Failure Reason:** Faculty directories may not reflect changes immediately.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Recent faculty appointments and departures.
* **Required Source:** Dean (Faculty) Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 9
* **Question:** Which faculty members currently hold administrative responsibilities?
* **Failure Reason:** Administrative roles change periodically.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Updated leadership and committee assignments.
* **Required Source:** Administration Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

## Level 3 Gaps (Human-Provided Knowledge Missing)

### Gap 10
* **Question:** Which professor would be most suitable for guidance in a particular career path?
* **Failure Reason:** Faculty expertise is available, but personalized recommendations require human judgment.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Informal mentoring strengths and faculty preferences.
* **Required Source:** Faculty Members / Academic Advisors.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 11
* **Question:** Which faculty members are approachable for undergraduate research discussions?
* **Failure Reason:** Such information is based on student and faculty experiences rather than official documentation.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Informal faculty engagement information.
* **Required Source:** Faculty Members / Senior Students.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 12
* **Question:** What qualities does Professor X expect from students before accepting them for a project?
* **Failure Reason:** Selection expectations are usually communicated personally and are not documented.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Faculty expectations for project participation.
* **Required Source:** Individual Faculty Members.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 13
* **Question:** How responsive is Professor X to emails and meeting requests?
* **Failure Reason:** Communication preferences are not formally documented.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Preferred communication channels and response expectations.
* **Required Source:** Individual Faculty Members.
* **Priority:** Low
