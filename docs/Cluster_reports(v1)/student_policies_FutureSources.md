# Future Sources: Policies & Administration

**Purpose:** Plan future knowledge acquisition to fill the gaps identified in the knowledge base.

### 1. Mess & Canteen Policies
* **Source Type:** Operational Document
* **Person/Committee:** Food & Beverage Services / Hostel Management Committee
* **Information Required:** Official mess timings, mandatory vs. optional mess subscription details, rebate policies, canteen operational hours, and food wastage rules.
* **Expected Outcome:** Clear answers for all food-related queries for hostellers and day scholars.

### 2. Extension of Approved Leaves
* **Source Type:** Administrative Guideline
* **Person/Committee:** Academic Administration Office / Dean (Academics)
* **Information Required:** The exact procedure and timeframe for submitting extension medical certificates if a student has not recovered within the initial approved leave period.
* **Expected Outcome:** Ability for the RAG system to guide students through prolonged illnesses.

### 3. Co-curricular Leave Policy
* **Source Type:** Administrative Policy
* **Person/Committee:** Dean of Students Office
* **Information Required:** The official policy on attendance waivers and the specific document proofs required for participating in external hackathons, conferences, and sports competitions.
* **Expected Outcome:** Accurate guidance for students representing the university externally.

### 4. Detailed Hostel Noise Regulations
* **Source Type:** Committee Rules
* **Person/Committee:** Chief Warden / Hostel Management Committee
* **Information Required:** Explicit quiet hours, speaker-specific rules, and exact fine amounts for noise violations.
* **Expected Outcome:** Resolution of specific hostel discipline queries.

### 5. Hostel Laundry Operations
* **Source Type:** Service Guideline
* **Person/Committee:** Hostel Management Committee
* **Information Required:** The operational details of the campus laundry service including pricing, turnaround time, and vendor contact information.
* **Expected Outcome:** Ability to answer day-to-day utility queries for hostellers.
