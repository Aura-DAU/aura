#  Future Sources: Research Cluster

**Team C – Research Cluster**  
**Done By:** Bhagyashree Khemwani  
**Date:** 2026-06-08  

---

## Purpose
Plan future knowledge acquisition to improve completeness and accuracy of research-related information in the knowledge base.

---

## 1. Detailed Faculty–Research Mapping (Lab-wise)
- **Source Type:** Department Research Records / Lab Documentation  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / Research Lab Coordinators  
- **Information Required:** Structured mapping of faculty members to specific research labs, ongoing projects, and lab-level research focus areas.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Clear lab-wise visibility of research ownership and contributions.

---

## 2. Real-Time Sponsored Project Status Updates
- **Source Type:** Project Management System / Sponsored Research Office  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / Sponsored Projects Office  
- **Information Required:** Live status of funded projects including start date, progress stage, funding agency, milestones, and completion timelines.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Up-to-date tracking of all active research projects.

---

## 3. Research Grant Application & Approval Workflow
- **Source Type:** Institutional Funding Guidelines  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / Finance & Research Committee  
- **Information Required:** Step-by-step workflow for applying, reviewing, approving, and releasing research grants.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Transparent understanding of funding lifecycle.

---

## 4. Publication Classification & Quality Indexing System
- **Source Type:** Academic Publication Database  
- **Person/Committee:** Research Publication Committee  
- **Information Required:** Classification of publications based on journals, conferences, impact factor, indexing (Scopus/Web of Science), and internal quality grading.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Ability to evaluate research output quality, not just quantity.

---

## 5. Research Lab Infrastructure & Resource Inventory
- **Source Type:** Lab Management System / Department Records  
- **Person/Committee:** Lab In-charges / Department Heads  
- **Information Required:** Details of available equipment, software tools, datasets, computing resources, and lab access policies.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Better understanding of research capabilities and constraints.

---

## 6. Student Research Participation Tracking
- **Source Type:** Academic Project Records / Internship Records  
- **Person/Committee:** Faculty Supervisors / Dean (R&D) Office  
- **Information Required:** Record of student involvement in research projects, internships, publications, and assistantship roles.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Visibility of student contribution in research ecosystem.

---

## 7. Patent Filing & Intellectual Property Management System
- **Source Type:** IPR Cell / Legal & Research Office  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / IP Management Committee  
- **Information Required:** Details of patent filing process, approved patents, licensing, ownership rules, and commercialization pathways.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Structured understanding of innovation output and IP management.

---

## 8. Research Collaboration with External Institutes & Industry
- **Source Type:** MoU / Collaboration Records  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / External Relations Office  
- **Information Required:** Active collaborations with other universities, industries, and research organizations including scope and deliverables.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Mapping of external research ecosystem connectivity.

---

## 9. Research Impact & Citation Analytics Dashboard
- **Source Type:** Bibliometric Tools / Institutional Analytics System  
- **Person/Committee:** Research Analytics Cell  
- **Information Required:** Citation counts, h-index tracking, research impact metrics, and global ranking indicators of faculty and departments.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Quantitative measurement of research influence.

---

## 10. Research Funding Source Breakdown
- **Source Type:** Finance + Sponsored Research Records  
- **Person/Committee:** Dean (R&D) / Finance Office  
- **Information Required:** Breakdown of funding sources such as government grants, private funding, international agencies, and internal funding schemes.  
- **Expected Outcome:** Financial transparency of research ecosystem.
