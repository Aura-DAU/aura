# Knowledge Gaps: Research Cluster

**Purpose:** Identify missing knowledge based on failed evaluation questions and system audit.

## Level 1 Gaps (Static Knowledge Missing)

### Gap 1
* **Question:** Which research projects are currently accepting student researchers?
* **Failure Reason:** Research areas and faculty interests are available, but active project openings are not explicitly listed.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Project-wise student recruitment information.
* **Required Source:** Research Labs / Faculty Members.
* **Priority:** High

### Gap 2
* **Question:** What laboratory facilities and equipment are available for a specific research area?
* **Failure Reason:** Research domains are documented, but detailed laboratory infrastructure information is not consistently available.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Lab facilities, equipment lists, and access procedures.
* **Required Source:** Research Labs / Lab Coordinators.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 3
* **Question:** Which faculty members are collaborating on interdisciplinary research projects?
* **Failure Reason:** Faculty profiles and research areas are available separately, but collaboration networks are not documented.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Faculty collaboration and interdisciplinary project information.
* **Required Source:** Research & Development Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 4
* **Question:** Which ongoing research projects have industry partners?
* **Failure Reason:** Sponsored project information may exist, but industry collaboration details are not always publicly available.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Industry partnerships and collaborative projects.
* **Required Source:** Sponsored Research Office.
* **Priority:** Medium

### Gap 5
* **Question:** What datasets, software tools, or research resources are available for students?
* **Failure Reason:** Research outputs are documented, but available resources for participation are not.
* **Missing Knowledge:** Research infrastructure and shared resources.
* **Required Source:** Research Labs / Faculty Members.
* **Priority:** Medium
